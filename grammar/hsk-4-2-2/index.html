语法汇总 Grammar◇“差不多”和“几乎”
“差不多”和“几乎”副词/形容词Adverb / Adjective
chàbuduō / jīhū

差不多 means “almost / about the same”. 几乎 means “almost, nearly”.

结构 Structure差不多 + Adj/数量；几乎 + V/都chàbuduō + Adj/quantity; jīhū + V/dōu = about / almost
我们两个人差不多高。
Wǒmen liǎng ge rén chàbuduō gāo.
We are about the same height.
现在差不多十点了。
Xiànzài chàbuduō shí diǎn le.
It is almost ten now.
他几乎每天都运动。
Tā jīhū měi tiān dōu yùndòng.
He exercises almost every day.
※ “差不多”还能表示两者接近；“几乎”更常修饰动作或范围。差不多 can mean "close/similar"; 几乎 more often modifies an action or scope.
