语法汇总 Grammar◇固定短语“有A有B”
固定短语“有A有B”固定短语Set phrase
gùdìng duǎnyǔ yǒu A yǒu B

Use 有A有B to say a group contains different kinds, often two related or opposite types.

结构 Structure有 + A + 有 + Byǒu + A + yǒu + B = there is both A and B
这些苹果有大有小。
Zhèxiē píngguǒ yǒu dà yǒu xiǎo.
These apples are of different sizes.
参加活动的人有男有女。
Cānjiā huódòng de rén yǒu nán yǒu nǚ.
There are both men and women at the event.
生活有好有坏，我们都要面对。
Shēnghuó yǒu hǎo yǒu huài, wǒmen dōu yào miànduì.
Life has good and bad parts; we have to face them.
※ A和B通常意义相关、相近或相反，如“大/小、男/女、好/坏”。A and B are usually related, similar, or opposite — e.g. 大/小, 男/女, 好/坏.
