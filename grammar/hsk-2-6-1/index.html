疑问代词“怎么”询问原因疑问句Question
zěnme

Use 怎么 before a negative or unexpected action to ask “why / how come”.

结构 Structure怎么 + 不 / 没 + 动词？zěnme (“why / how come”) + bù / méi + Verb?
你怎么不吃了？
nǐ zěnme bù chī le?
Why did you stop eating?
他怎么没来？
tā zěnme méi lái?
Why did he not come?
你怎么不高兴？
nǐ zěnme bù gāoxìng?
Why are you unhappy?
※ HSK1的“怎么”主要问方法；这里学习“怎么”问原因。HSK1's 怎么 asks "how"; here 怎么 asks "why".
