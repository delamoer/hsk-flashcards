“宁可……也不/也要……”表示选择选择复句Alternative clause
nìngkě ... yě bù/yě yào ...

Use 宁可…也不/也要… to choose one option even if it is harder.

结构 Structure宁可 + A，也不/也要 + Bnìngkě + A, yě bù/yě yào + B = would rather A than B
我宁可走路，也不坐这么挤的车。
Wǒ nìngkě zǒulù, yě bú zuò zhème jǐ de chē.
I'd rather walk than take such a crowded bus.
我宁可少睡一会儿，也要把工作做完。
Wǒ nìngkě shǎo shuì yíhuìr, yě yào bǎ gōngzuò zuò wán.
I'd rather sleep less and finish the work.
他宁可自己吃亏，也不让朋友为难。
Tā nìngkě zìjǐ chīkuī, yě bú ràng péngyou wéinán.
He would rather suffer a loss himself than trouble his friend.
※ 用于两种选择中明确偏向一种。Used to clearly favor one of two choices.
