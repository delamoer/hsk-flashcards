语法汇总 Grammar◇让步列举“……也好，……也罢”
让步列举“……也好，……也罢”让步/列举结构Concessive / Listing
... yě hǎo, ... yě bà

Use …也好，…也罢 to mean “whether…or… / be it…or…”, showing the result is the same.

结构 StructureA + 也好，B + 也罢，+ 不变结果A + yě hǎo, B + yě bà, + unchanged result = whether A or B, …
成功也好，失败也罢，我们都要认真总结。
Chénggōng yě hǎo, shībài yě bà, wǒmen dōu yào rènzhēn zǒngjié.
Whether we succeed or fail, we should summarize carefully.
你去也好，不去也罢，都要早点儿决定。
Nǐ qù yě hǎo, bú qù yě bà, dōu yào zǎo diǎnr juédìng.
Whether you go or not, decide early.
工作也好，学习也罢，态度都很重要。
Gōngzuò yě hǎo, xuéxí yě bà, tàidu dōu hěn zhòngyào.
Whether at work or study, attitude is important.
※ A、B通常是两种可能，后面的态度或结果不变。A and B are usually two possibilities; the attitude or result after them stays the same.
