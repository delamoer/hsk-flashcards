介词“朝”表示方向/面对介词/动词Preposition / Verb
cháo

Use 朝 to mean “toward / facing”.

结构 Structure朝 + 方向/人 + V；坐…朝…cháo + direction/person + V; zuò…cháo… = face / towards
他朝我笑了笑。
Tā cháo wǒ xiào le xiào.
He smiled at me.
请朝前走。
Qǐng cháo qián zǒu.
Please walk forward.
这个房间朝南。
Zhège fángjiān cháo nán.
This room faces south.
※ 和“往”接近；“朝”还可表示建筑物朝向。Close to 往; 朝 can also mean which way a building faces.
