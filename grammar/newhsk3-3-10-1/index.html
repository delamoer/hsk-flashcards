“把”字句（1）：把 + 宾语 + 动词 + 在/到 + 地点把字句把 sentence
bǎ zì jù (yī)

Use 把 when an action moves or puts a specific object in a place.

结构 Structure主语 + 把 + 宾语 + 动词 + 在/到 + 地点Subject + bǎ + Object + Verb + zài/dào + Place
请把书放在桌子上。
Qǐng bǎ shū fàng zài zhuō zi shàng.
Please put the book on the table.
我把水果放到冰箱里了。
Wǒ bǎ shuǐ guǒ fàng dào bīng xiāng lǐ le.
I put the fruit in the refrigerator.
他把手机放在书包里。
Tā bǎ shǒu jī fàng zài shū bāo lǐ.
He put the phone in the schoolbag.
※ “把”后的宾语通常是明确、已知的事物；本点重点是动作后的地点。The 把 object is definite; focus here is the place after the verb.
