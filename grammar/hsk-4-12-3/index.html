“对于”和“关于”介词Preposition
duìyú / guānyú

对于 means “regarding / toward” a target of attitude. 关于 means “about” a topic.

结构 Structure对于 + 对象 + 评价；关于 + 话题 + 内容duìyú + object + judgement; guānyú + topic + content = regarding (an object) vs about (a topic)
对于这个问题，我有不同的看法。
Duìyú zhège wèntí, wǒ yǒu bùtóng de kànfǎ.
Regarding this question, I have a different view.
关于明天的会议，我想说两句。
Guānyú míngtiān de huìyì, wǒ xiǎng shuō liǎng jù.
About tomorrow’s meeting, I want to say a few words.
他对于中国文化很感兴趣。
Tā duìyú Zhōngguó wénhuà hěn gǎn xìngqù.
He is very interested in Chinese culture.
※ 配套课程标注为HSK5扩展；“对于”偏对象，“关于”偏话题。HSK5 extension: 对于 targets an object, 关于 a topic.
