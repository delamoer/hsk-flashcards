离合词（1）离合词Separable verb
líhécí (yī)

Some two-part verbs can be separated by words such as 了 or a quantity.

结构 Structure动词成分 + 了/数量 + 名词成分Verb Part + le / Quantity + Noun Part
我吃了饭。
Wǒ chī le fàn.
I ate.
他上了课。
Tā shàng le kè.
He attended class.
我睡了一觉。
Wǒ shuì le yí jiào.
I had a sleep.
※ 离合词形式像一个词，但中间可以插入“了、数量”等成分。Separable words look like one word but can take 了/quantity inside.
