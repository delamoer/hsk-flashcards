语法汇总 Grammar◇转折连接语“反之”
转折连接语“反之”连接语Linking phrase
fǎnzhī

Use 反之 to mean “conversely / the opposite is also true”.

结构 StructureA；反之，B（与A相反）A; fǎnzhī, B (the opposite of A) = A; conversely, B
运动有助于健康；反之，长期不运动会带来问题。
Yùndòng yǒuzhùyú jiànkāng; fǎnzhī, chángqī bú yùndòng huì dàilái wèntí.
Exercise helps health; conversely, long-term inactivity causes problems.
信任能改善关系，反之，不信任会让关系更差。
Xìnrèn néng gǎishàn guānxi, fǎnzhī, bú xìnrèn huì ràng guānxi gèng chà.
Trust can improve a relationship; conversely, distrust makes it worse.
准备充分更容易成功；反之，准备不足风险更大。
Zhǔnbèi chōngfèn gèng róngyì chénggōng; fǎnzhī, zhǔnbèi bùzú fēngxiǎn gèng dà.
Good preparation makes success more likely; conversely, poor preparation increases risk.
※ “反之”连接与前文相反的情况，后面常有停顿。反之 introduces the opposite of the previous situation and is usually followed by a pause.
