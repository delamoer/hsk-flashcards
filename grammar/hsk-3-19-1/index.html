趋向补语的引申义：“出来 / 下来 / 起来”趋向补语Directional compl.
qūxiàng bǔyǔ de yǐnshēnyì

Direction complements can have extended meanings: 出来 can show creation/recognition, 下来 a change becoming calmer/slower, and 起来 recalling.

结构 StructureV + 出来 / Adj-V + 下来 / V + 起来V + chūlai / Adj-V + xiàlai / V + qǐlai = figure out / calm down / start doing
我想出来一个好办法。
Wǒ xiǎng chūlai yí ge hǎo bànfǎ.
I came up with a good idea.
你看出来他是谁了吗？
Nǐ kàn chūlai tā shì shéi le ma?
Did you recognize who he is?
我想起来他的名字了。
Wǒ xiǎng qǐlai tā de míngzi le.
I remembered his name.
※ HSK3重点掌握“出来=产生/识别”，“下来=状态由强到弱/由动到静”，“起来=回忆有结果”。Focus: 出来 = produce/identify, 下来 = fading/settling, 起来 = successful recall.
