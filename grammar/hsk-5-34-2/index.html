趋向补语“V过”表示经过/越过趋向补语Directional compl.
V guò

Use V过 to show movement past/over something.

结构 StructureV + 过 + 地点/物体V + guò + place/object = pass / cross over sth
他跑过马路。
Tā pǎo guò mǎlù.
He ran across the road.
我每天都要走过这座桥。
Wǒ měi tiān dōu yào zǒu guò zhè zuò qiáo.
I walk across this bridge every day.
汽车从我身边开过。
Qìchē cóng wǒ shēnbiān kāi guò.
The car drove past me.
※ 这里的“过”是方向/路径，不是经历助词“过”。Here 过 is direction/path, not the experiential particle 过.
