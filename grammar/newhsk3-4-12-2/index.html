语法汇总 Grammar◇双重否定
双重否定否定结构Negation
shuāngchóng fǒudìng

Two negatives together can give a strong positive meaning.

结构 Structure没有……不……；不能不……；不得不……méiyǒu…bù…; bù néng bù…; bùdébù… = double negative = a strong affirmative
没有人不喜欢休息。
Méiyǒu rén bù xǐhuan xiūxi.
Everyone likes to rest.
这么重要的事，我不能不去。
Zhème zhòngyào de shì, wǒ bù néng bú qù.
This is so important that I have to go.
下雨了，我们不得不回家。
Xià yǔ le, wǒmen bùdébù huí jiā.
It rained, so we had to go home.
※ 两个否定词放在一起，常表示肯定，而且语气较强。Two negatives together make a positive — and a strong one.
