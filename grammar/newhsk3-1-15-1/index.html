语法汇总 Grammar◇并列复句“……，还 / 也……”
并列复句“……，还 / 也……”并列复句Coordinate clause
bìngliè fùjù ... hái / yě ...

Use 也 to add the same kind of information; use 还 to add one more related point.

结构 StructureA……，B也…… / A……，还……A..., B yě... / A..., hái... ("also / in addition")
我喜欢这个，也喜欢那个。
Wǒ xǐhuan zhège, yě xǐhuan nàge.
I like this one, and I also like that one.
我喜欢喝茶，还喜欢吃中国菜。
Wǒ xǐhuan hē chá, hái xǐhuan chī Zhōngguó cài.
I like drinking tea, and I also like Chinese food.
王老师是北京人，李文也是北京人。
Wáng lǎoshī shì Běijīng rén, Lǐ Wén yě shì Běijīng rén.
Teacher Wang is from Beijing, and Li Wen is also from Beijing.
※ “也”常表示另一人/事物情况相同；“还”常表示在已有信息上再增加一项。也 marks "same for another"; 还 adds one more item.
