“不如 / 与其……不如……”表示比较选择比较/选择复句Comparison / Alternative
bùrú / yǔqí ... bùrú ...

Use 不如 for “not as good as”; 与其…不如… means “rather than…, better to…”.

结构 StructureA 不如 B；与其 + A，不如 + BA bùrú B; yǔqí + A, bùrú + B = A is not as good as B / rather than A, better B
这家饭店不如那家安静。
Zhè jiā fàndiàn bùrú nà jiā ānjìng.
This restaurant is not as quiet as that one.
与其在家等，不如出去看看。
Yǔqí zài jiā děng, bùrú chūqu kànkan.
Rather than wait at home, it's better to go out.
坐车不如走路方便。
Zuò chē bùrú zǒulù fāngbiàn.
Taking a car is not as convenient as walking.
※ “与其”后是较不想选的做法，“不如”后是更好的选择。与其 introduces the less-preferred option; 不如 the better one.
