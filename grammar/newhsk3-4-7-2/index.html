语法汇总 Grammar◇因果复句“既然……，就……”
因果复句“既然……，就……”因果复句Cause–effect clause
yīnguǒ fùjù jìrán ... jiù ...

Use 既然…就… when a fact is already clear, so a result or decision follows.

结构 Structure既然 + 已知事实，就 + 结果/建议jìrán + known fact, jiù + result/suggestion = since …, then …
既然你累了，就早点儿休息吧。
Jìrán nǐ lèi le, jiù zǎo diǎnr xiūxi ba.
Since you are tired, go to bed early.
既然决定了，就别担心了。
Jìrán juédìng le, jiù bié dānxīn le.
Since you have decided, do not worry.
既然大家都同意，我们就开始吧。
Jìrán dàjiā dōu tóngyì, wǒmen jiù kāishǐ ba.
Since everyone agrees, let us begin.
※ “既然”后的情况通常是已经知道或已经确定的。What follows 既然 is something already known or already settled.
