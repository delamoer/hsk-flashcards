语法汇总 Grammar◇副词“一经”
副词“一经”副词/条件结构Adverb / Conditional
yījīng

Use 一经 to mean “once”, showing that once an action happens, a result will follow.

结构 Structure一经 + 动作，就/便 + 结果yìjīng + action, jiù/biàn + result = once …, then …
这个规定一经公布，就立即生效。
Zhège guīdìng yìjīng gōngbù, jiù lìjí shēngxiào.
Once this rule is announced, it takes effect immediately.
产品一经售出，不能随意退换。
Chǎnpǐn yìjīng shòuchū, bù néng suíyì tuìhuàn.
Once the product is sold, it cannot be returned freely.
消息一经确认，我们就通知大家。
Xiāoxi yìjīng quèrèn, wǒmen jiù tōngzhī dàjiā.
Once the news is confirmed, we will inform everyone.
※ 较正式，常用于规则、新闻、说明等。Fairly formal; common in rules, news and instructions.
