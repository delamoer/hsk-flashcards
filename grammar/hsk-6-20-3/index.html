语法汇总 Grammar◇选择格式“与其……倒不如……”
选择格式“与其……倒不如……”选择复句Alternative clause
yǔqí ... dào bùrú ...

Use 与其…倒不如… to mean “rather than…, it would be better to…”.

结构 Structure与其 + A，倒不如 + Byǔqí + A, dào bùrú + B = rather than A, it'd be better to B
与其一直担心，倒不如马上行动。
Yǔqí yìzhí dānxīn, dào bùrú mǎshàng xíngdòng.
Rather than keep worrying, it is better to act now.
与其开车堵在路上，倒不如坐地铁。
Yǔqí kāichē dǔ zài lù shang, dào bùrú zuò dìtiě.
Rather than get stuck driving, it is better to take the subway.
与其勉强答应，倒不如直接说明。
Yǔqí miǎnqiǎng dāying, dào bùrú zhíjiē shuōmíng.
Rather than agree unwillingly, it is better to explain directly.
※ “倒不如”比“不如”更有“反而选择这个更好”的语气。倒不如 adds more of a "better to choose this instead" tone than 不如.
