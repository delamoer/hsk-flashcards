语法汇总 Grammar◇频率副词“再次”
频率副词“再次”频率副词Frequency adverb
pínlǜ fùcí zàicì

Use 再次 to mean “again / once again”. It is more formal than 再.

结构 Structure再次 + 动词zàicì + Verb = once again (formal)
谢谢你再次帮助我。
Xièxie nǐ zàicì bāngzhù wǒ.
Thank you for helping me again.
我们再次见面了。
Wǒmen zàicì jiànmiàn le.
We met again.
老师再次提醒大家注意安全。
Lǎoshī zàicì tíxǐng dàjiā zhùyì ānquán.
The teacher reminded everyone about safety again.
※ “再次”较正式，常用于书面语、通知、讲话等；口语中“再”更常见。再次 is formal, used in writing, notices and speeches; speech prefers 再.
