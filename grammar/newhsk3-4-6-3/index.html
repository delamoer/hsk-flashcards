语法汇总 Grammar◇频率副词“偶尔”
频率副词“偶尔”频率副词Frequency adverb
pínlǜ fùcí ǒu’ěr

Use 偶尔 to mean “occasionally / once in a while”.

结构 Structure偶尔 + 动词ǒu'ěr + Verb = occasionally
我偶尔喝咖啡。
Wǒ ǒu'ěr hē kāfēi.
I occasionally drink coffee.
周末我偶尔去爬山。
Zhōumò wǒ ǒu'ěr qù páshān.
I sometimes go hiking on weekends.
他工作很忙，只能偶尔回家。
Tā gōngzuò hěn máng, zhǐ néng ǒu'ěr huí jiā.
He is very busy and can only go home occasionally.
※ “偶尔”表示次数少；和“经常、常常”相反。偶尔 means “occasionally” — the opposite of 经常 / 常常.
