动词、动词性短语、主谓短语作定语定语Attributive
dòngcí, dòngcíxìng duǎnyǔ, zhǔwèi duǎnyǔ zuò dìngyǔ

Put a verb or phrase + 的 before a noun to describe which person or thing you mean.

结构 Structure动词/短语 + 的 + 名词Verb / Phrase + de + Noun
这是我买的书。
Zhè shì wǒ mǎi de shū.
This is the book I bought.
学中文的学生很多。
Xué zhōng wén de xué sheng hěn duō.
There are many students who study Chinese.
这是朋友给我的杯子。
Zhè shì péng you gěi wǒ de bēi zi.
This is the cup my friend gave me.
※ 定语较长时通常要用“的”。Use 的 when the modifier is long.
