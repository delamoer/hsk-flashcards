概数表达法概数表达Approx. number
gàishù biǎodáfǎ

Use nearby numbers or words such as 来/左右 to give an approximate number.

结构 Structure相邻数字 + 量词；数量 + 来/左右Adjacent numbers + Measure Word; Quantity + lái/zuǒyòu (approximate number)
我等了两三分钟。
Wǒ děng le liǎng sān fēn zhōng.
I waited for two or three minutes.
教室里有三四十个人。
Jiào shì lǐ yǒu sān sì shí ge rén.
There are about thirty or forty people in the classroom.
他三十岁左右。
Tā sān shí suì zuǒ yòu.
He is around thirty years old.
※ 概数表示大概数量。本级重点复习相邻数字连用，并理解“来、左右”等常见约数形式。Approximate numbers: review adjacent-number pairs and forms like 来/左右.
