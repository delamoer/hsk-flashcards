简单趋向补语（2）：带宾语趋向补语Directional compl.
jiǎndān qūxiàng bǔyǔ (èr)

When a direction complement has an object, the object position depends on whether it is a place or a thing.

结构 Structure地点：V + 地点 + 来/去；事物：V + 来/去 + 事物 / V + 事物 + 来/去Place: V + Place + lái/qù; Thing: V + lái/qù + Thing OR V + Thing + lái/qù
快进教室来吧。
Kuài jìn jiào shì lái ba.
Come into the classroom quickly.
爸爸买回了很多水果。
Bà ba mǎi huí le hěn duō shuǐ guǒ.
Dad brought back a lot of fruit.
她给我送来了一本书。
Tā gěi wǒ sòng lái le yì běn shū.
She brought me a book.
※ 地点名词通常放在“来/去”前；事物宾语的位置更灵活。A place noun goes before 来/去; a thing object is more flexible.
