语法汇总 Grammar◇并列复句“一会儿……，一会儿……”
并列复句“一会儿……，一会儿……”并列复句Coordinate clause
yíhuìr ... yíhuìr ...

Use 一会儿…一会儿… when two actions or states alternate.

结构 Structure一会儿 + A，一会儿 + Byíhuìr + A, yíhuìr + B (now A, now B)
天气一会儿晴，一会儿下雨。
Tiān qì yí huìr qíng, yí huìr xià yǔ.
The weather is sunny one moment and rainy the next.
他一会儿看书，一会儿看手机。
Tā yí huìr kàn shū, yí huìr kàn shǒu jī.
He reads for a while, then looks at his phone.
孩子一会儿笑，一会儿哭。
Hái zi yí huìr xiào, yí huìr kū.
The child laughs one moment and cries the next.
※ 重点是两个动作或状态交替出现，不是同时发生。The point is two actions alternate, not happen at once.
