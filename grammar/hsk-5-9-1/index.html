动词“算”表示可以看作/勉强算动词Verb
suàn

Use 算 to mean “count as / be considered / can be regarded as”.

结构 Structure算 + N/Adj；算是 + Nsuàn + N/Adj; suànshì + N = count as / can be considered
这次算成功了。
Zhè cì suàn chénggōng le.
This can be considered a success.
他算是我的老朋友。
Tā suànshì wǒ de lǎo péngyou.
He can be considered an old friend of mine.
这个价格不算贵。
Zhège jiàgé bú suàn guì.
This price is not considered expensive.
※ “算”常表示按某个标准判断。算 often means to judge by some standard.
