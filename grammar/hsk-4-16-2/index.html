语法汇总 Grammar◇“恐怕”和“怕”
“恐怕”和“怕”副词/动词Adverb / Verb
kǒngpà / pà

恐怕 means “I’m afraid / probably”. 怕 can mean “be afraid” or “worry that”.

结构 Structure恐怕 + 句子；怕 + 名词/句子kǒngpà + Sentence; pà + Noun/Sentence = I'm afraid … / be afraid of …
恐怕今天来不及了。
Kǒngpà jīntiān lái bu jí le.
I am afraid there is not enough time today.
我怕坐飞机。
Wǒ pà zuò fēijī.
I am afraid of flying.
他怕明天会下雨。
Tā pà míngtiān huì xià yǔ.
He worries that it may rain tomorrow.
※ “恐怕”常是委婉地表达担心或不好的判断。恐怕 politely expresses worry or a bad guess.
