语法汇总 Grammar◇时间词的顺序
时间词的顺序时间语序Time word order
shíjiān cí de shùnxù

When there is more than one time expression, put the larger time range before the smaller one.

结构 Structure较大时间单位 + 较小时间单位Larger time unit + Smaller time unit
我早上六点起床。
Wǒ zǎoshang liù diǎn qǐchuáng.
I get up at six in the morning.
我们中午十二点半吃饭。
Wǒmen zhōngwǔ shí'èr diǎn bàn chī fàn.
We eat at 12:30 at noon.
他晚上九点下班。
Tā wǎnshang jiǔ diǎn xiàbān.
He gets off work at nine in the evening.
※ 教材原则：时间范围大的词在前，范围小的词在后。The larger time unit comes first, the smaller one after it.
