连词/副词“并”连词/副词Conjunction / Adverb
liáncí/fùcí bìng

并 can link actions in formal Chinese. Before 不/没, it strongly means “actually not / not at all”.

结构 StructureA并B；并不/并没 + 谓语A bìng B; bìng bù / bìng méi + predicate = and (formal) / not at all
请检查并保存文件。
Qǐng jiǎnchá bìng bǎocún wénjiàn.
Please check and save the file.
这个问题并不难。
Zhège wèntí bìng bù nán.
This problem is actually not difficult.
我并没有忘记你的话。
Wǒ bìng méiyǒu wàngjì nǐ de huà.
I did not forget what you said.
※ 教材同时介绍“并”的连词和副词用法；“并不/并没”常用来加强否定。The book covers 并 as both a conjunction and an adverb; 并不 / 并没 strengthen a negation.
