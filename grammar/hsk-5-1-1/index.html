“如何”表示方式/办法疑问/书面表达Question / Formal
rúhé

Use 如何 to ask or talk about “how”. It is more formal than 怎么.

结构 Structure如何 + V / 如何 + 处理…rúhé + V / rúhé + deal with … = how to … (formal)
我们应该如何解决这个问题？
Wǒmen yīnggāi rúhé jiějué zhège wèntí?
How should we solve this problem?
我不知道如何回答。
Wǒ bù zhīdào rúhé huídá.
I do not know how to answer.
你打算如何安排时间？
Nǐ dǎsuàn rúhé ānpái shíjiān?
How do you plan to arrange your time?
※ “如何”较书面，“怎么”更口语。如何 is more formal; 怎么 is more colloquial.
