语法汇总 Grammar◇转折复句“……，然而……”
转折复句“……，然而……”转折复句Adversative clause
zhuǎnzhé fùjù ... rán’ér ...

Use 然而 for a formal “however / but”.

结构 StructureA，然而 + BA, rán'ér + B = A; however, B (written)
他很努力，然而结果并不理想。
Tā hěn nǔlì, rán'ér jiéguǒ bìng bù lǐxiǎng.
He worked hard; however, the result was not ideal.
外面很冷，然而很多人还在运动。
Wàimiàn hěn lěng, rán'ér hěn duō rén hái zài yùndòng.
It is cold outside; however, many people are still exercising.
这个办法很简单，然而不一定适合每个人。
Zhège bànfǎ hěn jiǎndān, rán'ér bù yídìng shìhé měi ge rén.
This method is simple; however, it may not suit everyone.
※ “然而”比“但是/不过”更书面。然而 is more written than 但是 / 不过.
