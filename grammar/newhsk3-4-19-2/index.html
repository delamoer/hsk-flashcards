固定格式“动词 + 一×是一×”固定格式Fixed pattern
gùdìng géshì dòngcí + yī X shì yī X

Use V + 一X是一X to mean “do as much as possible, even if it is only a little”.

结构 Structure（能）+ 动词 + 一点儿/一页/一道…… + 是 + 一点儿/一页/一道……(néng) + Verb + yìdiǎnr/yí yè/yí dào… + shì + yìdiǎnr/yí yè/yí dào… = do as much as you can manage
今天能学一点儿是一点儿。
Jīntiān néng xué yìdiǎnr shì yìdiǎnr.
Today, learn as much as you can, even if it is only a little.
这本书很厚，看一页是一页。
Zhè běn shū hěn hòu, kàn yí yè shì yí yè.
This book is thick; read as many pages as you can.
题很多，能做一道是一道。
Tí hěn duō, néng zuò yí dào shì yí dào.
There are many questions; do as many as you can.
※ 教材用这一格式强调在一定条件下尽力去做，能完成多少就完成多少。This pattern means doing as much as you can under the circumstances.
