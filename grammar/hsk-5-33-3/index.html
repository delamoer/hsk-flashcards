“与其……（倒）不如/宁可……”表示选择选择复句Alternative clause
yǔqí ... (dào) bùrú / nìngkě ...

Use 与其…不如/宁可… to say “rather than A, better/prefer B”.

结构 Structure与其 + A，不如/宁可 + Byǔqí + A, bùrú/nìngkě + B = rather than A, better / would rather B
与其等别人，不如自己做。
Yǔqí děng biérén, bùrú zìjǐ zuò.
Rather than wait for others, it's better to do it yourself.
与其开车堵在路上，倒不如坐地铁。
Yǔqí kāichē dǔ zài lù shang, dào bùrú zuò dìtiě.
Rather than be stuck driving, it is better to take the subway.
与其后悔，我宁可现在试一试。
Yǔqí hòuhuǐ, wǒ nìngkě xiànzài shì yi shì.
Rather than regret it, I'd rather try now.
※ “与其”提出放弃的选择，后面给更愿意选择的方案。与其 gives the rejected option, then the preferred one.
