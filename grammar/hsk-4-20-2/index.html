“一……就……”表示一发生就马上……承接结构Sequential structure
yī ... jiù ...

Use 一…就… for “as soon as…”.

结构 Structure一 + V1，就 + V2yī + V1, jiù + V2 = as soon as V1, then V2
我一到家就给你打电话。
Wǒ yí dào jiā jiù gěi nǐ dǎ diànhuà.
I will call you as soon as I get home.
他一听就明白了。
Tā yì tīng jiù míngbai le.
He understood as soon as he heard it.
孩子一看到妈妈就笑了。
Háizi yí kàndào māma jiù xiào le.
The child smiled as soon as he saw Mom.
※ 两个动作相隔时间很短。The two actions are only a very short time apart.
