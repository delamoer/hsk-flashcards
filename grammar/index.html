疑问代词“什么”疑问句Question
shénme

Use 什么 to ask “what”. Put it where the unknown thing would normally go.

结构 Structure… + 什么 + …… + shénme (“what”) + …
你叫什么名字？
nǐ jiào shénme míngzi?
What is your name?
这是什么？
zhè shì shénme?
What is this?
你学习什么？
nǐ xuéxí shénme?
What do you study?
※ 疑问词句末一般不用“吗”。With a question word, you normally don't add 吗 at the end.
