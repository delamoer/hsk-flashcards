“连……都/也……”表示强调强调结构Emphatic structure
lián ... dōu/yě ...

Use 连…都/也… to mean “even…”, emphasizing an extreme example.

结构 Structure连 + 强调对象 + 都/也 + 谓语lián + the highlighted item + dōu/yě + predicate = even … does/doesn't …
这个问题连老师都不知道。
Zhège wèntí lián lǎoshī dōu bù zhīdào.
Even the teacher does not know this question.
他忙得连饭都没吃。
Tā máng de lián fàn dōu méi chī.
He was so busy that he did not even eat.
这么简单的字，连孩子也会写。
Zhème jiǎndān de zì, lián háizi yě huì xiě.
Even a child can write such a simple character.
※ “连”提出最意外或最极端的例子，“都/也”呼应。连 raises the most surprising/extreme case, echoed by 都/也.
