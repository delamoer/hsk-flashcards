状态形容词“通红、雪白”等状态形容词Vivid adjective
tōnghóng, xuěbái děng

Some fixed forms use a noun/adverb-like element to strongly describe color or state.

结构 Structure通/雪/冰/笔 + 单音节形容词tōng/xuě/bīng/bǐ + one-syllable Adj = vividly … (bright red, snow-white, …)
她跑得脸通红。
Tā pǎo de liǎn tōnghóng.
Her face turned bright red from running.
山顶的雪雪白雪白的。
Shāndǐng de xuě xuěbái xuěbái de.
The snow on the mountaintop is pure white.
她穿着一件雪白的衬衫。
Tā chuānzhe yí jiàn xuěbái de chènshān.
She is wearing a snow-white shirt.
※ 这类词强调状态很鲜明，很多是固定搭配，需要整体记忆。These words paint a vivid state; many are set combinations best learned as whole words.
