“在……看来”表示观点介词结构Prep. phrase
zài ... kànlái

Use 在…看来 to mean “in someone’s view / in the opinion of…”.

结构 Structure在 + 人 + 看来，……zài + person + kàn lái, … = in sb's view, …
在我看来，健康最重要。
Zài wǒ kàn lái, jiànkāng zuì zhòngyào.
In my view, health is the most important.
在老师看来，他进步很大。
Zài lǎoshī kàn lái, tā jìnbù hěn dà.
In the teacher’s view, he has improved a lot.
在很多人看来，这个办法不错。
Zài hěn duō rén kàn lái, zhège bànfǎ búcuò.
In many people’s view, this method is good.
※ 用于明确说明一个观点是从谁的角度出发。Makes clear whose viewpoint an opinion comes from.
