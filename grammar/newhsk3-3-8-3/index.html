时量补语（2）：表示动作结束后到现在的时间时量补语Duration complement
shíliàng bǔyǔ (èr)

Use a duration after a completed/change verb + 了 to say how long it has been since that event.

结构 Structure主语 + 动词/变化 + 时段 + 了Subject + Verb/Change + Duration + le
我来中国两年了。
Wǒ lái zhōng guó liǎng nián le.
It has been two years since I came to China.
他毕业三年了。
Tā bì yè sān nián le.
It has been three years since he graduated.
开学三个星期了。
Kāi xué sān ge xīng qī le.
It has been three weeks since school started.
※ 本点强调“从某动作完成/某变化发生，到现在已经过了多久”。Focus: how long it's been since an action finished or a change occurred.
