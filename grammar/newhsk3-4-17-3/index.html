语法汇总 Grammar◇兼语句“主语 + 表扬/批评 + 宾语1 + 动词 + 宾语2”
兼语句“主语 + 表扬/批评 + 宾语1 + 动词 + 宾语2”兼语句Pivotal sentence
jiānyǔ jù: zhǔyǔ + biǎoyáng/pīpíng + bīnyǔ1 + dòngcí + bīnyǔ2

Use 表扬/批评 + person + verb phrase to praise or criticize what that person did.

结构 Structure主语 + 表扬/批评 + 人 + 动词 + 其他成分Subject + biǎoyáng/pīpíng + person + Verb + other elements = praise / criticise sb for doing sth
教练表扬他取得了很大的进步。
Jiàoliàn biǎoyáng tā qǔdé le hěn dà de jìnbù.
The coach praised him for making great progress.
老师表扬白家月坚持每天学习中文。
Lǎoshī biǎoyáng Bái Jiāyuè jiānchí měi tiān xuéxí Zhōngwén.
The teacher praised Bai Jiayue for studying Chinese every day.
经理批评他没有按时完成工作。
Jīnglǐ pīpíng tā méiyǒu ànshí wánchéng gōngzuò.
The manager criticized him for not finishing the work on time.
※ 宾语1是被表扬或批评的人，后面的动词短语说明评价的是他做的什么事。Object 1 is the person praised or criticised; the verb phrase says what for.
