“使、叫、让”表示致使兼语句Pivotal sentence
shǐ / jiào / ràng

Use 使/叫/让 + person/thing + verb/adjective to mean “make / let”.

结构 Structure使 / 叫 / 让 + 人/事物 + 谓语shǐ / jiào / ràng + person/thing + predicate = make / let sb do sth
这件事让我很高兴。
Zhè jiàn shì ràng wǒ hěn gāoxìng.
This makes me very happy.
妈妈让我早点儿回家。
Māma ràng wǒ zǎo diǎnr huí jiā.
Mom makes/asks me to go home early.
运动使身体更健康。
Yùndòng shǐ shēntǐ gèng jiànkāng.
Exercise makes the body healthier.
※ “使”较书面；“叫、让”更口语。三者都可以表示“使某人/某物产生某种结果”。使 is more formal; 叫/让 are colloquial; all mean "cause someone/something to…".
