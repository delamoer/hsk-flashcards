语法汇总 Grammar◇助词“而已”
助词“而已”助词Particle
éryǐ

Use 而已 after a statement to mean “only / nothing more”.

结构 Structure小句 + 而已Clause + éryǐ = only … / that's all
我只是随便问问而已。
Wǒ zhǐshì suíbiàn wènwen éryǐ.
I was only asking casually.
这只是一个建议而已。
Zhè zhǐshì yí ge jiànyì éryǐ.
This is only a suggestion.
他开个玩笑而已，你别生气。
Tā kāi ge wánxiào éryǐ, nǐ bié shēngqì.
He was only joking; do not be angry.
※ 放在句末，弱化事情的重要性或范围。Goes at the end of the sentence and plays down the importance or scope.
