“第 + 数词”表示顺序序数Ordinal
dì + shùcí

Put 第 before a number to mean “first, second, third…”.

结构 Structure第 + 数词 (+ 量词/名词)dì + Number (+ Measure word / Noun) = ordinal number (1st, 2nd, 3rd ...)
这是第一课。
zhè shì dì yī kè.
This is Lesson 1.
他是第一个来的。
tā shì dì yī ge lái de.
He was the first to come.
今天是我们认识的第一天。
jīntiān shì wǒmen rènshi de dì yī tiān.
Today is the first day we have known each other.
※ “第”把基数词变成序数词。第 turns a cardinal number into an ordinal.
