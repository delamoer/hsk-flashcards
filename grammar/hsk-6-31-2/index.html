语法汇总 Grammar◇插入语“归根到底”
插入语“归根到底”插入语Parenthetical
guīgēn dàodǐ

Use 归根到底 to mean “in the final analysis / fundamentally”.

结构 Structure归根到底，+ 最根本的原因/结论guīgēn dàodǐ, + root cause / conclusion = in the final analysis, …
归根到底，健康最重要。
Guīgēn dàodǐ, jiànkāng zuì zhòngyào.
In the final analysis, health is the most important.
这个问题归根到底还是沟通不够。
Zhège wèntí guīgēn dàodǐ háishi gōutōng bú gòu.
Fundamentally, this problem is still caused by poor communication.
方法很多，归根到底还是要坚持。
Fāngfǎ hěn duō, guīgēn dàodǐ háishi yào jiānchí.
There are many methods, but in the end you still need persistence.
※ 用于总结最根本的原因、本质或结论。Sums up the most basic cause, nature or conclusion.
