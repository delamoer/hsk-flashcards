“V着V着……”表示动作进行中出现变化进行结构Progressive structure
V-zhe V-zhe

Use V着V着… when something happens while an action is continuing.

结构 StructureV着V着，+ 新情况V zhe V zhe, + new situation = while doing …, suddenly …
他走着走着，突然停下来了。
Tā zǒu zhe zǒu zhe, tūrán tíng xiàlai le.
He was walking when he suddenly stopped.
我看着看着就睡着了。
Wǒ kàn zhe kàn zhe jiù shuìzháo le.
I fell asleep while watching.
我们聊着聊着就忘了时间。
Wǒmen liáo zhe liáo zhe jiù wàng le shíjiān.
We were chatting and forgot the time.
※ 表示一个动作持续一段时间后发生新的情况。Shows a new situation arising after an action goes on for a while.
