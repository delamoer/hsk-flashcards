语法汇总 Grammar◇因为……，所以……
因为……，所以……因果复句Cause–effect clause
yīnwèi ... suǒyǐ ...

Use 因为…所以… to mean “because…so…”. The first part gives the reason.

结构 Structure因为 + 原因，所以 + 结果因为 (yīnwèi, because) + Reason, 所以 (suǒyǐ, so) + Result
因为下雨，所以我没出去。
Yīnwèi xià yǔ, suǒyǐ wǒ méi chūqu.
Because it rained, I did not go out.
因为他生病了，所以今天没来。
Yīnwèi tā shēngbìng le, suǒyǐ jīntiān méi lái.
Because he is sick, he did not come today.
因为这本书很有意思，所以我买了。
Yīnwèi zhè běn shū hěn yǒu yìsi, suǒyǐ wǒ mǎi le.
I bought this book because it is interesting.
※ “因为”提出原因，“所以”引出结果；汉语允许在语境清楚时省略其中一个，但初学先练完整形式。因为 gives the reason, 所以 the result. When the context is clear one of them can be dropped, but beginners should practice the full form first.
