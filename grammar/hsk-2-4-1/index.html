“是……的”强调动作的执行者强调句Emphatic sentence
shì ... de

Use 是…的 to emphasize who did a completed action.

结构 Structure主语 + 是 + 人 + 动词 + 的Subject + shì + Person + Verb + de = “It was [person] who ...”
这个工作是他帮我介绍的。
zhè ge gōngzuò shì tā bāng wǒ jièshào de.
He was the one who introduced this job to me.
这本书是老师给我的。
zhè běn shū shì lǎoshī gěi wǒ de.
The teacher was the one who gave me this book.
晚饭是妈妈做的。
wǎnfàn shì māma zuò de.
Mom was the one who made dinner.
※ 用于已经发生的事情，强调“谁做的”。Used for a past event, stressing "who did it".
