“有”字句（2）：“有 + 数量”表示达到某数量有字句有 sentence
yǒu zì jù (èr)

Use 有 + a number/amount to mean “reach / have as much as…”.

结构 Structure主语 + 有 + 数量 +（时间/年龄等）Subject + yǒu + Amount + (time / age, etc.) = reach / have as much as...
我有一年没回国了。
Wǒ yǒu yì nián méi huí guó le.
I have not returned home for a year.
她今年有十岁了吗？
Tā jīn nián yǒu shí suì le ma?
Is she already ten years old this year?
我有一个月没给家里打电话了。
Wǒ yǒu yí ge yuè méi gěi jiā lǐ dǎ diàn huà le.
I have not called home for a month.
※ 第一册“有（1）”主要表示拥有/存在；这里的“有”表示数量达到某个程度。Book 1's 有 meant have/exist; here 有 means reaching a certain amount.
