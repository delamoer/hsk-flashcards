语法汇总 Grammar◇量词“番”
量词“番”动量词Verbal measure word
fān

Use 番 after an action or noun to mean “a round / an occasion of effort, discussion, thought, etc.”.

结构 Structure一番 + 名词/动词性成分yì fān + Noun / verbal phrase = a round of … (effort, talk, thought)
经过一番讨论，我们终于决定了。
Jīngguò yì fān tǎolùn, wǒmen zhōngyú juédìng le.
After a round of discussion, we finally decided.
他为这件事费了一番心思。
Tā wèi zhè jiàn shì fèile yì fān xīnsi.
He put a lot of thought into this matter.
老师对我进行了一番鼓励。
Lǎoshī duì wǒ jìnxíngle yì fān gǔlì.
The teacher gave me some encouragement.
※ 常与“讨论、努力、考虑、心思、解释”等搭配。Often paired with 讨论, 努力, 考虑, 心思, 解释, etc.
