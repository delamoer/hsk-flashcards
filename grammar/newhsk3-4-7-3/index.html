固定格式“拿……来说”举例结构Example structure
gùdìng géshì ná ... láishuō

Use 拿…来说 to mean “take…for example / speaking of…”.

结构 Structure拿 + 人/事物 + 来说，……ná + person/thing + lái shuō, … = take … for example
拿我来说，我每天都用中文工作。
Ná wǒ lái shuō, wǒ měi tiān dōu yòng Zhōngwén gōngzuò.
Take me for example: I use Chinese at work every day.
拿北京来说，冬天比较冷。
Ná Běijīng lái shuō, dōngtiān bǐjiào lěng.
Take Beijing for example: winters are rather cold.
拿这个工作来说，经验很重要。
Ná zhège gōngzuò lái shuō, jīngyàn hěn zhòngyào.
For this job, experience is very important.
※ 用于举一个具体例子来说明前面的观点。Used to give a concrete example that backs up the point just made.
