“结果”连接意外结果名词/连词Noun / Conjunction
jiéguǒ

Use 结果 to introduce the actual result, often an unexpected one.

结构 Structure前面的情况，结果 + 最终情况the earlier situation, jiéguǒ + final outcome = …, and as a result …
我坐错了车，结果迟到了。
Wǒ zuò cuò le chē, jiéguǒ chídào le.
I took the wrong bus, and as a result I was late.
他没带伞，结果淋湿了。
Tā méi dài sǎn, jiéguǒ lín shī le.
He did not bring an umbrella, so he got wet.
我找了很久，结果手机就在桌子上。
Wǒ zhǎo le hěn jiǔ, jiéguǒ shǒujī jiù zài zhuōzi shang.
I looked for a long time; it turned out the phone was on the table.
※ 常表示事情最后出现的实际结果。Marks the actual result that finally occurred.
