“除此以外”表示另外/除了这个范围结构Scope structure
chúcǐ yǐwài

Use 除此以外 to mean “besides this / apart from this”.

结构 Structure前文内容。除此以外，……preceding content. Chú cǐ yǐwài, … = besides this, …
这个工作离家近。除此以外，工资也不错。
Zhège gōngzuò lí jiā jìn. Chú cǐ yǐwài, gōngzī yě búcuò.
This job is close to home. Besides this, the salary is good.
我会英语。除此以外，我还会一点儿日语。
Wǒ huì Yīngyǔ. Chú cǐ yǐwài, wǒ hái huì yìdiǎnr Rìyǔ.
I speak English. Besides that, I also speak a little Japanese.
这里交通方便，除此以外，环境也很好。
Zhèlǐ jiāotōng fāngbiàn, chú cǐ yǐwài, huánjìng yě hěn hǎo.
Transportation is convenient here; besides, the environment is good.
※ “此”指前面已经说过的内容。此 refers to what was just mentioned.
