再……也……表示“无论多么……”让步结构Concessive structure
zài ... yě ...

Use 再 + adjective/verb + 也… to mean “no matter how… still…”.

结构 Structure再 + Adj/V，也 + 结果zài + Adj/V, yě + result = no matter how …, still …
再忙也要吃饭。
Zài máng yě yào chī fàn.
No matter how busy you are, you still need to eat.
再难我也要试试。
Zài nán wǒ yě yào shìshi.
No matter how difficult it is, I will try.
天气再冷，他也坚持跑步。
Tiānqì zài lěng, tā yě jiānchí pǎobù.
No matter how cold it is, he keeps running.
