“是……的”强调已发生事情的时间/地点/方式强调句Emphatic sentence
shì ... de

Use 是…的 when an event already happened and you want to emphasize when, where or how it happened.

结构 Structure主语 + 是 + 时间/地点/方式 + 动词 + 的Subject + shì + Time/Place/Way + Verb + de = emphasize when/where/how a past event happened
我是坐飞机来的。
wǒ shì zuò fēijī lái de.
I came by plane.
她是昨天来的。
tā shì zuótiān lái de.
She came yesterday.
我们是在北京认识的。
wǒmen shì zài běijīng rènshi de.
We met in Beijing.
※ 肯定句中“是”有时可省；否定通常用“不是……的”。是 can sometimes be dropped in positive sentences; the negative is 不是…的.
