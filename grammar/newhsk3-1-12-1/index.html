语法汇总 Grammar◇非主谓句
非主谓句句型Sentence pattern
fēi zhǔwèi jù

Some short Chinese sentences do not need a normal subject-predicate structure, especially weather expressions.

结构 Structure动词/形容词性短语独立成句Verb / Adjective Phrase as a complete sentence
下雪了。
Xiàxuě le.
It snowed / It is snowing.
下雨了。
Xiàyǔ le.
It started raining.
很冷。
Hěn lěng.
It is cold.
※ 天气、自然现象等常可以不用明确主语。Weather and natural phenomena often need no explicit subject.
