语法汇总 Grammar◇“并非”表示否定判断
“并非”表示否定判断否定结构Negation
bìngfēi

Use 并非 to mean “is not actually / not necessarily”.

结构 Structure并非 + 名词/形容词/小句bìngfēi + Noun/Adj/Clause = it is not (actually) …
事情并非你想的那么简单。
Shìqing bìngfēi nǐ xiǎng de nàme jiǎndān.
The matter is not actually as simple as you think.
成功并非完全靠运气。
Chénggōng bìngfēi wánquán kào yùnqi.
Success does not depend entirely on luck.
他并非不愿意，只是没有时间。
Tā bìngfēi bú yuànyì, zhǐshì méiyǒu shíjiān.
It is not that he is unwilling; he just has no time.
※ 比“不是”更正式，常用于纠正判断或部分否定。More formal than 不是; often corrects a judgment or gives a partial negation.
