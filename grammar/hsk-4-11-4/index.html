语法汇总 Grammar◇连词“然而”
连词“然而”连词Conjunction
rán'ér

Use 然而 to mean “however / yet”. It is formal and often used in writing.

结构 StructureA，然而 + BA, rán'ér + B = A; however, B (written)
他很努力，然而结果并不好。
Tā hěn nǔlì, rán'ér jiéguǒ bìng bù hǎo.
He worked hard; however, the result was not good.
这本书很短，然而很有意思。
Zhè běn shū hěn duǎn, rán'ér hěn yǒu yìsi.
This book is short, yet very interesting.
外面很冷，然而他还是出去了。
Wàimiàn hěn lěng, rán'ér tā háishi chūqu le.
It was cold outside; however, he still went out.
※ 比“但是、可是”更书面。More formal than 但是/可是.
