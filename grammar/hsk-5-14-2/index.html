“则”表示对比或“那么就”副词/连词（书面）Adverb / Conj. (formal)
zé

Use 则 to mark contrast (“whereas”) or a result (“then”) in formal Chinese.

结构 StructureA，而B则…；如果A，则BA, ér B zé…; rúguǒ A, zé B = whereas / then (formal)
他喜欢安静，我则喜欢热闹。
Tā xǐhuan ānjìng, wǒ zé xǐhuan rènao.
He likes quiet places, whereas I like lively ones.
情况不同，方法则不同。
Qíngkuàng bùtóng, fāngfǎ zé bùtóng.
If the situation is different, the method is different.
努力则有可能成功。
Nǔlì zé yǒu kěnéng chénggōng.
If you work hard, then success is possible.
※ 常见书面结构：而……则……；如果……则……。Common written patterns: 而…则…; 如果…则….
