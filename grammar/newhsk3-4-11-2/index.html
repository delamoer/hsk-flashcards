语法汇总 Grammar◇让步复句“就是……，也……”
让步复句“就是……，也……”让步复句Concessive clause
ràngbù fùjù jiùshì ... yě ...

Use 就是…也… for a strong spoken “even if…, still…”.

结构 Structure就是 + 极端/假设情况，也 + 不变的结果jiùshì + an extreme/hypothetical case, yě + unchanged result = even if …, still … (spoken)
就是下雨，我们也要去。
Jiùshì xià yǔ, wǒmen yě yào qù.
Even if it rains, we will still go.
就是再累，我也要做完。
Jiùshì zài lèi, wǒ yě yào zuò wán.
Even if I am very tired, I will still finish it.
就是没有地图，我也能找到。
Jiùshì méiyǒu dìtú, wǒ yě néng zhǎodào.
Even without a map, I can still find it.
※ “就是……也……”比“即使……也……”更口语，常把条件说得更极端。就是…也… is more colloquial than 即使…也…, and usually states a more extreme condition.
