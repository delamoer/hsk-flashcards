语法汇总 Grammar◇连词“况且”
连词“况且”连词Conjunction
kuàngqiě

Use 况且 to add a stronger supporting reason: “besides / moreover”.

结构 Structure理由1，况且 + 理由2Reason 1, kuàngqiě + Reason 2 = besides / moreover
今天太晚了，况且外面还下雨，别出去了。
Jīntiān tài wǎn le, kuàngqiě wàimiàn hái xià yǔ, bié chūqu le.
It is late, and besides, it is raining; do not go out.
这件事不急，况且我们还需要更多信息。
Zhè jiàn shì bù jí, kuàngqiě wǒmen hái xūyào gèng duō xìnxī.
This matter is not urgent; moreover, we need more information.
他经验丰富，况且准备得很充分，应该没问题。
Tā jīngyàn fēngfù, kuàngqiě zhǔnbèi de hěn chōngfèn, yīnggāi méi wèntí.
He is experienced, and besides, he is well prepared, so it should be fine.
※ 常用于第二个理由，使论证更充分。Usually introduces a second reason, making the argument stronger.
