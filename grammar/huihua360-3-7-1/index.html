语法汇总 Grammar◇副词“就”和“才”
副词“就”和“才”时间副词Time adverb
fùcí jiù hé cái

Use 就 when something happens earlier than expected, and 才 when it happens later than expected.

结构 Structure时间 + 就 + 动词；时间 + 才 + 动词Time + 就 (jiù, already/early) + Verb; Time + 才 (cái, only/late) + Verb
他七点就到了。
Tā qī diǎn jiù dào le.
He arrived as early as seven.
他九点才起床。
Tā jiǔ diǎn cái qǐchuáng.
He did not get up until nine.
我五点就下班了。
Wǒ wǔ diǎn jiù xiàbān le.
I got off work as early as five.
※ 本课的重点是“时间早于预期（就）/晚于预期（才）”；“就”还有其他用法，初级这里先不混入。The point here is earlier than expected (就) versus later than expected (才). 就 has other uses too; we leave them out for now.
