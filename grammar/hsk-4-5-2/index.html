“再说”表示补充理由或以后再谈连词/动词Conjunction / Verb
zàishuō

Use 再说 for “besides / moreover” or “talk about it later”.

结构 Structure理由1，再说 + 理由2；以后再说reason 1, zàishuō + reason 2; yǐhòu zài shuō = besides … / talk about it later
今天太晚了，再说我也累了。
Jīntiān tài wǎn le, zàishuō wǒ yě lèi le.
It is too late; besides, I am tired.
这件事以后再说吧。
Zhè jiàn shì yǐhòu zài shuō ba.
Let us talk about this later.
我不想买，再说也太贵了。
Wǒ bù xiǎng mǎi, zàishuō yě tài guì le.
I do not want to buy it; besides, it is too expensive.
※ 要根据上下文判断是“补充理由”还是“以后再谈”。Context decides whether it adds a reason or means "talk about it later".
