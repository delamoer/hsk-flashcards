连词“要不”表示建议或“否则”连词Conjunction
yàobù

Use 要不 for “how about… / otherwise”.

结构 Structure要不 + 建议；A，要不 + 后果yàobù + suggestion; A, yàobù + consequence = how about … / otherwise …
要不我们明天再去吧。
Yàobù wǒmen míngtiān zài qù ba.
How about we go tomorrow instead?
你累了，要不休息一下？
Nǐ lèi le, yàobù xiūxi yíxià?
You're tired; how about taking a rest?
快点儿，要不就迟到了。
Kuài diǎnr, yàobù jiù chídào le.
Hurry, otherwise we will be late.
※ 口语很常见；既可提建议，也可表示“否则”。Very colloquial; can suggest, or mean "otherwise".
