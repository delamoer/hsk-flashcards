语法汇总 Grammar◇目的复句“为了……，……”
目的复句“为了……，……”目的复句Purpose clause
wèile ...

Use 为了 + goal to mean “in order to / for the purpose of…”.

结构 Structure为了 + 目的，主语 + 动作wèile + Purpose, Subject + Action
为了学好中文，我每天练习。
Wèi le xué hǎo zhōng wén, wǒ měi tiān liàn xí.
To learn Chinese well, I practice every day.
为了身体健康，他每天跑步。
Wèi le shēn tǐ jiàn kāng, tā měi tiān pǎo bù.
For his health, he runs every day.
为了不迟到，我们早点儿出发吧。
Wèi le bù chí dào, wǒ men zǎo diǎnr chū fā ba.
Let us leave early so we will not be late.
※ “为了”后面是目的，后面的句子说明为这个目的做什么。为了 states the purpose; the clause tells what's done for it.
