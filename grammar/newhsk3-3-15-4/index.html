固定短语“不一会儿”时间短语Time phrase
bù yíhuìr

Use 不一会儿 to mean “after a short while / soon”.

结构 Structure不一会儿，+ 新情况bù yíhuìr, + New Situation (after a short while ...)
不一会儿，车就来了。
Bù yí huìr, chē jiù lái le.
After a short while, the car came.
他进去不一会儿就出来了。
Tā jìn qù bù yí huìr jiù chū lái le.
He came out soon after going in.
不一会儿，大家都到了。
Bù yí huìr, dà jiā dōu dào le.
Soon, everyone arrived.
※ 表示经过的时间很短。Means only a short time passed.
