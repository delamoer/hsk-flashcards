语法汇总 Grammar◇V₁着V₂
V₁着V₂伴随动作Accompanying action
V₁ zhe V₂

Use Verb 1 + 着 + Verb 2 to show how someone does the second action.

结构 Structure主语 + 动词1 + 着 + 动词2 +（宾语）Subject + Verb 1 + 着 (zhe, manner/state) + Verb 2 + (Object)
他笑着说话。
Tā xiào zhe shuōhuà.
He talks with a smile.
她坐着看书。
Tā zuò zhe kàn shū.
She reads while sitting.
妈妈站着等我。
Māma zhàn zhe děng wǒ.
Mom waits for me while standing.
※ V1表示V2进行时的状态、方式或伴随动作；不同于两个并列动作“一边……一边……”。V1 shows the state, manner or accompanying action while V2 happens. This differs from two parallel actions with 一边…一边….
