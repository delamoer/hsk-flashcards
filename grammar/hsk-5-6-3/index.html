“像……似的 / ……似的”表示相似比喻结构Simile structure
shìde

Use 似的 after a noun/phrase to mean “like / as if”.

结构 Structure像 + N + 似的；N + 似的xiàng + N + shìde; N + shìde = like / as if
孩子像小鸟似的跑了出去。
Háizi xiàng xiǎo niǎo shìde pǎo le chūqu.
The child ran out like a little bird.
雨下得跟倒水似的。
Yǔ xià de gēn dào shuǐ shìde.
The rain poured as if water were being dumped.
他什么都没听见似的。
Tā shénme dōu méi tīngjiàn shìde.
He acted as if he had heard nothing.
※ “似”在这里通常读 shì。Here 似 is usually read shì.
