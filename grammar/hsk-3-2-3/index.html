反问句“能……吗？”反问句Rhetorical question
néng ... ma

Use 能…吗？ as a rhetorical question. It often means the opposite of the words inside.

结构 Structure能 + 肯定/否定 + 吗？néng + affirmative/negative + ma? = Can …? / Is … allowed?
这么晚了，你能不累吗？
Zhème wǎn le, nǐ néng bú lèi ma?
It is so late—how could you not be tired?
他每天都学习，能不进步吗？
Tā měi tiān dōu xuéxí, néng bú jìnbù ma?
He studies every day—how could he not improve?
这件事我能不知道吗？
Zhè jiàn shì wǒ néng bù zhīdào ma?
How could I not know this?
※ 反问不是在真正提问，而是强调说话人的判断。A rhetorical question isn't a real question; it stresses the speaker's judgement.
