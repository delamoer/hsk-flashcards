语法汇总 Grammar◇时量补语
时量补语时量补语Duration complement
shíliàng bǔyǔ

Put a length of time after a verb to say how long an action lasts.

结构 Structure主语 + 动词 + 了 + 时长 +（名词宾语）；代词宾语放在时长前Subject + Verb + 了 (le) + Length of Time + (Noun Object); Pronoun Object before the Time
他在北京待了三天。
Tā zài Běijīng dāi le sān tiān.
He stayed in Beijing for three days.
我看了一个小时电视。
Wǒ kàn le yí ge xiǎoshí diànshì.
I watched TV for one hour.
我等了你十分钟。
Wǒ děng le nǐ shí fēnzhōng.
I waited for you for ten minutes.
※ 本册教材区分名词宾语、代词宾语及离合词的时长位置；本表先用最清楚的常见句式。The textbook explains where the time length goes with noun objects, pronoun objects and separable verbs; here we use the clearest common pattern.
