语法汇总 Grammar◇“不无”表示并非没有
“不无”表示并非没有动词/书面否定Verb / Formal negation
bùwú

Use 不无 to mean “not without / there is some…”. It is formal.

结构 Structure不无 + 道理/遗憾/可能/影响bùwú + dàoli/yíhàn/kěnéng/yǐngxiǎng = not without (some) reason/regret/possibility/effect
他的说法不无道理。
Tā de shuōfǎ bùwú dàoli.
What he said is not without reason.
这次失败不无遗憾。
Zhè cì shībài bùwú yíhàn.
This failure is not without regret.
这种变化不无可能。
Zhè zhǒng biànhuà bùwú kěnéng.
This change is not impossible.
※ 双重否定式书面表达，语气通常比直接说“有”更委婉。A formal double negative; usually more tactful than simply saying 有.
