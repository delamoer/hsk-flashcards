语法汇总 Grammar◇连词/介词“由于”
连词/介词“由于”连词/介词Conjunction / Prep.
liáncí/jiècí yóuyú

Use 由于 to mean “because of / due to”. It is more formal than 因为.

结构 Structure由于 + 原因，结果yóuyú + reason, result = because of …, …
由于下雨，比赛取消了。
Yóuyú xià yǔ, bǐsài qǔxiāo le.
Because of the rain, the game was canceled.
由于工作太忙，他没来。
Yóuyú gōngzuò tài máng, tā méi lái.
Because he was too busy with work, he did not come.
由于天气原因，飞机晚了。
Yóuyú tiānqì yuányīn, fēijī wǎn le.
Because of the weather, the plane was late.
※ “由于”较正式，后面可以接名词性原因，也可以接小句。由于 is fairly formal; it can take either a noun phrase or a full clause as the reason.
