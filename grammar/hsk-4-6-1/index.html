副词“竟然”表示意外副词Adverb
jìngrán

Use 竟然 when something is very unexpected: “unexpectedly / actually”.

结构 Structure主语 + 竟然 + 谓语Subject + jìngrán + predicate = unexpectedly / to my surprise
他竟然忘了我的名字。
Tā jìngrán wàng le wǒ de míngzi.
He unexpectedly forgot my name.
这么难的题，她竟然做对了。
Zhème nán de tí, tā jìngrán zuò duì le.
She unexpectedly got such a hard question right.
今天这么冷，他竟然穿短袖。
Jīntiān zhème lěng, tā jìngrán chuān duǎnxiù.
It is so cold, yet he is wearing short sleeves.
※ “竟然”带明显的惊讶、出乎意料语气。竟然 carries clear surprise, "unexpectedly".
