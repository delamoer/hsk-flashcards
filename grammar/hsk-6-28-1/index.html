语法汇总 Grammar◇与“个”相关的格式
与“个”相关的格式结构Structure
yǔ “gè” xiāngguān de géshì

Two useful patterns with 个: “一个 + noun/verb phrase” can show a sudden action; “V + (了)个 + result” introduces a result vividly.

结构 Structure一个 + 名词/动词结构 + V；V +（了）个 + 形容词/动词结果yí ge + Noun/verb phrase + V; V + (le) ge + Adj/Verb result = suddenly …; V till it ends up …
他一个不小心，把杯子打碎了。
Tā yí ge bù xiǎoxīn, bǎ bēizi dǎsuì le.
He accidentally broke the cup in one careless moment.
孩子一个跟头摔倒了。
Háizi yí ge gēntou shuāidǎo le.
The child fell over in a sudden tumble.
他把房间收拾了个干干净净。
Tā bǎ fángjiān shōushile ge gāngānjìngjìng.
He cleaned the room completely.
※ 教材把两类“个”格式放在一起：一类突出突然发生，一类生动引出动作结果。The textbook groups two 个 patterns: one shows something happening suddenly, the other vividly introduces a result.
