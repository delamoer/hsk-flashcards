语法汇总 Grammar◇“被”字句
“被”字句被动句Passive
“bèi” zì jù

Use 被 when the subject receives an action. The action is done by someone or something else.

结构 Structure受事主语 + 被/叫/让 + 动作者 + 动词 + 结果Thing Affected + 被 (bèi, passive) / 叫 (jiào) / 让 (ràng) + Doer + Verb + Result
我的手机被弟弟拿走了。
Wǒ de shǒujī bèi dìdi ná zǒu le.
My phone was taken away by my younger brother.
门被风吹开了。
Mén bèi fēng chuī kāi le.
The door was blown open by the wind.
我的杯子被他打破了。
Wǒ de bēizi bèi tā dǎ pò le.
My cup was broken by him.
※ 教材还说明“叫、让”也可表达被动；被动句主语是受事，动词后一般有动作结果。叫 and 让 can also make a passive. The subject of a passive sentence receives the action, and the verb usually has a result after it.
