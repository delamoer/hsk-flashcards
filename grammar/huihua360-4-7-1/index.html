可能补语补语Complement
kěnéng bǔyǔ

Put 得 or 不 between a verb and its result to show if an action is possible.

结构 Structure动词 + 得/不 + 结果补语或趋向补语Verb + 得 (de, can) / 不 (bù, cannot) + Result (懂 understand / 动 move / 到 reach)
老师说的话我听得懂。
Lǎoshī shuō de huà wǒ tīng de dǒng.
I can understand what the teacher says.
这个箱子太重，我搬不动。
Zhège xiāngzi tài zhòng, wǒ bān bu dòng.
This box is too heavy; I cannot move it.
坐在教室后面，我听不到老师说话。
Zuò zài jiàoshì hòumian, wǒ tīng bu dào lǎoshī shuōhuà.
Sitting at the back of the classroom, I can't hear the teacher.
※ 教材对比“动词+得+结果”和“动词+不+结果”；否定不能误写“没+结果补语”。Compare verb + 得 + result (can) with verb + 不 + result (cannot). Don't write the negative as 没 + result.
