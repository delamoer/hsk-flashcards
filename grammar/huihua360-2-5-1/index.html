语法汇总 Grammar◇语气助词“了”（1）——表发生
语气助词“了”（1）——表发生语气助词Modal particle
yǔqì zhùcí le (yī) — biǎo fāshēng

Put 了 at the end to say something has happened.

结构 Structure主语 + 动词 +（宾语）+ 了；否定：没（有）+ 动词Subject + Verb + (Object) + 了 (le); Negative: 没(有) + Verb (no 了)
我昨天去学校了。
Wǒ zuótiān qù xuéxiào le.
I went to school yesterday.
她今天买水果了。
Tā jīntiān mǎi shuǐguǒ le.
She bought fruit today.
我昨天看电影了。
Wǒ zuótiān kàn diànyǐng le.
I watched a movie yesterday.
※ 与第6课表示情况变化的句末“了（2）”分开。教材用句末“了”确认事件已经发生，否定通常“没（有）”且去掉句末“了”。Keep this apart from the sentence-final 了 (2) for change in Lesson 6. Here sentence-final 了 confirms that something happened; the negative uses 没（有） and drops 了.
