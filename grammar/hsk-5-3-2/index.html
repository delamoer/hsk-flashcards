语法汇总 Grammar◇代词“各自”
代词“各自”代词Pronoun
gèzì

Use 各自 to mean “each person’s own / separately”.

结构 Structure各自 + V / 各自的 + Ngèzì + V / gèzì de + N = each on their own / their respective
大家各自准备自己的材料。
Dàjiā gèzì zhǔnbèi zìjǐ de cáiliào.
Everyone prepares their own materials.
我们回到各自的房间。
Wǒmen huí dào gèzì de fángjiān.
We returned to our own rooms.
两个人各自有不同的想法。
Liǎng ge rén gèzì yǒu bùtóng de xiǎngfǎ.
The two people each have different ideas.
※ “各自”强调每个人/每一方自己。各自 stresses each person's/side's own.
