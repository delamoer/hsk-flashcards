语法汇总 Grammar◇连动句（1）
连动句（1）连动句Serial-verb sentence
liándòng jù (yī)

Use two verbs in order when one person does two connected actions.

结构 Structure主语 + 动词1 +（地点）+ 动词2Subject + Verb 1 + (Place) + Verb 2
我去学校学习。
Wǒ qù xuéxiào xuéxí.
I go to school to study.
我去超市买苹果。
Wǒ qù chāoshì mǎi píngguǒ.
I go to the supermarket to buy apples.
他去电影院看电影。
Tā qù diànyǐngyuàn kàn diànyǐng.
He goes to the cinema to watch a movie.
※ 动作按照实际发生顺序排列。Actions are ordered as they actually happen.
