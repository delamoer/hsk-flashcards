数量短语的重叠数量结构Quantity structure
shùliàng duǎnyǔ de chóngdié

Repeat a number-measure phrase to mean “one by one / in groups / gradually”.

结构 Structure一A一A；两A两A；数量短语重叠 + 地/都yī A yī A; liǎng A liǎng A; repeated num-measure + de/dōu = one by one / two by two
大家一个一个地进去。
Dàjiā yí ge yí ge de jìnqu.
Everyone went in one by one.
请一页一页地看。
Qǐng yí yè yí yè de kàn.
Please read page by page.
学生两个人两个人地练习。
Xuésheng liǎng ge rén liǎng ge rén de liànxí.
The students practice in pairs.
※ 表示按相同数量依次、分组或逐步进行。Means doing something in equal amounts, in turn, in groups or step by step.
