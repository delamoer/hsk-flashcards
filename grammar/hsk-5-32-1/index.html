“密切”表示关系近/使关系更紧密形容词/动词Adjective / Verb
mìqiè

Use 密切 to mean “close” in relationship, or “strengthen” a relationship.

结构 Structure关系密切；密切 + 联系guānxi mìqiè; mìqiè + liánxì = be closely related / keep in close contact
我们和客户的关系很密切。
Wǒmen hé kèhù de guānxi hěn mìqiè.
Our relationship with customers is close.
这件事和健康密切相关。
Zhè jiàn shì hé jiànkāng mìqiè xiāngguān.
This matter is closely related to health.
新的交通方式密切了两地的联系。
Xīn de jiāotōng fāngshì mìqiè le liǎng dì de liánxì.
The new transportation strengthened the connection between the two places.
※ 常见：关系密切、密切联系、密切相关。Common: 关系密切, 密切联系, 密切相关.
