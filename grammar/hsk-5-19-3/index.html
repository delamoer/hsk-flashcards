介词“趁”表示利用某个机会/时间介词Preposition
chèn

Use 趁 to mean “while / take advantage of a good time”.

结构 Structure趁 + 时间/机会 + Vchèn + time/chance + V = take advantage of (a moment) to …
趁天气好，我们出去走走吧。
Chèn tiānqì hǎo, wǒmen chūqu zǒuzou ba.
While the weather is good, let's go out.
趁年轻，多学点儿东西。
Chèn niánqīng, duō xué diǎnr dōngxi.
While you are young, learn more.
你趁热吃吧。
Nǐ chèn rè chī ba.
Eat it while it is hot.
※ “趁”后面一般是有利时机。趁 is followed by a favorable opportunity.
