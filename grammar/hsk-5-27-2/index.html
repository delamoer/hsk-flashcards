“舍得 / 舍不得”表示愿不愿放弃可能补语式固定词Potential-compl. idiom
shěde / shěbude

Use 舍得/舍不得 to mean “be willing / reluctant to give up, spend, or leave”.

结构 Structure舍得/舍不得 + V/Oshěde / shěbude + V/O = be willing / unwilling to part with
我舍不得离开这里。
Wǒ shěbude líkāi zhèlǐ.
I am reluctant to leave here.
你舍得把这本书送人吗？
Nǐ shěde bǎ zhè běn shū sòng rén ma?
Are you willing to give this book away?
他舍不得花钱买贵东西。
Tā shěbude huā qián mǎi guì dōngxi.
He is reluctant to spend money on expensive things.
※ “舍”这里有放弃、割舍的意思。Here 舍 means to give up / part with.
