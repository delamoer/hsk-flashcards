“数词 + 形容词 + 量词”结构数量结构Quantity structure
shùcí + xíngróngcí + liàngcí jiégòu

Put an adjective between a number and a measure word to show the size of the amount/unit.

结构 Structure数词 + 大/小/长/厚等形容词 + 量词 + 名词number + dà/xiǎo/cháng/hòu etc. + measure word + Noun = a big cup / small piece / long strip of …
请给我一大杯水。
Qǐng gěi wǒ yí dà bēi shuǐ.
Please give me a large glass of water.
他吃了两大碗面。
Tā chī le liǎng dà wǎn miàn.
He ate two large bowls of noodles.
桌上有三小块蛋糕。
Zhuō shang yǒu sān xiǎo kuài dàngāo.
There are three small pieces of cake on the table.
※ 形容词修饰量词所表示的单位大小，如“大杯、小块、长条”等。The adjective sizes the unit named by the measure word: 大杯, 小块, 长条.
