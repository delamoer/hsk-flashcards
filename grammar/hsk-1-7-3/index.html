连动句（1）：去 + 地点 + 动作连动句Serial-verb sentence
qù + dìdiǎn + dòngcí

Use 去 + place + verb to say you go somewhere to do something.

结构 Structure主语 + 去 + 地点 + 动词Subject + qù + Place + Verb = go to + Place + to + Verb
我去学校学习。
wǒ qù xuéxiào xuéxí.
I go to school to study.
她去商店买东西。
tā qù shāngdiàn mǎi dōngxi.
She goes to the shop to buy things.
我们去饭店吃米饭。
wǒmen qù fàndiàn chī mǐfàn.
We go to the restaurant to eat rice.
※ 两个动作按发生顺序排列：先“去”，再做第二个动作。The two actions follow their real order: first 去, then the second action.
