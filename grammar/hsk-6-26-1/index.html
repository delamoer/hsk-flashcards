语法汇总 Grammar◇连词“别说”
连词“别说”连词Conjunction
biéshuō

Use 别说 to introduce a stronger comparison: “let alone / not to mention”.

结构 Structure别说 + 较难情况，连/就是 + 较容易情况 + 都/也……biéshuō + harder case, lián/jiùshì + easier case + dōu/yě … = let alone … , even … not …
别说跑十公里了，跑一公里我都累。
Biéshuō pǎo shí gōnglǐ le, pǎo yì gōnglǐ wǒ dōu lèi.
Let alone running ten kilometers; even one kilometer tires me.
别说外国人，有些中国人也不认识这个字。
Biéshuō wàiguórén, yǒuxiē Zhōngguórén yě bú rènshi zhège zì.
Not to mention foreigners; even some Chinese people do not know this character.
别说买房了，现在连房租都很贵。
Biéshuō mǎi fáng le, xiànzài lián fángzū dōu hěn guì.
Let alone buying a home; even rent is expensive now.
※ 通过较极端情况来强调连较普通情况都难以达到。Uses an extreme case to stress that even the ordinary case is hard to reach.
