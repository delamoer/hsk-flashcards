语法汇总 Grammar◇“值得 / 值”
“值得 / 值”动词Verb
zhíde / zhí

值得 + verb means “worth doing”. 值 + money means “be worth / cost”.

结构 Structure值得 + V；值 + 钱/数量zhíde + V; zhí + money/amount = be worth doing / be worth (an amount)
这本书值得看。
Zhè běn shū zhíde kàn.
This book is worth reading.
北京值得去一次。
Běijīng zhíde qù yí cì.
Beijing is worth visiting once.
这个手机值三千块钱。
Zhège shǒujī zhí sānqiān kuài qián.
This phone is worth 3,000 yuan.
※ “值得”后接动作；“值”常直接接价格或价值。值得 is followed by an action; 值 is followed by a price or value.
