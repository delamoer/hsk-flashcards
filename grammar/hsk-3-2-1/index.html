“……了，就……”表示两个动作连续发生复句Complex sentence
... le, jiù ...

Use …了，就… when one action happens and the next action follows soon after.

结构 Structure动作1 + 了，就 + 动作2Action 1 + le, jiù + Action 2 = as soon as 1 is done, then 2
我吃了饭就去学校。
Wǒ chī le fàn jiù qù xuéxiào.
I will go to school after I eat.
他下了班就回家。
Tā xià le bān jiù huí jiā.
He goes home after work.
你到了就给我打电话。
Nǐ dào le jiù gěi wǒ dǎ diànhuà.
Call me when you arrive.
※ 两个动作按时间先后排列，第二个动作紧接着发生。The two actions follow time order; the second happens right after the first.
