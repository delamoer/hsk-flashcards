量词重叠量词重叠Measure-word redup.
liàngcí chóngdié

Repeat a measure word to mean “every one / each one”.

结构 Structure量词重叠 + 都 + 谓语Reduplicated Measure Word + dōu + Predicate
这些照片张张都很好看。
Zhè xiē zhào piàn zhāng zhāng dōu hěn hǎo kàn.
Every one of these photos looks good.
这些苹果个个都很甜。
Zhè xiē píng guǒ gè gè dōu hěn tián.
Every one of these apples is sweet.
这些衣服件件都很漂亮。
Zhè xiē yī fu jiàn jiàn dōu hěn piào liang.
Every piece of clothing is beautiful.
※ 量词重叠通常和“都”搭配，表示没有例外。Reduplicated measure words pair with 都 to mean "without exception".
