语法汇总 Grammar◇“大 + 时间词”表示特殊时段
“大 + 时间词”表示特殊时段时间表达Time
dà + shíjiāncí

Use 大 + a time word in fixed expressions to strongly mark an unusual time, such as 大清早、大白天、大半夜.

结构 Structure大清早 / 大白天 / 大半夜dà qīngzǎo / dà báitiān / dà bànyè = so early in the morning / in broad daylight / in the dead of night
大清早他就出门了。
Dà qīngzǎo tā jiù chūmén le.
He went out very early in the morning.
大白天的，你怕什么？
Dà báitiān de, nǐ pà shénme?
It is broad daylight; what are you afraid of?
大半夜别给别人打电话。
Dà bànyè bié gěi biéren dǎ diànhuà.
Do not call people in the middle of the night.
※ 多为固定搭配，常带强调、惊讶或不满。Mostly set combinations; often adds emphasis, surprise or annoyance.
