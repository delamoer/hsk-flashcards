“有关”表示相关/关于动词/介词Verb / Preposition
yǒuguān

Use 有关 to mean “related to / concerning”.

结构 Structure与 + N + 有关；有关 + Nyǔ + N + yǒuguān; yǒuguān + N = related to N; N concerned
这个问题与安全有关。
Zhège wèntí yǔ ānquán yǒuguān.
This issue is related to safety.
我想了解有关申请的规定。
Wǒ xiǎng liǎojiě yǒuguān shēnqǐng de guīdìng.
I want to learn about the rules concerning the application.
有关情况我们以后再讨论。
Yǒuguān qíngkuàng wǒmen yǐhòu zài tǎolùn.
We will discuss the relevant situation later.
※ 可作动词“与……有关”，也可直接修饰名词“有关规定”。Works as a verb (与……有关) or directly before a noun (有关规定).
