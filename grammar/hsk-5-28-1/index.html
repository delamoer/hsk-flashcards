语法汇总 Grammar◇副词“从此”
副词“从此”时间副词Time adverb
cóngcǐ

Use 从此 to mean “from then on”.

结构 Structure从此 + 句子cóngcǐ + Sentence = from then on
从此以后，他每天早起。
Cóngcǐ yǐhòu, tā měi tiān zǎo qǐ.
From then on, he got up early every day.
那次以后，我们从此成了朋友。
Nà cì yǐhòu, wǒmen cóngcǐ chéng le péngyou.
After that, we became friends from then on.
他离开后，从此没有回来。
Tā líkāi hòu, cóngcǐ méiyǒu huílai.
After he left, he never came back.
※ “自从”后需接起点；“从此”本身就是“从那个时候起”。自从 needs a starting point; 从此 already means "from then on".
