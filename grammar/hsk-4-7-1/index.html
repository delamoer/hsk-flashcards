语法汇总 Grammar◇“估计”和“可能”
“估计”和“可能”动词/副词Verb / Adverb
gūjì / kěnéng

估计 means “estimate / guess”. 可能 means “possibly / may”.

结构 Structure估计 + 句子；可能 + 谓语gūjì + Sentence; kěnéng + predicate = I reckon … / may …
我估计他八点到。
Wǒ gūjì tā bā diǎn dào.
I guess he will arrive at eight.
明天可能下雨。
Míngtiān kěnéng xià yǔ.
It may rain tomorrow.
医生估计他很快就会好。
Yīshēng gūjì tā hěn kuài jiù huì hǎo.
The doctor estimates that he will get better soon.
※ “估计”强调人的判断；“可能”直接表示可能性。估计 stresses a person's estimate; 可能 states possibility directly.
