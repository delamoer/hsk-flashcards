语法汇总 Grammar◇并列复句“不是……，而是……”
并列复句“不是……，而是……”并列复句Coordinate clause
bìngliè fùjù bú shì ... ér shì ...

Use 不是…而是… to correct one idea and give the right one: “not…, but…”.

结构 Structure不是 + A，而是 + Bbú shì + A, ér shì + B = not A, but B
他不是老师，而是医生。
Tā bú shì lǎoshī, ér shì yīshēng.
He is not a teacher, but a doctor.
我不是不想去，而是今天没时间。
Wǒ bú shì bù xiǎng qù, ér shì jīntiān méi shíjiān.
It is not that I do not want to go; I just have no time today.
这不是我的书，而是他的。
Zhè bú shì wǒ de shū, ér shì tā de.
This is not my book; it is his.
※ 前半句否定错误判断，后半句给出真正情况。The first half rejects a wrong idea; the second half gives the real one.
