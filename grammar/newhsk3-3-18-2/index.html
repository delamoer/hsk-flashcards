语法汇总 Grammar◇“刚才”和“刚刚”
“刚才”和“刚刚”时间表达Time
gāngcái hé gānggāng

Both mean “just now”. 刚才 is a time noun; 刚刚 is an adverb and stays close to the verb.

结构 Structure刚才 + 句子；主语 + 刚刚 + 动词gāngcái + Sentence; Subject + gānggāng + Verb
刚才老师给我打电话了。
Gāng cái lǎo shī gěi wǒ dǎ diàn huà le.
The teacher called me just now.
我刚刚到家。
Wǒ gāng gāng dào jiā.
I just got home.
刚才你去哪儿了？
Gāng cái nǐ qù nǎr le?
Where did you go just now?
※ “刚才”可以单独作时间成分；“刚刚”是副词，通常放在动词前。刚才 can stand as a time word; 刚刚 is an adverb, usually before the verb.
