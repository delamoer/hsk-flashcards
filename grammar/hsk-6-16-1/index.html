插入语“换句话说”插入语Parenthetical
huàn jù huà shuō

Use 换句话说 to restate an idea more clearly: “in other words”.

结构 Structure前句，换句话说，+ 另一种解释Previous point, huàn jù huà shuō, + another way of putting it = in other words, …
他没有拒绝，换句话说，他可能同意了。
Tā méiyǒu jùjué, huàn jù huà shuō, tā kěnéng tóngyì le.
He did not refuse; in other words, he may have agreed.
成本下降了，换句话说，我们可以卖得更便宜。
Chéngběn xiàjiàng le, huàn jù huà shuō, wǒmen kěyǐ mài de gèng piányi.
Costs fell; in other words, we can sell more cheaply.
这个办法风险很小，换句话说，比较安全。
Zhège bànfǎ fēngxiǎn hěn xiǎo, huàn jù huà shuō, bǐjiào ānquán.
This method has little risk; in other words, it is relatively safe.
※ 用于解释、总结或用更容易理解的话重说前面内容。Used to explain, sum up, or restate the previous point in easier words.
