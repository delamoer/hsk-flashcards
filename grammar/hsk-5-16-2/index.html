“个别”表示少数/单独形容词/副词Adjective / Adverb
gèbié

Use 个别 to mean “individual / a few special cases”.

结构 Structure个别 + N；个别地 + Vgèbié + N; gèbié de + V = a few individual … / individually
个别学生还没交作业。
Gèbié xuésheng hái méi jiāo zuòyè.
A few individual students have not handed in homework.
这是个别现象，不代表全部。
Zhè shì gèbié xiànxiàng, bú dàibiǎo quánbù.
This is an individual case and does not represent all cases.
老师会和学生个别谈话。
Lǎoshī huì hé xuésheng gèbié tánhuà.
The teacher will talk with students individually.
※ 强调不是大多数。Stresses it is not the majority.
