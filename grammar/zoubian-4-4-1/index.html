动宾离合词
dòng-bīn líhécí

Some verb-object words can split. Put time, number, or 了 between the two parts.

结构 Structure动词 + 了/数量/时长 + 宾语；如 睡了一个好觉Verb Part + 了 (le) / Amount / Time + Object Part; e.g. 睡 + 了一个好 + 觉
我昨晚睡了一个好觉。
Wǒ zuówǎn shuì le yí ge hǎo jiào.
I slept well last night.
我们见过两次面。
Wǒmen jiàn guo liǎng cì miàn.
We have met twice.
他唱了一首歌。
Tā chàng le yì shǒu gē.
He sang a song.
※ 注意如“见面、睡觉、跳舞”中间可插入成分，不是每个动宾短语都以同样方式离合。Words like 见面, 睡觉 and 跳舞 can take other words in the middle, but not every verb-object phrase splits in the same way.
