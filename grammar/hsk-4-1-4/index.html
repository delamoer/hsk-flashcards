语法汇总 Grammar◇即使……，也……
即使……，也……让步复句Concessive clause
jíshǐ ... yě ...

Use 即使…也… for “even if…, still…”.

结构 Structure即使 + 条件，也 + 结果jíshǐ + condition, yě + result = even if …, still …
即使下雨，我也要去。
Jíshǐ xià yǔ, wǒ yě yào qù.
Even if it rains, I will still go.
即使很忙，他也每天运动。
Jíshǐ hěn máng, tā yě měi tiān yùndòng.
Even if he is busy, he exercises every day.
即使你不同意，我也会试试。
Jíshǐ nǐ bù tóngyì, wǒ yě huì shìshi.
Even if you disagree, I will still try.
※ 前面是假设条件，后面的结果不受这个条件影响。The first part is a hypothetical; the result holds regardless of it.
