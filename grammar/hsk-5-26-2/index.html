“至于”表示转到另一话题/达到某程度介词/动词Preposition / Verb
zhìyú

Use 至于 for “as for…”; 不至于 means “not to the extent of…”.

结构 Structure至于 + 话题；不至于 + V/Adjzhìyú + topic; bú zhìyú + V/Adj = as for … / it won't go so far as to …
我们决定去，至于时间以后再定。
Wǒmen juédìng qù, zhìyú shíjiān yǐhòu zài dìng.
We decided to go; as for the time, we'll decide later.
至于费用，我还要问一下。
Zhìyú fèiyòng, wǒ hái yào wèn yíxià.
As for the cost, I still need to ask.
他只是迟到，不至于被开除。
Tā zhǐshì chídào, bú zhìyú bèi kāichú.
He was only late; it shouldn't go as far as being fired.
※ 常用于话题转换；“不至于”表示程度没有那么严重。Used to switch topics; 不至于 means "not to that extent".
