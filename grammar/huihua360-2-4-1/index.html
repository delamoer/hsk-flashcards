语法汇总 Grammar◇动态助词“过”
动态助词“过”动态助词Aspect particle
dòngtài zhùcí guo

Use 过 after a verb to say you have had an experience before.

结构 Structure主语 + 动词 + 过 +（宾语）；否定：没（有）+ 动词 + 过Subject + Verb + 过 (guo, have done before) + (Object); Negative: 没(有) + Verb + 过
我去过上海。
Wǒ qù guo Shànghǎi.
I have been to Shanghai.
她吃过饺子。
Tā chī guo jiǎozi.
She has eaten dumplings before.
我没去过北京。
Wǒ méi qù guo Běijīng.
I have never been to Beijing.
※ 表示过去的经历，不直接表示动作正在发生；否定用“没（有）……过”。过 marks a past experience, not an action happening now. The negative is 没（有）…过.
