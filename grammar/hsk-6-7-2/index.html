语法汇总 Grammar◇动词/连词“致使”
动词/连词“致使”动词/连词Verb / Conjunction
zhìshǐ

Use 致使 to mean “cause / lead to”, usually for a negative result.

结构 Structure原因 + 致使 + 不良结果Cause + zhìshǐ + bad result = … causes / leads to …
大雨致使比赛取消。
Dàyǔ zhìshǐ bǐsài qǔxiāo.
Heavy rain caused the game to be canceled.
操作错误致使系统停止工作。
Cāozuò cuòwù zhìshǐ xìtǒng tíngzhǐ gōngzuò.
An operating error caused the system to stop working.
长期缺少睡眠可能致使身体出现问题。
Chángqī quēshǎo shuìmián kěnéng zhìshǐ shēntǐ chūxiàn wèntí.
Long-term lack of sleep may lead to health problems.
※ 较正式，后面的结果往往是不希望发生的。Fairly formal; the result that follows is usually unwanted.
