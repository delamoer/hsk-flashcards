动量补语（2）：宾语和次数的位置动量补语Verbal-measure compl.
dòngliàng bǔyǔ (èr)

The position of the object changes with different kinds of objects when you use a frequency complement.

结构 Structure事物：V + 次数 + O；代词：V + 代词 + 次数Thing: V + Frequency + O; Pronoun: V + Pronoun + Frequency
我看了两次这个电影。
Wǒ kàn le liǎng cì zhè ge diàn yǐng.
I watched this movie twice.
我找了他三次。
Wǒ zhǎo le tā sān cì.
I looked for him three times.
老师叫了我的名字两次。
Lǎo shī jiào le wǒ de míng zi liǎng cì.
The teacher called my name twice.
※ 人名、地点名等位置较灵活；代词通常放在次数补语前。Names/places are flexible; pronouns go before the frequency complement.
