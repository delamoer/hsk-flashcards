副词“瞎”表示没有目的/不正确地做副词（口语）Adverb (colloquial)
xiā

Use 瞎 before a verb to mean doing something blindly, carelessly, or without reason.

结构 Structure瞎 + Vxiā + V = do sth blindly / pointlessly
别瞎说。
Bié xiā shuō.
Don't talk nonsense.
你别瞎担心。
Nǐ bié xiā dānxīn.
Don't worry for no reason.
不知道就别瞎猜。
Bù zhīdào jiù bié xiā cāi.
Don't guess blindly if you don't know.
※ 口语，有时语气较直接。Colloquial, sometimes rather blunt.
