语法汇总 Grammar◇“第”表示序数
“第”表示序数序数表达Ordinal expression
dì biǎoshì xùshù

Put 第 before a number to mean “first, second, third…”.

结构 Structure第 + 数词 (+ 名词/量词)dì + Number + (Noun / Measure Word)
这是第一课。
Zhè shì dì yī kè.
This is Lesson 1.
他是第一个。
Tā shì dì yī ge.
He is the first one.
今天是第三天。
Jīntiān shì dì sān tiān.
Today is the third day.
※ “第”把普通数词变成序数。第 turns a plain number into an ordinal.
