语法汇总 Grammar◇副词/连词“同时”
副词/连词“同时”副词/连词Adverb / Conjunction
tóngshí

Use 同时 to mean “at the same time / meanwhile”.

结构 StructureA，同时 + BA, tóngshí + B = A, and at the same time B
学习语言要多听，同时也要多说。
Xuéxí yǔyán yào duō tīng, tóngshí yě yào duō shuō.
To learn a language, listen more and also speak more.
他工作，同时也在学习。
Tā gōngzuò, tóngshí yě zài xuéxí.
He works and studies at the same time.
这个办法省时间，同时也省钱。
Zhège bànfǎ shěng shíjiān, tóngshí yě shěng qián.
This method saves time and money at the same time.
※ 既可以表示两件事同一时间发生，也可以连接并列信息。Can mean two things happen at once, or link parallel information.
