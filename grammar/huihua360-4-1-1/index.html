动态助词“着”动态助词Aspect particle
dòngtài zhùcí zhe

Use 着 after a verb to show that a state continues. Something stays this way.

结构 Structure主语 + 动词 + 着 +（宾语/其他成分）Subject + Verb + 着 (zhe, continuing state) + (Object / Other words)
她穿着一件白色的衬衫。
Tā chuān zhe yí jiàn báisè de chènshān.
She is wearing a white shirt.
门开着。
Mén kāi zhe.
The door is open.
他手里拿着一本书。
Tā shǒu li ná zhe yì běn shū.
He is holding a book in his hand.
※ 教材重点是持续的状态，并非“正在做”的动作过程。例：门开着（保持打开）≠ 正在开门（正在执行动作）。着 shows a lasting state, not an action in progress. 门开着 means the door stays open; 正在开门 means someone is opening it.
