语法汇总 Grammar◇“来”表示估计数量/承担动作
“来”表示估计数量/承担动作动词/助词化用法Verb / Particle-like
lái

来 can be used to take responsibility for an action: “let me/us do it”, or in some approximate expressions.

结构 Structure我来 + V；由/让 + 人 + 来 + Vwǒ lái + V; yóu/ràng + person + lái + V = let me do it / have sb do it
这个问题我来回答。
Zhège wèntí wǒ lái huídá.
Let me answer this question.
饭我来做，你休息吧。
Fàn wǒ lái zuò, nǐ xiūxi ba.
I will make the food; you rest.
这件事让他来处理。
Zhè jiàn shì ràng tā lái chǔlǐ.
Let him handle this matter.
※ 本课重点是“来”表示由谁做某件事。Focus: 来 indicating who will take on an action.
