语法汇总 Grammar◇时间副词“从来”
时间副词“从来”时间副词Time adverb
shíjiān fùcí cónglái

Use 从来, usually with 不/没, to mean “never”; it can also mean “always” in some fixed uses.

结构 Structure从来 + 不/没（有）+ 动词cónglái + bù/méi (yǒu) + Verb = never (as a rule) / never once
我从来不喝酒。
Wǒ cónglái bù hē jiǔ.
I never drink alcohol.
他从来没有迟到过。
Tā cónglái méiyǒu chídào guo.
He has never been late.
我从来不觉得学习中文很无聊。
Wǒ cónglái bù juéde xuéxí Zhōngwén hěn wúliáo.
I never feel that learning Chinese is boring.
※ 最常见结构是“从来不……”或“从来没（有）……过”。The usual patterns are 从来不… and 从来没（有）…过.
