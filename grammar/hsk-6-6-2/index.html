副词“明明”副词Adverb
míngmíng

Use 明明 when a fact is very clear, often followed by a surprising contradiction.

结构 Structure明明 + 明确事实，却/还 + 相反情况míngmíng + obvious fact, què/hái + opposite situation = clearly …, yet …
他明明知道答案，却不告诉我。
Tā míngmíng zhīdào dá'àn, què bú gàosu wǒ.
He clearly knows the answer, yet he will not tell me.
明明是你迟到了，怎么怪别人？
Míngmíng shì nǐ chídào le, zěnme guài biéren?
You were clearly late; how can you blame others?
天气明明很好，他却带了两把伞。
Tiānqì míngmíng hěn hǎo, tā què dàile liǎng bǎ sǎn.
The weather is clearly good, yet he brought two umbrellas.
※ 常带不理解、不满或强调事实的语气。Often carries puzzlement, annoyance, or emphasis on the obvious fact.
