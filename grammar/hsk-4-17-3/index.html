动量词“趟”和“次”动量词Verbal measure word
tàng / cì

次 counts actions generally. 趟 counts a trip from one place to another.

结构 StructureV + 数字 + 次；去/跑 + 数字 + 趟V + number + cì; qù/pǎo + number + tàng = do sth N times / make N trips
我去过北京三次。
Wǒ qù guo Běijīng sān cì.
I have been to Beijing three times.
我今天跑了两趟银行。
Wǒ jīntiān pǎo le liǎng tàng yínháng.
I made two trips to the bank today.
这个电影我看了两次。
Zhège diànyǐng wǒ kàn le liǎng cì.
I watched this movie twice.
※ “趟”强调来回/去一处的行程；“次”适用范围更广。趟 stresses a trip to and from a place; 次 is broader.
