语法汇总 Grammar◇程度副词“多”表示程度很高
程度副词“多”表示程度很高程度副词Degree adverb
chéngdù fùcí duō

Use 多 + adjective + 啊 to exclaim “how / so…!”.

结构 Structure多 + 形容词 + 啊！duō + Adjective + a! = How / so ...!
一个人过年多没意思啊！
Yí ge rén guò nián duō méi yì si a!
How boring it is to spend Spring Festival alone!
这里多漂亮啊！
Zhè lǐ duō piào liang a!
How beautiful it is here!
大家一起吃饭多好啊！
Dà jiā yì qǐ chī fàn duō hǎo a!
How nice it is to eat together!
※ 这里的“多”不是“多少”，而是感叹程度很高。Here 多 isn't "how many"; it's an exclamation of high degree.
