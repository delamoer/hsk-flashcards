语法汇总 Grammar◇介词/构词成分“逐”
介词/构词成分“逐”介词/构词成分Preposition / Word-forming element
zhú

Use 逐 to mean “one by one / each / step by step”, mostly in formal expressions.

结构 Structure逐 + 个/项/年/日/步 + 动词/变化zhú + gè/xiàng/nián/rì/bù + Verb/change = one by one / item by item / year by year / step by step
请逐项检查这些内容。
Qǐng zhúxiàng jiǎnchá zhèxiē nèiróng.
Please check these items one by one.
公司的收入逐年增加。
Gōngsī de shōurù zhúnián zēngjiā.
The company's income increases year by year.
问题正在逐步解决。
Wèntí zhèngzài zhúbù jiějué.
The problem is being solved step by step.
※ 常见固定搭配：逐个、逐项、逐年、逐日、逐步。Common set combinations: 逐个, 逐项, 逐年, 逐日, 逐步.
