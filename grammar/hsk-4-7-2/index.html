语法汇总 Grammar◇“来不及”表示没有足够时间
“来不及”表示没有足够时间可能补语/固定结构Potential / Fixed
láibují

Use 来不及 to mean “not enough time / too late to do something”.

结构 Structure来不及 + Vlái bu jí + V = there isn't time to …
快点儿，要来不及了。
Kuài diǎnr, yào lái bu jí le.
Hurry, we are running out of time.
我来不及吃早饭。
Wǒ lái bu jí chī zǎofàn.
I do not have time to eat breakfast.
现在出发还来得及。
Xiànzài chūfā hái láidejí.
If we leave now, there is still time.
※ 反义形式是“来得及”。Its opposite is 来得及 (to have enough time).
