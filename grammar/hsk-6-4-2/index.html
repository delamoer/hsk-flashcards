连词“不料”连词/副词Conjunction / Adverb
bùliào

Use 不料 to introduce an unexpected result: “unexpectedly / to one's surprise”.

结构 Structure原来的计划/预期，不料 + 意外结果Original plan / expectation, búliào + unexpected result = but, unexpectedly, …
我以为他不会来，不料他第一个到了。
Wǒ yǐwéi tā bú huì lái, búliào tā dì-yī ge dào le.
I thought he would not come, but unexpectedly he arrived first.
天气预报说晴天，不料下午下起了雨。
Tiānqì yùbào shuō qíngtiān, búliào xiàwǔ xiàqǐle yǔ.
The forecast said it would be sunny, but it unexpectedly rained in the afternoon.
我们准备得很充分，不料还是出了问题。
Wǒmen zhǔnbèi de hěn chōngfèn, búliào háishi chūle wèntí.
We prepared well, yet an unexpected problem still occurred.
※ 常用于叙述中，强调结果和预想相反。Common in narration; stresses that the result is the opposite of what was expected.
