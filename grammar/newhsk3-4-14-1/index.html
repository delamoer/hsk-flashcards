语法汇总 Grammar◇语气副词“究竟”
语气副词“究竟”语气副词Modal adverb
yǔqì fùcí jiūjìng

Use 究竟 in a question to strongly ask “exactly / after all”.

结构 Structure究竟 + 疑问句jiūjìng + question = what exactly …?
你究竟想买什么？
Nǐ jiūjìng xiǎng mǎi shénme?
What exactly do you want to buy?
他究竟去哪儿了？
Tā jiūjìng qù nǎr le?
Where on earth did he go?
这究竟是怎么回事？
Zhè jiūjìng shì zěnme huí shì?
What exactly is going on?
※ “究竟”表示很想知道真正答案，语气比普通疑问更强。究竟 shows you really want the true answer; stronger than a plain question.
