“都……了”表示“已经到了这种程度”副词结构Adverbial structure
dōu ... le

Use 都…了 to mean “already”, often with surprise, urgency, or a reminder.

结构 Structure都 + 时间/数量/状态 + 了dōu + Time / Number / State + le = “already ...” (often surprise / reminder)
都十点了，快睡觉吧。
dōu shí diǎn le, kuài shuìjiào ba.
It is already ten; go to sleep.
你都二十岁了。
nǐ dōu èrshí suì le.
You are already twenty.
我们都等了半小时了。
wǒmen dōu děng le bàn xiǎoshí le.
We have already waited for half an hour.
※ 常带说话人的感情色彩：觉得时间晚、数量多、情况已经明显。Carries the speaker's feeling: it's late, it's a lot, or it's already obvious.
