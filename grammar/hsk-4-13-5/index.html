语法汇总 Grammar◇介词“随着”
介词“随着”介词Preposition
suízhe

Use 随着 to mean “as / along with” a change or development.

结构 Structure随着 + 变化，另一变化suízhe + a change, another change = as … , …
随着天气变冷，穿外套的人越来越多。
Suízhe tiānqì biàn lěng, chuān wàitào de rén yuè lái yuè duō.
As the weather gets colder, more people wear coats.
随着时间的变化，他成熟了很多。
Suízhe shíjiān de biànhuà, tā chéngshú le hěn duō.
As time passed, he became much more mature.
随着汉语水平提高，我越来越有信心。
Suízhe Hànyǔ shuǐpíng tígāo, wǒ yuè lái yuè yǒu xìnxīn.
As my Chinese improves, I become more confident.
※ 前后通常是一起发展或变化的情况。The two parts usually develop or change together.
