语法汇总 Grammar◇“虽然……但是……”表示转折
“虽然……但是……”表示转折复句Complex sentence
suīrán ... dànshì ...

Use 虽然…但是… for “although…but…”.

结构 Structure虽然 + 情况，但是 + 相反/不同结果suīrán + Situation, dànshì + Contrasting result = “Although ..., but ...”
虽然很冷，但是他去跑步了。
suīrán hěn lěng, dànshì tā qù pǎobù le.
Although it was cold, he went running.
虽然他很忙，但是他来了。
suīrán tā hěn máng, dànshì tā lái le.
Although he was busy, he came.
这个菜虽然贵，但是很好吃。
zhè ge cài suīrán guì, dànshì hěn hǎochī.
Although this dish is expensive, it is delicious.
※ “虽然”说明让步条件，“但是”引出转折结果。虽然 states a concession; 但是 introduces the contrasting result.
