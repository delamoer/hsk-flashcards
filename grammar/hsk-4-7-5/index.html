语法汇总 Grammar◇“既……又/也/还……”
“既……又/也/还……”并列复句Coordinate clause
jì ... yòu/yě/hái ...

Use 既…又… to say two qualities or actions are both true.

结构 Structure既 + A，又/也/还 + Bjì + A, yòu/yě/hái + B = both A and B
这个地方既安静又漂亮。
Zhège dìfang jì ānjìng yòu piàoliang.
This place is both quiet and beautiful.
他既会唱歌，也会跳舞。
Tā jì huì chànggē, yě huì tiàowǔ.
He can both sing and dance.
这份工作既有意思，还能学到东西。
Zhè fèn gōngzuò jì yǒu yìsi, hái néng xué dào dōngxi.
This job is interesting and also lets you learn things.
※ 强调两个特点或情况同时存在。Stresses that two features/situations coexist.
