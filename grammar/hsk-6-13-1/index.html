语法汇总 Grammar◇动词“便于”
动词“便于”动词Verb
biànyú

Use 便于 to mean “be convenient for / make it easier to…”.

结构 Structure便于 + 动词/名词biànyú + Verb/Noun = make it easy to …
这个表格便于大家比较。
Zhège biǎogé biànyú dàjiā bǐjiào.
This table makes comparison easier.
文件按日期分类，便于查找。
Wénjiàn àn rìqī fēnlèi, biànyú cházhǎo.
The files are sorted by date for easier searching.
请留下电话，便于我们联系你。
Qǐng liúxià diànhuà, biànyú wǒmen liánxì nǐ.
Please leave your phone number so we can contact you easily.
※ 常用于说明某种安排带来的便利。Often explains the convenience an arrangement brings.
