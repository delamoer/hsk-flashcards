语法汇总 Grammar◇目的复句“……，好……”
目的复句“……，好……”目的复句Purpose clause
mùdì fùjù ... hǎo ...

Use 好 before the second clause to show purpose: “so that…”.

结构 Structure动作1，好 + 动作2/目的Action 1, hǎo + Action 2/purpose = do 1 so as to do 2
把地址发给我，好让我找到你。
Bǎ dìzhǐ fā gěi wǒ, hǎo ràng wǒ zhǎodào nǐ.
Send me the address so I can find you.
你早点儿准备，好明天准时出发。
Nǐ zǎo diǎnr zhǔnbèi, hǎo míngtiān zhǔnshí chūfā.
Prepare early so we can leave on time tomorrow.
我把电话号码告诉你，以后有事好联系我。
Wǒ bǎ diànhuà hàomǎ gàosu nǐ, yǐhòu yǒu shì hǎo liánxì wǒ.
I will give you my phone number so you can contact me later.
※ 第二个分句说明前一个动作的目的；这里“好”相当于“以便、方便”。The second clause gives the purpose of the first action; here 好 means “so that / so it's easier to”.
