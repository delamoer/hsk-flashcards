“多”表概数概数表达Approx. number
“duō” biǎo gàishù

Put 多 after a number to mean “a little more than that number”. Its position depends on the number.

结构 Structure整十/百数 + 多 + 量词 + 名词；数词 + 量词 + 多Round Number + 多 (duō, more than) + Measure Word + Noun; or Number + Measure Word + 多 (duō)
教室里有二十多个学生。
Jiàoshì li yǒu èrshí duō ge xuésheng.
There are a little over twenty students in the classroom.
他三岁多了。
Tā sān suì duō le.
He is a little over three years old.
这个杯子三十多块钱。
Zhège bēizi sānshí duō kuài qián.
This cup costs a little over thirty yuan.
※ 教材列出“二十多度、一百多个人”与“三岁多、五十六块多钱”两种位置；练习先选常用表达。多 can go in two places: 二十多度, 一百多个人 versus 三岁多, 五十六块多钱. The exercises use the most common ones.
