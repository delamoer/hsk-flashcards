语法汇总 Grammar◇“无论”和“不管”
“无论”和“不管”条件复句Conditional
wúlùn / bùguǎn

Both mean “no matter…”. 无论 is a little more formal.

结构 Structure无论/不管 + 疑问形式，都/也 + 结果wúlùn/bùguǎn + question form, dōu/yě + result = no matter …, still …
无论多忙，我都要吃早饭。
Wúlùn duō máng, wǒ dōu yào chī zǎofàn.
No matter how busy I am, I eat breakfast.
不管你去哪儿，我都支持你。
Bùguǎn nǐ qù nǎr, wǒ dōu zhīchí nǐ.
No matter where you go, I support you.
无论结果怎么样，我们都要努力。
Wúlùn jiéguǒ zěnmeyàng, wǒmen dōu yào nǔlì.
No matter what the result is, we must work hard.
※ 两者基本意思相同；“无论”书面感稍强。The two are basically the same; 无论 feels a bit more formal.
