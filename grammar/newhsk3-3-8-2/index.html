离合词（2）离合词Separable verb
líhécí (èr)

A separable verb can have 了, a duration, or a quantity inserted between its two parts.

结构 StructureA + 了/时量/数量 + BA + le / Duration / Quantity + B (separable verb)
我住了一个星期院。
Wǒ zhù le yí ge xīng qī yuàn.
I stayed in the hospital for one week.
我们上了两个小时课。
Wǒ men shàng le liǎng ge xiǎo shí kè.
We had class for two hours.
我昨晚睡了一个好觉。
Wǒ zuó wǎn shuì le yí ge hǎo jiào.
I had a good sleep last night.
※ 离合词中间可以插入动态助词、时量或数量成分，不能总把它当作普通双音节动词。Separable verbs can take particles/duration/quantity inside; not ordinary two-syllable verbs.
