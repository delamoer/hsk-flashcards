语法汇总 Grammar◇紧缩句
紧缩句复句结构Complex sentence
jǐnsuō jù

A 紧缩句 compresses a complex sentence into a short form, often without a pause or full conjunction pair.

结构 Structure压缩条件/让步等关系：A + B（如“不喜欢就不买”）Compressed condition / concession: A + B (e.g. bù xǐhuan jiù bù mǎi) = if/even if A, B
不喜欢就别买。
Bù xǐhuan jiù bié mǎi.
If you do not like it, do not buy it.
她一回来我就告诉你。
Tā yì huílai wǒ jiù gàosu nǐ.
As soon as she comes back, I will tell you.
想赢也不能违反规则。
Xiǎng yíng yě bù néng wéifǎn guīzé.
Even if you want to win, you cannot break the rules.
※ 看起来像单句，但实际包含条件、让步、承接等复句关系。Looks like a simple sentence but actually contains a conditional, concessive or sequential relation.
