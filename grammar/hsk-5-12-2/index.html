“……的程度 / 到……程度”程度表达Degree
chéngdù

Use 程度 to talk about how serious/high/strong something is: “degree / extent”.

结构 StructureAdj + 的程度；到 + … + 的程度Adj + de chéngdù; dào + … + de chéngdù = to the point of …
问题已经到了很严重的程度。
Wèntí yǐjīng dào le hěn yánzhòng de chéngdù.
The problem has reached a serious level.
我没想到他忙到这个程度。
Wǒ méi xiǎngdào tā máng dào zhège chéngdù.
I didn't expect him to be this busy.
污染的程度正在下降。
Wūrǎn de chéngdù zhèngzài xiàjiàng.
The level of pollution is decreasing.
※ 常见“达到……程度、严重程度、一定程度上”。Common: 达到…程度, 严重程度, 一定程度上.
