介词“按照”介词Preposition
ànzhào

Use 按照 to mean “according to / following”.

结构 Structure按照 + 标准/方法/计划 + Vànzhào + standard/method/plan + V = according to / following …
请按照计划完成工作。
Qǐng ànzhào jìhuà wánchéng gōngzuò.
Please finish the work according to the plan.
我们按照老师说的做。
Wǒmen ànzhào lǎoshī shuō de zuò.
We do what the teacher said.
按照规定，这里不能停车。
Ànzhào guīdìng, zhèlǐ bù néng tíngchē.
According to the rules, you cannot park here.
※ “按照”后面常接计划、规定、方法、要求等。按照 is followed by a plan, rule, method, requirement, etc.
