语法汇总 Grammar◇连词“可是”
连词“可是”连词Conjunction
kěshì

Use 可是 to mean “but / however” in spoken Chinese.

结构 StructureA，可是 + BA, kěshì + B = A, but B
我想去，可是今天没时间。
Wǒ xiǎng qù, kěshì jīntiān méi shíjiān.
I want to go, but I have no time today.
这个办法很好，可是有点儿贵。
Zhège bànfǎ hěn hǎo, kěshì yǒudiǎnr guì.
This method is good, but a little expensive.
他很年轻，可是很有经验。
Tā hěn niánqīng, kěshì hěn yǒu jīngyàn.
He is young, but very experienced.
※ “可是”与“但是”意思接近，口语很常用。可是 is close to 但是 and very common in speech.
