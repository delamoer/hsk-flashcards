动词“嫌”引出评价兼语/动词Pivot verb
xián

Use 嫌 to mean “dislike / think something is too…”.

结构 Structure嫌 + 名词 + 形容词/小句；嫌 + 形容词xián + Noun + Adj/Clause; xián + Adj = find (sb/sth) too … / dislike that …
我嫌这个房间太小。
Wǒ xián zhège fángjiān tài xiǎo.
I think this room is too small.
他嫌路远，不想去。
Tā xián lù yuǎn, bù xiǎng qù.
He thinks the road is too far and does not want to go.
别嫌麻烦，再检查一遍。
Bié xián máfan, zài jiǎnchá yí biàn.
Do not think it is too troublesome; check it again.
※ “嫌 + 宾语 + 形容词”中，宾语也是后面状态的主体。In 嫌 + object + adjective, the object is also the one who has that state.
