语法汇总 Grammar◇连词“总之”
连词“总之”连词Conjunction
zǒngzhī

Use 总之 to summarize: “in short / all in all”.

结构 Structure前文。总之，+ 总结Preceding text. Zǒngzhī, + summary = in short, …
总之，我们要先解决最重要的问题。
Zǒngzhī, wǒmen yào xiān jiějué zuì zhòngyào de wèntí.
In short, we need to solve the most important problem first.
方法很多，总之适合自己最重要。
Fāngfǎ hěn duō, zǒngzhī shìhé zìjǐ zuì zhòngyào.
There are many methods; in short, what suits you matters most.
总之，这次旅行很顺利。
Zǒngzhī, zhè cì lǚxíng hěn shùnlì.
All in all, the trip went smoothly.
※ 放在总结性结论前。Placed before a summarizing conclusion.
