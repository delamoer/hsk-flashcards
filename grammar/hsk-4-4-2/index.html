语法汇总 Grammar◇“原来”和“本来”
“原来”和“本来”副词Adverb
yuánlái / běnlái

原来 often shows a newly discovered fact. 本来 shows the original situation or plan.

结构 Structure原来 + 新发现；本来 + 原计划/原情况yuánlái + new discovery; běnlái + original plan/state = so it turns out … vs originally
原来你也住在这里。
Yuánlái nǐ yě zhù zài zhèlǐ.
So you live here too.
我本来想坐地铁。
Wǒ běnlái xiǎng zuò dìtiě.
I originally planned to take the subway.
原来他就是新经理。
Yuánlái tā jiùshì xīn jīnglǐ.
So he is the new manager.
※ “原来”常有“现在才知道”的感觉；“本来”强调原先。原来 carries "I only just realized"; 本来 stresses the original state.
