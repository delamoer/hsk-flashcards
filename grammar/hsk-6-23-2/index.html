重叠形式“大大、远远”程度副词Degree adverb
dàdà, yuǎnyuǎn

Use 大大 or 远远 before a verb/adjective to strongly increase degree or difference.

结构 Structure大大 + 提高/降低/超过；远远 + 高于/低于/超过dàdà + tígāo/jiàngdī/chāoguò; yuǎnyuǎn + gāoyú/dīyú/chāoguò = greatly …; far …
这个办法大大提高了效率。
Zhège bànfǎ dàdà tígāole xiàolǜ.
This method greatly improved efficiency.
结果远远超过了我们的预期。
Jiéguǒ yuǎnyuǎn chāoguòle wǒmen de yùqī.
The result far exceeded our expectations.
新设备使成本大大降低。
Xīn shèbèi shǐ chéngběn dàdà jiàngdī.
The new equipment greatly reduced costs.
※ 这类重叠形式常作程度副词，强调变化大或差距远。These reduplicated forms work as degree adverbs, stressing a big change or a wide gap.
