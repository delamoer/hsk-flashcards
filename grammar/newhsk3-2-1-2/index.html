语法汇总 Grammar◇“是……的”句
“是……的”句强调句Emphatic sentence
shì ... de jù

Use 是…的 to emphasize the time, place, way, person, or purpose of a completed event.

结构 Structure主语 +（是）+ 强调信息 + 动词 + 的Subject + (shì) + Emphasized information + Verb + de
我是坐地铁来的。
Wǒ shì zuò dì tiě lái de.
I came by subway.
这本书是朋友送的。
Zhè běn shū shì péng you sòng de.
A friend gave me this book.
我们是来旅游的。
Wǒ men shì lái lǚ yóu de.
We came here to travel.
※ 肯定句中的“是”有时可以省略；本级先掌握最常见的强调方式、人物和目的。是 can be dropped in positive sentences; focus on stressing manner, person and purpose.
