语法汇总 Grammar◇递进复句“……，更……”
递进复句“……，更……”递进复句Progressive clause
... gèng ...

Use 更 in the second part to add a stronger or higher-degree point: “even more”.

结构 StructureA，B + 更 + 形容词/动词A, B + gèng + Adjective/Verb (even more ...)
这本书很好，那本书更有意思。
Zhè běn shū hěn hǎo, nà běn shū gèng yǒu yì si.
This book is good; that one is even more interesting.
白天很冷，晚上更冷。
Bái tiān hěn lěng, wǎn shàng gèng lěng.
It is cold in the daytime and even colder at night.
我喜欢中文，更喜欢中文课。
Wǒ xǐ huan zhōng wén, gèng xǐ huan zhōng wén kè.
I like Chinese, and I like Chinese class even more.
※ 第二部分在前一层意思上进一步加强程度。The second part intensifies the degree of the first.
