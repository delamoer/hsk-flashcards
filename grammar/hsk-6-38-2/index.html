“依据”表示根据名词/动词/介词Noun / Verb / Preposition
yījù

Use 依据 to mean “basis / according to / base on”.

结构 Structure依据 + 材料/规则 + 动词；以……为依据yījù + material/rule + Verb; yǐ … wéi yījù = according to …; take … as the basis
我们依据数据做出了判断。
Wǒmen yījù shùjù zuòchūle pànduàn.
We made the judgment based on data.
这个结论有充分的事实依据。
Zhège jiélùn yǒu chōngfèn de shìshí yījù.
This conclusion has sufficient factual basis.
请以合同为依据处理这个问题。
Qǐng yǐ hétong wéi yījù chǔlǐ zhège wèntí.
Please handle this issue based on the contract.
※ 既可作名词，也可作动词/介词性用法；常用于正式论证。Works as a noun or as a verb/preposition; common in formal argument.
