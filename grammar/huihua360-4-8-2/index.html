语法汇总 Grammar◇一……就……
一……就……承接复句Sequential clause
yī ... jiù ...

Use 一…就… to mean “as soon as…”. The second action happens immediately after the first.

结构 Structure主语 + 一 + 动作1，就 + 动作2Subject + 一 (yī, as soon as) + Action 1, 就 (jiù, immediately) + Action 2
我一到家就给你打电话。
Wǒ yí dào jiā jiù gěi nǐ dǎ diànhuà.
I will call you as soon as I get home.
他一听到消息就笑了。
Tā yì tīng dào xiāoxi jiù xiào le.
He smiled as soon as he heard the news.
我一下课就回家。
Wǒ yí xiàkè jiù huí jiā.
I go home as soon as class ends.
※ 教材说明前后可同一主语或不同主语，重点是动作一发生就紧接着出现结果。The two parts can have the same or different subjects; the point is that the second thing happens right after the first.
