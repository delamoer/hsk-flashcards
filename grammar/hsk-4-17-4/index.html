语法汇总 Grammar◇为了……而……
为了……而……目的结构Purpose structure
wèile ... ér ...

Use 为了…而… to mean “for the sake of / in order to…”.

结构 Structure为了 + 目的，而 + 行动wèile + purpose, ér + action = in order to …, do …
他为了健康而每天运动。
Tā wèile jiànkāng ér měi tiān yùndòng.
He exercises every day for his health.
我们为了这个目标而努力。
Wǒmen wèile zhège mùbiāo ér nǔlì.
We work hard for this goal.
她为了学习汉语而来到中国。
Tā wèile xuéxí Hànyǔ ér láidào Zhōngguó.
She came to China to study Chinese.
※ “而”有时可以省略；完整结构更正式。而 can sometimes be dropped; the full pattern is more formal.
