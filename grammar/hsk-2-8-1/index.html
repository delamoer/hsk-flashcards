语法汇总 Grammar◇“……，好吗？”征求意见
“……，好吗？”征求意见疑问句Question
... hǎo ma

Add 好吗？ to a suggestion to ask “okay?”.

结构 Structure建议句 + ，好吗？Suggestion + hǎo ma? = “..., okay?”
我们明天去，好吗？
wǒmen míngtiān qù, hǎo ma?
Let’s go tomorrow, okay?
你等我一下，好吗？
nǐ děng wǒ yíxià, hǎo ma?
Wait for me a moment, okay?
我们一起吃饭，好吗？
wǒmen yìqǐ chīfàn, hǎo ma?
Let’s eat together, okay?
※ 语气比较柔和，常用于建议或请求。The tone is soft; often used for suggestions or requests.
