语法汇总 Grammar◇“彼此”和“互相”
“彼此”和“互相”代词/副词Pronoun / Adverb
bǐcǐ / hùxiāng

彼此 means “each other” as a pronoun. 互相 is an adverb meaning “mutually”.

结构 Structure彼此 + V/N；互相 + Vbǐcǐ + V/N; hùxiāng + V = each other / mutually
我们应该互相帮助。
Wǒmen yīnggāi hùxiāng bāngzhù.
We should help each other.
两个人彼此都很了解。
Liǎng ge rén bǐcǐ dōu hěn liǎojiě.
The two people know each other well.
朋友之间要彼此信任。
Péngyou zhī jiān yào bǐcǐ xìnrèn.
Friends should trust each other.
※ “互相”不能直接作定语；“彼此”可作主语/宾语/定语。互相 can't be a modifier; 彼此 can be subject/object/modifier.
