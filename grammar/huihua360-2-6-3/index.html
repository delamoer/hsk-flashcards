能愿动词“能”能愿动词Modal verb
néngyuàn dòngcí néng

Use 能 before a verb for permission or when a situation makes an action possible.

结构 Structure主语 + 能/不能 + 动词 +（宾语）Subject + 能 (néng, can / be allowed to) / 不能 (bù néng) + Verb + (Object)
这儿能拍照吗？
Zhèr néng pāizhào ma?
Can I take pictures here?
这里不能抽烟。
Zhèlǐ bù néng chōuyān.
You cannot smoke here.
我今天能去医院。
Wǒ jīntiān néng qù yīyuàn.
I can go to the hospital today.
※ 教材主要讲条件、环境或规定允许，用“不能”否定；第3课“会”主要表示学会的技能。能 here is about what conditions, circumstances or rules allow; the negative is 不能. 会 (Lesson 3) is mainly for learned skills.
