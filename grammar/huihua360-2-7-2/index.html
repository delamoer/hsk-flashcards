语法汇总 Grammar◇介词“给”
介词“给”介词Preposition
jiècí gěi

Put 给 + person before a verb to show who receives the action.

结构 Structure主语 + 给 + 人 + 动词 +（宾语）Subject + 给 (gěi, to/for) + Person + Verb + (Object)
我给妈妈打电话。
Wǒ gěi māma dǎ diànhuà.
I call Mom.
她给朋友买蛋糕。
Tā gěi péngyou mǎi dàngāo.
She buys a cake for a friend.
他给老师发邮件。
Tā gěi lǎoshī fā yóujiàn.
He sends an email to the teacher.
※ 这里“给”是介词，引出动作对象或接受者；“给 + 人”放在主要动词前。Here 给 is a preposition introducing the receiver of the action; 给 + person goes before the main verb.
