比较句（4）：“比 + 形容词 + 多了/得多”比较句Comparison
bǐjiào jù (sì)

Use 多了 or 得多 after the adjective to show a big difference.

结构 StructureA + 比 + B + 形容词 + 多了/得多A + bǐ + B + Adjective + duō le / de duō = much more...
这里比北京冷多了。
Zhè lǐ bǐ běi jīng lěng duō le.
It is much colder here than in Beijing.
坐飞机比坐火车快得多。
Zuò fēi jī bǐ zuò huǒ chē kuài de duō.
Flying is much faster than taking the train.
这个房间比那个大多了。
Zhè ge fáng jiān bǐ nà ge dà duō le.
This room is much bigger than that one.
※ “多了/得多”表示差别比较大。多了/得多 shows a large difference.
