动态助词“着”表示状态持续助词Particle
zhe

Use 着 after a verb to show a state continues.

结构 Structure动词 + 着Verb + zhe = the state continues / be in a continuing state
门开着呢。
mén kāizhe ne.
The door is open.
桌子上放着一本书。
zhuōzi shàng fàngzhe yì běn shū.
There is a book lying on the table.
他手里拿着手机。
tā shǒu lǐ názhe shǒujī.
He is holding a phone in his hand.
※ “着”强调状态持续；不要和“正在……呢”的进行动作混淆。着 stresses a continuing state; don't confuse it with the ongoing action 正在…呢.
