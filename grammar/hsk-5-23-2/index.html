代词“某”表示不确定的某人/某事代词/定语Pronoun / Attributive
mǒu

Use 某 before a noun to mean “a certain / some unspecified”.

结构 Structure某 + 人/地/时间；某某mǒu + person/place/time; mǒumǒu = a certain … / so-and-so
某一天，他突然来找我。
Mǒu yì tiān, tā tūrán lái zhǎo wǒ.
One day, he suddenly came to see me.
某位老师提出了这个问题。
Mǒu wèi lǎoshī tíchū le zhège wèntí.
A certain teacher raised this question.
张某已经离开了公司。
Zhāng mǒu yǐjīng líkāi le gōngsī.
A person surnamed Zhang has left the company.
※ 常用于不知道、不方便说或不需要说具体是谁。Used when the specific person is unknown, awkward or needless to name.
