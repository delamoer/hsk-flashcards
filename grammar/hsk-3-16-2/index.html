语法汇总 Grammar◇复杂的状态补语：“形容词/动词 + 得 + 动词词组”
复杂的状态补语：“形容词/动词 + 得 + 动词词组”状态补语State complement
fùzá de zhuàngtài bǔyǔ

Use adjective/verb + 得 + a phrase to show a strong degree and its result.

结构 Structure形容词/动词 + 得 + 动词词组/小句Adjective/Verb + de + verb phrase/clause = … to the point that …
我累得不想说话。
Wǒ lèi de bù xiǎng shuōhuà.
I am so tired that I do not want to talk.
他忙得没时间吃饭。
Tā máng de méi shíjiān chī fàn.
He is so busy that he has no time to eat.
孩子高兴得又唱又跳。
Háizi gāoxìng de yòu chàng yòu tiào.
The child is so happy that he sings and jumps.
※ “得”后面不是简单形容词，而可以是一整个短语或小句。After 得 you can have a whole phrase or clause, not just one adjective.
