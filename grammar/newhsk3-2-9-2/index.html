动词“离”距离表达Distance expression
dòngcí lí

Use A 离 B + far/near to talk about distance between two places or times.

结构 StructureA + 离 + B + 远/近；离 + 时间 + 还有……A + lí + B + far/near; lí + Time + hái yǒu... = distance / time remaining
我家离学校很近。
Wǒ jiā lí xué xiào hěn jìn.
My home is close to the school.
这里离机场远吗？
Zhè lǐ lí jī chǎng yuǎn ma?
Is this place far from the airport?
离考试还有三天。
Lí kǎo shì hái yǒu sān tiān.
There are three days until the exam.
※ “离”可以表示空间距离，也可以表示时间距离。离 can express distance in space or in time.
