语法汇总 Grammar◇“快……了”和“要……了”
“快……了”和“要……了”将来/变化表达Future / Change
kuài ... le hé yào ... le

Use 快…了 or 要…了 to say something will happen soon.

结构 Structure快 + 动词/状态 + 了；要 + 动词/状态 + 了；快要 + 动词/状态 + 了快 (kuài, soon) / 要 (yào, about to) / 快要 (kuàiyào, soon) + Verb/Change + 了 (le)
快放假了。
Kuài fàngjià le.
The holiday is coming soon.
电影要开始了。
Diànyǐng yào kāishǐ le.
The movie is about to start.
我们快要下课了。
Wǒmen kuàiyào xiàkè le.
Our class will be over soon.
※ 书中第8课将“快……了”和“要……了”并列讲解，都表示情况即将变化；基本表达句末应有“了”。Lesson 8 teaches 快…了 and 要…了 together; both mean a change is about to happen. In the basic pattern, 了 is needed at the end.
