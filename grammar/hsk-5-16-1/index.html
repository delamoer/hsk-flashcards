“即”表示“就是/也就是”或“如果就”书面副词/连词Formal adverb / conj.
jí

Use 即 in formal Chinese for “namely / that is”, and sometimes “if…then…”.

结构 StructureA，即B；条件即结果A, jí B; condition jí result = that is / namely (formal)
春节，即农历新年。
Chūn Jié, jí nónglì xīnnián.
Spring Festival, that is, Lunar New Year.
价格下降，即表示需求减少吗？
Jiàgé xiàjiàng, jí biǎoshì xūqiú jiǎnshǎo ma?
Does a price drop mean demand decreased?
有问题即可联系我。
Yǒu wèntí jí kě liánxì wǒ.
If there is a problem, you can contact me.
※ “即”很书面，常见“即使、即将、即为、即可”。即 is very formal; e.g. 即使, 即将, 即为, 即可.
