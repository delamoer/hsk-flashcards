反问句疑问句Question
fǎnwèn jù

A rhetorical question is not a real request for an answer. It makes a point stronger.

结构 Structure难道 + 陈述句 + 吗？；不是……吗？；怎么……？难道 (nándào, surely) + Statement + 吗 (ma)?; 不是 (bú shì) + ... + 吗?; 怎么 (zěnme, how could) + ...?
难道你不知道吗？
Nándào nǐ bù zhīdào ma?
Don't you know that?
这不是你的书吗？
Zhè bú shì nǐ de shū ma?
Isn’t this your book?
你怎么能这样说呢？
Nǐ zěnme néng zhèyàng shuō ne?
How could you say that?
※ 课本例示“不是……吗、怎么……、难道……”等常见形式，目的在于强调而非获取新信息。Common forms are 不是…吗, 怎么… and 难道…. They are for emphasis, not for asking new information.
