语法汇总 Grammar◇动态助词“了”
动态助词“了”动态助词Aspect particle
dòngtài zhùcí le

Put 了 just after a verb to show that an action is completed.

结构 Structure主语 + 动词 + 了 + 数量/宾语Subject + Verb + 了 (le, completed action) + Amount/Object
我买了一件衣服。
Wǒ mǎi le yí jiàn yīfu.
I bought a piece of clothing.
妈妈买了三个苹果。
Māma mǎi le sān ge píngguǒ.
Mom bought three apples.
我喝了一杯水。
Wǒ hē le yì bēi shuǐ.
I drank a glass of water.
※ 这是动词后的“了”，重点说明动作完成；与第二册学过的句末“了”（发生/变化）分别理解。This is 了 right after the verb, showing the action is completed. Keep it apart from the sentence-final 了 (happened/changed) from Book 2.
