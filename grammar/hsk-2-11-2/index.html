“比”字句表示比较比较句Comparison
bǐ

Use A 比 B + adjective to say A is more … than B.

结构 StructureA + 比 + B + 形容词 (+ 数量)A + bǐ + B + Adjective (+ Amount) = A is ...er than B
他比我大三岁。
tā bǐ wǒ dà sān suì.
He is three years older than me.
今天比昨天冷。
jīntiān bǐ zuótiān lěng.
Today is colder than yesterday.
这件衣服比那件便宜。
zhè jiàn yīfu bǐ nà jiàn piányi.
This item of clothing is cheaper than that one.
※ “比”字句里形容词前一般不用“很”。In a 比 sentence, don't put 很 before the adjective.
