介词/方位结构“以”介词/方位词Prep. / Locative
yǐ

Use 以 with directions or limits, such as 以上/以下/以东/以西.

结构 Structure数字/地点 + 以上/以下/以东/以西number/place + yǐshàng/yǐxià/yǐdōng/yǐxī = above/below/east of/west of
十八岁以上的人可以买票。
Shíbā suì yǐshàng de rén kěyǐ mǎi piào.
People aged 18 and above can buy tickets.
三十度以下比较舒服。
Sānshí dù yǐxià bǐjiào shūfu.
Below 30 degrees is more comfortable.
学校以东有一个公园。
Xuéxiào yǐdōng yǒu yí ge gōngyuán.
There is a park east of the school.
※ “以”常和上、下、东、西等组成固定方位/范围表达。以 combines with 上/下/东/西 into fixed position/range expressions.
