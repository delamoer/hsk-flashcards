量词/名词“片”量词Measure word
piàn

Use 片 for flat pieces, areas, or waves/groups of things.

结构 Structure一片 + 面包/叶子/云/掌声yí piàn + bread/leaf/cloud/applause = one slice/leaf/cloud/burst of …
桌上有一片面包。
Zhuō shang yǒu yí piàn miànbāo.
There is a slice of bread on the table.
天空中有一片云。
Tiānkōng zhōng yǒu yí piàn yún.
There is a patch of cloud in the sky.
演出结束后响起一片掌声。
Yǎnchū jiéshù hòu xiǎng qǐ yí piàn zhǎngshēng.
A wave of applause arose after the show.
※ 可表示“一片叶子/面包/海/掌声”等。Can count 一片叶子/面包/海/掌声, etc.
