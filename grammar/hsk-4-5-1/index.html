语法汇总 Grammar◇副词/动词“肯定”
副词/动词“肯定”副词/动词Adverb / Verb
kěndìng

Use 肯定 to mean “certainly / be sure”.

结构 Structure肯定 + 谓语；肯定 + 句子kěndìng + predicate; kěndìng + Sentence = definitely / surely
他肯定会来。
Tā kěndìng huì lái.
He will definitely come.
我不能肯定他什么时候到。
Wǒ bù néng kěndìng tā shénme shíhou dào.
I cannot be sure when he will arrive.
这次考试你肯定没问题。
Zhè cì kǎoshì nǐ kěndìng méi wèntí.
You will definitely be fine on this test.
※ 作副词时表示很有把握；作动词时表示“确认/认为确定”。As an adverb it means "surely"; as a verb, "to confirm/be sure".
