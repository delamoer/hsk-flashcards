语法汇总 Grammar◇“假设”表示假定
“假设”表示假定动词/名词Verb / Noun
jiǎshè

Use 假设 to mean “suppose / assumption”.

结构 Structure假设 + 句子；一个假设jiǎshè + Sentence; a jiǎshè = suppose that … / a hypothesis
假设明天下雨，我们怎么办？
Jiǎshè míngtiān xià yǔ, wǒmen zěnme bàn?
Suppose it rains tomorrow; what will we do?
这个结论建立在一个假设上。
Zhège jiélùn jiànlì zài yí ge jiǎshè shang.
This conclusion is based on an assumption.
假设你有一个月假期，你想去哪儿？
Jiǎshè nǐ yǒu yí ge yuè jiàqī, nǐ xiǎng qù nǎr?
Suppose you had a month off; where would you go?
※ 既可作动词引出假设条件，也可作名词。Can be a verb introducing a hypothesis, or a noun.
