语法汇总 Grammar◇固定短语“就是说”
固定短语“就是说”固定短语Set phrase
gùdìng duǎnyǔ jiùshìshuō

Use 就是说 to explain the previous idea more clearly: “that is to say / in other words”.

结构 Structure前一句，就是说 + 更清楚的解释the previous sentence, jiùshì shuō + a clearer restatement = …, that is to say, …
他明天没空，就是说，我们要改时间。
Tā míngtiān méi kòng, jiùshì shuō, wǒmen yào gǎi shíjiān.
He is not free tomorrow; in other words, we need to change the time.
这家店周一休息，就是说，今天不开门。
Zhè jiā diàn zhōuyī xiūxi, jiùshì shuō, jīntiān bù kāimén.
The shop is closed on Mondays; that means it is not open today.
学习不是一天的事，就是说，我们要一直学习。
Xuéxí bú shì yì tiān de shì, jiùshì shuō, wǒmen yào yìzhí xuéxí.
Learning is not a one-day thing; that is to say, we need to keep learning.
※ 用于进一步解释前面的内容，常换一种更清楚的说法。Used to explain what was just said, usually restating it more clearly.
