时量补语（1）时量补语Duration complement
shíliàng bǔyǔ (yī)

Put a duration after a verb to say how long an action lasts.

结构 Structure动词 +（了）+ 时段；V + O + V + 了 + 时段Verb + (le) + Duration; V + O + V + le + Duration
我等了十分钟。
Wǒ děng le shí fēn zhōng.
I waited for ten minutes.
他们学了两年中文。
Tā men xué le liǎng nián zhōng wén.
They studied Chinese for two years.
他跑步跑了一个小时。
Tā pǎo bù pǎo le yí ge xiǎo shí.
He ran for one hour.
※ 时量补语表示动作持续的时间长度。A duration complement shows how long an action lasts.
