被动结构“为……所……”书面被动句Formal passive
wéi ... suǒ ...

Use 为…所… as a formal passive structure: “be … by…”.

结构 StructureA + 为 + B + 所 + VA + wéi + B + suǒ + V = A is …-ed by B (formal passive)
他的故事为很多人所熟悉。
Tā de gùshi wéi hěn duō rén suǒ shúxī.
His story is familiar to many people.
这个地方为游客所喜爱。
Zhège dìfang wéi yóukè suǒ xǐ'ài.
This place is loved by tourists.
他的意见没有为大家所接受。
Tā de yìjiàn méiyǒu wéi dàjiā suǒ jiēshòu.
His opinion was not accepted by everyone.
※ 较正式、书面，相当于“被”。Formal/written, equivalent to 被.
