语法汇总 Grammar◇并列复句“既……，又/也……”
并列复句“既……，又/也……”并列复句Coordinate clause
bìngliè fùjù jì ... yòu/yě ...

Use 既…又/也… to say two qualities or situations are both true.

结构 Structure既 + A，又/也 + Bjì + A, yòu/yě + B = both A and B
这个工作既有意思，又能学到东西。
Zhège gōngzuò jì yǒu yìsi, yòu néng xué dào dōngxi.
This job is interesting and also lets you learn things.
他既会说英语，也会说中文。
Tā jì huì shuō Yīngyǔ, yě huì shuō Zhōngwén.
He can speak both English and Chinese.
坐地铁既快又方便。
Zuò dìtiě jì kuài yòu fāngbiàn.
Taking the subway is both fast and convenient.
※ “既……又……”和“又……又……”都表示并列，但“既”更适合完整分句。Both 既…又… and 又…又… list qualities side by side, but 既 suits full clauses better.
