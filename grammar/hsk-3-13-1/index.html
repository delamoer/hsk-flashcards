复合趋向补语趋向补语Directional compl.
fùhé qūxiàng bǔyǔ

Use a direction word + 来/去 after a verb to show a more specific direction.

结构 StructureV + 上/下/进/出/回/过/起 + 来/去V + shàng/xià/jìn/chū/huí/guò/qǐ + lái/qù = compound directional complement
请走进来。
Qǐng zǒu jìnlai.
Please walk in.
他跑出去了。
Tā pǎo chūqu le.
He ran out.
我把书拿回来了。
Wǒ bǎ shū ná huílai le.
I brought the book back.
※ “来”朝说话人方向，“去”离开说话人方向。来 is toward the speaker; 去 is away from the speaker.
