中国风Chinese-style pop

中国风 zhōngguó fēng is a pop style that blends modern music with traditional instruments and the imagery of classical poetry — ink, moonlight, old inns, West Lake. Listen, then learn the words hidden in each song.

题跋 · The story

The best-known 中国风 song of all. Every line paints a piece of blue-and-white porcelain — “the sky-blue glaze waits for the misty rain, and I am waiting for you.” It won Song of the Year at Taiwan's 2008 Golden Melody Awards.

中国风最有名的一首，获 2008 年金曲奖年度歌曲：「天青色等烟雨，而我在等你」。
