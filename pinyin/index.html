声母不能单独发音，这里读的是教学「呼读音」（b→bo）。Initials are voiced with a helper vowel.

点每个声调分别听。Tap each tone to hear it.

单韵母 Single vowels

alike a in "father"
olike o in "for" (rounded)
elike u in "duh" (unrounded)
ilike ee in "see"
ulike oo in "food"
üsay ee with rounded lips

复韵母 Compound vowels

ailike "eye"
eilike ay in "day"
uilike "way"
aolike ow in "cow"
oulike o in "go"
iulike yo in "yo-yo"
ielike ye in "yes"
üeü + "eh"

特殊韵母 Special

erlike er in "her" (curled tongue)

前鼻韵母 -n endings

anlike an in "ban"
enlike en in "broken"
inlike in in "bin"
unlike "won"
ünü + "n"

后鼻韵母 -ng endings

anglike ong in "song"
englike ung in "sung"
inglike ing in "sing"
onglike "oong" (oo + ng)
