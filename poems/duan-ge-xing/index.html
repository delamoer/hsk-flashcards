导读About this poem

A feast, a cup of wine and a sudden thought: life is as short as the morning dew. Cao Cao turns that worry into a call to talented people — come and join me, and together we can win the world.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗是曹操在宴会上唱的歌。开头四句很直接：一边喝酒一边唱歌，人生能有多长呢？就像早上的露水，太阳一出来就没了，过去的日子已经太多了。所以心中激动，忧愁难忘，什么能解除忧愁？只有酒。“杜康”传说是最早造酒的人，后来就用他的名字代指酒——这种用相关的人或物代替本来的东西的方法叫“借代”。诗人愁的不只是人生短，更是大业还没完成、人才还不够。结尾四句写月明星稀，乌鹊向南飞，绕着树飞了好几圈，找不到可以落脚的树枝——比喻那些还在犹豫、不知道投奔谁的人才。最后诗人说：山不嫌高，海不嫌深；我也要像周公那样热情对待人才，让天下人都归心于我。从忧愁写到豪迈，这就是曹操诗歌“慷慨悲凉”的特点。

In English

Cao Cao sang this song at a banquet. It begins very directly: as I drink and sing, how long is a human life? Like morning dew, it is gone as soon as the sun comes up, and too many days are already behind me. My heart is stirred and my worries won’t go away — what can cure them? Only Du Kang. Du Kang was the legendary first maker of wine, so his name came to mean wine itself; using a related name in place of the thing is called jièdài (metonymy). But the poet is not just afraid of growing old. He is worried that his great task — reuniting China — is unfinished, and that he does not have enough able people. In the closing lines, crows fly south under a bright moon, circling a tree three times with no branch to land on: a picture of talented men who have not yet decided whom to serve. Then comes his answer: a mountain never minds being too high, the sea never minds being too deep. Like the Duke of Zhou, who would stop in the middle of a meal to greet a guest, he will welcome them all, and the world will give him its heart. The song moves from sadness to bold confidence — exactly the mixture Cao Cao is famous for.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Cao Cao (155–220) was the most powerful warlord at the end of the Han dynasty, and a fine poet. The date of this song is unknown; it is a yuefu, a poem written to be sung, and was probably performed at a feast for his officers and guests.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

多音字：对酒当歌、慨当以慷的“当”读 dāng；朝露 zhāo lù（早晨，不读 cháo）；几何 jǐ hé；山不厌高 yàn。生僻字：三匝 zā、吐哺 bǔ。

版本说明 About the text

采用统编语文教材必修上册文本。全诗共三十二句（四言，一百二十八字），本页节选两部分：开头八句（“对酒当歌”到“唯有杜康”，第1段）和结尾八句（“月明星稀”到“天下归心”，第2段）；中间“青青子衿”“呦呦鹿鸣”等十六句引用《诗经》、写渴求人才，此处略去。异文：“人生几何”句末一作问号；“唯有杜康”一作“惟有杜康”；“何枝可依”一作“无枝可依”；“海不厌深”一作“水不厌深”。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

对酒当歌，人生几何！

duì jiǔ dāng gē, rén shēng jǐ hé

With wine before us, let us sing — how short a life is!

譬如朝露，去日苦多。

pì rú zhāo lù, qù rì kǔ duō

Like dew at dawn it disappears; too many days are already gone.

慨当以慷，忧思难忘。

kǎi dāng yǐ kāng, yōu sī nán wàng

My heart is stirred and will not settle; my worries will not leave me.

何以解忧？唯有杜康。

hé yǐ jiě yōu, wéi yǒu dù kāng

What can untie this worry? Only wine.

月明星稀，乌鹊南飞。

yuè míng xīng xī, wū què nán fēi

The moon is bright and the stars are few; crows fly off to the south.

绕树三匝，何枝可依？

rào shù sān zā, hé zhī kě yī

Three times they circle the tree — which branch will hold them?

山不厌高，海不厌深。

shān bù yàn gāo, hǎi bù yàn shēn

No mountain is ever too high, no sea ever too deep.

周公吐哺，天下归心。

zhōu gōng tǔ bǔ, tiān xià guī xīn

The Duke of Zhou left his meal to greet each guest — and the whole world came to him.
