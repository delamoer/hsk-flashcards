三五七言（秋风清）

Sān Wǔ Qī Yán
Lines of Three, Five and Seven (The Autumn Wind Is Clear)
李白Lǐ Bái · Li Bai唐 Tang

导读About this poem

Also called “Autumn Wind Song”: the lines grow from three characters to five to seven, as longing grows on a clear autumn night. It ends with a sigh many lovers still quote — if I’d known it would hurt like this, I’d rather we had never met.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗又叫《秋风词》，形式很特别：开头两句各三个字，接着两句五个字，再两句七个字，所以叫“三五七言”，句子越来越长，就像心里的思念越来越多。前六句写秋夜：秋风清凉，秋月明亮；落叶聚在一起又被风吹散，乌鸦已经栖息又被惊起——落叶和寒鸦都不安定，正像思念的人睡不着。接着说：相思的人什么时候才能相见？这样的夜晚真让人难受。后六句直接说出相思之苦：一旦走进相思的门，才知道相思有多苦；长久的相思让人一直回忆，短暂的相思也没有尽头；早知道这样牵挂人心，还不如当初不认识。最后两句非常直白，今天还常被人引用。

In English

This poem, also called “Autumn Wind Song”, has an unusual shape: two lines of three characters, two of five, then two of seven — hence “Three, Five, Seven”. The lines lengthen the way longing builds up. The first part paints an autumn night: the wind is cool and clear, the moon is bright; fallen leaves gather and scatter again in the wind, crows settle to roost and are startled up again. Neither leaves nor birds can rest — just like the person who cannot sleep for thinking of someone. Then the question: when will lovers who miss each other meet again? On a night like this, the feeling is hard to bear. The second part, often treated as an addition to the original six lines, speaks openly: once you step through the gate of longing, you learn how bitter it is; long longing means endless remembering, and even brief longing has no end. Had I known it would tie up my heart like this, it would have been better never to have met you. Those last two lines are so plain and true that people still quote them today.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Traditionally attributed to Li Bai (701–762). Early editions contain only the first six lines; the last six appear in later collections and some scholars doubt they are his. The three-five-seven pattern later influenced the song lyric (ci) form.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

聚还散 huán（还：又、再；今人也有读 hái 的，）；栖 qī；难为情 nán wéi qíng；长相思 cháng；相识 shí。机器朗读“还”已在朗读用字中替换同音字。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行的十二句文本。宋本《李太白文集》只收前六句（至“此时此夜难为情”），后六句见于后来的选本，有人认为是后人所加；网站按通行本收全。题目又作《秋风词》。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

秋风清，秋月明，

qiū fēng qīng, qiū yuè míng

Cool, clear autumn wind; bright autumn moon.

落叶聚还散，寒鸦栖复惊。

luò yè jù huán sàn, hán yā qī fù jīng

Fallen leaves drift together and blow apart; cold crows settle, then start up again.

相思相见知何日？此时此夜难为情！

xiāng sī xiāng jiàn zhī hé rì, cǐ shí cǐ yè nán wéi qíng

We miss each other — when will we meet? This night, this very hour, is more than I can bear.

入我相思门，知我相思苦，

rù wǒ xiāng sī mén, zhī wǒ xiāng sī kǔ

Step through the gate of longing, and you will know how bitter it is.

长相思兮长相忆，短相思兮无穷极。

cháng xiāng sī xī cháng xiāng yì, duǎn xiāng sī xī wú qióng jí

Long longing means endless remembering; even short longing never ends.

早知如此绊人心，何如当初莫相识。

zǎo zhī rú cǐ bàn rén xīn, hé rú dāng chū mò xiāng shí

Had I known it would tangle my heart like this, I would rather we had never met.
