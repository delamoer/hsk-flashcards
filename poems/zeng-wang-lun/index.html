导读About this poem

Li Bai is about to sail away when he hears his friend Wang Lun singing goodbye on the shore. The Peach Blossom Pool is a thousand feet deep — but not as deep as this friendship.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

李白游览泾县桃花潭的时候，当地人汪伦热情地招待了他。李白坐上船正要走，忽然听见岸上传来“踏歌”声——一边走一边用脚打拍子唱歌，原来是汪伦带着人来送行了。诗的前两句像在讲故事：一个要走，一个来送，“忽闻”写出李白没想到的惊喜。后两句把看不见的感情和看得见的潭水放在一起比：桃花潭的水有一千尺深，也比不上汪伦送我的情谊深。这种写法叫“比较”，用具体的东西衬出抽象的感情。诗人还直接把自己和朋友的名字写进诗里，读起来自然、亲切、真诚。从此汪伦这个普通人因为一首诗被记住了一千多年，“桃花潭水”也成了深厚友谊的代称。

In English

While visiting the Peach Blossom Pool in Jing County (in today’s Anhui), Li Bai was warmly hosted by a local man named Wang Lun. Li Bai had boarded a boat and was about to leave when he suddenly heard tà gē on the shore — singing while stamping out the rhythm with your feet. It was Wang Lun, come with friends to see him off. The first two lines tell the story: one man leaving, one man arriving to say goodbye, and 忽闻 (“suddenly heard”) catches Li Bai’s happy surprise. The last two lines compare something invisible with something you can see: the Peach Blossom Pool is a thousand feet deep, yet still not as deep as Wang Lun’s feeling for him. Using a concrete thing to measure an abstract feeling makes it easy to grasp. Li Bai even puts his own name and his friend’s right into the poem, which makes it sound natural, warm and sincere. Thanks to these four lines, an ordinary man has been remembered for over a thousand years, and “the water of Peach Blossom Pool” became a byword for deep friendship.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Li Bai (701–762) visited Jing County around 755. Little is known about Wang Lun; later stories say he invited Li Bai with a playful letter promising “ten li of peach blossoms” and “ten thousand wine shops” — which turned out to be a pool called Peach Blossom and an inn run by a family named Wan. The story is traditional and may not be true.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

李白乘舟将欲行，

lǐ bái chéng zhōu jiāng yù xíng

I, Li Bai, had boarded, ready to sail,

忽闻岸上踏歌声。

hū wén àn shàng tà gē shēng

when singing and stamping rose from the bank.

桃花潭水深千尺，

táo huā tán shuǐ shēn qiān chǐ

Peach Blossom Pool is a thousand feet deep,

不及汪伦送我情。

bù jí wāng lún sòng wǒ qíng

yet not as deep as Wang Lun’s farewell to me.
