导读About this poem

After twenty-three years of exile, Liu Yuxi meets his friend Bai Juyi at a banquet and answers his poem. It starts in bitterness — and then turns into one of the most hopeful couplets in Chinese: past the sunken boat, a thousand sails go by.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

公元826年，刘禹锡结束了长达二十三年的贬谪生活，回洛阳途中在扬州遇到了白居易（字乐天）。白居易在酒席上写诗送他，替他的遭遇抱不平，刘禹锡就写了这首诗回赠。前四句写心中的悲凉：我被丢在巴山楚水这些荒凉地方，整整二十三年；怀念去世的老朋友，只能空吟《思旧赋》；回到家乡，像那个看棋烂了斧柄的王质，人事全非。这里用了两个典故（古书里的故事），把伤感说得很深。五六句忽然一转：沉没的船旁边，千万只帆船照样驶过；病树前面，万棵树木照样迎来春天。诗人把自己比作“沉舟”“病树”，却不悲观——个人的不幸挡不住世界向前，新事物总会蓬勃生长。最后两句说：今天听你唱了这首歌，就借这杯酒振作起精神吧。“沉舟侧畔千帆过，病树前头万木春”今天常用来说新事物必然取代旧事物。

In English

In 826 Liu Yuxi’s exile finally ended after twenty-three years. On his way north he met the poet Bai Juyi (courtesy name Letian) in Yangzhou. At a banquet Bai wrote a poem for him, lamenting how unfairly he had been treated, and Liu replied with this one. The first four lines are full of grief: I was thrown away in the wild lands of Ba and Chu for twenty-three years; remembering friends who have died, all I can do is recite an old lament for the departed; coming home, I am like the woodcutter in the legend who watched a game of chess in the mountains and found his axe handle rotted away — everything has changed. These two allusions (references to old stories) make the sadness run deep. Then the fifth and sixth lines turn sharply: beside the sunken boat, a thousand sails sail past; in front of the sick tree, ten thousand trees burst into spring. Liu calls himself the sunken boat and the sick tree — yet he isn’t bitter. One person’s misfortune cannot stop the world moving on, and new life will always flourish. The last couplet: hearing your song today, let this cup of wine lift my spirits. The famous couplet is still quoted today to say that the new will always replace the old.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Liu Yuxi (772–842) was exiled in 805 after a failed reform movement and spent most of the next twenty-three years in remote southern posts. He wrote this in 826, when he met Bai Juyi (772–846) in Yangzhou; the two became close friends and poetic partners for the rest of their lives.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

“乐天”的“乐”读 lè；“弃置”的“置”读 zhì；“烂柯”的“柯”读 kē；“侧畔”读 cè pàn；“千帆过”的“过”读 guò；“长精神”的“长”读 zhǎng（增长）。“翻似”的“翻”是“反而”的意思，读 fān。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

巴山楚水凄凉地，

bā shān chǔ shuǐ qī liáng dì

In the bleak hills and rivers of the far south

二十三年弃置身。

èr shí sān nián qì zhì shēn

I lay discarded for twenty-three years.

怀旧空吟闻笛赋，

huái jiù kōng yín wén dí fù

Mourning old friends, I can only recite a lament;

到乡翻似烂柯人。

dào xiāng fān sì làn kē rén

home again, I’m a stranger, as if a hundred years had passed.

沉舟侧畔千帆过，

chén zhōu cè pàn qiān fān guò

Past the sunken boat a thousand sails go by;

病树前头万木春。

bìng shù qián tóu wàn mù chūn

beyond the sick tree, a whole forest greens in spring.

今日听君歌一曲，

jīn rì tīng jūn gē yī qǔ

Today I’ve heard your song, my friend —

暂凭杯酒长精神。

zàn píng bēi jiǔ zhǎng jīng shén

let this cup of wine put heart in me again.
