导读About this poem

Why love chrysanthemums? Not because they are the loveliest flower, the poet says — but because once they have finished blooming, there are no flowers left at all.

中文解析

这是一首咏菊的小诗。第一句说：屋子四周开满了秋菊，好像到了陶渊明的家——东晋诗人陶渊明最爱菊花，写过“采菊东篱下”，从此菊花和他分不开。第二句写诗人绕着篱笆一圈一圈地看，不知不觉太阳都快落山了，看得入迷。后两句说出爱菊的理由，很特别：不是我在所有花里偏爱菊花，而是菊花开完以后，一年里就再也没有花了。菊花在深秋开放，是一年中最后的花，所以格外值得珍惜。诗人没有直接夸菊花美，而是从“最后”这个角度说，既新奇又有道理。很多人也从中读出菊花不怕寒冷、开到最后的品格。

In English

This short poem is in praise of chrysanthemums. Line one: autumn clumps of them circle the house, as if this were the home of Tao Yuanming — the 4th–5th-century poet who loved chrysanthemums and wrote “I pick chrysanthemums by the eastern fence”; ever since, the flower has been tied to his name. Line two: the poet walks round and round the hedge looking at them until, without noticing, the sun is sinking — he is completely absorbed. The last two lines give an unusual reason for his love: it is not that he favours the chrysanthemum above all flowers, but that once it has finished blooming, there are no more flowers for the rest of the year. Chrysanthemums open in late autumn, the last bloom of the year, so they are all the more precious. Instead of simply calling them beautiful, he praises them for being last — fresh and convincing at once. Many readers also see in it the flower’s strength: braving the cold and blooming to the very end.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Yuan Zhen (779–831) was a Tang official and poet, close friend of Bai Juyi, with whom he championed plain, accessible verse. The date of this poem is unknown.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

秋丛绕舍似陶家，

qiū cóng rào shè sì táo jiā

Autumn blooms ring my cottage — it could be Tao Yuanming’s home;

遍绕篱边日渐斜。

biàn rào lí biān rì jiàn xié

I circle the fence again and again till the sun hangs low.

不是花中偏爱菊，

bù shì huā zhōng piān ài jú

It isn’t that I love this flower more than all the rest —

此花开尽更无花。

cǐ huā kāi jìn gèng wú huā

but when it is done blooming, there are no flowers left.
