The very first poem of the Book of Songs, sung around 3,000 years ago: a young man hears water birds calling to each other, falls for a girl, lies awake longing for her — and dreams of winning her with music.

直译Literal“Guan, guan” cry the ospreys, on the islet in the river.

意译Meaning“Guan, guan,” the ospreys call on a sandbar in the river.

02
窈窕淑女，君子好逑。

直译LiteralThe graceful, good young lady — a fine match for the gentleman.

意译MeaningThat gentle, lovely girl is the match a good man hopes for.

03

直译LiteralLong and short grow the floating-hearts; left and right we pick them from the stream.

意译MeaningLong and short, the water plants drift as hands reach for them, left and right.

04
窈窕淑女，寤寐求之。

直译LiteralThe graceful, good young lady — awake and asleep, he seeks her.

意译MeaningThat gentle, lovely girl — awake or asleep, he goes looking for her.

第三章Stanza 3
05

直译LiteralSeeking her, he cannot win her; awake and asleep, he longs for her.

意译MeaningHe seeks her and cannot win her; awake or asleep, she is on his mind.

06
悠哉悠哉，辗转反侧。

直译LiteralEndless, endless — he turns over, from one side to the other.

意译MeaningOn and on the longing goes; all night he tosses and turns.

第四章Stanza 4
07
参差荇菜，左右采之。

直译LiteralLong and short grow the floating-hearts; left and right we gather them.

意译MeaningLong and short, the water plants are gathered, left and right.

08
窈窕淑女，琴瑟友之。

直译LiteralThe graceful, good young lady — with zither and lute he befriends her.

意译MeaningThat gentle, lovely girl — he would play the zither and lute to win her heart.

09
参差荇菜，左右芼之。

直译LiteralLong and short grow the floating-hearts; left and right we pick out the best.

意译MeaningLong and short, the water plants are chosen, left and right.

10
窈窕淑女，钟鼓乐之。

直译LiteralThe graceful, good young lady — with bells and drums he delights her.

意译MeaningThat gentle, lovely girl — he would bring bells and drums to make her happy.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

《关雎》是《诗经》的第一篇，也是中国现存最早的爱情诗之一，大约两三千年前在黄河一带流传，当时是可以唱的歌。诗从河中小洲上互相鸣叫的雎鸠写起，引出对一位美好姑娘的爱慕——先写眼前的景物，再引出想说的事，这种手法叫“起兴”，是《诗经》最常用的写法。中间写追求不到时“辗转反侧”、整夜睡不着，非常真实；最后想象用琴瑟和钟鼓去亲近她、迎娶她，热闹而圆满。全诗反复出现“参差荇菜，左右……之”，每次只换一个动词（流、采、芼），这是民歌“重章叠句”的特点，方便唱，也方便记。孔子评价它“乐而不淫，哀而不伤”——快乐不过分，忧愁不伤人。

In English

This is the first poem in the Book of Songs (Shījīng), China’s oldest poetry collection, and one of its earliest love poems — a song people sang along the Yellow River some 3,000 years ago. It opens with a pair of ospreys calling to each other on a sandbar, and that image leads into the young man’s feelings for a girl. Starting from a scene in front of you and moving to what you really want to say is called xìng (“evoking”), the Book of Songs’ signature move. The middle is touchingly real: he can’t win her, so he tosses and turns all night. The end imagines winning her with zithers, bells and drums — the music of a wedding. Notice the refrain 参差荇菜，左右…之 coming back with only one verb changed each time: repetition like this made folk songs easy to sing and remember. Confucius praised the poem as “joyful without excess, sad without being hurtful”.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

The Book of Songs gathers 305 songs from roughly the 11th to the 6th century BC. Most authors are unknown. This one belongs to the “Airs of Zhounan” (周南), folk songs from the lands south of the Zhou capital.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

以下按统编教材注音：好逑 hǎo qiú（不读 hào）；钟鼓乐之 lè（旧注有 yào、yuè）。生僻字：雎鸠 jū jiū、荇 xìng、芼 mào、寤寐 wù mèi。多音字：参差 cēn cī（不是 cān chà）、辗转 zhǎn zhuǎn。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

关关雎鸠，在河之洲。

guān guān jū jiū, zài hé zhī zhōu

“Guan, guan,” the ospreys call on a sandbar in the river.

窈窕淑女，君子好逑。

yǎo tiǎo shū nǚ, jūn zǐ hǎo qiú

That gentle, lovely girl is the match a good man hopes for.

参差荇菜，左右流之。

cēn cī xìng cài, zuǒ yòu liú zhī

Long and short, the water plants drift as hands reach for them, left and right.

窈窕淑女，寤寐求之。

yǎo tiǎo shū nǚ, wù mèi qiú zhī

That gentle, lovely girl — awake or asleep, he goes looking for her.

求之不得，寤寐思服。

qiú zhī bù dé, wù mèi sī fú

He seeks her and cannot win her; awake or asleep, she is on his mind.

悠哉悠哉，辗转反侧。

yōu zāi yōu zāi, zhǎn zhuǎn fǎn cè

On and on the longing goes; all night he tosses and turns.

参差荇菜，左右采之。

cēn cī xìng cài, zuǒ yòu cǎi zhī

Long and short, the water plants are gathered, left and right.

窈窕淑女，琴瑟友之。

yǎo tiǎo shū nǚ, qín sè yǒu zhī

That gentle, lovely girl — he would play the zither and lute to win her heart.

参差荇菜，左右芼之。

cēn cī xìng cài, zuǒ yòu mào zhī

Long and short, the water plants are chosen, left and right.

窈窕淑女，钟鼓乐之。

yǎo tiǎo shū nǚ, zhōng gǔ lè zhī

That gentle, lovely girl — he would bring bells and drums to make her happy.
