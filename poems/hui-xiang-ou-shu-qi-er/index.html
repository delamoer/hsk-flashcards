导读About this poem

After fifty years away, an old man comes home and finds that almost everything has changed — except the lake by his door, still rippling in the spring wind as it always did.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

贺知章年轻时离开家乡越州（今天浙江绍兴一带），八十多岁才告老回乡。《回乡偶书》一共两首，第一首“少小离家老大回”很多人都会背，这是第二首。前两句说：离开家乡的时间太长了，近年来家乡的人和事已经变了一大半——老朋友有的去世了，有的搬走了。“消磨”两个字很平静，却让人感到时间的无情。后两句笔锋一转：只有门前的镜湖水，春风吹过时，泛起的波浪还和从前一模一样。用不变的湖水来对比变化的人事，这种写法叫“对比”或“反衬”：湖水越是不变，人事的变化越让人感慨。诗人没有说一句伤心话，可是“惟有”两个字里，藏着很深的怅惘。

In English

He Zhizhang left his hometown in Yuezhou (near today’s Shaoxing, Zhejiang) as a young man and only came back in his eighties. He wrote two poems about returning; the first, “I left home young and came back old”, is one of the best-known Tang poems. This is the second. The first two lines are quiet and plain: he has been away for many years, and lately half of the people and things he knew at home have “worn away” (消磨) — old friends have died or moved on. The verb sounds gentle, but it carries the slow, unstoppable work of time. Then the poem turns. Only one thing is the same: Mirror Lake (镜湖) in front of his gate. When the spring wind blows, it still makes the same ripples it made long ago. Setting something that never changes beside things that have changed is a technique called contrast. The more the lake stays the same, the more we feel how much the human world has moved on. The poet never says he is sad; the words 惟有 (“only … remains”) carry all of that feeling. It is a gentle, wise poem about getting old and coming home.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

He Zhizhang (659–744) was a senior official in Chang’an, famous for his calligraphy, his love of wine and his friendship with the young Li Bai. In 744, aged about 85, he retired and went home to Yuezhou, where he wrote these poems and died the same year.

版本说明 About the text

采用通行文本（《全唐诗》卷一百十二），共两首，此为第二首。“惟有”一作“唯有”，“消磨”一作“销磨”（维基文库所录《全唐诗》本作“唯有”“销磨”），意思相同。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

离别家乡岁月多，

lí bié jiā xiāng suì yuè duō

So many years since I left my home,

近来人事半消磨。

jìn lái rén shì bàn xiāo mó

and half of what I knew has worn away.

惟有门前镜湖水，

wéi yǒu mén qián jìng hú shuǐ

Only Mirror Lake before my door

春风不改旧时波。

chūn fēng bù gǎi jiù shí bō

still ripples in the spring wind just as it used to.
