导读About this poem

A serious philosopher takes a stroll on a mild spring day and feels quietly happy — and jokes that people will think he is just a middle-aged man playing truant like a schoolboy.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗是《千家诗》的第一首，很多中国孩子从它开始读古诗。前两句写春天中午的好天气：云很淡，风很轻，诗人靠着花、跟着柳树，走过前面的小河。“傍”和“随”两个动词，写出他悠闲散步、被春景吸引的样子。后两句写心情：路上的人不懂我心里的快乐，还以为我是在偷懒，学年轻人出来玩呢。作者程颢是有名的理学家（研究“理”的哲学家），平时很严肃。他的快乐不只是看风景，而是在自然中体会到一种自在和谐的心境。诗里有一点自嘲的幽默：大学者也有像孩子一样贪玩的时候。语言平易，像随口说出来的，题目“偶成”就是“偶然写成”的意思。

In English

This poem opens the Poems by a Thousand Masters (Qiānjiā Shī), an old children’s primer, so for generations it was the first classical poem many Chinese children read. The first two lines describe a perfect spring noon: thin clouds, a light breeze, and the poet strolling beside the flowers, following the willows, across the stream ahead. The two verbs 傍 (“keeping close to”) and 随 (“following”) show him wandering at leisure, drawn along by the scenery. The last two lines turn to his feelings: people he passes don’t understand the joy in his heart — they probably think he’s skiving off, trying to act young again. Cheng Hao was a famous Neo-Confucian philosopher, known as a serious thinker. His happiness is not just about pretty views; it is the calm, natural joy of feeling in tune with the world. There is a touch of self-mocking humour too: even a great scholar can feel like a boy skipping school. The language is plain, almost offhand — the title 偶成 means “written on the spur of the moment”.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Cheng Hao (1032–1085) was a Northern Song philosopher who, with his younger brother Cheng Yi, laid the foundations of Neo-Confucianism. He is said to have written this while serving as a local official.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

傍花：傍 bàng（靠近）。过前川：过 guò。余心乐：乐 lè。将谓：将 jiāng。少年：少 shào。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行教材版本（《千家诗》卷首）。“余心乐”一作“予心乐”（余、予都是“我”）；“时人”有本作“旁人”。网站用教材版。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

云淡风轻近午天，

yún dàn fēng qīng jìn wǔ tiān

Thin clouds, a soft breeze, nearly noon —

傍花随柳过前川。

bàng huā suí liǔ guò qián chuān

I stroll by the flowers, follow the willows across the stream.

时人不识余心乐，

shí rén bù shí yú xīn lè

Passers-by can’t see the happiness inside me —

将谓偷闲学少年。

jiāng wèi tōu xián xué shào nián

they’ll think I’m playing truant, pretending to be young.
