乡村四月

Xiāng Cūn Sì Yuè
The Village in the Fourth Month

导读About this poem

Green hills, white flooded paddies, the cuckoo calling through misty rain — and not one idle person in the village, because the silkworms are done and the rice is waiting to be planted.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗写江南农村初夏四月（农历）的景色和农忙。前两句写景：山坡和原野一片绿，河里、田里的水白茫茫的，映着天光；杜鹃鸟（子规）在叫，细雨像烟一样笼罩着一切。一句写颜色——绿和白；一句写声音和天气——鸟叫和烟雨，画面清新又有点朦胧。后两句写人：四月的乡村里没有闲人，刚忙完养蚕采桑，又要去田里插秧。“才……又……”写出农活一件接一件，农民非常忙碌。前两句是安静美丽的风景，后两句是紧张忙碌的劳动，一静一动，配合得很好。诗人没有直接夸农民勤劳，可是“闲人少”三个字已经说明了一切。这首诗像一幅江南水乡的农忙图，被选入小学课本。

In English

This poem shows a village south of the Yangtze in the fourth lunar month, at the start of summer, when farm work is at its busiest. The first two lines are scenery. The hills and plains are green all over, and the rivers and flooded paddy fields shine white with reflected sky. The cuckoo is calling, and a fine rain hangs over everything like smoke. One line gives colour — green and white; the other gives sound and weather — birdsong and misty rain. The picture is fresh and a little hazy. The last two lines turn to people. In the fourth month hardly anyone in the village is idle: they have only just finished with the silkworms and the mulberry leaves, and now it is time to plant out the rice seedlings. The pattern 才…又… (“no sooner… than…”) shows one job following straight after another. So the poem moves from stillness to activity: calm, beautiful scenery first, then busy work. The poet never praises the farmers for working hard, yet the three characters 闲人少, “few idle people”, say it all. It is a small picture of a water-country village in its busiest season, and Chinese children learn it at school.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Weng Juan was a Southern Song poet from Yongjia (today’s Wenzhou, Zhejiang); his dates are not known. He was one of the “Four Spirits of Yongjia”, a group who wrote short, fresh poems about everyday scenes, and this is his best-known work.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

山原 shān yuán；子规 zǐ guī；闲人少 shǎo；才了 cái liǎo（“了”是完毕的意思，读 liǎo，不读 le）；蚕桑 cán sāng。机器朗读“了”易读成 le，已在“朗读用字”列替换同音字。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

绿遍山原白满川，

lǜ biàn shān yuán bái mǎn chuān

Green over every hill and field, white water filling the streams,

子规声里雨如烟。

zǐ guī shēng lǐ yǔ rú yān

the cuckoo calling through a rain as fine as smoke.

乡村四月闲人少，

xiāng cūn sì yuè xián rén shǎo

In the village in the fourth month, no one is idle:

才了蚕桑又插田。

cái liǎo cán sāng yòu chā tián

the silkworms are barely done, and it’s time to plant the rice.
