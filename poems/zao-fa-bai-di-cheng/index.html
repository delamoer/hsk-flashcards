导读About this poem

Pardoned on his way into exile, Li Bai rides a light boat down the Yangtze Gorges — a thousand li in a single day. Four lines that move as fast as the river.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗写李白坐船从白帝城出发，顺着长江穿过三峡，一天就回到了千里之外的江陵。第一句“彩云间”写白帝城在高山上，早晨被彩色的云霞围着，又高又美。第二句“千里”和“一日”放在一起对比，写出船速飞快。第三句写两岸猿猴的叫声一声接一声，还没停下来，第四句船已经穿过了万重高山——声音还在耳边，人已经很远了，读起来就像坐在船上一样快。这首诗特别轻快，是因为李白当时被流放到夜郎，走到白帝城时忽然接到赦免的消息，心情一下子轻松了。所以诗里的“轻舟”，也是诗人轻松的心。

In English

Li Bai sets off at dawn from Baidi City, perched high above the Yangtze at the head of the Three Gorges, and sails downstream to Jiangling — about a thousand li (roughly 500 km) away — in a single day. The first line places Baidi “among coloured clouds”: the town sits so high that the morning clouds surround it, glowing in the sunrise. The second line sets “a thousand li” against “one day”, and that contrast gives you the speed. The third and fourth lines are the fun part: on both banks the gibbons keep calling, one cry after another, and before their calls have even stopped, the light boat has already shot past ten thousand mountains. Sound lingers, but the boat is faster than the sound — you can feel the current pulling you along. Why so joyful? Li Bai had been sentenced to exile in the far southwest. When he reached Baidi, news came that he was pardoned, and he turned straight back east. So the “light boat” is also his light heart. Students should notice 还: here it means “to return” and is read huán, not hái. The poem is also known by the title “Going Down to Jiangling” (下江陵).

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Li Bai (701–762) had joined the staff of Prince Yong during the An Lushan Rebellion; when the prince was defeated, Li Bai was condemned to exile in Yelang. In 759 he was pardoned at Baidi City, and this poem is traditionally read as his reaction to the news.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

“千里江陵一日还”的“还”读 huán（返回），不读 hái。“朝辞”的“朝”读 zhāo（早晨），不读 cháo。“万重山”的“重”读 chóng（层层），不读 zhòng。

版本说明 About the text

采用统编小学语文教材版本，题目作《早发白帝城》，一作《下江陵》。异文：“两岸猿声啼不住”一作“啼不尽”，网站不用。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

朝辞白帝彩云间，

zhāo cí bái dì cǎi yún jiān

At dawn I leave Baidi, high in the rosy clouds,

千里江陵一日还。

qiān lǐ jiāng líng yī rì huán

and reach Jiangling, a thousand li away, before the day is done.

两岸猿声啼不住，

liǎng àn yuán shēng tí bù zhù

From both banks the gibbons call and call —

轻舟已过万重山。

qīng zhōu yǐ guò wàn chóng shān

and my light boat has already slipped past ten thousand hills.
