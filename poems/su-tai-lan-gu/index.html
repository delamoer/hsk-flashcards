导读About this poem

Li Bai visits the ruins of a famous royal pleasure terrace near Suzhou. The willows are green and girls are singing, but the king and his palace are long gone — only the moon is the same one that once shone on them.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

姑苏台在今天的苏州，是春秋时吴王修建的游乐宫台，吴王夫差曾在这里和美女西施饮酒作乐，后来吴国被越国灭掉，宫台也就荒废了。诗人来到这里：旧日的园林、荒废的高台上，杨柳又长出了新叶；采菱姑娘清亮的歌声传来，春色浓得让人受不了。“旧”“荒”和“新”放在一句里，形成强烈的对比：人间的繁华早已消失，自然却年年照样新鲜。后两句更深一层：如今只剩下西江上的月亮，它曾经照过吴王宫里的人。月亮是唯一的“见证人”，古今都在它眼底下。诗人不发议论，只用景物写出“兴亡无常”的感慨，这就是怀古诗常用的“以景抒情”。

In English

The Gusu Terrace near today’s Suzhou was a pleasure palace of the ancient kings of Wu. King Fuchai is said to have feasted there with the famous beauty Xi Shi — until the rival state of Yue destroyed Wu and the terrace fell to ruin. Li Bai walks through what is left. On the old gardens and the crumbling terrace the willows have put out fresh leaves, and the clear songs of girls picking water chestnuts drift over, almost too full of spring to bear. One line holds old, ruined and new side by side: human splendour has vanished, but nature comes back fresh every year. The second half goes deeper. “Now there is only the moon over the western river — the same moon that once shone on the people in the King of Wu’s palace.” The moon is the one witness left, and past and present both lie under its light. Li Bai makes no speech about the rise and fall of kingdoms; he lets the scenery carry the feeling. This is the classic method of poems on history (huáigǔ shī): look at the place, and let it speak.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Li Bai (701–762) travelled widely in the Wu and Yue regions (today’s Jiangsu and Zhejiang) as a young man. This poem and its companion “Remembering the Past in Yue” are usually dated to those travels, around 726.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

旧苑荒台杨柳新，

jiù yuàn huāng tái yáng liǔ xīn

Old gardens, a crumbling terrace, and the willows freshly green;

菱歌清唱不胜春。

líng gē qīng chàng bù shèng chūn

girls sing as they pick water chestnuts, their clear voices brimming with spring.

只今惟有西江月，

zhǐ jīn wéi yǒu xī jiāng yuè

Now there is only the moon above the western river —

曾照吴王宫里人。

céng zhào wú wáng gōng lǐ rén

the moon that once shone on the ladies of the King of Wu.
