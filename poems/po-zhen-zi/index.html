导读About this poem

A drunk old soldier pulls out his sword by lamplight and dreams himself back in an army camp — horns, roasting meat, galloping horses, twanging bows. Then the dream ends with one bitter line.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首词是辛弃疾写给好朋友陈亮（字同甫）的。两人都主张收复被金国占领的北方，却都得不到朝廷重用。前九句一气呵成，写一个“梦”：喝醉了挑亮油灯看宝剑，梦里回到军营，号角声连成一片；把烤牛肉分给部下，乐器奏起边塞的雄壮曲子，秋天在战场上检阅军队。下阕写打仗：战马像名马“的卢”一样飞奔，弓弦像霹雳一样响。打完胜仗，替君王完成收复天下的大事，也给自己赢得生前死后的名声。这里越写越热烈，最后却只有五个字：“可怜白发生！”——一下子从梦里跌回现实：人已经老了，头发白了，理想一件也没实现。前面写得越壮，结尾越悲，这种强烈的对比就是这首词最打动人的地方。题目说是“壮词”，其实壮中有悲。

In English

Xin Qiji wrote this for his close friend Chen Liang (Chen Tongfu). Both men longed to win back northern China from the Jin, and both were ignored by the court. Nine of the ten lines are one rush of energy. Drunk, he trims the lamp to look at his sword, and in a dream he is back among the camps, where army horns sound from tent to tent. Roast beef is shared out among the troops, the fifty-stringed zither plays tunes from the frontier, and in autumn he reviews his soldiers on the battlefield. In the second half the battle comes: horses as fast as Dilu, the famous steed of the Three Kingdoms, bows snapping like thunder. He finishes the king’s great task of reuniting the land and wins a name that will last after death. Then the last line drops like a stone — just five characters: “Pity the white hair growing!” The dream is over; he is an old man and nothing has been done. The title promises a “heroic lyric”, and it is heroic, but the louder the dream, the sadder the waking. That sudden contrast is why readers remember it.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Xin Qiji (1140–1207) led troops against the Jin as a young man before coming south, but spent much of his later life out of office. His friend Chen Liang visited him in 1188 and the two talked for days about the war; this lyric is usually dated to around that meeting.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

醉里挑灯看剑，

zuì lǐ tiǎo dēng kàn jiàn

Drunk, I trim the lamp to look at my sword,

梦回吹角连营。

mèng huí chuī jiǎo lián yíng

and dream I’m back where horns call from camp to camp.

八百里分麾下炙，

bā bǎi lǐ fēn huī xià zhì

Roast beef is handed round to the men under my flag,

五十弦翻塞外声，

wǔ shí xián fān sài wài shēng

the fifty-stringed zither plays the songs of the frontier —

沙场秋点兵。

shā chǎng qiū diǎn bīng

autumn on the battlefield, and I review the troops.

马作的卢飞快，

mǎ zuò dí lú fēi kuài

Our horses fly like the famous Dilu,

弓如霹雳弦惊。

gōng rú pī lì xián jīng

our bowstrings crack like thunder.

了却君王天下事，

liǎo què jūn wáng tiān xià shì

I finish the king’s great task of winning back the land

赢得生前身后名。

yíng dé shēng qián shēn hòu míng

and earn a name that lives on after me —

可怜白发生！

kě lián bái fà shēng

but look: my hair has turned white.
