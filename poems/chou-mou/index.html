导读About this poem

A wedding-night song from about 2,700 years ago: firewood tied in bundles, three stars in the sky, and a playful, amazed question — “What night is tonight, that I get to see you?”

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这是一首新婚之夜的歌。古代婚礼常在黄昏、夜里举行，诗里“束薪”“束刍”“束楚”（捆紧的柴草）是婚礼上用的东西，也暗示两个人从此紧紧连在一起。“三星”从“在天”到“在隅”（东南角）再到“在户”（门口），星星慢慢移动，说明夜越来越深。诗中人惊喜地问：“今夕何夕？”——今天到底是什么好日子，让我见到这样好的人！最后一句“子兮子兮，如此良人何”像是自言自语，又像对新人开玩笑：哎呀呀，这么好的人，叫我怎么办才好？三章重章叠句，只换几个字，热闹又有点害羞。“今夕何夕”后来成了常用语，表示美好得不敢相信的时刻。成语“未雨绸缪”里的“绸缪”也来自《诗经》，意思是“缠绕、捆紧”。

In English

This is a song for a wedding night. In ancient China weddings were held at dusk, and the bundles of firewood and grass tied tight (束薪, 束刍, 束楚) were part of the ceremony — and a quiet image of two people now bound together. The “three stars” move across the three verses: first in the sky, then in the corner of the sky, then right over the doorway. Without saying so, the poem tells us the night is getting late. The speaker can hardly believe their luck: “What night is tonight, that I get to see this good person?” The last line of each verse, “Oh you, oh you, what shall I do with someone this good?”, could be the bride or groom talking to themselves, or friends teasing the couple — the poem leaves it open, and that playfulness is part of the fun. As in many Book of Songs pieces, each verse repeats the one before with only a few words changed. The phrase 今夕何夕 is still used today for a moment too wonderful to believe, and 绸缪 survives in the idiom 未雨绸缪, “to tie up the windows before it rains” — to prepare in advance.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

The Book of Songs gathers 305 songs from roughly the 11th to the 6th century BC. This one is from the “Airs of Tang” (唐风), folk songs of the state of Jin in today’s Shanxi; its author is unknown.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

绸缪束薪，三星在天。

chóu móu shù xīn, sān xīng zài tiān

The firewood is tied up tight; three stars have risen in the sky.

今夕何夕，见此良人？

jīn xī hé xī, jiàn cǐ liáng rén

What night is this, that I should meet someone so good?

子兮子兮，如此良人何？

zǐ xī zǐ xī, rú cǐ liáng rén hé

Oh you, oh you — what shall I do with someone so good?

绸缪束刍，三星在隅。

chóu móu shù chú, sān xīng zài yú

The hay is tied up tight; three stars hang low in the corner of the sky.

今夕何夕，见此邂逅？

jīn xī hé xī, jiàn cǐ xiè hòu

What night is this, that brings us together?

子兮子兮，如此邂逅何？

zǐ xī zǐ xī, rú cǐ xiè hòu hé

Oh you, oh you — what shall I do with a night like this?

绸缪束楚，三星在户。

chóu móu shù chǔ, sān xīng zài hù

The twigs are tied up tight; three stars shine over the door.

今夕何夕，见此粲者？

jīn xī hé xī, jiàn cǐ càn zhě

What night is this, that I should see someone so radiant?

子兮子兮，如此粲者何？

zǐ xī zǐ xī, rú cǐ càn zhě hé

Oh you, oh you — what shall I do with someone so lovely?
