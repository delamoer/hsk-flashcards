导读About this poem

A man dreams his way back to the house of the woman he loved — the winding corridor, the slanting railings — and wakes to find only the spring moon still shining for him on fallen petals.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗是写给从前的恋人的。第一句说：分别以后，我在梦里依依不舍地又来到了你家（“谢家”是唐诗里对女子家的雅称）。第二句写梦里看到的地方：小小的走廊弯弯曲曲，栏杆曲折倾斜——这些都是当年他们见面的地方，记得清清楚楚。可是梦里没有见到她。后两句说：最多情的只有春天庭院里的月亮，它还在为我这个离别的人照着满地落花。月亮当然不会有感情，诗人这样写，其实是在说：人走了，只有月亮还记得我们。也带着一点埋怨——她反而不如月亮多情。落花又暗示青春和爱情都已经过去。

In English

This poem is addressed to a woman the poet once loved. “Since we parted, my lingering dream has carried me back to your house” — 谢家, “the Xie family”, was a common Tang way of naming a beloved woman’s home. The second line is what he sees in the dream: the little corridor turning back on itself, the winding railings slanting away, every detail remembered from the places where they used to meet. But she is not there. The last two lines turn to the moon: only the moon over the spring courtyard is truly faithful — it is still shining for me, the one who left, on the fallen petals. A moon cannot feel anything, of course. By saying it does, the poet tells us that he has been forgotten by everyone but the moon, and there is a gentle reproach in that too: she seems to care less than the moon does. The fallen flowers quietly add that the spring of their love is over. The poem is dreamlike and delicate, and every image carries his longing without his ever saying it.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Zhang Bi was a poet of the late Tang and Five Dynasties period (10th century). Tradition says he wrote this to a girl he had loved when young and had been kept from marrying; little about his life is certain.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

别梦依依到谢家，

bié mèng yī yī dào xiè jiā

Since we parted, my dreams drift back to your house,

小廊回合曲阑斜。

xiǎo láng huí hé qū lán xié

the little corridor turning, the railings winding away.

多情只有春庭月，

duō qíng zhǐ yǒu chūn tíng yuè

Only the moon over the spring courtyard remembers —

犹为离人照落花。

yóu wèi lí rén zhào luò huā

it still shines on the fallen petals for me.
