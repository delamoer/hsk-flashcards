导读About this poem

Plant one seed in spring, harvest ten thousand in autumn. Every field in the land is farmed — so why do farmers still starve? Twenty simple characters that ask a hard question.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗用最简单的话，提出了一个尖锐的问题。前两句写农民的劳动成果：春天种下一粒种子，秋天能收获成千上万颗粮食。“一粒”和“万颗”对比，写出土地的丰饶和农民的辛勤。第三句更进一步：普天之下，没有一块闲着的田地，所有土地都种上了庄稼。按理说，粮食这么多，人人都应该吃得饱。可最后一句突然一转：种地的农民，竟然还会饿死！前三句越写丰收，最后一句就越让人震惊。诗人没有直接说是谁造成的，但读者自然会想：粮食都到哪里去了？这就是诗的力量——用对比让人自己去思考社会的不公。它和《悯农》第二首（“锄禾日当午”）一起，成了中国孩子最早背的诗之一。

In English

In the plainest language, this poem asks a sharp question. The first two lines show what farmers produce: plant one grain in spring, and in autumn you harvest ten thousand. “One grain” against “ten thousand” shows how rich the land is and how hard the farmers work. The third line goes further: across the whole land, not a single field lies idle — every scrap of earth is planted. With so much grain, surely everyone should have enough to eat. Then the last line turns on us: the farmers who grow it still starve to death! The more the first three lines celebrate the harvest, the more shocking the ending becomes. The poet doesn’t say who is to blame, but the reader can’t help asking: where did all the grain go? (The answer, in Tang times, was heavy taxes and rents.) That is the power of the poem — it uses contrast to make us think about injustice for ourselves. Together with its companion piece, “Hoeing grain at noon”, it is one of the first poems Chinese children learn by heart.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Li Shen (772–846) was a Tang poet and official who later rose to be chief minister. He is said to have written his two “Pity the Farmers” poems as a young man, and they made his name.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

“春种”的“种”读 zhòng（种植），不读 zhǒng；“粟”读 sù；“颗子”的“子”读 zǐ（种子、粮食粒）。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

春种一粒粟，

chūn zhòng yī lì sù

Sow a single seed in spring,

秋收万颗子。

qiū shōu wàn kē zǐ

reap ten thousand in the fall.

四海无闲田，

sì hǎi wú xián tián

No field in all the land lies bare —

农夫犹饿死。

nóng fū yóu è sǐ

and still the farmers starve.
