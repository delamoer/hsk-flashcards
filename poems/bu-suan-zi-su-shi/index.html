导读About this poem

Fresh from prison and newly exiled, Su Shi walks alone under a broken moon and sees a lone wild goose that will not settle on any cold branch. One of the loneliest poems in Chinese — and one of the proudest.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首词是苏轼刚到黄州、住在定慧院时写的。上阕写夜景：一弯残缺的月亮挂在稀疏的梧桐树上，漏壶的水滴完了，夜深人静。谁看见一个幽居的人独自走来走去？只有那隐隐约约的孤雁的影子。“幽人”是诗人自己，“孤鸿”也是诗人自己——人和雁在月下合成了一个孤独的形象。下阕专写孤鸿：它突然受惊飞起，又回过头来，心里有怨恨却没有人理解；它把寒冷的树枝都挑遍了，也不肯落在上面，宁愿待在寂寞冰冷的沙洲上。这里用了托物言志的手法——借写一样东西来表达自己的志向：孤鸿受了惊、无人理解，还不肯随便栖身，正像苏轼经历“乌台诗案”后的处境——心有余悸，孤独无助，却依然清高，不愿随波逐流。

In English

Su Shi wrote this soon after arriving in Huangzhou, while staying at Dinghui Temple. The first half sets a night scene: a broken moon hangs in a bare wutong tree; the water clock has run dry, and the world is asleep. Who sees the recluse pacing back and forth alone? Only the faint shadow of a lone wild goose. The “recluse” is the poet, and the goose is the poet too — under the moon, man and bird blur into a single lonely figure. The second half is all about the goose. Startled, it takes off, then turns its head back; it carries a hurt that no one understands. It tries every cold branch and refuses to perch on any, settling instead on a lonely, freezing sandbar. This technique is called tuō wù yán zhì — speaking of your own ideals through an object. The goose, frightened and misunderstood but still too proud to roost just anywhere, is Su Shi after the Crow Terrace trial: shaken, isolated, and yet unwilling to bend to fit in. The word 省 (“to understand”) is pronounced xǐng here — a good one to remember.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Su Shi (1037–1101) was jailed for over four months in 1079 for poems said to criticize the government, and only narrowly escaped death. Sent to Huangzhou in 1080, he lodged at first in Dinghui Temple, where this was written, probably in 1080–1082.

缺月挂疏桐，漏断人初静。

惊起却回头，有恨无人省。

jīng qǐ què huí tóu, yǒu hèn wú rén xǐng

Startled, it rises, then looks back — its hurt understood by no one.

jiǎn jìn hán zhī bù kěn qī, jì mò shā zhōu lěng

It tries every cold branch and will settle on none — the sandbar is lonely and cold.
