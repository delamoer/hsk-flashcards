导读About this poem

The song that opens Romance of the Three Kingdoms: the Yangtze rolls east and washes away every hero, while an old fisherman and woodcutter laugh about it all over a jug of wine. Many Chinese know it as the theme song of the 1994 TV series.

这首诗被谱成了歌Sung as a song

杨洪基《滚滚长江东逝水》Yang Hongji

The theme song of the 1994 TV series Romance of the Three Kingdoms. Its lyric is 临江仙 by the Ming poet Yang Shen, the poem the novel opens with. Here Yang Hongji sings it live at a CCTV gala.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

上阕从滚滚东流的长江写起：江水一去不回，就像历史上的英雄一个个被浪花“淘”走，谁对谁错、谁成谁败，一转头就都空了。只有青山还在，夕阳还一次次变红——人事变化，自然不变，这是强烈的对比。下阕出现两个白发老人：一个打鱼的，一个砍柴的。他们在江边看惯了秋月春风，见面就喝一壶便宜的浊酒，把古往今来的大事都当作聊天的笑话。前面写英雄，后面写普通人；前面写历史的沉重，后面写看透以后的轻松。这首词本来是杨慎《廿一史弹词》里的一段，清代毛宗岗把它放在《三国演义》的开头，从此家喻户晓。1994年电视剧《三国演义》用它做主题曲，杨洪基的歌声让很多人一听就能跟着唱。

In English

The first half begins with the Yangtze rolling east. Water that flows away never returns, and in the same way the waves have “washed away” the heroes of history one after another. Who was right and who was wrong, who won and who lost — turn your head and it is all gone. Only the green hills are still there, and the sunset still turns red, again and again. That contrast — human affairs change, nature does not — is the heart of the poem. The second half brings in two white-haired old men, a fisherman and a woodcutter, sitting on a sandbank. They have watched so many autumn moons and spring breezes that nothing surprises them. When they meet, they share a jug of cheap, cloudy wine and turn the great events of the past into small talk and laughter. So the poem moves from heroes to ordinary people, and from the weight of history to the calm of someone who has seen through it. The lyric was first part of Yang Shen’s long ballad on Chinese history. In the Qing dynasty the editor Mao Zonggang put it at the very start of Romance of the Three Kingdoms, and it became famous. The 1994 TV series used it as its theme song, sung by Yang Hongji, so many people can sing it today.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Yang Shen (1488–1559) came first in the national palace examination of 1511, but in 1524 he angered the Jiajing Emperor and was exiled to Yunnan for the rest of his life. This lyric opens one section of his long historical ballad 廿一史弹词; it was later placed at the start of Mao Zonggang’s edition of Romance of the Three Kingdoms.

版本说明 About the text

采用通行本（《三国演义》卷首词，与电视剧歌词一致）。原为杨慎《廿一史弹词》第三段《说秦汉》的开场词，清毛宗岗本《三国演义》移作卷首。“是非成败转头空”个别本子作“是非成败转眼空”，网站不用。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

滚滚长江东逝水，

gǔn gǔn cháng jiāng dōng shì shuǐ

On and on, the Yangtze flows away to the east,

浪花淘尽英雄。

làng huā táo jìn yīng xióng

its waves have washed away every hero.

是非成败转头空。

shì fēi chéng bài zhuǎn tóu kōng

Right or wrong, won or lost — look back, and nothing is left.

青山依旧在，

qīng shān yī jiù zài

The green hills stand as they always stood;

几度夕阳红。

jǐ dù xī yáng hóng

how many sunsets have reddened over them?

白发渔樵江渚上，

bái fà yú qiáo jiāng zhǔ shàng

On a sandbank, an old fisherman and an old woodcutter, hair gone white,

惯看秋月春风。

guàn kàn qiū yuè chūn fēng

have watched autumn moons and spring winds come and go.

一壶浊酒喜相逢。

yī hú zhuó jiǔ xǐ xiāng féng

A jug of rough wine, and they are glad to meet.

古今多少事，

gǔ jīn duō shǎo shì

All that has happened, then and now,

都付笑谈中。

dōu fù xiào tán zhōng

becomes something to laugh about over wine.
