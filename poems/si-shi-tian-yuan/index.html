中文解析

范成大晚年回到家乡苏州石湖，写了六十首《四时田园杂兴》，按春夏秋冬记录农村生活，这是写夏天的一首。前两句写大人：白天到田里锄草，晚上在家搓麻线，村里的男男女女各自担起一份家务，没有闲人。“昼”和“夜”对照，写出农民从早忙到晚。后两句写孩子：小孙子们还不懂怎样耕田织布，也在桑树的树荫下学着种瓜。“未解”和“也傍”一转，孩子虽然小，却在模仿大人干活，样子天真可爱。全诗语言朴素，像说家常话，没有一句直接说“勤劳”，可是从大人到孩子人人都在劳动的画面，让我们感到农家的勤劳和生活的生气。范成大被称为“田园诗”的集大成者，他写的不只是风景，还有真实的农民生活。

In English

In his last years Fan Chengda retired to Stone Lake near Suzhou and wrote sixty short poems called “Country Life Through the Seasons”, recording village life from spring to winter. This is one of the summer poems. The first two lines are about the adults: by day they go out to hoe the fields, by night they twist hemp into thread at home, and every man and woman in the village carries a share of the household work. Day and night set side by side show how the farmers work from morning to dark — nobody is idle. The last two lines turn to the children. The little grandchildren don’t yet understand how to plough or weave, but there they are in the shade of the mulberry trees, trying to plant melons too. Too young to help, they copy the grown-ups anyway, and the picture is sweet and funny. The language is as plain as kitchen-table talk, and the poem never uses the word “hard-working”; it simply shows everyone, from grandparents to toddlers, busy at something, and the feeling of a lively, industrious household comes through. Fan Chengda is admired for writing about real farm life, not just pretty scenery.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Fan Chengda (1126–1193) was a senior Southern Song official and diplomat who once went as an envoy to the Jin court. He retired to Stone Lake (Shihu) near Suzhou and in 1186 wrote the sixty poems of this series; this one belongs to the summer group.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

题目“杂兴”的“兴”读 xìng。耘田 yún；绩麻 jì má；当家 dāng jiā；未解 jiě；傍 bàng；种瓜 zhòng guā。“供耕织”的“供”：本页按统编教材注 gōng（从事），也有读本作 gòng。机器朗读已在“朗读用字”列替换同音字。

版本说明 About the text
