导读About this poem

Two friends who live in the same city still spend long seasons apart. The last two lines are a famous word game: “When I left, the snow was like flowers; now I’m back, the flowers are like snow.”

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首小诗写朋友之间的分别。两个人都住在洛阳城里（南朝诗人常用“洛阳”代指都城），一个城东一个城西，却常常一别就是很长时间。后两句最妙：“昔去雪如花，今来花似雪”——我走的时候，雪花飘得像花一样；我回来的时候，花瓣落得像雪一样。同样是“雪”和“花”两个字，只是换了个位置，就写出了从冬天到春天的时间变化，也写出了分别有多久。这种把前一句的词语颠倒过来再用一次的写法，叫“回环”，读起来像绕口令一样好听好记。诗里没有直接说“想你”，但从冬等到春的漫长，已经说明了一切。后来很多诗人都学过这种写法。

In English

This little poem is about two friends who keep being separated. They live in the same city — “Luoyang”, a name southern poets often borrowed for their own capital — one on the east side, one on the west, yet again and again they spend long stretches apart. The last two lines are the famous part: 昔去雪如花，今来花似雪 — “When I went away, snow fell like flowers; now that I’m back, flowers fall like snow.” The same two words, snow and flowers, simply swap places, and in that swap a whole winter turns into spring. We feel how long the separation was without being told a single date. Repeating words in reverse order like this is called huíhuán (“turning back”), and it makes the lines as catchy as a tongue twister. The poem never says “I missed you”; the long wait from snow to blossom says it instead. Later poets borrowed this trick many times.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Fan Yun (451–503) was a poet and statesman of the Southern Qi and Liang dynasties, a close adviser to Emperor Wu of Liang. The poem is said to come from a linked-verse session with his younger friend, the poet He Xun.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

版本说明 About the text

采用通行本，四句。《玉台新咏》“今来花似雪”作“今来花如雪”。这四句又见于何逊集中的《范广州宅联句》（文字略有不同，如“长作经时别”作“却作经年别”），一般认为是范云所作。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

洛阳城东西，

luò yáng chéng dōng xī

You on the east side of the city, I on the west —

长作经时别。

cháng zuò jīng shí bié

and still we part for whole seasons at a time.

昔去雪如花，

xī qù xuě rú huā

When I left, the snow was falling like blossoms;

今来花似雪。

jīn lái huā sì xuě

now I return, and blossoms are falling like snow.
