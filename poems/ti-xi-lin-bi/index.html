导读About this poem

Seen from the front it’s a ridge, from the side a peak: Su Shi can’t see the true face of Mount Lu because he is standing inside it. A four-line poem that gave Chinese a saying for “you can’t see clearly when you’re too close.”

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

苏轼游览庐山，最后在山上的西林寺墙壁上写下了这首诗。前两句写看山：从正面看，庐山是一道连绵的山岭；从侧面看，又变成高高的山峰；远看、近看、从高处看、从低处看，样子都不一样。后两句说出原因：我之所以看不清庐山真正的样子，是因为我自己就在这座山里。这首诗由写景变成讲道理：一个人身在事情当中，往往看不清全局；站远一点、换个角度，才能看明白。中国人常说的“当局者迷，旁观者清”就是这个意思。“不识庐山真面目”今天还常用，比喻看不清事情或人的本来面目。

In English

Su Shi spent days touring Mount Lu, and at the end wrote this poem on the wall of Xilin Temple on the mountain. The first two lines are about looking: from the front the mountain is a long ridge, from the side a tall peak; from far or near, high or low, it never looks the same. The last two lines give the reason. He cannot see the mountain’s true face because he himself is standing in the middle of it. So a travel poem turns into a thought about life: when you are inside a situation, you often can’t see the whole of it; step back or change your angle, and it becomes clear. There is a common Chinese saying with the same idea: 当局者迷，旁观者清 — “the player is confused, the onlooker sees clearly.” The line 不识庐山真面目 is still used today when someone can’t see the real nature of a thing or a person. The words are simple enough for a beginner, which is one reason Chinese children learn this poem in primary school.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Su Shi (1037–1101) wrote this in 1084, on his way from exile in Huangzhou to a new post, after touring Mount Lu (Lushan) in Jiangxi for more than ten days. Xilin Temple is a Buddhist temple at the foot of the mountain.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

横看成岭侧成峰，

héng kàn chéng lǐng cè chéng fēng

From the front, a long ridge; from the side, a single peak —

远近高低各不同。

yuǎn jìn gāo dī gè bù tóng

from far or near, high or low, it is never the same.

不识庐山真面目，

bù shí lú shān zhēn miàn mù

I cannot see the true face of Mount Lu

只缘身在此山中。

zhǐ yuán shēn zài cǐ shān zhōng

only because I am standing inside it.
