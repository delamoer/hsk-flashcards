导读About this poem

“I wish for one true heart, and never to part till our hair is white.” A woman who has learned her husband loves another says goodbye — with dignity, and with one of the most quoted lines about love in Chinese.

意译MeaningI pace along the palace moat, where the water splits and flows east and west.

直译LiteralA man should value loyalty and feeling; what use is money!

意译MeaningA man should prize love and loyalty — what good is money?

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗写一个女子得知丈夫变了心，决定和他分手。开头用山上的雪、云间的月来比喻自己的爱情：洁白、纯净。“听说你有了二心，所以我来跟你断绝关系。”今天我们最后一次一起喝酒，明天早上就在沟水边分别。她在御沟边慢慢走，沟里的水一东一西流去，就像两个人从此各走各的路。接着她说：女孩子出嫁的时候总是哭哭啼啼，其实不必哭，只要能找到一个一心一意的人，白头到老不分离就好了。最后用钓鱼比喻男女相爱：竹竿多么柔长，鱼尾多么灵动。男人应该看重情义，要钱财有什么用呢？“愿得一心人，白头不相离”是全诗最有名的两句，今天仍常被用来表达对忠贞爱情的向往。全诗语气坚定，不哭不闹，表现出一个女子的骨气和尊严。

In English

A woman learns that her husband’s heart has turned to someone else, and she decides to leave him. She opens by comparing her love to snow on the mountains and the moon among the clouds — white and pure. “I hear you have two minds, so I have come to break it off.” Today they share one last cup of wine; tomorrow morning they will part beside the canal. She walks slowly along the palace moat, watching the water split and flow east and west — the two of them going separate ways. Then she reflects: girls always cry at their weddings, but there is no need. All you need is “one true heart, and never to part till our hair is white.” The last lines use fishing as an image of courtship — the long, swaying bamboo rod, the lively flick of a fish’s tail — and end with a sharp rebuke: a man should value love and loyalty, so what is money for? The two lines 愿得一心人，白头不相离 are among the most quoted in Chinese and are still used today to wish for faithful love. The voice is firm and calm: no tears, no pleading, just dignity.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

The earliest sources record this as an anonymous Han song (古辞). The Xijing zaji, a later collection of anecdotes, tells that when the poet Sima Xiangru (179–117 BC) planned to take a concubine, his wife Zhuo Wenjun wrote a “Song of White Hair” and he changed his mind — so the poem is traditionally attributed to her, though most scholars doubt she wrote this text.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

相决绝 xiāng；斗酒 dǒu；明旦 dàn；躞蹀 xiè dié；袅袅 niǎo niǎo；重意气 zhòng；何用钱刀为 wéi（句末语气词）。簁簁 shī shī（鱼尾摆动、像沾湿羽毛的样子），也有注本读 shāi shāi 或 xǐ xǐ，网站暂读 shī shī。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行的“本辞”文本（《玉台新咏》题作《皑如山上雪》），全诗十六句，网站按一联一行排版。《宋书·乐志》《乐府诗集》另收晋乐所奏的较长版本，文字多有不同。本辞中“相决绝”《玉台新咏》作“相诀绝”，“袅袅”作“嫋嫋”，“簁簁”作“蓰蓰”（蓰 xǐ），网站从通行写法。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

皑如山上雪，皎若云间月。

ái rú shān shàng xuě, jiǎo ruò yún jiān yuè

White as snow upon the mountain, bright as the moon between the clouds —

闻君有两意，故来相决绝。

wén jūn yǒu liǎng yì, gù lái xiāng jué jué

I hear your heart is divided, so I’ve come to say goodbye.

今日斗酒会，明旦沟水头。

jīn rì dǒu jiǔ huì, míng dàn gōu shuǐ tóu

Today one last cup of wine together; at dawn we part beside the canal.

躞蹀御沟上，沟水东西流。

xiè dié yù gōu shàng, gōu shuǐ dōng xī liú

I pace along the palace moat, where the water splits and flows east and west.

凄凄复凄凄，嫁娶不须啼。

qī qī fù qī qī, jià qǔ bù xū tí

Sad, so sad — but a bride need not cry at her wedding;

愿得一心人，白头不相离。

yuàn dé yī xīn rén, bái tóu bù xiāng lí

all she wants is one true heart, and never to part till their hair is white.

竹竿何袅袅，鱼尾何簁簁！

zhú gān hé niǎo niǎo, yú wěi hé shī shī

How the bamboo rod sways! How the fish’s tail flickers!

男儿重意气，何用钱刀为！

nán ér zhòng yì qì, hé yòng qián dāo wéi

A man should prize love and loyalty — what good is money?
