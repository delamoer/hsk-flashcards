导读About this poem

One great poet writing about another: Du Fu praises Li Bai’s genius, then pictures the two of them under different skies, wishing they could share a cup of wine and talk about poetry again.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

杜甫和李白是唐代最有名的两位诗人，两人曾一起游玩，结下深厚友谊，分别后杜甫写过很多首怀念李白的诗，这是其中最有名的一首。前四句夸李白的诗：天下无敌，想法超出常人，像南北朝的庾信一样清新，像鲍照一样俊逸——用前代名家作比，评价非常高。五六句是全诗最动人的地方：“渭北春天树，江东日暮云”，杜甫在渭水北边看春天的树，李白在江东望傍晚的云，一句写自己，一句写对方，没有一个“想”字，两地相思却都在景物里。后人把“春树暮云”当作怀念朋友的固定说法。最后两句盼望再见：什么时候能一起喝一杯酒，再细细地谈论文章？以诗开头，以论诗结尾，可见两人是真正的知音。

In English

Du Fu and Li Bai are the two most famous poets of the Tang dynasty. They met in 744, travelled together for a while and became close friends; after they parted, Du Fu wrote many poems missing Li Bai, and this is the best loved. The first four lines praise Li Bai’s poetry: no one can match it, his thoughts soar above the crowd, he is as fresh as Yu Xin and as elegant as Bao Zhao — two admired poets of earlier centuries. Comparing a living friend to old masters is very high praise. Lines five and six are the heart of the poem: “trees in spring north of the Wei, clouds at dusk east of the river.” One line is where Du Fu stands, the other is where Li Bai is; the word “miss” never appears, yet the longing sits in the landscape. Later writers turned “spring trees, evening clouds” (春树暮云) into a set phrase for thinking of a distant friend. The ending asks when they can share a cup of wine and talk about writing again, line by line. The poem begins and ends with poetry — the sign of two people who truly understood each other.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Du Fu (712–770), honoured as the “Sage of Poetry”, lived through the An Lushan Rebellion and wrote movingly about ordinary people’s suffering. He wrote this around 746–747 in Chang’an, a year or two after parting from Li Bai, who was then wandering in the lower Yangtze region (Jiangdong).

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

庾 yǔ（姓）；参军 cān jūn（官名，不读 shēn）；樽 zūn；重与 chóng yǔ（“再次”，不读 zhòng）。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行文本。“一樽酒”的“樽”，《李太白集注》等本作“尊”（同义异体）。庾开府指北周庾信，曾任开府仪同三司；鲍参军指南朝宋鲍照，曾任参军。

白也诗无敌，

bái yě shī wú dí

Li Bai — no one writes poems like yours,

飘然思不群。

piāo rán sī bù qún

your thoughts drift high above the crowd.

清新庾开府，

qīng xīn yǔ kāi fǔ

Fresh as the verses of Yu Xin,

俊逸鲍参军。

jùn yì bào cān jūn

graceful as the lines of Bao Zhao.

渭北春天树，

wèi běi chūn tiān shù

Here north of the Wei, spring trees;

江东日暮云。

jiāng dōng rì mù yún

there east of the river, evening clouds.

何时一樽酒，

hé shí yī zūn jiǔ

When will we sit with a cup of wine

重与细论文。

chóng yǔ xì lùn wén

and talk over poems, slowly, once more?
