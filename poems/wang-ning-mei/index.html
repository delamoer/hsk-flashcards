导读About this poem

“One is a fairy flower, one is flawless jade” — the doomed love of Lin Daiyu and Jia Baoyu, the lovers of Dream of the Red Chamber, told in one short song. Their love is like the moon in water and flowers in a mirror: real to look at, impossible to hold.

这首诗被谱成了歌Sung as a song

陈力《枉凝眉》Chen Li

The best-known song from the 1987 CCTV TV series Dream of the Red Chamber: Wang Liping set Cao Xueqin's own lyric from the novel, sung by Chen Li. It is the series' theme of Daiyu and Baoyu's doomed love.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

《枉凝眉》是《红楼梦》第五回“红楼梦十二支曲”中的一支，写的是林黛玉和贾宝玉的爱情悲剧。“阆苑仙葩”是仙境里的花，指林黛玉；“美玉无瑕”指衔玉而生的贾宝玉。接着说：如果说两人没有缘分，为什么这辈子偏偏遇到了？如果说有缘分，为什么心事最后成了一场空？一个白白地叹息，一个白白地牵挂。“水中月”“镜中花”是比喻：看得见，却拿不到，比喻他们的爱情美好却不能实现。最后写黛玉的眼泪：眼里能有多少泪水，怎么经得起从秋流到冬、从春流到夏！小说里说黛玉前世是一株仙草，今生要用一生的眼泪来报答宝玉，所以这里的泪有特别的意义。“枉凝眉”就是白白地皱着眉头发愁。全曲语言像口语，对仗整齐，充满哀伤。

In English

This song is one of the “Twelve Songs of the Dream of the Red Chamber” in Chapter 5 of the novel, and it tells the tragic love of Lin Daiyu and Jia Baoyu. “A fairy flower from paradise” is Daiyu; “flawless jade” is Baoyu, who was born with a piece of jade in his mouth. Then come two questions: if they were not meant for each other, why did they meet in this life? And if they were, why did all their hopes come to nothing? One sighs in vain; the other worries in vain. “The moon in the water, the flower in the mirror” is a famous comparison for something you can see but never take hold of — their love is beautiful and impossible. The ending is about Daiyu’s tears: how many tears can her eyes hold, to flow from autumn to the end of winter, from spring into summer? In the novel, Daiyu in a former life was a fairy plant that Baoyu watered, and she was born to repay him with a lifetime of tears — so the tears here carry special meaning. The title 枉凝眉 means “knitting one’s brows in vain”. The language is close to speech, the lines pair up neatly, and the whole song is full of sorrow.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Cao Xueqin (c. 1715–c. 1763) wrote Dream of the Red Chamber, China’s best-loved classic novel. In Chapter 5, Baoyu dreams of a fairy realm where he hears twelve songs that secretly foretell the fates of the novel’s women; this one is about Lin Daiyu.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

阆苑 làng yuàn（不读 láng）；仙葩 pā；无瑕 xiá；嗟呀 jiē yā；泪珠儿的“儿”是儿化，读轻声 er；经得 de。遇着 zháo（遇到的意思；也有人读轻声 zhe）。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行的《红楼梦》（人民文学出版社）文本。末句维基文库所收版本作“怎经得秋流到冬，春流到夏”（无“尽”字），网站从通行本作“秋流到冬尽”。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

一个是阆苑仙葩，

yī gè shì làng yuàn xiān pā

One is a flower from the gardens of heaven,

一个是美玉无瑕。

yī gè shì měi yù wú xiá

one is a piece of jade without a flaw.

若说没奇缘，今生偏又遇着他；

ruò shuō méi qí yuán, jīn shēng piān yòu yù zháo tā

If they were never meant to be, why did they meet in this life?

若说有奇缘，如何心事终虚化？

ruò shuō yǒu qí yuán, rú hé xīn shì zhōng xū huà

And if they were, why did every hope dissolve to nothing?

一个枉自嗟呀，

yī gè wǎng zì jiē yā

One sighs and sighs for nothing,

一个空劳牵挂。

yī gè kōng láo qiān guà

one aches with worry for nothing.

一个是水中月，

yī gè shì shuǐ zhōng yuè

One is the moon in the water,

一个是镜中花。

yī gè shì jìng zhōng huā

one is a flower in a mirror.

想眼中能有多少泪珠儿，

xiǎng yǎn zhōng néng yǒu duō shǎo lèi zhū er

How many tears can two eyes hold,

怎经得秋流到冬尽，春流到夏！

zěn jīng de qiū liú dào dōng jìn, chūn liú dào xià

to flow from autumn through to winter’s end, and on from spring into summer?
