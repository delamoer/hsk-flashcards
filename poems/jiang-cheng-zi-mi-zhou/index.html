导读About this poem

Hounds, falcons, a thousand riders and a governor who wants to shoot a tiger himself. Su Shi’s loud, swaggering hunting song — and a wish to be sent to fight on the frontier.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首词写苏轼在密州当太守时一次打猎的场面。上阕写出猎：我这个老头子姑且也发一回少年人的狂劲，左手牵着黄狗，右臂托着苍鹰，随从们戴着锦帽、穿着貂皮衣，上千人马像风一样卷过平坦的山冈。为了报答全城百姓跟着来看热闹，我要像当年的孙权一样，亲自射杀老虎。下阕写抱负：喝足了酒，胸怀更加开阔，鬓角有点白了，又有什么关系！什么时候朝廷能像汉文帝派冯唐去云中那样，派人来重新任用我呢？到那时，我要把雕弓拉得像满月一样圆，朝西北方向，射下天狼星。“天狼”比喻当时威胁北宋边境的西夏和辽。这首词节奏快、气势大，一扫当时词多写儿女情长的风气，被看作豪放词的开山之作之一。

In English

Su Shi was governor of Mizhou when he led this hunt. The first half is the hunt itself. “This old man,” he says, “will let loose a young man’s wildness for once”: a yellow hound on a leash in his left hand, a grey falcon on his right arm, his men in brocade hats and sable furs, a thousand horsemen sweeping over the flat ridge like a gust of wind. The whole town has come out to watch, so to thank them he will shoot the tiger himself, like the young warlord Sun Quan centuries before. The second half moves from the hunt to his ambitions. Warmed by wine, he feels bold and big-hearted; a little white at the temples — so what? He then borrows a Han-dynasty story: an emperor once sent the old official Feng Tang to Yunzhong with a pardon for a disgraced frontier general. When, Su Shi asks, will someone be sent to call me back to serve? Then he would draw his carved bow round as a full moon, face northwest, and shoot down the Wolf Star — Sirius, a traditional symbol of invaders, here meaning the Western Xia and Liao states threatening the Song border. Fast, loud and proud, the poem broke with the soft love songs most ci were in those days, and is counted among the first works of the “bold and unrestrained” style.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Su Shi (1037–1101), also called Su Dongpo, was sent out of the capital after disagreeing with Wang Anshi’s reforms. He wrote this in 1075, aged about 38, as governor of Mizhou (in today’s Shandong), on a hunt on the way back from praying for rain at Mount Chang.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

老夫聊发少年狂，

lǎo fū liáo fā shào nián kuáng

Old as I am, I’ll be wild as a young man today —

左牵黄，右擎苍，

zuǒ qiān huáng, yòu qíng cāng

a yellow hound on the leash, a grey falcon on my arm;

锦帽貂裘，千骑卷平冈。

jǐn mào diāo qiú, qiān jì juǎn píng gāng

in brocade caps and sable furs, a thousand riders roll across the ridge.

为报倾城随太守，

wèi bào qīng chéng suí tài shǒu

The whole town has come out behind their governor —

亲射虎，看孙郎。

qīn shè hǔ, kàn sūn láng

so I’ll shoot the tiger myself, like young Sun Quan of old.

酒酣胸胆尚开张，

jiǔ hān xiōng dǎn shàng kāi zhāng

The wine has warmed me; my heart feels wide and brave.

鬓微霜，又何妨！

bìn wēi shuāng, yòu hé fáng

A little frost at my temples — what of it!

持节云中，何日遣冯唐？

chí jié yún zhōng, hé rì qiǎn féng táng

When will the court send its envoy, as it once sent Feng Tang, to call me back?

会挽雕弓如满月，

huì wǎn diāo gōng rú mǎn yuè

Then I’ll bend my carved bow round as the full moon,

西北望，射天狼。

xī běi wàng, shè tiān láng

turn to the northwest, and shoot down the Wolf Star.
