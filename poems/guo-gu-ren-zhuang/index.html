过故人庄

Guò Gù Rén Zhuāng
Visiting an Old Friend’s Farm
孟浩然Mèng Hàorán · Meng Haoran唐 Tang

导读About this poem

An old friend kills a chicken and invites the poet to his farm. They drink, talk about crops, and before leaving the poet promises to come back for the chrysanthemums in autumn — a perfect day in the countryside.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗写诗人到老朋友的农家做客。开头很朴素：朋友准备了鸡和黄米饭，邀请我到他家。“鸡黍”是农家最好的饭菜，写出朋友的热情。三、四句写村庄的风景：绿树把村子围起来，青山在城外斜斜地伸向远方，近处是绿，远处是青，像一幅画。五、六句写吃饭的情景：打开窗户对着打谷场和菜园，端着酒杯聊庄稼的事——不谈官场，不谈名利，只说桑和麻长得怎么样，非常轻松自在。最后两句是告别时的约定：等到重阳节，我还要来这里赏菊花。不说“今天很开心”，只说“还要再来”，快乐和友情都在这句话里。全诗语言平淡自然，是田园诗的代表作。

In English

An old friend has prepared chicken and millet — the best a farm household can offer — and invited the poet over. The second couplet paints the village: green trees close in around it, and blue hills slant away beyond the town wall; near and far, it looks like a landscape painting. Then the visit itself: they open the window onto the threshing ground and the vegetable garden, raise their cups and chat about mulberry and hemp — that is, about crops. No talk of careers, office or fame, only the easy concerns of farm life. The last couplet is the promise made at the gate: “When the Double Ninth comes, I’ll be back for the chrysanthemums.” The poet never says he had a wonderful time; wanting to come again says it for him. The language is plain and calm, and the poem is one of the best-known examples of Tang “pastoral poetry” — poems about the peace of country life.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Meng Haoran (689–740) lived most of his life as a recluse near Xiangyang in Hubei and never held office. He was a close friend of Wang Wei, and the two are seen as the leading Tang poets of landscape and country life.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

故人具鸡黍，

gù rén jù jī shǔ

My old friend got chicken and millet ready

邀我至田家。

yāo wǒ zhì tián jiā

and asked me to his farm.

绿树村边合，

lǜ shù cūn biān hé

Green trees gather round the village;

青山郭外斜。

qīng shān guō wài xié

blue hills slope off beyond the town wall.

开轩面场圃，

kāi xuān miàn chǎng pǔ

We open the window on yard and garden,

把酒话桑麻。

bǎ jiǔ huà sāng má

cups in hand, we talk about the crops.

待到重阳日，

dài dào chóng yáng rì

When the Double Ninth comes round,

还来就菊花。

huán lái jiù jú huā

I’ll come back for the chrysanthemums.
