导读About this poem

Riding west until it seems he will reach the sky, a traveller counts two full moons since he left home — and tonight there is nowhere to sleep, only ten thousand li of empty sand.

中文解析

岑参是唐代最有名的边塞诗人之一，曾经两次到西北边疆。这首诗写他骑马穿越大沙漠时的感受。第一句很夸张：骑马一直往西走，好像要走到天边去了，写出路途的遥远。第二句用月亮记时间：离开家以后，已经看见月亮圆了两次，也就是两个月了。“月圆”在中国文化里象征团圆，自己却离家越来越远。后两句是一个问题和一个画面：今晚不知道在哪里住宿？眼前只有平平的沙漠，一万里都看不见人烟。“绝人烟”三个字写出了沙漠的荒凉和旅人的孤独，但诗的语气并不消沉，反而有一种开阔、豪迈的味道，这是岑参边塞诗的特点。

In English

Cen Shen is one of the most famous frontier poets of the Tang dynasty; he travelled twice to the far north-west. This poem records crossing a great desert on horseback. The first line exaggerates: riding west and west, it feels as if he will reach the edge of the sky — a way of showing how endless the journey is. The second line keeps time by the moon: since leaving home he has seen it grow full twice, so two months have passed. In Chinese culture the full moon means family reunion, yet he is moving ever farther from his own. The last two lines give a question and a picture: where will I sleep tonight? All around there is only flat sand, ten thousand li without a wisp of chimney smoke. 绝人烟 (“no trace of human smoke”) catches the emptiness of the desert and the loneliness of the traveller. Still, the tone is not gloomy; there is something broad and bold in it, which is typical of Cen Shen’s frontier poetry.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Cen Shen (c. 715–770) served on the staff of Tang generals in the Western Regions (today’s Xinjiang). This poem is usually dated to 749, his first journey out to Anxi (near today’s Kucha), across the deserts west of the Jade Gate.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

走马西来欲到天，

zǒu mǎ xī lái yù dào tiān

Riding west, I seem about to reach the sky;

辞家见月两回圆。

cí jiā jiàn yuè liǎng huí yuán

since I left home, the moon has twice grown full.

今夜不知何处宿，

jīn yè bù zhī hé chù sù

Where will I sleep tonight? I cannot say —

平沙万里绝人烟。

píng shā wàn lǐ jué rén yān

flat sand for ten thousand li, and not one chimney’s smoke.
