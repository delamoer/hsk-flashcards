望庐山瀑布

Wàng Lú Shān Pùbù
Gazing at the Waterfall on Mount Lu
李白Lǐ Bái · Li Bai唐 Tang

导读About this poem

Purple mist rising from a peak, a waterfall hanging like a curtain, water plunging three thousand feet — is the Milky Way falling from the sky? Li Bai’s imagination at full power.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

庐山在今天江西九江，香炉峰是庐山的一座山峰，样子像一个香炉。第一句写阳光照着香炉峰，水汽升起来，变成紫色的烟雾——山真的像一个正在冒烟的香炉。第二句远远看去，瀑布像一条白布挂在山前。一个“挂”字把流动的瀑布写成静止的画面，这是远看的感觉。第三句写近看：水飞一样地直冲下来，有三千尺长！第四句再加一个大胆的想象：这是不是天上的银河从最高的天上落下来了？“三千尺”“九天”都是夸张，“疑是银河”是比喻，李白用这样的写法，把瀑布的高和气势写到了极点。“飞流直下三千尺”今天常被用来形容瀑布的壮观。

In English

Mount Lu is in today’s Jiujiang, Jiangxi province. Incense Burner Peak (Xiānglú) is one of its summits, shaped like an incense burner. In the first line, sunlight on the peak turns the rising mist purple — the mountain really looks like a burner giving off smoke. In the second, seen from afar, the waterfall seems to hang in front of the mountain like a strip of white cloth. The single verb 挂 (“to hang”) turns rushing water into a still picture: that is how it looks from a distance. The third line comes closer: the water flies straight down, three thousand feet! And the fourth adds a bold leap of imagination: could this be the Milky Way, falling from the highest heaven? “Three thousand feet” and “ninth heaven” are exaggerations; “the Milky Way” is a metaphor. With them Li Bai pushes the waterfall’s height and power as far as they can go. Today 飞流直下三千尺 is a stock phrase for any spectacular waterfall.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Li Bai (701–762) is said to have written this when he visited Mount Lu as a young man, around 725, on his first journey away from Sichuan. He wrote two poems with this title; this seven-character quatrain is the famous one.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

遥看 yáo kàn（统编教材注 kàn）；瀑布 pù bù（不读 bào）；香炉 xiāng lú。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行的教材版本。李白同题有两首，这是第二首（七言绝句）；第一首是五言古诗。“挂前川”一作“挂长川”，网站用通行本。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

日照香炉生紫烟，

rì zhào xiāng lú shēng zǐ yān

Sunlit, Incense Burner Peak breathes purple haze;

遥看瀑布挂前川。

yáo kàn pù bù guà qián chuān

far off, a waterfall hangs above the river.

飞流直下三千尺，

fēi liú zhí xià sān qiān chǐ

It flies straight down three thousand feet —

疑是银河落九天。

yí shì yín hé luò jiǔ tiān

is the Milky Way pouring down from the top of the sky?
