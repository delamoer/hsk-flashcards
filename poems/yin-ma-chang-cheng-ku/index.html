饮马长城窟行（青青河畔草）

Yìn Mǎ Cháng Chéng Kū Xíng
Watering Horses at a Spring by the Great Wall
佚名（汉乐府）Anonymous (Han yuefu folk song)汉 Han
体裁Form
乐府诗
难度Level
挑战 Advanced
主题Themes

导读About this poem

A wife dreams of her husband far away, wakes alone, and then a traveller brings a “pair of carp” — a letter from him. Its message is as plain as love gets: eat well; I always think of you. The source of the classic image of the fish-shaped letter.

02
远道不可思，宿昔梦见之。

直译LiteralThe far road cannot be thought of; last night I saw him in a dream.

意译MeaningIt hurts too much to think how far away you are — but last night I saw you in a dream.

03
梦见在我旁，忽觉在他乡。

直译LiteralIn the dream he was by my side; suddenly I woke, and he was in a far land.

意译MeaningIn the dream you were right beside me; I woke, and you were somewhere far away.

04
他乡各异县，展转不相见。

直译LiteralFar lands, each a different county; moving from place to place, we do not see each other.

意译MeaningYou go from town to town, each one strange to me — always moving, never where I can see you.

05
枯桑知天风，海水知天寒。

直译LiteralWithered mulberries know the wind of the sky; sea water knows the cold of the sky.

意译MeaningEven bare mulberry trees can feel the wind; even the sea knows when the weather turns cold.

06
入门各自媚，谁肯相为言！

直译LiteralComing home, each one dotes on his own; who is willing to speak to me!

意译MeaningBut others come home and dote on their own families — not one of them has a word for me.

第二段Part 2
07
客从远方来，遗我双鲤鱼。

直译LiteralA traveller came from far away and gave me a pair of carp.

意译MeaningThen a traveller came from far away and handed me a pair of carp.

08
呼儿烹鲤鱼，中有尺素书。

直译LiteralI call my child to cook the carp; inside there is a foot-long letter on silk.

意译MeaningI called my child to “cook the fish” — and inside was a letter on a strip of silk.

09
长跪读素书，书中竟何如？

直译LiteralKneeling upright, I read the silk letter; what, in the end, does the letter say?

意译MeaningI knelt up straight to read it. And what did it say?

10
上言加餐食，下言长相忆。

直译LiteralFirst it says: eat more; then it says: I will always remember you.

意译MeaningIt began, “Be sure you eat well,” and it ended, “I think of you always.”

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这是一首汉乐府民歌，写妻子思念远行的丈夫。开头由河边连绵不断的青草，联想到通向远方的长路，思念也像青草一样“绵绵”不断——这是“起兴”的写法。白天想不到，夜里就梦见他在身边；忽然醒来，他却在遥远的他乡，而且四处漂泊，连在哪里都不知道。“枯桑知天风，海水知天寒”是很有名的两句：没有叶子的桑树也能感到风，海水不结冰也知道天冷——我的孤单和痛苦，别人却不懂；别人家的丈夫回来了，各自亲热，谁肯来安慰我一句？最后，客人带来一封装在鲤鱼形木盒里的信（“双鲤鱼”），她让孩子打开（“烹”），恭敬地跪着读。信里只有两句最平常的话：“好好吃饭”“我一直想着你”。越平常越动人。后来“鱼”“鲤鱼”“尺素”在诗词里就成了书信的代称。

In English

This Han-dynasty folk song is about a wife missing her husband who has gone far away. It opens with grass stretching endlessly along the river, which leads her mind down the long road he took — her longing runs on and on (绵绵) like the grass. This “start from a scene” technique is called 起兴. By night she dreams he is beside her; she wakes, and he is in a distant land, moving from place to place where she can never find him. Then a famous couplet: “Even bare mulberry trees feel the wind; even the sea knows when it is cold.” Things that seem unfeeling still feel — so how can people not understand her pain? Other men come home and their families are happy together; no one has a word for her. At last a traveller brings a “pair of carp”: a letter in a wooden case carved like two fish. She has her child open it (烹, “cook”, is a playful word for this) and kneels upright to read it. The letter says only the plainest things: eat well, I think of you always. Its ordinariness is exactly what moves us. Ever since, “fish”, “carp” and 尺素 (“a foot of silk”) have been poetic words for a letter.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Yuefu were songs collected by the Han-dynasty Music Bureau, many from ordinary people. This one appears as an anonymous “old song” in the 6th-century Wen Xuan; another early anthology attributes it to the scholar Cai Yong (133–192), but most scholars treat it as anonymous. Men were often sent far away for frontier service or work, leaving their wives behind.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

饮马 yìn mǎ（给马喝水，不读 yǐn）；窟 kū；宿昔 sù xī；觉 jué（醒）；展转 zhǎn zhuǎn；相为言 xiāng wèi yán；遗 wèi（赠送）；烹 pēng；长跪 cháng guì；长相忆 cháng。机器朗读的“遗”“为”已在“朗读用字”列替换同音字。

版本说明 About the text

采用《文选》所收“古辞”的通行文本，全诗二十句。《玉台新咏》题作蔡邕，首句作“青青河边草”，末两句作“上有加餐食，下有长相忆”；“加餐食”一作“加餐饭”，“展转”一作“辗转”。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

青青河畔草，绵绵思远道。

qīng qīng hé pàn cǎo, mián mián sī yuǎn dào

Green, green the grass along the river, running on and on, like my thoughts down the long road to you.

远道不可思，宿昔梦见之。

yuǎn dào bù kě sī, sù xī mèng jiàn zhī

It hurts too much to think how far away you are — but last night I saw you in a dream.

梦见在我旁，忽觉在他乡。

mèng jiàn zài wǒ páng, hū jué zài tā xiāng

In the dream you were right beside me; I woke, and you were somewhere far away.

他乡各异县，展转不相见。

tā xiāng gè yì xiàn, zhǎn zhuǎn bù xiāng jiàn

You go from town to town, each one strange to me — always moving, never where I can see you.

枯桑知天风，海水知天寒。

kū sāng zhī tiān fēng, hǎi shuǐ zhī tiān hán

Even bare mulberry trees can feel the wind; even the sea knows when the weather turns cold.

入门各自媚，谁肯相为言！

rù mén gè zì mèi, shuí kěn xiāng wèi yán

But others come home and dote on their own families — not one of them has a word for me.

客从远方来，遗我双鲤鱼。

kè cóng yuǎn fāng lái, wèi wǒ shuāng lǐ yú

Then a traveller came from far away and handed me a pair of carp.

呼儿烹鲤鱼，中有尺素书。

hū ér pēng lǐ yú, zhōng yǒu chǐ sù shū

I called my child to “cook the fish” — and inside was a letter on a strip of silk.

长跪读素书，书中竟何如？

cháng guì dú sù shū, shū zhōng jìng hé rú

I knelt up straight to read it. And what did it say?

上言加餐食，下言长相忆。

shàng yán jiā cān shí, xià yán cháng xiāng yì

It began, “Be sure you eat well,” and it ended, “I think of you always.”
