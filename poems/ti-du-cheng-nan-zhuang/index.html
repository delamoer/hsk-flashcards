导读About this poem

Last year a face and peach blossoms glowed pink together at this gate. This year the blossoms are back, still smiling in the spring wind — but the face is gone.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗只有四句，讲了一个故事。去年的今天，诗人在长安城南的一户人家门口，看见一位姑娘，她的脸和桃花互相映照，都是红红的。今年同一天他又来了，桃花还像去年一样在春风里开得灿烂，可是那位姑娘已经不知去了哪里。诗里用了对比：去年和今年，人和花，有和无。“人面”和“桃花”出现了两次，第一次在一起，第二次分开了。桃花“笑”春风，用的是拟人（把花当作人来写），花越开心，人越失落。后来“人面桃花”成了成语，比喻见过面、后来又见不到的心上人。

In English

Four lines, one whole story. On this day last year, at a farmhouse gate south of Chang’an, the poet saw a young woman: her face and the peach blossoms lit each other up, both glowing pink. Exactly a year later he comes back. The peach blossoms are as bright as ever, smiling in the spring breeze — but the woman is gone, and he has no idea where. The poem is built on contrasts: last year and this year, the face and the flowers, there and not there. “Face” and “peach blossom” each appear twice — together the first time, apart the second. Describing the flowers as “smiling” treats them like people, and the happier they look, the emptier he feels. A later story fills in a romantic ending, but the poem itself stops at the empty gate, and that is its power. 人面桃花 became an idiom for someone you once met and lost, and the sense of returning to a place to find the person gone.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Cui Hu was a Tang poet who passed the highest examination in 796; little else is known about him. Tradition, recorded in a late-Tang story collection, says he wrote this on the farmhouse gate on his second visit.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

去年今日此门中，

qù nián jīn rì cǐ mén zhōng

A year ago today, inside this gate,

人面桃花相映红。

rén miàn táo huā xiāng yìng hóng

a face and peach blossoms glowed pink side by side.

人面不知何处去，

rén miàn bù zhī hé chù qù

Where the face has gone, I cannot tell;

桃花依旧笑春风。

táo huā yī jiù xiào chūn fēng

the peach blossoms still smile in the spring wind.
