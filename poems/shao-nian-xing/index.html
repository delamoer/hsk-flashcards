导读About this poem

Expensive wine, swaggering young swordsmen, horses tied by a tall tavern under the willows: Wang Wei captures the confidence and generosity of youth in the golden age of the Tang.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这是王维《少年行》四首中的第一首，写长安一带意气风发的少年游侠。第一句说新丰的美酒很贵，一斗值十千钱；第二句说咸阳的游侠大多是年轻人。第三句是全诗的核心：大家萍水相逢，因为意气相投，就为了你痛快地喝一杯。“意气”是一种豪爽、讲义气的精神，不在乎钱，也不在乎认识多久。最后一句不写喝酒的场面，只写一个画面：大家把马拴在高楼旁边的垂柳下。高楼、垂柳、骏马，色彩明亮，好像一个电影镜头，热闹和豪气都留给读者去想象。整首诗充满青春的自信和盛唐的昂扬气象。

In English

This is the first of Wang Wei’s four “Songs of Youth”, about the dashing young knights-errant (游侠) of the Chang’an area. The first line tells us the wine of Xinfeng is famous and costly — ten thousand coins a measure. The second says that most of the knights of Xianyang are young men. The third line is the heart of the poem: they have only just met, but because their spirits click (意气), they drink to each other without a second thought. 意气 is a mix of boldness, generosity and loyalty — not caring about money, and not needing years of friendship before you trust someone. Then, instead of describing the party, the last line gives a single picture: the horses tied up beneath the drooping willows beside a tall tavern. Tall building, green willows, fine horses — bright colours, like a shot in a film, and the noise and laughter upstairs are left to our imagination. The whole poem breathes the confidence of youth and the high spirits of the Tang at its height.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Wang Wei (701–761) is usually thought to have written the four “Songs of Youth” when he was young himself. “Song of Youth” (少年行) was an old yuefu title used by many poets for scenes of bold young men.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

斗十千：斗 dǒu（量词）。多少年：少 shào（少年）。为君饮：为 wèi（替、给）。系马：系 jì（拴，不读 xì）。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

新丰美酒斗十千，

xīn fēng měi jiǔ dǒu shí qiān

Xinfeng’s wine is fine — ten thousand coins a jug;

咸阳游侠多少年。

xián yáng yóu xiá duō shào nián

Xianyang’s swordsmen are nearly all young.

相逢意气为君饮，

xiāng féng yì qì wèi jūn yǐn

We’ve only just met, but our spirits match — I drink to you!

系马高楼垂柳边。

jì mǎ gāo lóu chuí liǔ biān

Our horses wait, tied by the tall tavern under the willows.
