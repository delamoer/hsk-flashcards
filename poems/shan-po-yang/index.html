导读About this poem

Standing at a famous mountain pass, an old official looks toward the ruins of ancient capitals and reaches a blunt conclusion: when a dynasty rises, the people suffer; when it falls, the people suffer.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首小令是张养浩去关中救灾的路上，经过潼关时写的。开头三句写潼关的形势：群山像从四面聚拢过来，黄河的波涛像在发怒，潼关外有黄河、内有华山，地势非常险要。“聚”“怒”两个字把山和水写活了，好像有感情一样，这叫“拟人”。接着诗人望向西边的古都长安，心里起伏不平。秦朝、汉朝曾经在这里建起无数宫殿，如今全都变成了泥土。最后两句是全曲的中心，也是最有名的话：“兴，百姓苦；亡，百姓苦。”一个朝代兴起时，要大修宫殿、打仗，老百姓受苦；一个朝代灭亡时，战乱不断，老百姓还是受苦。不管谁当皇帝，吃苦的都是普通人。“怀古”就是借古代的事来表达对现实的看法。这六个字说出了很多人想说却没说出来的道理，所以流传了七百年。

In English

Zhang Yanghao wrote this song on his way to bring famine relief to the Guanzhong region, when he passed through Tongguan, a strategic pass between the Yellow River and Mount Hua. The opening paints the place: the peaks crowd in as if gathering together, the waves of the Yellow River seem furious, and the road to the pass runs between river and mountains. The verbs “gather” and “rage” give the landscape feelings of its own — a device called nǐrén (personification). Then the poet looks west toward Chang’an, the old capital, and his mind is uneasy. The Qin and Han dynasties built countless palaces there, and now every one has turned to dust. The last two lines are the heart of the song and among the most quoted in Chinese: “Rise — the people suffer; fall — the people suffer.” When a dynasty rises, it builds palaces and fights wars on the people’s backs; when it falls, war and chaos crush them again. Whoever rules, ordinary people pay. This is what huáigǔ (“thinking of the past”) poems do: they look at history to say something about the present. Six plain characters put into words what many felt, and they have been repeated for seven hundred years.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Zhang Yanghao (1270–1329) was a Yuan official known for his honesty who retired early. In 1329, at nearly sixty, he accepted a post to fight a severe drought and famine in Shaanxi, and wrote this on the way; he died of exhaustion a few months later.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

“望西都”的“都”读 dū（都城），“都做了土”的“都”读 dōu（全）。“经行处”的“行”读 xíng。“宫阙”的“阙”读 què。“踌躇”读 chóu chú。“兴，百姓苦”的“兴”读 xīng（兴盛），不读 xìng。

版本说明 About the text

采用统编语文教材九年级下册文本，全曲九句，此处完整收录。“山坡羊”是曲牌，“潼关怀古”是题目，“中吕”是宫调。张养浩同曲牌另有多首怀古之作。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

峰峦如聚，

fēng luán rú jù

The peaks crowd in from every side,

波涛如怒，

bō tāo rú nù

the river’s waves rage,

山河表里潼关路。

shān hé biǎo lǐ tóng guān lù

river without, mountains within — this is the road through Tongguan.

望西都，意踌躇。

wàng xī dū, yì chóu chú

I look toward the old western capital, and my heart is troubled.

伤心秦汉经行处，

shāng xīn qín hàn jīng xíng chù

It hurts to see where the Qin and Han once marched:

宫阙万间都做了土。

gōng què wàn jiān dōu zuò le tǔ

ten thousand palace halls, every one turned to dust.

兴，百姓苦；亡，百姓苦。

xīng, bǎi xìng kǔ, wáng, bǎi xìng kǔ

When an empire rises, the people suffer; when it falls, the people suffer.
