导读About this poem

A riddle with no answer: something that is like a flower and like mist, comes at midnight and leaves at dawn, as brief as a spring dream. Readers have argued for a thousand years about what it is.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗很短，却像一个谜语。“花非花，雾非雾”：它像花又不是花，像雾又不是雾。“夜半来，天明去”：半夜来，天一亮就走了。后两句用两个比喻：来的时候像春天的梦，能有多久呢？走的时候像早上的云，再也找不到了。诗人始终没说“它”是什么，所以后人有很多猜测：有人说是夜里的露水或霜，有人说是一段短暂的爱情，有人说是人生中一切美好又留不住的东西。“朝云”还让人想到古代巫山神女“早上是云，晚上是雨”的传说，所以很多人把它读成爱情诗。全诗三字句和七字句交替，节奏轻快，像一首歌，后来还变成了一个词牌名。它的魅力就在“说不清”：每个读者都能放进自己心里那件留不住的事。

In English

This poem is tiny, and it reads like a riddle. “A flower, yet not a flower; mist, yet not mist” — it resembles both, but is neither. “It comes at midnight and leaves at daybreak.” Then two comparisons: when it comes, it is like a spring dream — how long can that last? When it goes, it is like morning cloud, gone without a trace. The poet never says what “it” is, so readers have guessed for centuries: dew or frost in the night, a brief love affair, or every lovely thing in life that cannot be held. 朝云 (“morning cloud”) also recalls the old legend of the goddess of Mount Wu, who was “morning cloud by day and rain by night” after a night with a king — which is why many read it as a love poem. The lines alternate between three and seven characters, giving it the light rhythm of a song, and its pattern later became a tune name for song lyrics. Its charm lies in never being pinned down: every reader can fill it with something of their own that slipped away.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Bai Juyi (772–846) wrote in many forms, from long ballads to tiny songs like this one. Its date and occasion are unknown; it was included among his “miscellaneous songs” and later became a tune pattern for ci lyrics.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

花非花，雾非雾。

huā fēi huā, wù fēi wù

A flower that is no flower, a mist that is no mist —

夜半来，天明去。

yè bàn lái, tiān míng qù

it comes at midnight and is gone by dawn.

来如春梦几多时？

lái rú chūn mèng jǐ duō shí

It comes like a spring dream — how long can it stay?

去似朝云无觅处。

qù sì zhāo yún wú mì chù

It leaves like morning cloud, and nowhere can it be found.
