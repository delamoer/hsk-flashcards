导读About this poem

Hiding her face from the sun, too sad to get up and dress: a woman hurt in love writes to herself as if to a neighbour — and gives us the famous line “A priceless treasure is easy to find; a man who truly loves you is hard to find.”

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

鱼玄机被丈夫抛弃后写了这首诗，题目说是送给邻家女子，其实是在写自己。开头说：怕见阳光，用丝绸袖子遮住脸；春天让人发愁，懒得起床梳妆。接着是最有名的两句：无价的宝贝容易找到，真心相爱的人却很难得到。然后写她偷偷在枕头上流泪，在花丛里暗自伤心。最后两句态度一转：我自己就能去看中宋玉那样的美男子，何必再为王昌这样的人难过呢？宋玉、王昌都是古代有名的美男子，这里王昌指负心的人。全诗从悲伤写到自我安慰，最后带着一点倔强和骄傲，表现出一个唐代女子少见的大胆和独立。

In English

Yu Xuanji wrote this after the man she loved abandoned her. The title says it is for the girl next door, but she is really writing about herself. She hides her face from the sun behind a silk sleeve; spring only makes her sad, and she can’t be bothered to get up and do her make-up. Then come the famous lines: a priceless treasure is easy enough to find, but a man who truly loves you is hard to come by. She weeps secretly into her pillow and grieves alone among the flowers. The last couplet turns around: I can set my eyes on someone as handsome as Song Yu myself — why go on grieving over a Wang Chang? Song Yu and Wang Chang were both legendary handsome men; here Wang Chang stands for the one who let her down. The poem moves from sorrow to self-comfort and ends on a note of pride and defiance — unusually bold and independent for a woman’s voice in Tang poetry. The line about treasure and true love is still widely quoted.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Yu Xuanji (c. 844–c. 868) was one of the best-known women poets of the Tang. She became the concubine of an official, Li Yi, was driven out by his wife, and lived as a Daoist nun; she is said to have been executed young. The poem is also titled “Sent to Vice-Minister Li Yi”.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

羞日遮罗袖，

xiū rì zhē luó xiù

Ashamed to face the sun, I hide behind my sleeve;

愁春懒起妆。

chóu chūn lǎn qǐ zhuāng

spring makes me sad, too weary to rise and dress.

易求无价宝，

yì qiú wú jià bǎo

A priceless jewel is easy to come by;

难得有情郎。

nán dé yǒu qíng láng

a man who truly loves you is hard to find.

枕上潜垂泪，

zhěn shàng qián chuí lèi

I weep in secret on my pillow,

花间暗断肠。

huā jiān àn duàn cháng

and grieve alone among the flowers.

自能窥宋玉，

zì néng kuī sòng yù

I can find myself a Song Yu —

何必恨王昌。

hé bì hèn wáng chāng

why go on grieving for a Wang Chang?
