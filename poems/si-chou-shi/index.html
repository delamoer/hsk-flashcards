导读About this poem

The one he loves is on Mount Tai, but the road is blocked; she sent him a gold-inlaid knife, and he can’t even send jade back. One of the earliest Chinese poems in seven-character lines, by the same man who built the earliest known seismoscope.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

《四愁诗》共四首，结构相同，分别写所思之人在东方的泰山、南方的桂林、西方的汉阳、北方的雁门，这里是第一首。诗人说：我思念的人在泰山，想去找她，却被梁父山挡住了路，只能侧身向东望，眼泪打湿了衣襟和笔。她送我一把金错刀，我想用美玉回报她，可是路太远送不到，只能徘徊不安，心中烦闷——为什么我要这样忧愁呢？表面看是一首情诗，但根据诗前的小序（一般认为是后人所加），张衡是借“美人”比喻君主或理想，借道路艰难比喻小人当道、志向难以实现。这种“香草美人”的写法来自屈原。“美人赠我金错刀，何以报之英琼瑶”化用了《诗经》“投我以木瓜，报之以琼瑶”。这首诗是中国早期的七言诗之一，在诗歌形式的发展上很重要。注意“逍遥”在这里是“徘徊不安”，和今天“逍遥自在”正好相反。

In English

“Four Sorrows” is a set of four poems with the same pattern: the one the poet longs for is in the east at Mount Tai, then in the south, the west and the north. This is the first. The one I love is on Mount Tai, he says; I want to go to her, but the hills of Liangfu block the way, so I can only turn and gaze east, my tears falling on my writing brush. She gave me a knife inlaid with gold; I want to send her fine jade in return, but the road is too long, and I can only pace about, restless and troubled. On the surface it is a love poem. But according to the preface attached to it (perhaps written by a later hand), Zhang Heng used the “beauty” to stand for his ruler or his ideals, and the blocked road for the scheming men at court who kept him from his goals — the “fragrant herbs and beautiful women” tradition begun by Qu Yuan. The gift-and-jade lines echo the Book of Songs (“you gave me a quince; I gave you fine jade”). This is one of the earliest Chinese poems built on seven-character lines, so it matters for the history of verse. Watch out for 逍遥: here it means pacing about uneasily — the opposite of its modern sense, “carefree”.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Zhang Heng (78–139) was an Eastern Han scientist, official and writer — an astronomer famous for building an early seismoscope. A preface attached to “Four Sorrows” (possibly added later) says he wrote it late in his career, while serving as chancellor of the kingdom of Hejian, frustrated that he could not set things right.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

梁父 liáng fǔ（山名，“父”读 fǔ）；何为 hé wèi（为什么）；涕 tì；翰 hàn；错 cuò；逍遥 xiāo yáo。机器朗读的“父”“为”已在“朗读用字”列替换同音字。

版本说明 About the text

采用《文选》所收《四愁诗》第一首的通行文本（七句）。《文选》本每首开头有“一思曰”等字，通行本多不录；诗前原有一段小序（宋人王观国已指出是后人伪托），网站未收。“太山”即泰山。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

我所思兮在太山，

wǒ suǒ sī xī zài tài shān

The one I long for lives on Mount Tai —

欲往从之梁父艰，

yù wǎng cóng zhī liáng fǔ jiān

I want to go to her, but the hills of Liangfu bar the way.

侧身东望涕沾翰。

cè shēn dōng wàng tì zhān hàn

I turn and look east, and my tears fall on my brush.

美人赠我金错刀，

měi rén zèng wǒ jīn cuò dāo

My lovely one gave me a knife inlaid with gold;

何以报之英琼瑶。

hé yǐ bào zhī yīng qióng yáo

I would give her bright jade in return.

路远莫致倚逍遥，

lù yuǎn mò zhì yǐ xiāo yáo

But the road is too long to send it, and I can only pace about, uneasy.

何为怀忧心烦劳。

hé wèi huái yōu xīn fán láo

Why must I carry this sorrow, my heart so tired and torn?
