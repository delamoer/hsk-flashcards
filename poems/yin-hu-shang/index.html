饮湖上初晴后雨

Yǐn Hú Shàng Chū Qíng Hòu Yǔ
Drinking on the Lake: Fine at First, Then Rain (No. 2)

导读About this poem

Sunshine or rain, Hangzhou’s West Lake is lovely — as lovely as the legendary beauty Xi Shi, whether she wears light make-up or heavy. This little poem is why West Lake is still called 西子湖.

中文解析

苏轼在杭州做官时，有一天在西湖上喝酒，先是晴天，后来下起雨来，他写了两首诗，这是第二首，也是最有名的一首。前两句写两种天气下的西湖：晴天时，阳光照在水面上，波光闪闪，最好看；下雨时，远处的山在雨雾中朦朦胧胧，也很奇妙。“晴方好”“雨亦奇”是一组对仗（两句字数相同、意思相对）。后两句用了一个比喻：如果把西湖比作古代美女西施，那么不管她化淡妆还是浓妆，都一样美。这个比喻很巧：晴天像浓妆，雨天像淡妆，西湖怎么样都美。从此西湖有了一个别名叫“西子湖”。

In English

While serving as an official in Hangzhou, Su Shi went drinking on West Lake one day. It was sunny at first, then it began to rain, and he wrote two short poems about it. This is the second and far more famous one. The first two lines show the lake in both kinds of weather. In sunshine the water ripples and sparkles — it looks its best. In rain the hills around it fade into mist — and that is wonderful too. These two lines mirror each other word for word (水光 / 山色, 晴 / 雨, 好 / 奇), a technique called duìzhàng, or parallelism. Then comes the comparison everyone remembers: if you liken West Lake to Xi Shi, the most famous beauty of ancient China, then whether she wears light make-up or heavy, she always looks just right. The sunny lake is the bright, made-up face; the rainy lake is the soft, natural one. Since this poem, West Lake has had a second name, 西子湖, “Lake Xizi”. If you visit Hangzhou, you will see the poem everywhere.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Su Shi (1037–1101) served as vice-prefect of Hangzhou from 1071 to 1074, and this poem is traditionally dated to 1073. He later returned as prefect and built the causeway across the lake still called the Su Causeway (苏堤).

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

水光潋滟晴方好，

shuǐ guāng liàn yàn qíng fāng hǎo

Sun on the water, rippling and glittering — the lake is at its best in fine weather;

山色空蒙雨亦奇。

shān sè kōng méng yǔ yì qí

hills soft and hazy in the rain — and that is a marvel too.

欲把西湖比西子，

yù bǎ xī hú bǐ xī zǐ

If you compare West Lake to the beauty Xi Shi,

淡妆浓抹总相宜。

dàn zhuāng nóng mǒ zǒng xiāng yí

light make-up or heavy, she always looks just right.
