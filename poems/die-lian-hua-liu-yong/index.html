蝶恋花（伫倚危楼风细细）

Butterflies Love Flowers: Leaning on a High Tower

导读About this poem

A man stands a long time on a high tower in the spring breeze, sick with longing. He tries to drink and sing it away, but nothing tastes right. His belt grows looser as he grows thinner — and he says he regrets nothing: she is worth it.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首词写对心上人的思念。上阕写登楼。他久久地靠在高楼上，微风轻轻吹着，向远方望去，春天的愁绪从天边慢慢升起，好像看得见一样。“黯黯”是心情暗淡、迷迷茫茫的样子。夕阳下，青草的颜色和烟雾的光影交织在一起，景色很美，可是他一句话也不说——有谁能懂我靠着栏杆远望的心意呢？下阕写怎么排解。他打算放纵一下，痛痛快快地喝醉一场，一边喝酒一边唱歌，可是勉强找来的快乐还是没有味道。到这里，读者以为他要说怎样忘掉愁苦了，结尾却来了一个转折：衣带一天天变宽，人一天天消瘦，但我始终不后悔，为了她，憔悴也值得。这两句把执着的爱写到了极致，今天常被用来形容为了理想或事业坚持不懈、甘愿付出。王国维把它当作成就大事业要经过的第二种境界：为了目标，再苦也不放弃。

In English

This lyric is about longing for someone you love. The first half is the tower. He has been leaning on a high tower for a long time in a light breeze. Gazing as far as he can, he watches “spring sorrow” rise dimly from the horizon, as if it were something you could see. In the evening sun, the colour of the grass and the shimmer of the mist blend together — a lovely view, but he says nothing. Who would understand why he leans on this railing? The second half is about getting rid of the feeling. He plans to let himself go and get properly drunk, raising a cup and singing — but forced cheer has no taste. At this point we expect him to find a way to forget. Instead the poem turns: his robe’s belt keeps getting looser as he wastes away, yet he will never regret it; for her, it is worth growing thin and worn. These last two lines capture a stubborn, devoted love so well that today people quote them for anyone who gives everything to a goal or a dream. The critic Wang Guowei used them as the second of his “three stages” of great achievement: holding on, whatever the cost.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Liu Yong (c. 984–c. 1053) was the most popular lyricist of the early Northern Song, famous for songs written for the entertainers of the capital — it was said that “wherever there is a well (i.e. people), Liu Yong’s lyrics are sung.” He failed the civil-service exams several times and spent years wandering. This lyric is also known under the tune name 凤栖梧.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

伫倚危楼风细细，

zhù yǐ wēi lóu fēng xì xì

I lean a long while on the high tower in the soft breeze,

望极春愁，黯黯生天际。

wàng jí chūn chóu, àn àn shēng tiān jì

and gaze until spring sorrow rises, dim and grey, along the horizon.

草色烟光残照里，无言谁会凭阑意。

cǎo sè yān guāng cán zhào lǐ, wú yán shuí huì píng lán yì

Green grass and shimmering haze in the last of the sun. I say nothing — who would understand why I stand here?

拟把疏狂图一醉，

nǐ bǎ shū kuáng tú yī zuì

I mean to let myself go and drink till I’m drunk,

对酒当歌，强乐还无味。

duì jiǔ dāng gē, qiǎng lè hái wú wèi

to sing over my wine — but forced cheer tastes of nothing.

衣带渐宽终不悔，为伊消得人憔悴。

yī dài jiàn kuān zhōng bù huǐ, wèi yī xiāo dé rén qiáo cuì

My belt grows looser and looser, and I regret nothing — she is worth every bit of this wasting away.
