导读About this poem

Two lovers live at opposite ends of the Yangtze and never meet, yet they drink from the same river. Simple as a folk song, it ends with a wish that his heart be as true as hers.

这首诗被谱成了歌Sung as a song

Teresa Teng's 1983 album Light Exquisite Feeling (淡淡幽情) set twelve classical poems to new music; "Thinking of You" sings Li Zhiyi's "I live at the head of the Long River" verbatim, repeating the second half.

中文解析

这首词像一首民歌，语言非常朴素，用的是一个女子的口吻。我住在长江上游，你住在长江下游，天天想你却见不到你，我们一起喝着同一条江的水。一条长江，既是隔开两人的距离，又是连接两人的纽带——见不到面，但喝的是同一江水，心就连在一起了。下阕说：这江水什么时候才会停止流动？这离别的痛苦什么时候才会结束？江水不会停，相思也不会停。最后两句是她的心愿：只希望你的心和我的心一样，我就一定不会辜负这份相思。全词反复出现“长江”“君”“水”，节奏回环，很像唱歌，感情真挚，所以流传很广。

In English

This lyric reads like a folk song, in very plain language, spoken by a woman. I live at the head of the Yangtze, you live at its tail; I think of you every day and never see you — and yet we drink from the same river. The river is two things at once: the distance that keeps them apart, and the thread that ties them together. They cannot meet, but the same water reaches them both, and so do their feelings. In the second half she asks when this river will ever stop flowing, and when this ache of separation will ever end. The answer is in the question: the river never stops, and neither will her longing. The last two lines are her wish: if only your heart is like mine, I will never betray this love. Notice how “Long River”, “you” and “water” keep coming back. That circling repetition gives the poem the rhythm of a song and helps explain why it is still so widely loved.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Li Zhiyi (dates uncertain, c. 1048 – c. 1117) was a Northern Song official and a friend of Su Shi. He is said to have written this lyric while in exile at Dangtu, on the Yangtze in today’s Anhui, possibly for a singer who later became his companion.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

我住长江头，

wǒ zhù cháng jiāng tóu

I live where the Yangtze begins,

君住长江尾。

jūn zhù cháng jiāng wěi

you live where it meets the sea.

日日思君不见君，

rì rì sī jūn bù jiàn jūn

Every day I miss you, and never see you,

共饮长江水。

gòng yǐn cháng jiāng shuǐ

yet we drink from the same river.

此水几时休，

cǐ shuǐ jǐ shí xiū

When will this river ever stop?

此恨何时已。

cǐ hèn hé shí yǐ

When will this ache ever end?

只愿君心似我心，

zhǐ yuàn jūn xīn sì wǒ xīn

If only your heart is like mine,

定不负相思意。

dìng bù fù xiāng sī yì

I will never let this love down.
