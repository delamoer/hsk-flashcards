留别妻（结发为夫妻）

Liú Bié Qī
Parting from My Wife
苏武（传）Sū Wǔ · Su Wu (attributed)汉 Han
体裁Form
五言古诗

导读About this poem

On the last night before he leaves for war, a husband watches the stars fade and makes his wife one promise: if I live, I will come back; if I die, I will never stop missing you.

01
结发为夫妻，恩爱两不疑。

直译LiteralTying up our hair, we became husband and wife; love and kindness, neither of us doubting.

意译MeaningSince we tied our hair as bride and groom, our love has never known a doubt.

02
欢娱在今夕，嬿婉及良时。

直译LiteralJoy is in this evening; tenderness, while the good time lasts.

意译MeaningTonight is ours for joy; let us be tender while the good hours last.

03
征夫怀远路，起视夜何其。

直译LiteralThe soldier thinks of the road ahead; he rises to see how far the night has gone.

意译MeaningBut a soldier’s mind is on the road; I rise to see how late it is.

04
参辰皆已没，去去从此辞。

直译LiteralThe stars Shen and Chen have all set; go, go — from here I take my leave.

意译MeaningThe stars have all gone down. I must go — this is goodbye.

05
行役在战场，相见未有期。

直译LiteralOn duty I will be on the battlefield; our meeting again has no set date.

意译MeaningI march away to the battlefield; who knows when we will meet again.

06
握手一长叹，泪为生别滋。

直译LiteralHolding hands, a long sigh; tears flow more for this parting in life.

意译MeaningWe hold hands and sigh, and tears pour down for this living parting.

07
努力爱春华，莫忘欢乐时。

直译LiteralMake an effort to cherish your spring blossom; do not forget the happy times.

意译MeaningTake care of your youth while it blooms, and don’t forget our happy days.

08
生当复来归，死当长相思。

直译LiteralAlive, I shall come back again; dead, I shall long for you forever.

意译MeaningIf I live, I will come home to you; if I die, I will miss you forever.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗写一个丈夫出征前和妻子告别。开头说：我们从结发成婚起就做了夫妻，恩爱从来没有怀疑过。今晚是最后的欢聚，要珍惜这美好的时光。可是要出征的人心里惦记着远方的路，一次次起来看夜里到了什么时候。等到天上的星星都落下去了，他只能说：走吧，就此告别。去战场打仗，不知道哪一天才能再见。两个人握着手长长地叹气，眼泪因为这活生生的分别流个不停。最后他嘱咐妻子：好好珍惜你的青春年华，别忘了我们快乐的日子。如果我活着，一定回来；如果我死了，也会永远想念你。“生当复来归，死当长相思”这两句非常直接，没有一点修饰，却比任何华丽的话都动人。诗里的“结发夫妻”今天仍用来指原配夫妻，“起视夜何其”写他舍不得天亮，细节很真实。

In English

A husband says goodbye to his wife on the night before he leaves for war. He begins: since the day we tied up our hair as bride and groom, we have been husband and wife, and never once doubted our love. Tonight is their last night of happiness, and they hold on to it. But a man about to march has the road on his mind; again and again he gets up to see how late it is. When the stars have all sunk below the horizon, there is nothing left to say but “I must go.” He is heading for the battlefield, and no one knows when they will meet again. They hold hands and sigh, and tears fall for a parting between two living people. His last words: cherish your youth, and don’t forget our happy times. “If I live, I will come back to you; if I die, I will miss you forever.” Those final two lines are completely plain — no ornament at all — and that is exactly why they move readers more than any elegant phrase could. 结发夫妻 (“tied-hair couple”) is still used today for a couple married since youth, and the small detail of him getting up to check the night makes the scene feel real.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

The poem is traditionally attributed to Su Wu (c. 140–60 BC), the Han envoy who was held by the Xiongnu for nineteen years and refused to surrender. It appears in the Wenxuan as the third of “Four Poems by Su Ziqing”, but most scholars believe these “Su–Li poems” were written by unknown poets of the later Eastern Han.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

结发 fà（头发，不读 fā）；为夫妻 wéi；嬿婉 yàn wǎn；何其 jī（语气词，“夜如何其”，不读 qí）；参辰 shēn chén（星名，不读 cān）；没 mò（落下）；行役 xíng yì；相见 xiāng；长叹、长相思 cháng；泪为生别滋 wèi（因为）；当 dāng。

版本说明 About the text

采用《文选》卷二十九所收《诗四首》之三（旧题苏武作）的通行文本，题目通称《留别妻》，全诗十六句，网站按一联一行排版。“嬿婉”一本作“燕婉”。“泪为生别滋”，《玉台新咏》作“泪为别生滋”，网站从通行的“生别”。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

结发为夫妻，恩爱两不疑。

jié fà wéi fū qī, ēn ài liǎng bù yí

Since we tied our hair as bride and groom, our love has never known a doubt.

欢娱在今夕，嬿婉及良时。

huān yú zài jīn xī, yàn wǎn jí liáng shí

Tonight is ours for joy; let us be tender while the good hours last.

征夫怀远路，起视夜何其。

zhēng fū huái yuǎn lù, qǐ shì yè hé jī

But a soldier’s mind is on the road; I rise to see how late it is.

参辰皆已没，去去从此辞。

shēn chén jiē yǐ mò, qù qù cóng cǐ cí

The stars have all gone down. I must go — this is goodbye.

行役在战场，相见未有期。

xíng yì zài zhàn chǎng, xiāng jiàn wèi yǒu qī

I march away to the battlefield; who knows when we will meet again.

握手一长叹，泪为生别滋。

wò shǒu yī cháng tàn, lèi wèi shēng bié zī

We hold hands and sigh, and tears pour down for this living parting.

努力爱春华，莫忘欢乐时。

nǔ lì ài chūn huá, mò wàng huān lè shí

Take care of your youth while it blooms, and don’t forget our happy days.

生当复来归，死当长相思。

shēng dāng fù lái guī, sǐ dāng cháng xiāng sī

If I live, I will come home to you; if I die, I will miss you forever.
