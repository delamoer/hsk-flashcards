导读About this poem

The most famous aria in Chinese opera. A sixteen-year-old girl, kept indoors all her life, steps into her family’s garden for the first time, sees it full of spring flowers and no one there to enjoy them — and suddenly feels her own youth slipping away.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这是明代戏曲《牡丹亭》里最有名的一段唱词。女主角杜丽娘是官家小姐，十六岁了还从没进过自家的后花园。这一天她第一次走进园子，看到满园的花已经开遍，“姹紫嫣红”，美得惊人；可是这些花开在破井和倒塌的矮墙旁边，没有人来欣赏。她马上想到自己：美好的青春也像这些花一样，被关在深闺里，白白地过去了。“良辰美景奈何天，赏心乐事谁家院”是借景抒情（借写景物来表达感情）：好时光、好风景都在，可是叫人无可奈何。后面几句写朝霞、晚云、细雨、轻风、湖上的画船，画面越美，越显得“锦屏人”——关在屏风里的闺中人——把这春光看得太轻了。这段唱词写出了一个少女对青春和自由的觉醒，四百年来在昆曲舞台上一直被演唱。

In English

This is the best-known passage of The Peony Pavilion, a Ming-dynasty play. The heroine, Du Liniang, is the daughter of an official. At sixteen she has never been allowed into her own family’s back garden. One spring day she finally goes in — and finds it bursting with flowers, “brilliant purples and tender reds”, all blooming beside a ruined well and crumbling walls, with no one to look at them. At once she sees herself in them: her youth, too, is shut away and wasted. The famous couplet 良辰美景奈何天，赏心乐事谁家院 says it all: the lovely day and the lovely view are here, yet what can one do with them? Whose garden ever gets to enjoy such pleasures? This is “expressing feeling through scenery”: the flowers carry her sadness for her. The last lines paint a dreamy scene — morning clouds flying, evening mists rolling, threads of rain, wisps of wind, painted boats on misty water — and the more beautiful it gets, the sharper her complaint: people living behind brocade screens think far too little of this spring light. The aria captures a young woman waking up to beauty, desire and the shortness of youth. It has been sung on the Kunqu opera stage for four hundred years, and many Chinese people can hum its first line.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Tang Xianzu (1550–1616) was a Ming official and playwright, sometimes compared with his contemporary Shakespeare. The Peony Pavilion (finished in 1598) tells of a girl who dies of longing for a man she met in a dream and is brought back to life by love. This aria comes from the scene “Waking from a Dream” (惊梦).

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

姹紫嫣红 chà zǐ yān hóng；颓垣 tuí yuán；赏心乐事 lè；朝飞暮卷 zhāo；忒 tè（这里是“太”的意思）；看的 kàn de（“的”同“得”）；韶光 sháo。“朝”“忒”“垣”机器朗读容易读错，已在“朗读用字”列替换，音频需人工听验。

版本说明 About the text

节选自《牡丹亭》第十出《惊梦》中的【皂罗袍】一支。文字与维基文库所收《牡丹亭·惊梦》一致。原剧本在“赏心乐事谁家院”之后还有杜丽娘一句念白“恁般景致，我老爷和奶奶再不提起”，网站不录；昆曲舞台演唱时“赏心乐事”前常加衬字“便”。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

原来姹紫嫣红开遍，

yuán lái chà zǐ yān hóng kāi biàn

So this is it — purple and crimson blooming everywhere,

似这般都付与断井颓垣。

sì zhè bān dōu fù yǔ duàn jǐng tuí yuán

and all of it given away to a broken well and fallen walls.

良辰美景奈何天，

liáng chén měi jǐng nài hé tiān

A perfect hour, a perfect view — and nothing I can do with them;

赏心乐事谁家院！

shǎng xīn lè shì shuí jiā yuàn

joy and delight — whose garden ever gets to keep them?

朝飞暮卷，云霞翠轩；

zhāo fēi mù juǎn, yún xiá cuì xuān

Clouds drift at dawn and curl at dusk around the green-roofed pavilion;

雨丝风片，烟波画船——

yǔ sī fēng piàn, yān bō huà chuán

threads of rain, wisps of wind, a painted boat on misty water —

锦屏人忒看的这韶光贱！

jǐn píng rén tè kàn de zhè sháo guāng jiàn

and we who live behind silk screens think this spring light is worth nothing!
