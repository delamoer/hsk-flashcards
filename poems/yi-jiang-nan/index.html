导读About this poem

Five short lines, one burst of colour: an old poet remembers spring south of the Yangtze — river flowers redder than fire, water greener than dye — and asks how anyone could forget it.

中文解析

白居易年轻时在江南住过，后来又在杭州、苏州做过官。晚年住在洛阳，他回想起江南，写下这首小词。开头直接说“江南好”，三个字就是全词的感情。“旧曾谙”是说：这些风景我以前很熟悉。中间两句是全词最有名的画面：太阳出来，江边的花比火还红；春天来了，江水绿得像蓝草染出来的颜色。一红一绿，对比鲜明，这种两句字数相同、意思相对的写法叫“对偶”。最后用一个问句结尾：“能不忆江南？”——怎么能不想念江南呢？用反问代替直说，感情更强烈。全词只有二十七个字，语言像口语一样简单，却让人一下子看见江南的春天。

In English

Bai Juyi lived in the south as a young man and later served as governor of Hangzhou and Suzhou. In old age, living in Luoyang in the north, he looked back and wrote this little song lyric. It begins bluntly: “The South is lovely” — those three characters hold the whole feeling. 旧曾谙 means “I once knew it well.” The middle two lines are the famous picture: at sunrise the riverside flowers are redder than fire; in spring the river turns a green deeper than indigo dye. Red against green, line against line — two lines of equal length that mirror each other are called duìǒu (parallel couplets), a favourite device of Chinese poetry. The poem ends with a question instead of a statement: “How could I not remember the South?” Asking it this way makes the longing stronger than simply saying “I miss it.” With only twenty-seven characters and words as plain as speech, it lets you see a southern spring in a single glance.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Bai Juyi (772–846) was one of the Tang dynasty’s most popular poets, famous for writing so plainly that ordinary people could follow him. He governed Hangzhou (822–824) and Suzhou (825–826); he is said to have written this set of three lyrics around 838, in his sixties, while living in Luoyang.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

江南好，

jiāng nán hǎo

Oh, the South is lovely —

风景旧曾谙。

fēng jǐng jiù céng ān

I knew its views by heart.

日出江花红胜火，

rì chū jiāng huā hóng shèng huǒ

At sunrise, flowers by the river burn redder than flame;

春来江水绿如蓝。

chūn lái jiāng shuǐ lǜ rú lán

in spring, the river runs green as indigo dye.

能不忆江南？

néng bù yì jiāng nán

How could I not remember the South?
