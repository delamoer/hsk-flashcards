The Ballad of Mulan

导读About this poem

Yes, this is the original Mulan — the folk song behind the Disney film. A girl takes her old father’s place in the army, fights for twelve years, comes home, puts her dress back on, and stuns her comrades.

第一段Part 1
01
唧唧复唧唧，木兰当户织。

直译LiteralClick-clack, again click-clack — Mulan weaves facing the door.

意译MeaningClick-clack, click-clack goes the loom: Mulan sits weaving by the door.

02
不闻机杼声，唯闻女叹息。

直译LiteralNo sound of the loom and shuttle is heard; only the girl’s sighs are heard.

意译MeaningThen the loom falls silent — all you can hear is the girl sighing.

03
问女何所思，问女何所忆。

直译LiteralAsk the girl what she is thinking of, ask the girl what she is remembering.

意译Meaning“What are you thinking of, girl? What is on your mind?”

04
女亦无所思，女亦无所忆。

直译LiteralThe girl is thinking of nothing, the girl is remembering nothing.

意译Meaning“I’m not thinking of anything, nothing is on my mind.”

05
昨夜见军帖，可汗大点兵，

直译LiteralLast night I saw the army notice: the Khan is calling up soldiers on a large scale.

意译Meaning“Last night I saw the army notice — the Khan is calling up men.

06
军书十二卷，卷卷有爷名。

直译LiteralThe army lists are twelve scrolls; every scroll has Father’s name.

意译MeaningOn every one of the twelve scrolls, there is Father’s name.

07
阿爷无大儿，木兰无长兄，

直译LiteralFather has no grown son; Mulan has no elder brother.

意译MeaningFather has no grown-up son, and I have no older brother.

08
愿为市鞍马，从此替爷征。

直译LiteralI wish to buy a saddle and horse for this, and from now on march in Father’s place.

意译MeaningI will buy a horse and saddle, and go to war in Father’s place.”

第二段Part 2
09
开我东阁门，坐我西阁床。

直译LiteralI open the door of my east room, I sit on the bed of my west room.

意译MeaningI open the door of my old room and sit on my own bed again.

10
脱我战时袍，著我旧时裳。

直译LiteralI take off my wartime robe, I put on my old-time clothes.

意译MeaningI take off my soldier’s coat and put on the clothes I used to wear.

11
当窗理云鬓，对镜帖花黄。

直译LiteralFacing the window I arrange my cloud-like hair; facing the mirror I stick on a yellow flower.

意译MeaningBy the window I do my hair; at the mirror I put on a golden flower.

12
出门看火伴，火伴皆惊忙：

直译LiteralI go out to see my comrades; my comrades are all startled and flustered:

意译MeaningI go out to see my comrades — and they all stare in shock:

13
同行十二年，不知木兰是女郎。

直译LiteralIn the same ranks for twelve years, we did not know Mulan was a girl.

意译Meaning“Twelve years in the army together, and we never knew Mulan was a girl!”

第三段Part 3
14
雄兔脚扑朔，雌兔眼迷离；

直译LiteralThe male rabbit’s feet kick about; the female rabbit’s eyes are half-closed;

意译MeaningThe buck kicks his feet, the doe narrows her eyes —

15
双兔傍地走，安能辨我是雄雌？

直译LiteralWhen two rabbits run side by side along the ground, how can you tell whether I am male or female?

意译Meaningbut when two rabbits race side by side, who can tell which one I am?

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这是中国最有名的叙事诗之一，讲的就是花木兰代父从军的故事，迪士尼电影《花木兰》就是根据它改编的。本页选了三段。第一段：木兰在家织布，却不停叹气。原来是皇帝要征兵，每一卷名册上都有父亲的名字，可父亲年老，家里又没有成年的儿子，于是木兰决定买马备鞍，替父亲出征。“问女何所思，问女何所忆”一问一答反复出现，这是民歌常用的“重复”，读起来像唱歌。第二段：打了十二年仗回到家，木兰打开房门，脱下战袍，换上女装，对着镜子梳头、贴花黄。连用好几个“我”，写出她回家的轻松和高兴。出门见到战友，大家都惊呆了：一起打仗十二年，竟然不知道木兰是女孩子！第三段是一个比喻：兔子跑起来的时候，谁分得清是雄是雌？意思是女子的本领一点也不比男子差。

In English

This is one of the most famous narrative poems in Chinese, and yes, it is the source of Disney’s Mulan. This page gives three parts of it. In the first, Mulan sits weaving but keeps sighing. The emperor is calling up soldiers, and her father’s name is on every scroll of the army list — but he is old and there is no grown-up son. So Mulan decides to buy a horse and saddle and go in his place. Notice the question-and-answer that repeats almost word for word (“What are you thinking of? What are you remembering?”): repetition like this is typical of folk songs and makes them easy to sing. In the second part she comes home after twelve years of war. She opens her old room, takes off her armour, puts on her old clothes, does her hair at the window and sticks a yellow flower decoration on her forehead. The string of “my… my… my…” sounds light and happy. When she steps outside, her army comrades are stunned: twelve years together, and no one knew Mulan was a girl! The last two lines are a riddle-like comparison: when two rabbits run side by side, who can tell which is male and which is female? In other words, a woman can do anything a man can.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

The poem is an anonymous folk song from the Northern Dynasties (4th–6th centuries), when nomadic peoples ruled north China — which is why the ruler is called both “Khan” (可汗) and “Son of Heaven”. It was probably polished by later writers and is first recorded in a Song-dynasty collection of yuefu songs.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

可汗 kè hán（古代北方民族对君主的称呼，不读 kě hàn）；军帖 tiě；军书十二卷 juàn；愿为市鞍马 wèi（替）；阿爷 yé；木兰无长兄 zhǎng；著我旧时裳 zhuó、cháng；对镜帖花黄 tiē（同“贴”）；双兔傍地走 bàng。“同行十二年”的“行”，统编教材注 háng（行伍、军队），本页采用 háng；也有读 xíng（同行走）的。

版本说明 About the text

采用统编语文教材七年级下册文本。全诗六十二句，三百余字，本页节选三部分：开头十六句（“唧唧复唧唧”到“从此替爷征”，第1段）、回家换装十句（“开我东阁门”到“不知木兰是女郎”，第2段）和结尾四句（“雄兔脚扑朔”到“安能辨我是雄雌”，第3段）。异文：“唯闻”一作“惟闻”；“火伴”一作“伙伴”；“惊忙”一作“惊惶”；“双兔”一作“两兔”。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

唧唧复唧唧，木兰当户织。

jī jī fù jī jī, mù lán dāng hù zhī

Click-clack, click-clack goes the loom: Mulan sits weaving by the door.

不闻机杼声，唯闻女叹息。

bù wén jī zhù shēng, wéi wén nǚ tàn xī

Then the loom falls silent — all you can hear is the girl sighing.

问女何所思，问女何所忆。

wèn nǚ hé suǒ sī, wèn nǚ hé suǒ yì

“What are you thinking of, girl? What is on your mind?”

女亦无所思，女亦无所忆。

nǚ yì wú suǒ sī, nǚ yì wú suǒ yì

“I’m not thinking of anything, nothing is on my mind.”

昨夜见军帖，可汗大点兵，

zuó yè jiàn jūn tiě, kè hán dà diǎn bīng

“Last night I saw the army notice — the Khan is calling up men.

军书十二卷，卷卷有爷名。

jūn shū shí èr juàn, juàn juàn yǒu yé míng

On every one of the twelve scrolls, there is Father’s name.

阿爷无大儿，木兰无长兄，

ā yé wú dà ér, mù lán wú zhǎng xiōng

Father has no grown-up son, and I have no older brother.

愿为市鞍马，从此替爷征。

yuàn wèi shì ān mǎ, cóng cǐ tì yé zhēng

I will buy a horse and saddle, and go to war in Father’s place.”

开我东阁门，坐我西阁床。

kāi wǒ dōng gé mén, zuò wǒ xī gé chuáng

I open the door of my old room and sit on my own bed again.

脱我战时袍，著我旧时裳。

tuō wǒ zhàn shí páo, zhuó wǒ jiù shí cháng

I take off my soldier’s coat and put on the clothes I used to wear.

当窗理云鬓，对镜帖花黄。

dāng chuāng lǐ yún bìn, duì jìng tiē huā huáng

By the window I do my hair; at the mirror I put on a golden flower.

出门看火伴，火伴皆惊忙：

chū mén kàn huǒ bàn, huǒ bàn jiē jīng máng

I go out to see my comrades — and they all stare in shock:

同行十二年，不知木兰是女郎。

tóng háng shí èr nián, bù zhī mù lán shì nǚ láng

“Twelve years in the army together, and we never knew Mulan was a girl!”

雄兔脚扑朔，雌兔眼迷离；

xióng tù jiǎo pū shuò, cí tù yǎn mí lí

The buck kicks his feet, the doe narrows her eyes —

双兔傍地走，安能辨我是雄雌？

shuāng tù bàng dì zǒu, ān néng biàn wǒ shì xióng cí

but when two rabbits race side by side, who can tell which one I am?
