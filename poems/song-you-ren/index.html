导读About this poem

Green hills, a white river, a drifting cloud and the setting sun: Li Bai says goodbye to a friend outside the city walls, and even the horses whinny as they part.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这是一首送别诗。开头两句先画出送别的地方：青山横卧在城北，白亮的河水绕着城东流过，颜色一青一白，对得很工整。接着说：在这里一分别，朋友就要像一棵被风吹断的蓬草，独自飘到万里之外。第三联是名句：天上飘浮的白云，像游子漂泊不定的心意；慢慢落下的太阳，像老朋友依依不舍的感情——景物和感情合在一起，这叫“借景抒情”。最后两人挥手告别，连他们骑的马也不愿分开，萧萧地叫着。诗人没有写眼泪，只写了马的叫声，离别的难过却更深了。

In English

This is a farewell poem. The first two lines set the scene: green hills lie across the north of the city, and a bright river winds past its east side — green against white, the two lines perfectly matched. Then the moment of parting: once they separate here, his friend will be like a tumbleweed (蓬), torn loose by the wind and blown ten thousand miles alone. The third couplet is the famous one. The drifting cloud is the traveller’s restless mind; the slowly setting sun is the old friend’s reluctance to let go. Scenery and feeling become one — Chinese critics call this “expressing feeling through scenery.” Finally they wave and ride off, and even their horses, now separated from each other, neigh mournfully. There are no tears in the poem, only the sound of the horses, and that makes the sadness sharper. The couplets are carefully balanced, as the regulated-verse form requires, yet the language stays plain and natural.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Li Bai (701–762) wrote many farewell poems during his years of wandering. We do not know who this friend was or exactly where they parted; it is traditionally dated to his travels in the lower Yangtze region.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

青山横北郭，

qīng shān héng běi guō

Green hills stretch beyond the northern wall;

白水绕东城。

bái shuǐ rào dōng chéng

bright water curls around the eastern town.

此地一为别，

cǐ dì yī wéi bié

Once we part in this place,

孤蓬万里征。

gū péng wàn lǐ zhēng

you’ll be a lone tumbleweed blown a thousand miles.

浮云游子意，

fú yún yóu zǐ yì

The drifting cloud is your wandering heart;

落日故人情。

luò rì gù rén qíng

the lingering sunset, the love of an old friend.

挥手自兹去，

huī shǒu zì zī qù

We wave, and from here you ride away —

萧萧班马鸣。

xiāo xiāo bān mǎ míng

our horses, pulled apart, cry out to each other.
