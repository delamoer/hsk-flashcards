导读About this poem

Back in the north, Du Mu sends a teasing note to an old colleague still enjoying Yangzhou: on a moonlit night like this, where are you teaching some beauty to play the flute? Fond, playful, and full of longing for the south.

中文解析

杜牧离开扬州后，写诗寄给还在扬州做判官的老同事韩绰。前两句写远望：青山隐隐约约，江水长长远远；已经是深秋了，江南的草木还没有凋零。这既是想象中的扬州风景，也暗含对那里的留恋。“隐隐”“迢迢”两个叠词，读起来有一种悠长的味道。后两句转到一个画面：二十四桥上，明月照着夜晚，你这位“玉人”此刻在哪里教美人吹箫呢？这是朋友之间亲切的玩笑，也是对当年一起度过的扬州夜晚的怀念。“二十四桥明月夜”后来成了扬州的代名词，宋代姜夔还写了“二十四桥仍在，波心荡，冷月无声”。全诗轻快、风趣，又带着淡淡的思念。

In English

After leaving Yangzhou, Du Mu sent this poem to Han Chuo, a former colleague still serving there as a magistrate. The first two lines look into the distance: green hills faint on the horizon, the river stretching far away; autumn is ending, yet the grass of the south has not withered. This is Yangzhou as he imagines it, and the warmth of the scene hints at how much he misses it. The doubled words 隐隐 yǐnyǐn (“faint”) and 迢迢 tiáotiáo (“far, far away”) give the lines a long, drawn-out sound. Then the poem cuts to a single picture: a moonlit night on the Twenty-Four Bridges — and where, Du Mu asks, is his handsome friend teaching some girl to play the flute? It is an affectionate joke between friends, and also nostalgia for the evenings they spent together in the city. “The Twenty-Four Bridges on a moonlit night” became a byword for Yangzhou itself; centuries later the Song poet Jiang Kui still wrote of the bridges and the silent cold moon. Light, witty and a little wistful, the poem shows how friendship can be expressed through teasing.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Du Mu (803–c. 852) served on the governor’s staff in Yangzhou from 833 to 835, where Han Chuo was a colleague. The poem was probably written soon after Du Mu left for the capital.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

迢迢 tiáo tiáo；绰 chuò。教：按“教人吹箫”理解读 jiāo，网站采用此读；若理解为“使、让”则读 jiào，部分注本如此。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行教材版本。“秋尽江南草未凋”一作“秋尽江南草木凋”；“二十四桥”一说指扬州城内二十四座桥，一说是一座桥名（又称红药桥），解析里不展开。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

青山隐隐水迢迢，

qīng shān yǐn yǐn shuǐ tiáo tiáo

Green hills fade into the distance, the river runs on and on;

秋尽江南草未凋。

qiū jìn jiāng nán cǎo wèi diāo

autumn is over, yet the southern grass is still green.

二十四桥明月夜，

èr shí sì qiáo míng yuè yè

On the Twenty-Four Bridges, this moonlit night,

玉人何处教吹箫？

yù rén hé chù jiāo chuī xiāo

where are you, my handsome friend, teaching someone the flute?
