导读About this poem

A painter famous for bamboo looks at this year’s new shoots, already taller than the old stalks — and sees a lesson about the young and the old helping each other.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

郑板桥一生爱画竹、写竹。这首诗写新竹：新长出来的竹子比老竹子还高，但它能长这么高，全靠老竹的枝干扶持。后两句往前看：明年还会有新竹长出来，长到十丈高，绕满整个池塘。“龙孙”是竹笋、新竹的美称，“凤池”是池塘的美称，一龙一凤，显得新竹生机勃勃。表面写竹，其实写人：年轻一代超过老一辈，是因为老一辈的扶持；而一代又一代传下去，事业才越来越兴旺。这种借写景物来讲道理的写法叫“托物言志”。诗的语言很浅，适合初学者读，也常被老师用来说师生、父子之间的关系。

In English

Zheng Banqiao spent his life painting and writing about bamboo. Here he notices that the new bamboo has grown taller than the old — yet it could only grow so tall because the old stalks held it up. Then he looks ahead: next year more shoots will come, rising ten zhang high (over thirty metres, a deliberate exaggeration) and circling the whole pond. “Dragon grandchildren” is a fond name for bamboo shoots and “phoenix pool” a fine name for a pond, so the poem ends with dragons and phoenixes — full of life. On the surface it is about plants; underneath it is about people. The young surpass the old because the old support them, and each generation passes things on to the next. Saying something about life through an object like this is called tuō wù yán zhì, “using a thing to express an idea”. The words are simple, which makes it a good poem for beginners, and teachers often quote it when talking about students and teachers, or children and parents.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Zheng Xie (1693–1765), better known as Zheng Banqiao, was a Qing official, calligrapher and painter, one of the “Eight Eccentrics of Yangzhou”. Bamboo, orchids and rocks were his favourite subjects, and many of his poems were written on his own paintings.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

干：老干 lǎo gàn（树干的“干”，不读 gān）。为：为扶持 wéi（作为、成为之义，口语也有读 wèi 的）。机器朗读已替换。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行文本。维基文库所据本第三句作“下年再有新生者”，网站从通行本作“明年”。郑燮生卒年一般作 1693–1765（或 1766）。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

新竹高于旧竹枝，

xīn zhú gāo yú jiù zhú zhī

The new bamboo stands taller than the old,

全凭老干为扶持。

quán píng lǎo gàn wéi fú chí

but only because the old stems held it up.

明年再有新生者，

míng nián zài yǒu xīn shēng zhě

Next year, when new shoots rise again,

十丈龙孙绕凤池。

shí zhàng lóng sūn rào fèng chí

a forest of young dragons will ring the pool.
