导读About this poem

Three questions asked from a riverside tower, three short answers. Looking north at land lost to the enemy, Xin Qiji praises the young Sun Quan — and quietly asks where today’s heroes are.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首词也写在镇江北固楼上，和《永遇乐》差不多同时，但短小明快。全词用三个问题组织起来：哪里能望见中原？——满眼只有北固楼的风光，北方的故土望不见。千古以来有多少兴亡大事？——悠悠，说不完，就像不停滚滚东流的长江。天下英雄谁是孙权的对手？——只有曹操和刘备。一问一答，像在跟读者聊天。下阕赞美三国时的孙权：年纪轻轻就统领千军万马，占据东南，不停地打仗，连曹操都说“生子当如孙仲谋”——生儿子就该生孙权这样的。表面上夸古人，其实是对比南宋朝廷：如今占着东南，却没有人敢像孙权那样抗敌。“不尽长江滚滚流”一句借用杜甫“不尽长江滚滚来”，把历史和江水写在一起，气象很大。

In English

Written at the same tower in Zhenjiang around the same time as “Eternal Joy”, this lyric is shorter and brisker. It is built on three questions, each with a quick answer. Where can I see the lost central plains? From Beigu Tower all I see is the view around me — the north is out of sight. How many rises and falls have there been through the ages? Endless, endless — like the Yangtze rolling past without end. Who among the heroes of the world could match Sun Quan? Only Cao Cao and Liu Bei. The question-and-answer style makes it feel like a conversation. The second half praises Sun Quan of the Three Kingdoms: still a young man, he commanded a huge army, held the southeast and kept on fighting. Even his enemy Cao Cao is said to have sighed, “If you have a son, he should be like Sun Zhongmou.” On the surface this is praise for a figure of the past; underneath it is a comparison with Xin’s own Southern Song court, which also held the southeast but had no one willing to fight like Sun Quan. The line about the endless Yangtze echoes Du Fu, tying the flow of history to the flow of the river.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Xin Qiji (1140–1207) served as prefect of Zhenjiang (ancient Jingkou) in 1204–1205, when the court was preparing to attack the Jin. Beigu Hill and its tower overlook the Yangtze; across the river lay the north he had spent his life hoping to recover.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

兴亡 xīng wáng；年少 shào；兜鍪 dōu móu（头盔，生僻字）；坐断 zuò duàn；当如 dāng rú；长江 cháng。机器朗读“鍪”可能读错，已在“朗读用字”列替换同音字。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

何处望神州？

hé chù wàng shén zhōu

Where can I look out and see our lost homeland?

满眼风光北固楼。

mǎn yǎn fēng guāng běi gù lóu

All I see is the view from Beigu Tower.

千古兴亡多少事？悠悠。

qiān gǔ xīng wáng duō shǎo shì? yōu yōu

How many rises and falls through the ages? No end to them —

不尽长江滚滚流。

bù jìn cháng jiāng gǔn gǔn liú

like the Yangtze, rolling on and on.

年少万兜鍪，

nián shào wàn dōu móu

Still young, he led ten thousand helmets,

坐断东南战未休。

zuò duàn dōng nán zhàn wèi xiū

held the southeast and never stopped fighting.

天下英雄谁敌手？曹刘。

tiān xià yīng xióng shuí dí shǒu? cáo liú

Who in the world could match him? Only Cao Cao and Liu Bei.

生子当如孙仲谋。

shēng zǐ dāng rú sūn zhòng móu

“A son should be like Sun Quan.”
