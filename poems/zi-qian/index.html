导读About this poem

A poet who failed the exams again and again tells himself to stop worrying: “If there’s wine today, get drunk today; if trouble comes tomorrow, worry tomorrow.” The third line became an everyday saying.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

“自遣”就是自己安慰自己、排解心里的烦恼。诗人罗隐考了十次科举都没考上，这首诗像是他对自己说的话。第一句：得到了就放声高歌，失去了就算了，不要放在心上。第二句：愁也好，恨也好，都让它慢慢过去吧。后两句最有名：“今朝有酒今朝醉，明日愁来明日愁”——今天有酒，今天就喝个痛快；明天的烦恼，等明天来了再说。诗句用重复的词（今朝、明日、愁），读起来顺口好记，所以“今朝有酒今朝醉”成了今天还常说的俗语，不过现在多带一点批评，指只顾眼前、不考虑将来。读这首诗时要知道背后的心酸：表面潇洒，其实是一个怀才不遇的人在苦中作乐。

In English

The title means “to console oneself” — to talk oneself out of one’s troubles. Luo Yin failed the imperial exam about ten times, and the poem reads like advice he is giving himself. Line one: when you win, sing out loud; when you lose, let it go. Line two: all the worry and resentment — let it drift away. Then come the famous lines: “If there’s wine today, get drunk today; if worries come tomorrow, worry tomorrow.” The repeated words (today, tomorrow, worry) give the lines a bouncy, easy rhythm, which is why 今朝有酒今朝醉 became a common saying. Today, though, people usually use it with a hint of criticism, for someone who lives only for the moment and doesn’t plan ahead. Reading the poem, remember the sadness underneath: the carefree tone is the brave face of a gifted man who never got his chance, finding what joy he can in hard times.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Luo Yin (833–909) is said to have failed the civil service exam around ten times over many years; he even changed his given name to Yin (“hidden”) in frustration. This short poem shows his mix of humour and bitterness.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

得即高歌失即休，

dé jí gāo gē shī jí xiū

Win, and I sing out loud; lose, and I let it go.

多愁多恨亦悠悠。

duō chóu duō hèn yì yōu yōu

All my worries and regrets can drift away.

今朝有酒今朝醉，

jīn zhāo yǒu jiǔ jīn zhāo zuì

If there’s wine today, I’ll be drunk today;

明日愁来明日愁。

míng rì chóu lái míng rì chóu

if trouble comes tomorrow, tomorrow I’ll worry.
