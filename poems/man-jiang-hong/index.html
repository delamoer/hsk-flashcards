导读About this poem

The most famous patriotic poem in Chinese, traditionally credited to the general Yue Fei: rage at the loss of the north, thirty years of fighting counted as dust, and a vow to win back the old homeland.

上阕First stanza
01
怒发冲冠，凭栏处、潇潇雨歇。

直译LiteralAngry hair pushes up my cap; where I lean on the railing, the pattering rain stops.

意译MeaningSo angry my hair lifts my cap — I lean on the rail as the driving rain dies away.

02
抬望眼、仰天长啸，壮怀激烈。

直译LiteralRaising my gazing eyes, I look up at the sky and give a long cry; my heroic heart surges fiercely.

意译MeaningI lift my eyes and roar at the sky, my heart on fire.

03
三十功名尘与土，八千里路云和月。

直译LiteralThirty years of merit and fame — dust and earth; eight thousand li of road — cloud and moon.

意译MeaningThirty years of honours are so much dust; eight thousand miles marched under cloud and moon.

04
莫等闲、白了少年头，空悲切。

直译LiteralDo not idle, letting a young man’s head turn white, grieving in vain.

意译MeaningDon’t let the years slip by till your young hair is white and all that’s left is regret.

下阕Second stanza
05
靖康耻，犹未雪。臣子恨，何时灭。

直译LiteralThe shame of Jingkang is still not washed away; the subject’s hatred — when will it end?

意译MeaningThe shame of Jingkang is still not avenged. When will a loyal man’s anger be put out?

06
驾长车，踏破贺兰山缺。

直译LiteralDriving long chariots, I will trample through the gaps of the Helan Mountains.

意译MeaningI’ll drive the war chariots through the passes of the Helan Mountains.

07
壮志饥餐胡虏肉，笑谈渴饮匈奴血。

直译LiteralWith heroic will, hungry I eat the invaders’ flesh; laughing and talking, thirsty I drink the Xiongnu’s blood.

意译MeaningHungry, I’ll feed on the invaders’ flesh; thirsty, I’ll laugh and drink their blood.

08
待从头、收拾旧山河，朝天阙。

直译LiteralWait until, from the beginning, I recover the old mountains and rivers, then pay homage at Heaven’s gate.

意译MeaningAnd when I have won back our old land, every river and hill, I will go to the palace and report.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首词写的是收复失地的决心。1127年金兵攻破北宋都城，俘虏了两位皇帝，史称“靖康之耻”。上阕：一场急雨刚停，作者靠着栏杆，愤怒得头发都要竖起来顶起帽子；他抬头向天长啸，心中豪情激荡。三十年的功名不过是尘土，八千里的征战伴着云和月。不要虚度光阴，等到少年人头发白了，只能白白悲伤。下阕直接写国耻：靖康之耻还没洗雪，做臣子的恨什么时候才能消？要驾着战车踏破敌人的关口，饿了吃敌人的肉、渴了喝敌人的血——这是夸张的说法，表现恨之深。最后发誓：等到从头收复旧日的山河，再去朝见皇帝。全词感情激烈，节奏短促有力，几百年来成了中国人最熟悉的爱国诗词。“莫等闲，白了少年头”今天常用来劝年轻人珍惜时间。

In English

This lyric is a vow to win back lost land. In 1127 the Jurchen Jin captured the Northern Song capital, Kaifeng, and carried off two emperors — the “Humiliation of Jingkang”. The first half opens just after a burst of rain. Leaning on a railing, the speaker is so angry his hair seems to stand up and lift his cap. He looks up and gives a long cry to the sky. Thirty years of rank and fame are only dust; eight thousand lǐ of campaigning have passed under clouds and moon. Don’t waste your time, he warns, or your young head will turn white with nothing to show but grief. The second half names the shame directly: Jingkang is not yet avenged, so when will a loyal subject’s hatred end? He will drive war chariots through the enemy’s mountain passes, eat their flesh when hungry and drink their blood when thirsty — deliberate exaggeration that shows how deep the anger runs. Finally: once the old rivers and mountains are recovered, he will go to court and report to the emperor. The short, hammering lines make it a poem people shout as much as read. The line 莫等闲，白了少年头 is still used today to tell young people not to waste their years.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Yue Fei (1103–1142) was a Southern Song general famed for his campaigns against the Jin; he was executed on false charges and later honoured as a national hero. The lyric is traditionally attributed to him, but no copy is known before the Ming dynasty (about 350 years after his death), so many scholars doubt he wrote it; the question is still debated.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

怒发 fà（头发）；冲冠 guān；凭栏处 chù；少年 shào；悲切 qiè；朝天阙 cháo què；血 xuè。“驾长车”的“车”一般读 chē，旧读 jū。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行文本。“凭栏”一作“凭阑”；“朝天阙”早期本作“朝金阙”（据维基文库注，约明中后期改为“天”）。作者归属有争议：此词最早见于明代，宋元文献未载，学界有真伪之争，网站按传统说法署岳飞。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

怒发冲冠，凭栏处、潇潇雨歇。

nù fà chōng guān, píng lán chù, xiāo xiāo yǔ xiē

So angry my hair lifts my cap — I lean on the rail as the driving rain dies away.

抬望眼、仰天长啸，壮怀激烈。

tái wàng yǎn, yǎng tiān cháng xiào, zhuàng huái jī liè

I lift my eyes and roar at the sky, my heart on fire.

三十功名尘与土，八千里路云和月。

sān shí gōng míng chén yǔ tǔ, bā qiān lǐ lù yún hé yuè

Thirty years of honours are so much dust; eight thousand miles marched under cloud and moon.

莫等闲、白了少年头，空悲切。

mò děng xián, bái le shào nián tóu, kōng bēi qiè

Don’t let the years slip by till your young hair is white and all that’s left is regret.

靖康耻，犹未雪。臣子恨，何时灭。

jìng kāng chǐ, yóu wèi xuě, chén zǐ hèn, hé shí miè

The shame of Jingkang is still not avenged. When will a loyal man’s anger be put out?

驾长车，踏破贺兰山缺。

jià cháng chē, tà pò hè lán shān quē

I’ll drive the war chariots through the passes of the Helan Mountains.

壮志饥餐胡虏肉，笑谈渴饮匈奴血。

zhuàng zhì jī cān hú lǔ ròu, xiào tán kě yǐn xiōng nú xuè

Hungry, I’ll feed on the invaders’ flesh; thirsty, I’ll laugh and drink their blood.

待从头、收拾旧山河，朝天阙。

dài cóng tóu, shōu shí jiù shān hé, cháo tiān què

And when I have won back our old land, every river and hill, I will go to the palace and report.
