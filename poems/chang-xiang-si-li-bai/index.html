导读About this poem

On an autumn night in Chang’an a man lies awake longing for a beauty who seems as far away as the clouds. Sky above, waves below, mountain passes between — even his dreams can’t reach her.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗写一个人在长安的秋夜里，深深思念一位远方的美人。开头三个字“长相思”直接点题，然后写秋夜的景物：纺织娘在井栏边叫着，薄霜冷冷的，竹席也透出寒意；一盏孤灯昏暗不明，相思得快要断了气，卷起窗帘望着月亮，只能白白叹气。“美人如花隔云端”是全诗的中心：她美得像花，却像隔在云的那一头，看得见、够不着。下半首写距离：上有高远辽阔的青天，下有清水的波浪，天那么长、路那么远，连做梦都飞不过关山。最后又回到“长相思”，加上“摧心肝”，痛到心碎。诗的句子有长有短（三言、七言交错），读起来一会儿急一会儿缓，像心情在起伏。古人还认为“美人”可能是比喻君王或理想，表达诗人离开长安后得不到重用的苦闷。

In English

A man in Chang’an lies awake on an autumn night, longing for a beautiful woman far away. The poem opens with its title, “Endless longing — in Chang’an”, then sets the scene: a katydid chirps by the golden well-rail, a thin frost settles, even the bamboo mat feels cold. A single lamp burns dim; his longing is almost unbearable, and he rolls up the curtain to gaze at the moon, sighing for nothing. Then the heart of the poem: “My beauty, lovely as a flower, is beyond the clouds.” She is visible in his mind, but out of reach. The second half is all distance — the blue-black sky above, clear waves below, sky so wide and road so long that even his dreaming soul can’t fly over the mountain passes. It ends where it began, with “endless longing”, now “breaking my heart”. Short three-character lines alternate with long seven-character ones, so the rhythm hurries and slows like a troubled mind. Traditional readers often took the “beauty” as a symbol of the emperor or of the poet’s ideals, written after Li Bai had to leave the court in Chang’an. Either way, the longing is real.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Li Bai (701–762) spent less than two years at the imperial court in Chang’an before being sent away in 744. “Endless Longing” is an old yuefu title for love poems; this one is often thought to date from after his departure, though the date is uncertain.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

络纬 luò wěi；金井阑 lán；簟 diàn；思欲绝的“思”读 sī；卷帷 juǎn wéi；青冥 míng；渌水 lù shuǐ；摧 cuī；“长相思”“长安”“长叹”“长天”“天长”的“长”都读 cháng。机器朗读“簟”“渌”容易读错，已在“朗读用字”列替换同音字，音频需人工听验。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行本，为李白《长相思》二首之第一首（“长相思，在长安”），网站按句读排为十一行。异文：“微霜”一作“凝霜”，“孤灯不明”一作“孤灯不寐”，“美人如花”一作“佳期迢迢”。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

长相思，在长安。

cháng xiāng sī, zài cháng ān

Endless longing, here in Chang’an.

络纬秋啼金井阑，

luò wěi qiū tí jīn jǐng lán

A katydid sings its autumn song beside the golden well;

微霜凄凄簟色寒。

wēi shuāng qī qī diàn sè hán

a thin frost falls, chill, and even my bamboo mat feels cold.

孤灯不明思欲绝，

gū dēng bù míng sī yù jué

By one dim lamp my longing is near breaking;

卷帷望月空长叹。

juǎn wéi wàng yuè kōng cháng tàn

I roll up the curtain, look at the moon, and sigh — for nothing.

美人如花隔云端。

měi rén rú huā gé yún duān

My beauty, lovely as a flower, is far beyond the clouds.

上有青冥之长天，

shàng yǒu qīng míng zhī cháng tiān

Above me, the vast dark-blue sky;

下有渌水之波澜。

xià yǒu lù shuǐ zhī bō lán

below me, the waves of clear water.

天长路远魂飞苦，

tiān cháng lù yuǎn hún fēi kǔ

The sky so wide, the road so long — my soul tires as it flies,

梦魂不到关山难。

mèng hún bù dào guān shān nán

and even in dreams it can’t cross the mountain passes to her.

长相思，摧心肝。

cháng xiāng sī, cuī xīn gān

Endless longing tears my heart apart.
