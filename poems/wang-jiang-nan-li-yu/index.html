导读About this poem

Five short lines: a captive king dreams of an old spring outing in his royal park — carriages like a flowing river, horses like a dragon. The dream is bright and happy; the first word tells us what he feels on waking.

中文解析

这首小词只有二十七个字，是李煜被俘以后写的。第一句“多少恨”就把全词的感情说了出来：有多少遗憾啊！恨什么？“昨夜梦魂中”——昨天夜里做了一个梦。下面三句全写梦里的景象：好像又回到从前，在皇家园林“上苑”游玩，车子一辆接一辆，像流水一样不断；马一匹接一匹，像一条长龙；花正开，月正好，春风正暖。“车如流水马如龙”写得非常生动，今天还用来形容街上热闹的样子。妙的是，梦写得越热闹、越快乐，读者越能感到醒来以后的冷清和痛苦。词人没有写醒来以后怎么样，只在开头说了一个“恨”字，用快乐的梦来写悲哀，这叫“以乐景写哀情”。

In English

This little lyric has just 27 characters, written by Li Yu after he became a prisoner of the Song. The first words, 多少恨 (“how much regret!”), give away the feeling of the whole poem. Regret about what? “Last night, in a dream.” The rest describes the dream: he seems to be back in the old days, out on a spring outing in the royal park. Carriages roll by one after another like flowing water; horses follow each other like a long dragon; the flowers are in bloom, the moon is lovely, and the spring breeze is warm. The image 车如流水马如龙 is so vivid that people still use it today to describe busy, bustling streets. The clever part is this: the livelier and happier the dream, the more we feel how cold and painful the waking must have been. The poet never tells us what happened when he woke — he only puts the word 恨 at the very start. Using a joyful scene to express sorrow (以乐景写哀情) is a classic technique in Chinese poetry, and this is one of its simplest, clearest examples.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Li Yu (937–978) was the last ruler of the Southern Tang, with its capital at Jinling (today’s Nanjing). After surrendering to the Song in 975 he was held in Kaifeng, and wrote this looking back on his days as king.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

多少恨，昨夜梦魂中。

duō shǎo hèn, zuó yè mèng hún zhōng

So much regret — all from last night’s dream.

还似旧时游上苑，车如流水马如龙。

hái sì jiù shí yóu shàng yuàn, chē rú liú shuǐ mǎ rú lóng

It was the old days again, an outing in the royal park — carriages streaming like a river, horses winding like a dragon.

花月正春风。

huā yuè zhèng chūn fēng

Flowers, moonlight, and the spring wind at its best.
