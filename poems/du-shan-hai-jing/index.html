导读About this poem

Early summer, the trees around his cottage are thick and green, the planting is done, and the poet has time to read a book of strange myths. A small, sunny poem about the happiness of a quiet life.

01
孟夏草木长，绕屋树扶疏。

直译LiteralIn early summer grass and trees grow; around the house the trees are thick and spreading.

意译MeaningEarly summer: grass and trees grow tall, leafy branches crowding round the house.

02
众鸟欣有托，吾亦爱吾庐。

直译LiteralThe many birds are glad to have a place to rest; I too love my hut.

意译MeaningThe birds are glad to have a home — and I love my little cottage too.

03
既耕亦已种，时还读我书。

直译LiteralI have ploughed and also planted; at times I come back to read my books.

意译MeaningThe ploughing is done, the seeds are in; now and then I come home to my books.

04
穷巷隔深辙，颇回故人车。

直译LiteralMy remote lane is cut off from deep wheel-ruts; it often turns back old friends’ carriages.

意译MeaningMy lane is far from the deep ruts of the highway; even old friends’ carts tend to turn back.

05
欢言酌春酒，摘我园中蔬。

直译LiteralHappily I pour spring wine and pick vegetables from my garden.

意译MeaningCheerfully I pour some spring wine and pick greens from my own garden.

06
微雨从东来，好风与之俱。

直译LiteralA light rain comes from the east; a good wind comes along with it.

意译MeaningA fine rain drifts in from the east, and a kind breeze comes with it.

07
泛览周王传，流观山海图。

直译LiteralI browse the Tale of King Zhou; I glance over the pictures of Mountains and Seas.

意译MeaningI leaf through the story of King Mu and let my eyes roam the maps of Mountains and Seas.

08
俯仰终宇宙，不乐复何如？

直译LiteralLooking down and up, I cover the whole universe; if this is not joy, what is?

意译MeaningIn one glance down and up I travel the whole universe — what could be happier than this?

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这是陶渊明《读山海经》十三首的第一首，像一篇序：先写读书时的环境和心情，后面十二首才写书里的神话。初夏草木长得茂盛，树枝围着小屋；鸟儿有了可以栖身的树，诗人也喜爱自己的小屋——“众鸟欣有托，吾亦爱吾庐”，把自己和鸟放在一起，说明他像鸟回到树林一样，找到了属于自己的地方。农活干完了，才回来读书；住在偏僻的小巷，贵人的车子很少来。他一边喝自酿的春酒，一边摘园子里的菜，小雨和好风一起吹来。最后读着《穆天子传》，看着《山海经》的图，一低头一抬头之间，好像游遍了整个宇宙。全诗没有大事，只有平常的快乐，语言朴素自然，这正是陶渊明的风格。

In English

This is the first of Tao Yuanming’s thirteen poems “On Reading the Classic of Mountains and Seas”, and it works like a preface: before the other twelve dive into myths and monsters, this one shows us the reader and his mood. It is early summer; grass and trees have grown thick, and leafy branches wrap around his little house. “The birds are glad to have a place to rest — and I, too, love my cottage”: by putting himself beside the birds, he tells us he has found where he belongs, just as they have. The planting is finished, so now there is time to read. He lives down a remote lane whose deep ruts turn back the carriages of important visitors — he doesn’t mind. He pours homemade spring wine, picks vegetables from his garden, and a light rain arrives from the east with a pleasant breeze. Finally he leafs through the Tale of King Mu and the pictures of the Mountains and Seas, and in a glance up and down he has toured the whole universe. Nothing dramatic happens. The poem is made of ordinary pleasures told in plain words, and that plainness is exactly what later readers loved in Tao.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Tao Yuanming (c. 365–427) quit his last government post in 405 and spent the rest of his life farming in the countryside near Mount Lu. This series is thought to date from those farming years; the Classic of Mountains and Seas is an ancient book of geography, myths and strange creatures.

版本说明 About the text

采用通行本，全诗八联十六句，为《读山海经》十三首之第一首。“欢言酌春酒”一作“欢然酌春酒”。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

孟夏草木长，绕屋树扶疏。

mèng xià cǎo mù zhǎng, rào wū shù fú shū

Early summer: grass and trees grow tall, leafy branches crowding round the house.

众鸟欣有托，吾亦爱吾庐。

zhòng niǎo xīn yǒu tuō, wú yì ài wú lú

The birds are glad to have a home — and I love my little cottage too.

既耕亦已种，时还读我书。

jì gēng yì yǐ zhòng, shí huán dú wǒ shū

The ploughing is done, the seeds are in; now and then I come home to my books.

穷巷隔深辙，颇回故人车。

qióng xiàng gé shēn zhé, pō huí gù rén chē

My lane is far from the deep ruts of the highway; even old friends’ carts tend to turn back.

欢言酌春酒，摘我园中蔬。

huān yán zhuó chūn jiǔ, zhāi wǒ yuán zhōng shū

Cheerfully I pour some spring wine and pick greens from my own garden.

微雨从东来，好风与之俱。

wēi yǔ cóng dōng lái, hǎo fēng yǔ zhī jù

A fine rain drifts in from the east, and a kind breeze comes with it.

泛览周王传，流观山海图。

fàn lǎn zhōu wáng zhuàn, liú guān shān hǎi tú

I leaf through the story of King Mu and let my eyes roam the maps of Mountains and Seas.

俯仰终宇宙，不乐复何如？

fǔ yǎng zhōng yǔ zhòu, bù lè fù hé rú

In one glance down and up I travel the whole universe — what could be happier than this?
