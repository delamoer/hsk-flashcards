导读About this poem

Alone among the flowers with a jug of wine, Li Bai invites the moon and his own shadow to drink with him — and turns loneliness into a party of three. One of the most playful poems in Chinese.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

诗人在花丛中独自喝酒，身边没有一个亲近的人。他忽然想出一个办法：举起酒杯请月亮来，再加上地上自己的影子，正好“对影成三人”。可是月亮不会喝酒，影子也只会跟着他，这份热闹其实是假的。他并不在意，干脆趁着春光及时行乐：他唱歌，月亮好像在徘徊倾听；他跳舞，影子跟着乱晃。清醒时三个“朋友”一起高兴，喝醉了就各自散去。最后他和月亮、影子约定，要在遥远的银河再相会。全诗用了拟人的手法——把月亮和影子当成人来写，越写得热闹，越让人感到诗人的孤独；但他的孤独不是悲伤的，而是潇洒、浪漫的，这正是李白的风格。

In English

Li Bai sits among the flowers with a jug of wine and nobody to share it. So he invents company: he raises his cup to invite the moon, and with his shadow on the ground, “we make three.” Then he admits the joke — the moon can’t drink, and the shadow only follows him around. It doesn’t stop him. While spring lasts, he will enjoy it: when he sings, the moon seems to linger and listen; when he dances, his shadow staggers along. Sober, the three of them have a fine time; drunk, they drift apart. At the end he makes a promise to these companions “without feelings”: to meet again far away, in the River of Stars. The poem works through personification — treating the moon and the shadow as if they were people. The livelier the little party sounds, the more we feel how alone he really is. Yet the loneliness is not gloomy. It is free, a little wild and full of imagination — exactly what readers love in Li Bai.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Li Bai (701–762), the “Immortal of Poetry”, was famous for his love of wine and the moon. This is the first of four poems with the same title, usually dated to around 744 in Chang’an, when his hopes at the imperial court were fading.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

暂伴月将影：将 jiāng，意思是“和、与”，不读 jiàng。行乐：xíng lè。相期：xiāng qī。邈 miǎo（遥远）。醉后各分散：散 sàn。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行的教材及选本文本。“我舞影零乱”的“零乱”一作“凌乱”；“醒时同交欢”一作“醒时相交欢”。原题共四首，此为第一首。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

花间一壶酒，

huā jiān yī hú jiǔ

A jug of wine among the flowers,

独酌无相亲。

dú zhuó wú xiāng qīn

I pour alone, no friend in sight.

举杯邀明月，

jǔ bēi yāo míng yuè

I lift my cup and ask the moon to join me —

对影成三人。

duì yǐng chéng sān rén

with my shadow, that makes three.

月既不解饮，

yuè jì bù jiě yǐn

But the moon has never learned to drink,

影徒随我身。

yǐng tú suí wǒ shēn

and my shadow only trails behind me.

暂伴月将影，

zàn bàn yuè jiāng yǐng

Still, for now, moon and shadow will do for friends:

行乐须及春。

xíng lè xū jí chūn

joy must be caught while spring is here.

我歌月徘徊，

wǒ gē yuè pái huái

I sing — the moon stays, wavering, to listen;

我舞影零乱。

wǒ wǔ yǐng líng luàn

I dance — my shadow tumbles all around.

醒时同交欢，

xǐng shí tóng jiāo huān

While I’m sober, we’re merry together;

醉后各分散。

zuì hòu gè fēn sàn

once I’m drunk, we each wander off.

永结无情游，

yǒng jié wú qíng yóu

Let us be friends for good, beyond all human ties,

相期邈云汉。

xiāng qī miǎo yún hàn

and meet again far off, in the River of Stars.
