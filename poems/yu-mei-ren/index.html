导读About this poem

A fallen king, now a prisoner, sees spring come round again and remembers the palace he lost. It ends with one of the most famous images of sorrow in Chinese: grief as endless as a river in spring flood, flowing east.

这首诗被谱成了歌Sung as a song

Also from 淡淡幽情: Li Yu's 虞美人, which ends “my sorrow is a whole river of spring water flowing east.” The footage is from the same 1983 TV special.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

李煜本来是南唐的国君，国家被宋朝灭亡后，他成了俘虏，被关在汴京。这首词写的就是亡国之后的痛苦。开头一句很特别：春花、秋月本来是美好的东西，他却问“什么时候才能结束”——因为每一次花开月圆，都让他想起过去的好日子。“小楼昨夜又东风”，又是一年春天；“故国不堪回首”，故乡的宫殿他连想都不敢想。下阕说：宫殿的栏杆和台阶大概还在，只是住在里面的人已经变老、变憔悴了——景物不变，人事全非，这是一种对比。最后用问答的方式，把看不见的“愁”比作一江向东奔流的春水：又多、又长、流不完。这种把抽象感情写成具体景物的方法，让这两句成了千古名句。传说宋太宗读到这首词后大怒，李煜不久就被毒死，所以这也被看作他的绝笔。

In English

Li Yu was the last ruler of the Southern Tang kingdom. When the Song dynasty conquered it, he was taken north to the Song capital and kept as a prisoner. This lyric is about that loss. The opening is surprising: spring flowers and the autumn moon are lovely things, yet he asks when they will finally end — because every new season reminds him of the life he once had. “Last night the east wind came to my small tower again”: another spring has arrived, and he cannot bear to look back toward his old kingdom in the moonlight. The second half sets things against people: the carved railings and marble steps of his palace are probably still there; only the faces have changed. Then comes the question and answer that made the poem famous. How much sorrow can one person hold? As much as a whole river of spring water, rushing east. Grief is something you cannot see, so he turns it into something you can — a river swollen with spring rain, wide, long and never-ending. Chinese readers still quote these last two lines whenever they want to say “my sadness has no end.” Tradition says the Song emperor was angered by this poem and had Li Yu poisoned soon after, so it is often called his last work.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Li Yu (937–978) was the last ruler of the Southern Tang, one of the small kingdoms of the Five Dynasties and Ten Kingdoms period; on this site he is grouped with the Song because he wrote his best-known lyrics as a Song prisoner. After his surrender in 975 he lived under guard in Kaifeng, and this lyric is traditionally said to be the last he wrote before his death in 978.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

春花秋月何时了，往事知多少。

chūn huā qiū yuè hé shí liǎo, wǎng shì zhī duō shǎo

Spring flowers, autumn moon — when will they stop coming back? So much of the past I cannot forget.

小楼昨夜又东风，

xiǎo lóu zuó yè yòu dōng fēng

Last night the east wind found my little tower again;

故国不堪回首月明中。

gù guó bù kān huí shǒu yuè míng zhōng

under the bright moon I cannot bear to look back toward the kingdom I lost.

雕栏玉砌应犹在，只是朱颜改。

diāo lán yù qì yīng yóu zài, zhǐ shì zhū yán gǎi

The carved railings and marble steps must still be standing — only the young faces have grown old.

问君能有几多愁？

wèn jūn néng yǒu jǐ duō chóu

You ask how much sorrow one man can hold?

恰似一江春水向东流。

qià sì yī jiāng chūn shuǐ xiàng dōng liú

As much as a river in spring flood, pouring east without end.
