导读About this poem

A rare visitor comes to Du Fu's riverside cottage. The path hasn't been swept in ages, the food is plain and the wine is cloudy — so he calls the old neighbour over the fence to finish the cups with them. Warm, funny and very human.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗写杜甫在成都草堂招待客人。第一、二句写草堂的环境：房前房后都是春天的江水，只看见一群群鸥鸟天天飞来。鸥鸟常来，说明这里很安静，也暗示平时没有什么客人。第三、四句是有名的对句：长满花的小路，从来没有因为有客人来而打扫过；简陋的柴门，今天第一次为您打开。这两句写出主人见到客人的惊喜和真诚。第五、六句写招待：离集市太远，盘子里的菜只有一样；家里穷，杯里只有自家酿的旧酒。主人一边道歉，一边热情，很亲切。最后两句最有意思：您愿意和隔壁的老人一起喝吗？我隔着篱笆喊他过来，把剩下的酒都喝完！整首诗像一段家常话，写出了乡村生活的淳朴（朴实、真诚）和朋友之间的温暖。

In English

Here Du Fu is entertaining a guest at his thatched cottage in Chengdu. The first couplet sets the scene: spring water all around the house, north and south, and nothing to see but flocks of gulls that come day after day. The gulls hint at how quiet it is — and at how seldom anyone visits. The second couplet is often quoted: the flower-lined path has never been swept for a guest; the rough wicker gate opens today, for the first time, for you. You can feel the host's delight and slight embarrassment. The third couplet is about the meal. The market is far, so there's only one dish on the plate; the family is poor, so the wine in the jar is old home-brew. He apologises and fusses, like any host. Then the ending: would you mind drinking with the old man next door? I'll call him over the hedge, and we'll finish off what's left. The whole poem reads like friendly conversation, full of the simple warmth of country life and friendship.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Du Fu (712–770) wrote this around 761 at his thatched cottage in Chengdu. A note under the original title says the guest was a Mr Cui, a county magistrate; tradition says he was a relative on Du Fu's mother's side.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

舍南舍北皆春水，

shè nán shè běi jiē chūn shuǐ

Spring water all around my cottage, north and south,

但见群鸥日日来。

dàn jiàn qún ōu rì rì lái

and all I see are the gulls that come each day.

花径不曾缘客扫，

huā jìng bù céng yuán kè sǎo

The flowery path has never been swept for a visitor;

蓬门今始为君开。

péng mén jīn shǐ wèi jūn kāi

today my rough gate opens for the first time — for you.

盘飧市远无兼味，

pán sūn shì yuǎn wú jiān wèi

The market's far, so there's just one dish on the table;

樽酒家贫只旧醅。

zūn jiǔ jiā pín zhǐ jiù pēi

we're poor, so the wine is only old home-brew.

肯与邻翁相对饮，

kěn yǔ lín wēng xiāng duì yǐn

Would you share a drink with the old man next door?

隔篱呼取尽余杯。

gé lí hū qǔ jìn yú bēi

I'll call him over the hedge, and we'll empty every cup.
