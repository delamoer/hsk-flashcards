导读About this poem

A rider gallops toward the mountain palace in a cloud of dust; the gates swing open; the Emperor’s favourite smiles. No one guesses he is carrying fresh lychees. A whole lesson about a dynasty’s decline in one snapshot.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

华清宫在长安东边的骊山上，是唐玄宗和杨贵妃常去游乐的行宫。杜牧路过这里时，安史之乱已经过去几十年。第一句从长安回头望骊山，林木花草像一堆堆锦绣。第二句写山顶的宫门一道一道打开。为什么开门？第三句：一匹快马卷着红尘飞奔而来，贵妃笑了。第四句才揭开谜底：没有人知道，送来的是荔枝。杨贵妃爱吃新鲜荔枝，皇帝就让人从南方骑快马一站一站接力送来，不知累死多少人和马。诗人没有发一句议论，只用一个“笑”字和一句“无人知”，就把皇帝为了宠妃劳民伤财的荒唐写了出来。小事背后，是唐朝由盛转衰的大历史。

In English

The Huaqing Palace stood on Mount Li, east of Chang’an, where Emperor Xuanzong spent his winters with his beloved Consort Yang Guifei. By the time Du Mu passed by, the An Lushan Rebellion (755–763) had shattered the dynasty decades earlier. Line one: looking back from Chang’an, the mountain’s trees and flowers look like heaps of embroidery. Line two: on the summit, a thousand palace gates open one after another. Why? Line three: a lone horseman races up in a cloud of red dust, and the Consort smiles. Only line four gives away the secret: nobody knows that what he brings is lychees. Yang Guifei loved fresh lychees, which grow only in the far south, so relay riders galloped them north at a deadly pace — horses and men worn out so the fruit would arrive fresh. Du Mu adds no comment at all. One smile and the phrase “no one knows” are enough to show an emperor wasting his people’s strength on a favourite’s whim. Behind the tiny scene stands the big story of the Tang’s fall from glory.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Du Mu (803–c. 852) wrote three quatrains on passing the Huaqing Palace; this is the first and best known. Like many late Tang writers, he looked back at Xuanzong’s reign as a warning about how splendour turns to ruin.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

长安回望绣成堆，

cháng ān huí wàng xiù chéng duī

Looking back from Chang’an, the mountain is heaped with brocade;

山顶千门次第开。

shān dǐng qiān mén cì dì kāi

on its peak a thousand gates swing open, one by one.

一骑红尘妃子笑，

yī jì hóng chén fēi zǐ xiào

A lone rider in a cloud of red dust — and the Consort smiles;

无人知是荔枝来。

wú rén zhī shì lì zhī lái

no one guesses he is bringing lychees.
