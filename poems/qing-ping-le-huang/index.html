Qīng Píng Yuè

导读About this poem

The poet treats spring like a friend who has wandered off: where did it go? If anyone knows, call it back! Maybe the oriole knows — but nobody understands its song, and it flies away.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首词写“惜春”（舍不得春天离开）。作者把春天当成一个人来写：春天回到哪里去了？到处冷冷清清，找不到它走过的路。如果有人知道春天去了哪儿，请把它叫回来，和我住在一起。下阕更有意思：春天没留下脚印，谁知道它在哪儿呢？大概只能问黄鹂鸟了。可是黄鹂叫了又叫，没有人听得懂；它借着风，飞过蔷薇花丛，也走了。蔷薇是初夏开的花，看到蔷薇，就知道春天真的走了。全词用问答的形式，一问再问，越找越找不到，语言像说话一样自然，却写出了一种天真又落寞的心情。

In English

This lyric is about “cherishing spring” — not wanting it to leave. Huang Tingjian writes about spring as if it were a person who has wandered off. Where has spring gone? Everything is quiet and lonely, and there is no path to follow. If anyone knows where it went, please call it back to live with me. The second half is playful. Spring left no footprints, so who could know? Perhaps only the oriole. But the bird sings and sings, and no one can understand it; then it rides the wind over the roses and away. Roses bloom in early summer, so the moment we see them we know spring really has gone. The whole poem is a chain of questions, each answer leading to another dead end, written in language as natural as speech. Under the childlike game of hide-and-seek lies a quiet loneliness — the feeling of reaching for something beautiful just after it has slipped away.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Huang Tingjian (1045–1105) was one of the most famous poets and calligraphers of the Northern Song and a close friend and follower of Su Shi. He suffered repeated exile for political reasons; some scholars date this lyric to his final exile in Yizhou, in today’s Guangxi.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

词牌“清平乐”的“乐”读 yuè。“谁知”的“谁”按诗词传统读 shuí（统编教材在诗词中多注 shuí，今天口语一般读 shéi）。“去处”的“处”读 chù。“唤取”“问取”的“取”是助词，仍读 qǔ。啭 zhuàn。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

春归何处？

chūn guī hé chù

Where has spring gone home to?

寂寞无行路。

jì mò wú xíng lù

All is quiet, and it left no trail.

若有人知春去处，

ruò yǒu rén zhī chūn qù chù

If anyone knows where spring has gone,

唤取归来同住。

huàn qǔ guī lái tóng zhù

call it back to stay here with me.

春无踪迹谁知？

chūn wú zōng jì shuí zhī

Spring left no footprints — who could know?

除非问取黄鹂。

chú fēi wèn qǔ huáng lí

No one, unless we ask the oriole.

百啭无人能解，

bǎi zhuàn wú rén néng jiě

It sings and sings, but no one understands,

因风飞过蔷薇。

yīn fēng fēi guò qiáng wēi

and on the wind it flies off over the roses.
