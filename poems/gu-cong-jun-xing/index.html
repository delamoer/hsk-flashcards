古从军行

Gǔ Cóng Jūn Xíng
An Old Song of Marching to War
李颀Lǐ Qí · Li Qi唐 Tang
体裁Form

边塞 Frontier & war

导读About this poem

A Tang poet borrows a Han-dynasty war to protest the wars of his own day: soldiers freeze and die in the desert year after year, and all the empire gets in return is grapes.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

“从军行”是乐府旧题，前面加个“古”字，表面写汉朝的事，其实是借古讽今，批评唐朝皇帝不断开边打仗。前四句写军中的一天：白天登山看烽火，黄昏到河边饮马，夜里只听见巡夜的刁斗声和像汉朝远嫁公主那样哀怨的琵琶。中间四句写环境：万里荒野没有城郭，大雪连着沙漠，连大雁和本地胡人都在哀鸣落泪，更不用说远来的士兵。最后四句是全诗的重点：听说玉门关被挡住，不许回家，只能拼命跟着将军继续打；年年有人战死埋在荒野，换来的只是葡萄运进皇宫。用这么大的代价和这么小的收获作对比，讽刺不说破却很尖锐。

In English

“Marching to War” was an old folk-song title; adding 古 (“old”) lets Li Qi set the poem in the Han dynasty while really aiming at the Tang emperors of his own time, who kept pushing the frontier outward. This trick of criticising the present through the past is very common in Chinese poetry. The first four lines give a soldier’s day: watching for beacon fires by day, watering horses at dusk, and at night hearing only the watchman’s copper pot and a pipa as mournful as the one played by a Han princess married off to a nomad king. The middle four lines paint the land: endless clouds, no towns, snow running into the desert. Even the wild geese cry, and even the soldiers born out here weep — so imagine the men from far away. The last four lines carry the point. In Han times the emperor once closed the Jade Gate so a failing army could not come home; these men, too, can only risk their lives and press on. Every year more bones are buried in the waste, and what reaches the capital? Grapes. Setting that huge cost against that tiny prize is the poem’s quiet, sharp protest.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Li Qi (c. 690–c. 751) was a minor official in the High Tang, admired for his frontier poems and his friendships with Daoist recluses. This poem is usually read as a protest against Emperor Xuanzong’s expensive campaigns in the west, disguised as a story about Emperor Wu of Han.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

饮马 yìn mǎ（让马喝水，不读 yǐn）；傍 bàng（靠近）；刁斗 diāo dǒu；城郭 guō；将 jiāng（拿）；轻车 chē（旧读 jū，今按 chē）。雨雪纷纷：“雨”作动词“下”，传统读 yù；此处参照统编教材《采薇》“雨雪霏霏”的注音采用 yù，也有人按今音读 yǔ。机器朗读“饮马”“雨雪”易读错，已在“朗读用字”列替换同音字，音频需人工听验。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行的《唐诗三百首》文本。“野云万里无城郭”一作“野营万里无城郭”；“蒲桃”即“葡萄”，古书多写作“蒲桃”或“蒲萄”，有的选本直接写“葡萄”。“公主琵琶”用汉代乌孙公主远嫁、马上弹琵琶的传说。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

白日登山望烽火，

bái rì dēng shān wàng fēng huǒ

By day we climb the ridge to watch for signal fires;

黄昏饮马傍交河。

huáng hūn yìn mǎ bàng jiāo hé

at dusk we lead the horses down to drink at the Jiao River.

行人刁斗风沙暗，

xíng rén diāo dǒu fēng shā àn

Through the dark of blowing sand, the night watch beats its copper pot,

公主琵琶幽怨多。

gōng zhǔ pí pá yōu yuàn duō

and a pipa, like the exiled princess’s, plays its sorrow.

野云万里无城郭，

yě yún wàn lǐ wú chéng guō

Wild clouds for a thousand miles, and not one walled town;

雨雪纷纷连大漠。

yù xuě fēn fēn lián dà mò

the snow keeps falling, on and on into the desert.

胡雁哀鸣夜夜飞，

hú yàn āi míng yè yè fēi

Wild geese of the steppe fly crying, night after night,

胡儿眼泪双双落。

hú ér yǎn lèi shuāng shuāng luò

and even the men born out here are weeping.

闻道玉门犹被遮，

wén dào yù mén yóu bèi zhē

We hear the Jade Gate is still barred against us —

应将性命逐轻车。

yīng jiāng xìng mìng zhú qīng chē

so we must stake our lives and follow the general on.

年年战骨埋荒外，

nián nián zhàn gǔ mái huāng wài

Year after year, soldiers’ bones are buried in the waste,

空见蒲桃入汉家。

kōng jiàn pú táo rù hàn jiā

and all there is to show for it: grapes, carried home to the emperor.
