导读About this poem

A cicada high in a tree cries itself hoarse all night, and the tree does not care. Li Shangyin sees himself in it — an honest, poor official whose voice no one hears — and in the last line speaks to the insect as a fellow sufferer.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

古人以为蝉住在高树上，只喝露水，所以常用蝉比喻清高廉洁的人。这首诗前四句写蝉：它住得高，本来就难吃饱，叫得再辛苦也是白费力气；叫到五更天，声音稀稀落落快断了，可整棵树依旧碧绿，一点也不同情它。“一树碧无情”把树写得像冷漠的人，这是全诗最有名的一句。后四句转到自己：我官职低微，像水上漂着的树枝一样四处漂泊，家乡的田园早已长满荒草。最后说：麻烦你（蝉）用叫声来提醒我，我和你一样，全家清贫。前面写蝉，句句也是写人，这种手法叫“托物言志”——借写一样东西来表达自己的志向和处境。

In English

People in ancient China believed the cicada lived high in the trees and drank nothing but dew, so it became a symbol of an honest person who stays pure and poor. The first four lines are about the insect. Living so high, it was never going to eat its fill, and all its crying is wasted effort. By the fifth watch, just before dawn, its voice is thin and breaking — yet the whole tree stays green and does not care in the least. “A whole tree of green, without feeling” turns the tree into a cold, indifferent person, and it is the poem’s most famous line. The second half turns to the poet. He is a low-ranking official drifting from post to post like a twig floating on water, and the fields at his old home are overgrown with weeds. In the last couplet he addresses the cicada directly: thank you for the warning your cry gives me — my whole family, like you, is “clean”, that is, honest and poor. Every line about the insect is also about the man. This way of writing — describing an object to express your own situation and values — is called 托物言志, “speaking one’s mind through a thing”.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Li Shangyin (c. 813–858) was one of the great late-Tang poets, known for dense, beautiful and often puzzling verse. Caught between two rival court factions, he spent his life in minor posts far from the capital; this poem is usually read as a picture of that frustrated career.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

本以高难饱，

běn yǐ gāo nán bǎo

Up so high, you were never going to eat your fill;

徒劳恨费声。

tú láo hèn fèi shēng

all your aggrieved crying is wasted breath.

五更疏欲断，

wǔ gēng shū yù duàn

Before dawn your voice thins and nearly fails,

一树碧无情。

yī shù bì wú qíng

and the whole tree stays green, and does not care.

薄宦梗犹泛，

bó huàn gěng yóu fàn

A minor official, I drift like a twig on the water;

故园芜已平。

gù yuán wú yǐ píng

at home, the weeds have long since swallowed the garden.

烦君最相警，

fán jūn zuì xiāng jǐng

Thank you, cicada, for the warning in your cry —

我亦举家清。

wǒ yì jǔ jiā qīng

like you, my whole household lives clean, and lives poor.
