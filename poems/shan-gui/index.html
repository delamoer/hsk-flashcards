九歌·山鬼

Jiǔ Gē · Shān Guǐ
Nine Songs: The Mountain Spirit

导读About this poem

A goddess of the mountains dresses in vines and orchids, rides a red leopard to meet her lover — and he never comes. As rain and thunder roll in and monkeys cry in the dark, she waits alone. One of the most beautiful love poems of ancient China.

第一章Stanza 1
01
若有人兮山之阿，被薜荔兮带女罗。

直译LiteralThere seems to be someone in the fold of the mountain, wearing climbing fig, belted with lichen.

意译MeaningSomeone is there in the bend of the hills, draped in vines, a sash of moss at her waist.

02
既含睇兮又宜笑，子慕予兮善窈窕。

直译LiteralHer eyes hold feeling, and she smiles sweetly; you adore me for my grace.

意译MeaningMy eyes speak softly, my smile comes easily — you love me, for I am lovely.

03
乘赤豹兮从文狸，辛夷车兮结桂旗。

直译LiteralRiding a red leopard, followed by striped wildcats; a magnolia carriage with cassia flags tied on.

意译MeaningI ride a red leopard, spotted wildcats at my heels, in a magnolia carriage flying cassia flags.

04
被石兰兮带杜衡，折芳馨兮遗所思。

直译LiteralWearing rock orchids, belted with wild ginger; I pick fragrant flowers to give the one I long for.

意译MeaningOrchids on my shoulders, wild ginger at my waist, I pick sweet flowers for the one I love.

第二章Stanza 2
05
余处幽篁兮终不见天，路险难兮独后来。

直译LiteralI live in a dark bamboo grove, never seeing the sky; the road is steep and hard, so I alone arrive late.

意译MeaningI live where the bamboo is so thick I never see the sky; the path is steep, and I come late.

06
表独立兮山之上，云容容兮而在下。

直译LiteralStanding out alone on top of the mountain; clouds billow below.

意译MeaningAlone I stand on the mountaintop; clouds surge and drift beneath me.

07
杳冥冥兮羌昼晦，东风飘兮神灵雨。

直译LiteralDeep and dark — the day turns dim; the east wind blows, and the gods send rain.

意译MeaningEverything goes dark, day turns to dusk; the east wind gusts and the heavens pour down rain.

08
留灵修兮憺忘归，岁既晏兮孰华予？

直译LiteralWaiting for my beloved, I linger and forget to go home; when the year grows late, who will make me bloom?

意译MeaningI stay for you, forgetting to go home; but when my youth is spent, who will make me beautiful again?

第三章Stanza 3
09
采三秀兮于山间，石磊磊兮葛蔓蔓。

直译LiteralI gather magic mushrooms among the hills; rocks are piled high, kudzu vines spread everywhere.

意译MeaningI gather magic mushrooms on the slopes, among heaped rocks and tangled vines.

10
怨公子兮怅忘归，君思我兮不得闲。

直译LiteralI resent my lord, and in sorrow forget to go home; you think of me, but cannot get free.

意译MeaningI am angry with you, too sad to go home — yet surely you miss me, you just can’t get away.

11
山中人兮芳杜若，饮石泉兮荫松柏。

直译LiteralThe one in the mountains is fragrant as pollia; she drinks from rock springs and shelters under pines and cypresses.

意译MeaningI, the one of the mountains, sweet as wild herbs, drink from stone springs in the shade of pines.

12
君思我兮然疑作。

直译LiteralYou think of me — believing and doubting arise together.

意译MeaningDo you think of me? I believe it, then I doubt it.

13
雷填填兮雨冥冥，猿啾啾兮狖夜鸣。

直译LiteralThunder rumbles, rain falls dark and heavy; apes chatter, monkeys cry in the night.

意译MeaningThunder rolls, the rain blurs everything; the apes wail, and monkeys cry into the night.

14
风飒飒兮木萧萧，思公子兮徒离忧。

直译LiteralWind whistles, trees rustle; longing for my lord, I suffer sorrow in vain.

意译MeaningThe wind howls, the trees moan; I long for you, and all I get is grief.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

“山鬼”不是可怕的鬼，而是山里的女神。全诗用她自己的口吻来写。开头她出场：身披薜荔、腰系女萝，含情地看人、甜甜地笑，骑着赤豹、带着花狸，车上插着桂枝做的旗，还折下香花要送给心上人。可是她住在竹林深处，路又难走，来晚了。她独自站在山顶，云在脚下翻滚；天一下子暗了，东风吹起，下起雨来。她等啊等，忘了回家，又担心青春一年年过去，谁还会让我像花一样美？接着她在山间采灵芝，心里又怨又替他找理由：“你是想我的，只是没有空吧？”可是很快又怀疑起来。最后雷声隆隆、大雨蒙蒙，猿猴在夜里哀叫，风吹树木萧萧作响——她只能白白地忧伤。全诗把等待、期盼、怀疑、失望写得非常细腻，环境越来越阴暗，心情也越来越低沉，这叫“以景衬情”。

In English

The “mountain spirit” is not a frightening ghost but a goddess of the hills, and she tells the poem herself. She makes a grand entrance: dressed in climbing fig and lichen, glancing with soft eyes and a ready smile, riding a red leopard with a spotted wildcat at her side, her carriage decked with magnolia and cassia flags. She picks fragrant flowers to give the one she loves. But she lives deep in a bamboo grove where the path is hard, and she arrives late. Alone on a peak, clouds rolling beneath her feet, she watches the day darken; the east wind rises and the rain begins. She waits so long she forgets to go home, and wonders: once my youth is gone, who will make me bloom again? Gathering magic mushrooms among the rocks, she half-blames him and half-excuses him — “you do think of me, you just can’t get away” — then doubts even that. In the end thunder rumbles, rain blurs everything, monkeys wail in the night, wind roars through the trees, and she grieves for nothing. The poem follows every turn of a waiting heart — hope, worry, excuse, doubt, despair — while the weather darkens step by step to match it. Chinese critics call this “using the scene to set off the feeling” (以景衬情).

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Qu Yuan (c. 340–278 BC) was a nobleman and minister of the state of Chu who was exiled after his advice was rejected; he is traditionally said to have drowned himself in the Miluo River. The Nine Songs are traditionally credited to him, reworking ritual songs of Chu, where shamans sang to gods; some scholars think he only edited them.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

阿 ē（山的弯曲处）；被 pī（同“披”）；薜荔 bì lì；睇 dì；予 yú（我）；狸 lí；遗 wèi（赠送）；篁 huáng；杳 yǎo；羌 qiāng（句首语气词）；憺 dàn；华 huá；葛 gé；荫 yìn；狖 yòu；飒 sà；离 lí（通“罹”，遭受）。“东风飘兮神灵雨”的“雨”作动词“下雨”，传统读 yù，网站按 yù 注音；也有注本读 yǔ。机器朗读易错处已在“朗读用字”列替换同音字，音频需人工听验。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行文本（王逸《楚辞章句》系统，与古诗文网等通行本一致）。异文：“猿啾啾兮狖夜鸣”，王逸本作“猨啾啾兮又夜鸣”；“采三秀兮于山间”的“于”，郭沫若认为是“巫”（巫山），网站按通行本。全诗二十七句，网站一行一联，按意思分三段。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

若有人兮山之阿，被薜荔兮带女罗。

ruò yǒu rén xī shān zhī ē, pī bì lì xī dài nǚ luó

Someone is there in the bend of the hills, draped in vines, a sash of moss at her waist.

既含睇兮又宜笑，子慕予兮善窈窕。

jì hán dì xī yòu yí xiào, zǐ mù yú xī shàn yǎo tiǎo

My eyes speak softly, my smile comes easily — you love me, for I am lovely.

乘赤豹兮从文狸，辛夷车兮结桂旗。

chéng chì bào xī cóng wén lí, xīn yí chē xī jié guì qí

I ride a red leopard, spotted wildcats at my heels, in a magnolia carriage flying cassia flags.

被石兰兮带杜衡，折芳馨兮遗所思。

pī shí lán xī dài dù héng, zhé fāng xīn xī wèi suǒ sī

Orchids on my shoulders, wild ginger at my waist, I pick sweet flowers for the one I love.

余处幽篁兮终不见天，路险难兮独后来。

yú chǔ yōu huáng xī zhōng bù jiàn tiān, lù xiǎn nán xī dú hòu lái

I live where the bamboo is so thick I never see the sky; the path is steep, and I come late.

表独立兮山之上，云容容兮而在下。

biǎo dú lì xī shān zhī shàng, yún róng róng xī ér zài xià

Alone I stand on the mountaintop; clouds surge and drift beneath me.

杳冥冥兮羌昼晦，东风飘兮神灵雨。

yǎo míng míng xī qiāng zhòu huì, dōng fēng piāo xī shén líng yù

Everything goes dark, day turns to dusk; the east wind gusts and the heavens pour down rain.

留灵修兮憺忘归，岁既晏兮孰华予？

liú líng xiū xī dàn wàng guī, suì jì yàn xī shú huá yú

I stay for you, forgetting to go home; but when my youth is spent, who will make me beautiful again?

采三秀兮于山间，石磊磊兮葛蔓蔓。

cǎi sān xiù xī yú shān jiān, shí lěi lěi xī gé màn màn

I gather magic mushrooms on the slopes, among heaped rocks and tangled vines.

怨公子兮怅忘归，君思我兮不得闲。

yuàn gōng zǐ xī chàng wàng guī, jūn sī wǒ xī bù dé xián

I am angry with you, too sad to go home — yet surely you miss me, you just can’t get away.

山中人兮芳杜若，饮石泉兮荫松柏。

shān zhōng rén xī fāng dù ruò, yǐn shí quán xī yìn sōng bǎi

I, the one of the mountains, sweet as wild herbs, drink from stone springs in the shade of pines.

君思我兮然疑作。

jūn sī wǒ xī rán yí zuò

Do you think of me? I believe it, then I doubt it.

雷填填兮雨冥冥，猿啾啾兮狖夜鸣。

léi tián tián xī yǔ míng míng, yuán jiū jiū xī yòu yè míng

Thunder rolls, the rain blurs everything; the apes wail, and monkeys cry into the night.

风飒飒兮木萧萧，思公子兮徒离忧。

fēng sà sà xī mù xiāo xiāo, sī gōng zǐ xī tú lí yōu

The wind howls, the trees moan; I long for you, and all I get is grief.
