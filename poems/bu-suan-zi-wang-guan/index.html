导读About this poem

A light-hearted farewell: the rivers of Zhejiang are a pretty woman’s glancing eyes, its hills her eyebrows — and if you catch up with spring there, the poet tells his friend, stay with it.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这是一首送朋友去浙东的词，写得轻松俏皮，没有一般送别诗的眼泪。开头两句很新奇：江水像美人流动的眼波，青山像美人皱起的眉峰——把山水比作美人的眉眼，这种手法叫“比喻”，而且比得又活泼又美。接着问：朋友要去哪里？就是那山水像眉眼一样美丽的地方。下阕说：刚刚送走了春天，又要送你走，两件伤心事碰在一起。但词人没有沉在悲伤里，反而祝福朋友：如果你到了江南，还赶得上那里的春天，千万要和春天住在一起啊！“和春住”三个字把春天当成可以同住的朋友，既是祝福，也是不舍。全词语言像说话一样自然，比喻新巧，是宋词送别作品中很有个性的一首。

In English

This is a farewell to a friend heading for eastern Zhejiang, and it is surprisingly cheerful — no tears, just affection and wit. The opening is unusual: the rivers there are a beautiful woman’s glancing eyes, the green hills her gathered eyebrows. Comparing a landscape to a lovely face is a metaphor, and here it is fresh and playful. Where is my friend going? To the place where hills and rivers look like a smiling face. The second stanza admits a double sadness: spring has only just left, and now the friend is leaving too. Instead of dwelling on it, the poet turns it into a wish: if you reach the south in time to catch spring there, be sure to stay with it. “Live together with spring” treats the season like a companion — a blessing and a goodbye at once. The language is as easy as conversation, which makes this one of the most charming farewell lyrics of the Song.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Wang Guan (dates uncertain, c. 1035–1100) passed the imperial examination in 1057 and served as an official; few details of his life survive. Bao Haoran was a friend from eastern Zhejiang returning home — nothing else is known about him.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

那边 nǎ biān（“那”同“哪”，旧时写作“那”）；盈盈 yíng yíng；和春住 hé。机器朗读“那边”会读成 nà，已在“朗读用字”列换成“哪边”，音频需人工听验。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行文本。“欲问行人去那边”一作“欲问行人去哪边”。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

水是眼波横，

shuǐ shì yǎn bō héng

The rivers there are a woman’s glancing eyes,

山是眉峰聚。

shān shì méi fēng jù

the hills, her eyebrows gently knit.

欲问行人去那边？

yù wèn xíng rén qù nǎ biān

Where is my friend off to, you ask?

眉眼盈盈处。

méi yǎn yíng yíng chù

To where those brows and eyes are smiling.

才始送春归，

cái shǐ sòng chūn guī

I have only just said goodbye to spring,

又送君归去。

yòu sòng jūn guī qù

and now I am saying goodbye to you.

若到江南赶上春，

ruò dào jiāng nán gǎn shàng chūn

If you get south in time to catch up with spring,

千万和春住。

qiān wàn hé chūn zhù

whatever you do, stay there with it.
