导读About this poem

A young woman remembers a summer outing: a bit tipsy at sunset, she rows home the wrong way into a thick bed of lotus flowers, and her frantic paddling sends a whole flock of water birds flying up. Pure, carefree fun in 33 characters.

这首诗被谱成了歌Sung as a song

Tsai Chin sings Li Qingzhao's "As in a Dream" ("I often recall the creek pavilion at dusk") on CCTV's poetry-in-song show Everlasting Classics (season 2, 2019), setting the original text to music.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首小词是李清照年轻时写的，回忆一次快乐的郊游。她常常想起那天在溪边的亭子里玩到太阳下山，喝醉了，连回家的路都找不到了。玩得尽兴了，天色已晚，才划船往回走，结果迷了路，划进了荷花丛的深处。“争渡，争渡”是拼命地划呀划，想赶快划出去，结果把一滩正在休息的水鸟都惊得飞了起来。全词像一个短短的电影镜头：夕阳、醉意、荷花、慌乱的小船、飞起的鸥鹭，画面生动，充满少女的活泼和快乐。古代女子很少能这样自由地出游、喝酒，所以这首词也让人看到李清照开朗、自由的性格。词牌《如梦令》本身就是一个很短的小调，适合写这种一闪而过的回忆。

In English

Li Qingzhao wrote this short lyric as a young woman, looking back on a happy day out. She often remembers it: playing at a pavilion by a creek until sunset, getting so tipsy she couldn’t find the way home. When the fun was over and it was getting late, she turned her boat back — and paddled the wrong way, deep into a thick bed of lotus flowers. 争渡，争渡 is the sound of her paddling hard, trying to get out — and the commotion startles a whole sandbank of gulls and egrets into the air. The lyric works like a few seconds of film: the setting sun, the wine, the lotus, the little boat in a panic, the birds bursting up. It is full of a girl’s high spirits. Women in Song China were rarely free to go on outings and drink like this, so the poem also shows how lively and independent Li Qingzhao was. The tune “As in a Dream” is one of the shortest in Song lyric — just right for a memory that flashes by. Compare it with her late, grief-filled “Slow Song” (声声慢) to see how much her life changed.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Li Qingzhao (1084–c.1155) is the most celebrated woman poet in Chinese history. This lyric is generally thought to be an early work, written when she was young and living a happy life in the capital, Bianjing (Kaifeng), before war and loss darkened her later years.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

争渡：争 读 zhēng（统编教材注音），意思是“怎么渡”或“抢着划”，有人认为“争”通“怎”。兴尽：兴 读 xìng（兴致），不读 xīng。藕花 ǒu。一滩 tān。鸥鹭 ōu lù。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

常记溪亭日暮，

cháng jì xī tíng rì mù

I often think of that evening at the pavilion by the creek,

沉醉不知归路。

chén zuì bù zhī guī lù

so tipsy I could not find the way home.

兴尽晚回舟，

xìng jìn wǎn huí zhōu

When the fun was over, late, I turned the boat around —

误入藕花深处。

wù rù ǒu huā shēn chù

and drifted by mistake deep into the lotus.

争渡，争渡，惊起一滩鸥鹭。

zhēng dù, zhēng dù, jīng qǐ yī tān ōu lù

Row, row, row hard to get through — and up flew a whole bank of gulls and egrets!
