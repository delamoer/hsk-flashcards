导读About this poem

Last year at the Lantern Festival two lovers met under the moon; this year the moon and lanterns are the same, but she is gone. Eight short lines, built on a simple “last year / this year” mirror.

From 淡淡幽情: the Lantern Festival poem 生查子·元夕 — last year we met by the lanterns, this year you are gone. It is usually credited to Ouyang Xiu, though some say Zhu Shuzhen wrote it. Footage from the 1983 TV special.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首词写的是元宵节（农历正月十五）的回忆。古代女子平时很少出门，元宵夜可以上街看灯，所以这一夜常常是年轻人相会的时候。上阕写去年：花市的灯亮得像白天，月亮升到柳树梢上，两个人约在黄昏以后见面，又浪漫又甜蜜。下阕写今年：月亮和灯都和去年一样，可是去年的那个人不见了，眼泪湿透了春衫的袖子。上下两阕结构几乎一样，只换了几个字，这种“今昔对比”（把现在和过去放在一起比较）让快乐和悲伤一下子显出来。“月上柳梢头，人约黄昏后”今天还常被用来形容约会。

In English

This lyric is set on the Lantern Festival, the fifteenth night of the first lunar month. In old China young women rarely went out, but on this night everyone filled the streets to see the lanterns — so it was a natural time for lovers to meet. The first half remembers last year: the flower market is lit up as bright as day, the moon climbs to the tips of the willows, and two people have arranged to meet after dusk. The second half is this year. The moon and the lanterns are exactly as before, but the person from last year is nowhere to be seen, and tears soak the sleeve of the speaker’s spring robe. The two halves are almost mirror images, with only a few words changed. Putting “then” and “now” side by side like this, called jīn xī duìbǐ (contrast of past and present), makes the joy of the first half and the emptiness of the second hit at once. Nothing is explained — we never learn why they parted — and that silence is part of the effect. “The moon above the willow tips, a date after dusk” is still a favourite way to describe a romantic meeting.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

This lyric is usually attributed to Ouyang Xiu (1007–1072), a leading statesman and writer of the Northern Song. Some old collections credit it to the woman poet Zhu Shuzhen instead; most scholars today accept Ouyang Xiu, and Chinese textbooks follow that attribution.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

词牌“生查子”的“查”读 zhā（不读 chá）。元夜、元夕 都指元宵节夜晚。如昼 zhòu。泪湿 shī。正文无其他易错字。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行选本文本。重要异文：“月上柳梢头”一作“月到柳梢头”；“泪湿春衫袖”一作“泪满春衫袖”。作者一说为朱淑真（见《断肠集》），今多从欧阳修（《欧阳文忠公近体乐府》收录）。全词上下两阕，每阕两行。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

去年元夜时，花市灯如昼。

qù nián yuán yè shí, huā shì dēng rú zhòu

Last year, on the Lantern Festival night, the market lanterns turned the dark to day.

月上柳梢头，人约黄昏后。

yuè shàng liǔ shāo tóu, rén yuē huáng hūn hòu

The moon climbed to the tips of the willows, and we met when dusk was over.

今年元夜时，月与灯依旧。

jīn nián yuán yè shí, yuè yǔ dēng yī jiù

This year, on the Lantern Festival night, the moon and the lanterns are just the same.

不见去年人，泪湿春衫袖。

bù jiàn qù nián rén, lèi shī chūn shān xiù

But the one from last year is not here, and my spring sleeves are wet with tears.
