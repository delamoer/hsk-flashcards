题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗只有三章，每章三句，是《诗经》里最短的诗之一。心上人去采葛、采萧、采艾了，“我”一天没见到她，就觉得像过了三个月、三个秋天、三年那么久。葛可以织布，萧可以祭祀，艾可以治病，都是当时女子常做的劳动。时间一章比一章长——三月、三秋（九个月）、三岁（三年），这种越说越重的写法叫“层层递进”，思念也跟着越来越浓。明明只过了一天，却说得这么夸张，正说明想得有多厉害。今天的成语“一日三秋”和常说的“一日不见，如隔三秋”就是从这首诗来的，中国人至今还用它来表达对亲人、朋友或爱人的想念。

In English

With just three verses of three short lines each, this is one of the shortest poems in the Book of Songs. The person the speaker loves has gone out to gather plants — kudzu vine, southernwood, mugwort — and after only one day apart it feels like three months, then three autumns, then three years. All three plants were part of everyday work at the time: kudzu fibre was woven into cloth, southernwood was burned in offerings, mugwort was used as medicine. Notice how the time grows with each verse: three months, three autumns (nine months), three years. Building a feeling step by step like this is called céngcéng dìjìn, “rising by degrees”, and the longing grows stronger with every step. Only a single day has really passed; the wild exaggeration is exactly what shows how much the speaker misses the other person. The poem gave Chinese the idiom 一日三秋 and the everyday saying 一日不见，如隔三秋, “one day apart is like three autumns”, which people still use for missing family, friends or a lover.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

The Book of Songs gathers 305 songs from about the 11th to the 6th century BC. The Airs of the Royal Domain (王风) come from the area around Luoyang, the eastern Zhou capital. The author is unknown.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

葛 gé（植物名读 gé；作姓氏读 gě）；萧 xiāo（艾蒿的一种）；艾 ài。无争议读音。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

彼采葛兮，

bǐ cǎi gé xī

She’s out gathering kudzu vine —

一日不见，如三月兮！

yī rì bù jiàn, rú sān yuè xī

one day without her feels like three months!

彼采萧兮，

bǐ cǎi xiāo xī

She’s out gathering southernwood —

一日不见，如三秋兮！

yī rì bù jiàn, rú sān qiū xī

one day without her feels like three autumns!

彼采艾兮，

bǐ cǎi ài xī

She’s out gathering mugwort —

一日不见，如三岁兮！

yī rì bù jiàn, rú sān suì xī

one day without her feels like three years!
