导读About this poem

A cowherd boy rides along singing at the top of his voice — then suddenly goes silent and freezes, because he wants to catch a cicada. A tiny, funny snapshot of country life.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

题目“所见”就是“看到的东西”，这首诗像一张抓拍的照片。前两句写一个放牛的孩子骑在黄牛背上，一边走一边大声唱歌，歌声把树林都震响了，热闹又自在。后两句突然一转：他想捉树上正在叫的蝉，马上闭上嘴，一动不动地站住。从“振”到“闭口立”，从响到静，从动到停，对比非常鲜明，孩子天真、机灵的样子一下子就出来了。诗人没有说孩子可爱，只写了他的动作，读的人自己就能想象出那个屏住呼吸、眼睛盯着蝉的小牧童。语言简单得像口语，二十个字里几乎没有难字，很适合初学者读。

In English

The title simply means “what I saw”, and the poem is like a snapshot. In the first two lines a cowherd boy rides on the back of a yellow ox, singing so loudly that the whole wood rings with his voice — noisy, carefree, completely at ease. Then the poem turns. He wants to catch a cicada singing in a tree, so in an instant he shuts his mouth and stands stock-still. Loud becomes silent, movement becomes stillness: this sharp contrast is what makes the little scene so lively. The poet never tells us the boy is cute or clever. He only shows what the boy does, and we can picture the rest ourselves — a child holding his breath, eyes fixed on the insect, trying not to scare it away. Notice also the small joke hidden in the scene: the boy was making the most noise in the forest, and now he wants the cicada, the other noisy singer, to keep singing so he can find it. The language is as plain as speech and there are almost no hard characters, which makes this an easy and charming poem for beginners.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Yuan Mei (1716–1798) was a Qing poet and essayist. He left official life in his thirties and lived for decades in his Garden of Contentment (随园) in Nanjing, writing poems that valued natural, personal feeling over imitation of old models.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

牧童骑黄牛，

mù tóng qí huáng niú

A cowherd boy rides on a yellow ox,

歌声振林樾。

gē shēng zhèn lín yuè

his song rings through the shady woods.

意欲捕鸣蝉，

yì yù bǔ míng chán

Then he wants to catch a cicada singing there —

忽然闭口立。

hū rán bì kǒu lì

and all at once he hushes and stands stock-still.
