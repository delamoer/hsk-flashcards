青玉案（凌波不过横塘路）

Qīng Yù Àn
The Green Jade Table (She Never Came Past Hengtang Road)

导读About this poem

A man watches a beautiful woman walk past without coming his way, and spends the evening wondering about her. How much sorrow does he feel? As much as misty grass, flying catkins and the endless rains of early summer.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首词写一次没有结果的相思。上阕：一位美丽的女子轻盈地走过，却没有走到横塘路这边来，他只能目送她远去。“凌波”是形容女子步子轻盈，好像走在水波上。他想：她美好的青春年华和谁一起度过呢？是在月下的小桥、开满花的院子，还是在雕花的窗户、朱红的门后？大概只有春天知道。下阕写傍晚：云慢慢飘过长满香草的水边，他提笔写下伤心的句子。最后一问一答是全词最有名的地方：要问我的闲愁有多少？就像满河的烟雾笼罩的青草，满城飞舞的柳絮，梅子黄熟时下个不停的雨。用三个景物来比喻愁，愁变得看得见、摸得着，又多又乱又绵长。贺铸也因此得了“贺梅子”的外号。

In English

This is a lyric about longing that goes nowhere. In the first half, a lovely woman passes by with light steps — “skimming the waves”, a phrase borrowed from an old poem about a river goddess — but she never comes along Hengtang Road, where he is, and he can only watch the dust behind her. Who, he wonders, will share her beautiful years? Is she by a moonlit bridge, in a flowering courtyard, behind carved windows and a red door? Only spring knows. The second half moves to evening: clouds drift slowly over the fragrant shore, and he picks up his brush to write heartbroken lines. Then comes the famous ending, a question and three answers. How much idle sorrow do I have? A whole river of misty grass, a whole city of wind-blown willow catkins, and the rain that falls when the plums turn yellow. Three images make an invisible feeling visible — vast, tangled and endless — and they are all images of late spring and early summer in the south. The last line was so admired that people nicknamed the poet “He of the Plums”.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

He Zhu (1052–1125), a descendant of an empress’s family, held minor military and civil posts and was known for his forthright character. In his later years he retired to Suzhou, where Hengtang, mentioned in this lyric, is a place name.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

词牌“青玉案”的“案”读 àn。“谁与度”的“与”读 yǔ（和、跟）。冉冉 rǎn rǎn。蘅皋 héng gāo。琐窗 suǒ chuāng。风絮 fēng xù。“几许”的“许”读 xǔ。“知处”的“处”读 chù。

版本说明 About the text

采用今天最通行的文本（各种宋词选本、鉴赏辞典多用此本）。各本异文较多：“锦瑟华年”一作“锦瑟年华”；“月桥花院”一作“月台花榭”；“只有春知处”一作“惟有春知处”；“飞云冉冉”一作“碧云冉冉”；“试问闲愁都几许”一作“若问闲情都几许”或“试问闲情都几许”。维基文库所收即“锦瑟年华……月台花榭……惟有……碧云”本。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

凌波不过横塘路，

líng bō bù guò héng táng lù

Light as if gliding on water, she never came down Hengtang Road;

但目送、芳尘去。

dàn mù sòng, fāng chén qù

I could only watch the dust of her steps drift away.

锦瑟华年谁与度？

jǐn sè huá nián shuí yǔ dù

Who will share the bright years of her youth?

月桥花院，琐窗朱户，

yuè qiáo huā yuàn, suǒ chuāng zhū hù

A moonlit bridge, a courtyard of flowers, a carved window, a red door —

只有春知处。

zhǐ yǒu chūn zhī chù

only spring knows where she is.

飞云冉冉蘅皋暮，

fēi yún rǎn rǎn héng gāo mù

Clouds drift slowly over the fragrant shore as evening falls;

彩笔新题断肠句。

cǎi bǐ xīn tí duàn cháng jù

with my brush I write new lines that break the heart.

试问闲愁都几许？

shì wèn xián chóu dōu jǐ xǔ

You ask how much idle sorrow I have?

一川烟草，满城风絮，

yī chuān yān cǎo, mǎn chéng fēng xù

A whole river of grass in mist, a whole city of catkins on the wind,

梅子黄时雨。

méi zǐ huáng shí yǔ

and the rain that falls when the plums turn yellow.
