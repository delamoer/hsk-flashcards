导读About this poem

Waking from a dream and a drunken sleep, a man finds last spring’s sadness back again. He stands alone among falling petals while swallows fly in pairs through the drizzle — and remembers the girl who played the pipa for him under the same moon.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首词写对一位歌女的怀念。上阕写现在：梦醒以后，只见楼台紧紧锁着；酒醒以后，只见帘幕低低垂着。人去楼空，十分冷清。去年春天的离愁，又在这个时候回来了。接着是全词最美的两句：“落花人独立，微雨燕双飞”——花瓣纷纷落下，人一个人站着；蒙蒙细雨中，燕子成双成对地飞。燕子成双，人却孤单，一个对比就写出了心里的寂寞。这两句本来是五代诗人翁宏的诗，在原诗里并不出名，被晏几道放进这首词里，才成了千古名句。下阕写回忆：记得第一次见到小蘋，她穿着绣有两重“心”字图案的丝衣，在琵琶弦上弹出相思的情意。最后说：当时的明月今天还在，它曾经照着像彩云一样的小蘋回去。月亮还是那个月亮，人却已经不见了。全词语言清丽，感情真挚，是晏几道最有名的作品。

In English

This lyric remembers a singing girl the poet once loved. The first half is the present. Waking from a dream, he sees the tower locked up high; sobering up, he sees the curtains hanging low. The people are gone and the place is empty. The sadness he felt last spring has come back at the same season. Then come the two most beautiful lines: 落花人独立，微雨燕双飞 — “among falling petals a man stands alone; in the light rain swallows fly in pairs.” The swallows have each other and he has no one; one simple contrast says all his loneliness. Interestingly, these two lines first appeared in a poem by the little-known Five Dynasties poet Weng Hong; only when Yan Jidao set them in this lyric did they become famous. The second half is memory. He recalls first meeting Xiaoping: she wore a silk dress embroidered with a double “heart” (心) pattern, and on the strings of her pipa she spoke of love without saying a word. The ending: the moon that shone then is still here; it once lit her way home, like a bright cloud drifting off. The moon is the same, but she is gone. Clear, graceful language and deep, honest feeling make this Yan Jidao’s best-known lyric.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Yan Jidao (c. 1038–1110) was the youngest son of the chief minister and poet Yan Shu. Born into luxury, he later lived in reduced circumstances. In the preface to his collection he mentions singing girls named Lian, Hong, Ping (蘋) and Yun in his friends’ households who sang his lyrics; Xiaoping here is traditionally identified with Ping.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

却来时 què；记得 jì de；两重 chóng（两层）；曾照 céng。小蘋：人名，一般读 xiǎo píng（“蘋”另有 pín 音，指水草），网站暂采用 píng。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行文本（《小山词》）。“小蘋”多数简体本保留“蘋”字，网站从之（今简化字作“苹”）。“落花人独立，微雨燕双飞”原出五代翁宏《春残》诗。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

梦后楼台高锁，酒醒帘幕低垂。

mèng hòu lóu tái gāo suǒ, jiǔ xǐng lián mù dī chuí

I wake from a dream: the tower is locked. I sober up: the curtains hang low.

去年春恨却来时。

qù nián chūn hèn què lái shí

Last spring’s sorrow has found its way back.

落花人独立，微雨燕双飞。

luò huā rén dú lì, wēi yǔ yàn shuāng fēi

Petals fall where I stand alone; through the drizzle, swallows fly in pairs.

记得小蘋初见，两重心字罗衣。

jì de xiǎo píng chū jiàn, liǎng chóng xīn zì luó yī

I remember the first time I saw Xiaoping, in silk embroidered with twin hearts.

琵琶弦上说相思。

pí pá xián shàng shuō xiāng sī

Her pipa strings told me what she felt.

当时明月在，曾照彩云归。

dāng shí míng yuè zài, céng zhào cǎi yún guī

The same moon is still in the sky that once lit her home, drifting off like a bright cloud.
