The golden age of Chinese poetry — nearly 50,000 poems survive. The strict four-line and eight-line forms took their final shape. Li Bai the free spirit, Du Fu the conscience, Wang Wei the quiet painter-poet: most poems Chinese children learn by heart are Tang.
