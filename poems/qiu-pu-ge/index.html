导读About this poem

“My white hair is three thousand zhang long” — Li Bai looks in a mirror and is shocked by his grey hair. A famous example of Chinese poetic exaggeration, and of how worry makes a person old.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

《秋浦歌》是李白在秋浦（今安徽池州）写的一组诗，共十七首，这是第十五首。第一句就让人吃惊：“白发三千丈”。一丈大约三米多，三千丈的头发当然不可能，这是极度的夸张。第二句给出原因：因为愁，头发才像这样长。愁有多深，白发就有多长——把看不见的愁变成了看得见的白发。后两句写诗人照镜子：不知道明亮的镜子里，从哪里得来了这么多“秋霜”？“秋霜”比喻白发。诗人好像不认识镜子里的自己了，这一问里有惊讶，也有无奈和悲伤。李白这时已经五十多岁，理想没有实现，到处漂泊，愁苦让他一下子老了。“白发三千丈”今天还常被用来说明中国诗歌的夸张手法。

In English

The “Songs of Qiupu” are a set of seventeen poems Li Bai wrote in Qiupu, in today’s Chizhou, Anhui; this is No. 15. The first line is startling: “My white hair, three thousand zhang long.” A zhang is over three metres, so this is wild exaggeration. The second line gives the reason: it is because of sorrow that the hair has grown so long. The deeper the grief, the longer the hair — an invisible feeling turned into something you can see. In the last two lines he looks into a mirror: in that bright glass, where did all this “autumn frost” come from? “Autumn frost” means white hair. He seems not to recognise himself; the question carries surprise, helplessness and sadness all at once. Li Bai was in his fifties by then, his political hopes had come to nothing, and years of wandering had aged him. The first line is still the classic example people give of exaggeration in Chinese poetry.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Li Bai (701–762) visited Qiupu several times in his later years. The series is usually dated to around 753–754, about a decade after he left the court in Chang’an.

白发三千丈，

bái fà sān qiān zhàng
