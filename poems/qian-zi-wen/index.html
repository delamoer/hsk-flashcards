导读About this poem

For over a thousand years Chinese children learned to read with this text: one thousand characters, none repeated, in rhyming four-character lines. Its opening describes how the universe began and how the seasons turn.

点 虚线字 看读音和意思 · Tap a dotted word for its sound and meaning

01
天地玄黄，宇宙洪荒。

直译LiteralHeaven is dark, earth is yellow; the universe is vast and wild.

意译MeaningThe sky is deep and dark, the earth is yellow; the universe began vast and untamed.

02
日月盈昃，辰宿列张。

直译LiteralThe sun and moon grow full and slant; the stars and constellations are spread in rows.

意译MeaningThe moon waxes, the sun sinks west; the stars spread out in their places.

03
寒来暑往，秋收冬藏。

直译LiteralCold comes, heat goes; in autumn we harvest, in winter we store.

意译MeaningCold arrives and heat departs; autumn brings the harvest, winter keeps it safe.

04
闰余成岁，律吕调阳。

直译LiteralLeftover days make a leap month to complete the year; pitch-pipes tune the yang of the seasons.

意译MeaningExtra days add up to a leap month; the pitch-pipes keep the seasons in tune.

05
云腾致雨，露结为霜。

直译LiteralClouds rise and bring rain; dew gathers and becomes frost.

意译MeaningClouds rise and turn to rain; dew settles and turns to frost.

06
金生丽水，玉出昆冈。

直译LiteralGold is found in the Li River; jade comes from the Kunlun hills.

意译MeaningGold comes from the Li River, jade from the Kunlun Mountains.

07
剑号巨阙，珠称夜光。

直译LiteralThe sword is named Juque; the pearl is called Night Shine.

意译MeaningThe finest sword is Juque, the finest pearl is Night Shine.

08
果珍李柰，菜重芥姜。

直译LiteralAmong fruits, the plum and crab apple are prized; among vegetables, mustard greens and ginger are valued.

意译MeaningPlums and crab apples are the prize fruits; mustard greens and ginger, the prize vegetables.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

《千字文》是中国古代最有名的识字课本之一，一千个字没有一个重复，四个字一句，押韵整齐，非常适合背诵。这里选的是开头十六句。最前面“天地玄黄，宇宙洪荒”，说天是深青色的，地是黄色的，宇宙刚开始时广大而混沌。接下来写太阳月亮的圆缺升落、星星的排列，一年四季冷热交替，秋天收获、冬天储藏，还讲到古人用闰月调整历法、用音律测定节气。然后写云变成雨、露凝成霜，再写各地的宝物：丽水出金子，昆仑山出美玉，最好的剑叫“巨阙”，最好的珠叫“夜光”，水果里李子和柰最珍贵，蔬菜里芥菜和姜最重要。短短六十四个字，从天到地，从时间到物产，就像一部小小的百科全书。其中不少句子今天还在用，比如成语“寒来暑往”。

In English

The Thousand Character Classic was one of China’s most famous primers: exactly one thousand characters with not a single one repeated, arranged in rhyming four-character phrases that children could chant and memorise. This selection is its first sixteen phrases. It begins at the very beginning: “Heaven is dark, earth is yellow; the universe was vast and wild.” Then the sun and moon wax, wane and set, and the stars take their places. Cold comes and heat goes; autumn is for harvest, winter for storing. Ancient astronomy appears too — leap months that keep the calendar in line, and musical pitch-pipes used to track the seasons. Clouds rise and bring rain, dew gathers and turns to frost. Then the text moves down to earth and its treasures: gold from the Li River, jade from the Kunlun Mountains, the sword called Juque, the pearl called Night Shine, the plum and crab apple as the best fruits, mustard greens and ginger as the most valued vegetables. In sixty-four characters it travels from the sky to the market garden — a tiny encyclopedia. For a learner it is also a word bank: many of these phrases, like 寒来暑往 (“cold comes, heat goes”), are still used today.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Zhou Xingsi (died 521) was an official under Emperor Wu of Liang. Tradition says the emperor gave him a thousand separate characters copied from the calligraphy of Wang Xizhi and asked him to turn them into a rhyming text — and that he finished in one night, his hair turning white from the effort.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

辰宿 chén xiù（星宿的“宿”读 xiù，不读 sù）；昃 zè；冬藏 cáng；律吕 lǜ lǚ；调阳 tiáo；露结为霜的“为”读 wéi；丽水 lì shuǐ（古地名，指今云南金沙江一带，传统读 lì；今浙江丽水市读 lí）；巨阙 jù què；称 chēng；李柰 nài；重 zhòng；芥 jiè。机器朗读“宿”“柰”容易读错，已在“朗读用字”列替换同音字，音频需人工听验。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行本。《千字文》全文二百五十句、一千字，这里节选开头十六句（从“天地玄黄”到“菜重芥姜”），每两句排成一行，共八行。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

天地玄黄，宇宙洪荒。

tiān dì xuán huáng, yǔ zhòu hóng huāng

The sky is deep and dark, the earth is yellow; the universe began vast and untamed.

日月盈昃，辰宿列张。

rì yuè yíng zè, chén xiù liè zhāng

The moon waxes, the sun sinks west; the stars spread out in their places.

寒来暑往，秋收冬藏。

hán lái shǔ wǎng, qiū shōu dōng cáng

Cold arrives and heat departs; autumn brings the harvest, winter keeps it safe.

闰余成岁，律吕调阳。

rùn yú chéng suì, lǜ lǚ tiáo yáng

Extra days add up to a leap month; the pitch-pipes keep the seasons in tune.

云腾致雨，露结为霜。

yún téng zhì yǔ, lù jié wéi shuāng

Clouds rise and turn to rain; dew settles and turns to frost.

金生丽水，玉出昆冈。

jīn shēng lì shuǐ, yù chū kūn gāng

Gold comes from the Li River, jade from the Kunlun Mountains.

剑号巨阙，珠称夜光。

jiàn hào jù què, zhū chēng yè guāng

The finest sword is Juque, the finest pearl is Night Shine.

果珍李柰，菜重芥姜。

guǒ zhēn lǐ nài, cài zhòng jiè jiāng

Plums and crab apples are the prize fruits; mustard greens and ginger, the prize vegetables.
