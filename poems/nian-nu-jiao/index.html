导读About this poem

Standing by the Yangtze where a famous Three Kingdoms battle was fought, an exiled Su Shi pictures the young general Zhou Yu winning it with a laugh — then looks at his own grey hair and pours his wine out to the river and the moon.

直译LiteralRiver and mountains like a painting — at that time, how many heroes!

意译MeaningRiver and hills like a painting — and in that age, so many heroes!

下阕Second stanza
05
遥想公瑾当年，小乔初嫁了，雄姿英发。

直译LiteralI think far back to Gongjin in those years: Xiao Qiao had just married him; heroic in bearing, brilliant and spirited.

意译MeaningI picture Zhou Yu back then — Xiao Qiao newly his bride, and he in his prime, bold and bright.

06
羽扇纶巾，谈笑间，樯橹灰飞烟灭。

直译LiteralFeather fan, silk scarf; amid talk and laughter, masts and oars flew off as ash and vanished as smoke.

意译MeaningFeather fan in hand, silk scarf on his head, he laughed and talked as the enemy fleet went up in ash and smoke.

07
故国神游，多情应笑我，早生华发。

直译LiteralMy spirit wanders the old land; the sentimental one should laugh at me — early grown grey hair.

意译MeaningMy mind wanders through that old kingdom — anyone would laugh at me, so full of feeling, grey before my time.

08
人生如梦，一尊还酹江月。

直译LiteralLife is like a dream; one cup I pour back as an offering to the river moon.

意译MeaningLife is a dream. I pour a cup of wine onto the river, for the moon.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

苏轼因为写诗被人诬告，被贬到黄州（今湖北黄冈）。他游览城外的赤壁，想起三国时周瑜在赤壁大败曹操的故事，写下这首词。开头“大江东去”气势很大：长江向东奔流，千百年来的英雄人物都被浪花冲走了。接着写赤壁的景色：乱石冲向天空，大浪拍打江岸，卷起的浪花像千堆白雪。下阕集中写周瑜：他当年刚娶了美女小乔，英俊潇洒，拿着羽毛扇、戴着青丝头巾，说说笑笑之间就把曹操的战船烧成了灰。这是用对比的手法：周瑜年轻有为，而苏轼自己四十多岁、头发已白、被贬在外，一事无成。最后他说“人生如梦”，把一杯酒洒向江中的明月来祭奠。这里有感伤，但更多的是看开。这首词风格豪放（气势雄壮、感情奔放），是宋词“豪放派”的代表作。注意：苏轼游的黄州赤壁并不是真正的古战场，他在词里也只说“人道是”（人们说是）。

In English

Su Shi had been falsely accused over his poems and banished to Huangzhou (today’s Huanggang in Hubei). Visiting the Red Cliff outside town, he thought of the Battle of Red Cliff in 208, when the young general Zhou Yu destroyed Cao Cao’s huge fleet with fire. The opening line is enormous: the great river flows east, and its waves have washed away every hero of a thousand years. Then the scene: jagged rocks pierce the sky, waves smash the shore and throw up spray like “a thousand heaps of snow.” The second half is about Zhou Yu. Newly married to the beautiful Xiao Qiao, dashing and confident, holding a feather fan and wearing a silk scarf, he turned the enemy’s ships to ash and smoke while chatting and laughing. Then the poem turns on a contrast: Su Shi, in his forties, already grey and stuck in exile, has achieved nothing like that. “Life is like a dream,” he says, and pours a cup of wine onto the river and the reflected moon as an offering. There is sadness here, but also a man letting go. Its grand scale and bold feeling made this the model of the “heroic and unrestrained” (豪放) style of Song lyrics. Note the small honest phrase 人道是, “people say”: the cliff Su Shi visited was probably not the real battlefield, and he knew it.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Su Shi (1037–1101) was banished to Huangzhou in 1080 after the “Crow Terrace Poetry Case”, in which his poems were said to mock the government. This lyric is traditionally dated to 1082, during that exile; the battle it recalls took place in 208, near the end of the Han dynasty.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

羽扇纶巾：纶 读 guān（青丝头巾），不读 lún。小乔初嫁了：了 本网站按通行朗读读 le，也有人读 liǎo。一尊还酹江月：还 本网站读 huán（意为“回、再”，把酒洒还给江月；也有人读 hái）；酹 lèi。早生华发：华 huá，发 fà（头发）。雄姿英发：发 fā。樯橹 qiáng lǔ。故垒 lěi。卷起 juǎn。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

大江东去，浪淘尽，千古风流人物。

dà jiāng dōng qù, làng táo jìn, qiān gǔ fēng liú rén wù

The great river rolls east, and its waves have carried off the heroes of a thousand years.

故垒西边，人道是，三国周郎赤壁。

gù lěi xī biān, rén dào shì, sān guó zhōu láng chì bì

West of the old ramparts, people say, is the Red Cliff where young Zhou fought in the Three Kingdoms.

乱石穿空，惊涛拍岸，卷起千堆雪。

luàn shí chuān kōng, jīng tāo pāi àn, juǎn qǐ qiān duī xuě

Jagged rocks stab at the sky, wild waves slam the shore and toss up a thousand drifts of snow.

江山如画，一时多少豪杰。

jiāng shān rú huà, yī shí duō shǎo háo jié

River and hills like a painting — and in that age, so many heroes!

遥想公瑾当年，小乔初嫁了，雄姿英发。

yáo xiǎng gōng jǐn dāng nián, xiǎo qiáo chū jià le, xióng zī yīng fā

I picture Zhou Yu back then — Xiao Qiao newly his bride, and he in his prime, bold and bright.

羽扇纶巾，谈笑间，樯橹灰飞烟灭。

yǔ shàn guān jīn, tán xiào jiān, qiáng lǔ huī fēi yān miè

Feather fan in hand, silk scarf on his head, he laughed and talked as the enemy fleet went up in ash and smoke.

故国神游，多情应笑我，早生华发。

gù guó shén yóu, duō qíng yīng xiào wǒ, zǎo shēng huá fà

My mind wanders through that old kingdom — anyone would laugh at me, so full of feeling, grey before my time.

人生如梦，一尊还酹江月。

rén shēng rú mèng, yī zūn huán lèi jiāng yuè

Life is a dream. I pour a cup of wine onto the river, for the moon.
