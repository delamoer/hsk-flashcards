导读About this poem

A thousand mountains without a bird, ten thousand paths without a footprint — and in the middle of the snow, one old man fishing alone. Twenty characters that paint total stillness.

中文解析

这首诗像一幅黑白山水画。前两句画背景：千座山上没有一只鸟在飞，万条小路上看不到一个人的脚印——世界大得空荡荡，冷得静悄悄。后两句画人物：江上一条小船，船上一个穿蓑衣、戴斗笠的老人，一个人在大雪里钓鱼。“千山”“万径”是大，“孤舟”“独钓”是小，一大一小，对比特别强烈，越显得老人孤单。可他并不慌张，安安静静地坐着，好像在坚持什么。这首诗是柳宗元被贬到永州时写的，大家认为老渔翁就是诗人自己：处境寒冷孤独，心里却不肯低头。有意思的是，四句的第一个字连起来是“千万孤独”，这可能是巧合，却正好说出了全诗的感觉。

In English

This poem reads like an ink painting in black and white. The first two lines set the background: on a thousand mountains, not one bird is flying; on ten thousand paths, not one footprint. The world is huge, empty, frozen and silent. The last two lines add a figure: on the river, a small boat; in the boat, an old man in a straw raincoat and hat, fishing alone in the snow. “A thousand mountains” and “ten thousand paths” are vast; “a lone boat” and “fishing alone” are tiny. The contrast makes the old man look even lonelier — yet he is calm, sitting quietly as if holding on to something. Liu Zongyuan wrote this after being exiled to Yongzhou in the south, and readers have long seen the fisherman as the poet himself: in a cold, lonely place, but refusing to give in. A fun detail: the first characters of the four lines read 千万孤独, “utterly alone”. It may be a coincidence, but it sums up the poem perfectly.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Liu Zongyuan (773–819) was a leading writer of prose and poetry in the mid-Tang. After a failed political reform in 805 he was exiled to Yongzhou (in today’s Hunan) for ten years; this poem is thought to date from that time.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

千山鸟飞绝，

qiān shān niǎo fēi jué

Over a thousand hills, no bird in flight;

万径人踪灭。

wàn jìng rén zōng miè

on ten thousand paths, no trace of man.

孤舟蓑笠翁，

gū zhōu suō lì wēng

One boat — an old man in straw cape and hat

独钓寒江雪。

dú diào hán jiāng xuě

fishing alone in the snow on the cold river.
