导读About this poem

A homesick traveller drifts by boat through rain in late spring. It ends with one of the best-loved lines in Chinese about time slipping away: “the cherries turn red, the banana leaves turn green.”

这首诗被谱成了歌Sung as a song

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

作者坐船经过吴江（今江苏苏州一带），心里有一片“春愁”，想用酒来浇灭。上阕写一路所见：江上的船在摇，岸上酒楼的酒旗在招手；经过“秋娘渡”和“泰娘桥”这些好听的地名，可是风雨飘飘萧萧，让人更愁。下阕写想家：什么时候才能回家，洗去旅途的风尘？想象家里调笙弹曲、点着心字形的香，温暖安宁。最后三句最有名：时光最容易把人抛在后面，樱桃红了，芭蕉绿了，春天就这样过去了。“红了”“绿了”把形容词当动词用，颜色在变，就是时间在走，写得非常生动。词里“风又飘飘，雨又萧萧”“银字笙调，心字香烧”句式整齐，读起来有音乐感。蒋捷因为这一句，被人称为“樱桃进士”。

In English

The poet is travelling by boat past Wujiang, near Suzhou, carrying a “spring sorrow” he would like to drown in wine. The first half is what he sees on the way: his boat rocking on the river, a tavern flag beckoning from a building on the shore. He passes places with lovely names — Autumn Maiden Ferry, Tai Maiden Bridge — but the wind blows and the rain patters, and the sorrow only grows. The second half turns to home: when will he get back and wash the dust of the road from his robe? He pictures home — someone tuning a reed-pipe, coil incense burning in the shape of the character 心 (“heart”). Then the famous ending: time so easily leaves people behind — the cherries have turned red, the banana leaves have turned green. Here the colour words 红 and 绿 work as verbs: the colours are changing, and that change is time passing. The paired, rhythmic phrases (“wind blowing and blowing, rain pattering and pattering”) give the whole piece a musical feel. The last line was so admired that Jiang Jie was nicknamed “the Cherry Scholar”.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Jiang Jie (c. 1245–c. 1305) passed the highest civil examination in 1274, just before the Southern Song fell to the Mongols. Refusing to serve the new Yuan rulers, he lived in seclusion, and much of his work reflects a life of wandering.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

酒浇：浇 jiāo。秋娘渡：渡 dù。笙调：调 tiáo（调弄乐器）。红了、绿了：了 le，轻声。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行教材版本。个别本子“秋娘渡与泰娘桥”作“秋娘容与泰娘娇”，“何日归家洗客袍”作“何日云帆卸浦桥”，网站不用。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

一片春愁待酒浇。

yī piàn chūn chóu dài jiǔ jiāo

A whole spring’s sorrow, waiting for wine to drown it.

江上舟摇，楼上帘招。

jiāng shàng zhōu yáo, lóu shàng lián zhāo

My boat rocks on the river; a tavern flag waves from a window.

秋娘渡与泰娘桥，

qiū niáng dù yǔ tài niáng qiáo

Past Autumn Maiden Ferry, past Tai Maiden Bridge,

风又飘飘，雨又萧萧。

fēng yòu piāo piāo, yǔ yòu xiāo xiāo

the wind keeps gusting, the rain keeps whispering down.

何日归家洗客袍？

hé rì guī jiā xǐ kè páo

When will I be home, washing the road from my robe?

银字笙调，心字香烧。

yín zì shēng tiáo, xīn zì xiāng shāo

Tuning the silver-inlaid pipe, lighting heart-shaped incense.

流光容易把人抛，

liú guāng róng yì bǎ rén pāo

How easily time runs on and leaves us behind:

红了樱桃，绿了芭蕉。

hóng le yīng táo, lǜ le bā jiāo

the cherries have turned red, the banana leaves green.
