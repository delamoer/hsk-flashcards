Mooring Below Beigu Hill

导读About this poem

A traveller’s boat sails down the wide Yangtze at the turn of the year; the sun rises out of the sea before night is over, and spring arrives before the old year ends. He sends his longing home with the geese.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

“次”是停船过夜的意思。北固山在今天江苏镇江的长江边。诗人从洛阳一带到江南，在北固山下停船时写了这首诗。第一联：旅途在青山之外延伸，小船在绿水上前进。第二联：春潮涨满，江面和两岸齐平，显得格外开阔；顺风吹着，一片帆端端正正地挂着。这两句画面宽阔平稳，是写江景的名句。第三联最有名：夜还没完全过去，一轮红日已经从海上升起；旧的一年还没结束，江上已经有了春意。这里不说“太阳升起”，而说海日从“残夜”里“生”出来；不说“春天来了”，而说江春“入”了旧年——新的东西在旧的东西还没走完时就到来，既写景，又含有自然更替、新生力量不可阻挡的道理。宰相张说非常喜欢这两句，亲手写下来挂在政事堂里。最后一联回到思乡：家书怎么寄回去呢？只能托北归的大雁带到洛阳了。统编教材七年级上册选了这首诗。

In English

次 means to stop a boat for the night. Beigu Hill stands on the Yangtze at today’s Zhenjiang, Jiangsu. The poet, travelling from the Luoyang area to the south, moored here and wrote this poem. Couplet one: the traveller’s road runs on beyond the green hills; his boat moves forward on green water. Couplet two: the spring tide has risen so high that the river is level with its banks and looks enormously wide; the wind blows straight, and a single sail hangs full and steady. It is a calm, spacious picture, often quoted. Couplet three is the most famous: the sun is born out of the sea while the night is not yet over; spring on the river enters the old year before it has ended. The poet doesn’t just say “the sun rose” or “spring came” — the new arrives while the old is still leaving. It is a scene, but also an idea: nature renews itself, and new life cannot be held back. The chief minister Zhang Yue loved these lines so much that he wrote them out by hand and hung them in the government hall. The last couplet returns to the traveller’s feelings: how can his letter reach home? Only the geese flying north can carry it to Luoyang. The poem is taught in Chinese middle schools.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Wang Wan (c. 693–751) was from Luoyang and passed the imperial examination around 712; only about ten of his poems survive. He wrote this on a journey south along the Yangtze, probably in the early 720s.

客路青山外，

kè lù qīng shān wài

My road runs on beyond the green hills;

行舟绿水前。

xíng zhōu lǜ shuǐ qián

my boat glides ahead on the green water.

潮平两岸阔，

cháo píng liǎng àn kuò

The tide is full, the river wide between its banks;

风正一帆悬。

fēng zhèng yī fān xuán

the wind blows true, and a single sail hangs high.

海日生残夜，

hǎi rì shēng cán yè

The sun is born from the sea before the night is gone;

江春入旧年。

jiāng chūn rù jiù nián

spring reaches the river while the old year lingers.

乡书何处达？

xiāng shū hé chù dá

How will my letter ever get home?

归雁洛阳边。

guī yàn luò yáng biān

Let the geese flying north carry it to Luoyang.
