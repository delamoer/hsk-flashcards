导读About this poem

Caught in a spring shower without rain gear, everyone else scrambles; Su Shi keeps walking and singing. A small walk in the rain becomes his whole attitude to life — written in exile, after he had nearly been executed.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

词前有一段小序：三月七日，在沙湖路上遇到下雨，拿雨具的人先走了，同行的人都很狼狈，只有我不觉得；不久天晴了，所以写了这首词。上阕写雨中：别去听雨穿过树林、打在叶子上的声音，不妨一边吟诗长啸，一边慢慢走。拄着竹杖、穿着草鞋，比骑马还轻快，“谁怕？”——一件蓑衣，在风雨里过一辈子也无所谓。下阕写雨后：带着寒意的春风把酒意吹醒，有点冷，山头的斜阳却迎面照来。回头看刚才风雨萧瑟的地方，走吧，对我来说，既没有风雨，也没有晴天。“风雨”和“晴”表面写天气，其实比喻人生的挫折和顺利。苏轼当时刚经历“乌台诗案”，被贬黄州，这首词写出他面对困境的从容和豁达：不被坏事打倒，也不为好事狂喜。

In English

A short preface explains the scene: on the seventh day of the third month, on the road to Sandy Lake, it began to rain. The servants carrying the rain gear had gone ahead; everyone was miserable, except Su Shi, who didn’t mind. Soon it cleared, so he wrote this. The first half is in the rain. Don’t listen to it drumming on the leaves, he says — why not chant a poem and walk on slowly? With a bamboo stick and straw sandals he feels lighter than on horseback. “Who’s afraid?” A straw raincoat is enough to live a whole life in mist and rain. The second half comes after the rain. A chilly spring wind sobers him up; it is a little cold, but the setting sun on the hilltop comes out to meet him. Looking back at the place where the storm was, he heads home: for him there is “neither wind and rain, nor clear sky”. The weather is a picture of life’s ups and downs. Su Shi had just survived a political trial, the “Crow Terrace case”, and been exiled to Huangzhou. The poem shows a mind that bad luck cannot knock over and good luck cannot carry away — which is why Chinese readers still quote it in hard times.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Su Shi (1037–1101), also called Su Dongpo, was a Song official, poet and calligrapher. In 1079 he was jailed for poems said to mock government policy and was then exiled to Huangzhou (in today’s Hubei). He wrote this there in the spring of 1082.

版本说明 About the text

采用通行版本。词前原有小序：“三月七日，沙湖道中遇雨。雨具先去，同行皆狼狈，余独不觉。已而遂晴，故作此词。”网站正文只收词，不收序。“芒鞋”古本或写作“芒鞵”（异体字），无其他重要异文。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

莫听穿林打叶声，

mò tīng chuān lín dǎ yè shēng

Never mind the rain that rattles through the leaves —

何妨吟啸且徐行。

hé fáng yín xiào qiě xú xíng

why not sing out loud and take my time?

竹杖芒鞋轻胜马，

zhú zhàng máng xié qīng shèng mǎ

A bamboo stick, straw sandals: I go lighter than on horseback.

谁怕？一蓑烟雨任平生。

shuí pà, yī suō yān yǔ rèn píng shēng

Afraid? Not I. In one straw cape I’ll take whatever rain life brings.

料峭春风吹酒醒，

liào qiào chūn fēng chuī jiǔ xǐng

A sharp spring wind blows the wine out of my head —

微冷，山头斜照却相迎。

wēi lěng, shān tóu xié zhào què xiāng yíng

a little cold, and yet the low sun on the hill comes out to meet me.

回首向来萧瑟处，

huí shǒu xiàng lái xiāo sè chù

I look back at the stretch where the storm was blowing,

归去，也无风雨也无晴。

guī qù, yě wú fēng yǔ yě wú qíng

and walk home — to me it was neither storm nor sunshine.
