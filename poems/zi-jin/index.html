导读About this poem

A girl paces on the city gate tower, waiting for her love, who hasn’t come or even sent word. Its last line — one day apart feels like three months — is still how Chinese people talk about missing someone.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

诗中的女子在城楼上等她的心上人。“青青子衿”——她记得他衣领的颜色，一想到就心里悠悠不断地思念。第一、二章她有点埋怨：就算我不去找你，难道你就不能捎个信、不能自己来吗？这种又想念又生气的语气非常真实。第三章写她在城楼上走来走去，坐立不安，最后说出全诗最有名的一句：“一日不见，如三月兮！”一天没见到你，就像过了三个月那么长。诗人用夸张的说法写出思念的焦急，今天中国人还常说“一日不见，如隔三秋”。后来曹操在《短歌行》里借用“青青子衿，悠悠我心”来表达对人才的渴望，所以这两句又有了新的意思。

In English

A young woman waits for her love on the city wall. “Blue, blue is your collar” — she remembers even the colour of his collar, and the thought of it fills her with longing. In the first two verses she sounds a little cross: even if I don’t go to you, couldn’t you at least send word? Couldn’t you come yourself? That mix of missing someone and being annoyed with them feels completely modern. In verse 3 she paces back and forth on the gate tower, unable to settle, and then comes the famous line: 一日不见，如三月兮 — “one day without seeing you is like three months.” This kind of deliberate exaggeration (kuāzhāng) makes her impatience easy to feel. Chinese people still say 一日不见，如隔三秋, “one day apart is like three autumns.” Centuries later the warlord-poet Cao Cao borrowed the opening lines in his “Short Song” to say how much he longed for talented men to join him, so 青青子衿，悠悠我心 also came to mean yearning for a worthy companion.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

The Book of Songs gathers 305 songs from about the 11th to the 6th century BC. This one is from the state of Zheng (郑, in today’s central Henan), whose songs were famous — and in Confucius’ eyes notorious — for their open, passionate love poems. The traditional commentary read “blue collar” as the dress of students, but most modern readers see a simple love song.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

衿 jīn；宁 nìng（难道）；嗣 sì；挑兮达兮 tāo xī tà xī（来回走动的样子，“挑”不读 tiǎo，“达”不读 dá）；城阙 què。“宁”“挑”“达”机器朗读易错，已在“朗读用字”列替换同音字。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

青青子衿，悠悠我心。

qīng qīng zǐ jīn, yōu yōu wǒ xīn

Blue, so blue, your collar — on and on my heart goes missing you.

纵我不往，子宁不嗣音？

zòng wǒ bù wǎng, zǐ nìng bù sì yīn

Even if I don’t come to you, couldn’t you send me a word?

青青子佩，悠悠我思。

qīng qīng zǐ pèi, yōu yōu wǒ sī

Blue, so blue, the cord of your jade — on and on my thoughts go after you.

纵我不往，子宁不来？

zòng wǒ bù wǎng, zǐ nìng bù lái

Even if I don’t come to you, couldn’t you come to me?

挑兮达兮，在城阙兮。

tāo xī tà xī, zài chéng què xī

Back and forth, back and forth I pace on the gate tower.

一日不见，如三月兮！

yī rì bù jiàn, rú sān yuè xī

One day without you feels like three whole months!
