导读About this poem

A boatman sings to a prince in a language the prince can’t understand; translated, it becomes one of China’s oldest love songs. Its last line — “The hill has trees, the trees have branches; my heart loves you, and you do not know” — is still quoted today.

这首诗被谱成了歌Sung as a song

周迅《越人歌》Zhou Xun

In Feng Xiaogang's film The Banquet (2006), Zhou Xun sings the ancient "Song of the Boatman of Yue" to music by Tan Dun; the words are the pre-Qin original, ending "my heart delights in you, but you do not know".

中文解析

据西汉刘向《说苑》记载，楚国的鄂君子晳坐船游玩，划船的越国人用越语唱了一首歌，鄂君听不懂，请人翻译成楚语，就是这首诗。歌里说：今晚是什么样的晚上啊，我在河中划着船；今天是什么样的日子啊，能和王子同坐一条船。承蒙您看得起我，不嫌弃我身份低微；我心里乱极了，因为认识了王子。最后两句最有名：山上有树，树上有枝，我心里喜欢你，你却不知道。“枝”和“知”同音，这种借同音字表达心意的手法叫“双关”，后来民歌里常常用。鄂君听懂之后，拥抱了这位越人，还把绣花的被子盖在他身上。这首歌是中国最早的翻译诗之一，写出了身份悬殊的两个人之间说不出口的爱慕。

In English

According to the Han-dynasty book Garden of Stories (说苑), Lord E of Chu was out boating when the boatman, a man of Yue, sang to him in the Yue language. The lord couldn’t understand, so he had it translated into the language of Chu — and this is the result. The singer marvels: what night is this, that I am rowing in midstream; what day is this, that I share a boat with a prince? You honour me and do not scorn my low birth; my heart is in turmoil, because I have come to know a prince. Then come the famous last lines: the hill has trees, the trees have branches; my heart delights in you, and you do not know. In Chinese, “branch” (枝 zhī) and “know” (知 zhī) sound the same, so the image of the tree carries a hidden echo of the feeling. This kind of pun, called shuāngguān (双关, “double meaning”), became a favourite trick of Chinese love songs. The story says the lord, once he understood, embraced the boatman and wrapped him in an embroidered quilt. It is one of the earliest translated poems in China, and a tender picture of love across a great gap in rank.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

The song is recorded in Liu Xiang’s Garden of Stories (1st century BC), which places it in the 6th century BC. The singer was a boatman from Yue serving Lord E (Zixi), a prince of Chu; the original Yue words are also preserved, written in Chinese characters for their sound.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

搴 qiān（拔取，这里指划船）；訾 zǐ（嫌弃）；诟 gòu；几 jī（几乎）；被 bèi。机器朗读易错处已在“朗读用字”列替换同音字，音频需人工听验。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

今夕何夕兮，搴舟中流。

jīn xī hé xī xī, qiān zhōu zhōng liú

What night is this, that I row out into the middle of the river?

今日何日兮，得与王子同舟。

jīn rì hé rì xī, dé yǔ wáng zǐ tóng zhōu

What day is this, that I share a boat with a prince?

蒙羞被好兮，不訾诟耻。

méng xiū bèi hǎo xī, bù zǐ gòu chǐ

I am nobody, yet you are kind to me and do not look down on me.

心几烦而不绝兮，得知王子。

xīn jī fán ér bù jué xī, dé zhī wáng zǐ

My heart is in a tangle that will not ease, because I have met you, my prince.

山有木兮木有枝，心悦君兮君不知。

shān yǒu mù xī mù yǒu zhī, xīn yuè jūn xī jūn bù zhī

On the hill there are trees, on the trees there are branches — and in my heart there is you, though you don’t know.
