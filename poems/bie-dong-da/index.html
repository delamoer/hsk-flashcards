导读About this poem

A farewell on a cold, windy day that turns gloom into encouragement: don’t worry about having no friends ahead — everyone under heaven knows you.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这是高适送别朋友董大时写的诗。董大是当时有名的琴师董庭兰，“大”是他在兄弟中排行第一。前两句写送别时的天气：千里天空都是昏黄的云，太阳也暗淡无光；北风呼呼地吹，大雁南飞，大雪纷纷落下。这样阴冷的景色，本来最容易让人伤感。可后两句却一下子振作起来：不要担心前面的路上没有知心朋友，天下哪个人不认识你呢？一般的送别诗多写依依不舍、伤心落泪，这首诗却用豪迈的语气安慰和鼓励朋友。当时高适和董大都很不得志，生活贫困，诗人还能说出这么有力量的话，更让人感动。“莫愁前路无知己，天下谁人不识君”今天仍常用来为远行的人送上祝福。

In English

Gao Shi wrote this when parting from his friend Dong Da — the famous zither player Dong Tinglan; “Da” (“eldest”) means he was the first-born son. The first two lines describe the day: for a thousand li the sky is full of yellowish clouds and the sun is dim; the north wind blows, wild geese head south, and snow falls thick and fast. A bleak scene like that usually leads to sad goodbyes. But the second half suddenly lifts: don’t worry that you’ll have no true friends on the road ahead — who under heaven doesn’t know you? Most farewell poems dwell on tears and reluctance; this one cheers the friend on in a bold, generous voice. At the time both men were poor and out of luck, which makes the encouragement even more moving. The last two lines are still used today to send someone off on a long journey with good wishes.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Gao Shi (c. 704–765) was a Tang poet best known for frontier poems; unusually for a poet, he later rose to high office. He wrote two poems for Dong Tinglan, a celebrated qin player, around 747, when both were struggling; this is the first.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

千里黄云白日曛，

qiān lǐ huáng yún bái rì xūn

Yellow clouds for a thousand miles, the sun gone dim;

北风吹雁雪纷纷。

běi fēng chuī yàn xuě fēn fēn

the north wind drives the geese through falling snow.

莫愁前路无知己，

mò chóu qián lù wú zhī jǐ

Don’t fear the road ahead will hold no friends —

天下谁人不识君。

tiān xià shuí rén bù shí jūn

is there anyone under heaven who doesn’t know you?
