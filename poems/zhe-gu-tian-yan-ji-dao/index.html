导读About this poem

Years after a night of wine, song and dance, a poet meets the singer he has dreamed of ever since — and keeps lifting the lamp to her face, afraid this, too, is only a dream.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首词写久别重逢。上阕回忆当年的宴会：一位穿着彩色衣袖的歌女热情地捧着酒杯劝酒，词人也不顾一切地喝到满脸通红。“舞低杨柳楼心月，歌尽桃花扇底风”是名句：跳舞跳到月亮从柳树梢的楼头落下去，唱歌唱到手里画着桃花的扇子再也扇不出风——用夸张的写法说那一夜玩得多么尽兴。下阕写分别以后，多少次在梦里和她相见；今天真的见面了，却拿着银灯一次又一次地照她的脸，还怕这又是一场梦。全词用“对比”串起来：过去和现在、梦和真。上阕颜色浓艳（彩、玉、红、杨柳、桃花），下阕语言朴素，正好写出从热闹到思念、再到又惊又喜的心情。

In English

This lyric is about meeting someone again after a long separation. The first stanza looks back at a party long ago: a singer in brightly coloured sleeves kept offering him wine, and he drank without holding back until his face was red. Lines three and four are the famous ones: they danced until the moon sank below the willows by the tower, and sang until the fans painted with peach blossoms had no breeze left in them. It is playful exaggeration — a way of saying the night went on and on. The second stanza jumps to the years apart, when he met her again and again only in dreams. Now she is really in front of him, yet he keeps raising the silver lamp to look at her, afraid he will wake up. The whole poem is built on contrast — then and now, dream and reality. The first half is full of rich colour (silk, jade, red, willow green, peach pink); the second half is plain and quiet, which makes the final moment of disbelief feel even more real.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Yan Jidao (c. 1038–1110) was the youngest son of the prime minister and poet Yan Shu; together they are known as the “Two Yans”. He grew up in luxury, saw his family decline, and wrote mostly tender love lyrics, many about singing girls he had known in better days.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

拚却 pàn què（“拚”这里是舍弃、豁出去的意思，读 pàn，也有读 pīn 的）。殷勤 yīn qín；银釭 yín gāng（釭：灯）；剩把 shèng bǎ。机器朗读“拚、釭”容易读错，已在“朗读用字”列换成同音字，音频需人工听验。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行文本（《小山词》）。维基文库本“拚却”作“拼却”、“银釭”作“银缸”，意思相同；“剩把”一作“賸把”。另有版本作“楼头月”“歌罢”，网站从通行本作“楼心月”“歌尽”。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

彩袖殷勤捧玉钟，

cǎi xiù yīn qín pěng yù zhōng

Your bright silk sleeves, the jade cup you kept pressing on me —

当年拚却醉颜红。

dāng nián pàn què zuì yán hóng

that year I let myself go, and drank until my face was red.

舞低杨柳楼心月，

wǔ dī yáng liǔ lóu xīn yuè

We danced until the moon above the willow tower sank low,

歌尽桃花扇底风。

gē jìn táo huā shàn dǐ fēng

sang until your peach-blossom fan had no breeze left to give.

从别后，忆相逢，

cóng bié hòu, yì xiāng féng

Ever since we parted I have gone back to that night;

几回魂梦与君同。

jǐ huí hún mèng yǔ jūn tóng

how often my dreaming soul has found its way to you.

今宵剩把银釭照，

jīn xiāo shèng bǎ yín gāng zhào

Tonight I keep raising the silver lamp to your face,

犹恐相逢是梦中。

yóu kǒng xiāng féng shì mèng zhōng

still afraid that this meeting, too, is only a dream.
