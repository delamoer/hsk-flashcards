导读About this poem

A boat drifts up a mountain creek near Shaoxing, and the poet discovers a strange truth: the cicadas’ noise makes the woods feel quieter, birdsong makes the hills feel deeper. One couplet here became one of the most quoted in Chinese poetry.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

若耶溪在今天浙江绍兴，是有名的风景地。诗人坐船进入溪中：船儿轻轻漂荡，天空和溪水一样悠远辽阔。远山上升起阴凉的云霞，阳光追着回旋的水流闪动。接下来是全诗最有名的一联：“蝉噪林逾静，鸟鸣山更幽。”蝉叫得越热闹，树林反而显得越安静；鸟一叫，山就更幽深了。这是“以动衬静”（用声音、动作来衬托安静）的手法：在真正安静的地方，一点声音都听得特别清楚，而听得清楚，正说明四周很静。这两句在当时就被认为是“文外独绝”，后来王维“月出惊山鸟，时鸣春涧中”也是同一种写法。最后两句转到自己：这样的地方让人想回家、想隐居，多年在外奔波做官，真是厌倦了。美景越是宁静，越让人感到奔波的疲惫。

In English

Ruoye Creek, near today’s Shaoxing in Zhejiang, was a famous beauty spot. The poet’s boat drifts in; sky and water stretch away together, endless and calm. Shady clouds rise from the far peaks, and sunlight chases the swirling current. Then comes the couplet everyone knows: “The cicadas shrill, and the woods grow stiller; a bird calls, and the hills feel deeper.” It sounds like a paradox, but anyone who has walked in a forest knows it is true: only in a really quiet place can you hear one bird so clearly — so the sound proves the silence. Chinese critics call this “using movement to set off stillness”. People of his own time already praised these two lines as unmatched, and later poets such as Wang Wei used the same trick. The last two lines turn inward: a place like this makes him long to go home and live quietly; after so many years of travelling for office, he is weary of it all. The more peaceful the scenery, the more tired of his busy life he feels.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Wang Ji (dates uncertain, active early 6th century) was an official and poet of the Liang dynasty who held posts in the Kuaiji region (around today’s Shaoxing). He is remembered almost entirely for this poem, and above all for its third couplet.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

艅艎 读 yú huáng（大船）。岫 读 xiù（山峰）。阳景 的“景”读 jǐng，指日光。逾 读 yú。“鸟鸣山更幽”的“更”读 gèng。长年 读 cháng nián。机器朗读“艅艎”“岫”易读错，已在“朗读用字”列换成同音字，音频需人工听验。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行本（与《古今图书集成》所录一致）。末句“悲倦游”，《佩文斋咏物诗选》作“叹倦游”。《梁书·王籍传》只引了“蝉噪林逾静，鸟鸣山更幽”一联，称当时以为“文外独绝”。全诗八句，网站每句一行。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

艅艎何泛泛，

yú huáng hé fàn fàn

Our boat drifts lightly on and on;

空水共悠悠。

kōng shuǐ gòng yōu yōu

sky and water stretch away as one.

阴霞生远岫，

yīn xiá shēng yuǎn xiù

Cool clouds gather over far-off peaks,

阳景逐回流。

yáng jǐng zhú huí liú

and sunlight chases the eddying stream.

蝉噪林逾静，

chán zào lín yú jìng

The cicadas shrill, and the woods grow stiller;

鸟鸣山更幽。

niǎo míng shān gèng yōu

a bird calls, and the hills feel deeper.

此地动归念，

cǐ dì dòng guī niàn

A place like this makes me long to go home —

长年悲倦游。

cháng nián bēi juàn yóu

so many years, so tired of roaming for office.
