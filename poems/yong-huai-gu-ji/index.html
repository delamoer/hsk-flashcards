咏怀古迹·其三

Yǒng Huái Gǔ Jì · Qí Sān
Thoughts on Historic Sites (No. 3)

导读About this poem

Passing the village where the beauty Wang Zhaojun was born, Du Fu retells her story — married off to a nomad king, buried in the northern desert — and hears her sorrow still alive in the music of the pipa.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

王昭君是汉代宫女，传说宫中画师毛延寿因为她不肯行贿，把她画得很丑，汉元帝从没见过她；后来朝廷和匈奴和亲，她被送去塞外，元帝临别才发现她非常美，但已经来不及了。她最后死在北方，墓叫“青冢”。杜甫路过她的家乡，写下这首诗。首联气势很大：千山万壑都奔向荆门，那里还留着昭君出生长大的村子——好像山也为她而来。颔联写她一生：一离开汉宫，就是连着北方沙漠的路；最后只留下一座青色的坟墓，对着黄昏。颈联批评皇帝：只凭画像怎么能认识她美丽的面容？她只能在月夜让灵魂戴着环佩空空地回来。尾联说：千年来琵琶弹着胡地的曲子，曲中分明诉说着她的怨恨。诗人同情昭君，也寄托了自己有才不被重用、漂泊他乡的感慨。

In English

Wang Zhaojun was a palace lady of the Han dynasty. The story goes that a court painter, angry that she would not bribe him, painted her portrait plain, so the emperor never sent for her. When the Han made a marriage alliance with the Xiongnu nomads, she was chosen to go north; only at the farewell did the emperor see how beautiful she was, and by then it was too late. She died in the north, and her grave is called the Green Tomb (青冢). Du Fu passed her home village and wrote this poem. The opening is grand: a thousand mountains and ten thousand valleys rush toward Jingmen, where the village she grew up in still stands — as if the land itself came to honour her. The second couplet is her whole life in two lines: once she left the palace, the road led into the northern desert; all that remains is a green grave facing the dusk. The third couplet blames the emperor: how could a painting show him her face? Only on moonlit nights does her spirit come home, its jade pendants jingling, to an empty palace. The last couplet: for a thousand years the pipa has played tunes of the nomad lands, and in them her grief is clearly heard. Du Fu pities Zhaojun — and through her, voices his own sense of talent wasted and a life spent far from home.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Du Fu (712–770) wrote the five “Thoughts on Historic Sites” in 766 in Kuizhou, each about a famous person linked to the region. Wang Zhaojun (1st century BC) is traditionally said to have come from Zigui, in the Three Gorges area.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

群山万壑赴荆门，

qún shān wàn hè fù jīng mén

A thousand peaks, ten thousand valleys run toward Jingmen,

生长明妃尚有村。

shēng zhǎng míng fēi shàng yǒu cūn

where the village that raised Lady Zhaojun still stands.

一去紫台连朔漠，

yī qù zǐ tái lián shuò mò

She left the palace, and the road ran into northern sands;

独留青冢向黄昏。

dú liú qīng zhǒng xiàng huáng hūn

now only her green grave is left, facing the dusk.

画图省识春风面，

huà tú xǐng shí chūn fēng miàn

A painting was all he knew of her lovely face;

环佩空归夜月魂。

huán pèi kōng guī yè yuè hún

on moonlit nights her spirit comes home, jade chiming, to no one.

千载琵琶作胡语，

qiān zǎi pí pá zuò hú yǔ

For a thousand years the pipa has played the songs of the steppe,

分明怨恨曲中论。

fēn míng yuàn hèn qǔ zhōng lún

and her sorrow still speaks, plain as day, in the music.
