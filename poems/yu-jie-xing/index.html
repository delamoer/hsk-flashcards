导读About this poem

Leaves fall on the steps, the Milky Way hangs low, and the moon is as white as silk — every year on this night, the one he loves is a thousand li away. Too sad even to get drunk, he lies awake by a flickering lamp: the feeling sits on his brow and in his heart, and there is no escaping it.

上阕First stanza
01
纷纷坠叶飘香砌。

直译LiteralThick and fast, falling leaves drift onto the fragrant steps.

意译MeaningLeaves come drifting down onto the scented steps.

02
夜寂静，寒声碎。

直译LiteralThe night is still; cold sounds are broken and small.

意译MeaningThe night is hushed; only small cold sounds break it.

03
真珠帘卷玉楼空，天淡银河垂地。

直译LiteralThe pearl curtain is rolled up, the jade tower empty; the sky is pale, the Silver River hangs to the earth.

意译MeaningThe pearl blind is rolled up on an empty tower; in the pale sky the Milky Way hangs low to the ground.

04
年年今夜，月华如练，长是人千里。

直译LiteralYear after year, this night, the moonlight is like white silk — always the person is a thousand li away.

意译MeaningEvery year on this night the moon shines like white silk, and every year you are a thousand li away.

下阕Second stanza
05
愁肠已断无由醉。

直译LiteralMy sorrowful insides are already broken; there is no way to get drunk.

意译MeaningMy heart is already broken; I can’t even get drunk.

06
酒未到，先成泪。

直译LiteralBefore the wine arrives, it first becomes tears.

意译MeaningBefore the wine reaches me, it has turned to tears.

07
残灯明灭枕头欹，谙尽孤眠滋味。

直译LiteralThe dying lamp flickers, the pillow slants; I know to the full the taste of sleeping alone.

意译MeaningThe lamp gutters on and off, my pillow lies crooked — I know every taste of sleeping alone.

08
都来此事，眉间心上，无计相回避。

直译LiteralAll told, this matter — between the brows, upon the heart — there is no way to avoid it.

意译MeaningAdd it all up: this feeling sits on my brow or weighs on my heart, and there is no way around it.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首词写秋夜里对远方亲人的思念。上阕写景。黄叶纷纷落在台阶上，夜很静，只听见落叶和秋虫细碎的声音。珍珠帘卷起来，楼里空空的，只剩自己一个人；天色清淡，银河低低地垂向大地。月光像白色的丝绸一样，年年这一夜，月亮都这么美，可是心上人总在千里之外。下阕写愁。愁到肠都断了，连借酒醉一场都做不到：酒还没喝下去，就先变成了眼泪。这比“酒入愁肠，化作相思泪”又进了一步。灯快灭了，忽明忽暗，枕头歪斜着，一个人睡不着的滋味已经尝尽了。最后三句最有名：算来这件心事，不是挂在眉头，就是压在心上，没有办法躲开。“眉间心上”把看不见的愁写得好像有形状、会移动。后来李清照写“才下眉头，却上心头”，就是从这里化出来的。

In English

This lyric is about missing someone on an autumn night. The first half is the setting. Yellow leaves drift onto the fragrant stone steps; the night is so still that only small, cold sounds break it — falling leaves, autumn insects. The pearl curtain is rolled up and the jade tower is empty except for him. The sky is pale, and the Milky Way seems to hang down to the earth. Moonlight is “like white silk”: every year on this night the moon is just as lovely, and every year the one he loves is a thousand li away. The second half is pure sorrow. He is so heartbroken that he can’t even get drunk — before the wine reaches him, it has already become tears. (Compare Fan’s other lyric, where wine turns into tears after it is drunk; here it doesn’t even get that far.) The lamp is guttering, flaring and fading, the pillow is askew, and he has learned every flavour of sleeping alone. The famous ending: add it all up, and this feeling is either on his brow or in his heart — there’s no getting away from it. 眉间心上 makes an invisible sadness seem to have a shape and move around. The woman poet Li Qingzhao later reworked the idea in her own line “it has just left my brow, and now it climbs into my heart.”

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Fan Zhongyan (989–1052) was a Northern Song statesman, general and reformer. Only a handful of his lyrics survive; when and for whom this one was written is not known.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

纷纷坠叶飘香砌。

fēn fēn zhuì yè piāo xiāng qì

Leaves come drifting down onto the scented steps.

夜寂静，寒声碎。

yè jì jìng, hán shēng suì

The night is hushed; only small cold sounds break it.

真珠帘卷玉楼空，天淡银河垂地。

zhēn zhū lián juǎn yù lóu kōng, tiān dàn yín hé chuí dì

The pearl blind is rolled up on an empty tower; in the pale sky the Milky Way hangs low to the ground.

年年今夜，月华如练，长是人千里。

nián nián jīn yè, yuè huá rú liàn, cháng shì rén qiān lǐ

Every year on this night the moon shines like white silk, and every year you are a thousand li away.

愁肠已断无由醉。

chóu cháng yǐ duàn wú yóu zuì

My heart is already broken; I can’t even get drunk.

酒未到，先成泪。

jiǔ wèi dào, xiān chéng lèi

Before the wine reaches me, it has turned to tears.

残灯明灭枕头欹，谙尽孤眠滋味。

cán dēng míng miè zhěn tóu qī, ān jìn gū mián zī wèi

The lamp gutters on and off, my pillow lies crooked — I know every taste of sleeping alone.

都来此事，眉间心上，无计相回避。

dōu lái cǐ shì, méi jiān xīn shàng, wú jì xiāng huí bì

Add it all up: this feeling sits on my brow or weighs on my heart, and there is no way around it.
