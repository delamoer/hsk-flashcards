导读About this poem

At 65, standing where ancient heroes once stood, Xin Qiji remembers the generals who marched north — and the ones who rushed it and lost. A warning to his own government, ending with an old general asking: does anyone still want me?

千古江山，英雄无觅，孙仲谋处。

直译LiteralThe land through the ages — its hero cannot be found, the place of Sun Zhongmou.

意译MeaningThe land is ageless, but its heroes are gone — no Sun Quan here now.

02
舞榭歌台，风流总被雨打风吹去。

直译LiteralDance pavilions and song terraces — their glory all beaten by rain and blown away by wind.

意译MeaningHis dancing halls and singing terraces, all that splendour, washed off by rain, blown off by wind.

03
斜阳草树，寻常巷陌，人道寄奴曾住。

直译LiteralSlanting sun on grass and trees, ordinary lanes and alleys — people say Jinu once lived here.

意译MeaningLow sun on grass and trees, a plain little lane — they say Liu Yu lived here once.

04
想当年，金戈铁马，气吞万里如虎。

直译LiteralThink of those years: golden spears, iron horses, spirit swallowing ten thousand li like a tiger.

意译MeaningThink of him then — bright spears, armoured horses, a tiger swallowing ten thousand miles.

下阕Second stanza
05
元嘉草草，封狼居胥，赢得仓皇北顾。

直译LiteralIn Yuanjia, hastily, to raise an altar on Langjuxu — and won only a panicked look to the north.

意译MeaningIn the Yuanjia years they rushed to win a famous victory, and won only a frightened backward glance.

06
四十三年，望中犹记，烽火扬州路。

直译LiteralForty-three years — gazing, I still remember the beacon fires on the Yangzhou road.

意译MeaningForty-three years ago — looking out, I can still see the war fires along the Yangzhou road.

07
可堪回首，佛狸祠下，一片神鸦社鼓。

直译LiteralHow can I bear to look back? Below the shrine of Bili, all sacred crows and village drums.

意译MeaningHow can I bear to look back? At the shrine of the enemy emperor, crows peck offerings and festival drums beat.

08
凭谁问：廉颇老矣，尚能饭否？

直译LiteralWho will ask: Lian Po is old — can he still eat?

意译MeaningWho will come and ask: “Lian Po is old — can he still eat a good meal?”

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首词是辛弃疾六十多岁在镇江（古称京口）做官时，登上北固亭写的。“怀古”就是借古人古事说今天的事。上阕想起两个英雄：三国时的孙权（字仲谋）在这里建都，南朝宋武帝刘裕（小名寄奴）从这里起兵北伐，“金戈铁马，气吞万里如虎”。可是英雄已经找不到了，当年的歌舞楼台也被风雨吹打干净。下阕转向反面的例子：刘裕的儿子宋文帝在元嘉年间草草北伐，想像汉朝名将那样“封狼居胥”，结果大败，只落得仓皇回头看追兵。这是在提醒当时的朝廷：北伐要准备好，不能草率。接着词人想起四十三年前自己南归时扬州一带战火连天；如今北魏皇帝的祠庙前，百姓烧香敲鼓，好像忘了那是敌人。最后用战国名将廉颇自比：廉颇老了，还有人来问他“还能吃饭吗”，可是谁会来问我呢？用了很多典故，但每个典故都在说同一件事：他想报国，却没人用他。

In English

Xin Qiji wrote this in his mid-sixties as prefect of Zhenjiang (old name Jingkou), climbing the Beigu Pavilion above the Yangtze. 怀古, “thinking of the past”, means using history to talk about the present. The first half recalls two heroes of this very place: Sun Quan of the Three Kingdoms, who made his base here, and Liu Yu (childhood name Jinu), who set out from here and marched north “like a tiger swallowing ten thousand li”. But the heroes are gone, and wind and rain have swept away their singing halls. The second half turns to a warning. Liu Yu’s son launched a hasty northern campaign in the Yuanjia era, dreaming of a famous Han victory, and fled in panic, looking back at the enemy behind him. Xin is telling his own court: don’t rush a war you haven’t prepared for. He then remembers riding south through burning Yangzhou forty-three years earlier, and notes with alarm that local people now beat drums at a shrine to a former enemy emperor, as if they had forgotten. Finally he becomes Lian Po, the old general of the Warring States whose king sent someone to ask whether he could still eat a good meal. The difference is, nobody is asking Xin. The many allusions all say one thing: he is ready to serve, and no one wants him.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Xin Qiji (1140–1207) was recalled to office in old age and made prefect of Zhenjiang in 1204, as the chief minister Han Tuozhou planned a war against the Jin. This lyric is dated to 1205; the campaign of 1206 was launched in haste and failed, much as he had feared.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

词牌“永遇乐”的“乐”读 yuè。舞榭 xiè；斜阳 xié yáng；巷陌 xiàng mò；寄奴 jì nú；曾住 céng；当年 dāng；狼居胥 láng jū xū；仓皇 cāng huáng；佛狸 bì lí（北魏太武帝的小名，“佛”在此读 bì，不读 fó）；可堪 kě kān；廉颇 lián pō；饭否 fàn fǒu。机器朗读“佛狸”必错，已在“朗读用字”列替换同音字。

千古江山，英雄无觅，孙仲谋处。

qiān gǔ jiāng shān, yīng xióng wú mì, sūn zhòng móu chù

The land is ageless, but its heroes are gone — no Sun Quan here now.

舞榭歌台，风流总被雨打风吹去。

wǔ xiè gē tái, fēng liú zǒng bèi yǔ dǎ fēng chuī qù

His dancing halls and singing terraces, all that splendour, washed off by rain, blown off by wind.

斜阳草树，寻常巷陌，人道寄奴曾住。

xié yáng cǎo shù, xún cháng xiàng mò, rén dào jì nú céng zhù

Low sun on grass and trees, a plain little lane — they say Liu Yu lived here once.

想当年，金戈铁马，气吞万里如虎。

xiǎng dāng nián, jīn gē tiě mǎ, qì tūn wàn lǐ rú hǔ

Think of him then — bright spears, armoured horses, a tiger swallowing ten thousand miles.

元嘉草草，封狼居胥，赢得仓皇北顾。

yuán jiā cǎo cǎo, fēng láng jū xū, yíng dé cāng huáng běi gù

In the Yuanjia years they rushed to win a famous victory, and won only a frightened backward glance.

四十三年，望中犹记，烽火扬州路。

sì shí sān nián, wàng zhōng yóu jì, fēng huǒ yáng zhōu lù

Forty-three years ago — looking out, I can still see the war fires along the Yangzhou road.

可堪回首，佛狸祠下，一片神鸦社鼓。

kě kān huí shǒu, bì lí cí xià, yī piàn shén yā shè gǔ

How can I bear to look back? At the shrine of the enemy emperor, crows peck offerings and festival drums beat.

凭谁问：廉颇老矣，尚能饭否？

píng shuí wèn: lián pō lǎo yǐ, shàng néng fàn fǒu

Who will come and ask: “Lian Po is old — can he still eat a good meal?”
