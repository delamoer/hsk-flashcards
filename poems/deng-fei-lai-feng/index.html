导读About this poem

A young official climbs a tall pagoda on a mountain and declares that drifting clouds can’t block his view — because he is standing at the very top. A short poem full of ambition.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

王安石三十岁左右写了这首诗，当时他年轻有为，对未来充满信心。前两句写飞来峰上有一座很高的塔（“千寻”是夸张，形容极高），听说在塔上鸡一叫就能看到太阳升起。后两句是全诗的精华：不怕浮云遮住远望的眼睛，因为自己站在最高的地方。这两句有双层意思：表面写登高看景，实际上说的是人生道理——只要站得高、看得远，就不会被眼前的小事或坏人（“浮云”）迷惑。这让人想起杜甫的“会当凌绝顶，一览众山小”。今天人们仍常引用“不畏浮云遮望眼”来表示有远见、不怕困难。

In English

Wang Anshi wrote this at about thirty, a rising young official full of confidence. The first two lines describe a pagoda on Feilai (“Flown-Here”) Peak, so tall that it measures “a thousand xun” — an exaggeration, since a xun was about eight feet. He has heard that from the top, when the rooster crows at dawn, you can already watch the sun come up. The last two lines are the heart of the poem: he is not afraid of drifting clouds blocking his view, because he is standing on the highest level. On the surface it is about sightseeing; underneath it is about how to live. If your vision is high and broad enough, petty distractions — or people who try to mislead you, the “floating clouds” — can’t cloud your judgement. Readers often pair it with Du Fu’s famous line about climbing Mount Tai and seeing all other mountains look small. Chinese people still quote “not afraid of floating clouds blocking the view” to praise someone with vision and courage.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Wang Anshi (1021–1086) was a chancellor of the Northern Song famous for his sweeping political reforms. He wrote this poem in 1050, passing through on his way home after a term as a county magistrate in Zhejiang, years before he came to power.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

“千寻”的“寻”读 xún（古代长度单位）。“自缘”的“缘”读 yuán（因为）。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行教材文本（统编初中语文七年级下册）。飞来峰的位置有两说：教材注为浙江绍兴城外的宝林山，也有人认为是杭州灵隐寺前的飞来峰。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

飞来山上千寻塔，

fēi lái shān shàng qiān xún tǎ

On Flying-Here Peak stands a pagoda that soars into the sky;

闻说鸡鸣见日升。

wén shuō jī míng jiàn rì shēng

they say that at first cockcrow you can watch the sun come up.

不畏浮云遮望眼，

bù wèi fú yún zhē wàng yǎn

I don’t fear drifting clouds will block my view,

自缘身在最高层。

zì yuán shēn zài zuì gāo céng

for I am standing at the very top.
