导读About this poem

A thousand-year-old travel poster for Hangzhou: misty willows and painted bridges, a hundred thousand households, the roaring Qiantang tide, West Lake with its osmanthus and lotus, and music on the water day and night.

上阕First stanza
01
东南形胜，三吴都会，钱塘自古繁华。

直译LiteralThe finest site of the southeast, the great city of the Three Wu — Qiantang has been flourishing since ancient times.

意译MeaningThe jewel of the southeast, capital of the old Wu lands — Qiantang has thrived since time began.

02
烟柳画桥，风帘翠幕，参差十万人家。

直译LiteralMisty willows, painted bridges, wind-curtains, green drapes — uneven rows of a hundred thousand households.

意译MeaningWillows like mist, painted bridges, curtains and green drapes stirring in the breeze, a hundred thousand homes rising high and low.

03
云树绕堤沙，怒涛卷霜雪，天堑无涯。

直译LiteralCloud-high trees circle the sandy dykes; angry waves roll up frost and snow; the natural moat has no shore.

意译MeaningTowering trees line the sandy dykes; the raging tide flings up waves like frost and snow across a river with no far shore.

04
市列珠玑，户盈罗绮，竞豪奢。

直译LiteralMarkets display pearls and gems; households are full of silks — vying in luxury.

意译MeaningMarkets glitter with pearls, homes overflow with silk, each outdoing the next in splendour.

下阕Second stanza
05
重湖叠巘清嘉，有三秋桂子，十里荷花。

直译LiteralLayered lakes and stacked peaks are clear and fine; there is osmanthus in autumn and lotus for ten li.

意译MeaningLake beyond lake, peak upon peak, clear and lovely — osmanthus in autumn, and lotus flowers for miles.

06
羌管弄晴，菱歌泛夜，嬉嬉钓叟莲娃。

直译LiteralFlutes play in the sunshine; water-chestnut songs float at night; merry are the old anglers and lotus girls.

意译MeaningFlutes play under clear skies, picking songs drift over the night water, and old fishermen and lotus girls are all smiles.

07
千骑拥高牙，乘醉听箫鼓，吟赏烟霞。

直译LiteralA thousand horsemen escort the tall banner; flushed with wine he listens to pipes and drums, chanting and admiring the mist and clouds.

意译MeaningA thousand riders escort the governor’s banner; merry with wine, he hears pipes and drums and sings of the misty hills.

08
异日图将好景，归去凤池夸。

直译LiteralAnother day, paint this fine scenery, and go back to the Phoenix Pool to boast.

意译MeaningSomeday you’ll paint this view and take it back to court to show it off.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首词描写北宋时杭州的繁华和美景。上阕写城市。开头三句总写：杭州地处东南，地势优越，是三吴的大都会，自古繁华。接着一层层展开：如烟的柳树，彩绘的小桥，挡风的帘子，翠绿的帷幕，高高低低住着十万户人家。城外，大树环绕着钱塘江的沙堤，汹涌的潮水卷起霜雪一样的白浪，江面宽得望不到边。城里，市场摆满珍珠宝石，家家堆满绫罗绸缎，比赛谁更豪华。下阕写西湖和人：湖山重叠，清秀美丽；秋天桂花飘香，夏天荷花十里。白天笛声悠扬，夜里菱歌飘荡，钓鱼的老人、采莲的姑娘都很快乐。长官带着上千骑兵出游，醉中听乐，欣赏山水。最后说：将来把美景画下来，带回朝廷去夸耀。全词从大到小、由远及近，层次分明。相传金国皇帝读到“三秋桂子，十里荷花”，就动了南下的念头，这虽是传说，却说明这几句多么动人。

In English

This lyric celebrates Hangzhou at the height of the Northern Song — its wealth and its beauty. The first half is the city. Three opening lines sum it up: the best-placed spot in the southeast, the great metropolis of the Wu region, rich since ancient times. Then the details unfold layer by layer: willows soft as mist, painted bridges, curtains against the wind, green drapes, a hundred thousand households of every height. Outside the walls, tall trees ring the sandy dykes of the Qiantang River, whose famous tidal bore rolls in with waves white as frost and snow; the river is so wide it seems to have no far bank. Inside, the markets are full of pearls and gems and every home is stacked with silks, each trying to outshine the others. The second half moves to West Lake and its people: layers of water and peaks, clear and lovely; osmanthus in autumn, lotus for miles in summer; flutes playing on sunny days, water-chestnut songs drifting over the lake at night; old anglers and lotus-picking girls all enjoying themselves. The governor rides out with a thousand horsemen, listens to pipes and drums a little drunk, and admires the misty scenery. One day, the poet says, you’ll paint this and take it back to court to show off. The description is laid out from large to small, far to near, in clear layers. Legend says a Jin emperor read “osmanthus in autumn, lotus for ten li” and decided to invade the south — only a story, but it shows how seductive these lines are.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Liu Yong (c. 984–c. 1053) was the best-loved lyricist of the early Northern Song and a master of the long, slow lyric form (慢词). Tradition says he wrote this for Sun He, then the official in charge of Hangzhou, as a way of being introduced to him.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

东南形胜，三吴都会，钱塘自古繁华。

dōng nán xíng shèng, sān wú dū huì, qián táng zì gǔ fán huá

The jewel of the southeast, capital of the old Wu lands — Qiantang has thrived since time began.

烟柳画桥，风帘翠幕，参差十万人家。

yān liǔ huà qiáo, fēng lián cuì mù, cēn cī shí wàn rén jiā

Willows like mist, painted bridges, curtains and green drapes stirring in the breeze, a hundred thousand homes rising high and low.

云树绕堤沙，怒涛卷霜雪，天堑无涯。

yún shù rào dī shā, nù tāo juǎn shuāng xuě, tiān qiàn wú yá

Towering trees line the sandy dykes; the raging tide flings up waves like frost and snow across a river with no far shore.

市列珠玑，户盈罗绮，竞豪奢。

shì liè zhū jī, hù yíng luó qǐ, jìng háo shē

Markets glitter with pearls, homes overflow with silk, each outdoing the next in splendour.

重湖叠巘清嘉，有三秋桂子，十里荷花。

chóng hú dié yǎn qīng jiā, yǒu sān qiū guì zǐ, shí lǐ hé huā

Lake beyond lake, peak upon peak, clear and lovely — osmanthus in autumn, and lotus flowers for miles.

羌管弄晴，菱歌泛夜，嬉嬉钓叟莲娃。

qiāng guǎn nòng qíng, líng gē fàn yè, xī xī diào sǒu lián wá

Flutes play under clear skies, picking songs drift over the night water, and old fishermen and lotus girls are all smiles.

千骑拥高牙，乘醉听箫鼓，吟赏烟霞。

qiān jì yōng gāo yá, chéng zuì tīng xiāo gǔ, yín shǎng yān xiá

A thousand riders escort the governor’s banner; merry with wine, he hears pipes and drums and sings of the misty hills.

异日图将好景，归去凤池夸。

yì rì tú jiāng hǎo jǐng, guī qù fèng chí kuā

Someday you’ll paint this view and take it back to court to show it off.
