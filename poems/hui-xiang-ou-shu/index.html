导读About this poem

He left home as a boy and comes back an old man; his accent hasn’t changed, but the village children laugh and ask the “guest” where he’s from. Gentle, funny and a little sad.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

贺知章年轻时离开家乡去长安做官，八十多岁才回到故乡，写下这首诗。第一句“少小离家老大回”，用“少小”和“老大”对比，一句话就过去了五十多年。第二句说家乡口音一点没变，可是两鬓的头发已经稀疏斑白——不变的是乡音和对家乡的感情，变了的是容貌和岁月。后两句写了一个小场面：村里的孩子看见他，不认识，笑着问：“客人，你从哪里来呀？”孩子天真有礼，诗人却百感交集：明明回到了自己家，却被当成了客人。诗人没有直接说难过，而是用这个带点幽默的场景，写出人生易老、物是人非的感慨。语言像说话一样自然，所以流传很广，“乡音无改鬓毛衰”和“笑问客从何处来”今天都常被引用。

In English

He Zhizhang left his hometown as a young man to make a career in the capital and came back in his mid-eighties. The first line covers half a century in seven characters by setting “young” against “old”: left as a boy, back as an old man. The second line holds two things side by side: his home accent hasn’t changed at all, but the hair at his temples has grown thin and grey. What stayed the same is his speech and his love for home; what changed is his face. Then comes a small, vivid scene. The village children see him and don’t know who he is. Smiling and polite, they ask, “Where have you come from, sir?” — treating him as a visitor. The children are only being friendly, but for him the moment is full of feeling: he has come home, and he is a stranger in it. The poem never says “I am sad”. It lets a gently funny scene carry a big truth about time, ageing and the way places move on without us. Because the language is as plain as conversation, every child in China learns it, and 笑问客从何处来 is still quoted, often jokingly, whenever someone returns after a long time away.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

He Zhizhang (659–744) was a high official at the Tang court, a famous calligrapher and a cheerful drinker; it was he who first called Li Bai a “banished immortal”. In 744 he retired, became a Daoist priest and went home to Yuezhou (today’s Shaoxing, Zhejiang), where he wrote two poems titled “Coming Home”; this is the first.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

“少小”的“少”读 shào（年轻），不读 shǎo。“鬓毛衰”的“衰”：统编教材注 shuāi（衰老、稀疏）；旧读 cuī（与“回”“来”押韵），不少老师仍读 cuī。网站拼音按统编教材采用 shuāi。“何处”的“处”读 chù。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

少小离家老大回，

shào xiǎo lí jiā lǎo dà huí

I left home young, and come back old;

乡音无改鬓毛衰。

xiāng yīn wú gǎi bìn máo shuāi

my accent is the same, but my hair is thin and grey.

儿童相见不相识，

ér tóng xiāng jiàn bù xiāng shí

The children look at me — they don’t know who I am;

笑问客从何处来。

xiào wèn kè cóng hé chù lái

smiling, they ask, “Sir, where are you from?”
