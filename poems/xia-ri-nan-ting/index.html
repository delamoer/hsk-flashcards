夏日南亭怀辛大

Xià Rì Nán Tíng Huái Xīn Dà
Missing Xin the Eldest at the South Pavilion on a Summer Day
孟浩然Mèng Hàorán · Meng Haoran唐 Tang

导读About this poem

A summer evening at its most pleasant — moonrise over a pond, the scent of lotus on the breeze, dew dripping from bamboo — and one thing missing: a friend to share it with.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗写夏天傍晚在水亭里乘凉，因而想念朋友辛大。前六句写景，处处是“清凉”：太阳忽然从西边落下，池上的月亮慢慢从东边升起；诗人披散开头发，打开窗户，躺在宽敞安静的亭子里。最美的是“荷风送香气，竹露滴清响”——风吹过荷花，把香气送过来；竹叶上的露水滴下来，发出清脆的声音。一句写嗅觉，一句写听觉，能听见露水滴落，说明夜有多静。这么好的夜晚，诗人想拿琴来弹，却遗憾没有“知音”来听。“知音”出自伯牙和钟子期的故事：钟子期死后，伯牙再也不弹琴，因为没有人懂他的琴声了。最后两句点题：因此想起老朋友，半夜里还在梦中想念他。全诗从景写到情，淡淡的，却很真挚。

In English

On a summer evening the poet cools off in a pavilion by the water and finds himself missing his friend Xin. The first six lines are all about coolness: the sun suddenly drops in the west, the moon slowly rises over the pond in the east; he lets his hair down, opens the window and lies back in the quiet, airy room. The best-known couplet is “the lotus breeze brings its fragrance, dew drips from the bamboo with a clear sound” — one line for smell, one for hearing. If you can hear dew falling, the night must be very still. On such a perfect night he wants to play his qin zither, but there is no zhīyīn (知音), “one who knows the music”, to listen. The word comes from the story of Bo Ya and Zhong Ziqi: when Zhong died, Bo Ya never played again, because no one else understood his music. Today 知音 still means a true friend who understands you. The last two lines bring us to the title: thinking of this, he misses his old friend, and at midnight he is still dreaming of him. The feeling moves gently from scenery to friendship — understated, but sincere.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Meng Haoran (689–740) spent most of his life as a recluse near his hometown of Xiangyang (in today’s Hubei) and is famous for nature poetry. Xin the Eldest (辛大, “eldest” in his family) is thought to be his friend Xin E, who lived in the hills nearby; little else is known of him.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

山光忽西落，

shān guāng hū xī luò

The sun slips suddenly behind the western hills;

池月渐东上。

chí yuè jiàn dōng shàng

slowly the moon climbs over the pond in the east.

散发乘夕凉，

sàn fà chéng xī liáng

I let down my hair to take in the evening cool,

开轩卧闲敞。

kāi xuān wò xián chǎng

open the window, and lie back in the airy quiet.

荷风送香气，

hé fēng sòng xiāng qì

A breeze from the lotus carries its scent;

竹露滴清响。

zhú lù dī qīng xiǎng

dew drips from the bamboo — I can hear each drop.

欲取鸣琴弹，

yù qǔ míng qín tán

I would take down my zither and play,

恨无知音赏。

hèn wú zhī yīn shǎng

but there is no one here who would understand.

感此怀故人，

gǎn cǐ huái gù rén

And so I think of you, my old friend,

中宵劳梦想。

zhōng xiāo láo mèng xiǎng

and all night long I miss you, even in my dreams.
