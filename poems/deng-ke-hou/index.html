导读About this poem

After years of failure, a 46-year-old finally passes the imperial exam — and gallops through the capital in a single day, drunk on joy. This short poem gave Chinese two everyday idioms for success.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

孟郊一生贫苦，考进士考了好几次都没考上，四十六岁才终于考中。这首诗写的就是放榜后那一刻的狂喜。前两句是对比：过去那些不如意、窘迫的日子，不值得再提了；今天心情完全放开，思绪飞得没有边际。“昔日”和“今朝”一对照，压了多年的委屈一下子都放了出来。后两句把高兴写成画面：骑着马在春风里飞奔，一天之内就把长安城的花看了个遍。长安那么大，花那么多，一天怎么看得完？这正是夸张，写出人得意时觉得什么都快、什么都美的心情。“春风得意”和“走马观花”（由“一日看尽长安花”而来）后来都成了成语，今天还常用，不过“走马观花”现在多指看得粗略、不深入。

In English

Meng Jiao was poor all his life and failed the top-level imperial exam several times; he finally passed at forty-six. This poem captures the moment the results came out. The first two lines are a contrast: the cramped, humiliating days of the past are not worth mentioning any more; today his heart is thrown wide open and his thoughts fly without limit. Setting “former days” against “this morning” releases years of pent-up frustration at once. The last two lines turn joy into a picture: he rides his horse at full speed through the spring breeze and sees every flower in Chang’an in a single day. The capital was huge — no one could really see all its flowers in a day. The exaggeration is the point: when you are on top of the world, everything feels fast and everything looks beautiful. Two idioms came from this poem and are still used daily: 春风得意 (“riding high in the spring breeze”, i.e. flushed with success) and 走马观花 (“viewing flowers from horseback”) — though the second now means looking at something only superficially.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Meng Jiao (751–814) was a Tang poet known for his bleak, hard-won verse and lifelong poverty; he and Jia Dao were later paired as poets of “cold and thin” style. He wrote this in 796 in Chang’an, on passing the jinshi exam after earlier failures.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

龌龊 wò chuò；“今朝”的“朝”读 zhāo（早晨，今天）；“看尽”的“看”读 kàn；“长安”的“长”读 cháng。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

昔日龌龊不足夸，

xī rì wò chuò bù zú kuā

Those years of scraping by are not worth a word —

今朝放荡思无涯。

jīn zhāo fàng dàng sī wú yá

today my heart breaks loose and my thoughts run free.

春风得意马蹄疾，

chūn fēng dé yì mǎ tí jí

Proud in the spring breeze, I let my horse fly,

一日看尽长安花。

yī rì kàn jìn cháng ān huā

and see every flower in Chang’an in a single day.
