七哀诗（明月照高楼）

Qī Āi Shī
Seven Sorrows (The Bright Moon Shines on a High Tower)
曹植Cáo Zhí · Cao Zhi魏晋南北朝 Wei, Jin & Southern and Northern Dynasties

导读About this poem

On a moonlit night a wife alone in a high tower pours out her grief: her husband left more than ten years ago, and their lives have drifted as far apart as dust on the road and mud in the water.

点 虚线字 看读音和意思 · Tap a dotted word for its sound and meaning

01
明月照高楼，流光正徘徊。

直译LiteralThe bright moon shines on the high tower; its flowing light is just lingering, back and forth.

意译MeaningThe bright moon shines on a high tower, its light drifting, unwilling to leave.

02
上有愁思妇，悲叹有余哀。

直译LiteralUp there is a sorrowful, longing wife; her sad sighs carry grief that never ends.

意译MeaningUp there a lonely wife is grieving; every sigh trails more sorrow.

03
借问叹者谁？言是客子妻。

直译LiteralMay I ask who is sighing? She says she is the wife of a traveller.

意译MeaningWho is it sighing? “I am the wife of a man who went far away.”

04
君行逾十年，孤妾常独栖。

直译LiteralYou left more than ten years ago; I, alone, always stay by myself.

意译Meaning“You have been gone more than ten years, and I have slept alone the whole time.

05
君若清路尘，妾若浊水泥。

直译LiteralYou are like dust on a clear road; I am like mud in muddy water.

意译MeaningYou are dust lifting off a clean road, I am mud at the bottom of a cloudy pool.

06
浮沉各异势，会合何时谐？

直译LiteralFloating and sinking, each takes a different way; when will meeting again be possible?

意译MeaningOne rises, one sinks — when could the two of us ever come together?

07
愿为西南风，长逝入君怀。

直译LiteralI wish to become the south-west wind, going far away into your arms.

意译MeaningI wish I were the south-west wind, blowing all the long way into your arms.

08
君怀良不开，贱妾当何依？

直译LiteralIf your arms truly do not open, on whom should I, humble wife, depend?

意译MeaningBut if your arms stay closed to me, where can I turn?”

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

诗从月光写起：明月照着高楼，月光好像在楼上来回移动，不肯离开——楼上正坐着一位满怀愁思的女子。接着诗人用问答的方式让她自己开口：丈夫离家已经十多年，她一直一个人住。最有名的是“君若清路尘，妾若浊水泥”：丈夫像路上飘起的轻尘，自己像水底沉下的泥，本来是同一种土，现在一浮一沉，再也难以相聚。这种用具体事物来比喻抽象感情的手法叫“比喻”，这里比得又新又准。她甚至想变成一阵西南风，吹进丈夫怀里；可最后一句又冷下来：如果他的怀抱不肯打开，我又能依靠谁？古人常认为曹植是借女子的口，写自己被哥哥曹丕冷落的苦闷，所以这首诗既能读成爱情诗，也能读成政治寄托。

In English

The poem opens with moonlight on a high tower — light that seems to linger and drift back and forth, as if it cannot bear to leave. Up in the tower sits a woman full of sorrow. The poet then lets her speak through a simple question and answer: who is sighing? The wife of a man who left home more than ten years ago, and she has lived alone ever since. The most famous lines compare the couple to dust and mud: he is like light dust rising from a clear road, she is like mud sinking in murky water. Dust and mud are really the same earth, but one floats and one sinks, so how can they ever meet again? Turning a feeling into a concrete picture like this is called bǐyù (metaphor), and this one is both fresh and exact. She wishes she could become the south-west wind and blow straight into his arms — but the last line turns cold again: if his arms will not open, whom can she lean on? Traditional readers believed Cao Zhi was speaking through the wife about himself, pushed aside by his brother the emperor. So the poem can be read as a love lament, or as a quiet political complaint — and it works both ways.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Cao Zhi (192–232) was a son of the warlord Cao Cao and one of the most gifted poets of his age. After his elder brother Cao Pi became emperor, Cao Zhi was kept away from court and closely watched; many readers have taken this poem as a veiled picture of that rejection.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

逾 yú；栖 qī；浊 zhuó；谐 xié；“愿为西南风”的“为”读 wéi（变成）；“借问叹者谁”的“谁”诗中读 shuí。

版本说明 About the text

采用《文选》本，全诗八联十六句。“言是客子妻”，有的本子作“宕子妻”或“荡子妻”，意思都是长年在外的人的妻子。《乐府诗集》另收一首文字相近而较长的《怨诗行》，此不涉及。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

明月照高楼，流光正徘徊。

míng yuè zhào gāo lóu, liú guāng zhèng pái huái

The bright moon shines on a high tower, its light drifting, unwilling to leave.

上有愁思妇，悲叹有余哀。

shàng yǒu chóu sī fù, bēi tàn yǒu yú āi

Up there a lonely wife is grieving; every sigh trails more sorrow.

借问叹者谁？言是客子妻。

jiè wèn tàn zhě shuí, yán shì kè zǐ qī

Who is it sighing? “I am the wife of a man who went far away.”

君行逾十年，孤妾常独栖。

jūn xíng yú shí nián, gū qiè cháng dú qī

“You have been gone more than ten years, and I have slept alone the whole time.

君若清路尘，妾若浊水泥。

jūn ruò qīng lù chén, qiè ruò zhuó shuǐ ní

You are dust lifting off a clean road, I am mud at the bottom of a cloudy pool.

浮沉各异势，会合何时谐？

fú chén gè yì shì, huì hé hé shí xié

One rises, one sinks — when could the two of us ever come together?

愿为西南风，长逝入君怀。

yuàn wéi xī nán fēng, cháng shì rù jūn huái

I wish I were the south-west wind, blowing all the long way into your arms.

君怀良不开，贱妾当何依？

jūn huái liáng bù kāi, jiàn qiè dāng hé yī

But if your arms stay closed to me, where can I turn?”
