导读About this poem

A cold, rainy farewell by the river — and one of the most famous lines in Chinese about keeping a clean conscience: “a heart of ice in a jade pot”.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这是王昌龄在镇江芙蓉楼送别朋友辛渐时写的。辛渐要去洛阳，王昌龄当时被贬到南方做小官。前两句写景：冰冷的夜雨下满了江面，好像跟着人一起来到吴地；天亮送走朋友，只剩楚地的山孤零零地立着。“孤”字表面写山，其实写的是送走朋友后自己的孤单。后两句是诗人托朋友带的话：洛阳的亲友如果问起我，就告诉他们，我的心像放在玉壶里的一块冰，清清白白。王昌龄当时受人诽谤，这句话是在说：不管别人怎么说，我的品格没有变。用一样具体的东西（冰、玉壶）来代表看不见的品质，这种手法叫“托物言志”。“一片冰心在玉壶”后来成了表示为人纯洁、坦荡的名句。

In English

Wang Changling wrote this while seeing off his friend Xin Jian at Hibiscus Tower in Runzhou (today’s Zhenjiang), on the Yangtze. Xin Jian was heading north to Luoyang; Wang himself had been sent south to a minor post, a demotion. The first two lines set the scene: cold rain spreads over the river all night, as if it had followed them into the old land of Wu; at dawn he sees his friend off, and the mountains of Chu stand alone. On the surface “alone” (孤) describes the mountains, but really it is the poet who is left alone. The last two lines are a message he asks his friend to carry: if family and friends in Luoyang ask about me, tell them my heart is like a piece of ice in a jade pot — clear and spotless. Wang had been the target of slander, so this is his answer: whatever people say, I have not changed. Using a concrete object (ice, a jade pot) to stand for an invisible quality is called tuō wù yán zhì, “expressing one’s ideals through an object”. The line 一片冰心在玉壶 is still quoted today to describe someone pure and honest.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Wang Changling (c. 698–757) was a leading High Tang poet, known for his quatrains. He wrote this around 742, while serving as an assistant magistrate in Jiangning (near today’s Nanjing) after being demoted; the original set has two poems, and this is the first.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

寒雨连江夜入吴，

hán yǔ lián jiāng yè rù wú

All night cold rain merged with the river as it swept into Wu;

平明送客楚山孤。

píng míng sòng kè chǔ shān gū

at dawn I see you off, and the hills of Chu stand alone.

洛阳亲友如相问，

luò yáng qīn yǒu rú xiāng wèn

If my family and friends in Luoyang ask after me,

一片冰心在玉壶。

yī piàn bīng xīn zài yù hú

say: my heart is still clear ice in a jade pot.
