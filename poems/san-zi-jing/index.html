导读About this poem

For 700 years Chinese children began school by chanting these three-character lines: people are born good, learning keeps them that way, and parents and teachers share the job. Easy rhythm, real ideas about education — and great reading practice.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

《三字经》是古代儿童的启蒙课本，每句三个字，两句一押韵，读起来像儿歌，好记好背。这里选的是开头一段，讲的都是教育。开头说：人刚出生时，本性都是善良的，彼此差不多；后来因为环境和学习不同，才变得差别很大。所以如果不好好教育，好的本性就会改变；教育最重要的是专心。接着讲了两个故事：孟子的母亲为了孩子的成长三次搬家，选择好邻居；孟子逃学，母亲剪断了正在织的布，告诉他学习半途而废就像这匹布一样没用。窦燕山教育有方，五个儿子都很有名。最后几句说：只养不教是父亲的错，教得不严是老师的懒；“玉不琢，不成器”——玉石不经过雕刻成不了器物，人不学习就不懂道理。今天的中国人还常常说“人之初，性本善”和“玉不琢，不成器”。

In English

The Three-Character Classic was the first schoolbook of traditional China. Every line has three characters and the lines rhyme in pairs, so it reads like a chant and sticks in the memory. This excerpt is the opening, and it is all about education. It begins: when people are born, their nature is good, and we are all much alike; it is habits and learning that later push us far apart. So without teaching, our good nature drifts away, and the key to teaching is focus. Then come two stories. Mencius’s mother moved house three times to find good neighbours for her son; when the boy skipped school, she cut through the cloth on her loom to show him that quitting halfway makes everything useless. Dou Yanshan raised his five sons the right way, and all of them became famous. The last lines spread the responsibility: feeding a child without teaching him is the father’s fault; teaching without strictness is the teacher’s laziness. “Jade not carved does not become a vessel; a person who does not learn does not know what is right.” Chinese people still quote 人之初，性本善 and 玉不琢，不成器 today.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

The Three-Character Classic is traditionally attributed to the Song scholar Wang Yinglin (1223–1296), though some credit other Song or later authors; it was expanded over the centuries. For about 700 years it was the first text Chinese children memorised at school.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

“教”按通行注音：苟不教、教之道、养不教、教不严读 jiào，教五子读 jiāo。择邻处 chǔ（居住）；窦燕山 dòu yān shān（燕读第一声）；老何为 wéi；琢 zhuó。机器朗读这几处容易读错，已在“朗读用字”替换同音字，音频需人工听验。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行本《三字经》。全文一千多字（各版本长短不一），这里节选开头十四联，从“人之初”到“不知义”。章太炎《重订三字经》把“窦燕山，有义方，教五子”改为“荀季和，有义方，教八子”，网站用通行本。网站两个三字句一行。

人之初，性本善。

rén zhī chū, xìng běn shàn

When we are born, our nature is good.

性相近，习相远。

xìng xiāng jìn, xí xiāng yuǎn

By nature we are alike; by habit we grow apart.

苟不教，性乃迁。

gǒu bù jiào, xìng nǎi qiān

Without teaching, that nature will change;

教之道，贵以专。

jiào zhī dào, guì yǐ zhuān

the heart of teaching is focus.

昔孟母，择邻处。

xī mèng mǔ, zé lín chǔ

Mencius’s mother once moved to find good neighbours;

子不学，断机杼。

zǐ bù xué, duàn jī zhù

when her son skipped lessons, she cut the cloth on her loom.

窦燕山，有义方。

dòu yān shān, yǒu yì fāng

Dou Yanshan knew the right way to raise a child;

教五子，名俱扬。

jiāo wǔ zǐ, míng jù yáng

he taught five sons, and all five won renown.

养不教，父之过。

yǎng bù jiào, fù zhī guò

To feed a child but not teach him is the father’s fault;

教不严，师之惰。

jiào bù yán, shī zhī duò

to teach without care is the teacher’s idleness.

子不学，非所宜。

zǐ bù xué, fēi suǒ yí

A child who will not learn is not as he should be;

幼不学，老何为。

yòu bù xué, lǎo hé wéi

not learning when young, what will he do when old?

玉不琢，不成器。

yù bù zhuó, bù chéng qì

Uncarved jade will never be a fine vessel;

人不学，不知义。

rén bù xué, bù zhī yì

a person who does not learn will never know what is right.
