导读About this poem

A tiny night scene in twenty characters: one fishing lamp on a dark river, a breath of wind — and suddenly the whole river is full of stars.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

题目的意思是“在船上过夜，写下看到的景色”。前两句写静：没有月亮的黑夜里，只看见远处一盏渔船上的灯，孤零零的一点光，像一只萤火虫。后两句写动：微风吹来，水面泛起细细的波浪，灯光的倒影被打碎，散开来，变成满河的星星。一盏灯变成满河星，从“一点”到“满河”，从静到动，画面一下子亮了起来。诗人用了两个比喻：把灯光比作萤火，把碎开的倒影比作星星。“簇”字很传神，写出风把浪推成一小堆一小堆的样子。这首诗没有讲什么大道理，只是把一瞬间的美景准确地抓住了，语言简单，非常适合初学者读。

In English

The title simply means “spending the night on a boat, writing down what I saw”. The first two lines are still: on a moonless night, all he can see is one lamp on a distant fishing boat, a single point of light like a firefly. The last two lines move: a light breeze ruffles the water into little waves, and the lamp’s reflection breaks apart and scatters into a river full of stars. One light becomes countless lights; stillness becomes motion; the dark picture suddenly sparkles. The poet uses two comparisons — the lamp is a firefly, and its broken reflection is a sky of stars on the water. The verb 簇 (“to bunch up”) is precise: the wind pushes the water into small clustered ripples. There is no big message here, just one beautiful moment caught exactly as it happened. The vocabulary is simple, which makes it a lovely first poem for learners — and a good reminder that 见 (“to see”) and 星 (“star”) haven’t changed in three hundred years.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Zha Shenxing (1650–1727) was an early Qing poet from Haining, Zhejiang, admired for his fresh, precise descriptions of scenery. He spent many years travelling, and this poem records a night on a boat.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

月黑见渔灯，

yuè hēi jiàn yú dēng

No moon tonight — just one fishing lamp,

孤光一点萤。

gū guāng yī diǎn yíng

a lonely glow, small as a firefly.

微微风簇浪，

wēi wēi fēng cù làng

A soft wind gathers the water into ripples,

散作满河星。

sàn zuò mǎn hé xīng

and the light breaks into a river of stars.
