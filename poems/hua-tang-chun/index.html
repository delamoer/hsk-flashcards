导读About this poem

“One life, one lifetime, one pair of people” — the first line is so famous that couples put it on wedding invitations. The poem itself is sadder: two lovers who belong together are kept apart, and the poet reaches for old legends to say how impossible it feels.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

开头一句“一生一代一双人”最有名：一辈子，只想和一个人在一起。可是第二句马上转折：怎么偏偏让我们两地分开、各自伤心？“相思相望不相亲”，彼此想念、彼此望着，却不能在一起；连春天都不知道是为谁而来。下阕连用三个典故（古代的故事）：唐代裴航在蓝桥向一位老婆婆讨水喝，后来娶到了仙女，所以说“浆向蓝桥易乞”——遇到爱人好像不难；可是嫦娥偷吃灵药飞上月宫，再也回不来，所以“药成碧海难奔”——真正团聚却难上加难。最后说：如果能让我沿着天河去找到你，就算一起受穷也愿意。“饮牛津”指天河边牛郎饮牛的渡口，暗用牛郎织女的故事。全词用很多“相”字和对比，写出一种明知不可能却还是想要的深情。

In English

The opening line, 一生一代一双人, is the famous one: one life, one lifetime, and just the two of us. Then the second line turns: so why must we be in two places, each heartbroken? “Missing each other, gazing at each other, yet never close” — even spring seems to have no one to bloom for. The second half piles up allusions, old stories that a Chinese reader would know. In a Tang tale, a scholar named Pei Hang asked for a drink of water at Blue Bridge and ended up marrying an immortal girl: finding love, the poet says, can be easy. But the goddess Chang’e swallowed the elixir of immortality and flew to the moon, never to return: once the elixir is made, there is no running back across the sky. Meeting is easy; being together is the hard part. The last two lines dream of a way out: if only I could travel up the Milky Way to the ford where the Cowherd waters his ox and find you there, we would face each other and forget we were poor. The Cowherd and the Weaver Girl are the lovers allowed to meet only once a year. Notice how often 相 (“each other”) appears — the whole poem is about two people facing each other across a gap.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Nalan Xingde (1655–1685) was a Manchu noble and the Qing dynasty’s most celebrated ci poet. Who this poem was written for is not known; it is traditionally read either as a lament for his late wife or as a poem about a woman he loved but could not marry.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

一生一代一双人，

yī shēng yī dài yī shuāng rén

One life, one lifetime, the two of us —

争教两处销魂。

zhēng jiāo liǎng chù xiāo hún

so why are we left to grieve in two different places?

相思相望不相亲，

xiāng sī xiāng wàng bù xiāng qīn

We long for each other, we look toward each other, and never draw close;

天为谁春。

tiān wèi shuí chūn

who is this spring for, then?

浆向蓝桥易乞，

jiāng xiàng lán qiáo yì qǐ

A cup of water at Blue Bridge is easy enough to ask for,

药成碧海难奔。

yào chéng bì hǎi nán bēn

but once the elixir is swallowed, there is no flying back across the sky.

若容相访饮牛津，

ruò róng xiāng fǎng yìn niú jīn

If I could find you at the ford in the Milky Way where the Cowherd waters his ox,

相对忘贫。

xiāng duì wàng pín

we would sit face to face and never mind being poor.
