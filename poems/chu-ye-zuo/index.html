导读About this poem

Everyone else is home for Chinese New Year’s Eve; a traveller lies awake in a cold inn and realises his family, a thousand miles away, is thinking of him too — and tomorrow his grey hair will be one year older.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

除夕是中国人一年里最重要的团圆夜，可是诗人却一个人住在旅馆里。第一句写环境：旅馆里一盏冷冷的灯，只有他睡不着。第二句问自己：作客在外的心，为什么变得这么凄凉？答案在后两句，写得很巧妙：不说自己想家，而说家乡的亲人今夜正在思念千里之外的我——从对方写起，思念变成了双向的，更加深切。最后一句：明天早上，两鬓斑白的我又老了一岁。除夕是旧年和新年的交界，“又一年”三个字里，有思乡，也有时光流逝、一事无成的感叹。全诗只有二十八个字，却把节日里游子的孤独写得非常真切。

In English

New Year’s Eve is the most important family reunion of the Chinese year, yet the poet is alone in an inn. Line one sets the scene: a cold lamp in a lodging house, and he alone cannot sleep. Line two asks himself: why has a traveller’s heart turned so bleak? The answer comes in the last two lines, and it is cleverly told. He doesn’t say “I miss my family”; he says that tonight, back home, they are thinking of him, a thousand miles away. Writing from the other side makes the longing go both ways, and feel even deeper. The final line: tomorrow morning, with frost-grey temples, I will be one year older. New Year’s Eve is the line between the old year and the new, and the words “another year” hold homesickness as well as the feeling of time slipping by with nothing achieved. In just twenty-eight characters, the poem captures exactly how lonely a holiday can be far from home.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Gao Shi (c. 704–765) is best known for frontier poems; unusually for a poet, he rose to high office late in life. The date of this poem is uncertain; it is traditionally read as written during his long, poor years of travel before he found an official post.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

旅馆寒灯独不眠，

lǚ guǎn hán dēng dú bù mián

A cold lamp in the inn, and I alone awake —

客心何事转凄然。

kè xīn hé shì zhuǎn qī rán

why has a traveller’s heart turned so bleak?

故乡今夜思千里，

gù xiāng jīn yè sī qiān lǐ

Tonight, back home, they are thinking of me, a thousand miles off;

霜鬓明朝又一年。

shuāng bìn míng zhāo yòu yī nián

tomorrow my grey temples will be one year older.
