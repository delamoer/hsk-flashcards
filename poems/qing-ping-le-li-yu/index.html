清平乐（别来春半）

Qīng Píng Yuè
Pure Serene Music: Half of Spring Since We Parted
李煜Lǐ Yù · Li Yu宋 Song

导读About this poem

Half the spring has passed since someone dear went away. Plum petals fall like snow — brush them off and you’re covered again — and the sorrow of parting grows like spring grass: the farther you walk, the more of it there is.

直译LiteralSince parting, spring is half gone; whatever meets my eye breaks my tender heart.

意译MeaningHalf the spring has passed since you left; everything I see tears at my heart.

02

直译LiteralBelow the steps, fallen plum blossoms whirl like snow; I brush them off, and my whole body is covered again.

意译MeaningPlum petals tumble like snow below the steps; I brush them away, and I’m covered again.

下阕Second stanza
03
雁来音信无凭，路遥归梦难成。

直译LiteralThe geese come, but there is no news to rely on; the road is long, a dream of return is hard to have.

意译MeaningThe geese return with no word from you; the road is so long I can’t even dream my way home.

04
离恨恰如春草，更行更远还生。

直译LiteralThe sorrow of parting is just like spring grass: the farther you go, the more it still grows.

意译MeaningThis grief is like spring grass — walk as far as you like, it keeps on growing.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首词写离别后的思念。开头说：分别以后，春天已经过了一半，眼前看到的一切都让人伤心，“柔肠断”就是心都要碎了。接着是一幅很美的画面：台阶下白色的梅花瓣像雪一样纷纷飘落，落在人身上，刚拂掉，又落满了一身。这个动作写出了人站在花下很久，也写出了愁绪赶不走、拂不掉。下阕写盼望：大雁飞回来了，却没有带来远方的音信；路太远，连回家的梦都做不成。最后两句是全词最有名的比喻：离别的愁恨就像春天的草，人走得越远，草长得越多，愁也越深，没有尽头。把看不见的“愁”比作看得见、到处生长的春草，非常新鲜，又很贴切。传统说法认为，这首词是李煜思念被扣留在北宋的弟弟李从善而作。

In English

This lyric is about missing someone after a parting. It begins: since we separated, half the spring has gone by, and everything I see breaks my heart (柔肠断, “my tender insides are torn”). Then comes a lovely picture. Below the stone steps, white plum petals swirl down like snow; they land on him, he brushes them off, and in a moment he is covered again. That small gesture shows he has been standing under the tree for a long time — and that his sadness, like the petals, can’t be brushed away. The second half is about waiting. Wild geese, said in Chinese tradition to carry letters, have flown back, but they bring no news; the road is so long that he can’t even dream his way home. The last two lines hold the poem’s famous comparison: the sorrow of parting is like spring grass — the farther you walk, the more it grows, and it never runs out. Turning an invisible feeling into something you can see spreading everywhere is fresh and exactly right. Traditionally the poem is said to express Li Yu’s longing for his younger brother Li Congshan, who was held at the Song court.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Li Yu (937–978) was the last ruler of the Southern Tang. In 971 he sent his brother Li Congshan on a mission to the Song court, where he was detained; this lyric is traditionally said to have been written while Li Yu waited in vain for his return.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

清平乐 yuè（词牌名，“乐”指音乐，不读 lè）；砌 qì；拂了 le；还满、还生 hái；更行更远 gèng。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行文本。写作背景（思念弟弟李从善）是传统说法，并无确证。异文：“触目柔肠断”一作“触目愁肠断”，网站从通行本作“柔肠”。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

别来春半，触目柔肠断。

bié lái chūn bàn, chù mù róu cháng duàn

Half the spring has passed since you left; everything I see tears at my heart.

砌下落梅如雪乱，拂了一身还满。

qì xià luò méi rú xuě luàn, fú le yī shēn hái mǎn

Plum petals tumble like snow below the steps; I brush them away, and I’m covered again.

雁来音信无凭，路遥归梦难成。

yàn lái yīn xìn wú píng, lù yáo guī mèng nán chéng

The geese return with no word from you; the road is so long I can’t even dream my way home.

离恨恰如春草，更行更远还生。

lí hèn qià rú chūn cǎo, gèng xíng gèng yuǎn hái shēng

This grief is like spring grass — walk as far as you like, it keeps on growing.
