导读About this poem

Dusk thickens over the woods and hills, someone stands alone on a tower, and birds hurry home to their nests. One of the earliest ci (song lyrics) in Chinese — it ends with a question every traveller knows: which way is home?

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首词写黄昏时的思归之情。上阕写远景：平原上的树林笼罩着一层层烟雾，像织出来的一样；远处一带寒冷的山，绿得让人伤心。“伤心碧”很特别——颜色本身不会伤心，是看山的人伤心，这叫“移情”，把人的感情加到景物身上。天色暗下来，暮色慢慢走进高楼，楼上有一个人正在发愁。下阕写这个人：他在玉石台阶上白白地站了很久，看着归巢的鸟儿急急忙忙飞回去——鸟都能回家，人却回不了。最后一问一答：“何处是归程？长亭连短亭。”回家的路在哪里？只看见一个接一个的长亭短亭（古代路边供行人休息、送别的亭子），路好像永远走不完。全词由景到人，由远到近，结尾用景物回答问题，余味很长。宋代人把它和《忆秦娥》称为“百代词曲之祖”。

In English

This lyric captures the homesickness that comes at dusk. The first stanza is a wide view: mist hangs over the woods on the plain, thick and even as if woven, and a band of cold hills in the distance is a “heart-breaking green”. Colours can’t feel sad, of course — the person looking at them does. Lending human feelings to scenery like this is a favourite move in Chinese poetry. Evening darkness creeps into a high tower, where someone stands full of sorrow. The second stanza moves close. He has stood for a long time on the jade steps, for nothing, watching birds rush back to their nests. The birds can go home; he can’t. Then a question and an answer: “Where is the road home? Long pavilions, then short pavilions.” Rest pavilions stood along old Chinese roads, and the answer is only more of them, one after another, a road with no end in sight. The poem moves from landscape to person, from far to near, and answers its question with a picture instead of words, which is why it lingers. Song-dynasty critics called this and “Yi Qin E” the ancestors of all later ci.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Since the Song dynasty this lyric has been attributed to Li Bai (701–762), but many scholars doubt it, since the ci form was still young in his day; the attribution is traditional, not certain. It is first recorded in Song sources, said to have been found written on a wall.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

暝色 míng sè；伫立 zhù lì；宿鸟 sù niǎo（归巢过夜的鸟）。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行本，上下两阕共八句。“玉阶空伫立”一作“玉梯空伫立”；“长亭连短亭”一作“长亭更短亭”，网站用“连”。作者归属自古存疑，网站照传统题李白。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

平林漠漠烟如织，

píng lín mò mò yān rú zhī

Mist lies over the flat woodland, fine as woven cloth;

寒山一带伤心碧。

hán shān yī dài shāng xīn bì

a band of cold hills glows a green that hurts the heart.

暝色入高楼，

míng sè rù gāo lóu

Dusk climbs into the high tower,

有人楼上愁。

yǒu rén lóu shàng chóu

where someone stands in sorrow.

玉阶空伫立，

yù jiē kōng zhù lì

Long, and for nothing, I stand on the jade steps,

宿鸟归飞急。

sù niǎo guī fēi jí

watching birds hurry home to roost.

何处是归程？

hé chù shì guī chéng

Where is the road that leads me home?

长亭连短亭。

cháng tíng lián duǎn tíng

Long pavilion after short pavilion, on and on.
