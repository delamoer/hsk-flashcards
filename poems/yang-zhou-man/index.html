导读About this poem

A young poet rides into Yangzhou — once the most glamorous city in China — and finds it empty and ruined by war. Bright memories from old poems collide with a cold, silent present.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

扬州在唐代是最繁华的城市，杜牧写过“春风十里扬州路”“二十四桥明月夜”等名句。可是南宋时，金兵几次南下，扬州被烧杀一空。1176年冬天，二十出头的姜夔路过这里，写下这首词，曲调是他自己创作的“自度曲”。上阕写眼前：当年“春风十里”的热闹大街，现在只剩一片青青的荠菜和麦子；连荒废的池塘和老树都“厌言兵”——不愿再提打仗。黄昏的号角声在空城里回荡，“吹寒”二字把声音写成了冷。下阕借杜牧来写：就算杜牧再来，也会吃惊，再好的文笔也写不出这份伤心。最后写二十四桥还在，桥下水波里映着冷冷的月亮，桥边的芍药年年照样开花，却不知开给谁看。用不变的自然对比变了的人间，这叫“今昔对比”，让哀伤更深。

In English

In the Tang dynasty Yangzhou was China’s richest, liveliest city, and the poet Du Mu had made it famous with lines about “ten miles of spring breeze” and “the moonlit night at the Twenty-Four Bridges”. But in the Southern Song, Jin armies swept south more than once and left it in ruins. In the winter of 1176 the young Jiang Kui passed through and wrote this ci to a tune he composed himself. The first half shows what he sees: where the famous avenue once bustled, there is only green shepherd’s purse and wheat. Even the abandoned ponds and old trees seem “sick of talk of war”. At dusk a bugle “blows cold” through the empty city — sound turned into temperature. The second half calls on Du Mu himself: if he came back, he would be shocked, and even his skill could not put this grief into words. Then the ending: the Twenty-Four Bridges still stand, a cold moon trembles silently in the water, and the red peonies by the bridge bloom every year — for whom? Setting unchanged nature against a changed human world is a classic way to deepen sorrow: the beauty that remains makes the loss sharper.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Jiang Kui (c. 1155–c. 1221) was a Southern Song poet and musician who never held office and lived as a guest of patrons. His preface says he wrote this at the winter solstice of 1176, after the Jin invasions had left Yangzhou desolate; he was about twenty-two.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

少驻：少 shǎo（稍微）。荠麦：荠 jì。厌言兵：厌 yàn。清角：角 jiǎo（号角）。重到：重 chóng。豆蔻 dòu kòu。红药：芍药，药 yào。为谁生：为 wèi。名都：都 dū；都在空城：都 dōu。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行教材版本，只收正文，不收小序。小序大意：淳熙丙申（1176）冬至日，作者经过扬州，雪后放晴，满眼荠麦；进城只见一片萧条，寒水自碧，暮色中戍角悲鸣，感慨今昔，于是自创此曲；千岩老人（萧德藻）认为它有《诗经·黍离》那样的亡国之悲。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

淮左名都，竹西佳处，

huái zuǒ míng dū, zhú xī jiā chù

The famous city east of the Huai, lovely Bamboo West —

解鞍少驻初程。

jiě ān shǎo zhù chū chéng

here I unsaddle for a short rest, early on my road.

过春风十里，尽荠麦青青。

guò chūn fēng shí lǐ, jìn jì mài qīng qīng

I ride the “ten miles of spring breeze” — now nothing but weeds and green wheat.

自胡马窥江去后，

zì hú mǎ kuī jiāng qù hòu

Ever since the northern horsemen came to eye the river and rode away,

废池乔木，犹厌言兵。

fèi chí qiáo mù, yóu yàn yán bīng

even ruined ponds and old trees are sick of any talk of war.

渐黄昏，清角吹寒，都在空城。

jiàn huáng hūn, qīng jiǎo chuī hán, dōu zài kōng chéng

Dusk comes on; a thin bugle blows the cold through the empty city.

杜郎俊赏，算而今、重到须惊。

dù láng jùn shǎng, suàn ér jīn, chóng dào xū jīng

Du Mu, who loved this place so well — were he to come again today, he would be stunned.

纵豆蔻词工，青楼梦好，难赋深情。

zòng dòu kòu cí gōng, qīng lóu mèng hǎo, nán fù shēn qíng

However fine his lines on young girls and pleasure-house dreams, they could not hold a grief this deep.

二十四桥仍在，波心荡、冷月无声。

èr shí sì qiáo réng zài, bō xīn dàng, lěng yuè wú shēng

The Twenty-Four Bridges still stand; in the water a cold moon rocks, without a sound.

念桥边红药，年年知为谁生。

niàn qiáo biān hóng yào, nián nián zhī wèi shuí shēng

And the red peonies by the bridge, blooming year after year — for whom?
