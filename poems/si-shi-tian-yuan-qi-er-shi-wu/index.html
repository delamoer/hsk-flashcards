导读About this poem

Golden plums, plump apricots, white buckwheat flowers — and not a person on the path, because everyone is out at work. A colourful early-summer snapshot of a Song-dynasty village.

中文解析

这首诗写初夏的江南农村。第一句写果子：梅子熟了，金黄金黄；杏子长得又大又圆。第二句写花：麦花雪白一片，油菜花已经快谢了，变得稀稀落落。两句里有金黄、雪白，颜色鲜亮，也告诉我们季节：春天的菜花落了，夏天的果子熟了。第三句写村子里很安静：夏天白天长，篱笆边一整天没有人经过——不是因为没人，而是大家都下地干活去了。第四句用动的东西来衬托静：只有蜻蜓和蝴蝶在飞来飞去。这种写法叫“以动衬静”，用一点动静让人更感到四周的安静。诗里一个忙字都没有，读者却能想到田里农民的忙碌。范成大晚年退居乡下，写了六十首《四时田园杂兴》，这是其中很有名的一首。

In English

This poem shows a village south of the Yangtze in early summer. The first line is about fruit: the plums have ripened to gold, the apricots are plump and round. The second is about flowers: the wheat — in the Song, 麦花 often meant buckwheat — is in white bloom, while the rapeseed flowers are nearly over and only a few are left. Gold and snow-white make the picture bright, and they also tell the time of year: spring’s flowers are fading, summer’s fruit is ready. The third line turns to the quiet. The days are long, and all day nobody walks past the bamboo fences. Not because the village is empty — everyone is out in the fields. The last line uses movement to bring out the stillness: only dragonflies and butterflies flit about. Chinese critics call this “using motion to set off quiet” (以动衬静): a small movement makes you notice how still everything else is. The poem never mentions work, yet we can picture the farmers busy just out of sight. Fan Chengda wrote sixty poems on country life through the four seasons after retiring to the countryside; this is one of the best loved.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Fan Chengda (1126–1193) was a senior Southern Song official, famous for a brave diplomatic mission to the Jin court. In 1186, retired at Stone Lake (Shihu) near Suzhou, he wrote a series of sixty quatrains on village life through the year.

版本说明 About the text

采用通行教材文本。“惟有”有的版本作“唯有”。原组诗六十首，分春日、晚春、夏日、秋日、冬日五组，这是第二十五首（夏日组第一首）。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

梅子金黄杏子肥，

méi zǐ jīn huáng xìng zǐ féi

The plums have turned gold, the apricots are plump,

麦花雪白菜花稀。

mài huā xuě bái cài huā xī

the wheat is in white bloom, the rape flowers thinning out.

日长篱落无人过，

rì cháng lí luò wú rén guò

All the long day no one walks by the fences —

惟有蜻蜓蛱蝶飞。

wéi yǒu qīng tíng jiá dié fēi

only dragonflies and butterflies drift past.
