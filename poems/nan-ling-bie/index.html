导读About this poem

At forty-one Li Bai finally gets the call he has waited for all his life: the emperor wants him at court. This is his explosion of joy — home-brewed wine, roast chicken, dancing children, and the famous last line: “Laughing up at the sky, I walk out the door.”

第一段Part 1
01
白酒新熟山中归，黄鸡啄黍秋正肥。

直译LiteralThe white wine is newly ripe, I return from the hills; yellow chickens peck millet, in autumn just plump.

意译MeaningThe new wine is ready as I come down from the hills, and the hens, fat on autumn millet, peck in the yard.

02
呼童烹鸡酌白酒，儿女嬉笑牵人衣。

直译LiteralI call the boy to cook a chicken and pour white wine; sons and daughters laugh and pull at my clothes.

意译MeaningI call the boy to roast a chicken and pour the wine; my children laugh and tug at my sleeves.

03
高歌取醉欲自慰，起舞落日争光辉。

直译LiteralSinging loudly, getting drunk, I want to comfort myself; rising to dance, I compete with the setting sun for brightness.

意译MeaningI sing out loud and drink to my heart’s content, then dance, outshining the setting sun.

第二段Part 2
04
游说万乘苦不早，著鞭跨马涉远道。

直译LiteralTo persuade the lord of ten thousand chariots — sadly not early enough; I raise the whip, mount the horse, and cross the long road.

意译MeaningMy only regret is not going sooner to advise the emperor — now, whip in hand, I ride out on the long road.

05
会稽愚妇轻买臣，余亦辞家西入秦。

直译LiteralThe foolish wife of Kuaiji looked down on Maichen; I too leave home and go west into Qin.

意译MeaningThe foolish wife of Kuaiji scorned poor Zhu Maichen; I too say goodbye and head west for the capital.

06
仰天大笑出门去，我辈岂是蓬蒿人。

直译LiteralLooking up at the sky, laughing loudly, I go out the door; how could people like us be people of the weeds?

意译MeaningLaughing up at the sky, I walk out the door — were men like me born to stay among the weeds?

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

公元742年，唐玄宗召李白进京。李白等这一天等了很多年，于是写下这首痛快淋漓的诗。前三联写家里的欢乐：秋天新酿的白酒熟了，黄鸡吃饱了黍米正肥，诗人叫童仆杀鸡斟酒，孩子们笑着拉住他的衣服；他高声唱歌、喝得大醉，起身跳舞，好像要和落日比一比光彩。后三联写心里的志向：他怪自己没能早点去游说君王，现在要赶紧扬鞭上马走远路；又用朱买臣的典故——朱买臣穷的时候被妻子看不起，后来做了大官——暗暗说那些小看自己的人看错了。最后“仰天大笑出门去，我辈岂是蓬蒿人”，意思是：我们这样的人，怎么会一辈子埋没在草野之中！全诗由家里的小场面写到天下大志，感情层层升高，最后一句冲到顶点，是李白最有个性的诗句之一。

In English

In 742 Emperor Xuanzong summoned Li Bai to the capital. Li Bai had dreamed for years of doing great things for the empire, and when the call finally came he wrote this burst of joy. The first three couplets are a family celebration. The new autumn wine is ready, the yellow hens are plump from pecking millet; he calls the servant boy to cook a chicken and pour the wine, and his children laugh and tug at his clothes. He sings, drinks himself merry, and dances as if competing with the setting sun for brightness. The last three couplets turn to ambition. He regrets not going to persuade the emperor sooner, and now he will whip up his horse and take the long road. Then an allusion: Zhu Maichen, a poor scholar of the Han dynasty, was abandoned by his wife for being a failure, then rose to become a governor. Li Bai is hinting that people who looked down on him were wrong. The final line is one of his most famous: “Laughing up at the sky, I walk out the door — are people like me meant to stay among the weeds?” From a small scene at home to the largest ambition, the feeling rises couplet by couplet until it peaks in that last proud shout.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Li Bai (701–762) was summoned to the court of Emperor Xuanzong in 742, at about forty-one. He wrote this on leaving his home — traditionally placed at Nanling (in today’s Anhui), though some scholars put it in Shandong, where his children lived. His time at court would last less than two years.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

游说 yóu shuì（不读 shuō）；万乘 wàn shèng（指君王）；著鞭 zhuó biān；会稽 kuài jī（不读 huì）；啄 zhuó；黍 shǔ；蓬蒿 péng hāo。机器朗读“游说”“万乘”“著”“会稽”容易读错，已在“朗读用字”列替换同音字，音频需人工听验。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行本，全诗六联十二句。“白酒新熟山中归”一作“白酒初熟山中归”。题中“南陵”所在有争议，一说在今安徽，一说在今山东。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

白酒新熟山中归，黄鸡啄黍秋正肥。

bái jiǔ xīn shú shān zhōng guī, huáng jī zhuó shǔ qiū zhèng féi

The new wine is ready as I come down from the hills, and the hens, fat on autumn millet, peck in the yard.

呼童烹鸡酌白酒，儿女嬉笑牵人衣。

hū tóng pēng jī zhuó bái jiǔ, ér nǚ xī xiào qiān rén yī

I call the boy to roast a chicken and pour the wine; my children laugh and tug at my sleeves.

高歌取醉欲自慰，起舞落日争光辉。

gāo gē qǔ zuì yù zì wèi, qǐ wǔ luò rì zhēng guāng huī

I sing out loud and drink to my heart’s content, then dance, outshining the setting sun.

游说万乘苦不早，著鞭跨马涉远道。

yóu shuì wàn shèng kǔ bù zǎo, zhuó biān kuà mǎ shè yuǎn dào

My only regret is not going sooner to advise the emperor — now, whip in hand, I ride out on the long road.

会稽愚妇轻买臣，余亦辞家西入秦。

kuài jī yú fù qīng mǎi chén, yú yì cí jiā xī rù qín

The foolish wife of Kuaiji scorned poor Zhu Maichen; I too say goodbye and head west for the capital.

仰天大笑出门去，我辈岂是蓬蒿人。

yǎng tiān dà xiào chū mén qù, wǒ bèi qǐ shì péng hāo rén

Laughing up at the sky, I walk out the door — were men like me born to stay among the weeds?
