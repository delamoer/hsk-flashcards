导读About this poem

A poem about limestone — hammered out of the mountain, burned in fire, crushed to powder — that is really about a person who will keep his integrity whatever it costs. Chinese schoolchildren learn it as a model of courage.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗表面写石灰怎么做成：先从深山里一锤一凿地开采石头，再放进烈火里烧，最后变成白色的粉末。可是每一句都在写人。“若等闲”是说把烈火焚烧看得很平常；“粉骨碎身浑不怕”是说就算粉身碎骨也完全不怕；最后一句点明心愿：要把清清白白留在人间。“清白”一语双关（一个词有两层意思）：石灰是白色的，人也要品格干净、不做坏事。这种借写一样东西来表达自己志向的写法叫“托物言志”。传说于谦十几岁时写了这首诗，后来他真的做到了：保卫北京立了大功，最后却被冤杀，一生清廉。诗和人一致，所以特别打动人。

In English

On the surface this is about how lime is made. Limestone is cut out of the deep mountains with a thousand hammer blows, then burned in a fierce kiln, and finally crumbles into white powder that is used to whitewash walls. But every line is also about a person. “As if it were nothing” (若等闲) means facing the fire calmly. “Bones ground to powder, body broken — not afraid at all” is a vow to accept any suffering. The last line states the wish: to leave something clean and white in the world. The word 清白 works on two levels — lime is white, and a person who is 清白 is honest, clean, untouched by corruption. Writing about an object in order to state your own ideals is called 托物言志 (“using a thing to express one’s aims”), a very common method in Chinese poetry. Tradition says Yu Qian wrote this as a teenager, and his life matched the poem: he saved Beijing in a national crisis, stayed famously honest, and was later executed on false charges. Because the poet lived what he wrote, the poem is still quoted when people talk about integrity, and it is in every primary-school textbook.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Yu Qian (1398–1457) was a Ming official who organised the defence of Beijing in 1449, after the emperor was captured by Mongol forces. He was executed in 1457 after a palace coup and later cleared of all charges. The poem is traditionally said to be a work of his youth, though the attribution has been questioned.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

千锤万凿出深山，

qiān chuí wàn záo chū shēn shān

Hammered and chiselled out of the deep mountain,

烈火焚烧若等闲。

liè huǒ fén shāo ruò děng xián

it takes the blazing fire as if it were nothing.

粉骨碎身浑不怕，

fěn gǔ suì shēn hún bù pà

Ground to powder, body and bone, it has no fear —

要留清白在人间。

yào liú qīng bái zài rén jiān

it only wants to leave something clean and white in this world.
