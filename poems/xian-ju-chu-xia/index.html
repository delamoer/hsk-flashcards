导读About this poem

Sour plums on the tongue, green light through the window, children chasing willow fluff: a lazy early-summer afternoon caught in four lines. A perfect poem for learning how Chinese poets paint a mood with small details.

中文解析

这首诗写初夏一个悠闲的下午。前两句写两样初夏的东西：吃过梅子，酸味还留在牙齿上，让牙齿都软了；芭蕉的大叶子把绿色“分”给了窗纱，窗纱也映成了绿色。一个“软”字写出味觉，一个“分”字写出颜色，都非常生动——芭蕉好像会主动把绿色送给窗户。后两句写人：夏天白天长，午睡起来，懒洋洋的，什么心思也没有，就闲闲地看孩子们追着捉空中飘的柳絮。“无情思”就是没精打采、无所用心的样子。全诗没有大事，只有酸味、绿色和孩子的笑声，却把初夏的慵懒、安闲写得让人如在眼前。杨万里最擅长写这种日常小景，语言活泼，被称为“诚斋体”。

In English

This poem is about one easy afternoon at the start of summer. The first two lines give two tastes of the season. He has eaten green plums, and the sourness is still on his teeth, leaving them soft and tingling. Outside, the huge leaves of a banana plant “share their green” with the window gauze, so the screen itself glows green. The verb 软 (“to make soft”) gives us taste; the verb 分 (“to share out”) gives us colour — as if the plant were handing its green to the window on purpose. The last two lines turn to the poet himself. Summer days are long; he wakes from his nap with no thoughts and no energy, and simply watches the children run about catching the willow fluff drifting in the air. 无情思 means having nothing on one’s mind, too drowsy to care. Nothing important happens — just a sour taste, a green light, children laughing — yet the lazy peace of early summer is right there in front of us. Yang Wanli was famous for this kind of fresh, playful look at everyday things; his lively style is known as the “Chengzhai style”, after his pen name.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Yang Wanli (1127–1206), pen name Chengzhai, was a Southern Song official and one of the most productive poets of his age, said to have written over 20,000 poems. This pair of poems is traditionally dated to a period when he was living at home in Jiangxi.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

情思 qíng sī：有注本按古音读 qíng sì（名词“心绪”），教材注 qíng sī，当前采用 sī。闲看 xián kàn。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行教材文本。异文：“软齿牙”一作“溅齿牙”，“分绿与窗纱”一作“分绿上窗纱”。原题共两首，这是第一首。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

梅子留酸软齿牙，

méi zǐ liú suān ruǎn chǐ yá

The plums’ sharp taste still lingers on my teeth;

芭蕉分绿与窗纱。

bā jiāo fēn lǜ yǔ chuāng shā

the banana leaves lend their green to the window screen.

日长睡起无情思，

rì cháng shuì qǐ wú qíng sī

Long day — I wake from my nap, my mind a blank,

闲看儿童捉柳花。

xián kàn ér tóng zhuō liǔ huā

and idly watch the children chase the willow fluff.
