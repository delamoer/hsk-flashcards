石壕吏

Shí Háo Lì
The Officer at Shihao Village
杜甫Dù Fǔ · Du Fu唐 Tang

导读About this poem

Du Fu stops for the night in a village and overhears an officer rounding up men for the army. The old man escapes over the wall, and by morning the old woman has been taken instead — a whole wartime tragedy told in 120 characters, almost without comment.

直译LiteralAt dusk I lodge in Shihao village; there is an officer seizing people at night.

意译MeaningAt dusk I find a bed in Shihao village. In the night an officer comes to take men away.

02

直译LiteralThe old man climbs over the wall and runs; the old woman goes out of the gate to look.

意译MeaningThe old man scrambles over the wall and flees; the old woman goes out to the gate.

第二段Part 2
03
吏呼一何怒！妇啼一何苦！

直译LiteralThe officer shouts — how angry! The woman cries — how bitter!

意译MeaningHow the officer bellows in rage! How bitterly the woman weeps!

04
听妇前致词：三男邺城戍。

直译LiteralI hear the woman step forward and speak: “My three sons are guarding the city of Ye.

意译MeaningI hear her step up and plead: “All three of my sons were sent to hold the city of Ye.

05
一男附书至，二男新战死。

直译LiteralOne son has sent a letter home; two sons have just died in battle.

意译MeaningA letter has come from one of them: the other two were killed just now in the fighting.

06
存者且偷生，死者长已矣！

直译LiteralThe one who survives is just stealing a little life; the dead are gone forever!

意译MeaningThe one still living lives from day to day; the dead are gone, and gone for good.

07
室中更无人，惟有乳下孙。

直译LiteralIn the house there is no one else, only a grandson still at the breast.

意译MeaningThere is no one else in the house, only my grandson, still nursing.

08
有孙母未去，出入无完裙。

直译LiteralBecause of the grandson, his mother has not left; going in and out, she has no whole skirt.

意译MeaningFor the baby’s sake his mother has stayed, but she hasn’t a whole skirt to be seen in.

09
老妪力虽衰，请从吏夜归。

直译LiteralThough this old woman’s strength is failing, I ask to follow you, officer, back tonight.

意译MeaningI’m old and my strength is going, but let me go back with you tonight, sir.

10
急应河阳役，犹得备晨炊。

直译LiteralQuickly answering the call for service at Heyang, I can still prepare the morning meal.”

意译MeaningIf I hurry to the camp at Heyang, I can still cook the soldiers’ breakfast.”

第三段Part 3
11
夜久语声绝，如闻泣幽咽。

直译LiteralThe night grows late and the voices stop; it seems I hear weeping, low and choked.

意译MeaningDeep in the night the talking stops. I seem to hear someone sobbing under her breath.

12
天明登前途，独与老翁别。

直译LiteralAt daybreak I set out on the road ahead, and take leave of the old man alone.

意译MeaningAt first light I take to the road again, and there is only the old man to say goodbye to.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗像一篇很短的小说。杜甫傍晚住进石壕村，夜里有官吏来抓人当兵。老翁翻墙逃走，老妇出门应付。诗的大部分是老妇的话：三个儿子都去守邺城，两个刚刚战死，活着的也朝不保夕；家里只剩还在吃奶的孙子，孙子的妈妈连一条完整的裙子都没有，没法出门见人。说到最后，老妇只好自己跟官吏走，去河阳军营给士兵做早饭。夜深了，说话声没有了，只隐约听到有人低声哭泣。天亮时诗人上路，只能和老翁一个人告别——老妇已经被带走了。全诗几乎不发议论，只客观地写看到和听到的事，这叫“寓褒贬于叙事”，把态度藏在故事里。官吏的“怒”和老妇的“苦”对比强烈，战争给普通百姓带来的痛苦，就在这些平静的叙述里。这首诗和另外五首合称“三吏三别”。

In English

This poem works like a very short story. Du Fu arrives at Shihao village at dusk; that night an officer comes to seize men for the army. The old man climbs over the back wall and runs, and the old woman goes out to face the officer. Most of the poem is her speech, which we hear through the poet’s ears: her three sons were sent to defend the city of Ye; two have just been killed, and the one still alive is only clinging on. At home there is just a grandson still at the breast, and his mother has no whole skirt to go out in. In the end the old woman offers herself: let her go with the officer tonight to cook breakfast for the troops at Heyang. Late at night the voices stop, and the poet seems to hear someone sobbing quietly. At dawn he sets off and says goodbye — to the old man alone. Du Fu hardly comments at all; he just reports what he saw and heard and lets the facts speak. The contrast in one line — the officer’s shouting “so angry”, the woman’s crying “so bitter” — carries the whole poem. It is one of six poems known as the “Three Officers and Three Farewells”, written about the cost of the war on ordinary families.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Du Fu (712–770) wrote this in the spring of 759, during the An Lushan Rebellion. After the Tang army’s defeat at Ye (near today’s Anyang), troops were urgently conscripted, and Du Fu, travelling from Luoyang back to his post in Huazhou, is said to have witnessed scenes like this on the road.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

多音字：长已矣 cháng（永远）；更无人 gèng；急应 yìng（应征、服役）；泣幽咽 yè（哽咽）；乳下孙 rǔ。生僻字：邺 yè、戍 shù、妪 yù、逾 yú。“一何”读 yī hé（一不变调）。“老妇出门看”的“看”，现代通行读 kàn，网站采用 kàn；也有人按古音读 kān，以求和上句押韵。

版本说明 About the text

采用统编教材（八年级上册）文本，全诗二十四句、一百二十字。已与维基文库本逐句核对，文字一致；“惟有乳下孙”有的版本作“唯有”。网站按一联一行排版，共十二行、三段。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

暮投石壕村，有吏夜捉人。

mù tóu shí háo cūn, yǒu lì yè zhuō rén

At dusk I find a bed in Shihao village. In the night an officer comes to take men away.

老翁逾墙走，老妇出门看。

lǎo wēng yú qiáng zǒu, lǎo fù chū mén kàn

The old man scrambles over the wall and flees; the old woman goes out to the gate.

吏呼一何怒！妇啼一何苦！

lì hū yī hé nù, fù tí yī hé kǔ

How the officer bellows in rage! How bitterly the woman weeps!

听妇前致词：三男邺城戍。

tīng fù qián zhì cí, sān nán yè chéng shù

I hear her step up and plead: “All three of my sons were sent to hold the city of Ye.

一男附书至，二男新战死。

yī nán fù shū zhì, èr nán xīn zhàn sǐ

A letter has come from one of them: the other two were killed just now in the fighting.

存者且偷生，死者长已矣！

cún zhě qiě tōu shēng, sǐ zhě cháng yǐ yǐ

The one still living lives from day to day; the dead are gone, and gone for good.

室中更无人，惟有乳下孙。

shì zhōng gèng wú rén, wéi yǒu rǔ xià sūn

There is no one else in the house, only my grandson, still nursing.

有孙母未去，出入无完裙。

yǒu sūn mǔ wèi qù, chū rù wú wán qún

For the baby’s sake his mother has stayed, but she hasn’t a whole skirt to be seen in.

老妪力虽衰，请从吏夜归。

lǎo yù lì suī shuāi, qǐng cóng lì yè guī

I’m old and my strength is going, but let me go back with you tonight, sir.

急应河阳役，犹得备晨炊。

jí yìng hé yáng yì, yóu dé bèi chén chuī

If I hurry to the camp at Heyang, I can still cook the soldiers’ breakfast.”

夜久语声绝，如闻泣幽咽。

yè jiǔ yǔ shēng jué, rú wén qì yōu yè

Deep in the night the talking stops. I seem to hear someone sobbing under her breath.

天明登前途，独与老翁别。

tiān míng dēng qián tú, dú yǔ lǎo wēng bié

At first light I take to the road again, and there is only the old man to say goodbye to.
