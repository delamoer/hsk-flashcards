导读About this poem

A fisherman in a straw hat and rain cape stays out on the water in the soft spring rain — he simply doesn’t want to go home. One of the earliest ci (song lyrics), and a perfect little picture of a free and easy life.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首小词像一幅水墨画。第一句写远景：西塞山前，白鹭在飞。第二句写近景：桃花开了，落在流水上，水里的鳜鱼正肥。白鹭、桃花、绿水，颜色很鲜亮，一下子就是江南的春天。后面换了短句“青箬笠，绿蓑衣”，人物才出场——一个戴竹叶斗笠、穿草编雨衣的渔夫。最后一句是全词的核心：风斜斜地吹，雨细细地下，他却“不须归”，不用回家。别人遇到下雨赶快回去，他反而更喜欢在雨里钓鱼。作者借这个渔夫，写出自己不愿做官、自由自在的生活态度。全词只有二十七个字，有景、有色、有人、有心情，是早期文人词的代表作。

In English

This little song reads like an ink painting. The first line is the wide view: white egrets flying in front of Xisai Mountain. The second moves in close: peach blossoms on the flowing water, and the mandarin fish in the river are plump. White birds, pink blossoms, green water — in two lines we are in a bright southern spring. Then the rhythm changes to short phrases, “green bamboo hat, green straw cape”, and only now does a person appear: a fisherman dressed for the rain. The last line is the heart of the poem. The wind blows slantwise, the rain is fine, and he “needn’t go home”. Most people hurry indoors when it rains; this man is happy to keep fishing. Through him the poet shows his own ideal: a life without office or ambition, free to enjoy the water and the weather. In just twenty-seven characters there is landscape, colour, a person and a mood — which is why it became a model for early literary ci.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Zhang Zhihe (c. 732–c. 774) passed the imperial exam young and served at court, but after a demotion he gave up office and lived as a recluse on the rivers and lakes of the south, calling himself the “Old Fisherman of Mist and Waves”. He is said to have written a set of five Fisherman’s Songs; this is the first and best known.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

西塞山的“塞”读 sài（边塞的塞）。生僻字：鳜鱼 guì yú、箬笠 ruò lì、蓑衣 suō yī。斜风细雨的“斜”按今音读 xié。机器朗读易读错的字已在“朗读用字”列替换同音字，音频需人工听验。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行教材版本。全词二十七字，一阕。张志和原作《渔父》五首，此为第一首。“箬笠”古本或作“篛笠”（异体字）。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

西塞山前白鹭飞，

xī sài shān qián bái lù fēi

White egrets wheel before Xisai Mountain;

桃花流水鳜鱼肥。

táo huā liú shuǐ guì yú féi

peach petals ride the stream, and the fish are fat.

青箬笠，绿蓑衣，

qīng ruò lì, lǜ suō yī

A green bamboo hat, a green straw cape —

斜风细雨不须归。

xié fēng xì yǔ bù xū guī

in slanting wind and fine rain, why go home?
