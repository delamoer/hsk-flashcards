导读About this poem

A poor girl with real skill and good taste cannot find a husband, because everyone admires flashy fashion — so year after year she embroiders other women’s wedding dresses. The last line gave Chinese the phrase “making wedding clothes for others”.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

诗用一个贫家女子的口吻说话。她住在草房子里，从没穿过绫罗绸缎；想托媒人说亲，又更加自伤身世。谁会欣赏她高雅的品格呢？大家喜欢的都是时下流行的打扮。她敢夸自己一双手针线活最好，却不愿意把眉毛画得长长的去和别人比美。最让她痛苦的是：年年拿着金线刺绣，却都是在给别人缝做嫁衣。表面上写贫女嫁不出去，其实是写有才华的读书人：品德好、本领大，却因为出身低、不会讨好，一直得不到重用，只能替别人做事、为别人出力。这种借女子写读书人的手法，在古诗里很常见。“为他人作嫁衣裳”后来变成常用语，比喻白白为别人辛苦忙碌。

In English

The poem speaks in the voice of a poor young woman. She lives behind a door of woven brush and has never worn fine silk. She would like to ask a matchmaker to find her a husband, but even thinking about it makes her feel worse about her lot. Who appreciates real grace and taste? Everyone admires whatever style of make-up is in fashion. She is proud of her ten skilful fingers and her fine needlework, but she refuses to compete with other girls by painting her eyebrows longer. What hurts most is this: year after year she embroiders with gold thread, and every piece is someone else’s wedding dress. On the surface the poem is about a girl who cannot marry. Readers have always understood it as being about talented scholars from humble families — honest and capable, but unwilling to flatter, and so never given a chance, while they spend their lives working for others’ success. Using a woman’s situation to speak about a scholar’s career is a long tradition in Chinese poetry. The last line became a common saying: 为他人作嫁衣裳, “to make wedding clothes for others”, means working hard only for someone else’s benefit.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Qin Taoyu was a late-Tang poet whose dates are unknown. He failed the examinations for years and is said to have been granted the jinshi degree only in 882, by special decree, after following the emperor Xizong to Sichuan during Huang Chao’s rebellion. This is by far his best-known poem.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

绮罗 qǐ luó；格调 gé diào；“为他人作嫁衣裳”的“为”读 wèi（替）；“压金线”的“压”读 yā（刺绣的一种针法）。末句“衣裳”的“裳”是韵脚，读 cháng，与香、伤、妆、长押韵（口语读 yīshang）。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行版本（如《唐诗三百首》所收）。“敢将十指夸针巧”的“针巧”，有的版本作“偏巧”；“共怜时世俭梳妆”的“俭”，一说通“险”，指奇异的时髦打扮；网站用“针巧”“俭”，按“时髦的打扮”理解。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

蓬门未识绮罗香，

péng mén wèi shí qǐ luó xiāng

Behind my door of woven brush I’ve never worn scented silk;

拟托良媒益自伤。

nǐ tuō liáng méi yì zì shāng

just thinking of a matchmaker makes me sadder still.

谁爱风流高格调，

shuí ài fēng liú gāo gé diào

Who cares for true grace and taste?

共怜时世俭梳妆。

gòng lián shí shì jiǎn shū zhuāng

Everyone admires whatever look is in fashion.

敢将十指夸针巧，

gǎn jiāng shí zhǐ kuā zhēn qiǎo

I’ll boast my ten fingers sew better than anyone’s,

不把双眉斗画长。

bù bǎ shuāng méi dòu huà cháng

but I won’t paint my brows longer just to outshine the others.

苦恨年年压金线，

kǔ hèn nián nián yā jīn xiàn

What hurts is this: year after year I stitch with gold thread,

为他人作嫁衣裳。

wèi tā rén zuò jià yī cháng

making wedding gowns for other brides.
