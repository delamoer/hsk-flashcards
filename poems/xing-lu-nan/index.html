导读About this poem

A feast is laid out, but the poet cannot eat: every road ahead seems blocked. Then, in the last two lines, he lifts himself up — one day he will ride the wind across the sea. Few poems swing so fast from despair to hope.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

“行路难”是乐府旧题，意思是人生的路很难走。开头写一场丰盛的宴会：金杯里是一斗值十千钱的好酒，玉盘里是价值万钱的好菜。可是诗人放下杯子、扔下筷子，吃不下去；拔出宝剑四面看看，心里一片茫然。为什么？接下来两句用比喻说明：想渡黄河，冰堵住了河道；想登太行山，大雪盖满了山——理想的路处处被挡住。“闲来垂钓”一句用了两个典故：姜太公在溪边钓鱼，后来遇到周文王；伊尹梦见自己坐船经过太阳旁边，后来受到商汤重用。诗人想：我也许还会有机会。然后是一连串短促的呼喊：“行路难！行路难！多歧路，今安在？”岔路这么多，我的路在哪里？最后两句情绪一下子高昂起来：总有一天我会乘着长风、破开巨浪，挂起高高的船帆，渡过大海。全诗在失望和希望之间来回起伏，最后落在信心上，“长风破浪会有时”今天还常用来鼓励人。

In English

“Hard Is the Road” was an old yuefu title about how difficult life can be. Li Bai opens with a rich banquet: wine worth ten thousand coins a measure in golden cups, delicacies worth a fortune on jade plates. Yet he puts down his cup, throws aside his chopsticks and cannot eat; he draws his sword, looks all around, and feels lost. Two images explain why: he wants to cross the Yellow River, but ice blocks it; he wants to climb the Taihang Mountains, but snow covers them. Every path to his ambitions is shut. Then he remembers two old stories: Jiang Ziya was fishing by a stream when King Wen of Zhou found him, and Yi Yin dreamed of sailing past the sun before King Tang of Shang made him a minister. Maybe his chance will still come. Next come short, broken cries — “Hard is the road! Hard is the road! So many forks — where is mine now?” And suddenly the mood soars: one day he will ride the long wind, break through the waves, hoist his sail high and cross the vast sea. The poem swings back and forth between despair and hope and ends on confidence. The line 长风破浪会有时 is still used today to encourage someone who is going through a hard time.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Li Bai (701–762) was called to the court in Chang’an in 742 and hoped to serve as a statesman, but he was given only a literary post and left the capital about two years later. This poem, the first of three under the same title, is traditionally dated to his departure from Chang’an.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

樽 zūn；珍羞 zhēn xiū（同“珍馐”）；直 zhí（同“值”）；箸 zhù；塞川 sè（不读 sāi）；将登 jiāng；太行 tài háng（不读 xíng）；垂钓 diào；忽复 fù；乘舟 chéng；行路难 xíng（与“太行”的 háng 不同）；歧路 qí；长风 cháng；会有时 huì；济沧海 jì（渡过，不读 jǐ）。

版本说明 About the text

采用统编教材（九年级上册）文本，与维基文库所收《全唐诗》本一致（文库“箸”作“筯”、“歧”作“岐”，异体字）。“珍羞”今多写作“珍馐”；“直万钱”一作“值万钱”。其他异文：“清酒”一作“美酒”，“雪满山”一作“雪暗天”，“碧溪”一作“坐溪”，“忽复”一作“忽然”。《行路难》共三首，这是第一首。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

金樽清酒斗十千，

jīn zūn qīng jiǔ dǒu shí qiān

Golden cups of clear wine, ten thousand coins a jug,

玉盘珍羞直万钱。

yù pán zhēn xiū zhí wàn qián

jade plates piled with dishes worth a fortune.

停杯投箸不能食，

tíng bēi tóu zhù bù néng shí

I set down my cup, push my chopsticks away — I cannot eat.

拔剑四顾心茫然。

bá jiàn sì gù xīn máng rán

I draw my sword and look around me, lost.

欲渡黄河冰塞川，

yù dù huáng hé bīng sè chuān

I would cross the Yellow River, but ice has choked it;

将登太行雪满山。

jiāng dēng tài háng xuě mǎn shān

I would climb the Taihang, but snow buries every peak.

闲来垂钓碧溪上，

xián lái chuí diào bì xī shàng

Idle, I cast a line by a green stream —

忽复乘舟梦日边。

hū fù chéng zhōu mèng rì biān

then dream of sailing a boat past the sun.

行路难！行路难！

xíng lù nán, xíng lù nán

Hard is the road! Hard is the road!

多歧路，今安在？

duō qí lù, jīn ān zài

So many forks — which one is mine?

长风破浪会有时，

cháng fēng pò làng huì yǒu shí

A day will come to ride the long wind and split the waves;

直挂云帆济沧海。

zhí guà yún fān jì cāng hǎi

I’ll raise my sail into the clouds and cross the open sea.
