导读About this poem

Fine rain in the capital, and from a distance the grass looks green — but walk up close and it vanishes. Han Yu catches the very first hint of spring, and argues it is lovelier than spring in full bloom.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗写早春，抓住了一个很细微的现象。第一句写京城街道上的小雨，滋润得像酥油一样细腻柔滑。第二句是全诗最妙的地方：远远看去，地上有一片淡淡的绿色；走近一看，草芽太小太稀，反而看不出绿了。这是很真实的观察，只有细心的人才能发现。后两句是诗人的评价：一年里春天最好的时候，就是现在；它远远胜过晚些时候满城柳树如烟的景色。为什么？因为早春是刚刚开始的希望，淡淡的、若有若无，最让人心动。诗写给朋友张籍，有邀他趁早出门赏春的意思。“草色遥看近却无”今天还常被引用，形容事物初生时朦胧的美。

In English

This poem about early spring is built on one tiny, precise observation. The first line: a light rain falls on the streets of the capital, soft and smooth as butter-cream. The second line is the brilliant part: from a distance the ground has a faint wash of green, but up close the new shoots are so small and sparse that the green disappears. It is exactly what you see in very early spring, but only a careful eye notices it. The last two lines give the poet’s verdict: this is the best moment of the whole year’s spring — far better than later, when the city is full of willows hazy as smoke. Why? Because early spring is hope just beginning; faint, half there and half not, it moves us most. The poem was sent to his friend Zhang Ji, partly as an invitation to come out and enjoy the season before it is “too late”. The line “grass colour seen from afar, gone up close” is still quoted for the hazy beauty of anything just beginning.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Han Yu (768–824) was a leading Tang writer and scholar, famous for reviving plain classical prose. He wrote this in 823, in his last years, to his friend the poet Zhang Ji, an official in the Ministry of Works who was eighteenth among his cousins — hence “Zhang Eighteen”.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

“遥看”的“看”：按格律此处应为平声 kān，教材和日常读法多为 kàn，网站读 kàn。“好处”读 hǎo chù；“绝胜”的“胜”读 shèng。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

天街小雨润如酥，

tiān jiē xiǎo yǔ rùn rú sū

Fine rain on the city streets, soft as cream —

草色遥看近却无。

cǎo sè yáo kàn jìn què wú

from far off the grass looks green; come closer and it’s gone.

最是一年春好处，

zuì shì yī nián chūn hǎo chù

This is the loveliest moment of the year’s spring,

绝胜烟柳满皇都。

jué shèng yān liǔ mǎn huáng dū

far finer than willows hazing the whole capital later on.
