Zhàn Chéng Nán
Fighting South of the City

导读About this poem

Soldiers lie dead and unburied on the battlefield, and the poet asks the crows to mourn them before they eat. One of the oldest and most shocking anti-war poems in Chinese, with lines of uneven length that sound like a cry.

第一段Part 1
01
战城南，死郭北，野死不葬乌可食。

直译LiteralThey fought south of the city, died north of the outer wall; dead in the wild, unburied, the crows can eat them.

意译MeaningThey fought south of the city and died north of the wall — left in the open, unburied, for the crows.

02
为我谓乌：“且为客豪！

直译LiteralTell the crows for me: “First wail for the strangers!

意译MeaningTell the crows for me: “Before you feed, weep a little for these men so far from home.

03
野死谅不葬，腐肉安能去子逃？”

直译LiteralDead in the wild, surely they will not be buried — how could rotting flesh run away from you?”

意译MeaningDead in the open, no one will bury them — how could rotting flesh escape you?”

第二段Part 2
04
水深激激，蒲苇冥冥；

直译LiteralThe water is deep and rushing; the reeds are dark and dense;

意译MeaningThe deep water rushes past, the reeds grow thick and dark;

05
枭骑战斗死，驽马徘徊鸣。

直译LiteralThe brave horsemen died fighting; the worn-out horses wander and neigh.

意译Meaningthe bravest riders fell in battle, and their tired horses wander, neighing.

第三段Part 3
06
梁筑室，何以南？何以北？

直译LiteralBuildings are put up on the bridge — how to go south? how to go north?

意译MeaningThey have built forts on the bridges — how can anyone go south, or north?

07
禾黍不获君何食？

直译LiteralIf grain and millet are not harvested, what will the lord eat?

意译MeaningIf no one brings in the harvest, what will our ruler eat?

08
愿为忠臣安可得？

直译LiteralWishing to be loyal subjects — how can that be achieved?

意译MeaningThey wanted to serve faithfully — how can they, now?

09
思子良臣，良臣诚可思：

直译LiteralI think of you, good subjects; good subjects are truly worth thinking of:

意译MeaningI think of you, good and loyal men — you truly deserve to be remembered:

10
朝行出攻，暮不夜归！

直译LiteralIn the morning you went out to attack; at evening, you did not come back by night!

意译Meaningyou marched out to fight at dawn, and by nightfall you had not come home.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这是一首汉代的反战民歌。开头三句就很惨烈：在城南打仗，死在城北，尸体扔在野外没人埋，成了乌鸦的食物。接着诗人对乌鸦说话：请先为这些客死他乡的人哭号几声吧！他们死在野外，反正没人埋，腐烂的肉还能逃出你的嘴吗？这种对乌鸦说话的写法，叫“拟人”和“呼告”，冷静的话里藏着巨大的悲痛。然后写战场：河水湍急，芦苇幽深，勇猛的骑兵战死了，只剩疲惫的马在原地徘徊悲鸣。后面几句责问：在桥上修营垒，南北都不通了；庄稼没人收，君王吃什么？想做忠臣又怎么做得到？最后怀念这些好士兵：早上出去作战，到夜里却再也没有回来。全诗句子长短不一（三字到七字都有），像一声声哭喊，比整齐的诗句更有冲击力。

In English

This Han-dynasty song is one of the earliest anti-war poems in Chinese. The first three lines are brutal: men fought south of the city, died north of it, and lie unburied in the open — food for crows. Then the poet speaks to the crows: “Before you eat, wail a little for these men who died far from home! They lie out in the wild and no one will bury them — how could their rotting flesh escape you?” Talking to the birds like this (a mix of personification and direct address) hides enormous grief behind calm, almost bitter words. Next, the battlefield: the deep water rushing past, the reeds dark and thick, the bravest riders dead, and their exhausted horses wandering and neighing. Then come angry questions: forts are built on the bridges, so no one can go south or north; if no one brings in the harvest, what will the ruler eat? How can anyone be a loyal subject now? The poem ends by remembering these good men, who marched out in the morning and never came home that night. The lines are uneven, from three characters to seven, and that broken rhythm sounds like sobbing — more shattering than neat verse could be.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

This is one of the eighteen “Nao songs” (铙歌), Han-dynasty military songs preserved in the Song-dynasty collection Yuefu Shiji. The author is unknown; the Han fought many long wars on its frontiers, and the song is usually read as a lament for the common soldiers who died in them.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

为我谓乌、且为客豪 wèi（替、为了）；豪 háo（通“号”，哭号）；谅 liàng；激激 jī jī；冥冥 míng míng；枭骑 xiāo jì：“骑”作名词（骑兵）时旧读 jì，今普通话统读 qí，当前采用 jì；驽马 nú mǎ；愿为 wéi；朝行 zhāo。机器朗读的“为”“骑”已在“朗读用字”列替换同音字。

版本说明 About the text

采用《乐府诗集·鼓吹曲辞》所收汉铙歌的通行文本。此诗流传中文字有讹误，“梁筑室”等句历来解释不一，这里取“在桥上修筑营垒”的说法。“乌可食”有的本子误作“鸟可食”，“徘徊”或作“裴徊”。全诗网站按意思分为十行。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

战城南，死郭北，野死不葬乌可食。

zhàn chéng nán, sǐ guō běi, yě sǐ bù zàng wū kě shí

They fought south of the city and died north of the wall — left in the open, unburied, for the crows.

为我谓乌：“且为客豪！

wèi wǒ wèi wū, qiě wèi kè háo

Tell the crows for me: “Before you feed, weep a little for these men so far from home.

野死谅不葬，腐肉安能去子逃？”

yě sǐ liàng bù zàng, fǔ ròu ān néng qù zǐ táo

Dead in the open, no one will bury them — how could rotting flesh escape you?”

水深激激，蒲苇冥冥；

shuǐ shēn jī jī, pú wěi míng míng

The deep water rushes past, the reeds grow thick and dark;

枭骑战斗死，驽马徘徊鸣。

xiāo jì zhàn dòu sǐ, nú mǎ pái huái míng

the bravest riders fell in battle, and their tired horses wander, neighing.

梁筑室，何以南？何以北？

liáng zhù shì, hé yǐ nán, hé yǐ běi

They have built forts on the bridges — how can anyone go south, or north?

禾黍不获君何食？

hé shǔ bù huò jūn hé shí

If no one brings in the harvest, what will our ruler eat?

愿为忠臣安可得？

yuàn wéi zhōng chén ān kě dé

They wanted to serve faithfully — how can they, now?

思子良臣，良臣诚可思：

sī zǐ liáng chén, liáng chén chéng kě sī

I think of you, good and loyal men — you truly deserve to be remembered:

朝行出攻，暮不夜归！

zhāo xíng chū gōng, mù bù yè guī

you marched out to fight at dawn, and by nightfall you had not come home.
