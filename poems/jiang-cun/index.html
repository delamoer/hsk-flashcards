导读About this poem

A rare happy poem from Du Fu: after years of war and wandering, he has a quiet home by a river in Chengdu, where swallows come and go, his wife draws a chessboard and his son bends a needle into a fishhook.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

杜甫一生多半在战乱和漂泊中度过，这首诗却难得地安宁。他刚在成都浣花溪边盖好草堂，一家人终于安顿下来。首联写全景：清澈的江水弯弯地抱着村子流过，长长的夏天里，村里一切都很清静。颔联写动物：堂上的燕子自由地飞来飞去，水上的鸥鸟和人亲近，一点不怕人——“自去自来”“相亲相近”用重复的字，读起来轻快。颈联写家人：老妻在纸上画棋盘，小儿子把缝衣针敲弯做鱼钩，都是生活小事，却充满温情。尾联说：我身体多病，需要的只是药物，除此以外，我这微小的身躯还有什么别的要求呢？知足里也透出一点辛酸——老了，病了，一家人的生活也并不宽裕。

In English

Du Fu spent most of his life amid war and on the road, but this poem is unusually peaceful. He had just built a thatched cottage beside a stream in Chengdu, and his family had finally settled down. The first couplet gives the wide view: a clear river curls around the village like an embrace, and through the long summer everything is quiet. The second couplet turns to animals: swallows nesting in the hall fly in and out as they please, and gulls on the water stay close, unafraid of people. The repeated characters (自去自来, 相亲相近) give the lines a light, easy rhythm. The third couplet is about family: his old wife draws a chessboard on paper, and his little son hammers a sewing needle into a fishhook — small domestic details full of warmth. The last couplet says: I am often ill, and all I need is medicine — beyond that, what more could this small body of mine ask for? The contentment carries a trace of sadness, too: he is old and sick, and the family is far from well off.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Du Fu (712–770) reached Chengdu in 759 after fleeing war-torn north China, and with friends’ help built his famous thatched cottage by the Huanhua Stream. This poem is from the summer of 760, one of the calmest periods of his life.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

一曲 yī qū（弯曲处，不读 qǔ）；长夏 cháng xià；画纸为棋局 wéi（做成）；所须 xū；更何求 gèng。

版本说明 About the text

采用《全唐诗》《千家诗》文本。“堂上燕”一作“梁上燕”；尾联上句一作“但有故人供禄米”（古诗文网等选本采用），意思是“只要有老朋友接济米粮”。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

清江一曲抱村流，

qīng jiāng yī qū bào cūn liú

A clear river bends and holds the village in its arm;

长夏江村事事幽。

cháng xià jiāng cūn shì shì yōu

all through the long summer, everything here is still.

自去自来堂上燕，

zì qù zì lái táng shàng yàn

Swallows in the hall come and go as they like;

相亲相近水中鸥。

xiāng qīn xiāng jìn shuǐ zhōng ōu

gulls on the water drift close, unafraid.

老妻画纸为棋局，

lǎo qī huà zhǐ wéi qí jú

My wife draws a chessboard on a sheet of paper;

稚子敲针作钓钩。

zhì zǐ qiāo zhēn zuò diào gōu

my little boy taps a needle into a fishhook.

多病所须唯药物，

duō bìng suǒ xū wéi yào wù

Sick so often, all I need is medicine —

微躯此外更何求。

wēi qū cǐ wài gèng hé qiú

what else could a small man like me want?
