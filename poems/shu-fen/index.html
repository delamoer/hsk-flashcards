导读About this poem

At sixty-one, Lu You looks in the mirror: the young man who dreamed of winning back the north has grey hair, and the north is still lost. A poem of patriotism and bitter disappointment.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗是陆游六十一岁、被罢官闲居在家乡时写的。“书愤”就是写下心中的愤慨。前两句回忆年轻时：那时哪里知道世事这么艰难，北望被金人占领的中原，豪气像山一样。三、四句是千古名句，全由名词组成：“楼船、夜雪、瓜洲渡”，“铁马、秋风、大散关”，没有一个动词，却像电影镜头一样，写出当年宋军在东南水上、西北关口抗敌的壮阔场面，这种写法叫“名词并列”。后四句转到现在：我曾经自比为“塞上长城”，要保卫国家，现在看来只是空想；镜子里两鬓已经斑白。最后用诸葛亮作比：他的《出师表》真是闻名后世，千百年来，谁能和他相比？表面是赞诸葛亮，其实是感叹自己报国无门，也暗讽朝廷里没有这样的人。全诗由壮到悲，愤慨深沉。

In English

Lu You wrote this at sixty-one, dismissed from office and living at home. The title means “writing down my anger”. The opening recalls his youth: back then he had no idea how hard the world could be; looking north toward the lost heartland, now held by the Jurchen Jin, he felt a spirit as mighty as a mountain. Lines three and four are among the most famous in Chinese poetry, and they contain no verbs at all — just six images, three in each line: towered warships, night snow, the Guazhou ferry; armoured horses, autumn wind, Dasan Pass. Like film shots, they show Song armies holding the river in the southeast and the mountain passes in the northwest. Then the poem turns to the present. He once called himself a “Great Wall on the frontier”, sworn to defend the country; it came to nothing, and the mirror shows his temples already grey. Finally he names Zhuge Liang, whose famous memorial before marching north is still admired: in a thousand years, who has come close to him? It praises Zhuge Liang, but it also mourns his own wasted life, and quietly accuses a court with no one of that stature. The poem moves from heroism to grief, with anger running underneath.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Lu You (1125–1210) lived when northern China had fallen to the Jurchen Jin, and he spent his life urging the Southern Song to win it back. He wrote this in 1186 at his home in Shanyin (today’s Shaoxing), after being dismissed; the “Guazhou ferry” and “Dasan Pass” recall real battle lines of his time — he had served on the northwestern front near Dasan Pass in 1172.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

那知 nǎ zhī（那，同“哪”）；大散关 Dàsǎn Guān；塞上 sài；衰鬓 shuāi bìn；千载 zǎi；伯仲 bó zhòng。机器朗读“那、散、塞、载”容易读错，已在“朗读用字”列换成同音字，音频需人工听验。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行文本（《剑南诗稿》）。原题《书愤》共五首，此为第一首，即教材通行的一首。“瓜洲渡”一作“瓜州渡”。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

早岁那知世事艰，

zǎo suì nǎ zhī shì shì jiān

When I was young, how could I know how hard the world would be?

中原北望气如山。

zhōng yuán běi wàng qì rú shān

I looked north to the lost heartland with a spirit tall as a mountain.

楼船夜雪瓜洲渡，

lóu chuán yè xuě guā zhōu dù

Towered warships, snow at night, the ferry at Guazhou;

铁马秋风大散关。

tiě mǎ qiū fēng dà sǎn guān

armoured horses, autumn wind, the Dasan Pass.

塞上长城空自许，

sài shàng cháng chéng kōng zì xǔ

I once vowed to be a Great Wall on the frontier — empty words;

镜中衰鬓已先斑。

jìng zhōng shuāi bìn yǐ xiān bān

the mirror shows my thinning hair already grey.

出师一表真名世，

chū shī yī biǎo zhēn míng shì

Zhuge Liang’s memorial before the march still rings through the ages —

千载谁堪伯仲间！

qiān zǎi shuí kān bó zhòng jiān

in a thousand years, who has ever stood beside him?
