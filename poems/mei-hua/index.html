导读About this poem

A few branches of plum blossom open alone in a corner in the depth of winter. From far away they look like snow — but a faint scent tells you otherwise.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗只有二十个字，写墙角的几枝梅花。“墙角”很不起眼，梅花却在最冷的时候“独自”开放——别的花都不开，它不怕冷，也不需要人看。后两句很巧：远远看去，白白的梅花像雪一样，但作者知道那不是雪，因为有一阵淡淡的香气飘过来。“暗香”是看不见、悄悄传来的香味。作者没有直接夸梅花，而是通过“像雪又不是雪”的对比，同时写出了梅花的颜色（白）和香气。这首诗也是“托物言志”（借写东西表达自己的志向）：梅花在寒冷中坚持开放，象征一个人在困境中仍保持高洁的品格。

In English

Just twenty characters about a few branches of plum blossom in the corner of a wall. A wall corner is an easy place to overlook, yet the plum flowers open there “all alone” in the bitter cold, when nothing else is blooming — they don’t need warmth and they don’t need an audience. The second half is a neat little puzzle. From a distance the white blossoms look like snow, but the poet knows they aren’t, because a faint fragrance is drifting his way. 暗香 (“hidden fragrance”) means a scent you can’t see, one that reaches you quietly. Without praising the flowers directly, the comparison “like snow, but not snow” tells us two things at once: they are pure white, and they are fragrant. Chinese poets often describe an object to express their own ideals, and the plum is a favourite example: flowering in the cold, it stands for a person who stays upright and clean even in hard times.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Wang Anshi (1021–1086) was a chancellor of the Northern Song famous for his sweeping political reforms. The poem is often linked to his later years, after his reforms met fierce opposition and he withdrew to Jiangning (today’s Nanjing).

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

“数枝”的“数”读 shù（几），不读 shǔ。“为有”的“为”读 wèi（因为）。“凌寒”的“凌”读 líng。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

墙角数枝梅，

qiáng jiǎo shù zhī méi

A few plum branches in a corner of the wall

凌寒独自开。

líng hán dú zì kāi

brave the cold and bloom all on their own.

遥知不是雪，

yáo zhī bù shì xuě

Even from far off I can tell it isn’t snow —

为有暗香来。

wèi yǒu àn xiāng lái

a faint sweet scent comes drifting my way.
