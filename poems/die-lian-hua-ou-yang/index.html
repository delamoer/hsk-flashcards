导读About this poem

A woman shut away in a deep courtyard waits for a man who is out enjoying himself. Spring is ending in wind and rain, and she asks the flowers why — but they only blow away.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首词用一个女子的口吻写春天将尽时的寂寞。开头“庭院深深深几许”连用三个“深”字：院子一层又一层，杨柳像烟一样浓，帘子数也数不清——她被关在最深处。她的丈夫骑着华丽的马去寻欢作乐，她登上高楼也看不见他去的地方（“章台路”指游乐的街巷）。下阕写暮春的风雨：门在黄昏里关上，她没有办法把春天留住，就像留不住青春和爱情。结尾两句最有名：她含着泪问花，花不说话，只是一片片飞过秋千，飞走了。人问花，花不答，这种“以景结情”的写法（用一个画面结束，把感情留给读者）让伤心更深一层。李清照非常喜欢第一句，还借它写过好几首词。

In English

The poem speaks in the voice of a woman alone at the end of spring. It opens with three “deep”s in a row: courtyard behind courtyard, willows thick as smoke, curtain after curtain — and she is at the very centre, closed in. Her husband has ridden off on a fine horse with a jade bridle to the pleasure quarters; even from the top of her tower she can’t see the road he took (Zhangtai Road was a byword for such places). The second half brings late-spring wind and rain. The gate closes on the dusk, and there is no way to make spring stay — nor youth, nor love. The last two lines are the famous ones: with tears in her eyes she asks the flowers, the flowers say nothing, and a flurry of petals blows past the swing and away. Ending on an image instead of a statement, and letting the scene carry the feeling, is a classic move in Chinese lyrics. Li Qingzhao loved the first line so much that she borrowed it to open several poems of her own.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Ouyang Xiu (1007–1072) was a leading statesman, historian and essayist of the Northern Song. This lyric also appears in the collection of the earlier poet Feng Yansi, so its authorship is disputed; it is traditionally attributed to Ouyang Xiu, as Li Qingzhao already did.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

“帘幕无重数”：重 chóng（层），数 shù。“玉勒”的“勒”读 lè。“游冶”的“冶”读 yě。“雨横风狂”的“横”读 hèng（凶猛、粗暴），不读 héng。“几许”的“许”读 xǔ。“游冶处”的“处”读 chù。机器朗读容易把“重数”读错，已在“朗读用字”换成同音字；“横”无合适的同音常用字，音频需人工听验。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行文本。此词亦见冯延巳《阳春集》，作者有争议，一般归欧阳修（李清照《临江仙》序即称“欧阳公作《蝶恋花》”）。“乱红飞过秋千去”有的本子作“乱红飞去秋千去”，不用。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

庭院深深深几许，

tíng yuàn shēn shēn shēn jǐ xǔ

Deep, deep the courtyard — how deep can it go?

杨柳堆烟，帘幕无重数。

yáng liǔ duī yān, lián mù wú chóng shù

Willows heaped like mist, curtains hung layer behind layer.

玉勒雕鞍游冶处，

yù lè diāo ān yóu yě chù

He has ridden off, jade bridle, carved saddle, to his pleasures;

楼高不见章台路。

lóu gāo bù jiàn zhāng tái lù

high as my tower is, I can’t see the road he took.

雨横风狂三月暮，

yǔ hèng fēng kuáng sān yuè mù

Rain lashing, wind raging, spring’s last month running out;

门掩黄昏，无计留春住。

mén yǎn huáng hūn, wú jì liú chūn zhù

I close the gate on the dusk, but nothing I do can make spring stay.

泪眼问花花不语，

lèi yǎn wèn huā huā bù yǔ

Through tears I ask the flowers why; the flowers don’t answer.

乱红飞过秋千去。

luàn hóng fēi guò qiū qiān qù

A flurry of red petals drifts past the swing and is gone.
