导读About this poem

An old fisherman sleeps under a cliff, makes his breakfast fire with bamboo, and is gone by the time the mist lifts — leaving only the creak of an oar and a mountain stream turned suddenly green.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗是柳宗元被贬到永州时写的。前两句写渔翁的生活：晚上靠着西边的山岩过夜，早上打来清清的湘江水，烧竹子煮饭，一切都那么简单自然。第三四句最有名：太阳出来，烟雾散了，却看不见人影；只听见“欸乃”一声摇橹的声音，山水一下子绿了起来。人去了哪里？诗人不说，只写声音和颜色，好像渔翁已经和山水融在一起。用声音来写色彩，这种写法叫“通感”，让画面非常奇妙。最后两句写渔翁的船已经顺流而下到了天边，回头一看，只有山岩上的白云，无心地互相追逐。“无心”说白云不求什么，也说渔翁、诗人想要的那种放下一切的心境。苏轼认为最后两句可以删掉，也有人反对，这是文学史上一个有名的讨论。

In English

Liu Zongyuan wrote this while in exile in Yongzhou, in the far south. The first two lines show the fisherman’s life: at night he moors under the western cliffs; at dawn he draws clear water from the Xiang River and burns bamboo to cook — everything simple and natural. The next two lines are the famous ones. The sun comes up, the mist melts away, and there is no one to be seen; there is only the creak of an oar — “ai-nai” — and suddenly the mountains and water are green. Where has he gone? The poet doesn’t say. He gives us only a sound and a colour, as if the man has dissolved into the landscape. Using a sound to bring out a colour is a kind of synaesthesia, and it makes the scene feel almost magical. In the last two lines the boat has drifted downstream to the edge of the sky; looking back, there are only clouds above the cliff, chasing each other “without intention”. That word 无心 (“no purpose”) describes the clouds, but also the calm, let-it-all-go state of mind the fisherman — and the exiled poet — longs for. The great poet Su Shi thought the last two lines should be cut; others disagreed, and the debate became famous in its own right.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Liu Zongyuan (773–819) was a Tang official and one of the great prose writers of his age. After a failed reform movement in 805 he was exiled to Yongzhou (in today’s Hunan) for ten years; this poem is traditionally dated to that period.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

“欸乃”是摇橹声，通行注本读 ǎi nǎi，另有旧读 ǎo ǎi，网站读 ǎi nǎi。“夜傍”的“傍”读 bàng（靠近）；“晓汲”的“汲”读 jí；“回看”的“看”读 kàn；“相逐”的“相”读 xiāng。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行版本，全诗六句。苏轼曾说末两句“虽不必亦可”，主张删去，后世有争论；网站保留全诗。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

渔翁夜傍西岩宿，

yú wēng yè bàng xī yán sù

At night the old fisherman moors beneath the western cliff;

晓汲清湘燃楚竹。

xiǎo jí qīng xiāng rán chǔ zhú

at dawn he dips the clear Xiang and lights a bamboo fire.

烟销日出不见人，

yān xiāo rì chū bù jiàn rén

The mist lifts, the sun is up — and he is nowhere to be seen;

欸乃一声山水绿。

ǎi nǎi yī shēng shān shuǐ lǜ

one creak of an oar, and the hills and river turn green.

回看天际下中流，

huí kàn tiān jì xià zhōng liú

Drifting downstream to the edge of the sky, he looks back:

岩上无心云相逐。

yán shàng wú xīn yún xiāng zhú

over the cliff, clouds chase each other, wanting nothing.
