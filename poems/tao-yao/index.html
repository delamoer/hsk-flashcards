导读About this poem

A wedding song from about 3,000 years ago: a young bride is compared to a peach tree in full bloom, and the singers wish her a happy new home. Short, repetitive and joyful — and the source of a famous Chinese pun.

中文解析

这是一首送姑娘出嫁的祝福歌。每一章都从桃树写起：先是桃花开得红艳艳（灼灼其华），再是结满了大桃子（有蕡其实），最后是叶子长得又密又绿（其叶蓁蓁）。用正当盛开的桃花比喻年轻漂亮的新娘，这个比喻后来被无数诗人用过，“人面桃花”就从这里来。从花到果到叶，也暗含着祝福：婚后生活美满、子孙兴旺、家族繁盛。每章后两句几乎一样，只换最后两个字（室家、家室、家人），反复唱“宜其室家”——她会让这个家和和美美。这种“重章叠句”的写法，正适合婚礼上大家一起唱。有趣的是，今天的成语“逃之夭夭”（溜走了）就是拿“桃之夭夭”谐音开的玩笑。

In English

This is a song for a girl on her wedding day. Every stanza begins with a peach tree. First its blossoms blaze red (灼灼其华), then it hangs heavy with plump fruit (有蕡其实), and finally its leaves grow thick and green (其叶蓁蓁). A peach tree in full bloom as a picture of a lovely young bride became one of the most lasting images in Chinese poetry — later poets kept pairing a pretty face with peach blossoms. Moving from flowers to fruit to leaves also carries a blessing: a happy marriage, many children, a flourishing family. The second half of each stanza barely changes — only the last two characters shift (室家, 家室, 家人) — as the singers wish again and again that she will “suit her new home” and bring it harmony. Repetition like this is perfect for a crowd singing together at a wedding. A fun footnote: the modern idiom 逃之夭夭 (“to run off and vanish”) is a pun on this poem’s first line, swapping 桃 “peach” for 逃 “to flee”, which sounds the same.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

The poem belongs to the “Airs of Zhounan” (周南) in the Book of Songs, folk songs from the lands south of the Zhou capital, collected roughly between the 11th and 6th centuries BC. Its author is unknown. Spring, when peach trees flower, was traditionally a season for weddings.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

灼灼其华的“华”读 huā（古同“花”，通行注本注 huā，不读 huá）；蕡 fén；蓁蓁 zhēn zhēn；夭夭 yāo yāo；灼灼 zhuó zhuó；之子于归的“于”读 yú。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

桃之夭夭，灼灼其华。

táo zhī yāo yāo, zhuó zhuó qí huā

The young peach tree is in its prime, its blossoms blazing pink.

之子于归，宜其室家。

zhī zǐ yú guī, yí qí shì jiā

This girl is off to be married — she will bring harmony to her new home.

桃之夭夭，有蕡其实。

táo zhī yāo yāo, yǒu fén qí shí

The young peach tree is in its prime, heavy with round, ripe fruit.

之子于归，宜其家室。

zhī zǐ yú guī, yí qí jiā shì

This girl is off to be married — her new family will be glad of her.

桃之夭夭，其叶蓁蓁。

táo zhī yāo yāo, qí yè zhēn zhēn

The young peach tree is in its prime, dense with green leaves.

之子于归，宜其家人。

zhī zǐ yú guī, yí qí jiā rén

This girl is off to be married — she will make the whole family happy.
