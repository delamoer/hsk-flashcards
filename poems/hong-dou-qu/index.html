导读About this poem

A song from the novel Dream of the Red Chamber: line after line of things that “cannot” be done — cannot stop crying, cannot sleep, cannot swallow, cannot smooth one’s brows — all because of longing.

这首诗被谱成了歌Sung as a song

王洁实、陈力《红豆曲》Wang Jieshi & Chen Li

From the 1987 Dream of the Red Chamber series: Baoyu's drinking-party song in chapter 28 of the novel, set by Wang Liping. Every line is Cao Xueqin's text — a chain of "can't stop… can't…" images of lovesickness.

中文解析

这首曲子出自清代小说《红楼梦》第二十八回，是贾宝玉在宴会上唱的，写的是一个女子的相思之苦。全曲最大的特点是一连串“××不××”：滴不尽、开不完、睡不稳、忘不了、咽不下、照不见、展不开、挨不明……一句接一句，把相思写得没完没了。开头把相思的眼泪比作红豆——红豆在中国又叫“相思豆”，是相思的象征。接着写春天花开满楼，她却无心欣赏；风雨黄昏睡不着；新愁旧愁忘不掉；山珍海味吃不下；镜子里的人越来越瘦；眉头总皱着，长夜熬不到天亮。最后用青山、绿水作比：愁就像遮不住的青山、流不断的绿水，永远没有尽头。语言口语化，节奏像民歌，所以后来被谱成曲，传唱很广。

In English

This song comes from chapter 28 of Dream of the Red Chamber, the great 18th-century novel. The young hero Jia Baoyu sings it at a party, but the voice is that of a woman sick with longing. What makes it memorable is the chain of “can’t” phrases — 滴不尽 “can’t stop dripping”, 睡不稳 “can’t sleep soundly”, 忘不了 “can’t forget”, 咽不下 “can’t swallow”, 展不开 “can’t smooth out” — piled up line after line until the longing seems endless. It begins by comparing her tears to red beans, which in China are called “longing beans” and stand for missing someone. Spring flowers fill the tower, but she can’t enjoy them; wind and rain keep her awake; old sorrows and new ones won’t leave; the finest food sticks in her throat; the face in the mirror grows thin; her brows stay knitted; the night drips on without ever reaching dawn. Finally she compares her sorrow to green hills that nothing can hide and green water that never stops flowing. For learners, this repeated verb + 不 + result pattern (吃不下, 睡不着, 忘不了) is exactly the potential complement of modern Chinese — a nice bridge between grammar class and classical literature.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Cao Xueqin (c. 1715–c. 1763) wrote Dream of the Red Chamber, the best-known Chinese novel, after his once-powerful family lost its fortune. The novel is full of poems and songs written for its characters; this one is sung by Jia Baoyu at a friend’s banquet.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

血泪 xuè lèi（书面读音）。了：忘不了 liǎo。咽 yàn（吞下，不读 yān）。噎 yē。莼 chún（莼菜）。菱 líng。挨 ái（熬过，不读 āi）。更漏 gēng lòu（不读 gèng）。呀 yā（叹词）。机器朗读已把“咽”“挨”“更”替换为同音字，音频需人工听验。

版本说明 About the text

采用人民文学出版社通行本《红楼梦》第二十八回文本，已与维基文库本核对一致（文库作“捱不明”，捱即挨的异体，今规范作“挨”）。“玉粒金莼”有的版本作“玉粒金波”；“照不见”有的版本作“照不尽”。全曲完整收录。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

滴不尽相思血泪抛红豆，

dī bù jìn xiāng sī xuè lèi pāo hóng dòu

Tears of longing, red as blood, fall like red beans without end;

开不完春柳春花满画楼，

kāi bù wán chūn liǔ chūn huā mǎn huà lóu

spring willows, spring flowers, endlessly blooming round the painted tower;

睡不稳纱窗风雨黄昏后，

shuì bù wěn shā chuāng fēng yǔ huáng hūn hòu

no easy sleep, with wind and rain at the window after dusk;

忘不了新愁与旧愁，

wàng bù liǎo xīn chóu yǔ jiù chóu

no forgetting the new sorrows, or the old;

咽不下玉粒金莼噎满喉，

yàn bù xià yù lì jīn chún yē mǎn hóu

fine rice and rare soup stick in my throat — I cannot swallow;

照不见菱花镜里形容瘦。

zhào bù jiàn líng huā jìng lǐ xíng róng shòu

the mirror can hardly hold the thin face looking back at me.

展不开的眉头，

zhǎn bù kāi de méi tóu

Brows that will not smooth,

挨不明的更漏。

ái bù míng de gēng lòu

a water clock dripping through a night that will not end.

呀！恰便似遮不住的青山隐隐，

yā, qià biàn sì zhē bù zhù de qīng shān yǐn yǐn

Ah — it is like the green hills, faint but never hidden,

流不断的绿水悠悠。

liú bù duàn de lǜ shuǐ yōu yōu

and the green water flowing on and on, never ending.
