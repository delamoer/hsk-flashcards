导读About this poem

Li Bai’s great drinking song: life is short, hair turns white overnight, so drink with your friends now — “Heaven made me, and my talent will be of use.” Loud, proud, and secretly heartbroken.

第一段Part 1
01
君不见黄河之水天上来，奔流到海不复回。

直译LiteralHave you not seen the Yellow River’s water coming from the sky, rushing to the sea, never to return?

意译MeaningHave you not seen the Yellow River pour down from the sky, racing to the sea, never to come back?

02
君不见高堂明镜悲白发，朝如青丝暮成雪。

直译LiteralHave you not seen, in the bright mirror of the high hall, grief at white hair — in the morning like black silk, by evening turned to snow?

意译MeaningHave you not seen a man at his mirror, grieving over his white hair — black silk at dawn, snow by nightfall?

第二段Part 2
03
人生得意须尽欢，莫使金樽空对月。

直译LiteralWhen life goes your way, you must enjoy it fully; do not let the golden cup face the moon empty.

意译MeaningWhen life goes well, enjoy it to the full — never leave a golden cup empty under the moon.

04
天生我材必有用，千金散尽还复来。

直译LiteralHeaven gave birth to my talent, it must have a use; a thousand in gold, scattered and spent, will return again.

意译MeaningHeaven made me, and my gifts will be of use; spend a fortune — it will all come back.

05
烹羊宰牛且为乐，会须一饮三百杯。

直译LiteralCook the sheep, slaughter the ox, and for now make merry; we really must drink three hundred cups in one go.

意译MeaningRoast the lamb, kill the ox, let us be happy while we can — three hundred cups, and we drink them all!

第三段Part 3
06
岑夫子，丹丘生，将进酒，杯莫停。

直译LiteralMaster Cen, young Danqiu, please drink the wine, do not stop your cups.

意译MeaningMaster Cen, my friend Danqiu — drink up, drink up, don’t put your cups down!

07
与君歌一曲，请君为我倾耳听。

直译LiteralLet me sing you a song; please, for my sake, bend your ears and listen.

意译MeaningLet me sing you a song — lean close, and listen to me.

第四段Part 4
08
钟鼓馔玉不足贵，但愿长醉不复醒。

直译LiteralBells, drums, fine food and jade are not worth valuing; I only wish to stay drunk and never wake again.

意译MeaningBells and drums, rich food and jade — what are they worth? I only want to stay drunk and never wake.

09
古来圣贤皆寂寞，惟有饮者留其名。

直译LiteralSince ancient times, sages and worthies have all been lonely; only drinkers have left their names.

意译MeaningThe sages of old all died lonely and forgotten; only the drinkers are remembered.

10
陈王昔时宴平乐，斗酒十千恣欢谑。

直译LiteralThe Prince of Chen once feasted at Pingle Lodge; a dou of wine cost ten thousand, and they joked and laughed without restraint.

意译MeaningOnce the Prince of Chen feasted at Pingle Lodge — wine at ten thousand a jug, laughter without end.

第五段Part 5
11
主人何为言少钱，径须沽取对君酌。

直译LiteralHost, why do you say you are short of money? Just go and buy wine, and pour it facing you.

意译MeaningHost, why talk about being short of money? Just buy more wine, and let us pour for each other.

12
五花马，千金裘，呼儿将出换美酒，与尔同销万古愁。

直译LiteralThe dappled horse, the fur coat worth a thousand in gold — call the boy to take them out and trade them for fine wine, and with you I’ll melt away the sorrow of ten thousand ages.

意译MeaningMy dappled horse, my thousand-gold furs — call the boy, trade them all for good wine, and let us drown together the sorrows of all time.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

《将进酒》是乐府旧题，意思是“请喝酒吧”。李白和朋友岑勋、元丹丘一起喝酒，借酒唱出了心里的豪情和苦闷。开头两个“君不见”气势很大：黄河从天上奔流到海，一去不回；早上还是黑发，晚上就白了——用夸张写人生短暂。于是他说：得意的时候就要尽情欢乐，“天生我材必有用，千金散尽还复来”，自信得不得了。中间他劝朋友“杯莫停”，又说富贵不值得珍惜，古代的圣贤都很寂寞，只有喝酒的人留下了名字——这是愤激的话，背后是怀才不遇（有才华却不被重用）的痛苦。最后他愿意拿名马和皮衣去换酒，“与尔同销万古愁”。全诗句子长短自由，感情一会儿狂欢、一会儿悲伤，像大河一样奔腾，是李白最有代表性的作品之一。

In English

“Qiang jin jiu” is an old song title meaning “Please, drink!” Li Bai is drinking with two friends, Cen Xun (“Master Cen”) and Yuan Danqiu, and the poem is his toast — part party, part cry of pain. It opens with two huge images, each introduced by “Have you not seen…?”: the Yellow River pouring down from the sky to the sea, never to return; and a man looking in the mirror, his black hair turned white between morning and evening. Both are exaggerations, and both say the same thing: time runs out fast. So, Li Bai says, when life goes well, enjoy it to the full — and then comes the line every Chinese person knows: “Heaven made me, and my talent will be of use; spend a thousand in gold, and it will all come back.” In the middle he turns directly to his friends — “Drink up, don’t put your cups down!” — and sings that riches are worth nothing and the old sages all died lonely, while only great drinkers are remembered. That sounds like a joke, but it is bitter: Li Bai believed he was born for great things, yet the court never gave him a real role. At the end he tells his host to trade his fine horse and fur coat for more wine, “to drown with you the sorrow of ten thousand ages.” Notice how freely the lines change length, from three characters to eleven, rushing and stopping like the river in the first line.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Li Bai (701–762) is traditionally thought to have written this around 752, years after he left his post at the Chang’an court in disappointment. He was drinking at Mount Song with his friends Cen Xun and Yuan Danqiu, a Daoist.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

以下按统编高中教材注音：题目“将进酒”的“将”读 qiāng（请），不读 jiāng。“还复来”的“还”读 huán。“朝如青丝”的“朝”读 zhāo。“白发”的“发”读 fà。“且为乐”的“为”读 wéi；“请君为我”的“为”读 wèi；“主人何为言少钱”的“为”读 wèi（何为：为什么）。“宴平乐”的“乐”读 lè（平乐观，宫观名）。“斗酒”的“斗”读 dǒu。“少钱”的“少”读 shǎo。“将出”的“将”读 jiāng（拿）。生僻字：岑 cén、馔 zhuàn、恣 zì、谑 xuè、沽 gū、酌 zhuó、裘 qiú。“不复醒”的“醒”读 xǐng。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

君不见黄河之水天上来，奔流到海不复回。

jūn bù jiàn huáng hé zhī shuǐ tiān shàng lái, bēn liú dào hǎi bù fù huí

Have you not seen the Yellow River pour down from the sky, racing to the sea, never to come back?

君不见高堂明镜悲白发，朝如青丝暮成雪。

jūn bù jiàn gāo táng míng jìng bēi bái fà, zhāo rú qīng sī mù chéng xuě

Have you not seen a man at his mirror, grieving over his white hair — black silk at dawn, snow by nightfall?

人生得意须尽欢，莫使金樽空对月。

rén shēng dé yì xū jìn huān, mò shǐ jīn zūn kōng duì yuè

When life goes well, enjoy it to the full — never leave a golden cup empty under the moon.

天生我材必有用，千金散尽还复来。

tiān shēng wǒ cái bì yǒu yòng, qiān jīn sàn jìn huán fù lái

Heaven made me, and my gifts will be of use; spend a fortune — it will all come back.

烹羊宰牛且为乐，会须一饮三百杯。

pēng yáng zǎi niú qiě wéi lè, huì xū yī yǐn sān bǎi bēi

Roast the lamb, kill the ox, let us be happy while we can — three hundred cups, and we drink them all!

岑夫子，丹丘生，将进酒，杯莫停。

cén fū zǐ, dān qiū shēng, qiāng jìn jiǔ, bēi mò tíng

Master Cen, my friend Danqiu — drink up, drink up, don’t put your cups down!

与君歌一曲，请君为我倾耳听。

yǔ jūn gē yī qǔ, qǐng jūn wèi wǒ qīng ěr tīng

Let me sing you a song — lean close, and listen to me.

钟鼓馔玉不足贵，但愿长醉不复醒。

zhōng gǔ zhuàn yù bù zú guì, dàn yuàn cháng zuì bù fù xǐng

Bells and drums, rich food and jade — what are they worth? I only want to stay drunk and never wake.

古来圣贤皆寂寞，惟有饮者留其名。

gǔ lái shèng xián jiē jì mò, wéi yǒu yǐn zhě liú qí míng

The sages of old all died lonely and forgotten; only the drinkers are remembered.

陈王昔时宴平乐，斗酒十千恣欢谑。

chén wáng xī shí yàn píng lè, dǒu jiǔ shí qiān zì huān xuè

Once the Prince of Chen feasted at Pingle Lodge — wine at ten thousand a jug, laughter without end.

主人何为言少钱，径须沽取对君酌。

zhǔ rén hé wèi yán shǎo qián, jìng xū gū qǔ duì jūn zhuó

Host, why talk about being short of money? Just buy more wine, and let us pour for each other.

五花马，千金裘，呼儿将出换美酒，与尔同销万古愁。

wǔ huā mǎ, qiān jīn qiú, hū ér jiāng chū huàn měi jiǔ, yǔ ěr tóng xiāo wàn gǔ chóu

My dappled horse, my thousand-gold furs — call the boy, trade them all for good wine, and let us drown together the sorrows of all time.
