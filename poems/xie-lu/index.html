导读About this poem

A funeral song only three lines long: dew on a shallot leaf dries in a moment, but comes back tomorrow — a person who dies never does.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

《薤露》是汉代的挽歌，就是送葬时唱的歌，全诗只有三句。薤是一种像葱的植物，叶子细长，露水在上面很难停留，太阳一出来很快就干了。诗人说：薤叶上的露水，多么容易干啊！可是露水干了，明天早上还会再落下来；人死了，一去就再也回不来了。这里用的是对比：露水虽然短暂，却能一次次回来；人的生命只有一次。用自然现象和人生作对比，悲伤一下子就加深了。这首诗语言非常简单，却说出了人类最普遍的感受——对死亡的无奈。据说它原本是为秦末齐国贵族田横而作，田横自杀后，他的门客作了这首歌来哀悼他。后来汉武帝时，《薤露》被定为送王公贵族下葬时唱的挽歌。“薤露”也成了一个典故，比喻人生短暂。

In English

“Dew on the Shallots” is a Han funeral song, sung as a coffin was carried to the grave, and it is only three lines long. Xiè is a plant like a spring onion, with thin leaves that dew can barely cling to; once the sun comes up, the dew is gone. The singer says: the dew on the shallot leaf — how quickly it dries! Yet dried dew falls again tomorrow morning; a person who dies goes once and never comes back. The whole song is built on one contrast. Dew is fleeting, but it returns again and again; a human life happens only once. Setting nature beside human life makes the grief sharper in very few words. The language could hardly be simpler, yet it says something everyone feels — how helpless we are before death. Tradition says the song was first sung for Tian Heng, a nobleman of Qi who killed himself rather than submit to the new Han dynasty, and that his followers wrote it to mourn him. Later it became the official funeral song for princes and nobles, and 薤露 itself became a byword for the shortness of life.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

According to Cui Bao’s Gujin zhu (4th century), the song was written by the followers of Tian Heng (d. 202 BC), and in the reign of Emperor Wu of Han it was assigned to the funerals of princes and nobles, while its companion song “Haoli” (蒿里) was sung for officials and commoners.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

薤上露，何易晞！

xiè shàng lù, hé yì xī

Dew on a shallot leaf — how fast it dries!

露晞明朝更复落，

lù xī míng zhāo gèng fù luò

Dried dew will settle again tomorrow at dawn —

人死一去何时归！

rén sǐ yī qù hé shí guī

but when someone dies and goes, when will they ever come back?
