导读About this poem

Written by a captured Song commander when his enemies demanded he urge his own side to surrender. His answer was this poem — ending in two lines every Chinese student knows: “Who in all history has not died? Let my loyal heart shine in the records.”

Wang Yaoqing sings Wen Tianxiang's "Crossing the Lingding Sea" on CCTV's poetry-in-song show Everlasting Classics (season 4, 2021), adding a few newly written lines around the complete original text.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

1279年，文天祥被元军俘虏后，被押在船上经过广东的零丁洋。元军将领要他写信劝宋朝最后的将领投降，他就写了这首诗作为回答。首联回顾自己的一生：靠精通儒家经典考中状元做官，起兵抗元已经四年。颔联写国家和个人：山河破碎，像风里飘散的柳絮；自己的命运起起落落，像雨打的浮萍。这两句用比喻，把国破家亡写得很形象。颈联巧用地名：前两年在江西惶恐滩打了败仗，心里惶恐；如今在零丁洋上，只能感叹自己孤苦零丁。地名和心情正好重合，这叫“双关”。尾联是千古名句：自古以来谁能不死？我要留下一颗赤诚的心，照耀在史册上。“汗青”指史书（古人在竹简上写字前要先烤出竹子的水分）。前六句越写越悲，最后两句却一下子变得坚定昂扬，表明宁死不降的决心。

In English

In 1279 Wen Tianxiang, a captured Song commander, was taken by ship across the Lingding Sea at the mouth of the Pearl River. The Yuan general ordered him to write to the last Song admiral urging surrender; this poem was his reply. The first couplet looks back on his life: he rose through mastering a Confucian classic and coming first in the imperial exam, and for four years now he has been fighting the Yuan. The second couplet sets the country beside himself: the land is shattered like willow fluff scattered by the wind; his own life rises and sinks like duckweed beaten by rain. The third couplet plays with place names. Two years earlier he was defeated at Huangkong (“Fear”) Rapids in Jiangxi, and he says he spoke of fear there; now, on the Lingding (“Alone”) Sea, he sighs at how alone he is. Place and feeling match exactly — a double meaning. Then the famous ending: since ancient times, who has not died? Let me leave a loyal, red heart to shine in the history books. 汗青, literally “sweated green”, means history, because bamboo strips were heated until they “sweated” before people wrote on them. Six lines of grief turn into two of iron resolve: he would rather die than surrender.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Wen Tianxiang (1236–1283) came first in the imperial exam of 1256 and later raised troops to resist the Mongol Yuan invasion. Captured in 1278, he refused every offer to serve the Yuan and was executed in Dadu (Beijing) after more than three years in prison; he is remembered as a model of loyalty.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

辛苦遭逢起一经，

xīn kǔ zāo féng qǐ yī jīng

Through hard years, I rose by mastering a classic;

干戈寥落四周星。

gān gē liáo luò sì zhōu xīng

four years of war now, and my army dwindling.

山河破碎风飘絮，

shān hé pò suì fēng piāo xù

The land lies broken, like willow fluff blown by the wind;

身世浮沉雨打萍。

shēn shì fú chén yǔ dǎ píng

my life rises and sinks, like duckweed beaten by rain.

惶恐滩头说惶恐，

huáng kǒng tān tóu shuō huáng kǒng

At Fear Rapids I spoke of my fear;

零丁洋里叹零丁。

líng dīng yáng lǐ tàn líng dīng

on the Lonely Sea I sigh that I am alone.

人生自古谁无死？

rén shēng zì gǔ shuí wú sǐ

Since time began, who has ever escaped death?

留取丹心照汗青。

liú qǔ dān xīn zhào hàn qīng

Let me leave a loyal heart to shine in history.
