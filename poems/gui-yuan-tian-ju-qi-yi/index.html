Back to My Farm (No. 1)
陶渊明Táo Yuānmíng · Tao Yuanming魏晋南北朝 Wei, Jin & Southern and Northern Dynasties

导读About this poem

After years in government jobs he hated, Tao Yuanming quits and goes home to farm. Here he walks us round his little place — the thatched house, the willows, smoke over the village, a dog barking down the lane — and breathes: free at last.

第一段Part 1
01
少无适俗韵，性本爱丘山。

直译LiteralWhen young I had no knack for fitting in with the world; by nature I loved hills and mountains.

意译MeaningEven as a boy I never fit the world’s ways; my heart belonged to the hills.

02
误落尘网中，一去三十年。

直译LiteralBy mistake I fell into the dusty net; once gone, thirty years passed.

意译MeaningBy mistake I fell into the world’s dusty net, and the years slipped away.

03
羁鸟恋旧林，池鱼思故渊。

直译LiteralThe caged bird longs for its old woods; the pond fish misses its former pool.

意译MeaningA caged bird pines for its old forest; a pond fish dreams of its deep pool.

04
开荒南野际，守拙归园田。

直译LiteralI clear wasteland at the edge of the southern fields; keeping my clumsiness, I return to garden and field.

意译MeaningNow I break new ground in the southern fields — still my simple self, home on the farm.

第二段Part 2
05
方宅十余亩，草屋八九间。

直译LiteralMy plot is some ten mu square; a thatched house of eight or nine rooms.

意译MeaningA dozen mu of land around the house, a thatched roof over eight or nine rooms.

06
榆柳荫后檐，桃李罗堂前。

直译LiteralElms and willows shade the back eaves; peaches and plums are ranged before the hall.

意译MeaningElms and willows shade the eaves behind; peach and plum trees line up in front.

07
暧暧远人村，依依墟里烟。

直译LiteralDim and hazy, the distant villages; soft and lingering, the smoke above the hamlet.

意译MeaningFar villages blur in the haze; smoke rises slowly from the hamlet’s roofs.

08
狗吠深巷中，鸡鸣桑树颠。

直译LiteralDogs bark deep in the lanes; cocks crow on the tops of mulberry trees.

意译MeaningA dog barks somewhere down the lane; a rooster crows from the top of a mulberry tree.

第三段Part 3
09
户庭无尘杂，虚室有余闲。

直译LiteralMy gate and courtyard are free of worldly clutter; in my empty rooms there is leisure to spare.

意译MeaningNo worldly clutter at my gate; in my bare rooms, time to spare.

10
久在樊笼里，复得返自然。

直译LiteralLong I was inside a cage; again I can return to nature.

意译MeaningSo long shut up in a cage — and now I am back where I belong, with nature.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这是陶渊明辞官回乡后写的组诗第一首，写他回到田园的喜悦。开头说自己从小就不会迎合世俗，天性喜爱山林，却误入官场这张“尘网”，一去就是很多年。接着用两个比喻：笼中的鸟想念从前的树林，池里的鱼想念原来的深潭——这正是他做官时的心情。中间八句像一幅田园画：十几亩宅地，八九间草屋，榆树柳树遮着后屋檐，桃树李树排在堂前；远处的村庄模模糊糊，村子里的炊烟轻轻飘着；深巷里狗在叫，桑树顶上鸡在鸣。这些都是最普通的农村景物，在他笔下却安静、亲切。最后四句回到心情：家里没有杂事打扰，屋里空空的，人很清闲；在“樊笼”（鸟笼）里关了那么久，终于又回到了自然。开头的“尘网”和结尾的“樊笼”前后呼应。“守拙”是说保持自己笨拙、不会投机取巧的本性，这正是陶渊明的人生选择。这首诗语言平淡，却越读越有味道，是中国田园诗的代表作。

In English

This is the first in a series Tao Yuanming wrote after resigning from office, and it is full of the joy of coming home. He begins by saying he never fitted in with the world; by nature he loved hills and mountains, yet he blundered into the “dusty net” of official life and stayed caught for years. Two comparisons show how he felt: a caged bird misses its old woods, a fish in a pond misses its deep pool. The middle eight lines are a painting of his farm: a plot of a dozen mu, a thatched house of eight or nine rooms, elms and willows shading the back eaves, peach and plum trees lined up in front. Far off, a village blurs in the haze; smoke drifts gently from its chimneys. A dog barks deep in a lane, a rooster crows from the top of a mulberry tree. These are the plainest country sights, yet he makes them feel calm and dear. The last four lines return to his mood: no clutter, no busywork, plenty of quiet time. After so long in a cage, he is back with nature. The “net” at the start and the “cage” at the end frame the whole poem. 守拙, “keeping to my clumsiness”, means refusing to play clever games for advancement — the choice that defines Tao’s life. The language is plain, but the more you read it, the more you taste: this is the model for all later Chinese poems of country life.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Tao Yuanming (365?–427) served in a series of minor posts before resigning for good in 405 as magistrate of Pengze, famously refusing to “bow for five pecks of rice”. He spent the rest of his life farming in poverty near Mount Lu and is regarded as the father of Chinese pastoral poetry. This series of five poems was written in 406, the year after he went home.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

少 shào（年轻时）；羁鸟 jī；守拙 zhuō；榆柳荫后檐 yìn（遮蔽）；暧暧 ài ài；墟里 xū；桑树颠 diān；樊笼 fán lóng。“三十年”一说应作“十三年”，读法不变。

版本说明 About the text

采用统编教材（高中语文必修上册）文本，全诗二十句，网站一联一行（共十行）。“一去三十年”：陶渊明从初仕到归隐约十三年，故有学者认为“三十年”是“十三年”之误，或“已十年”之误，教材仍作“三十年”。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

少无适俗韵，性本爱丘山。

shào wú shì sú yùn, xìng běn ài qiū shān

Even as a boy I never fit the world’s ways; my heart belonged to the hills.

误落尘网中，一去三十年。

wù luò chén wǎng zhōng, yī qù sān shí nián

By mistake I fell into the world’s dusty net, and the years slipped away.

羁鸟恋旧林，池鱼思故渊。

jī niǎo liàn jiù lín, chí yú sī gù yuān

A caged bird pines for its old forest; a pond fish dreams of its deep pool.

开荒南野际，守拙归园田。

kāi huāng nán yě jì, shǒu zhuō guī yuán tián

Now I break new ground in the southern fields — still my simple self, home on the farm.

方宅十余亩，草屋八九间。

fāng zhái shí yú mǔ, cǎo wū bā jiǔ jiān

A dozen mu of land around the house, a thatched roof over eight or nine rooms.

榆柳荫后檐，桃李罗堂前。

yú liǔ yìn hòu yán, táo lǐ luó táng qián

Elms and willows shade the eaves behind; peach and plum trees line up in front.

暧暧远人村，依依墟里烟。

ài ài yuǎn rén cūn, yī yī xū lǐ yān

Far villages blur in the haze; smoke rises slowly from the hamlet’s roofs.

狗吠深巷中，鸡鸣桑树颠。

gǒu fèi shēn xiàng zhōng, jī míng sāng shù diān

A dog barks somewhere down the lane; a rooster crows from the top of a mulberry tree.

户庭无尘杂，虚室有余闲。

hù tíng wú chén zá, xū shì yǒu yú xián

No worldly clutter at my gate; in my bare rooms, time to spare.

久在樊笼里，复得返自然。

jiǔ zài fán lóng lǐ, fù dé fǎn zì rán

So long shut up in a cage — and now I am back where I belong, with nature.
