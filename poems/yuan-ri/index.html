导读About this poem

Firecrackers, warm spring wind, a special New Year wine, and fresh charms on every door: the Chinese New Year as it was celebrated a thousand years ago — and in many ways still is.

中文解析

这首诗写的是春节（古代叫“元日”，农历正月初一）。第一句：在噼噼啪啪的爆竹声中，旧的一年过去了。第二句：春风带来暖意，人们喝着屠苏酒（一种过年喝的药酒，据说能驱病）。第三句：太阳升起来，照着千家万户。第四句：家家都把门上的旧桃符换成新的。桃符是挂在门上的两块桃木板，写着神的名字，用来驱邪，后来慢慢变成了今天的春联。四句诗写了四件过年的事，热闹又充满希望。“新”和“旧”的对比还有更深的意思：王安石是一位改革家，很多人认为这首诗也表达了他除旧布新、推行变法的信心。

In English

This poem is about the Spring Festival — the first day of the lunar year, which in old Chinese was called Yuánrì. Each line shows one custom. Line one: amid the crackle of firecrackers, the old year is “removed”. Line two: the spring wind brings warmth as people drink túsū, a herbal wine taken at New Year to keep illness away. Line three: the rising sun shines on a thousand gates and ten thousand doors. Line four: every family swaps the old peach-wood charms on its door for new ones. These charms were two boards of peach wood painted with the names of door gods to ward off evil; over the centuries they turned into today’s red couplets (chūnlián) — so the scene is still familiar to every Chinese family. The contrast between “new” and “old” may carry a second meaning too. Wang Anshi was a famous reformer, and many readers take the poem as an expression of his confidence that the old ways could be swept out and new policies brought in.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Wang Anshi (1021–1086) was a chancellor of the Northern Song famous for his sweeping political reforms, and also one of the great prose writers of his age. The poem is often linked to the start of his reforms around 1069, though the date is not certain.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

爆竹声中一岁除，

bào zhú shēng zhōng yī suì chú

Firecrackers bang, and the old year is gone;

春风送暖入屠苏。

chūn fēng sòng nuǎn rù tú sū

the spring wind brings warmth into our New Year wine.

千门万户曈曈日，

qiān mén wàn hù tóng tóng rì

The morning sun shines bright on every door in town,

总把新桃换旧符。

zǒng bǎ xīn táo huàn jiù fú

and every home puts up new charms in place of the old.
