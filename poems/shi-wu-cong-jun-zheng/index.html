十五从军征

Shí Wǔ Cóng Jūn Zhēng
Off to War at Fifteen

导读About this poem

A soldier who left at fifteen comes home at eighty. His family are all graves, rabbits run through his house — and he cooks a meal with no one to share it. A short, devastating folk song about war.

01
十五从军征，八十始得归。

直译LiteralAt fifteen I joined the army to march; at eighty I could at last return.

意译MeaningAt fifteen I went off to war; at eighty I came home at last.

02
道逢乡里人：“家中有阿谁？”

直译LiteralOn the road I met a man from my village: “At home, who is there?”

意译MeaningI met a villager on the road: “Who is left of my family?”

03
“遥看是君家，松柏冢累累。”

直译Literal“Looking from afar, that is your house — pines, cypresses, graves one after another.”

意译Meaning“Your house is over there — among the pines, a row of graves.”

04
兔从狗窦入，雉从梁上飞。

直译LiteralRabbits enter through the dog hole; pheasants fly from the roof beams.

意译MeaningRabbits slip in through the dog hole; pheasants flap down from the beams.

05
中庭生旅谷，井上生旅葵。

直译LiteralIn the courtyard grows wild grain; by the well grows wild mallow.

意译MeaningWild grain has seeded the yard, wild mallow the edge of the well.

06
舂谷持作饭，采葵持作羹。

直译LiteralI pound the grain to make rice; I pick the mallow to make soup.

意译MeaningI husk the grain to cook some rice, pick the mallow for a soup.

07
羹饭一时熟，不知饴阿谁。

直译LiteralSoup and rice are soon cooked; I do not know whom to give them to.

意译MeaningSoon the meal is ready — but who is there to share it?

08
出门东向看，泪落沾我衣。

直译LiteralGoing out the gate, I look toward the east; tears fall and wet my clothes.

意译MeaningI walk out of the gate and look east, and tears run down onto my clothes.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首汉代民歌讲了一个让人心酸的故事。一个人十五岁就去当兵，到八十岁才回家。路上遇到同乡，他急忙问：“我家里还有谁？”同乡指给他看：“远远看去那就是你家，现在只剩松柏和一个个坟堆了。”他走到家里，看到野兔从狗洞里钻进去，野鸡在房梁上飞，院子里、井边长满了野生的谷子和葵菜。他舂了谷子做饭，摘了葵菜做汤，饭和汤很快做好了，却不知道给谁吃。最后他走出门向东望，眼泪落下来，打湿了衣服。诗里没有一句骂战争，却通过一个老兵回家看到的景象，写出了战争对普通人的伤害：六十五年的青春没有了，家也没有了。“兔”“雉”“旅谷”“旅葵”这些细节写出家园荒凉；“不知饴阿谁”写出他的孤独。语言像讲故事一样简单，力量却很大。

In English

This Han folk song tells a heartbreaking story. A man joined the army at fifteen and only came home at eighty. On the road he meets someone from his village and asks at once: “Who is still at home?” The neighbour points: “That’s your house over there — now just pines, cypresses and grave mounds.” At the house, rabbits slip in through the dog hole and pheasants fly from the rafters; wild grain and wild mallow have taken over the courtyard and the well. He husks the grain for rice and picks the mallow for soup. Soon the meal is ready — and he has no idea who to give it to. He walks out of the gate, looks east, and his tears fall on his clothes. The song never criticises war directly. It simply shows what one old soldier finds when he comes home: sixty-five years of his life gone, and his whole family with them. Small details — rabbits, pheasants, grain that sowed itself — show how empty the home has become, and the unshared meal shows how alone he is. It reads like plain storytelling, which is exactly why it hits so hard.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

This is an anonymous folk song preserved in the Yuefu collections (乐府诗集) under the “Horizontal Flute Songs”. Yuefu (“Music Bureau”) songs were folk songs collected by Han officials; this one reflects the endless border wars of the period.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

冢 zhǒng；累累 léi léi（一个连一个的样子，不读 lěi）；窦 dòu；雉 zhì；舂 chōng（不是“春”）；羹 gēng；饴 yí（同“贻”，送给）；阿谁 ā shuí。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行的汉乐府文本（《乐府诗集》卷二十五），全诗十六句，网站按一联一行排版。“遥看是君家”，一本作“遥望是君家”；“出门东向看”，一本作“出门东向望”。“不知饴阿谁”，一本作“不知贻阿谁”（饴通“贻”）。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

十五从军征，八十始得归。

shí wǔ cóng jūn zhēng, bā shí shǐ dé guī

At fifteen I went off to war; at eighty I came home at last.

道逢乡里人：“家中有阿谁？”

dào féng xiāng lǐ rén, jiā zhōng yǒu ā shuí

I met a villager on the road: “Who is left of my family?”

“遥看是君家，松柏冢累累。”

yáo kàn shì jūn jiā, sōng bǎi zhǒng léi léi

“Your house is over there — among the pines, a row of graves.”

兔从狗窦入，雉从梁上飞。

tù cóng gǒu dòu rù, zhì cóng liáng shàng fēi

Rabbits slip in through the dog hole; pheasants flap down from the beams.

中庭生旅谷，井上生旅葵。

zhōng tíng shēng lǚ gǔ, jǐng shàng shēng lǚ kuí

Wild grain has seeded the yard, wild mallow the edge of the well.

舂谷持作饭，采葵持作羹。

chōng gǔ chí zuò fàn, cǎi kuí chí zuò gēng

I husk the grain to cook some rice, pick the mallow for a soup.

羹饭一时熟，不知饴阿谁。

gēng fàn yī shí shú, bù zhī yí ā shuí

Soon the meal is ready — but who is there to share it?

出门东向看，泪落沾我衣。

chū mén dōng xiàng kàn, lèi luò zhān wǒ yī

I walk out of the gate and look east, and tears run down onto my clothes.
