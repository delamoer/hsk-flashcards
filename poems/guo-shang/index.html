Nine Songs: For Those Who Died for the State

导读About this poem

A battlefield hymn from 2,300 years ago, sung to honour soldiers of the state of Chu who died fighting. It moves from the chaos of chariots and arrows to the silent bodies on the plain — and ends by calling them heroes even among ghosts.

01
操吴戈兮被犀甲，车错毂兮短兵接。

直译LiteralHolding Wu spears, wearing rhino armour; chariot hubs clash, short weapons meet.

意译MeaningSpears from Wu in hand, rhino-hide on our backs — wheel grinds on wheel, and blade meets blade.

02
旌蔽日兮敌若云，矢交坠兮士争先。

直译LiteralBanners hide the sun, the enemy like clouds; arrows cross and fall, soldiers vie to be first.

意译MeaningBanners blot out the sun, the enemy thick as clouds; arrows rain down, and our men charge on.

03
凌余阵兮躐余行，左骖殪兮右刃伤。

直译LiteralThey overrun our line, trample our ranks; the left horse falls dead, the right is cut by blades.

意译MeaningThey break through our lines and tread down our ranks; one horse lies dead, the other bleeds.

04
霾两轮兮絷四马，援玉枹兮击鸣鼓。

直译LiteralTwo wheels buried, four horses tangled; he grasps the jade drumstick and beats the sounding drum.

意译MeaningThe wheels sink deep, the horses are caught fast — he seizes the drumstick and beats the drum.

05
天时怼兮威灵怒，严杀尽兮弃原野。

直译LiteralHeaven is bitter, the mighty spirits rage; cruelly slain to the last, they are left on the plain.

意译MeaningHeaven turns against them, the gods are angry; all are cut down and left on the open field.

06
出不入兮往不反，平原忽兮路超远。

直译LiteralThey went out and did not come in, went and did not return; the plain is vast, the road endlessly far.

意译MeaningThey marched out and never came home; the plain stretches empty, the road home is far away.

07
带长剑兮挟秦弓，首身离兮心不惩。

直译LiteralWearing long swords, carrying Qin bows; head and body parted, their hearts do not regret.

意译MeaningLong swords at their sides, Qin bows in their arms — heads severed, but their hearts unbroken.

08
诚既勇兮又以武，终刚强兮不可凌。

直译LiteralTruly brave, and skilled in arms as well; strong to the end, they could not be humbled.

意译MeaningBrave they were, and skilled in war; strong to the very end, no one could shame them.

09
身既死兮神以灵，魂魄毅兮为鬼雄。

直译LiteralTheir bodies dead, their spirits live on; their souls are resolute, heroes among ghosts.

意译MeaningTheir bodies are gone, but their spirits live — even among the dead, they are heroes.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

“国殇”指为国家战死的人。《九歌》本是楚国祭神的歌，这一首祭的是阵亡将士。前半写战斗：拿着吴国的戈、穿着犀牛皮的甲，战车车轴相撞，短刀相接；旗子遮住太阳，敌人多得像云，箭像雨一样落下，战士们却争着向前。敌人冲破阵地，战马倒下，战车陷进泥里，主帅还拿起鼓槌击鼓。接着画面突然安静：将士全部战死，尸体留在原野上——“出不入兮往不反”，出征的人再也回不来了。后半是赞歌：他们身首分离，心却不后悔；真是勇敢又有武艺，到死刚强、不可欺负。最后两句是全诗的高潮：身体虽死，精神永存，魂魄也是鬼中的英雄。全诗每句中间用“兮”，节奏急促有力，像战鼓。这首诗是中国最早歌颂为国牺牲的作品之一。

In English

国殇 means those who died for their country. The Nine Songs were hymns sung at religious rites in the state of Chu, and this one honours fallen soldiers. The first half is the battle itself, told at speed: soldiers grip spears from Wu and wear rhinoceros-hide armour; chariot hubs crash together and men fight hand to hand; banners blot out the sun, enemies swarm like clouds, arrows fall like rain — and still the soldiers race forward. The line breaks, horses fall, wheels sink into the mud, and the commander keeps beating the war drum. Then the noise suddenly stops. Everyone is dead, left lying on the open plain: “they went out and never came in, set off and never returned.” The second half turns into praise. Their heads are cut from their bodies, but their hearts feel no regret; they were brave and skilled, strong to the very end. The closing lines lift the poem: the body has died, but the spirit lives on — even among ghosts, these men are heroes. The 兮 in the middle of every line gives a hard, pounding rhythm, like drumbeats. It is one of the earliest Chinese poems to honour people who died for their country.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Qu Yuan (c. 340–278 BC) was a nobleman and minister of the state of Chu who was exiled after his advice was rejected; he is traditionally said to have drowned himself in the Miluo River, which the Dragon Boat Festival remembers. The Nine Songs are traditionally credited to him, reworking Chu’s ritual songs, though some scholars think he only edited them.

版本说明 About the text

采用通行的教材文本（王逸《楚辞章句》系统）。异文：“魂魄毅兮为鬼雄”，王逸本作“子魂魄兮为鬼雄”，通行教材用“魂魄毅兮”；“天时怼兮”一作“天时坠兮”。全诗十八句，网站一行一联（共九行）。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

操吴戈兮被犀甲，车错毂兮短兵接。

cāo wú gē xī pī xī jiǎ, chē cuò gǔ xī duǎn bīng jiē

Spears from Wu in hand, rhino-hide on our backs — wheel grinds on wheel, and blade meets blade.

旌蔽日兮敌若云，矢交坠兮士争先。

jīng bì rì xī dí ruò yún, shǐ jiāo zhuì xī shì zhēng xiān

Banners blot out the sun, the enemy thick as clouds; arrows rain down, and our men charge on.

凌余阵兮躐余行，左骖殪兮右刃伤。

líng yú zhèn xī liè yú háng, zuǒ cān yì xī yòu rèn shāng

They break through our lines and tread down our ranks; one horse lies dead, the other bleeds.

霾两轮兮絷四马，援玉枹兮击鸣鼓。

mái liǎng lún xī zhí sì mǎ, yuán yù fú xī jī míng gǔ

The wheels sink deep, the horses are caught fast — he seizes the drumstick and beats the drum.

天时怼兮威灵怒，严杀尽兮弃原野。

tiān shí duì xī wēi líng nù, yán shā jìn xī qì yuán yě

Heaven turns against them, the gods are angry; all are cut down and left on the open field.

出不入兮往不反，平原忽兮路超远。

chū bù rù xī wǎng bù fǎn, píng yuán hū xī lù chāo yuǎn

They marched out and never came home; the plain stretches empty, the road home is far away.

带长剑兮挟秦弓，首身离兮心不惩。

dài cháng jiàn xī xié qín gōng, shǒu shēn lí xī xīn bù chéng

Long swords at their sides, Qin bows in their arms — heads severed, but their hearts unbroken.

诚既勇兮又以武，终刚强兮不可凌。

chéng jì yǒng xī yòu yǐ wǔ, zhōng gāng qiáng xī bù kě líng

Brave they were, and skilled in war; strong to the very end, no one could shame them.

身既死兮神以灵，魂魄毅兮为鬼雄。

shēn jì sǐ xī shén yǐ líng, hún pò yì xī wéi guǐ xióng

Their bodies are gone, but their spirits live — even among the dead, they are heroes.
