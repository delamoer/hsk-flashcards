导读About this poem

Bowstrings sing in a hard wind as a general rides out hunting. Wang Wei, best known for quiet landscapes, here writes something fast, loud and full of energy — and ends on a wide, calm sky.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗写一位将军在渭城郊外打猎。一开头先写声音：风很猛，角弓的弦声嗡嗡作响，然后才说出“将军猎渭城”，先声夺人。第二联是名句：秋冬草枯了，老鹰的眼睛看猎物更快更准；雪化了，马跑起来蹄子格外轻快。“疾”和“轻”两个字写出了打猎的速度和兴奋。第三联写将军满载而归，一下子经过新丰，又回到细柳营——“细柳营”是汉代名将周亚夫的军营，暗中称赞这位将军治军严整。最后回头看刚才射雕的地方，只见千里暮云平平地铺在天边。从热闹紧张到开阔平静，节奏一张一弛，很有画面感。“射雕”也暗用了北齐名将“射雕手”的典故，夸将军箭术高超。

In English

A general goes hunting outside Weicheng, near Chang’an. The poem opens with sound before sight: the wind is fierce and the horn-tipped bows twang — only then are we told who it is: “the general hunts at Weicheng.” The second couplet is the most admired: the grass has withered, so the hawk’s eye is quick to spot its prey; the snow has gone, so the horses’ hooves run light. Two single words, 疾 (swift) and 轻 (light), carry all the speed and excitement of the chase. In the third couplet the hunt is over in a flash: they sweep past Xinfeng and are back at Xiliu Camp. Xiliu (“Slender Willow”) was the camp of the Han general Zhou Yafu, famous for strict discipline, so the name quietly praises this general too. Finally he looks back at the place where he shot the eagles, and sees only evening clouds lying flat across a thousand li of sky. After all the noise and speed, the poem ends in wide, still space — tension and release, like a camera pulling back. “Shooting eagles” also recalls a famous Northern Qi general nicknamed the “eagle-shooter”, a compliment to the general’s archery.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

This is generally thought to be an early work by Wang Wei (701–761), written in his youth or early career; the general is not named. It shows a bold, energetic side of a poet better known for calm landscapes.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

风劲角弓鸣，

fēng jìng jiǎo gōng míng

In a hard wind the horn bows sing —

将军猎渭城。

jiāng jūn liè wèi chéng

the general is hunting outside Weicheng.

草枯鹰眼疾，

cǎo kū yīng yǎn jí

The grass is dry, and the hawk’s eye is quick;

雪尽马蹄轻。

xuě jìn mǎ tí qīng

the snow has melted, and the hooves fly light.

忽过新丰市，

hū guò xīn fēng shì

In a flash they are past the market at Xinfeng,

还归细柳营。

huán guī xì liǔ yíng

and back again at Slender Willow Camp.

回看射雕处，

huí kàn shè diāo chù

He turns to look where the eagles fell:

千里暮云平。

qiān lǐ mù yún píng

evening clouds lie level for a thousand miles.
