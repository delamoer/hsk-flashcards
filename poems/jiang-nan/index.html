导读About this poem

A 2,000-year-old song sung while picking lotus: green leaves cover the water and fish dart east, west, south and north. Simple enough for a first lesson — Chinese children learn it in their first year of school.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这是一首采莲时唱的民歌。江南水多，夏天湖里长满了莲。第一句说“可以采莲了”，第二句写莲叶又多又圆，一片连着一片，“田田”两个字就把满湖碧绿的样子写出来了。后面五句都在写鱼：鱼在莲叶中间游来游去，一会儿在东，一会儿在西，一会儿在南，一会儿在北。这几句看起来很简单，甚至有点重复，可是正是这种重复，让人看到鱼儿活泼自由的样子，也感受到采莲人轻松快乐的心情。有人认为后四句是大家一起唱的“和声”，一人领唱，众人应和。这首诗用字简单，还能顺便学会“东、西、南、北”，所以是中国孩子最早学的古诗之一。

In English

This is a folk song people sang while picking lotus. The region south of the Yangtze River is full of lakes and waterways, and in summer the water is covered with lotus plants. The first line simply says it is time to pick lotus. The second shows the leaves — round, green and crowded together — with the vivid word 田田, which paints a whole lake of green. The remaining five lines are all about fish: they play among the leaves, now to the east, now to the west, now to the south, now to the north. The lines look almost too simple, even repetitive, but that repetition is the point: you can see the fish darting freely, and feel how relaxed and happy the lotus pickers are. Some scholars think the last four lines were a chorus — one person sang the first lines, and everyone joined in. With easy characters and the four directions 东、西、南、北 built in, it is one of the very first poems Chinese children learn at school.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Yuefu (乐府) was the Music Bureau of the Han court, greatly expanded by Emperor Wu (r. 141–87 BC), which collected folk songs from around the country. This anonymous song was preserved in Guo Maoqian’s Collection of Yuefu Poems (12th century).

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

鱼戏莲叶间的“间”读 jiān（中间）。莲叶何田田的“何”表示“多么”。无其他特殊读音。

版本说明 About the text

采用统编教材一年级上册的通行文本（出自宋郭茂倩《乐府诗集·相和歌辞》），全诗七句。各版本标点略有不同，此处按通行标点。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

江南可采莲，

jiāng nán kě cǎi lián

Down south by the river, it’s time to pick lotus —

莲叶何田田，

lián yè hé tián tián

lotus leaves spread everywhere, round and green.

鱼戏莲叶间。

yú xì lián yè jiān

Fish play among the leaves:

鱼戏莲叶东，

yú xì lián yè dōng

they play to the east,

鱼戏莲叶西，

yú xì lián yè xī

they play to the west,

鱼戏莲叶南，

yú xì lián yè nán

they play to the south,

鱼戏莲叶北。

yú xì lián yè běi

they play to the north.
