Tíng Zhōng Yǒu Qí Shù
A Rare Tree in the Courtyard

导读About this poem

A woman breaks off a blossoming branch for her absent husband, holds it until her sleeves smell of flowers — and knows she can never send it. Eight short lines about how long he has been gone.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗写一位女子思念远行的丈夫。院子里有一棵美丽的树，绿叶茂盛，开满了花。她攀着树枝折下一枝花，想送给心里想的那个人。花香充满了她的怀抱和衣袖，可是路太远，没办法送到。最后两句她对自己说：这枝花有什么珍贵的呢？只是因为分别太久了，看到花开，才感到时间过去了这么久。全诗从树写到花，从花写到香，再从香写到人，一层一层推进，可是一直没有直接说“我想你”。花开了又一年，说明丈夫走了很久；花香满袖却送不出去，说明思念没有出口。最后一句“但感别经时”把一切感情都轻轻收住，含蓄而深沉，这就是《古诗十九首》“温柔敦厚”的风格。

In English

A woman misses her husband, who is far away. In the courtyard stands a lovely tree, its leaves lush and its branches full of blossom. She pulls down a branch and breaks off a spray of flowers, meaning to send it to the one she thinks of. The scent fills her arms and sleeves — but the road is too long, and there is no way to get it to him. In the last two lines she talks herself down: what is so precious about a flower? It is only that seeing it bloom makes her feel how long they have been apart. The poem moves step by step — tree, flowers, scent, person — and never once says “I miss you”. The blossoms tell us another year has passed; the scent clinging to her sleeves is love with nowhere to go. The quiet last line gathers all that feeling and holds it back. This gentle, restrained style is exactly what readers have loved about the Nineteen Old Poems for almost two thousand years.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

This is the ninth of the Nineteen Old Poems (古诗十九首), written by unknown poets of the late Eastern Han (around the 2nd century AD). Like many poems in the set, it speaks in the voice of a wife left behind.

庭中有奇树，绿叶发华滋。

Its scent fills my arms and sleeves, but the road is too long to send it.

cǐ wù hé zú guì, dàn gǎn bié jīng shí

What is a flower worth? It only shows me how long you have been gone.
