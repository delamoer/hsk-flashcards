导读About this poem

A rainy summer night, frogs croaking everywhere, and a friend who promised to come still hasn’t shown up. The poet sits alone, tapping chess pieces — and we feel his waiting without being told.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗写一个雨夜等朋友的小场景。“黄梅时节”是江南初夏的梅雨季，天天下雨，所以“家家雨”；池塘边青草茂盛，到处都是青蛙在叫，所以“处处蛙”。前两句写外面热闹的雨声、蛙声，正好反衬屋里的安静。第三句点题：约好的客人没来，已经过了半夜。最后一句最妙：诗人没说自己着急、失望，只写他一个人无聊地敲着棋子，把灯芯烧结成的“灯花”都震落了。一个小动作，写出等了很久、有点焦急又有点无奈的心情。这种不直接说感情、用动作和景物来表达的方法，叫“借景抒情”“以动写情”。

In English

This short poem captures a small moment: waiting on a rainy night for a friend who doesn’t come. The “plum-yellow season” is early summer south of the Yangtze, when it rains day after day — so “rain on every house”. The ponds are thick with grass and full of croaking frogs. These first two lines are noisy with rain and frogs, and that noise makes the quiet inside the room feel even quieter. The third line tells us the situation: a guest promised to come, and now it is past midnight. The last line is the famous one. The poet never says he is impatient or disappointed. He just sits there idly tapping a chess piece on the board — tapping so long that the burnt “flower” on the lamp wick falls off. One small gesture shows how long he has waited and how he feels: a little restless, a little resigned. Letting an action carry the emotion, instead of naming it, is something Chinese poets love to do.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Zhao Shixiu (1170–1219) was a Southern Song poet from Yongjia (today’s Wenzhou, Zhejiang), one of the “Four Spirits of Yongjia” who wrote in a plain, finely observed style. This quatrain is his best-known poem.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

过夜半：过 guò。灯花：灯芯燃烧后结成的花状小块，不是真的花。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

黄梅时节家家雨，

huáng méi shí jié jiā jiā yǔ

Plum-rain season: rain on every roof,

青草池塘处处蛙。

qīng cǎo chí táng chù chù wā

frogs croaking in every grassy pond.

有约不来过夜半，

yǒu yuē bù lái guò yè bàn

He promised to come — midnight has passed;

闲敲棋子落灯花。

xián qiāo qí zǐ luò dēng huā

idly I tap a chess piece, and the lamp’s charred wick drops away.
