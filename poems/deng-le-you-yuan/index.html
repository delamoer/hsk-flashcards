导读About this poem

Feeling restless at dusk, the poet drives up to a hill above Chang’an and sees a glorious sunset. Its last line — “it’s just that dusk is near” — is one of the most quoted sighs in Chinese.

中文解析

这首诗只有二十个字。傍晚的时候，诗人心里不舒服，就驾着车登上长安城东南的乐游原散心。乐游原地势高，能看到整个长安城。他看到的是一片美丽的夕阳：“夕阳无限好”，美得没法形容；可是“只是近黄昏”——天快黑了，这美景马上就要消失。一个“只是”，把赞叹变成了叹息。诗人为什么不高兴，诗里没说；后人读出了很多意思：感叹时光流逝、人到晚年，也有人从中读出对晚唐国运的担忧。“夕阳无限好，只是近黄昏”今天仍常常被人引用，用来形容美好却短暂的事物，也有人反过来用它鼓励老年人珍惜晚年的美好。

In English

Only twenty characters long, this poem begins with a mood: toward evening the poet feels uneasy, so he drives his carriage up to the Leyou Plateau, the highest ground in Chang’an, where you could look out over the whole capital. What he sees is a magnificent sunset. “The setting sun is endlessly beautiful” — and then the turn: “it’s just that dusk is close.” With the single word 只是 (“only, it’s just that”) admiration becomes a sigh. The beauty is real, and it is about to disappear. The poem never explains why he was unhappy, and readers have found many meanings: the passing of time, growing old, or worry about the fading Tang dynasty itself. The last two lines are still quoted all the time, for anything beautiful but short-lived. Some people even turn them around and use them to encourage the elderly to enjoy the golden light of later life.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Li Shangyin (c. 813–858) was one of the great poets of the late Tang, famous for dense, beautiful and often mysterious verse. Caught between two rival court factions, he spent most of his life in minor posts. The Leyou Plateau, in the south-east of Chang’an, was a popular spot for outings with a view over the city.

向晚意不适，

xiàng wǎn yì bù shì

As evening comes, I feel uneasy,

驱车登古原。

qū chē dēng gǔ yuán

so I drive up to the old plateau.

夕阳无限好，

xī yáng wú xiàn hǎo

The setting sun is beautiful beyond words —

只是近黄昏。

zhǐ shì jìn huáng hūn

it’s just that dusk is drawing near.
