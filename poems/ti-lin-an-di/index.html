导读About this poem

The north has been lost to invaders, but in the new capital the parties never stop. Scribbled on an inn wall, this angry little poem says the rulers are so drunk on pleasure they’ve mistaken Hangzhou for their lost home.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

北宋的都城汴州（今河南开封）在1127年被金兵攻破，皇帝被俘，宋朝南迁，后来把临安（今浙江杭州）当作都城。本来应该想着收复北方，可是很多权贵在西湖边过起了享乐的日子。这首诗是诗人写在临安一家旅店墙上的。第一句写杭州的繁华：青山外还有青山，楼阁外还有楼阁，一层又一层。第二句突然发问：西湖边的歌舞什么时候才能停下来？第三句说温暖的春风把游人吹得醉醺醺的——“暖风”既是真的风，也指享乐的风气；“游人”指那些只顾游玩的达官贵人。最后一句最辛辣：他们简直把杭州当成了汴州，忘了故都还在敌人手里。这种不直接骂、却句句带刺的写法叫“讽刺”。诗只有二十八个字，却把对国家的忧虑和对权贵的愤怒都写了出来。

In English

In 1127 the Northern Song capital, Bianzhou (today’s Kaifeng), fell to the Jin; two emperors were taken prisoner, and the court fled south and settled at Lin’an (today’s Hangzhou). It should have been a time for planning to win back the north, but many officials and nobles simply settled into a life of pleasure around West Lake. This poem was written on the wall of an inn in Lin’an. The first line paints Hangzhou’s wealth: green hills beyond green hills, towers beyond towers. The second line suddenly asks: when will the singing and dancing on West Lake ever stop? The third line says the warm breeze makes the “travellers” drunk — the breeze is real spring air, but also the warm wind of luxury, and the “travellers” are the officials who think only of enjoying themselves. The last line is the sharpest: they have simply taken Hangzhou for Bianzhou, forgetting that their real capital is in enemy hands. Saying something bitter without shouting, so that every line has a sting, is satire. In just twenty-eight characters the poem carries both worry for the country and anger at its rulers.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Lin Sheng was a scholar of the Southern Song; little is known about his life beyond that he is thought to have lived in the later twelfth century. This poem, traditionally said to have been written on the wall of an inn in the capital, is the only reason his name is remembered.
