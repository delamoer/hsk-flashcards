汉广

Hàn Guǎng
The Han River Is Wide
佚名（《诗经·周南》）Anonymous (Book of Songs, Airs of Zhounan)先秦 Pre-Qin
体裁Form
四言诗 · 国风

导读About this poem

A woodcutter loves a girl he can never have, and he knows it: she is as far away as the other side of the wide Han River. He still dreams of feeding her horses on her wedding day. A gentle song about loving without hope.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

唱歌的是一个在汉水边砍柴的年轻人，他爱上了一位在江边游玩的姑娘，可是他知道自己追不到她。他用了一连串比喻：南边有高大的树，却不能在树下休息；汉水那么宽，游不过去；长江那么长，坐木筏也到不了对岸。树、水、江，都在说“可望而不可即”——看得见，却到不了。第二、三章他一边砍柴一边幻想：如果那位姑娘出嫁，我愿意为她喂马！明明得不到，却还愿意为她做点事，这份感情克制又温柔。每章结尾重复“汉之广矣……江之永矣……”，像一遍遍叹气，越唱越惆怅。这是中国诗歌里最早写“单相思”的作品之一。

In English

The singer is a young woodcutter on the banks of the Han River. He has fallen for a girl he sees there, and he knows she is out of reach. He says so through a string of images: in the south there are tall trees, but you can’t rest in their shade; the Han is too wide to swim; the Yangtze is too long to cross by raft. Tree, river and river again all say the same thing — kě wàng ér bù kě jí, “you can see it, but you can’t reach it.” In verses 2 and 3 he cuts firewood and daydreams: if that girl is ever married, let me be the one to feed her horses. He can’t have her, but he would still like to do something for her — a love that is restrained and tender at the same time. Every verse ends with the same refrain about the wide Han and the long Yangtze, like a sigh repeated again and again, and the sadness gathers with each repetition. It is one of the earliest Chinese poems about unrequited love.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

The Book of Songs gathers 305 songs from about the 11th to the 6th century BC. This is from the Airs of Zhounan (周南), from the region of the Han River and the middle Yangtze. The author is unknown; later legends made the “wandering girl” a river goddess.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

乔木 qiáo mù；思：句末语气词，读 sī；翘翘 qiáo qiáo（高高的样子）；刈 yì；楚 chǔ（荆条）；秣 mò；蒌 lóu（蒌蒿）；驹 jū。“刈”“秣”“蒌”机器朗读易错，已在“朗读用字”列替换同音字。

版本说明 About the text

“不可休思”采用今通行本；《毛诗》传本作“不可休息”，历代学者多认为“息”是“思”之误（与下文“求思”“泳思”“方思”一致）。全诗三章，每章八句；网站按两句为一行排版。
