静女

Jìng Nǚ
The Quiet Girl
佚名（《诗经·邶风》）Anonymous (Book of Songs, Airs of Bei)先秦 Pre-Qin

导读About this poem

A young man waits by the city wall for a girl who is playfully hiding from him, then treasures the little gifts she gives — even a blade of grass. A light, funny, tender love scene from about 3,000 years ago.

第一章Stanza 1
01
静女其姝，俟我于城隅。

直译LiteralThe quiet girl is lovely; she waits for me at the corner of the city wall.

意译MeaningMy quiet girl, so lovely, said she’d wait for me where the city wall turns.

02
爱而不见，搔首踟蹰。

直译LiteralShe hides and is not seen; I scratch my head and pace about.

意译MeaningBut she’s hiding and won’t show herself — I scratch my head and pace up and down.

第二章Stanza 2
03
静女其娈，贻我彤管。

直译LiteralThe quiet girl is pretty; she gives me a red tube.

意译MeaningMy quiet girl, so pretty, gave me a little red reed.

04
彤管有炜，说怿女美。

直译LiteralThe red tube is bright; I delight in your beauty.

意译MeaningHow the red reed glows! I love how beautiful you are, little reed.

第三章Stanza 3
05
自牧归荑，洵美且异。

直译LiteralFrom the pastures she gives me a grass shoot, truly lovely and unusual.

意译MeaningFrom the pastures she brought me a shoot of new grass — truly lovely, like no other.

06
匪女之为美，美人之贻。

直译LiteralIt is not you that are beautiful; it is that the beauty gave you.

意译MeaningLittle grass, it isn’t you that’s so beautiful — it’s that she gave you to me.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这是一首写约会的小诗，读起来很轻松。第一章：一位文静美丽的姑娘约“我”在城墙角落见面，可她故意躲起来不出现，急得“我”抓头挠耳、走来走去。“搔首踟蹰”四个字把小伙子着急的样子写活了。第二章：姑娘送给他一支红色的“彤管”，他觉得这东西闪闪发亮，越看越喜欢。第三章：姑娘又从郊外带回一根茅草芽送他，他说这草真美——其实不是草美，而是因为它是心上人送的。最后两句点出了全诗的意思：礼物本身不重要，重要的是送礼物的人。今天我们说“礼轻情意重”，就是这个道理。诗里没有一句直接说“爱”，爱却藏在每个小动作里。

In English

This is a little date poem, and it is fun to read. In verse 1 a quiet, lovely girl has arranged to meet the speaker at a corner of the city wall — but she hides on purpose, and he scratches his head and paces back and forth. The four characters 搔首踟蹰 (“scratching his head, pacing”) bring his nervous waiting to life. In verse 2 she gives him a red tóngguǎn, a small red tube (scholars still argue whether it was a flute, a writing brush or a red shoot of grass); he finds it bright and beautiful. In verse 3 she brings him a tender blade of grass from the pastures, and he says it is unusually lovely. Then the last two lines explain the joke: it’s not that the grass is beautiful — it’s beautiful because she gave it to him. Chinese still has a saying for this, 礼轻情意重: “the gift is light, but the feeling is heavy.” The poem never uses the word “love”, yet love is in every small gesture.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

The Book of Songs collects 305 songs from about the 11th to the 6th century BC. This one belongs to the Airs of Bei (邶), a small area later absorbed into the state of Wey in today’s northern Henan. The author is unknown.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

姝 shū；俟 sì；隅 yú；踟蹰 chí chú；娈 luán；贻 yí；彤 tóng；炜 wěi；说怿 yuè yì（“说”通“悦”）；女 rǔ（通“汝”，你，这里指彤管和荑草）；荑 tí；洵 xún。爱：通“薆”，隐藏，仍读 ài。“自牧归荑”的“归”通“馈”（赠送），多数注本读 kuì，也有直接读 guī 的；网站暂采用 kuì。“说”“女”“归”机器朗读易错，已在“朗读用字”列替换同音字。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行《毛诗》文本，全诗三章，每章四句；网站按句读两句为一行排版。“彤管”具体指什么有争议（红色管状乐器、红管笔、红色草茎），解析里不作定论。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

静女其姝，俟我于城隅。

jìng nǚ qí shū, sì wǒ yú chéng yú

My quiet girl, so lovely, said she’d wait for me where the city wall turns.

爱而不见，搔首踟蹰。

ài ér bù jiàn, sāo shǒu chí chú

But she’s hiding and won’t show herself — I scratch my head and pace up and down.

静女其娈，贻我彤管。

jìng nǚ qí luán, yí wǒ tóng guǎn

My quiet girl, so pretty, gave me a little red reed.

彤管有炜，说怿女美。

tóng guǎn yǒu wěi, yuè yì rǔ měi

How the red reed glows! I love how beautiful you are, little reed.

自牧归荑，洵美且异。

zì mù kuì tí, xún měi qiě yì

From the pastures she brought me a shoot of new grass — truly lovely, like no other.

匪女之为美，美人之贻。

fěi rǔ zhī wéi měi, měi rén zhī yí

Little grass, it isn’t you that’s so beautiful — it’s that she gave you to me.
