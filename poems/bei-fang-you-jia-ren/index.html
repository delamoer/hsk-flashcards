导读About this poem

A court musician sang this to Emperor Wu of Han about a woman so beautiful that one glance could topple a city — and it worked: the emperor asked to meet her. It gave Chinese its standard phrase for stunning beauty, 倾国倾城.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗是汉武帝时宫廷音乐家李延年唱的。他在武帝面前边舞边唱：北方有一位美人，美得世上没人能比，独自站在那里。她看一眼，全城的人都为她倾倒；再看一眼，整个国家都为她倾倒。难道不知道这样会让城和国都倒下吗？可是这样的美人，错过了就再也遇不到了！诗里用了夸张的手法：一个眼神就能“倾城”“倾国”，可见她有多美。最后一句有点像劝告：美人难得，要珍惜。汉武帝听了，叹息说世上哪有这样的人？于是有人告诉他，李延年的妹妹就是这样的美人。武帝召见了她，她后来就是很受宠爱的李夫人。成语“倾国倾城”就出自这首诗，今天仍用来形容女子非常美丽。

In English

Li Yannian, a musician at the court of Emperor Wu, danced and sang this in front of the emperor. In the north, he sings, there is a beauty unmatched in the world, standing apart from everyone. One glance from her and a city falls; a second glance and a whole country falls. Don’t you know what such a beauty can do to cities and kingdoms? And yet — a beauty like her, once missed, will never be found again! The poem works by exaggeration: a single look can topple a city, so imagine how lovely she must be. The last line sounds almost like advice: someone this rare is worth any risk. The emperor sighed and asked whether such a person really existed. He was told that Li Yannian’s own younger sister was that beauty; he summoned her, and she became his beloved Lady Li. The phrase 倾国倾城, “topple a country, topple a city”, comes from this song and is still the standard Chinese way to describe a woman of breathtaking beauty.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Li Yannian (died c. 90 BC) was a musician at the court of Emperor Wu of Han and head of the Music Bureau. The song and its story are recorded in Ban Gu’s History of the Han (Hanshu, 1st century AD).

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

北方有佳人，绝世而独立。

běi fāng yǒu jiā rén, jué shì ér dú lì

In the north there lives a beauty, peerless, standing apart from the world.

一顾倾人城，再顾倾人国。

yī gù qīng rén chéng, zài gù qīng rén guó

One look from her and a city falls; a second look, and a kingdom.

宁不知倾城与倾国？佳人难再得。

nìng bù zhī qīng chéng yǔ qīng guó, jiā rén nán zài dé

Who doesn’t know what such beauty can bring down? But a beauty like her will not come twice.
