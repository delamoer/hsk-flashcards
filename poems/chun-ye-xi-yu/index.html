导读About this poem

A spring rain arrives at night, exactly when the crops need it, falling so softly no one hears it. Du Fu stays up to watch — and pictures the whole city full of wet, heavy blossoms by morning.

中文解析

这首诗写成都春天的一场夜雨。开头就把雨当成人来写：好雨“知”道时节，春天一到就来了——这种把东西当人写的手法叫“拟人”。三、四句是全诗最有名的两句：雨跟着风，悄悄在夜里落下，滋润万物却没有一点声音。今天人们常用“润物细无声”比喻老师和父母默默的付出。五、六句写诗人看到的夜景：田野小路和天上的云都是一片黑，只有江上渔船的一点灯火亮着，一黑一亮，对比很鲜明。最后两句是想象：明天早上去看，那湿漉漉的红色地方，一定是成都城里开满的花，被雨水压得沉甸甸的。题目里的“喜”字全诗一次也没出现，可每一句都透着喜悦。

In English

Du Fu wrote this in Chengdu, where he had finally found a few peaceful years. The poem treats the rain like a thoughtful person: a “good rain” that knows the season and comes just when spring begins. Giving human qualities to things like this is called personification. Lines three and four are the most quoted: the rain slips in with the wind under cover of night and nourishes everything without a sound. Today Chinese people use 润物细无声 for quiet, patient care — a teacher’s or a parent’s. Lines five and six describe what the poet sees from his door: the country path and the clouds are all black, and only a single lamp on a riverboat glows — a sharp contrast of dark and light. The ending jumps ahead to the next morning: where things look red and wet, the flowers of Chengdu will be heavy with rain. The word 喜 (“glad”) is in the title but never in the poem; the joy is in every image instead.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Du Fu (712–770) is often called the “Sage of Poetry”. After years of war and wandering he settled in a thatched cottage outside Chengdu around 760, and this poem is traditionally dated to the spring of 761.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

好雨知时节，

hǎo yǔ zhī shí jié

A good rain knows its moment:

当春乃发生。

dāng chūn nǎi fā shēng

the moment spring arrives, it comes.

随风潜入夜，

suí fēng qián rù yè

It steals in with the wind after dark,

润物细无声。

rùn wù xì wú shēng

soaking all things, fine and soundless.

野径云俱黑，

yě jìng yún jù hēi

Field paths and clouds are one black mass;

江船火独明。

jiāng chuán huǒ dú míng

one lamp on a riverboat glows alone.

晓看红湿处，

xiǎo kàn hóng shī chù

At dawn, look where things are red and wet —

花重锦官城。

huā zhòng jǐn guān chéng

all Chengdu will be heavy with flowers.
