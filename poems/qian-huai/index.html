导读About this poem

Looking back on ten wild years in Yangzhou, Du Mu feels they passed like one dream and left him nothing but a bad reputation. A wry, self-mocking poem of regret.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这是杜牧回忆扬州生活的诗。“遣怀”就是抒发、排遣心中的感慨。第一句说自己当年仕途不得志，带着酒在江湖上四处漂泊。第二句用了两个典故：“楚腰”指楚灵王喜欢细腰的人，“掌中轻”说汉代赵飞燕身轻能在手掌上跳舞，这里都是形容扬州歌女的美丽。后两句是全诗的重点：十年的扬州生活，回头一看就像一场梦醒，最后只在青楼留下一个“薄幸”——薄情的名声。“赢得”本来是好词，这里却带着自嘲和苦笑。表面上写风流往事，深处是对虚度年华、怀才不遇的悔恨。“十年一觉扬州梦”后来成了名句，常用来形容回想往事恍如一梦。

In English

In this poem Du Mu looks back on his years in Yangzhou. The title means “to let out what is in one’s heart”. Line one: down on his luck in his career, he wandered from place to place carrying his wine. Line two uses two old allusions to describe the singing girls of Yangzhou: “Chu waists” recalls a king of Chu who loved slender waists, and “light in the palm” recalls Zhao Feiyan, a Han empress said to be so light she could dance on someone’s hand. Then come the lines everyone remembers. Ten years in Yangzhou now feel like waking from a single dream, and all he has “won” is a name for being fickle in the singing houses. The verb 赢得 (“to win, to gain”) normally sounds positive; here it is bitter and self-mocking. On the surface the poem is about romantic escapades, but underneath lies regret: years wasted, talent unused. The line 十年一觉扬州梦 became a set phrase for looking back on the past and finding it all like a dream.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Du Mu (803–c. 852) worked in Yangzhou in his early thirties, on the staff of the military governor Niu Sengru, and had a reputation as a romantic. The poem was probably written some years later, when he looked back on that time with mixed feelings.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

落魄：现代词典读 luò pò，也有注本按“落拓”读 luò tuò，网站暂读 luò pò。载 zài；纤 xiān；一觉 jué（睡醒，不读 jiào）；薄幸 bó xìng。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行本。“落魄江湖载酒行”一作“落拓江湖载酒行”（一作“江南”），“十年一觉扬州梦”一作“三年一觉扬州梦”，“薄幸”一作“薄倖”。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

落魄江湖载酒行，

luò pò jiāng hú zài jiǔ xíng

Down on my luck, I drifted the rivers and lakes with my wine;

楚腰纤细掌中轻。

chǔ yāo xiān xì zhǎng zhōng qīng

girls with willow waists, light enough to dance on a palm.

十年一觉扬州梦，

shí nián yī jué yáng zhōu mèng

Ten years in Yangzhou, and I wake as if from one dream —

赢得青楼薄幸名。

yíng dé qīng lóu bó xìng míng

all I have gained is a name in the singing houses as a heartless lover.
