To the Tune of “Yi Qin E” (The Flute Sobs)

导读About this poem

A flute sobs, a woman wakes from a dream under the moon, willows recall partings — and then the view opens onto ancient roads and the tombs of Han emperors in the west wind and the last light of day. Short, sad, and enormous.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

上阕写一位秦地女子的离愁：呜咽的箫声把她从梦中惊醒，窗外是秦楼上的月亮。看着这月亮，她想起每年柳树发绿的时候，人们都在灞陵桥边折柳送别——古人送别时常折柳枝相赠，“柳”和“留”读音相近，有挽留的意思。下阕把镜头拉远：重阳节前后的清秋，乐游原上游人登高；向西望去，通往咸阳的古道上，远行人的音讯早已断绝。最后八个字“西风残照，汉家陵阙”，只有景物：秋风里，夕阳的余光照着汉代皇帝的陵墓和宫阙。从一个人的思念，一下子扩大到整个朝代的兴亡，气象非常阔大。“秦楼月”“音尘绝”的重复是词牌规定的叠句，像一声声叹息。王国维说这八个字“遂关千古登临之口”——后来登高写景的人，都很难超过它了。

In English

The first stanza is a woman’s sorrow at parting. The sobbing notes of a flute wake her from a dream, and outside is the moon over her tower in the land of Qin. The moon reminds her that every year, when the willows turn green, people say goodbye at Baling Bridge outside Chang’an. Breaking off a willow twig for a departing friend was an old custom — 柳 (liǔ, willow) sounds like 留 (liú, “stay”). The second stanza pulls the camera far back. It is the clear autumn festival season, and people climb the Leyou Plateau above the city. Looking west along the ancient road to Xianyang, she hears no news at all from the traveller. The last eight characters are pure scenery: “West wind, the last of the sunset, the tombs and towers of the Han.” In one sweep, a single person’s longing becomes the rise and fall of a whole dynasty. Notice the repeated phrases 秦楼月 and 音尘绝 — the tune requires them, and they sound like sighs following one another. The scholar Wang Guowei said these final eight characters “closed the mouths” of every later poet who climbed a height to write; no one could do better.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Like “Pusa Man”, this lyric has been credited to Li Bai (701–762) since the Song dynasty, but its authorship is doubtful and many scholars date it later. Its setting is all around Tang Chang’an: Baling Bridge, the Leyou Plateau and the road to Xianyang.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

箫声咽的“咽”读 yè（呜咽），不读 yān/yàn；乐游原的“乐”读 lè；灞陵 bà líng；陵阙 què。机器朗读“咽”容易读错，已在“朗读用字”列替换同音字，音频需人工听验。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行本，上下两阕共四十六字，网站按句读排为八行（“秦楼月”“音尘绝”为词牌规定的叠句）。作者归属自古存疑，网站照传统题李白。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

箫声咽，

xiāo shēng yè

The flute sobs —

秦娥梦断秦楼月。

qín é mèng duàn qín lóu yuè

the lady of Qin wakes from her dream to the moon over her tower.

秦楼月，

qín lóu yuè

The moon over her tower —

年年柳色，灞陵伤别。

nián nián liǔ sè, bà líng shāng bié

year after year the willows turn green, and people part in tears at Baling.

乐游原上清秋节，

lè yóu yuán shàng qīng qiū jié

On the Leyou Plateau, the clear days of the autumn festival;

咸阳古道音尘绝。

xián yáng gǔ dào yīn chén jué

on the old road to Xianyang, no news, not even a trail of dust.

音尘绝，

yīn chén jué

No news at all —

西风残照，汉家陵阙。

xī fēng cán zhào, hàn jiā líng què

only the west wind, the last of the sunset, and the tombs of the Han emperors.
