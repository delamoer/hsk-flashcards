导读About this poem

A summer night walk through the countryside: moonlight, a startled magpie, cicadas, the smell of rice flowers and a whole field of frogs — then a sudden shower, and an old inn that appears just when you need it.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首词写夏天夜里走在乡间小路上的所见所闻。上阕全是声音和气味：月光惊动了树枝上的喜鹊，半夜的清风里传来蝉鸣，稻花飘香，一片蛙声好像在说“今年是个丰收年”。诗人把蛙声写成农民谈论丰年，这种把动物写得像人一样的手法叫“拟人”，读起来很有趣。下阕写天气变了：天边只剩七八颗星，山前落下两三点雨。正想找个地方避雨，路一转过溪上的小桥，那家熟悉的茅草小店忽然就出现在眼前。“七八个”“两三点”用数字写出稀疏的感觉；最后一句把“找不到”和“忽然看见”放在一起，写出意外的惊喜。辛弃疾大多写豪放的爱国词，这首却轻松安静，充满对农村生活的喜爱。

In English

This is a night walk through farmland in midsummer, told almost entirely through the senses. The first half is all sound and smell: the bright moon startles a magpie off a slanting branch, cicadas sing in the cool midnight breeze, the rice is in flower, and a whole chorus of frogs seems to be chatting about the good harvest to come. Giving the frogs human talk is personification, and it makes the scene feel friendly and alive — the poet shares the farmers’ hope. In the second half the weather turns. Only seven or eight stars are left at the edge of the sky, and two or three drops of rain fall in front of the hills. The small numbers make everything feel sparse and quiet. Just as he wonders where to shelter, the path bends over a little bridge, and there is the old thatched inn by the shrine woods he remembers — suddenly right in front of him. That last line captures a tiny moment of relief and surprise that anyone who has walked at night will recognise. Xin Qiji is famous for fierce, patriotic poems; this one shows his gentle side and his real affection for country life.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Xin Qiji (1140–1207) was born in the north under Jurchen Jin rule, fought in an uprising as a young man and fled south to the Song. Pushed out of office, he lived for about ten years at Daihu near Shangrao (Jiangxi), and this poem comes from that time; the Yellow Sand Ridge was a road west of Shangrao.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

“路转溪桥忽见”的“见”：本词按“同‘现’，出现”理解，注 xiàn；也有教材、读本读 jiàn。机器朗读已在“朗读用字”列换成“现”。“别枝”读 bié zhī；“鸣蝉”的“蝉”读 chán。

版本说明 About the text

采用统编小学语文教材文本。全词上下两阕，共八句。“别枝”一般解作斜伸出的树枝，也有人理解为“离开树枝”。“旧时茅店社林边”有的版本作“旧时茆店社林边”（“茆”同“茅”）。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

明月别枝惊鹊，

míng yuè bié zhī jīng què

The bright moon startles a magpie off its leaning branch;

清风半夜鸣蝉。

qīng fēng bàn yè míng chán

cicadas sing in the cool midnight breeze.

稻花香里说丰年，

dào huā xiāng lǐ shuō fēng nián

Through the scent of rice in bloom, talk of a fine harvest:

听取蛙声一片。

tīng qǔ wā shēng yī piàn

listen — a whole field of frogs.

七八个星天外，

qī bā gè xīng tiān wài

Seven or eight stars at the sky’s far edge,

两三点雨山前。

liǎng sān diǎn yǔ shān qián

two or three drops of rain before the hills.

旧时茅店社林边，

jiù shí máo diàn shè lín biān

The old thatched inn beside the shrine woods —

路转溪桥忽见。

lù zhuǎn xī qiáo hū xiàn

round the bend by the creek bridge, there it is.
