导读About this poem

A legend of an immortal who flew away on a yellow crane, a tower left behind, and a traveller at sunset wondering where home is — a poem Li Bai himself is said to have admired too much to compete with.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

黄鹤楼在今天的武汉，长江边上。传说古代有仙人在这里骑着黄鹤飞走了。诗的前四句从传说写起：仙人早已乘黄鹤离去，这里只留下一座空空的黄鹤楼；黄鹤一去再也没回来，只有白云千百年来悠悠飘荡。“黄鹤”三次出现，“空”字两次出现，打破了律诗不重复用字的规矩，读起来却像民歌一样自然流畅，写出人去楼空、时光流逝的感慨。后四句回到眼前：晴朗的江面上，对岸汉阳的树木一棵棵看得清清楚楚，鹦鹉洲上芳草茂盛。天快黑了，诗人望向远方：我的家乡在哪里呢？江上烟雾茫茫，让人发愁。从神话写到现实，从怀古写到思乡，情和景结合得非常好。传说李白登上黄鹤楼，看到这首诗后说：“眼前有景道不得，崔颢题诗在上头。”南宋严羽称它为唐人七言律诗第一。

In English

Yellow Crane Tower stands on the Yangtze in today’s Wuhan. Legend says an immortal once flew away from here on a yellow crane. The first four lines start from that story: the immortal left long ago on his crane, and all that remains is an empty tower; the crane is gone and never came back, and for a thousand years only white clouds have drifted by. “Yellow crane” appears three times and “empty/only” (空) twice — breaking the rule that a regulated poem shouldn’t repeat characters — yet it flows as naturally as a folk song, and the repetition drives home the feeling of people gone, a building left, time slipping away. The last four lines come back to the present: across the sunlit river every tree in Hanyang is clear to see, and fragrant grass grows thick on Parrot Island. As the sun sets the poet looks into the distance and asks: where is my home? Mist and waves on the river only make him sadder. The poem moves from myth to reality and from the past to his own homesickness, with scene and feeling woven together. Li Bai is said to have climbed the tower, read this poem on the wall, and sighed that there was a view he couldn’t write about, because Cui Hao had already done it. The Song critic Yan Yu called it the finest seven-character regulated poem of the Tang.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Cui Hao (c. 704–754) was a Tang poet; few of his poems survive, but this one made him famous. The story of Li Bai declining to write about the tower after reading it is traditional and may be a legend.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

乘 chéng；千载 zǎi（年，不读 zài；机器朗读可能读错，已在“朗读用字”换成同音字“宰”）；萋萋 qī qī；鹦鹉 yīng wǔ；使人愁 shǐ。

版本说明 About the text

采用统编教材（八年级上册）文本。首句有异文“昔人已乘白云去”（不少学者认为是原貌），网站用教材的“乘黄鹤去”；“芳草萋萋”一作“春草萋萋”。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

昔人已乘黄鹤去，

xī rén yǐ chéng huáng hè qù

The immortal flew off long ago on his yellow crane;

此地空余黄鹤楼。

cǐ dì kōng yú huáng hè lóu

here nothing is left but the Yellow Crane Tower.

黄鹤一去不复返，

huáng hè yī qù bù fù fǎn

The crane, once gone, has never come back;

白云千载空悠悠。

bái yún qiān zǎi kōng yōu yōu

for a thousand years white clouds have drifted by.

晴川历历汉阳树，

qíng chuān lì lì hàn yáng shù

Across the bright river, Hanyang’s trees stand clear;

芳草萋萋鹦鹉洲。

fāng cǎo qī qī yīng wǔ zhōu

sweet grass grows thick on Parrot Island.

日暮乡关何处是？

rì mù xiāng guān hé chù shì

The sun goes down — which way is home?

烟波江上使人愁。

yān bō jiāng shàng shǐ rén chóu

Mist on the river waves fills me with sorrow.
