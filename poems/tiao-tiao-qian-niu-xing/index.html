导读About this poem

Two stars on either side of the Milky Way — the Herd-boy and the Weaver Girl — are lovers who can see each other but never speak. This Han dynasty poem is one of the earliest tellings of the story behind Qixi, China’s Valentine’s Day.

直译LiteralSlender, slender, she raises her white hands; click-clack, she works the loom and shuttle.

03

直译LiteralAll day she cannot finish a pattern; her weeping tears fall like rain.

意译MeaningAll day she weaves, yet no pattern grows — her tears fall like rain.

04

直译LiteralThe Milky Way is clear and shallow; how far apart are they, after all?

意译MeaningThe River of Stars is clear and shallow — how far apart can they really be?

05

直译LiteralSeparated by one brimming stretch of water, they gaze silently and cannot speak.

意译MeaningJust one brimming stream between them — they gaze in silence, and cannot say a word.

中文解析

这首诗写的是天上的两颗星：牵牛星和织女星，它们隔着银河遥遥相对。诗人把它们写成一对分离的恋人。前六句写织女：她伸出白白细细的手，札札地织着布，可是一整天也织不成花纹，眼泪像雨一样落下——因为心里想念牛郎，根本没有心思织布。后四句是诗人的感叹：银河又清又浅，两人到底隔得有多远呢？只隔着一条清浅的河水，却只能默默相望，说不出一句话。全诗十句里有六句用了叠字（迢迢、皎皎、纤纤、札札、盈盈、脉脉），读起来音节柔和，感情绵长。后来这个故事发展成“牛郎织女”的传说：每年农历七月初七，喜鹊在银河上搭桥让他们相会，这一天就是七夕节。

In English

The poem is about two stars, the Herd-boy (Altair) and the Weaver Girl (Vega), which shine on opposite sides of the Milky Way. The poet imagines them as lovers kept apart. The first six lines are about the Weaver Girl. She lifts her slender white hands and works her loom — click, clack — yet after a whole day she still hasn’t finished a single pattern, and her tears fall like rain. Her heart is with the Herd-boy, not with her weaving. The last four lines are the poet’s own sigh: the River of Stars is clear and shallow, so how far apart can they really be? Only one brimming stream lies between them, yet all they can do is gaze at each other in silence. Six of the ten lines contain doubled characters (迢迢, 皎皎, 纤纤, 札札, 盈盈, 脉脉), which give the poem a soft, lingering music. The story later grew into the legend of the Cowherd and the Weaver Girl: once a year, on the seventh night of the seventh lunar month, magpies form a bridge across the Milky Way so the two can meet. That night is the Qixi Festival, often called Chinese Valentine’s Day.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

The “Nineteen Old Poems” (古诗十九首) are anonymous five-character poems written by scholars of the late Eastern Han (around the 2nd century AD) and gathered in the 6th-century anthology Selections of Refined Literature (文选). Their plain, moving style made them a model for Chinese five-character poetry.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

迢迢牵牛星，皎皎河汉女。

tiáo tiáo qiān niú xīng, jiǎo jiǎo hé hàn nǚ

Far off shines the Herd-boy Star; bright is the Weaver Girl across the Milky Way.

纤纤擢素手，札札弄机杼。

xiān xiān zhuó sù shǒu, zhá zhá nòng jī zhù

She lifts her slender white hands — click, clack goes the shuttle through her loom.

终日不成章，泣涕零如雨。

zhōng rì bù chéng zhāng, qì tì líng rú yǔ

All day she weaves, yet no pattern grows — her tears fall like rain.

河汉清且浅，相去复几许？

hé hàn qīng qiě qiǎn, xiāng qù fù jǐ xǔ

The River of Stars is clear and shallow — how far apart can they really be?

盈盈一水间，脉脉不得语。

yíng yíng yī shuǐ jiàn, mò mò bù dé yǔ

Just one brimming stream between them — they gaze in silence, and cannot say a word.
