导读About this poem

A winter outing near the Huai River ends with a cup of frothy tea and a plate of fresh spring greens — and a line every Chinese food lover knows: the real flavour of life is simple joy.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首词写苏轼和朋友冬末去南山游玩。上阕写景：清晨下着细雨，吹着斜风，有点冷；不久天晴了，淡淡的烟雾、稀疏的柳树，让河滩显得格外可爱（“媚”）；清澈的洛涧流进淮河，水面越来越宽广。下阕写吃：中午喝一杯茶，茶面上浮着雪白的泡沫；再尝一盘春天的野菜——蓼菜的嫩芽、蒿笋。最后一句是千古名句：“人间有味是清欢”——人世间真正有滋味的，是这种清淡的快乐。清欢，就是简单、不奢华、让人心里干净的快乐。苏轼刚经历了多年的贬谪，却能从一杯茶、一盘野菜里感受到幸福，这正是他豁达的人生态度。

In English

Su Shi wrote this after an outing to South Mountain with a friend at the end of winter. The first stanza is landscape: a dawn of fine rain and slanting wind, a little cold; then it clears, and thin mist and sparse willows make the river beach look charming. The clear Luo stream flows into the Huai River, and the water spreads wider and wider. The second stanza is food. At noon they drink tea, its surface covered in snow-white froth, and taste a “spring plate” of fresh wild greens — tender knotweed shoots and wormwood stems. Then comes one of Su Shi’s most quoted lines: 人间有味是清欢 — “what truly has flavour in this world is simple joy.” 清欢 is a light, uncomplicated happiness with nothing showy about it. After years of exile, Su Shi could still find real happiness in a cup of tea and a plate of greens. That is his attitude to life in a single line.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Su Shi (1037–1101) wrote this on the 24th day of the 12th lunar month of 1084, just after leaving his exile at Huangzhou for a new post. Near Sizhou (by the Huai River) he went walking on South Mountain with a local friend, Liu Qianshu.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

斜风 xié（旧读 xiá，今按 xié）；漫漫 màn màn；蓼茸 liǎo róng（蓼：一种水边野菜）；蒿笋 hāo sǔn；午盏 wǔ zhǎn。“蓼”机器朗读可能读错，音频需人工听验。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行文本。原词有小序：“元丰七年十二月二十四日，从泗州刘倩叔游南山。”（维基文库所据本作“十月二十四日”）网站正文不收小序。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

细雨斜风作晓寒，

xì yǔ xié fēng zuò xiǎo hán

Fine rain and a slanting wind bring a chill to the dawn;

淡烟疏柳媚晴滩。

dàn yān shū liǔ mèi qíng tān

then thin mist and sparse willows make the bright shore lovely.

入淮清洛渐漫漫。

rù huái qīng luò jiàn màn màn

The clear Luo pours into the Huai, wider and wider.

雪沫乳花浮午盏，

xuě mò rǔ huā fú wǔ zhǎn

Froth white as snow and milk floats on our noon tea;

蓼茸蒿笋试春盘。

liǎo róng hāo sǔn shì chūn pán

we taste a spring plate of tender shoots and greens.

人间有味是清欢。

rén jiān yǒu wèi shì qīng huān

What gives this world its true flavour is simple joy.
