导读About this poem

A young palace woman on a cool autumn night: she swats at fireflies with a little fan, then lies down to gaze at the lovers’ stars. Not one word of complaint — every image does the work.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗写深宫里一位宫女的秋夜。第一句：白色的蜡烛照着画屏，秋夜的光显得冷清。第二句：她拿着轻罗小扇扑萤火虫。秋天了还用扇子，暗示她像夏天过后被收起的扇子一样，被冷落了；萤火虫多的地方，往往是荒凉少人的地方。第三句写夜深了，石阶凉得像水。第四句她躺下来看天上的牵牛星和织女星。传说牛郎织女每年七夕还能相会，她却只能远远地看着，那份孤独和盼望不用说出来。全诗没有一个字写“愁”，只写了动作和景物，感情却藏在每个细节里。这种写宫女寂寞生活的诗叫“宫怨诗”，这是其中最含蓄的一首。

In English

This poem shows one autumn night in the life of a young woman in the imperial palace. Line one: white candles light a painted screen, and the autumn light feels cold. Line two: she flaps at fireflies with a small silk fan. Carrying a fan in autumn is a hint — like a summer fan put away when the weather cools, she has been forgotten. And fireflies gather in quiet, overgrown places where few people come. Line three: the night deepens, and the stone steps are as cool as water. Line four: she lies down and watches two stars, the Herd-boy and the Weaver-girl. In the legend these lovers are allowed to meet once a year, on the seventh night of the seventh month; she can only watch from far away. The poem never says she is sad or lonely. It shows only what she does and what she sees, and the feeling is hidden in every detail. Poems about the loneliness of palace women form a whole genre called gōngyuàn shī (“palace laments”), and this is one of the most understated.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Du Mu (803–c. 852) wrote many poems about palace life and the past. The poem has also been attributed to Wang Jian, but it is generally included among Du Mu’s works.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

银烛秋光冷画屏，

yín zhú qiū guāng lěng huà píng

White candles, autumn light, a painted screen gone cold;

轻罗小扇扑流萤。

qīng luó xiǎo shàn pū liú yíng

with a small silk fan she swats at drifting fireflies.

天阶夜色凉如水，

tiān jiē yè sè liáng rú shuǐ

The night on the palace steps is cool as water;

卧看牵牛织女星。

wò kàn qiān niú zhī nǚ xīng

she lies and watches the Herd-boy and the Weaver-girl in the sky.
