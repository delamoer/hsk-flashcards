长相思（山一程）

Cháng Xiāng Sī
Endless Longing: “Mile After Mile of Mountains”

导读About this poem

An army marches on through mountains and rivers toward the northeast frontier; at night, wind and snow beat on the tents and a homesick soldier cannot dream of home. Short, musical and full of repetition.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

1682年，纳兰性德跟随康熙皇帝出山海关，去东北祭祖，路上写了这首词。上阕写走：“山一程，水一程”，翻过一座山又过一条河，一程又一程，重复的句子让人感到路途没完没了；身体一直朝着山海关那边走，心却在背后的家乡。到了夜里，营地里上千座帐篷都点着灯，场面很大，却更显得人孤单。下阕写夜：“风一更，雪一更”，一更又一更（古人把夜晚分成五更），风雪整夜不停。风雪声吵得人心都碎了，想回家的梦也做不成。最后一句很平淡又很深：家乡没有这样的声音。这里用了对比——塞外的风雪和家乡的安静。全词用字简单，叠句像歌一样，很适合朗读。

In English

In 1682 Nalan Xingde travelled with the Kangxi Emperor beyond the Shanhai Pass to the northeast, where the emperor honoured his ancestors’ tombs. This lyric was written on that journey. The first half is about going. “One stage of mountains, one stage of rivers” — over a mountain, across a river, again and again. The repeated pattern makes the road feel endless. His body keeps moving toward the pass, but his heart is behind him, at home. At night a thousand tents in the camp are lit with lamps: a grand sight, but it only makes one person feel smaller and lonelier. The second half is about the night. “One watch of wind, one watch of snow” — the old Chinese night was divided into five watches (更 gēng), and the storm goes on through every one of them. The noise breaks his homesick heart to pieces and he cannot even dream of home. The last line is quiet and simple: back home there is no sound like this. The contrast between the howling frontier and the peaceful hometown says everything. The words are easy and the repetition makes it sound like a song, so this is a lovely lyric to read aloud.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Nalan Xingde (1655–1685) was a Manchu nobleman and imperial bodyguard to the Kangxi Emperor. In the spring of 1682 he accompanied the emperor on a journey east through the Shanhai Pass (榆关) to Shengjing (today’s Shenyang) to visit the imperial ancestral tombs.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

山一程，水一程，

shān yī chéng, shuǐ yī chéng

Mile after mile of mountains, mile after mile of rivers,

身向榆关那畔行，

shēn xiàng yú guān nà pàn xíng

on I go toward the land beyond the pass;

夜深千帐灯。

yè shēn qiān zhàng dēng

late at night, a thousand tents glow with lamps.

风一更，雪一更，

fēng yī gēng, xuě yī gēng

Wind through one watch, snow through the next,

聒碎乡心梦不成，

guō suì xiāng xīn mèng bù chéng

the din shatters my homesick heart, and no dream will come;

故园无此声。

gù yuán wú cǐ shēng

back home, there is no sound like this.
