野有蔓草

Wild Grass Spreading

导读About this poem

A dewy field of grass, and by chance a lovely girl with bright, clear eyes — exactly who he hoped to meet. A fresh, sunny love-at-first-sight poem from China’s oldest poetry collection.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这是一首写一见钟情的诗，充满清新的田野气息。清晨，野外的蔓草长得很茂盛，草叶上挂满了圆圆的露珠；就在这样的地方，“我”偶然遇到了一位美丽的姑娘，她的眉眼清秀明亮，正是“我”心里想要遇见的人。“邂逅相遇，适我愿兮”——没有约定，碰巧遇见，却正合心意，这种惊喜特别动人。第二章几乎重复第一章，最后一句变成“与子偕臧”：我愿意和你在一起，一切都好。开头写景，露水的清新和姑娘的美互相映衬，先写景物再引出人，这就是《诗经》常用的“起兴”。今天“邂逅”还是很常用的词，专指意外地遇见，常带一点浪漫的味道。

In English

This is a love-at-first-sight poem with the fresh air of the countryside in it. Early in the morning, wild grass spreads thick across the fields, every blade hung with round drops of dew. And there, by pure chance, the speaker meets a beautiful girl with clear, bright eyes — exactly the person he had hoped to meet. 邂逅相遇，适我愿兮: no plan, no appointment, just a lucky meeting that turns out to be everything he wished for. The second verse repeats the first almost word for word, but the last line becomes 与子偕臧, “with you, all is well” — he wants to be with her. The opening scene and the girl reflect each other: the freshness of the dew mirrors her clear beauty. Beginning with a scene and then bringing in the person is xìng (“evoking”), the favourite opening move of the Book of Songs. The word 邂逅 (xièhòu) is still common in modern Chinese for an unexpected meeting, usually with a hint of romance.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

The Book of Songs gathers 305 songs from about the 11th to the 6th century BC. This is from the state of Zheng (郑, central Henan), whose songs are full of young people meeting outdoors in spring. The author is unknown.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

蔓草 màn cǎo（不读 wàn）；漙 tuán（露水多）；瀼瀼 ráng ráng；婉 wǎn；邂逅 xiè hòu；偕臧 xié zāng。“漙”“瀼”机器朗读易错，已在“朗读用字”列替换同音字。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

野有蔓草，零露漙兮。

yě yǒu màn cǎo, líng lù tuán xī

Wild grass spreads across the field, beaded with round drops of dew.

有美一人，清扬婉兮。

yǒu měi yī rén, qīng yáng wǎn xī

And there is a lovely girl, her eyes so clear, her brows so fine.

邂逅相遇，适我愿兮。

xiè hòu xiāng yù, shì wǒ yuàn xī

We met by chance — just the one I’d hoped to find.

野有蔓草，零露瀼瀼。

yě yǒu màn cǎo, líng lù ráng ráng

Wild grass spreads across the field, soaked with heavy dew.

有美一人，婉如清扬。

yǒu měi yī rén, wǎn rú qīng yáng

And there is a lovely girl, so gentle, her eyes so bright.

邂逅相遇，与子偕臧。

xiè hòu xiāng yù, yǔ zǐ xié zāng

We met by chance — with you, everything is right.
