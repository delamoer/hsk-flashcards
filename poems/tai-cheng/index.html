导读About this poem

In the misty rain of Nanjing, six dynasties have come and gone like a dream; only the willows along the old palace wall go on turning green, as if nothing had happened. A quiet, haunting poem about how little nature cares for history.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

台城在今天的南京，是东晋和南朝宋、齐、梁、陈的皇宫所在地，加上更早的东吴，一共六个朝代在这里建都，合称“六朝”。到韦庄的时代，这些王朝早已灭亡，台城也荒废了。诗的开头是一片江南春景：江上细雨纷纷，江边的草长得整整齐齐。可是六朝的繁华就像一场梦，只剩鸟儿白白地啼叫——“空”字说明再热闹的叫声也唤不回过去。后两句最有名：最“无情”的是台城的柳树，它不管朝代兴亡，依旧像烟雾一样笼罩着十里长堤。其实柳树并没有感情，诗人说它“无情”，正是因为自己太“有情”。用不变的自然来衬托变化的人世，这种手法叫“以乐景写哀情”，越是春色美好，越让人感到伤感。

In English

Taicheng, the “Palace City”, stood in today’s Nanjing. Six short-lived dynasties — Wu, Eastern Jin, Song, Qi, Liang and Chen — made this city their capital between the third and sixth centuries, and together they are called the Six Dynasties. By Wei Zhuang’s time they were long gone and the palace was in ruins. The poem opens with a soft spring scene: fine rain drifts over the river, and the riverside grass grows thick and even. But the glory of the Six Dynasties has passed like a dream, and the birds sing “in vain” — no amount of singing can call the past back. The famous second half blames the willows. They are the most heartless thing of all, he says, because they still wrap the ten-li embankment in green mist, exactly as they did when emperors lived here. Of course a tree has no heart; the poet calls the willows unfeeling because he himself feels so much. Setting unchanging nature against changing human fortunes, and using a beautiful scene to deepen a sad mood, is a favourite move in Chinese poems about the past.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Wei Zhuang (c. 836–910) lived through the collapse of the Tang dynasty. After rebels took Chang’an in 880 he spent years in the Jiangnan region, where he is thought to have written this poem; late in life he became chief minister of the Former Shu kingdom in Sichuan.

版本说明 About the text

题目采用通行的《台城》（《才调集》同）；《唐诗三百首》《唐人万首绝句选》题作《金陵图》，后者与另一首“谁谓伤心画不成”并列。全诗完整。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

江雨霏霏江草齐，

jiāng yǔ fēi fēi jiāng cǎo qí

Fine rain drifts on the river, the grass grows thick along its banks;

六朝如梦鸟空啼。

liù cháo rú mèng niǎo kōng tí

six dynasties have passed like a dream, and the birds sing to no one.

无情最是台城柳，

wú qíng zuì shì tái chéng liǔ

Most heartless of all, the willows by the palace walls —

依旧烟笼十里堤。

yī jiù yān lǒng shí lǐ dī

they still veil the long embankment in green mist, as if nothing had changed.
