导读About this poem

Stuck far from home beside the Yangtze, the poet watches autumn wind blow yellow leaves off every hill. Twenty characters, and the last line paints the whole mood.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗是王勃流落在巴蜀（今天的四川一带）时写的。第一句说自己在长江边停留太久，心里很悲伤；第二句说家在万里之外，一直想着回去。第三句用“况”（何况）推进一层：本来就想家，何况又正赶上秋风高起、天色已晚的时候。最后一句只写景：一座座山上，黄叶在风里纷纷飘飞。前两句直接说心情，后两句把心情放进景色里，这叫“借景抒情”。黄叶离开树枝飞走，就像游子离开家乡漂泊；“山山”两个字重复，让人看到满眼都是落叶，秋天的萧瑟和思乡的愁一起扑过来。

In English

Wang Bo wrote this while drifting through Ba and Shu (today’s Sichuan), far from his home in the north. Line one: he has been held up by the Yangtze too long, and he is unhappy about it. Line two: home is ten thousand li away, and all he thinks about is going back. Line three adds weight with 况 (“what’s more”): he already misses home — and now it is late autumn, with a high wind blowing in the evening. Then the last line stops explaining and simply shows: on hill after hill, yellow leaves are flying. The first half states the feeling directly; the second half puts it into the landscape. This is called jiè jǐng shū qíng, “expressing feeling through scenery”. Leaves torn from their branches and blown far away look a lot like a traveller torn from home. The doubled word 山山 (“hill after hill”) fills the whole view with falling leaves, so autumn’s bleakness and the poet’s homesickness arrive together. It is an easy poem for beginners: the words are simple, and the picture in the last line is easy to remember.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Wang Bo (c. 650–676) was one of the “Four Paragons of the Early Tang”. Dismissed from a court post as a young man, he spent several years wandering in Sichuan around 669–671; this poem is traditionally dated to that time.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

况属 的“属”读 zhǔ（适逢、正赶上），不读 shǔ；多数注本注 zhǔ，也有读 shǔ 的。机器朗读容易读成 shǔ，已在“朗读用字”列替换。长江 cháng。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

长江悲已滞，

cháng jiāng bēi yǐ zhì

By the Yangtze I grieve — I have lingered far too long;

万里念将归。

wàn lǐ niàn jiāng guī

ten thousand li from home, I long to start back.

况属高风晚，

kuàng zhǔ gāo fēng wǎn

And now the autumn wind blows high at dusk,

山山黄叶飞。

shān shān huáng yè fēi

and on every hill the yellow leaves are flying.
