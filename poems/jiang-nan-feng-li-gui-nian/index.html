江南逢李龟年

Meeting Li Guinian in the South

导读About this poem

Two old men meet again in the south, decades after they knew each other in the glittering capital. The poem only says “what lovely scenery” — and lets everything that has been lost stay unsaid.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

李龟年是唐玄宗时最有名的歌唱家。杜甫年轻时在洛阳、长安的王公贵族家里，常常听他唱歌。前两句回忆那时：在岐王的府里经常见到他，在崔九的厅堂前也听过好几次他的歌——那是唐朝最繁华的年代。几十年后，安史之乱让国家由盛转衰，两人都流落到了江南。后两句写重逢：正是江南风景最好的时候，在落花纷飞的季节又遇见了您。字面上很平静，好像只是在说“真巧”，可“落花时节”既是暮春，也暗示两个人都老了、时代也败落了。诗人不说一句悲伤的话，感慨却全在里面，这种“含蓄”（不直接说出来）是中国诗歌很看重的写法。

In English

Li Guinian was the most famous singer of Emperor Xuanzong's court. As a young man Du Fu often heard him perform at the mansions of the great: “I saw you often at Prince Qi's house, and heard you more than once in Cui Jiu's hall.” Those two names alone call up the high noon of the Tang dynasty, when the capital was rich and confident. Then came the An Lushan Rebellion, the empire's sudden decline, and years of wandering. Now, some forty years later, both men have drifted south to the Yangtze region, and they meet again. The last two lines sound almost casual: it's the loveliest season in the south, and here I am running into you again among the falling flowers. But “the time of falling flowers” is doing a lot of work. It is late spring, the end of the season; it is also the two men in old age, and a dynasty past its best. Du Fu never says he is sad. Chinese critics prize exactly this restraint — the feeling is all there, but left for the reader to supply.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Du Fu (712–770) is traditionally said to have written this in 770, the last year of his life, when he met the aged singer Li Guinian in Tanzhou (today's Changsha, Hunan). Prince Qi was a younger brother of Emperor Xuanzong; Cui Jiu (Cui Di) was a court official, both known for hosting artists.
