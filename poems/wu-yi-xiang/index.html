导读About this poem

Black Robe Lane in Nanjing was once home to the two greatest families of their age. Now there are weeds by the bridge and swallows nesting in ordinary houses — a four-line lesson in how glory fades.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

乌衣巷在今天的南京，东晋时是王导、谢安两大豪门家族住的地方，非常繁华。到了刘禹锡的唐代，已经过去了四百多年。前两句写眼前的荒凉：朱雀桥边长满了野草野花，乌衣巷口只有斜斜的夕阳。“野草花”“夕阳斜”都带着冷落、衰败的味道。后两句是全诗最巧妙的地方：从前在王家、谢家厅堂前筑巢的燕子，如今飞进了普通百姓的家里。燕子每年都回到老地方，可“老地方”早已换了主人。诗人不直接说“世事变化太大了”，而是借一只燕子让读者自己想到：富贵荣华不会长久。这种借小东西写大道理的方法，让诗显得含蓄又深刻。“旧时王谢堂前燕，飞入寻常百姓家”今天常用来比喻曾经少数人才有的东西，现在普通人也能享有。

In English

Black Robe Lane is in today’s Nanjing. In the Eastern Jin dynasty it was home to the Wang and Xie clans, the two most powerful families of the age, and the area was splendid. By Liu Yuxi’s time, more than four hundred years later, that world was long gone. The first two lines show the emptiness now: wild grass and weeds flower by the Vermilion Bird Bridge, and at the mouth of the lane there is only the slanting evening sun. “Wild flowers” and “setting sun” both carry a sense of neglect and decline. The last two lines are the clever part: the swallows that once nested in front of the great halls of the Wangs and the Xies now fly into the homes of ordinary people. Swallows return to the same place every year — but the place has changed hands. The poet never says “how much the world has changed”; he lets a single swallow lead us to think it: wealth and glory do not last. Using something small to say something large makes the poem quiet and deep. Today the last two lines are often quoted, with a happier twist, for things once enjoyed by a privileged few that ordinary people can now have.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Liu Yuxi (772–842) was a Tang poet and official who spent over twenty years in exile after a failed reform movement. This is the second of his five “Jinling Poems”, written around 826; he is said to have composed them before ever visiting Jinling (Nanjing) himself.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

“夕阳斜”的“斜”：为与“花”“家”押韵，旧读 xiá；今天的教材多注今音 xié，网站读 xié。“寻常”读 xún cháng。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

朱雀桥边野草花，

zhū què qiáo biān yě cǎo huā

By Vermilion Bird Bridge the weeds are in flower;

乌衣巷口夕阳斜。

wū yī xiàng kǒu xī yáng xié

the low sun slants into Black Robe Lane.

旧时王谢堂前燕，

jiù shí wáng xiè táng qián yàn

Swallows that once nested in the halls of great lords

飞入寻常百姓家。

fēi rù xún cháng bǎi xìng jiā

now fly into plain people’s homes.
