导读About this poem

Five friends, exiled together to the far south, now live in five separate towns. From a city tower in a storm, one of them writes to the other four.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

柳宗元和四位朋友因为参加政治改革失败，被贬到南方边远地区当刺史，五人分在五个州，彼此很远。诗人登上柳州城楼，写下这首诗寄给他们。开头两句视野极大：高楼连着无边的荒野，愁思像海天一样茫茫无边。三、四句写近景：狂风乱吹水里的荷花，密雨斜打爬满薜荔的墙——表面写风雨，也暗指他们这些正直的人被打击。五、六句写远望：山上的树层层挡住视线，看不见朋友；弯弯曲曲的江水像愁肠打了九个弯。最后两句说：我们一起来到这遥远的百越之地，却连书信都难以相通。全诗把景、情和政治遭遇融在一起，是唐代七律中的名篇。

In English

In 805 Liu Zongyuan and his friends took part in a reform movement at court that failed within months. They were all sent away, and ten years later, in 815, five of them were posted as governors of distant southern prefectures, each far from the others. Climbing the city tower of Liuzhou, Liu writes to the other four. The opening is vast: the tower looks out over endless wilderness, and his sorrow stretches as wide as sea and sky. Lines 3–4 come close: a wild wind tosses the lotus in the pond, and slanting rain lashes the vine-covered wall. It is a real storm, but lotus and creeping fig were symbols of upright people — so it is also a picture of good men being battered. Lines 5–6 look far: layer upon layer of trees on the ridges block his view of his friends, and the river winds like a sorrowful gut twisted nine times (a Chinese image of deep grief). The ending is plain and painful: we came together to this land of tattooed tribes, and now we can’t even send each other letters. Scenery, feeling and politics are woven together here, which is why this is counted among the finest Tang regulated verses.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Liu Zongyuan (773–819) was a major Tang essayist and poet. After the failed Yongzhen Reform of 805 he spent the rest of his life in exile in the south; he wrote this in 815 on arriving in Liuzhou (in today’s Guangxi), where he died four years later.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

飐 zhǎn（风吹颤动）；薜荔 bì lì；斜 xié；重遮 chóng（层层）；曲 qū（弯曲）；滞 zhì。愁思：今音 chóu sī；按格律此处“思”旧读去声 sì，网站采用今音。机器朗读“飐”“薜”“重”“曲”易读错，已在“朗读用字”列替换同音字，音频需人工听验。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

城上高楼接大荒，

chéng shàng gāo lóu jiē dà huāng

From the tower on the wall, the wilds run on forever;

海天愁思正茫茫。

hǎi tiān chóu sī zhèng máng máng

my sorrow spreads as wide as sea and sky.

惊风乱飐芙蓉水，

jīng fēng luàn zhǎn fú róng shuǐ

A sudden wind whips the lotus in the pond,

密雨斜侵薜荔墙。

mì yǔ xié qīn bì lì qiáng

thick rain slants against the vine-covered wall.

岭树重遮千里目，

lǐng shù chóng zhē qiān lǐ mù

Trees on ridge after ridge cut off my distant gaze;

江流曲似九回肠。

jiāng liú qū sì jiǔ huí cháng

the river twists like grief that turns my insides nine times over.

共来百越文身地，

gòng lái bǎi yuè wén shēn dì

We came together to these far southern lands,

犹自音书滞一乡。

yóu zì yīn shū zhì yī xiāng

yet our letters can’t reach each other — each of us stuck alone.
