导读About this poem

Li Bai builds a mock-serious argument for drinking: heaven has a Wine Star, earth has a Wine Spring, so loving wine must be perfectly proper. Funny, clever and a little lonely.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

李白的《月下独酌》共四首，第一首“举杯邀明月”最有名，这是第二首。这首诗像在“讲道理”，其实是开玩笑：天上有一颗“酒星”，说明天爱酒；地上有个地方叫“酒泉”，说明地也爱酒；既然天地都爱酒，我爱酒当然不用惭愧。接着用了一个典故：三国时曹操禁酒，喝酒的人偷偷把清酒叫“圣人”、浊酒叫“贤人”。李白说，圣人贤人我都“喝”进肚子里了，还求什么神仙？三杯下去就懂了天地大道，一斗下去就和自然融为一体。最后两句说：只要自己体会到酒中的乐趣就好，不必讲给清醒的人听——他们不会懂。整首诗用一本正经的推理写出一种狂放和幽默，背后却藏着一点孤独：能懂他的人太少了。

In English

Li Bai wrote four poems called “Drinking Alone under the Moon”; the first, where he invites the moon to drink with him, is the famous one. This second poem is a playful, mock-serious argument. Heaven has a star called the Wine Star, so heaven must love wine; earth has a place called Wine Spring (Jiuquan, a real town in the northwest), so earth loves wine too. If heaven and earth both love it, why should he feel ashamed? Then he brings in an old story. When the warlord Cao Cao banned alcohol in the third century, drinkers secretly called clear wine “the sage” and cloudy wine “the worthy man”. Li Bai says he has drunk both the sage and the worthy, so what need does he have for immortals? Three cups and he understands the Great Way; a whole jug and he melts into Nature — a joking nod to Daoist ideas. The ending is the sharpest line: just enjoy the pleasure of wine, and don’t try to explain it to the sober, because they will never get it. Under the humour there is loneliness. The poem is called “drinking alone” for a reason: the people who could understand him are few.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Li Bai (701–762), the “Immortal of Poetry”, was famous for his love of wine and freedom. The four “Drinking Alone under the Moon” poems are traditionally dated to around 744, when he was in Chang’an and growing disappointed with court life.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

版本说明 About the text

采用通行文本（《李太白全集》）。“但得酒中趣”有的版本作“但得醉中趣”；“勿为醒者传”有的版本作“勿为醒者言”。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

天若不爱酒，

tiān ruò bù ài jiǔ

If heaven had no love for wine,

酒星不在天。

jiǔ xīng bù zài tiān

there’d be no Wine Star in the sky.

地若不爱酒，

dì ruò bù ài jiǔ

If earth had no love for wine,

地应无酒泉。

dì yīng wú jiǔ quán

there’d be no Wine Spring on the ground.

天地既爱酒，

tiān dì jì ài jiǔ

Since heaven and earth both love it,

爱酒不愧天。

ài jiǔ bù kuì tiān

why should I blush to love it too?

已闻清比圣，

yǐ wén qīng bǐ shèng

They say clear wine is like a sage,

复道浊如贤。

fù dào zhuó rú xián

and cloudy wine is like a wise man.

贤圣既已饮，

xián shèng jì yǐ yǐn

With sage and wise man both inside me,

何必求神仙。

hé bì qiú shén xiān

what need have I to chase immortals?

三杯通大道，

sān bēi tōng dà dào

Three cups, and I see into the Way;

一斗合自然。

yī dǒu hé zì rán

a whole jug, and I’m one with Nature.

但得酒中趣，

dàn dé jiǔ zhōng qù

Just enjoy the pleasure in the cup,

勿为醒者传。

wù wèi xǐng zhě chuán

and don’t explain it to the sober.
