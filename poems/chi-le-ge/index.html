导读About this poem

A song of the northern grasslands: the sky is a great round tent over endless plains, and when the wind blows the tall grass down, you suddenly see the cattle and sheep. Short, simple and huge in feeling.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这是一首北方草原上的民歌，原来是游牧民族敕勒人用自己的语言唱的，后来翻译成了汉语，所以句子有长有短。前两句交代地点：敕勒川，就在阴山脚下。接着诗人用一个很生动的比喻：天空就像牧民住的圆顶帐篷（穹庐），把四面八方的原野都罩住了。这个比喻只有草原上的人才想得出来。然后是“天苍苍，野茫茫”——天是青色的，一望无边；原野辽阔，看不到尽头。最后一句最有名：风一吹，高高的草低下头来，藏在草里的牛羊就露出来了。草长得比牛羊还高，说明草原多么肥美；牛羊多，说明牧民生活富足。这一句从静写到动，整个画面一下子活了起来。全诗只有二十七个字，却写出了草原的辽阔和牧民的自豪。

In English

This is a folk song from the northern grasslands. It was first sung by the Chile, a nomadic people, in their own language, and later translated into Chinese — which is why the lines are of uneven length. The first two lines set the scene: the Chile plain, at the foot of the Yin Mountains. Then comes a vivid comparison: the sky is like a qiónglú, the round felt tent nomads live in, covering the plains on every side. Only someone who grew up on the grasslands would think of that. Next: the sky is blue and endless, the plains stretch away out of sight. The last line is the famous one. A gust of wind bends the tall grass, and suddenly the cattle and sheep hidden in it appear. Grass taller than the animals shows how rich the pasture is; so many animals show a good life for the herders. The picture is still until this moment — then the wind moves through it and everything comes alive. In only 27 characters the song captures the vastness of the steppe and the pride of the people who lived there.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

The Chile (also known as the Tiele) were a nomadic people of the northern steppe in the 5th–6th centuries. The song is said to have been sung in 546 by the general Hulü Jin to raise the spirits of the Northern Qi army; the Chinese text was translated from the original language.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

“风吹草低见牛羊”的“见”，统编教材注 xiàn（同“现”，出现、露出），本页采用 xiàn；旧时也有读 jiàn 的。“笼盖四野”的“笼”统编教材注 lǒng（笼罩），“野”读 yě（旧时为押韵读 yǎ，今不采用）。“敕勒”读 chì lè。

版本说明 About the text

采用统编语文教材二年级上册文本，全诗二十七字，此处完整收录。本诗最早见于宋代郭茂倩《乐府诗集》，原为鲜卑语或敕勒语，后译为汉语。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

敕勒川，阴山下。

chì lè chuān, yīn shān xià

The Chile plain lies below the Yin Mountains.

天似穹庐，笼盖四野。

tiān sì qióng lú, lǒng gài sì yě

The sky is a great round tent, covering the plains on every side.

天苍苍，野茫茫，

tiān cāng cāng, yě máng máng

Blue, blue sky; wide, wide land —

风吹草低见牛羊。

fēng chuī cǎo dī xiàn niú yáng

the wind bends the grass, and there are the cattle and sheep.
