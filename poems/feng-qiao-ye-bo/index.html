枫桥夜泊

Fēng Qiáo Yè Bó
Moored at Night by Maple Bridge
张继Zhāng Jì · Zhang Ji唐 Tang

导读About this poem

A traveller can’t sleep on his boat outside Suzhou: the moon sets, crows call, frost fills the air — and at midnight the bell of Hanshan Temple reaches the boat. One sleepless night, remembered for 1,200 years.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗写一个旅人夜里把船停在苏州城外的枫桥边，心里有愁，睡不着。前两句把看到、听到、感到的东西排在一起：月亮落下去了，乌鸦在叫，寒气像霜一样布满天地；江边的枫树、渔船上的点点灯火，陪着满怀愁绪的诗人难以入睡。这些景物都带着冷清和孤单的色彩。后两句只写一个声音：半夜里，城外寒山寺的钟声一下一下传到客船上。夜越静，钟声越清楚；钟声越清楚，越显出夜的寂静和人的孤独。用声音来衬托安静，是中国诗常用的手法。诗里没有说愁什么，但“客船”“对愁眠”告诉我们，他是一个远离家乡的人。因为这首诗，寒山寺成了名胜，每年除夕很多人特地去那里听钟声，日本人也特别喜爱这首诗。

In English

A traveller has tied up his boat for the night at Maple Bridge, just outside the city of Suzhou, and he cannot sleep. The first two lines pile up what he sees, hears and feels: the moon is going down, crows are cawing, and the cold in the air is so sharp it feels as if frost fills the whole sky. Maples on the riverbank and the lamps of fishing boats keep him company as he lies awake with his worries. Every image is cold, dim and lonely. Then the second half gives us only one thing — a sound. At midnight the bell of Hanshan Temple, outside the city wall, comes drifting across the water to his boat. The quieter the night, the clearer the bell; the clearer the bell, the deeper the silence and the loneliness feel. Using a sound to make stillness more noticeable is a favourite trick of Chinese poets. The poem never explains what the sorrow is, but the “traveller’s boat” tells us he is far from home. Thanks to these four lines, Hanshan Temple became famous: crowds still go there on New Year’s Eve to hear the bell strike 108 times, and the poem is especially loved in Japan.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Little is known about Zhang Ji (dates uncertain, 8th century) except that he passed the imperial exam in 753 and later served as an official. He is traditionally thought to have written this poem while travelling south to escape the An Lushan Rebellion.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

“夜泊”的“泊”读 bó（停船），不读 pō。其余无特殊读音。

版本说明 About the text

采用统编小学语文教材版本。“江枫渔火”有不同解释（有人说“江枫”指江村桥和枫桥），网站按通行理解为江边枫树。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

月落乌啼霜满天，

yuè luò wū tí shuāng mǎn tiān

The moon goes down, crows cry, frost fills the sky;

江枫渔火对愁眠。

jiāng fēng yú huǒ duì chóu mián

river maples and fishing lamps keep watch over my restless sleep.

姑苏城外寒山寺，

gū sū chéng wài hán shān sì

From Hanshan Temple, outside the walls of Suzhou,

夜半钟声到客船。

yè bàn zhōng shēng dào kè chuán

the midnight bell comes floating to my boat.
