导读About this poem

Deep green shade, towers reflected in a pond, a crystal curtain stirring in a breeze, and the scent of roses filling the courtyard: a cool, quiet picture of a long summer day.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗写夏天山中亭子周围的风景，像一幅色彩鲜明的画。第一句：绿树长得很茂密，树荫很浓，夏天的白天特别长。第二句：楼台的倒影映在池塘里，水面像镜子一样平静。第三句最妙：水晶帘子轻轻一动，诗人才发现起了微风——风是看不见的，诗人通过帘子的晃动把它写了出来。第四句：一架子的蔷薇花开得正盛，满院子都是香气。四句诗分别写了颜色（绿树）、倒影（池塘）、动作（帘动）和香味（蔷薇），用眼睛、皮肤、鼻子一起感受夏天。全诗没有写热，反而让人觉得清凉、安静、舒服。

In English

This short poem paints a summer scene around a mountain pavilion, almost like a colour painting. Line one: the trees are thick and green, their shade is deep, and the summer day stretches on and on. Line two: the pavilion and its towers are mirrored upside down in the still pond. Line three is the clever one: a crystal-bead curtain sways, and only then do we know a breeze has started. Wind is invisible, so the poet shows it through what it moves. Line four: a whole trellis of roses is in bloom, and their fragrance fills the courtyard. Each line uses a different sense or effect — colour, reflection, movement, scent — so we see, feel and smell the summer at once. Notice that the poem never mentions heat. Instead it leaves you with a cool, calm, comfortable feeling, which is exactly what a summer afternoon in the hills should be.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Gao Pian (821–887) was a late Tang general and military governor who fought against the Huang Chao rebellion, though he later fell from power and was killed in an uprising. He was also a cultured poet; this is his best-known poem.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

绿树阴浓夏日长，

lǜ shù yīn nóng xià rì cháng

Green trees, deep shade, a long summer day;

楼台倒影入池塘。

lóu tái dào yǐng rù chí táng

the towers lie upside down in the pond.

水晶帘动微风起，

shuǐ jīng lián dòng wēi fēng qǐ

The crystal curtain stirs: a breeze has come,

满架蔷薇一院香。

mǎn jià qiáng wēi yī yuàn xiāng

and roses heaped on the trellis scent the whole yard.
