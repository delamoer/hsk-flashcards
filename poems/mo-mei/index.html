导读About this poem

A painter writes a poem on his own painting of plum blossoms in black ink: they don’t need bright colours to be admired — their fresh fragrance fills the world. It is really a portrait of the painter himself.

中文解析

王冕是元代有名的画家，最擅长画梅花，而且常用水墨画，不上颜色。这首诗是写在他的梅花画上的，叫“题画诗”。第一句用了一个典故：传说书法家王羲之练字，常在池边洗笔砚，把一池水都洗黑了。王冕也姓王，所以说“我家洗砚池头树”，既幽默又点出这是墨画的梅。第二句说每一朵花都带着淡淡的墨痕。后两句是全诗的中心：这些梅花不需要别人夸它颜色好看，只要把清香之气留在天地之间。这里“咏梅”其实是“咏人”——王冕一生不愿做官，不求名利，只想保持自己的清白和气节。借梅花来说自己的志向，这叫“托物言志”。“只留清气满乾坤”今天常被用来称赞品格高洁的人。

In English

Wang Mian was a Yuan-dynasty painter famous for plum blossoms, usually painted in plain black ink without any colour. He wrote this poem on one of his own paintings — a “poem inscribed on a painting”. The first line is a playful allusion: the great calligrapher Wang Xizhi is said to have washed his brushes and inkstone in a pond so often that the whole pond turned black. Wang Mian shares his surname, so he jokes that this tree grows “by my family’s inkstone-washing pond” — which is why its blossoms are ink-coloured. The second line says every flower carries faint traces of ink. The last two lines are the heart of the poem: these blossoms don’t need anyone to praise their pretty colour; they only want to leave their clean fragrance filling heaven and earth. Of course the poem is not just about plum blossoms. Wang Mian refused official posts all his life and cared nothing for fame; the plum is himself. Using an object to express your own ideals is a common move in Chinese poetry. The last line is still quoted today to praise people of integrity.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Wang Mian (1287–1359) grew up poor as a cowherd, is said to have taught himself to paint, and became famous for his ink plum blossoms. He turned down official posts and lived in seclusion; this is the best known of his poems inscribed on paintings.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

我家洗砚池头树，

wǒ jiā xǐ yàn chí tóu shù

A tree beside my family’s inkstone-washing pond:

朵朵花开淡墨痕。

duǒ duǒ huā kāi dàn mò hén

each blossom opens with a trace of pale ink.

不要人夸好颜色，

bù yào rén kuā hǎo yán sè

It asks no one to praise its colour;

只留清气满乾坤。

zhǐ liú qīng qì mǎn qián kūn

it only leaves its clean scent to fill the world.
