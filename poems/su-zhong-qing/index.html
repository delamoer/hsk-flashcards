导读About this poem

An old soldier remembers riding to the frontier to win glory. Now his fur coat gathers dust, his hair has turned grey, the enemy is still there — and his heart is in the far-off Tianshan while his body grows old by the sea.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

陆游四十八岁时曾在南郑（今陕西汉中）的前线军中任职，这是他一生最难忘的时光。这首词是晚年在家乡回忆那段经历写的。上阕：当年我奔赴万里之外，想要建功立业，单人匹马守卫梁州。如今守关的梦已经断了，它在哪里呢？只剩下旧日的貂皮大衣，落满了灰尘。“尘暗旧貂裘”用一件衣服写出多年的闲置和失落。下阕连用三个短句：敌人还没消灭，两鬓已经像秋霜一样白了，眼泪白白地流。最后三句是全词的感叹：这一辈子谁能料到，心还在遥远的天山前线，身体却要老死在水边的家乡。“心在天山，身老沧洲”，心与身、远与近的对比，写出理想和现实的巨大落差，读来非常悲凉。

In English

At forty-seven Lu You served at the front at Nanzheng (today’s Hanzhong in Shaanxi), the most vivid months of his life. This lyric, written in old age at home, looks back on them. The first half: back then I travelled ten thousand lǐ to win a noble title, and rode alone to guard Liangzhou. Now the dream of the frontier passes is broken — where has it gone? All that remains is my old sable coat, dull with dust. One neglected piece of clothing tells us about years of being unused and forgotten. The second half begins with three short, blunt phrases: the enemy is not yet destroyed; my temples have already turned autumn-grey; my tears flow for nothing. Then the closing sigh: who could have guessed, in this life, that my heart would be in the Tianshan mountains while my body grows old by the water? The Tianshan, far in the north-west, stands for the frontier; 沧洲, a watery place, is where a retired man lives out his days. Heart against body, far against near: the gap between what he wanted and what life gave him could hardly be put more simply, or more sadly.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Lu You (1125–1210) served in 1172 on the staff of the military commissioner Wang Yan at Nanzheng, on the front line against the Jin. He was soon transferred away and never fought again; this lyric is usually dated to his long retirement at Shanyin, after 1189.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

当年万里觅封侯，匹马戍梁州。

dāng nián wàn lǐ mì fēng hóu, pǐ mǎ shù liáng zhōu

Back then I went ten thousand miles to win my honours, and rode alone to guard Liangzhou.

关河梦断何处？尘暗旧貂裘。

guān hé mèng duàn hé chù, chén àn jiù diāo qiú

Now the dream of the frontier is broken — where did it go? Dust dulls my old fur coat.

胡未灭，鬓先秋，泪空流。

hú wèi miè, bìn xiān qiū, lèi kōng liú

The enemy still stands; my hair has gone grey first. Tears fall, and change nothing.

此生谁料，心在天山，身老沧洲。

cǐ shēng shuí liào, xīn zài tiān shān, shēn lǎo cāng zhōu

Who would have thought it — my heart still in the far Tianshan, my body growing old by the water.
