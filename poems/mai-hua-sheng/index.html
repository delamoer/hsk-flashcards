导读About this poem

Three famous scenes from history — a hero’s last stand, a great river battle, a general growing old at a frontier pass — and then the poet’s real point: in every age, it is ordinary people who suffer. A short Yuan-dynasty song that ends in one long sigh.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这是元代的一首散曲小令（可以配乐演唱的短曲）。前三句一口气写了三个历史场面：一是楚汉相争时，项羽兵败乌江，美人虞姬与他生死诀别；二是三国时赤壁之战，大火烧红了江边的山；三是东汉名将班超在西域守边几十年，到老才盼回玉门关。三句结构相同，一句一个故事，这叫“鼎足对”（三句互相对仗，像鼎的三条腿）。这些都是英雄的故事，可是作者接着笔锋一转：秦汉以来，打来打去，最痛苦的是老百姓，他们像陷在泥里、掉进火里一样，这就是“生民涂炭”。最后一句只说“读书人一声长叹”，没有多说，却把对战争的厌恶、对百姓的同情，都放在这一声叹息里。从写英雄到写百姓，这是这首曲最可贵的地方。

In English

This is a sanqu, a short lyric written to be sung, popular in the Yuan dynasty. The first three lines rush through three famous scenes from history. A beauty dies by the Wu River: when Xiang Yu lost his war with the founder of the Han, his beloved Consort Yu died with him at the end — tradition places the two tragedies together. Fire once burned the Red Cliffs: the great battle of the Three Kingdoms period, when a river fleet went up in flames. A general grows old in vain at Jade Gate Pass: Ban Chao of the Eastern Han spent some thirty years guarding the far west and begged in old age to return home through the pass. The three lines have the same shape, one story each — a pattern called 鼎足对, “tripod parallelism”, like the three legs of a bronze vessel. These are all stories about heroes. Then the song turns. Since the Qin and Han, through all this fighting, the ones who really suffered were the common people, “thrown into mud and fire” (生民涂炭). The last line says only: a scholar heaves one long sigh. He does not explain; his disgust at war and his pity for ordinary people are all inside that sigh. Moving the focus from heroes to the people is what makes this little song stand out.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Zhang Kejiu (c. 1270–after 1348) was a Yuan-dynasty writer of sanqu songs who spent his life as a minor clerk, travelling widely in the south. More than 850 of his songs survive, more than any other Yuan sanqu writer. This is the second of his two songs titled “Thinking of the Past”.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

版本说明 About the text

采用通行文本。原题《卖花声·怀古》共两首，这是第二首。曲牌一般题作【中吕·卖花声】。维基文库本“赤壁”误作“赤璧”，网站用“赤壁”。“美人”一般理解为虞姬，但史书记载虞姬并非死于乌江，曲中是把项羽乌江自刎与虞姬之死合在一起写，解析里按传统说法处理。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

美人自刎乌江岸，

měi rén zì wěn wū jiāng àn

By the Wu River a beauty takes her own life;

战火曾烧赤壁山，

zhàn huǒ céng shāo chì bì shān

the flames of war once burned the Red Cliffs;

将军空老玉门关。

jiāng jūn kōng lǎo yù mén guān

a general grows old for nothing at Jade Gate Pass.

伤心秦汉，

shāng xīn qín hàn

Heartbreak through the Qin and Han —

生民涂炭，

shēng mín tú tàn

the people trampled in mud and fire;

读书人一声长叹。

dú shū rén yī shēng cháng tàn

and a scholar lets out one long sigh.
