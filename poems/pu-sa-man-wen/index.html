导读About this poem

A lady wakes late, slowly does her make-up and puts on a new silk jacket — embroidered with golden partridges flying in pairs. Pure picture, no stated feeling, and a famous example of the ornate “Among the Flowers” style.

中文解析

这首词像一组慢镜头，写一位贵族女子早上起床梳妆。上阕：阳光照在屏风上，屏风上画的重重小山金光闪闪（也有人理解为女子画的“小山眉”）；她的鬓发像云一样，快要垂到雪白、带香气的脸颊上。她懒懒地起来，慢慢地画眉毛、化妆、梳头。“懒”和“迟”两个字很关键：没有人可看，打扮也就没有心思。下阕：她插上花，用前后两面镜子照，花和脸在镜中互相映照，人比花还美。最后她穿上新的丝绸短袄，上面绣着一对一对的金色鹧鸪。词人没有一个字写她孤单，可是成双的鹧鸪一出现，读者就明白了：鸟都成双，她却一个人。这种把感情藏在画面里的写法，是温庭筠和“花间派”的特点，读的时候要慢慢体会。

In English

This lyric unfolds like a series of slow close-ups as a noblewoman gets ready in the morning. In the first stanza, sunlight flickers on the gold of a folding screen painted with layered hills (some readers take the “little hills” as her painted eyebrows instead). Her cloud-like hair is slipping down toward her fragrant, snow-white cheeks. She gets up “lazily” and does her eyebrows and make-up “late” — two small words that tell us there is no one to dress up for. In the second stanza she pins a flower in her hair and checks it with a mirror in front and a mirror behind; flower and face shine back at each other. Finally she puts on a new silk jacket embroidered with golden partridges, “pair after pair”. The poet never says she is lonely. But the moment we see birds in pairs on the dress of a woman alone, we understand. Hiding feeling inside rich, carefully painted detail is the mark of Wen Tingyun and the Huajian school. Read it slowly, like looking at a painting, and let the last image do its work.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Wen Tingyun (c. 812–866) is regarded as the founder of the Huajian (“Among the Flowers”) school of ci lyrics, known for rich colour and boudoir scenes. He is said to have written a set of Pusaman lyrics for Emperor Xuanzong’s chief minister, who presented them as his own; fourteen survive, and this is the first.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

重叠 chóng dié；鬓 bìn；腮 sāi；帖 tiē（同“贴”）；襦 rú；鹧鸪 zhè gū。“帖”机器朗读易读成 tiě，已在“朗读用字”列替换为同音的“贴”，音频需人工听验。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行版本（《花间集》所收温庭筠《菩萨蛮》十四首之一），双调四十四字。“新帖绣罗襦”有版本作“新贴绣罗襦”。“小山”有两种理解：屏风上的山或女子的眉妆，解析里两说并存。

小山重叠金明灭，

xiǎo shān chóng dié jīn míng miè

On the screen, layered hills; the gold flickers in the light.

鬓云欲度香腮雪。

bìn yún yù dù xiāng sāi xuě

A cloud of hair slips toward her fragrant, snow-white cheek.

懒起画蛾眉，

lǎn qǐ huà é méi

Slow to rise, she paints her eyebrows fine,

弄妆梳洗迟。

nòng zhuāng shū xǐ chí

and takes her time with powder, comb and water.

照花前后镜，

zhào huā qián hòu jìng

A mirror before, a mirror behind, to see the flower in her hair —

花面交相映。

huā miàn jiāo xiāng yìng

flower and face light each other up.

新帖绣罗襦，

xīn tiē xiù luó rú

She puts on a new silk jacket, freshly embroidered:

双双金鹧鸪。

shuāng shuāng jīn zhè gū

golden partridges, two by two.
