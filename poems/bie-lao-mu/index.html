导读About this poem

A poor young poet must leave home to earn a living on a snowy night. In four lines he bows to his mother, sees she has cried her eyes dry, and ends with one of the harshest lines of guilt in Chinese poetry.

中文解析

黄景仁家里很穷，为了谋生不得不常年在外漂泊。这首诗写他在一个风雪之夜告别老母亲。第一句是动作：掀开门帘，跪拜母亲，然后要上路了。“河梁”本是桥，古诗里常指送别的地方。第二句写母亲：头发白了，满怀忧愁地望着儿子，眼泪已经流干了——“泪眼枯”比“泪流满面”更让人心痛，说明她哭了很久，也送别了很多次。第三句写环境：凄凉的柴门，风雪交加的夜晚，家境的贫寒一下子就出来了。最后一句是诗人的自责：这种时候，有我这个儿子还不如没有！他不是真的不爱母亲，而是恨自己不能留在身边照顾她。这句话非常直白，也非常沉痛，所以成了写母子离别最有名的句子之一。

In English

Huang Jingren grew up poor and spent most of his short life away from home, working wherever he could. This poem captures one departure on a winter night. The first line is all action: he lifts the door curtain, kneels to his mother, and sets off for the “river bridge” — in old poetry a set phrase for the place of parting. The second line turns to her: white-haired, she watches him with worry, her eyes already dry from crying. “Tears run dry” hurts more than “tears pour down” — it suggests long weeping, and many partings before this one. The third line gives the setting in a few words: a bleak brushwood gate, wind and snow, night. We understand at once how poor the family is. Then comes the last line, a blunt cry of guilt: at a moment like this, having a son is no better than having none. He does not mean he doesn’t love her; he blames himself for leaving her alone when she needs him. The words are almost like speech, which is exactly why the line has stayed in people’s memories as one of the most painful descriptions of a son leaving his mother.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Huang Jingren (1749–1783), also known as Huang Zhongze, lost his father at four and lived in poverty, travelling from post to post as a secretary to officials. He died of illness at 34. This poem is traditionally said to have been written when he left home to seek work.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

搴 qiān（掀起）。帷 wéi。看：按七绝格律此处应为平声，读 kān；现代多读 kàn，网站暂注 kān。柴 chái。机器朗读已把“搴”“看”替换为同音字，音频需人工听验。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

搴帷拜母河梁去，

qiān wéi bài mǔ hé liáng qù

I lift the curtain, kneel to Mother, and turn toward the bridge;

白发愁看泪眼枯。

bái fà chóu kān lèi yǎn kū

white-haired, she watches me go, her eyes cried dry.

惨惨柴门风雪夜，

cǎn cǎn chái mén fēng xuě yè

A poor brushwood gate, a night of wind and snow —

此时有子不如无。

cǐ shí yǒu zǐ bù rú wú

at a time like this, she’d be better off with no son at all.
