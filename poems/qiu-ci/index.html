导读About this poem

For a thousand years Chinese poets had called autumn the sad season. Liu Yuxi disagrees: autumn beats spring — look at that crane soaring through the clouds into a clear blue sky.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

中国古诗里，秋天几乎总是悲伤的：落叶、秋风、寒冷，让人想到衰老和离别，这叫“悲秋”传统。刘禹锡偏偏唱反调。第一句先承认：自古以来，人们一到秋天就感叹寂寞冷清。第二句马上转折：我却说秋天比春天还好！为什么？后两句给出一幅画：晴朗的天空里，一只白鹤推开云层直飞上去，把我的诗兴也一起带上了蓝天。一个“排”字写出鹤冲破云层的力量，也写出诗人不服输的精神。写这首诗时，刘禹锡正被贬在偏远的朗州，处境很不好，但他没有消沉，反而借秋天表达乐观、昂扬的心情。所以这首诗不只是写景，也是在写一个人面对困难的态度。

In English

In classical Chinese poetry, autumn is almost always sad: falling leaves, cold winds and the end of the year make poets think of ageing and parting. This tradition is called 悲秋, “grieving over autumn”. Liu Yuxi deliberately goes against it. The first line admits the tradition: since ancient times, people have sighed over autumn’s loneliness and emptiness. The second line turns it around at once: but I say autumn days are better than spring mornings! Why? The last two lines answer with a picture. In a clear sky a single crane pushes through the clouds and climbs straight up — and it carries the poet’s inspiration with it, all the way into the blue. The verb 排 (“to push aside”) gives the crane real force as it breaks through the clouds, and it also shows the poet’s refusal to be beaten. When he wrote this, Liu was in exile in remote Langzhou and his career was in ruins. Instead of sinking into gloom, he used autumn to show an upbeat, fighting spirit. So the poem is not only about the season — it is about how a person chooses to face hard times.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Liu Yuxi (772–842) was a Tang poet and official known for his wit and resilience. He wrote this pair of autumn poems in exile in Langzhou (today’s Changde, Hunan), where he was sent in 805 after a failed reform movement.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

“春朝”的“朝”读 zhāo（早晨）；“一鹤”的“鹤”读 hè；“碧霄”读 bì xiāo。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

自古逢秋悲寂寥，

zì gǔ féng qiū bēi jì liáo

Since ancient times, autumn has meant loneliness and sorrow;

我言秋日胜春朝。

wǒ yán qiū rì shèng chūn zhāo

I say an autumn day beats any spring morning.

晴空一鹤排云上，

qíng kōng yī hè pái yún shàng

In a clear sky a crane breaks through the clouds

便引诗情到碧霄。

biàn yǐn shī qíng dào bì xiāo

and lifts my poetry with it into the blue.
