Song of Yan

导读About this poem

On a cold autumn night a wife alone at home plays a sad tune, watches the Milky Way and asks the stars why lovers must be kept apart. It is the oldest complete poem in China written entirely in seven-character lines.

第一段Part 1
01
秋风萧瑟天气凉，草木摇落露为霜。

直译LiteralThe autumn wind is bleak, the weather cold; grass and trees shake and fall, dew turns to frost.

意译MeaningThe autumn wind blows thin and cold; leaves shake loose, and dew turns into frost.

02
群燕辞归雁南翔，念君客游思断肠。

直译LiteralFlocks of swallows take leave to return, wild geese fly south; thinking of you travelling far, my longing breaks my heart.

意译MeaningSwallows leave for home, the geese fly south — and I think of you far away until my heart breaks.

03
慊慊思归恋故乡，君何淹留寄他方？

直译LiteralRestless, you must long to return and miss your home; why do you linger, lodging in another place?

意译MeaningSurely you ache for home as well — so why do you linger in a stranger’s land?

第二段Part 2
04
贱妾茕茕守空房，忧来思君不敢忘，不觉泪下沾衣裳。

直译LiteralI, your humble wife, alone and lonely, keep the empty room; when sorrow comes I think of you and dare not forget; unaware, tears fall and wet my clothes.

意译MeaningAlone, I keep our empty room; when sadness comes I think of you — I could never forget — and tears soak my clothes before I know it.

05
援琴鸣弦发清商，短歌微吟不能长。

直译LiteralI take the zither, sound the strings, and play the qingshang mode; a short song, softly sung, cannot go on long.

意译MeaningI take up my zither and pluck a mournful tune, but my soft song breaks off before it’s done.

第三段Part 3
06
明月皎皎照我床，星汉西流夜未央。

直译LiteralThe bright moon, pure white, shines on my bed; the Milky Way flows west, the night is not yet over.

意译MeaningWhite moonlight falls across my bed; the River of Stars drifts west, and still the night goes on.

07
牵牛织女遥相望，尔独何辜限河梁？

直译LiteralThe Herd-boy and the Weaver Girl gaze at each other from afar; what crime have you alone committed, to be barred by the river?

意译MeaningHerd-boy and Weaver Girl gaze at each other across the sky — what did you do wrong, to be kept apart by that river?

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗用一位妻子的口吻，写她在秋夜思念远在北方（古代的燕地）的丈夫。开头先写秋景：秋风萧瑟，草木凋零，露水结成了霜，燕子和大雁都往南飞回去了——鸟都知道回家，人却还没回来。她猜丈夫也一定想家，可为什么还久久留在外地？接着写她自己：一个人守着空房，想着他，不知不觉泪水沾湿了衣裳。她拿起琴想排解忧愁，可弹出的是悲凉的“清商”调，歌声短促，唱不下去。夜深了，月光照到床前，银河向西转，夜还没有过完。最后她抬头看见天上的牵牛星和织女星隔着银河相望，忍不住问：你们犯了什么罪，要被隔在河的两边？这一问既是问星星，也是问自己的命运。全诗每句都押韵，一句七个字，是现存最早的完整七言诗，在诗歌史上很重要。

In English

The poem speaks in the voice of a wife whose husband is far away in the north (the old region of Yan). It opens with autumn: a bleak wind, leaves falling, dew turning to frost, swallows and wild geese flying south. Even the birds know when to go home — but he hasn’t come back. She imagines he must be homesick too, so why does he stay away so long? Then the poem turns to her: alone in an empty room, thinking of him until tears wet her clothes before she notices. She picks up her zither to ease the pain, but what comes out is the mournful qingshang mode; her song breaks off, too short to finish. Moonlight reaches her bed, the Milky Way swings west, and the night still isn’t over. Finally she looks up at the Herd-boy and Weaver Girl stars, kept apart by the river of stars, and asks them what crime they committed to be separated like this — a question really aimed at her own fate. Every line rhymes and every line has seven characters: this is the earliest complete poem in China written that way, a milestone for the seven-character form that later Tang poets made famous.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Cao Pi (187–226), Cao Cao’s son, ended the Han dynasty and became the first emperor of Wei in 220. He was also a fine poet and wrote one of the first works of literary criticism in China. “Song of Yan” was an old yuefu tune title; Yan was the northern frontier region where many men were sent to serve.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

萧瑟 xiāo sè；慊慊 qiàn qiàn（心里不满足、怨恨的样子）；淹留 yān liú；茕茕 qióng qióng；援琴 yuán；夜未央 yāng；何辜 gū；衣裳 cháng（古诗中“裳”读 cháng，指下身的衣服，并与全诗押韵；今天口语“衣裳”读 yī shang）。网站按 cháng 注音。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行文本（《曹丕集》、古诗文网通行本），共十五句，句句押韵，网站两句一行，“贱妾……衣裳”三句合为一行。重要异文：《文选》作“群燕辞归鹄南翔”“念君客游多思肠”；“君何淹留寄他方”一作“何为淹留寄他方”。曹丕《燕歌行》共两首，这是第一首。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

秋风萧瑟天气凉，草木摇落露为霜。

qiū fēng xiāo sè tiān qì liáng, cǎo mù yáo luò lù wéi shuāng

The autumn wind blows thin and cold; leaves shake loose, and dew turns into frost.

群燕辞归雁南翔，念君客游思断肠。

qún yàn cí guī yàn nán xiáng, niàn jūn kè yóu sī duàn cháng

Swallows leave for home, the geese fly south — and I think of you far away until my heart breaks.

慊慊思归恋故乡，君何淹留寄他方？

qiàn qiàn sī guī liàn gù xiāng, jūn hé yān liú jì tā fāng

Surely you ache for home as well — so why do you linger in a stranger’s land?

贱妾茕茕守空房，忧来思君不敢忘，不觉泪下沾衣裳。

jiàn qiè qióng qióng shǒu kōng fáng, yōu lái sī jūn bù gǎn wàng, bù jué lèi xià zhān yī cháng

Alone, I keep our empty room; when sadness comes I think of you — I could never forget — and tears soak my clothes before I know it.

援琴鸣弦发清商，短歌微吟不能长。

yuán qín míng xián fā qīng shāng, duǎn gē wēi yín bù néng cháng

I take up my zither and pluck a mournful tune, but my soft song breaks off before it’s done.

明月皎皎照我床，星汉西流夜未央。

míng yuè jiǎo jiǎo zhào wǒ chuáng, xīng hàn xī liú yè wèi yāng

White moonlight falls across my bed; the River of Stars drifts west, and still the night goes on.

牵牛织女遥相望，尔独何辜限河梁？

qiān niú zhī nǚ yáo xiāng wàng, ěr dú hé gū xiàn hé liáng

Herd-boy and Weaver Girl gaze at each other across the sky — what did you do wrong, to be kept apart by that river?
