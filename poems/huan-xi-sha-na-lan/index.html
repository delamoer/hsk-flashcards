导读About this poem

A young widower stands alone in the autumn wind and remembers small, happy moments with his late wife. The last line — “back then, I thought it was all just ordinary” — is one of the most quoted lines about loss in Chinese.

中文解析

这是纳兰性德怀念亡妻的词。上阕写现在：秋天的西风吹来，只有他一个人觉得冷，有谁关心呢？黄叶萧萧地落，他关上窗子，在夕阳里站着，想起过去的事。下阕写回忆，用了两个小故事：一是妻子在他喝醉后睡得很沉时，轻轻地不去惊动他；二是用了宋代李清照夫妇的典故（引用古代的故事）——两人比赛记书，说出某件事在哪本书哪一页，赢的人先喝茶，常常笑得把茶泼了，满身茶香。这些都是夫妻之间很平常的小事。最后一句“当时只道是寻常”是全词的重心：当时只觉得这些再普通不过，现在人不在了，才知道那是最珍贵的幸福。这句话说出了很多人的共同感受，所以流传很广。

In English

Nalan Xingde wrote this for his wife, who died young. The first half is the present. The west wind of autumn is blowing, and he alone feels its chill — who cares about that now? Yellow leaves rustle down; he shuts the window and stands in the last light of the setting sun, lost in memories. The second half is the past, told through two tiny scenes. In one, he has drunk too much and is sleeping heavily in spring, and she takes care not to wake him. In the other, they play a game borrowed from the famous Song-dynasty couple Li Qingzhao and her husband: one names a fact, the other must say which book and which page it is on, and the winner drinks tea first — they laughed so hard the tea spilled and the room smelled of it. Bringing in an old story like this is called using an allusion (diǎngù). Neither scene is dramatic; that is the point. The final line, 当时只道是寻常, turns everything: “at the time, I thought it was only ordinary.” Only now, with her gone, does he understand that those ordinary days were the happiness. It is a feeling almost everyone recognises, which is why the line is so often quoted.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Nalan Xingde (1655–1685) was a Manchu noble, an imperial bodyguard and the finest ci poet of the Qing dynasty. His wife, Lady Lu, died in 1677 after about three years of marriage; many of his most moving poems mourn her, and this one is traditionally read that way.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

谁念西风独自凉，

shuí niàn xī fēng dú zì liáng

Who cares that I stand alone and cold in the west wind?

萧萧黄叶闭疏窗，

xiāo xiāo huáng yè bì shū chuāng

Yellow leaves rustle down; I shut the lattice window,

沉思往事立残阳。

chén sī wǎng shì lì cán yáng

and stand in the last of the sunlight, thinking of the past.

被酒莫惊春睡重，

bèi jiǔ mò jīng chūn shuì zhòng

When I slept off the wine on spring days, she let me sleep;

赌书消得泼茶香，

dǔ shū xiāo dé pō chá xiāng

we played at quoting books, and laughed until the tea spilled.

当时只道是寻常。

dāng shí zhǐ dào shì xún cháng

Back then, I thought it was all just ordinary.
