子夜歌（人生愁恨何能免）

Zǐ Yè Gē
Midnight Song: Who in This Life Escapes Sorrow?
李煜Lǐ Yù · Li Yu宋 Song
体裁Form
词 Cí — lyric to a tune

导读About this poem

A captive king dreams he has gone home, then wakes in tears. Everyone has sorrows, he says — but none like his. Plain words, a dream, a memory of an autumn view, and the feeling that his whole past was only a dream.

上阕First stanza
01
人生愁恨何能免，销魂独我情何限！

直译LiteralIn life, how can sorrow and regret be avoided? Soul-melted, only I — how limitless my feeling!

意译MeaningWho in this life escapes sorrow and regret? Yet my heartbreak alone has no end.

02
故国梦重归，觉来双泪垂。

直译LiteralTo my old country, in a dream, I returned again; waking, a pair of tears fall.

意译MeaningIn a dream I went home again; I woke with tears running down both cheeks.

下阕Second stanza
03
高楼谁与上？长记秋晴望。

直译LiteralThe tall tower — with whom to climb it? I always remember gazing out on a clear autumn day.

意译MeaningWho will climb the high tower with me now? I still remember that clear autumn view.

04
往事已成空，还如一梦中。

直译LiteralPast events have already become empty, still as if within a dream.

意译MeaningAll that is past has come to nothing — as if it happened in a dream.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首词是李煜亡国被俘以后写的。开头两句像是自言自语：人这一生，谁能没有愁和恨呢？可是为什么偏偏只有我伤心到这种地步，愁苦没有边际？“销魂”就是伤心得像丢了魂一样。接着写原因：他在梦里又回到了故国，醒来以后，两行眼泪流了下来。梦里有多快乐，醒来就有多痛苦。下阕写回忆：从前在高楼上看秋天晴朗的景色，那时有人陪着一起登楼；现在“谁与上”——还有谁陪我登楼呢？那一幕却一直记在心里。最后两句说，过去的一切都已经成空，就好像只是一场梦。开头写梦，结尾又说往事像梦，真实的梦和人生如梦前后呼应。全词没有难懂的典故，用最平常的话说出了最深的悲哀。

In English

Li Yu wrote this after losing his kingdom and becoming a prisoner of the Song. The opening sounds like a man talking to himself: who in this life can avoid sorrow and regret? So why am I the one so heartbroken, with grief that has no limit? 销魂 literally means “to have one’s soul melt away” — to be shattered by sadness. Then comes the reason: in a dream he went back to his old kingdom, and on waking two lines of tears ran down his face. The happier the dream, the worse the waking. The second half turns to memory. Once he climbed a tall tower to enjoy a clear autumn view, with others beside him; now, who would climb it with him? Yet that scene stays with him. The last two lines say that everything in the past has come to nothing — it is all like a single dream. The poem begins with a real dream and ends by calling his whole past a dream, so the two frame each other. There are no difficult allusions here: the plainest words carry the deepest grief.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Li Yu (937–978) was the last ruler of the Southern Tang. After surrendering to the Song in 975 he lived as a captive in Kaifeng, and his lyrics from these years are filled with memories of his “old country” (故国). 子夜歌 is another name for the tune 菩萨蛮.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

梦重归 chóng（再次）；觉来 jué（醒来，不读 jiào）；长记 cháng；谁 shuí（诗词中常读 shuí，口语 shéi 亦可）；销魂 xiāo hún。“还如一梦中”的“还”按“仍然、仿佛”义读 hái，也有读本注 huán，网站暂采用 hái。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行文本。“销魂独我情何限”一作“销魂独我情无限”；“故国梦重归”各本相同。《子夜歌》即《菩萨蛮》的别名，有的选本题作《菩萨蛮》。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

人生愁恨何能免，销魂独我情何限！

rén shēng chóu hèn hé néng miǎn, xiāo hún dú wǒ qíng hé xiàn

Who in this life escapes sorrow and regret? Yet my heartbreak alone has no end.

故国梦重归，觉来双泪垂。

gù guó mèng chóng guī, jué lái shuāng lèi chuí

In a dream I went home again; I woke with tears running down both cheeks.

高楼谁与上？长记秋晴望。

gāo lóu shuí yǔ shàng, cháng jì qiū qíng wàng

Who will climb the high tower with me now? I still remember that clear autumn view.

往事已成空，还如一梦中。

wǎng shì yǐ chéng kōng, hái rú yī mèng zhōng

All that is past has come to nothing — as if it happened in a dream.
