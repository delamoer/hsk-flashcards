导读About this poem

Said to be Tang Wan’s answer to Lu You’s lyric on the garden wall. She has had to hide her grief from everyone: the refrain is “Hard, hard, hard” — and then “Hide it, hide it, hide it.”

世情薄，人情恶，雨送黄昏花易落。

直译LiteralThe ways of the world are thin, human feelings cruel; rain sees off the dusk, flowers fall easily.

意译MeaningThe world is cold and people are cruel; evening rain, and the flowers fall so easily.

02
晓风干，泪痕残。

直译LiteralThe dawn wind dries them; tear traces remain.

意译MeaningThe dawn wind dries my face, but the tear stains stay.

03
欲笺心事，独语斜阑。

直译LiteralI want to write my heart on paper; alone I talk, leaning on the slanting rail.

意译MeaningI want to write you what I feel, but only murmur to myself, leaning on the rail.

04
难、难、难！

直译LiteralHard, hard, hard!

意译MeaningSo hard — so hard — so hard.

下阕Second stanza
05
人成各，今非昨，病魂常似秋千索。

直译LiteralWe have become separate; today is not yesterday; my sick soul is often like a swing’s rope.

意译MeaningWe belong to others now; today is not the past. My sick heart swings like a swing-rope, back and forth.

06
角声寒，夜阑珊。

直译LiteralThe horn’s sound is cold; the night is waning.

意译MeaningA cold bugle call; the night is wearing thin.

07
怕人寻问，咽泪装欢。

直译LiteralAfraid people will ask, I swallow my tears and pretend to be happy.

意译MeaningAfraid that someone will ask, I swallow my tears and smile.

08
瞒、瞒、瞒！

直译LiteralHide, hide, hide!

意译MeaningHide it — hide it — hide it.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

传说唐琬看到陆游在沈园墙上题的《钗头凤》，也用同一个词牌和了这首，不久就郁郁而终。上阕：世态人情凉薄又险恶，就像黄昏时的雨，花很容易被打落——她自己就是那朵被风雨摧残的花。早上的风吹干了泪水，泪痕还在；想把心事写下来，却只能靠着栏杆自言自语。“难、难、难”。下阕：我们已经各自成家，今天不再是从前；我的病中魂魄像秋千的绳子一样晃来晃去，没有着落。夜里号角声凄寒，夜快尽了；怕别人问起，只好咽下眼泪，装出高兴的样子。“瞒、瞒、瞒”——这是全词最心酸的地方：她连哭都不能让人看见。和陆游那首对读，一个悔恨，一个隐忍，句式相同，声音相应，所以常被放在一起。

In English

Legend says that Tang Wan saw the lyric Lu You had written on the wall of the Shen Garden and answered it with this one, to the same tune — and died of grief not long after. The first half: the world is cold and people are cruel, like evening rain that easily knocks the blossoms down; she is that battered flower. The morning wind has dried her tears, but the stains remain. She wants to write down what is in her heart, yet can only lean on the railing and talk to herself. “Hard, hard, hard.” The second half: they each belong to someone else now, and today is not yesterday. Her sick spirit swings back and forth like the rope of a swing, with nothing to hold on to. The night bugle sounds cold; the night is nearly over. Afraid that someone will ask, she swallows her tears and puts on a cheerful face. “Hide it, hide it, hide it” — the saddest part of the poem: she is not even allowed to be seen crying. Read beside Lu You’s lyric, one voice is full of regret and the other of endurance, in matching lines that answer each other — which is why the two are always printed together.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Tang Wan (dates unknown, 12th century) was Lu You’s first wife, divorced at his mother’s insistence. Song sources quote only two lines of a reply by her; the full text appears much later, so many scholars think it was completed or written by someone else. The attribution is traditional.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

晓风干 gān；欲笺 jiān；斜阑 xié lán；人成各 gè；秋千索 suǒ；角声 jiǎo；阑珊 lán shān；咽泪 yàn（吞下）；瞒 mán。“干”“咽”机器朗读易误，已在朗读用字中替换同音字，音频需人工听验。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行文本。宋人笔记只记“世情薄，人情恶”等残句，全词见于后世笔记，归属存疑，网站署“唐琬（传）”。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

世情薄，人情恶，雨送黄昏花易落。

shì qíng bó, rén qíng è, yǔ sòng huáng hūn huā yì luò

The world is cold and people are cruel; evening rain, and the flowers fall so easily.

晓风干，泪痕残。

xiǎo fēng gān, lèi hén cán

The dawn wind dries my face, but the tear stains stay.

欲笺心事，独语斜阑。

yù jiān xīn shì, dú yǔ xié lán

I want to write you what I feel, but only murmur to myself, leaning on the rail.

难、难、难！

nán, nán, nán

So hard — so hard — so hard.

人成各，今非昨，病魂常似秋千索。

rén chéng gè, jīn fēi zuó, bìng hún cháng sì qiū qiān suǒ

We belong to others now; today is not the past. My sick heart swings like a swing-rope, back and forth.

角声寒，夜阑珊。

jiǎo shēng hán, yè lán shān

A cold bugle call; the night is wearing thin.

怕人寻问，咽泪装欢。

pà rén xún wèn, yàn lèi zhuāng huān

Afraid that someone will ask, I swallow my tears and smile.

瞒、瞒、瞒！

mán, mán, mán

Hide it — hide it — hide it.
