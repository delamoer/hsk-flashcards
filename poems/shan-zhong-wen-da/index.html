导读About this poem

Someone asks Li Bai why he lives up in the green mountains. He only smiles — and answers with a picture of peach blossoms drifting downstream into another world.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗用一问一答的形式写隐居山中的快乐。第一句是别人问：你为什么住在这青山里？第二句是回答——其实没有回答，只是笑笑，心里自在悠闲。为什么不回答？因为这种快乐说不清楚，说出来反而没意思了。第三四句用景物代替回答：桃花落在流水上，远远地漂走了，这里另有一片天地，不是人间。“桃花流水”让人想起陶渊明《桃花源记》里的世外桃源。诗人用“不答”来答，用画面代替道理，读者自己去体会，这就是中国诗常说的“言有尽而意无穷”——话说完了，意思还没完。语言轻松自然，像随口说出来一样。

In English

The poem is a tiny conversation about living in the mountains. Line one is the question: why do you choose to live up in these green hills? Line two is the answer — or rather, no answer: the poet just smiles, his heart at ease. Why not explain? Because some kinds of happiness can’t be put into words; explaining would spoil them. So the last two lines answer with a picture instead: peach petals ride the stream far away into the distance; here is a different world, not the world of ordinary people. Peach blossoms on flowing water would remind any Chinese reader of Tao Yuanming’s “Peach Blossom Spring”, the story of a hidden paradise found by following petals upstream. Answering by not answering, and giving an image instead of a reason, leaves room for readers to feel it for themselves — a quality Chinese critics praised as “the words end, but the meaning goes on”. The tone is light and offhand, as if Li Bai tossed it off with a grin.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Li Bai (701–762) spent time as a young man living in the mountains near Anlu (in today’s Hubei), reading and studying Daoism. The poem is usually dated to those years; an alternative title is “Answering Ordinary People in the Mountains” (山中答俗人).

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

问余何意栖碧山，

wèn yú hé yì qī bì shān

You ask me why I make my home among green hills;

笑而不答心自闲。

xiào ér bù dá xīn zì xián

I smile and say nothing; my heart is at peace.

桃花流水窅然去，

táo huā liú shuǐ yǎo rán qù

Peach petals float off on the stream, far out of sight —

别有天地非人间。

bié yǒu tiān dì fēi rén jiān

this is another world, not the one where people live.
