Eight Beats of Ganzhou (Evening Rain over the River)

导读About this poem

A traveller stands in a tower as autumn rain clears over the river, and everything he sees turns into homesickness. Then the poem flips: he imagines the woman at home, watching the river for his boat.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这是柳永写羁旅（长期在外漂泊）的名篇。上阕写景：一场傍晚的秋雨洗过江天，冷风越吹越紧，山河冷清，夕阳正照着他站的楼。花叶凋零，只有长江水默默向东流——“无语”二字，把江水写得像人一样沉默。下阕抒情：他不敢登高远望，因为一望就想家；又问自己，这些年为什么苦苦留在外地？最妙的是“想佳人”几句：他不直接说自己想她，而是想象她在楼上一次次看江上的船，以为是他回来了，又一次次失望。最后再回到自己：她怎么知道，我此刻也正靠着栏杆发愁呢？这种从对方写起、两地相望的写法，让思念更深。据说苏轼称赞“霜风凄紧，关河冷落，残照当楼”三句不比唐诗最好的句子差。

In English

Liu Yong spent much of his life on the road, and this is his best-known poem about it. The first half is all scenery: an evening shower has rinsed the sky over the river, the frosty wind grows sharper, the land lies cold and empty, and the last light of the sun falls on the tower where he stands. Flowers and leaves are fading; only the Yangtze keeps flowing east, “without a word” — the river is as silent as a person holding back his feelings. The second half turns inward. He can’t bear to climb high and look into the distance, because looking means thinking of home. Why has he stayed away so long? Then comes the most famous move: instead of saying he misses her, he imagines her at her window, watching boats on the horizon and thinking, again and again, that one of them is his. The last lines swing back to him: how could she know that at this very moment he is leaning on a railing, just as sad? Writing a feeling from both ends of the distance is a favourite technique of Chinese poets. Su Shi is said to have praised the lines about the frosty wind and the setting sun as equal to the best of Tang poetry.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Liu Yong (c. 984 – c. 1053) failed the civil-service exam several times and made his name writing song lyrics for entertainers in the capital, Kaifeng. He passed late in life and spent his career in minor posts far from home, which is the world of this poem.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

领字“渐”读 jiàn。“是处”“倚阑干处”的“处”读 chù。“红衰”的“衰”读 shuāi。“当楼”的“当”读 dāng（正对着）。颙望 yóng wàng（颙是生僻字）。“恁”读 nèn（如此、这样），不读 rèn。“归思”的“思”按统编教材读 sī；旧读 sì（名词，心绪）。“争知我”的“争”同“怎”，仍读 zhēng。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行文本（《乐章集》，高中语文教材选文）。“惟有长江水”有的本子作“唯有”；“倚阑干处”有的作“倚栏杆处”，意思相同。按句读分行，上下阕各六行。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

对潇潇暮雨洒江天，

duì xiāo xiāo mù yǔ sǎ jiāng tiān

Before me, a soft evening rain sweeps across the river and the sky,

一番洗清秋。

yī fān xǐ qīng qiū

and leaves the autumn rinsed and clear.

渐霜风凄紧，

jiàn shuāng fēng qī jǐn

Little by little the frosty wind bites harder;

关河冷落，残照当楼。

guān hé lěng luò, cán zhào dāng lóu

the hills and rivers lie bare, and the last of the sun falls on my tower.

是处红衰翠减，苒苒物华休。

shì chù hóng shuāi cuì jiǎn, rǎn rǎn wù huá xiū

Everywhere the reds are fading, the greens are thinning; slowly the year’s bright things wind down.

惟有长江水，无语东流。

wéi yǒu cháng jiāng shuǐ, wú yǔ dōng liú

Only the Yangtze keeps going, flowing east without a word.

不忍登高临远，

bù rěn dēng gāo lín yuǎn

I can’t bear to climb up and gaze into the distance —

望故乡渺邈，归思难收。

wàng gù xiāng miǎo miǎo, guī sī nán shōu

home is so far away, and once I look, the longing to go back won’t stop.

叹年来踪迹，何事苦淹留？

tàn nián lái zōng jì, hé shì kǔ yān liú

I sigh at where my years have taken me — why do I stay away so long?

想佳人妆楼颙望，

xiǎng jiā rén zhuāng lóu yóng wàng

I picture her at her window, looking out and waiting,

误几回、天际识归舟。

wù jǐ huí, tiān jì shí guī zhōu

how many times has she seen a far-off sail and thought, at last, it’s him?

争知我，倚阑干处，正恁凝愁！

zhēng zhī wǒ, yǐ lán gān chù, zhèng nèn níng chóu

How could she know that I, leaning on a railing far away, am just as lost in sorrow?
