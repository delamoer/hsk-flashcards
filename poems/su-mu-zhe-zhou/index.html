苏幕遮（燎沉香）

Sū Mù Zhē
Su Mu Zhe (Burning Aloeswood Incense)
周邦彦Zhōu Bāngyàn · Zhou Bangyan宋 Song
体裁Form
词 Cí — lyric to a tune
词牌Tune
苏幕遮 the tune name, not the title
难度Level
挑战 Advanced
主题Themes
思乡 Homesickness

导读About this poem

A summer morning in the capital: birds chatter under the eaves, and lotus leaves lift one by one in the breeze — and the sight sends the poet home, in a dream, to the lotus ponds of the south.

意译Meaningfresh and round on the water, one by one the lotus leaves rise in the breeze.

直译LiteralWith a small oar and a light boat, in dreams I enter the lotus shore.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首词写夏天的早晨引起的思乡之情。上阕写景：点上沉香驱散闷热潮湿的暑气；鸟雀叫着天晴了，天刚亮就在屋檐下探头探脑地说话。最有名的是后三句：初升的太阳晒干了荷叶上昨夜的雨珠，水面上的荷叶又清新又圆润，一片片在风中挺立起来。王国维说这几句“真能得荷之神理”——写出了荷花荷叶真正的神态。“举”字把荷叶写活了，好像它们在风里一个个抬起头来。下阕由荷花想到故乡：故乡那么远，什么时候才能回去？我家在江南吴地，却长久地客居京城。不知道家乡五月的渔夫朋友还记得我吗？我在梦里划着小船，划进了开满荷花的水边。从眼前的荷到梦中的荷，景和情连得很自然。

In English

A summer morning sets off this lyric of homesickness. The first stanza is the scene: incense smoke drives off the sticky heat; birds chirp that the sky has cleared, peeping and chattering under the eaves at dawn. The famous lines follow: the early sun dries last night’s rain on the lotus leaves, the leaves lie fresh and round on the water, and one by one they lift up in the wind. The scholar Wang Guowei said these lines truly capture the spirit of the lotus. The single verb 举 (“to lift”) brings the leaves to life, as if each one were raising its head. In the second stanza the lotus leads the poet home: home is so far away — when can he go back? His family lives in the south, in the old land of Wu, yet he has stayed in the capital as a guest for years. Do his fishermen friends back home remember him this fifth month? In a dream he paddles a little boat into a pond full of lotus. From the lotus in front of him to the lotus in his dream, scene and feeling flow together.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Zhou Bangyan (1056–1121) came from Qiantang (today’s Hangzhou) and spent long years in the Northern Song capital Bianjing; he was a gifted musician who headed the court music office. He is thought to have written this as a young man living in the capital; “Chang’an” in the poem stands for the capital in general.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

燎 liáo（烧）；溽暑 rù shǔ；干宿雨 gān sù yǔ（干：晒干；宿：隔夜）；相忆 xiāng；小楫 jí；芙蓉浦 pǔ。机器朗读“溽、干、宿、楫”容易读错，已在“朗读用字”列换成同音字，音频需人工听验。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行文本（《清真集》）。“侵晓窥檐语”无异文；“家住吴门”的吴门，泛指江南吴地。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

燎沉香，消溽暑。

liáo chén xiāng, xiāo rù shǔ

I burn aloeswood incense against the sticky heat.

鸟雀呼晴，侵晓窥檐语。

niǎo què hū qíng, qīn xiǎo kuī yán yǔ

Birds cry that the sky is clear, peeking and chattering under the eaves at dawn.

叶上初阳干宿雨，

yè shàng chū yáng gān sù yǔ

The early sun dries last night’s rain from the leaves;

水面清圆，一一风荷举。

shuǐ miàn qīng yuán, yī yī fēng hé jǔ

fresh and round on the water, one by one the lotus leaves rise in the breeze.

故乡遥，何日去？

gù xiāng yáo, hé rì qù

Home is so far — when will I go back?

家住吴门，久作长安旅。

jiā zhù wú mén, jiǔ zuò cháng ān lǚ

My family lives in the south; for years I’ve been a guest in the capital.

五月渔郎相忆否？

wǔ yuè yú láng xiāng yì fǒu

Do the fishermen back home remember me this fifth month?

小楫轻舟，梦入芙蓉浦。

xiǎo jí qīng zhōu, mèng rù fú róng pǔ

With a little oar and a light boat, I dream my way into the lotus pond.
