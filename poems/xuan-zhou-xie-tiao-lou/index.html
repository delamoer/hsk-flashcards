导读About this poem

At a farewell party in a high tower, Li Bai pours out his frustration, soars into dreams of grabbing the moon, then crashes back down. It contains one of the most quoted lines about sorrow in Chinese: “Cut water with a knife, and it flows on; drink to drown your grief, and the grief grows.”

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这是李白在宣州谢朓楼上为族叔李云送行时写的诗。开头两句很长，像一声叹息：抛弃我离去的，是留不住的昨天；扰乱我心情的，是今天这么多烦恼。可接下来情绪一转：万里长风送着南飞的秋雁，面对这样的景色，正好在高楼上痛快喝酒。然后他夸对方，也夸自己：你的文章有汉代的风骨，我的诗像谢朓一样清新俊逸（谢朓是南朝诗人，这座楼就是他建的）。两人都满怀豪情，想飞上青天去摘明月。可是理想和现实的距离太大，心情又跌下来：“抽刀断水水更流，举杯销愁愁更愁。”用刀砍水，水照样流；用酒浇愁，愁反而更多——这个比喻新鲜又准确，成了千古名句。最后诗人说：人活在世上不如意，不如明天散开头发，划着小船去江湖上漂流。全诗情绪大起大落，正是李白豪放的风格。

In English

Li Bai wrote this at a farewell party for a clansman, Li Yun, in Xie Tiao’s Tower in Xuanzhou. It opens with two long, sighing lines: what has left me is yesterday, which cannot be kept; what troubles my heart is today, so full of worries. Then the mood lifts: a wind blowing ten thousand miles carries the autumn geese south — a perfect sight to drink to in a high tower. He praises his friend and himself: your writing has the strength of the Han masters, and my poems are as fresh as those of Xie Tiao, the fifth-century poet who built this very tower. Both of them are full of soaring feelings, wanting to fly up to the sky and take hold of the moon. But the dream is too far from reality, and he falls back to earth: “Cut water with a knife, and it only flows on; raise a cup to drown your grief, and the grief only grows.” The comparison is fresh and exact, and it is still quoted all the time. In the end he decides that, since life in this world goes against his wishes, tomorrow he will let down his hair and drift away in a little boat. The wild rise and fall of feeling is Li Bai at his most typical.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Li Bai (701–762) is traditionally said to have written this in Xuanzhou (in today’s Anhui) around 753, about ten years after leaving the court in Chang’an. Some versions give a different title, and scholars disagree about who the farewell was for.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

朓 tiǎo；饯别 jiàn；校书 jiào shū（不读 xiào）；酣 hān；蓬莱 péng lái；中间 jiān；清发 fā；俱 jù；逸兴 xìng；壮思 sī（旧读 sì，网站按今音）；称意 chèn（不读 chēng）；明朝 zhāo；散发 sàn fà（披散头发）；扁舟 piān zhōu（不读 biǎn）；更流、更愁 gèng。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行文本。“欲上青天览明月”的“览”，一些选本作“揽”（摘取），意思更直观；“举杯销愁”一作“举杯消愁”。题目一作《陪侍御叔华登楼歌》，网站用通行题目。文本与维基文库本一致。
