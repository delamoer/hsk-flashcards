游园不值

Yóu Yuán Bù Zhí
Visiting a Garden, Finding No One Home
叶绍翁Yè Shàowēng · Ye Shaoweng宋 Song

导读About this poem

The poet knocks at a friend’s garden gate; nobody answers. A little disappointing — until he looks up and sees one branch of red apricot blossom reaching over the wall. Spring can’t be locked in.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

题目“游园不值”的意思是：去游园，却没遇到主人，没能进去。前两句写诗人去朋友的花园，大概是主人心疼门前的青苔，怕被木鞋的鞋齿踩坏，所以轻轻敲柴门，敲了很久也没人开。本来有点扫兴，可是后两句一下子转了：满园的春色是关不住的，一枝红杏已经伸出墙来。诗人虽然没进园子，却从这一枝红杏想象到了园里满满的春天，失望变成了惊喜。这种“以小见大”（从一个小东西看到整体）的写法，让短短四句很有画面感。“春色满园关不住，一枝红杏出墙来”今天也比喻美好的、新生的东西挡也挡不住。

In English

The title means “visiting a garden but not getting in” — the owner wasn’t home. In the first two lines the poet arrives at a friend’s garden. Perhaps, he jokes, the owner is protecting the green moss by the gate from the teeth of visitors’ wooden clogs, because he knocks gently on the brushwood gate for a long time and no one opens it. It is a small letdown. Then the last two lines turn everything around: a whole garden of spring cannot be shut in — one branch of red apricot blossom has pushed out over the wall. He never gets inside, but from that single branch he can picture the spring filling the garden, and disappointment becomes delight. Seeing the whole through one small detail gives these four lines a vivid, cheerful picture. The last couplet is still quoted to mean that something fresh and full of life cannot be held back.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Ye Shaoweng (dates unknown) lived in the Southern Song around the early 13th century and spent much of his life in seclusion near West Lake in Hangzhou. Little else is known about him; this poem is by far his most famous.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

版本说明 About the text

采用统编小学语文教材文本。异文：“小扣”一作“小叩”。此诗由陆游《马上作》“杨柳不遮春色断，一枝红杏出墙头”化出，后来居上。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

应怜屐齿印苍苔，

yīng lián jī chǐ yìn cāng tái

Perhaps he fears my wooden clogs would mark his moss —

小扣柴扉久不开。

xiǎo kòu chái fēi jiǔ bù kāi

I tap and tap at the wooden gate, but no one comes.

春色满园关不住，

chūn sè mǎn yuán guān bù zhù

Yet a garden full of spring cannot be locked away:

一枝红杏出墙来。

yī zhī hóng xìng chū qiáng lái

one branch of red apricot blossom leans out over the wall.
