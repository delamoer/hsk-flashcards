导读About this poem

A cicada sips clear dew high in a tree, and its song carries far — not because the wind helps it, but because it sits up high. Four lines about earning respect through character.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这是一首咏蝉的诗。第一句写蝉的样子：它垂着像帽带一样的触须，喝着清凉的露水。古人以为蝉只喝露水，所以觉得它干净、高洁。第二句写声音：蝉声从稀疏的梧桐树里传出来，连绵不断。后两句是全诗的道理：蝉的声音能传得很远，是因为它站得高，并不是借了秋风的力量。表面写蝉，其实写人：一个人只要品格高尚，自然会有好名声，不需要靠别人的权势或外力来抬高自己。这种借写东西来表达自己志向的手法叫“托物言志”。“緌”是古代帽带的下垂部分，诗人把蝉的触须比作官员的帽带，暗示这是一位有身份、又清廉的人。虞世南是唐太宗很敬重的大臣，这首诗也像他自己的写照。

In English

This is a poem about a cicada. Line one describes how it looks: its feelers hang down like the tassels of an official’s hat, and it drinks clear dew. People in ancient China believed cicadas lived on dew alone, so the insect seemed clean and pure. Line two is its sound, flowing steadily out from the sparse leaves of a parasol tree (梧桐). The last two lines give the point: the cicada’s voice carries far because it sits up high — not because it borrows the strength of the autumn wind. On the surface this is about an insect; underneath, it is about people. If your character is truly high, your good name will travel by itself; you don’t need powerful friends or outside help to lift you up. Using an object to express your own ideals is called tuō wù yán zhì. The word 緌 (the dangling ribbons of an official’s hat) quietly suggests the cicada is a dignified official — and an honest one, since it only drinks dew. Yu Shinan was a minister Emperor Taizong deeply respected for his learning and integrity, so the poem reads like a self-portrait. It is the earliest of the three famous Tang cicada poems, alongside Luo Binwang’s and Li Shangyin’s.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Yu Shinan (558–638) was a statesman, scholar and one of the great calligraphers of the early Tang. Emperor Taizong praised him for “five virtues”, including loyalty and learning; the poem is usually read as a statement of his own ideals.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

垂緌 的“緌”读 ruí（生僻字）；藉 jiè（凭借，不读 jí）；疏桐 shū。机器朗读“緌”“藉”容易读错，已在“朗读用字”列替换同音字。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行文本（《全唐诗》卷三十六），全诗四句。“非是藉秋风”一作“非是借秋风”，意思相同。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

垂緌饮清露，

chuí ruí yǐn qīng lù

Feelers dangling like hat ribbons, it sips clear dew;

流响出疏桐。

liú xiǎng chū shū tóng

its song flows out from the thin leaves of the parasol tree.

居高声自远，

jū gāo shēng zì yuǎn

It sits up high, so its voice carries far —

非是藉秋风。

fēi shì jiè qiū fēng

not because the autumn wind gives it a lift.
