导读About this poem

The last song of Xiang Yu, the mighty “Hegemon-King of Western Chu”, surrounded by enemy armies on the night before his defeat. Four lines to his horse and to Lady Yu — the story behind the famous Peking opera Farewell My Concubine.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这是项羽兵败前夜唱的歌。他被刘邦的大军围困在垓下，夜里听到四面都是楚地的歌声（成语“四面楚歌”就从这里来），以为楚地都被汉军占领了。他和最宠爱的虞姬饮酒，唱了这首歌。第一句写自己当年力气大得能拔起高山，气概压倒天下，何等英雄；第二句马上急转直下：时运不利，连心爱的战马乌骓也不肯往前跑了。后两句是无可奈何的叹息：马不走了，我能怎么办？虞姬啊虞姬，我又能把你怎么办！一个曾经天下无敌的英雄，到最后最放不下的是一匹马和一个女人，这种对比让人特别感动。据说虞姬也唱歌应和，后来自尽。项羽突围后在乌江边自刎。这段故事后来成了京剧《霸王别姬》。

In English

Xiang Yu sang this song on the night before his final defeat. Trapped at Gaixia by Liu Bang’s armies, he heard songs of his own home region, Chu, rising on every side — the origin of the idiom 四面楚歌, “Chu songs on all four sides”, meaning to be surrounded with no way out. Thinking all of Chu had fallen, he drank with his beloved companion, Lady Yu, and sang. The first line recalls his glory: his strength could uproot mountains, his spirit towered over the age. The second line falls hard: the times have turned against him, and even his beloved war horse, Zhui, will no longer charge. The last two lines are helpless sighs: if my horse won’t go on, what can I do? And Yu, my Yu — what will become of you? A hero once unbeatable, who at the very end cares most about a horse and a woman: that contrast is what makes the song so moving. Tradition says Lady Yu sang a reply and then took her own life. Xiang Yu broke out of the siege but killed himself on the bank of the Wu River. The story lives on in the Peking opera Farewell My Concubine (霸王别姬).

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Xiang Yu (232–202 BC), from a family of Chu generals, led the revolt that destroyed the Qin dynasty and called himself the Hegemon-King of Western Chu. Strictly speaking he belongs to the end of Qin rather than to the Han, but he died in 202 BC fighting Liu Bang, the year the Han dynasty was proclaimed, so the site places him under Han. The song is recorded in the Records of the Grand Historian (史记·项羽本纪).

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

骓 zhuī（毛色青白相杂的马，项羽的战马叫“乌骓”）；不逝的“逝”读 shì；奈若何的“若”读 ruò（你）；盖世的“盖”读 gài；兮 xī。

版本说明 About the text

采用《史记·项羽本纪》所载文本，全诗四句。虞姬的和歌（“汉兵已略地，四方楚歌声……”）见于《楚汉春秋》佚文，不录。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

力拔山兮气盖世，

lì bá shān xī qì gài shì

My strength could tear up mountains, my spirit towered over the age —

时不利兮骓不逝。

shí bù lì xī zhuī bù shì

but the times have turned against me, and my good horse will not go on.

骓不逝兮可奈何，

zhuī bù shì xī kě nài hé

If my horse will not go on, what can I do?

虞兮虞兮奈若何！

yú xī yú xī nài ruò hé

Oh Yu, my Yu — what will become of you?
