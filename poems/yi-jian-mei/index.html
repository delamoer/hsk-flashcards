A Spray of Plum Blossom
李清照Lǐ Qīngzhào · Li Qingzhao宋 Song

导读About this poem

China’s greatest woman poet misses her husband in early autumn. It ends with one of the most quoted lines about love: the longing leaves her brow — only to land on her heart.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首词写妻子思念远方的丈夫。上阕先写初秋：荷花谢了，竹席也凉了。她一个人划着小船散心，抬头看见大雁飞回，月光洒满西楼，就盼望云中有人捎来丈夫的信——古人传说大雁能送信，所以雁和月都带着思念。下阕“花自飘零水自流”，花自己落，水自己流，谁也留不住，就像时光和分离。“一种相思，两处闲愁”最动人：同一份想念，分在两个地方，说明丈夫也在想她，爱是双向的。最后三句把看不见的愁写成会移动的东西：刚从眉头放下，又爬上了心头。语言浅白，像说话，感情却很细，是李清照早期最有名的作品。

In English

A young wife misses her husband, who is far away. The first half sets the scene in early autumn: the pink lotus has faded and the jade-smooth bamboo mat already feels cool. To ease her mind she slips off her silk skirt and rows a little boat alone. She looks up: wild geese are flying back in their V-shaped lines, and moonlight fills the west tower. Geese were said to carry letters in old China, so she wonders who might send her one from among the clouds. The second half opens with flowers falling and water flowing “each on its own” — nothing can stop them, just as nothing can stop time or bring him nearer. Then comes a lovely line: “one longing, two places of sorrow”. The same feeling sits in two hearts at once, so she knows he misses her too. The famous ending turns sadness into something that moves: she smooths it from her brow, and it climbs straight up into her heart. The language is almost conversational, which is why these last lines are still quoted — and sung — today.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Li Qingzhao (1084–c. 1155) is the best-known woman poet in Chinese history. She is traditionally said to have written this early in her marriage to the scholar Zhao Mingcheng, when he was away from home.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

簟 diàn（竹席，生僻字）。罗裳：裳 读 cháng（古代指下身的裙子；现代“衣裳”读 shang），此处按古诗词通行注音采用 cháng。谁：诗词朗读习惯读 shuí（今也读 shéi）。一种 zhǒng、两处 chù。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

红藕香残玉簟秋。

hóng ǒu xiāng cán yù diàn qiū

The pink lotus has lost its scent; the smooth bamboo mat turns cool with autumn.

轻解罗裳，独上兰舟。

qīng jiě luó cháng, dú shàng lán zhōu

I slip off my silk skirt and step into the little boat alone.

云中谁寄锦书来？

yún zhōng shuí jì jǐn shū lái

Who will send me a letter down through the clouds?

雁字回时，月满西楼。

yàn zì huí shí, yuè mǎn xī lóu

When the geese fly home in their written lines, moonlight floods the west tower.

花自飘零水自流。

huā zì piāo líng shuǐ zì liú

Petals fall as they will, and the water runs on as it will.

一种相思，两处闲愁。

yī zhǒng xiāng sī, liǎng chù xián chóu

One longing, shared — two hearts, two places, both aching.

此情无计可消除，

cǐ qíng wú jì kě xiāo chú

There is no way to send this feeling away:

才下眉头，却上心头。

cái xià méi tóu, què shàng xīn tóu

the moment it leaves my brow, it rises in my heart.
