导读About this poem

“How far into the night is it?” A ruler, too anxious to sleep, keeps asking the time before dawn court, watching the courtyard torches and listening for the bells of his nobles’ carriages. Three short stanzas that move from deep night to first light.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

《庭燎》是《诗经·小雅》里的一首，写一位君王在天亮前等待上朝的情景。古代早朝很早，宫廷院子里要点起大火炬，叫“庭燎”。全诗三章，每章开头都问：“夜如何其？”——夜里几点了？回答一次比一次接近天亮：第一章“夜未央”，夜还没过半，火炬明亮；第二章“夜未艾”，夜还没有结束，火光渐渐微弱；第三章“夜乡晨”，夜快到早晨了，火光和天光一起出现。大臣们陆续到来，先是听到马车上鸾铃“将将”“哕哕”地响，最后天亮了，能看见车上的旗帜。这种每章只换几个字的写法叫“重章叠句”，时间就在这几个字的变化里慢慢推进，非常巧妙。传统上认为这首诗赞美周宣王勤于政事，一夜睡不安稳，盼着天亮处理国事。

In English

This poem from the “Lesser Odes” of the Book of Songs shows a ruler waiting for the dawn court. In ancient China the court met before sunrise, and great torches called 庭燎 were lit in the palace courtyard. Each of the three stanzas opens with the same question, 夜如何其 — “How far into the night is it?” Each answer is a little closer to morning. In the first, the night is not yet half over and the torches blaze. In the second, the night has not yet ended and the flames are flickering. In the third, night is turning to dawn and the torchlight mixes with daylight. Meanwhile the nobles are arriving: first we only hear the bells on their horses’ bits — “qiang-qiang”, then “hui-hui” — and at last, in the growing light, we can see their banners. Changing just a few words from stanza to stanza while keeping the rest the same is a typical Book of Songs technique, and here it lets time quietly move forward. Traditionally the poem is read as praise of King Xuan of Zhou, a ruler so devoted to his duties that he could not sleep for wanting the day to begin. The phrase 夜未央 (“the night is not yet over”) is still used in modern Chinese.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

The “Lesser Odes” (小雅) section of the Book of Songs contains songs mostly used at the Zhou court. Traditional commentators linked this poem to King Xuan of Zhou (reigned 827–782 BC), though its author is unknown.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

夜如何其：其 jī（语气词，旧注读 jī）。将将 qiāng qiāng（铃声）；晣晣 zhé zhé；哕哕 huì huì；夜乡晨：乡 xiàng（同“向”）；旂 qí。这几处机器朗读容易读错，已在“朗读用字”列替换同音字，音频需人工听验。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行的《毛诗》文本。“庭燎晣晣”一作“晢晢”，同义，都是光亮微弱的样子；“庭燎有辉”的“辉”，《毛诗》原作“煇”（同“辉”）。《毛诗序》说此诗“美宣王也，因以箴之”——赞美中也带着劝诫。全诗三章，每章五句，网站按每章两行排版。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

夜如何其？夜未央，庭燎之光。

yè rú hé jī, yè wèi yāng, tíng liáo zhī guāng

How late is it? Not yet midnight — the courtyard torches blaze.

君子至止，鸾声将将。

jūn zǐ zhì zhǐ, luán shēng qiāng qiāng

The lords are on their way — I hear their harness bells ring.

夜如何其？夜未艾，庭燎晣晣。

yè rú hé jī, yè wèi ài, tíng liáo zhé zhé

How late is it? The night is not yet done — the torches burn low.

君子至止，鸾声哕哕。

jūn zǐ zhì zhǐ, luán shēng huì huì

The lords are coming — their bells jingle nearer.

夜如何其？夜乡晨，庭燎有辉。

yè rú hé jī, yè xiàng chén, tíng liáo yǒu huī

How late is it? Night is turning to dawn — the torches glow in the first light.

君子至止，言观其旂。

jūn zǐ zhì zhǐ, yán guān qí qí

The lords have come — now I can see their banners.
