导读About this poem

Rivers in China flow east — but beside this temple the stream runs west. Seeing it, the exiled Su Shi decides that growing old need not mean giving up.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

词前有小序：游蕲水的清泉寺，寺在兰溪边，溪水向西流。上阕写景：山脚下，兰草的嫩芽短短的，浸在溪水里；松林间的沙路干干净净，没有泥；傍晚下着细雨，杜鹃鸟在啼叫。画面清新，但“暮雨”“子规啼”也带一点淡淡的愁。下阕转为议论：谁说人生不能再回到少年？你看门前的流水还能向西流！不要因为头发白了，就唱“黄鸡催晓”那样感叹时光流逝的歌。中国的河大多向东流，古人常用“东流水”比喻时光一去不回；苏轼看到一条向西流的溪水，就从中看到希望。当时他被贬黄州，又生着病，却写出这样乐观的句子，表现了他热爱生活、不服老的态度。

In English

The preface sets the scene: Su Shi visited Clear Spring Temple in Qishui, beside Orchid Brook, whose water flows west. The first half is pure landscape. Short new orchid shoots dip into the stream at the foot of the hill; the sandy path through the pines is clean, without a speck of mud; evening rain patters down while a cuckoo calls. It is fresh and quiet, though evening rain and the cuckoo’s cry carry a hint of sadness. The second half turns to thought. Who says we can never be young again? Look — the water outside the gate can still flow west! So don’t sing that sad old song about the yellow rooster crowing the hours away just because your hair is white. Most Chinese rivers flow east to the sea, and poets had long used “water flowing east” as a picture of time that never returns. A stream running the other way gives Su Shi a reason for hope. He was in exile and had just been ill, yet he wrote these cheerful lines. They show his love of life and his refusal to feel old.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Su Shi (1037–1101), also called Su Dongpo, wrote this in the spring of 1082 during his exile in Huangzhou. He had gone to nearby Qishui to see a doctor, Pang Anchang, and the two visited Clear Spring Temple together.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

少 shào（再少：再回到少年）；子规 zǐ guī；萧萧 xiāo xiāo。小序中的“蕲水”读 qí shuǐ。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行版本。原有小序：“游蕲水清泉寺，寺临兰溪，溪水西流。”网站只收词。苏轼《游沙湖》一文也记此词，作“君看流水尚能西”，通行本作“门前流水尚能西”。“唱黄鸡”化用白居易《醉歌》“黄鸡催晓丑时鸣”。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

山下兰芽短浸溪，

shān xià lán yá duǎn jìn xī

At the hill’s foot, short orchid shoots dip into the stream;

松间沙路净无泥，

sōng jiān shā lù jìng wú ní

the sandy path through the pines is clean, not a speck of mud;

萧萧暮雨子规啼。

xiāo xiāo mù yǔ zǐ guī tí

evening rain whispers down, and a cuckoo calls.

谁道人生无再少？

shuí dào rén shēng wú zài shào

Who says we can never be young again?

门前流水尚能西！

mén qián liú shuǐ shàng néng xī

Look, the stream outside the gate still runs west!

休将白发唱黄鸡。

xiū jiāng bái fà chàng huáng jī

White hair is no reason to sing the rooster’s sad song of passing time.
