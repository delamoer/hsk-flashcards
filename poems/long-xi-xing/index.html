导读About this poem

Five thousand soldiers fall in the desert. Their bones lie by a nameless river — and at home their wives still dream of them coming back. One of the saddest anti-war poems in Chinese.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

前两句写战争：士兵们发誓要扫平匈奴，奋不顾身；五千名穿着貂皮锦衣的精锐战士，却全都战死在胡地的沙尘里。后两句是全诗最动人的地方：可怜那无定河边的一堆堆白骨，还是家中妻子春夜梦里思念的人。一边是河边的白骨，一边是闺房里的梦；一边是死，一边是盼望。妻子不知道丈夫已经死了，还在梦里等他回来——这种对比让人读了心痛。诗人没有直接骂战争，只是把这两个画面放在一起，战争的残酷就全出来了。这里的“匈奴”是借汉代的说法指唐代边境的敌人。

In English

The first two lines tell of the battle. The soldiers swore to sweep away the Xiongnu and did not care for their own lives; yet five thousand elite troops in sable and brocade all died in the dust of the borderlands. Then comes the heart of the poem. Pity the bones lying by the Wuding River — they are still the men their wives see in their dreams on spring nights back home. On one side, bones by a river; on the other, a dream in a quiet bedroom. Death on one side, hope on the other. The wives don’t know their husbands are dead and keep waiting for them in their dreams, and that contrast is almost unbearable. The poet never openly condemns war. He simply sets the two pictures side by side and lets them speak. “Xiongnu”, the old Han-dynasty name for the northern nomads, is used here for the Tang’s frontier enemies in general, and the name of the river — Wuding, literally “never settled” — adds its own bitter irony.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Chen Tao was a late-Tang poet (9th century) who failed to win office and is said to have lived as a hermit in the mountains of Jiangxi. “Song of Longxi” is an old yuefu title about the northwestern frontier; this is the second of four poems under it.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

誓扫匈奴不顾身，

shì sǎo xiōng nú bù gù shēn

They swore to sweep the enemy away, heedless of their lives;

五千貂锦丧胡尘。

wǔ qiān diāo jǐn sàng hú chén

five thousand in sable and brocade were lost in the desert dust.

可怜无定河边骨，

kě lián wú dìng hé biān gǔ

And those poor bones beside the Wuding River

犹是春闺梦里人。

yóu shì chūn guī mèng lǐ rén

are still the men their wives dream of on spring nights.
