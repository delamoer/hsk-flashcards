导读About this poem

Down in the valley spring is over — but climbing a mountain, the poet finds peach trees just coming into bloom, and jokes that spring didn’t vanish after all: it simply moved uphill.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

农历四月，山下的花都已经谢了（“芳菲尽”），诗人登上庐山的大林寺，却意外看到桃花才刚刚盛开。山上气温低，花开得晚，这是一个自然现象；可诗人把它写得很有趣：我常常遗憾春天走了，找不到它去了哪里，原来它悄悄转到这山里来了。诗人把春天当成一个会走路、会躲藏的人来写，这种手法叫“拟人”。前两句是“人间”和“山寺”的对比，后两句从失望变成惊喜，感情有一个转折。这首诗短小活泼，读起来让人跟着高兴，也让人明白：换一个地方、换一个角度，失去的美好也许还能再找到。

In English

In the fourth lunar month (around May), the flowers in the lowlands have all faded. Then the poet climbs Mount Lu to Dalin Temple and is surprised to find the peach trees only now in full bloom. The reason is simple — it is colder up high, so flowers open later — but the poet turns it into a small joke: “I was always sorry spring had gone and I couldn’t find where; I never guessed it had slipped up here.” He writes about spring as if it were a person who can walk away and hide; giving human actions to something non-human is called nǐrén (personification). The first two lines contrast “the human world” below with the mountain temple above; the last two swing from disappointment to delight. The poem is light and playful, and it leaves a gentle thought behind: go somewhere new, look from another angle, and a lost good thing may turn up again.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Bai Juyi (772–846) was demoted in 815 to a minor post in Jiangzhou (today’s Jiujiang), near Mount Lu. He wrote this in the spring of 817 after visiting Dalin Temple on the mountain with friends.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

人间四月芳菲尽，

rén jiān sì yuè fāng fēi jìn

Down in the world, by the fourth month, every blossom is gone;

山寺桃花始盛开。

shān sì táo huā shǐ shèng kāi

up at the mountain temple, the peach trees are just bursting into flower.

长恨春归无觅处，

cháng hèn chūn guī wú mì chù

I always grieved that spring went off and left no trace —

不知转入此中来。

bù zhī zhuǎn rù cǐ zhōng lái

I never knew it had slipped away up here.
