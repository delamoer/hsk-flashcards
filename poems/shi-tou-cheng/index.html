导读About this poem

The old capital of six dynasties is now an empty city. Hills still ring it, the tide still beats against its walls, and the same moon still climbs over the battlements at night — but there is no one left to see it.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

石头城就是今天的南京，从三国东吴到南朝陈，先后有六个朝代在这里建都，十分繁华；到了唐代，这里已经冷落荒凉。诗人借眼前的景物，写出历史兴亡的感慨。第一句：群山依旧围绕着这座古都，山没有变。第二句：江潮拍打着空荡荡的城墙，又寂寞地退回去。“空城”“寂寞”写出了繁华消失后的冷清。后两句写月亮：秦淮河东边升起的，还是从前那轮月亮，深夜里依旧越过城上的矮墙照进来。山还在，潮还在，月还在，只有人和繁华不在了。用不变的自然来衬托变化的人事，这种写法让怀古的感情更深沉。全诗没有一个“悲”字，却处处是悲凉。据说白居易读了这首诗，赞叹后人再也写不出更好的了。

In English

The Stone City is today’s Nanjing. From the Three Kingdoms to the end of the Southern Dynasties, six dynasties made it their capital, and it was magnificent. By the Tang it had become quiet and run-down. The poet uses what he sees to reflect on the rise and fall of history. Line one: the hills still ring the old capital — the mountains have not changed. Line two: the river tide beats against the empty city walls and slips back again, lonely. “Empty city” and “lonely” show the silence left when the splendour was gone. The last two lines turn to the moon: the moon rising east of the Huai (the Qinhuai River) is the same moon as long ago, and late at night it still climbs over the low battlements and shines in. Hills, tide and moon are all still here; only the people and the glory are gone. Setting changeless nature against changing human affairs makes the feeling of loss run deeper. There is not one word of “sorrow” in the poem, yet sorrow is everywhere. Bai Juyi is said to have read it and declared that no later poet would ever do better.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Liu Yuxi (772–842) was a Tang poet and official. This is the first of his five “Jinling Poems”, written around 826; he is said to have composed them before he had ever visited Jinling (Nanjing).

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

“周遭”读 zhōu zāo（四周）；“潮打”的“潮”读 cháo。“夜深还过”的“还”：这里是“仍然、依旧”的意思，网站读 hái；也有注本读 huán（又、回来）。“女墙”读 nǚ qiáng。

版本说明 About the text

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

山围故国周遭在，

shān wéi gù guó zhōu zāo zài

The hills still ring the old capital on every side;

潮打空城寂寞回。

cháo dǎ kōng chéng jì mò huí

the tide slaps the empty walls and slips back, lonely.

淮水东边旧时月，

huái shuǐ dōng biān jiù shí yuè

The same old moon that rises east of the Huai

夜深还过女墙来。

yè shēn hái guò nǚ qiáng lái

still climbs over the battlements deep in the night.
