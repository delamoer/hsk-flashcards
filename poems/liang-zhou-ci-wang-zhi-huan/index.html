导读About this poem

The Yellow River climbing into the clouds, a tiny fort among huge mountains, a flute playing a sad tune — and the bitter joke that spring never gets past the Jade Gate.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗写唐代西北边塞的景色和戍边士兵的心情。第一句写远望黄河，好像一直流到白云中间去了，气势非常开阔；第二句镜头拉近：一座孤零零的城，立在万丈高山之间，越显得渺小、孤单。后两句写声音：羌笛吹起了《折杨柳》的曲子，曲调哀怨。诗人劝道：何必用笛声去埋怨杨柳不发绿呢？春风本来就吹不过玉门关啊！表面上是说玉门关外气候寒冷、看不到春天，实际上也说出了士兵心里的苦：他们离家太远，连春天、连朝廷的关怀都到不了这里。“折柳”在古代代表离别，所以笛声也勾起了思乡之情。全诗苍凉而不消沉，被不少人推为唐代七言绝句的压卷之作。

In English

This poem captures the north-western frontier of the Tang dynasty and the mood of the soldiers stationed there. Line one looks far off: the Yellow River seems to run up into the white clouds — a vast, sweeping view. Line two zooms in: one lonely fort stands among mountains thousands of feet high, looking all the smaller and more alone. The second half turns to sound. A soldier plays the tune “Breaking the Willow” on a Qiang flute, a sad melody. The poet gently asks: why should the flute complain that the willows aren’t green? The spring wind never makes it past the Jade Gate Pass anyway. On the surface he means it is too cold out here for spring to arrive; underneath he is voicing the soldiers’ bitterness — they are so far from home that neither spring nor the court’s care ever reaches them. Breaking off a willow twig was a farewell custom, so the tune also stirs up homesickness. Desolate but not despairing, the poem has often been named the finest seven-character quatrain of the Tang.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Wang Zhihuan (688–742) was a High Tang poet; only six of his poems survive, but two of them — this one and “Climbing Stork Tower” — are among the best known in Chinese. “Song of Liangzhou” is a tune title from the north-west (Liangzhou is today’s Wuwei in Gansu), and many poets wrote lyrics for it.

黄河远上白云间，

huáng hé yuǎn shàng bái yún jiān

The Yellow River climbs far off into white clouds;

yī piàn gū chéng wàn rèn shān

one small fort stands alone among towering peaks.

羌笛何须怨杨柳，

qiāng dí hé xū yuàn yáng liǔ

Why let the flute play its sad willow song?

春风不度玉门关。

chūn fēng bù dù yù mén guān

The spring wind never gets past the Jade Gate.
