导读About this poem

Just three lines, sung by the founding emperor of the Han dynasty when he returned in triumph to his home town: a great wind, flying clouds — and a worried question about who will guard his empire.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首歌只有三句，是汉朝开国皇帝刘邦回到家乡时唱的。第一句写大风刮起、白云飞扬，既是眼前的景色，也比喻秦末天下大乱、群雄并起，而他像大风一样扫平了天下。第二句说自己威震四海，衣锦还乡，非常得意。可是第三句突然一转：到哪里去找勇猛的将士，替我守卫国家的四方呢？刚刚平定了叛乱，他已经开始担心江山能不能守住。三句话里有胜利的豪迈，也有帝王的忧虑，所以显得气魄很大，又很真实。句中的“兮”是楚地民歌常用的语气词，刘邦是楚地沛县人，这首歌也属于“楚歌”。

In English

This song is only three lines long. Liu Bang, founder of the Han dynasty, sang it when he came home to his native town as emperor. The first line — a great wind rises, clouds go flying — is the scene in front of him, but it also stands for the chaos at the end of the Qin dynasty, when rebels rose everywhere, and for Liu Bang himself, who swept across the land like that wind. The second line is pure triumph: his power now reaches all within the seas, and he has come home in glory. Then the third line suddenly changes tone: where can he find brave warriors to guard the four frontiers? Having only just crushed a rebellion, he is already worried about holding on to what he has won. Victory and anxiety in just three lines — that is why the song feels both grand and honest. The particle 兮 (xī), a sigh like “oh”, was typical of the folk songs of Chu in the south; Liu Bang came from Pei in the old Chu region, so this is called a “Chu song” (楚歌).

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Liu Bang (256 or 247–195 BC), a commoner from Pei (in today’s Jiangsu), rose in the revolts against Qin, defeated his rival Xiang Yu, and founded the Han dynasty as Emperor Gaozu. According to the Records of the Grand Historian, around 196–195 BC, returning from putting down a rebellion, he feasted the elders of Pei, played the zhu (a struck zither) and sang this song, with 120 local children as his chorus.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

兮 xī（语气词，相当于“啊”）。无其他特殊读音。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

大风起兮云飞扬，

dà fēng qǐ xī yún fēi yáng

A great wind rises, and the clouds go flying —

威加海内兮归故乡，

wēi jiā hǎi nèi xī guī gù xiāng

my power now spans the land, and I have come home again.

安得猛士兮守四方！

ān dé měng shì xī shǒu sì fāng

Where can I find brave men to guard the four frontiers?
