导读About this poem

It opens with a word game — the character 愁 “sorrow” is made of 秋 “autumn” on top of 心 “heart” — and then shows a lonely traveller who can’t bear to climb the tower on a moonlit night.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

开头两句是一个文字游戏：“愁”字是怎么合成的？是离别的人心上加了一个“秋”——“秋”加“心”正好是“愁”。这样拆字，既巧妙又真切：秋天本来就让人伤感，离人在秋天更加发愁。接着说，就算不下雨，风吹芭蕉也飕飕作响，让人心里发冷。大家都说晚上凉快、天气好，我却怕在明月下登楼——因为一登楼就会望远、想人。下阕说往事像一场梦，花已落空，只有烟雾中的流水还在流；燕子都飞回南方去了，我这个客人却还滞留在外。最后埋怨柳树：它不能缠住她的裙带把她留下，却长长地拴住了我这条走不了的船。把柳树写得像有感情的人，这叫“拟人”。这首词明白如话，在吴文英一向难懂的词里是少见的清新之作。

In English

The opening is a word puzzle: where is “sorrow” put together? On the heart of someone parting, in autumn — and indeed the character 愁 (“sorrow”) is written as 秋 (“autumn”) on top of 心 (“heart”). It is clever, but also true: autumn already makes people melancholy, and for someone separated from a loved one it is worse. Even when it isn’t raining, the wind rattles the banana leaves with a chilly hiss. Everyone says the cool evenings are lovely — but he dreads climbing the tower under the bright moon, because looking out into the distance only makes him miss her more. The second half: the past has ended like a dream; the flowers are gone, only misty water flows on. The swallows have flown home for the winter, yet he, the traveller, is still stuck far away. Finally he blames the willow: it couldn’t wrap around her skirt sash to keep her from leaving, but its long branches tie up his boat so he can’t go. Treating the willow as if it had a will of its own is personification. Wu Wenying is known for dense, difficult ci; this one is unusually plain and fresh.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Wu Wenying (c. 1200–c. 1260), also known as Mengchuang, was a Southern Song ci poet who never held office and lived as a retainer to high officials. The poem is traditionally read as written when parting from a woman he loved, while he was stuck away from home.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

何处合成愁？离人心上秋。

hé chù hé chéng chóu, lí rén xīn shàng qiū

How is “sorrow” made? Put autumn on a parting heart.

纵芭蕉、不雨也飕飕。

zòng bā jiāo, bù yǔ yě sōu sōu

Even with no rain, the banana leaves rustle cold.

都道晚凉天气好，

dōu dào wǎn liáng tiān qì hǎo

Everyone says the cool evenings are lovely —

有明月、怕登楼。

yǒu míng yuè, pà dēng lóu

but with the moon so bright, I dare not climb the tower.

年事梦中休，花空烟水流。

nián shì mèng zhōng xiū, huā kōng yān shuǐ liú

The past has ended like a dream; the flowers are gone, only misty water flows.

燕辞归、客尚淹留。

yàn cí guī, kè shàng yān liú

The swallows have said goodbye and flown home; I am still stranded here.

垂柳不萦裙带住，

chuí liǔ bù yíng qún dài zhù

The willow would not twine her sash to keep her here —

漫长是、系行舟。

màn cháng shì, jì xíng zhōu

instead, its long strands only tie my boat to the shore.
