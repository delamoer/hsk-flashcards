浪淘沙令（帘外雨潺潺）

Làng Táo Shā Lìng
Waves Washing the Sand: Rain Beyond the Curtain

导读About this poem

A dethroned king wakes before dawn to the sound of spring rain. In his dream he was still at home and happy; awake, he is a prisoner — and he knows the lost kingdom is as far away as heaven is from earth.

直译LiteralIn the dream I did not know I was a guest; for a moment I greedily enjoyed myself.

意译MeaningDreaming, I forgot I live as a stranger here, and snatched a moment of joy.

直译LiteralAlone, do not lean on the railing — the boundless rivers and mountains.

意译MeaningDon’t lean on the railing alone: out there lies the endless land I lost.

05

直译LiteralThe time of parting is easy; the time of meeting is hard.

意译MeaningLeaving was easy; seeing it again is hard.

06

直译LiteralFlowing water, falling flowers, spring has gone — heaven above, the human world below.

意译MeaningThe water flows on, the petals fall, spring is gone — then and now, as far apart as heaven and earth.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

李煜是南唐最后一位皇帝，亡国后被押到北宋都城开封，过着软禁的生活。这首词写的就是他这时的心情。上阕从声音和感觉写起：帘外春雨潺潺，春天快要过去了，薄薄的丝被挡不住天亮前的寒气。冷醒以后，他才明白刚才的好梦是假的——“梦里不知身是客，一晌贪欢”，只有在梦里，他才忘了自己是个俘虏。下阕劝自己“莫凭栏”：一靠着栏杆远望，就会看见那片再也回不去的“无限江山”。“别时容易见时难”说出了很多人共同的感受，今天仍常被引用。最后一句用流水、落花、春天的离去来比喻一切美好都已经过去，“天上人间”说过去和现在相差得像天和地一样远。全词语言很平白，感情却极深，被看作李煜晚年的代表作之一。

In English

Li Yu was the last ruler of the Southern Tang. After his kingdom fell to the Song in 975, he was taken north to the Song capital and kept under house arrest. This lyric records that life. The first half is built from sounds and sensations: rain pattering outside the curtain, spring slipping away, a thin silk quilt that can’t keep out the chill of the last watch before dawn. The cold wakes him, and only then does he realise his happy dream was a lie: “in the dream I forgot I was a guest” — that is, a captive in someone else’s land. In the second half he warns himself not to lean on the railing and look out, because what he would see is the endless land he has lost. The line 别时容易见时难 (“parting is easy, meeting again is hard”) has become a saying people still quote today. The ending piles up flowing water, falling petals and departing spring: everything beautiful is gone, and the distance between then and now is as great as between heaven and the human world. The words are simple, but the grief is total — which is why this is counted among Li Yu’s finest late works.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Li Yu (937–978) was the third and last ruler of the Southern Tang during the Five Dynasties period, a gifted painter, musician and poet. He surrendered to the Song in 975 and spent his last years as a captive in Kaifeng; this lyric is traditionally dated to that time, shortly before his death.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

潺潺 chán chán；阑珊 lán shān；罗衾 luó qīn（衾：被子）；五更 wǔ gēng；一晌 yī shǎng（有的读本注 xiǎng，现代词典作 shǎng）；凭栏 píng lán。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行文本（选本、教材多用此本）。重要异文：“春意阑珊”一作“春意将阑”；“独自莫凭栏”一作“独自暮凭栏”，“栏”一作“阑”。网站采用“莫凭栏”。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

帘外雨潺潺，春意阑珊。

lián wài yǔ chán chán, chūn yì lán shān

Rain murmurs beyond the curtain; spring is running out.

罗衾不耐五更寒。

luó qīn bù nài wǔ gēng hán

My silk quilt is no match for the cold before dawn.

梦里不知身是客，一晌贪欢。

mèng lǐ bù zhī shēn shì kè, yī shǎng tān huān

Dreaming, I forgot I live as a stranger here, and snatched a moment of joy.

独自莫凭栏，无限江山。

dú zì mò píng lán, wú xiàn jiāng shān

Don’t lean on the railing alone: out there lies the endless land I lost.

别时容易见时难。

bié shí róng yì jiàn shí nán

Leaving was easy; seeing it again is hard.

流水落花春去也，天上人间。

liú shuǐ luò huā chūn qù yě, tiān shàng rén jiān

The water flows on, the petals fall, spring is gone — then and now, as far apart as heaven and earth.
