导读About this poem

Grape wine in jade cups, a pipa playing on horseback — soldiers on the frontier drink before battle. “Don’t laugh if I lie drunk on the battlefield: since ancient times, how many came home?”

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这是一首写边塞军中宴饮的诗。凉州在今天甘肃武威，是唐代西北边疆的重镇。第一句全是名词：葡萄酒、夜光杯，都是西域来的稀罕东西，一下子就有了异域风情和热闹的气氛。第二句写正要喝酒，马上弹起了琵琶——“催”可以理解为催人喝酒，也可以理解为催人出发上战场。后两句是士兵的话：“我要是喝醉了躺在沙场上，你可别笑我；从古到今出去打仗的，有几个人能活着回来？”这话听起来豪爽洒脱，其实藏着很深的悲凉：正因为明天可能回不来，今天才要痛快地喝。用豪放的语气写悲伤，让这首诗格外动人。另外，王之涣也写过一首有名的《凉州词》（“黄河远上白云间”），“凉州词”是当时流行的曲调名，很多诗人都用它写边塞诗。

In English

Liangzhou (today’s Wuwei in Gansu) was a key garrison town on the Tang dynasty’s northwest frontier, on the road to Central Asia. The first line is just a list of beautiful things: grape wine and “night-glowing cups” of fine jade — both exotic luxuries from the Western Regions. In seven characters we are at a lively frontier feast. In the second line, just as the soldiers lift their cups, someone plays the pipa on horseback. The word 催 (“to urge, hurry”) can be read two ways: the music urges them to drink up, or it urges them to ride out to battle. Both readings work, and the tension between them matters. The last two lines are a soldier talking: “If I lie drunk on the battlefield, don’t laugh at me. Since ancient times, how many men who went to war ever came back?” On the surface this is bold and carefree. Underneath it is deeply sad: they drink so hard tonight precisely because they may die tomorrow. Saying something tragic in a light, swaggering voice is what makes the poem unforgettable. Note that “Song of Liangzhou” was the name of a popular tune; many poets wrote words for it. The other famous one is Wang Zhihuan’s, which begins “The Yellow River climbs far up among white clouds” — don’t mix the two up.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Wang Han (687?–726?) was a Tang official known for his bold, extravagant personality and love of horses and music. Few of his poems survive; this one is by far the best known.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

“几人回”的“几”读 jǐ。“琵琶”读 pí pá（第二个字常读轻声，这里按本调标）。其余无特殊读音。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

葡萄美酒夜光杯，

pú táo měi jiǔ yè guāng bēi

Sweet grape wine in cups of glowing jade —

欲饮琵琶马上催。

yù yǐn pí pá mǎ shàng cuī

we lift them, and a pipa on horseback calls us on.

醉卧沙场君莫笑，

zuì wò shā chǎng jūn mò xiào

If I fall drunk on the battlefield, don’t laugh at me:

古来征战几人回？

gǔ lái zhēng zhàn jǐ rén huí

since time began, how many soldiers ever made it home?
