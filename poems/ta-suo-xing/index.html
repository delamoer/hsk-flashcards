导读About this poem

Fog swallows the towers, the paradise of Peach Blossom Spring is nowhere to be found, and a cuckoo calls at dusk outside a lonely inn: one of the saddest lyrics an exiled Song poet ever wrote.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首词是秦观被贬到郴州（今湖南）时，在旅店里写的。上阕写景：大雾吞没了楼台，月色让渡口变得模糊，想找到传说中的世外桃源，却望穿眼睛也找不到。“桃源”出自陶渊明《桃花源记》，代表理想中没有烦恼的地方。怎么受得了一个人关在孤零零的旅馆里，春寒料峭，还在夕阳西下时听到杜鹃一声声啼叫！杜鹃的叫声像“不如归去”，最能引起思乡之情。下阕写远方亲友寄来梅花和书信，本是安慰，却一封封堆起来，像砌墙一样把愁“砌”得越来越高——把看不见的愁写成可以一块块砌起来的东西，非常新奇。结尾问郴江：你本来好好地绕着郴山流，为什么要流到潇湘去呢？表面问江，其实是问自己：我为什么会被冲到这么远的地方？据说苏轼特别喜欢这两句，把它写在扇子上。

In English

Qin Guan wrote this at an inn in Chenzhou (in today’s Hunan) after being exiled there. The first stanza is landscape and mood: fog swallows the towers, moonlight blurs the ferry crossing, and he searches in vain for Peach Blossom Spring — the hidden paradise from Tao Yuanming’s famous story, a place free of trouble. How can he bear being shut in a lonely inn in the spring cold, hearing a cuckoo call as the sun goes down? The cuckoo’s cry was heard as “better go home”, the sound of homesickness itself. In the second stanza, friends send plum blossoms and letters from far away. They should comfort him, but they pile up instead, “building” his sorrow higher like bricks in a wall — a striking way to make a feeling solid. The ending asks the Chen River: you were happily circling Chen Mountain — for whom did you run off north to the Xiao and Xiang? He is really asking about himself: why was he swept so far from home? Su Shi is said to have loved these last two lines so much that he wrote them on his fan.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Qin Guan (1049–1100) was one of the “Four Scholars” who studied under Su Shi. Caught up in the factional struggles of the late Northern Song, he was exiled further and further south; he wrote this in Chenzhou around 1097 and died a few years later on his way back north.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

踏莎行 tà suō xíng（莎：莎草，不读 shā）；郴州 Chēnzhōu；可堪 kě kān；无重数 wú chóng shù；为谁 wèi shuí。机器朗读“郴、重、为”容易读错，已在“朗读用字”列换成同音字，音频需人工听验。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行文本（《淮海居士长短句》）。“雾失楼台，月迷津渡”无重要异文；“杜鹃声里斜阳暮”曾有人认为“斜阳”与“暮”意思重复，但通行本均作此。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

雾失楼台，月迷津渡，

wù shī lóu tái, yuè mí jīn dù

The towers are lost in fog, the ferry blurred by the moon;

桃源望断无寻处。

táo yuán wàng duàn wú xún chù

I strain my eyes for Peach Blossom Spring, but it is nowhere.

可堪孤馆闭春寒，

kě kān gū guǎn bì chūn hán

How can I bear it — shut in a lonely inn against the spring cold,

杜鹃声里斜阳暮。

dù juān shēng lǐ xié yáng mù

while the cuckoo calls and the last light dies?

驿寄梅花，鱼传尺素，

yì jì méi huā, yú chuán chǐ sù

Plum blossoms come by courier, letters come by fish —

砌成此恨无重数。

qì chéng cǐ hèn wú chóng shù

each one a brick in a wall of sorrow, too high to count.

郴江幸自绕郴山，

chēn jiāng xìng zì rào chēn shān

Chen River, you were content to wind around Chen Mountain —

为谁流下潇湘去？

wèi shuí liú xià xiāo xiāng qù

so for whom did you run off to the Xiao and Xiang?
