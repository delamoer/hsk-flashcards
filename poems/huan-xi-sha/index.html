导读About this poem

A new song, a cup of wine, the same old pavilion as last year — and a quiet realisation about time. Its middle couplet, about flowers that must fall and swallows that seem to come back, is still a common Chinese saying.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首小词写的是一个很普通的傍晚：听一首新词，喝一杯酒，天气和亭台都跟去年一样。可是太阳西下，还会再回来吗？今天过去了，就不会再有。下阕的“无可奈何花落去，似曾相识燕归来”是千古名句，对仗非常工整（上下两句字数相同、词性相对）：花落是没有办法阻止的，这是失去；燕子飞回来，好像是去年认识的那几只，这是回来。可是燕子真的是去年的燕子吗？时间带走了一些东西，又带来了一些相似的东西。最后诗人一个人在花园小路上走来走去，什么也没说，却留下淡淡的惆怅和思考。这首词没有大事，只写生活里的一刻，却说出了人人都有的感受：美好的东西会消逝，而生活还在继续。

In English

Nothing dramatic happens in this short lyric. On a spring evening the poet listens to a newly written song and drinks a cup of wine. The weather is just like last year’s, and so is the old pavilion. Yet the sun is setting in the west — and when will this exact moment ever come again? The second half holds the famous couplet: “Nothing to be done — the flowers fall away; as if I knew them before, the swallows come home.” The two lines match word for word, a technique called duìzhàng (parallel couplet). One line is about loss: flowers fall and no one can stop them. The other is about return: swallows come back and look like old friends. But are they really last year’s swallows? Time takes things away and brings back things that only seem the same. The poem ends with the poet walking alone, back and forth, on a fragrant garden path. He draws no lesson; he just lets the feeling hang in the air. Chinese people still say 无可奈何花落去 when something is slipping away, and 似曾相识 (“seem to have met before”) is an everyday idiom for déjà vu.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Yan Shu (991–1055) was a child prodigy who became a chief minister of the Northern Song and a patron of young talents like Ouyang Xiu. An old anecdote says he had the line about falling flowers for a long time but no match for it, until a young official, Wang Qi, offered “as if I knew them before, the swallows come home”.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

一曲 qǔ（歌曲的曲，不读 qū）。徘徊 pái huái。无特殊争议读音。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行教材文本，全词上下两阕，每阕三句。宋人笔记《能改斋漫录》载“似曾相识燕归来”为王琪替晏殊对出的下句，晏殊也曾把这一联用在七律《示张寺丞王校勘》里。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

一曲新词酒一杯，

yī qǔ xīn cí jiǔ yī bēi

A newly written song, a single cup of wine —

去年天气旧亭台。

qù nián tiān qì jiù tíng tái

the weather just like last year’s, the same old pavilion.

夕阳西下几时回？

xī yáng xī xià jǐ shí huí

The sun goes down in the west; when will this hour come back?

无可奈何花落去，

wú kě nài hé huā luò qù

Nothing to be done — the flowers fall and are gone;

似曾相识燕归来。

sì céng xiāng shí yàn guī lái

and swallows I seem to know come home again.

小园香径独徘徊。

xiǎo yuán xiāng jìng dú pái huái

Alone, I wander up and down the fragrant garden path.
