导读About this poem

The Cowherd and the Weaver Girl can meet only once a year, on a bridge of magpies across the Milky Way. Instead of pitying them, this lyric argues that true love doesn’t need to be together every day.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首词写的是七夕节的传说：天上的织女和人间的牛郎相爱，却被银河隔开，每年只有农历七月七日晚上，喜鹊搭成桥，他们才能见一面。上阕写相会：轻柔的云彩变出巧妙的花样，流星传递着离别的愁恨，他们悄悄渡过遥远的银河。在秋风白露的夜晚见上一面，就胜过人间无数次的相聚。下阕写分别：温柔的感情像水一样，美好的相会像梦一样短，怎么忍心回头看鹊桥上回去的路？最后两句是千古名句：只要两个人的感情长久不变，又何必天天在一起呢？别的诗写七夕多是悲伤，这首词却翻出新意，把离别写得深情又豁达。今天“两情若是久长时，又岂在朝朝暮暮”仍常被用来说异地恋。

In English

The lyric retells the legend behind Qixi, the Chinese Valentine’s Day. The Weaver Girl, a goddess, fell in love with a mortal cowherd, but the Milky Way was set between them; only on the seventh night of the seventh lunar month do magpies form a bridge so the lovers can meet. The first half is the meeting. Delicate clouds weave clever patterns, shooting stars carry the sorrow of separation, and the two quietly cross the vast River of Heaven. One meeting on a night of golden autumn wind and jade-white dew is worth more than countless meetings in the human world. The second half is the parting. Their tenderness flows like water, the reunion passes like a dream, and they can hardly bear to look back at the road home across the bridge. Then come the famous closing lines: if love is meant to last, why must the lovers be together every morning and every evening? Most Qixi poems dwell on the sadness; Qin Guan turns it around into something warm and open-hearted. People still quote these lines to couples living far apart.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Qin Guan (1049–1100) was one of the four famous disciples of Su Shi and a master of the gentle, lyrical style of Song song lyrics. Caught up in his teacher’s political fall, he spent his last years in exile in the far south.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

“纤云”的“纤”读 xiān，不读 qiàn。迢迢 tiáo tiáo。“胜却”的“胜”读 shèng。“朝朝暮暮”的“朝”读 zhāo（早晨），不读 cháo。“暗度”的“度”同“渡”。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行文本（《淮海居士长短句》）。“银汉迢迢暗度”有的本子作“暗渡”，意思相同。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

纤云弄巧，飞星传恨，

xiān yún nòng qiǎo, fēi xīng chuán hèn

Wisps of cloud weave their fine designs, shooting stars carry the ache of parting,

银汉迢迢暗度。

yín hàn tiáo tiáo àn dù

as the lovers slip across the endless Silver River.

金风玉露一相逢，

jīn fēng yù lù yī xiāng féng

One meeting in the golden wind and jade-white dew

便胜却人间无数。

biàn shèng què rén jiān wú shù

is worth more than countless meetings down on earth.

柔情似水，佳期如梦，

róu qíng sì shuǐ, jiā qī rú mèng

Their tenderness flows like water, their night together passes like a dream;

忍顾鹊桥归路。

rěn gù què qiáo guī lù

how can they bear to look back at the road home across the magpie bridge?

两情若是久长时，

liǎng qíng ruò shì jiǔ cháng shí

If two hearts are meant to last,

又岂在朝朝暮暮。

yòu qǐ zài zhāo zhāo mù mù

why must they be together morning after morning, night after night?
