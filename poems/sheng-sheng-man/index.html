导读About this poem

Widowed, homeless and alone in a war-torn south, Li Qingzhao spends an autumn day waiting for dark. It opens with fourteen doubled characters that sound like sighing, and ends: how could one word, “sorrow”, ever cover all this?

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首词是李清照晚年写的。那时北宋已经灭亡，她逃到南方，丈夫赵明诚去世，收藏多年的书画文物也大多丢失，她一个人孤苦生活。开头连用七组叠字（重复的字）：“寻寻觅觅，冷冷清清，凄凄惨惨戚戚”，好像在寻找什么，却什么也找不到，只剩下冷清和悲伤。这十四个字读起来像一声声叹息，是中国诗词里最有名的开头之一。接着写天气忽冷忽热，身体难以调养；喝几杯淡酒，也挡不住傍晚的冷风。大雁飞过，好像是以前的老相识——雁能传信，可现在已经没有人可以写信了。下阕写满地凋谢的菊花，没有人想去摘；她守着窗户，一个人怎么熬到天黑？黄昏时细雨打在梧桐叶上，一点一滴，好像滴在心上。最后说：这种情景，怎么能用一个“愁”字说得完！全词用的都是平常的词，却层层写出深深的孤独。

In English

Li Qingzhao wrote this late in life. The Northern Song had fallen to invaders, she had fled south, her husband Zhao Mingcheng had died, and most of the books and art they had collected together were lost. She was living alone. The lyric opens with seven pairs of doubled characters — 寻寻觅觅，冷冷清清，凄凄惨惨戚戚, “searching, searching; cold, cold, quiet, quiet; bleak, bleak, miserable, miserable, grieving, grieving”. She is looking for something and finding nothing, only chill and sorrow. Read aloud, the fourteen characters sound like sighs, and they are among the most famous openings in all Chinese poetry. The weather keeps switching between warm and cold; a few cups of weak wine can’t keep out the evening wind. Wild geese fly over — old acquaintances, she says, since geese were said to carry letters, but now there is no one left to write to. In the second half, faded chrysanthemums cover the ground and no one wants to pick them. She sits by the window: how can she possibly last alone until dark? At dusk a fine rain drips on the wutong leaves, drop by drop, as if onto her heart. Then the final line: how could one word, “sorrow”, ever be enough for all of this? Everything is said in ordinary words, layer upon layer.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Li Qingzhao (1084–c.1155) fled south after the Jurchen Jin conquered the north in 1127; her husband died in 1129. This lyric is traditionally dated to her later years in the south, though its exact date is unknown.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

寻寻觅觅，冷冷清清，凄凄惨惨戚戚。

xún xún mì mì, lěng lěng qīng qīng, qī qī cǎn cǎn qī qī

Searching, searching — cold and empty, empty and cold — bleak, wretched, full of grief.

乍暖还寒时候，最难将息。

zhà nuǎn huán hán shí hòu, zuì nán jiāng xī

In this weather, warm one moment and cold again the next, it is hardest to take care of myself.

三杯两盏淡酒，怎敌他、晚来风急！

sān bēi liǎng zhǎn dàn jiǔ, zěn dí tā, wǎn lái fēng jí

A few cups of thin wine — how could they hold off the hard wind coming at nightfall?

雁过也，正伤心，却是旧时相识。

yàn guò yě, zhèng shāng xīn, què shì jiù shí xiāng shí

Wild geese fly over just when my heart is breaking — old friends of mine, once.

满地黄花堆积，憔悴损，如今有谁堪摘？

mǎn dì huáng huā duī jī, qiáo cuì sǔn, rú jīn yǒu shuí kān zhāi

Yellow chrysanthemums lie heaped on the ground, faded and ruined — who would pick them now?

守着窗儿，独自怎生得黑！

shǒu zhe chuāng er, dú zì zěn shēng dé hēi

I sit by the window — alone, how will I ever last until dark?

梧桐更兼细雨，到黄昏、点点滴滴。

wú tóng gèng jiān xì yǔ, dào huáng hūn, diǎn diǎn dī dī

And now fine rain on the wutong leaves, into the dusk, drop after drop after drop.

这次第，怎一个愁字了得！

zhè cì dì, zěn yī gè chóu zì liǎo dé

All of this — how could one word, “sorrow”, ever hold it?
