导读About this poem

Two lovers part at a riverside pavilion after an autumn shower. The most famous goodbye in Song lyrics — and the line “willow bank, dawn wind, a waning moon” is the picture every Chinese reader remembers.

上阕First stanza
01
寒蝉凄切，对长亭晚，骤雨初歇。

直译LiteralCold cicadas cry sadly; facing the long pavilion at dusk; the sudden rain has just stopped.

意译MeaningAutumn cicadas cry and cry; evening falls on the farewell pavilion; the sudden shower has just passed.

02
都门帐饮无绪，留恋处，兰舟催发。

直译LiteralDrinking in a tent at the capital’s gate, no heart for it; just as we linger, the orchid boat urges departure.

意译MeaningWe drink outside the city gate with no heart for wine; just as we cling to each other, the boat calls me to leave.

03
执手相看泪眼，竟无语凝噎。

直译LiteralHolding hands, we look at each other’s tearful eyes, and in the end have no words, choked.

意译MeaningHand in hand, we look into each other’s wet eyes — and our throats close; not a word comes.

04
念去去，千里烟波，暮霭沉沉楚天阔。

直译LiteralThinking: going, going — a thousand li of misty waves; evening haze heavy, the Chu sky wide.

意译MeaningI think of the road ahead: a thousand miles of misty water, the evening haze hanging low over the wide southern sky.

下阕Second stanza
05
多情自古伤离别，更那堪，冷落清秋节！

直译LiteralSince ancient times the tender-hearted have grieved at parting — how much more can one bear it in the bleak, clear autumn season!

意译MeaningLovers have always been hurt by partings — and how much harder in the cold, empty days of autumn!

06
今宵酒醒何处？杨柳岸，晓风残月。

直译LiteralTonight, where will I sober up? A willow bank; dawn wind, a waning moon.

意译MeaningWhere will I wake from tonight’s wine? On a willow bank, in the dawn wind, under a fading moon.

07
此去经年，应是良辰好景虚设。

直译LiteralGone from here for years, fine times and lovely scenes will surely be set out in vain.

意译MeaningAfter this, year after year, every fine day and lovely view will be wasted on me.

08
便纵有千种风情，更与何人说？

直译LiteralEven if I have a thousand kinds of tender feeling, to whom could I tell them?

意译MeaningEven with a thousand tender things to say — who would be there to hear them?

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首词写一对恋人在汴京城外分别。上阕写分别的现场：秋天傍晚，雨刚停，寒蝉叫得凄凉；在长亭里喝送别酒，谁都没有心情，船夫却在催着开船。两人拉着手，满眼是泪，想说的话太多，反而一句也说不出来——“竟无语凝噎”非常真实。“念去去”以后，作者想象前面千里烟波、天空低沉辽阔，路越远越孤单。下阕从眼前转到将来：自古多情的人最怕离别，何况是在冷清的秋天。“今宵酒醒何处？杨柳岸，晓风残月”是全词最有名的句子：作者不直接说难过，只画出一幅画——明早酒醒，只剩岸边杨柳、清晨的冷风和快要落下的残月。这种用景物表达感情的手法叫“借景抒情”。最后说，以后再好的风景也没有意义，因为没有人可以说话了。

In English

Two lovers say goodbye outside the Song capital. The first half is the parting itself. It is an autumn evening, a sudden shower has just stopped, and cicadas are crying in the cold. At the pavilion where travellers were seen off, they share a farewell drink, but neither can enjoy it — and the boatman is already calling him aboard. They hold hands and look at each other through tears, with so much to say that nothing comes out at all. Then he imagines the road ahead: a thousand miles of misty water under a heavy, wide southern sky. The second half moves from now to later. People who love deeply have always suffered at partings, he says — and in bleak autumn it is worse. The famous lines follow: “Where will I wake from tonight’s wine? On a willow bank, in the dawn wind, under a waning moon.” He never says “I will be sad”; he paints the scene and lets it speak. Chinese critics call this jiè jǐng shū qíng, “expressing feeling through scenery”. The poem closes with a quiet, heavy thought: from now on, even the loveliest days will be wasted, because there will be no one to share them with.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Liu Yong (c. 987–c. 1053) wrote song lyrics so popular that it was said “wherever there is a well, people sing Liu Yong”. He failed the civil exams for years and spent much time among entertainers; this lyric is traditionally said to record his leaving the capital Bianjing (today’s Kaifeng).

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

凄切 qī qiè（切 读 qiè）。凝噎 níng yē。暮霭 mù ǎi。兰舟催发 fā。“更那堪”“更与何人说”：更 读 gèng；那 读 nǎ（同“哪”），按通行教材注音采用 nǎ。应是 yīng。留恋处、何处：处 chù。

版本说明 About the text

采用高中语文教材通行文本。重要异文：“竟无语凝噎”的“噎”一作“咽”；“今宵”一作“今霄”；“便纵有”一作“便总有”；“千种风情”一作“风流”；“更与何人说”一作“待与”。全词上下两阕，网站按句读分行。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

寒蝉凄切，对长亭晚，骤雨初歇。

hán chán qī qiè, duì cháng tíng wǎn, zhòu yǔ chū xiē

Autumn cicadas cry and cry; evening falls on the farewell pavilion; the sudden shower has just passed.

都门帐饮无绪，留恋处，兰舟催发。

dū mén zhàng yǐn wú xù, liú liàn chù, lán zhōu cuī fā

We drink outside the city gate with no heart for wine; just as we cling to each other, the boat calls me to leave.

执手相看泪眼，竟无语凝噎。

zhí shǒu xiāng kàn lèi yǎn, jìng wú yǔ níng yē

Hand in hand, we look into each other’s wet eyes — and our throats close; not a word comes.

念去去，千里烟波，暮霭沉沉楚天阔。

niàn qù qù, qiān lǐ yān bō, mù ǎi chén chén chǔ tiān kuò

I think of the road ahead: a thousand miles of misty water, the evening haze hanging low over the wide southern sky.

多情自古伤离别，更那堪，冷落清秋节！

duō qíng zì gǔ shāng lí bié, gèng nǎ kān, lěng luò qīng qiū jié

Lovers have always been hurt by partings — and how much harder in the cold, empty days of autumn!

今宵酒醒何处？杨柳岸，晓风残月。

jīn xiāo jiǔ xǐng hé chù, yáng liǔ àn, xiǎo fēng cán yuè

Where will I wake from tonight’s wine? On a willow bank, in the dawn wind, under a fading moon.

此去经年，应是良辰好景虚设。

cǐ qù jīng nián, yīng shì liáng chén hǎo jǐng xū shè

After this, year after year, every fine day and lovely view will be wasted on me.

便纵有千种风情，更与何人说？

biàn zòng yǒu qiān zhǒng fēng qíng, gèng yǔ hé rén shuō

Even with a thousand tender things to say — who would be there to hear them?
