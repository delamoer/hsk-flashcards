导读About this poem

Four short lines with four bright colours — green river, white birds, blue hills, flame-red flowers — and then a sigh: another spring is passing, and when will I go home?

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首小诗前两句像一幅色彩鲜艳的画：江水碧绿，衬得水上的鸟更白；山是青的，衬得山上的花红得像要燃烧起来。碧、白、青、红四种颜色互相映衬，“逾”（更加）和“欲燃”（好像要烧起来）两个词让画面活了起来。可是后两句一转：今年的春天眼看又要过去了，哪一天才是我回家的日子呢？春光越美，越让在外的人想家——这叫“以乐景写哀情”，用美好的景色来写悲伤的心情，反差让思念更深。全诗只有二十个字，用的都是学过的常用字，很适合初学者背诵。

In English

The first two lines are a painting in bright colours: the river is jade-green, which makes the birds over it look even whiter; the hills are blue-green, which makes the flowers on them so red they seem about to catch fire. Green, white, blue and red set each other off, and the words 逾 (“even more”) and 欲燃 (“about to burn”) bring the picture to life. Then the last two lines turn: this spring, too, is passing before my eyes — which day will be the year I go home? The more beautiful the spring, the more a person far from home misses it — and Du Fu, a war refugee in Chengdu for years, had watched several such springs go by. Chinese critics call this “using a happy scene to write a sad feeling”: the contrast makes the longing deeper. With just twenty characters, almost all common ones, it is a perfect poem for beginners to learn by heart.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Du Fu (712–770) wrote this in spring 764 in Chengdu, where he had lived for several years as a refugee from the wars in the north. The poem is the second of a pair of quatrains.

江碧鸟逾白，

jiāng bì niǎo yú bái

Green river — the birds look whiter still;

山青花欲燃。

shān qīng huā yù rán

blue hills — the flowers seem on fire.

今春看又过，

jīn chūn kàn yòu guò

Another spring slips by before my eyes.

何日是归年。

hé rì shì guī nián

When will the year come that I go home?
