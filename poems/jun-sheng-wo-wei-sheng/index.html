导读About this poem

Twenty characters, almost all of them among the first words a learner knows — you, me, born, old, late, early. Written on a Tang-dynasty teapot, it is the simplest possible poem about loving someone born at the wrong time.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗是写在唐代一把瓷壶上的，作者是谁已经不知道了。二十个字里只用了“君、我、生、未、已、老、恨、迟、早”几个简单的字，反复组合，却说出了一种很深的遗憾。“君”是“你”。你出生的时候，我还没有出生；等我出生了，你已经老了。你遗憾我生得太晚，我遗憾你生得太早。两个人隔着几十年的时间，相爱却不能相守。诗里的“恨”不是今天的“恨（hate）”，而是“遗憾、可惜”。前两句和后两句结构对称，像两个人互相说话，读起来朗朗上口。因为简单真挚，这首诗近年在网上流传很广，很多人用它表达“相遇太晚”的感情。后来网上流传的长版本多是现代人续写的，网站只收瓷壶上的这四句。

In English

This poem was written on a Tang-dynasty porcelain ewer, and no one knows who wrote it. It uses only a handful of simple characters — you, I, born, not yet, already, old, regret, late, early — combined and recombined, and yet it holds a deep sadness. 君 means “you”. When you were born, I wasn’t born yet; by the time I was born, you were already old. You regret that I was born too late; I regret that you were born too early. Two people who love each other are separated by decades and cannot share a life. Note that 恨 here does not mean “hate” as in modern Chinese; it means “to regret, to wish it were otherwise”. The first half and second half mirror each other, like two people answering each other, which makes it very easy to read aloud and remember. Because it is so simple and sincere, the poem has spread widely online in recent years as a way of saying “we met too late”. The longer versions circulating on the internet were mostly added by modern writers; we give only the four lines on the ewer.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

The poem is inscribed on a ewer from the Changsha kilns (Tongguan, Hunan), excavated in the 1970s and dated to the Tang dynasty. These kilns often decorated cheap everyday pottery with popular folk verses, so the poem is probably a song or saying from ordinary people of the time.

君生我未生，

jūn shēng wǒ wèi shēng

When you were born, I was not here;

我生君已老。

wǒ shēng jūn yǐ lǎo

by the time I came, you had grown old.

君恨我生迟，

jūn hèn wǒ shēng chí

You wish I had been born sooner;

我恨君生早。

wǒ hèn jūn shēng zǎo

I wish you had been born later.
