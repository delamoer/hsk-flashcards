导读About this poem

A half moon over Mount Emei, its reflection floating down the river with the boat: young Li Bai is leaving his home province for the first time, and the moon he can no longer see becomes the friend he misses.

中文解析

这首诗写李白第一次离开家乡四川，坐船顺江东下。秋夜，峨眉山上挂着半个月亮，月影映在平羌江里，随着江水一起流动——好像月亮也陪着他走。第三句一口气写出行程：夜里从清溪出发，向三峡驶去。到最后一句，船进入两岸高山的峡谷，再也看不见那轮月亮了，诗人心里很想念，只好继续往渝州去。“君”可以理解为月亮，也有人认为指家乡的朋友，其实月亮正是家乡和友人的化身。短短四句用了五个地名（峨眉山、平羌江、清溪、三峡、渝州），读起来却一点也不堆砌，反而让人感到船行得很快，离家越来越远。

In English

Li Bai wrote this as a young man leaving his home in Sichuan for the first time, sailing down the river at night. A half moon hangs over Mount Emei on an autumn evening, and its reflection drops into the Pingqiang River and flows along with the water — as if the moon were travelling with him. The third line covers the journey in one breath: setting out from Qingxi by night, heading for the Three Gorges. In the last line the boat enters the gorges, where high cliffs hide the sky; he can no longer see “you,” and must go on towards Yuzhou missing it. “You” (君) can be read as the moon, or as a friend back home — either way the moon stands for home and the people he is leaving. Remarkably, these four short lines contain five place names: Mount Emei, the Pingqiang River, Qingxi, the Three Gorges and Yuzhou. Instead of feeling crowded, they give a sense of speed: the boat races on, and home falls further and further behind.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Li Bai (701–762) grew up in Sichuan. He is said to have written this around 725, aged about 24, when he set off down the Yangtze to see the wider world.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

峨眉山月半轮秋，

é méi shān yuè bàn lún qiū

A half moon of autumn hangs over Mount Emei;

影入平羌江水流。

yǐng rù píng qiāng jiāng shuǐ liú

its image falls into the river and drifts along with me.

夜发清溪向三峡，

yè fā qīng xī xiàng sān xiá

By night I leave Clear Creek for the Three Gorges —

思君不见下渝州。

sī jūn bù jiàn xià yú zhōu

missing you, unable to see you, I sail on down to Yuzhou.
