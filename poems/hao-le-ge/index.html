导读About this poem

Everyone knows it’s good to be free like an immortal — but no one can let go of fame, money, a wife, or children. A wandering Taoist sings this mocking song in the first chapter of Dream of the Red Chamber, China’s greatest novel.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

《好了歌》出自《红楼梦》第一回，是一个跛脚道士唱的歌。全歌四段，结构完全一样：每段第一句都是“世人都晓神仙好”，大家都知道做神仙好；第二句说却忘不了一样东西——功名、金银、娇妻、儿孙。后两句再说出结局：古代的将相如今只剩一堆长满野草的坟；一辈子嫌钱不够多，等钱多了，眼睛也闭上了；丈夫活着时妻子天天说恩爱，丈夫一死就跟别人走了；父母为儿孙操碎了心，孝顺的儿孙又见过几个？这种反复的结构叫重章叠句，读起来像民歌一样顺口。每句都押“了”（liǎo）的韵，道士解释说：“好”就是“了”，“了”就是“好”——放下了，才是好。这首歌讽刺人们追名逐利，也预示了小说里大家族由盛到衰的命运。

In English

This song comes from the first chapter of Dream of the Red Chamber. A lame Taoist priest sings it as he wanders by. It has four verses with exactly the same shape. Each opens with “Everyone knows it’s good to be an immortal”, then names the one thing people cannot let go of: fame and rank, gold and silver, a lovely wife, children and grandchildren. The second couplet of each verse shows how it ends. The great generals and ministers of the past? A heap of earth overgrown with grass. All your life you complain you haven’t saved enough — and by the time you have, your eyes are closed for good. While you live, your wife talks every day of love; when you die, she goes off with someone else. Doting parents have always been many — but who has ever seen dutiful children? Repeating the same frame verse after verse makes it as easy to sing as a folk song. Every verse rhymes on 了 (liǎo, “ended, done”), and the priest explains the title: “good” (好) means “done” (了), and “done” means “good” — only when you let go is it truly good. The song mocks the chase for fame and wealth, and also foreshadows the fall of the great family at the centre of the novel.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Cao Xueqin (c. 1715–c. 1763) wrote Dream of the Red Chamber after his once-wealthy family lost everything. The novel follows the rise and fall of a great household, and is full of poems and songs written for its characters; this one appears in Chapter 1.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

题目和韵脚的“了”都读 liǎo（了结、完了），不读 le：好了 hǎo liǎo、忘不了、草没了 mò liǎo、眼闭了、人去了、谁见了。没 mò（淹没）；将相 jiàng xiàng；冢 zhǒng；终朝 zhāo。“了”“没”机器朗读容易读错，已在“朗读用字”列替换（了→蓼），音频需人工听验。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行的《红楼梦》（人民文学出版社）文本。全歌十六句，网站按一联一行排版，分四段。末句维基文库所收版本作“孝顺子孙谁见了”，网站从通行本作“儿孙”。原书紧接着还有甄士隐的《好了歌注》（“陋室空堂，当年笏满床……”），不收。

世人都晓神仙好，惟有功名忘不了！

shì rén dōu xiǎo shén xiān hǎo, wéi yǒu gōng míng wàng bù liǎo

Everyone knows it’s good to be an immortal — but fame and rank, they can’t let go!

古今将相在何方？荒冢一堆草没了。

gǔ jīn jiàng xiàng zài hé fāng, huāng zhǒng yī duī cǎo mò liǎo

Where are the generals and ministers of old? A mound of earth, lost under the weeds.

世人都晓神仙好，只有金银忘不了！

shì rén dōu xiǎo shén xiān hǎo, zhǐ yǒu jīn yín wàng bù liǎo

Everyone knows it’s good to be an immortal — but gold and silver, they can’t let go!

终朝只恨聚无多，及到多时眼闭了。

zhōng zhāo zhǐ hèn jù wú duō, jí dào duō shí yǎn bì liǎo

All day long they fret they haven’t saved enough — and when they have, their eyes are shut for good.

世人都晓神仙好，只有娇妻忘不了！

shì rén dōu xiǎo shén xiān hǎo, zhǐ yǒu jiāo qī wàng bù liǎo

Everyone knows it’s good to be an immortal — but a lovely wife, they can’t let go!

君生日日说恩情，君死又随人去了。

jūn shēng rì rì shuō ēn qíng, jūn sǐ yòu suí rén qù liǎo

While you live, she speaks of love every day; once you die, she’s off with someone new.

世人都晓神仙好，只有儿孙忘不了！

shì rén dōu xiǎo shén xiān hǎo, zhǐ yǒu ér sūn wàng bù liǎo

Everyone knows it’s good to be an immortal — but children and grandchildren, they can’t let go!

痴心父母古来多，孝顺儿孙谁见了？

chī xīn fù mǔ gǔ lái duō, xiào shùn ér sūn shuí jiàn liǎo

Doting parents there have always been — but dutiful children, who has ever seen one?
