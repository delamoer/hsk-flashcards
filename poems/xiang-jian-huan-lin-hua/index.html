导读About this poem

Only 36 characters: spring blossoms beaten down by rain and wind become a picture of a whole life’s losses. The last line — life is full of regret the way rivers always flow east — is one of the most quoted in Chinese.

这首诗被谱成了歌Sung as a song

邓丽君《胭脂泪》Teresa Teng

Teresa Teng's 1983 album Light Exquisite Feeling (淡淡幽情) set twelve classical poems to new music; this track (music by Liu Chia-chang) sings Li Yu's lyric "The forest flowers have shed their spring red" word for word. The clip is from the 1983 TV special filmed for the album.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首词从一件小事写起：林中的花谢了，春天的红色一下子不见了。“太匆匆”三个字，是看花的人忍不住的叹息。为什么谢得这么快？因为“朝来寒雨晚来风”——早上冷雨打，晚上大风吹，花怎么受得了？读到这里，人们自然会想到李煜自己：一个国君，经历了亡国、被俘，就像这些花一样，被风雨摧残。下阕把花和人连在一起：雨中的花瓣像女子带着胭脂的眼泪，人和花互相挽留、一起沉醉，可是这样的相聚什么时候才能再有？最后一句“自是人生长恨水长东”，把个人的悲伤扩大成对整个人生的感叹：人生的遗憾本来就和江水向东流一样，永远不会停止。从一朵花写到整个人生，以小见大，是这首词最打动人的地方。

In English

The lyric begins with something small: the blossoms in the grove have fallen, and spring’s red is suddenly gone. 太匆匆 (“far too soon”) is the sigh of someone watching it happen. Why so soon? Because of “cold rain in the morning, wind at night” — no flower could stand that. Readers naturally think of Li Yu himself: a ruler who lost his kingdom and became a prisoner, battered like the flowers. In the second half, flowers and people merge. Wet petals look like a woman’s tears stained with rouge (胭脂, the red make-up of the time); flowers and people urge each other to stay and drink a little longer — but when will such a meeting happen again? The final line widens one man’s sorrow into a statement about every life: regret is simply part of being human, as surely as rivers in China all flow east to the sea and never stop. Moving from a single fallen flower to the whole of life — seeing something large in something small — is what gives this tiny poem its weight.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Li Yu (937–978) was the last ruler of the Southern Tang. Most critics date this lyric to his years as a captive of the Song after 975, when his poems turned from court pleasures to loss and regret.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

朝来 zhāo lái（早晨，不读 cháo）；几时重 chóng（再一次）；长恨、水长东 两个“长”都读 cháng；胭脂 yān zhī；谢了 le。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行文本。重要异文：“胭脂泪，相留醉”一作“胭脂泪，留人醉”；“无奈朝来寒雨晚来风”一作“常恨朝来寒雨晚来风”（多数选本作“无奈”）。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

林花谢了春红，太匆匆。

lín huā xiè le chūn hóng, tài cōng cōng

The red of spring has fallen from the trees — so soon, so soon.

无奈朝来寒雨晚来风。

wú nài zhāo lái hán yǔ wǎn lái fēng

What can they do against cold rain at dawn and wind at dusk?

胭脂泪，相留醉，几时重。

yān zhī lèi, xiāng liú zuì, jǐ shí chóng

Tears tinged with rouge, a plea to stay and drink — when will we meet like this again?

自是人生长恨水长东。

zì shì rén shēng cháng hèn shuǐ cháng dōng

Life is made of endless regret, as rivers are made to flow forever east.
