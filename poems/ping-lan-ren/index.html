导读About this poem

A wife holds the warm clothes she has made for her husband at the front: if she sends them, he may feel no need to come home; if she doesn’t, he will be cold. Twenty-four characters, one impossible choice.

中文解析

这首小曲用一位妻子的口吻来写。她的丈夫在远方当兵，天冷了，她做好了冬衣，却犹豫要不要寄出去。如果寄去，怕丈夫有了暖和的衣服，就更不想回家了；如果不寄，又怕丈夫在外面挨冻。寄也不是，不寄也不是，所以她说：在寄与不寄之间，我真是千难万难。“妾”是古代女子对自己的谦称。全曲只有四句、二十四个字，几个字反复出现——“寄”“君”“衣”，像一个人自言自语、来回犹豫，读起来很真实。作者把思念和关心这两种感情放在一起，让它们互相“打架”：想让他回来，又怕他受冷。这种矛盾心理写得非常细腻，正是因为爱得深，才会这么难。语言像口语一样朴素，这是元曲的特点。

In English

This little song is spoken by a wife. Her husband is away as a soldier; winter is coming, and she has finished making his warm clothes — but she cannot decide whether to send them. If she sends them, she is afraid that, warm and comfortable, he will be in even less of a hurry to come home. If she doesn’t, he will be cold out there. Either way is wrong, so she says that between sending and not sending, she is caught in a thousand difficulties. 妾 is the humble word a woman used for “I” in old Chinese. The whole song is only four lines and twenty-four characters, and the same few words come back again and again — 寄 (“send”), 君 (“you”), 衣 (“clothes”) — just as someone talking to herself goes round and round. Two kinds of love pull against each other: she wants him home, and she wants him warm. The struggle is so finely drawn because her love is so deep. The plain, almost spoken language is typical of Yuan-dynasty songs.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Yao Sui (1238–1313) was a leading scholar and high official of the Yuan dynasty, known mainly for his prose. He also wrote sanqu songs, and this short one is his best known.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

不还 bù huán：“还”读 huán（回来）。妾 qiè；凭阑 píng lán。机器朗读第一句的“还”容易读成 hái，已在“朗读用字”替换同音字。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行文本。曲牌题作【越调·凭阑人】，“阑”也写作“栏”。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

欲寄君衣君不还，

yù jì jūn yī jūn bù huán

If I send your clothes, you may not come home;

不寄君衣君又寒。

bù jì jūn yī jūn yòu hán

if I don’t, you will be cold.

寄与不寄间，

jì yǔ bù jì jiān

Torn between sending and not sending,

妾身千万难。

qiè shēn qiān wàn nán

I am caught in a thousand troubles.
