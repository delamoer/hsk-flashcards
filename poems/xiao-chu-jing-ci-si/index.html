导读About this poem

A goodbye poem that never says goodbye: instead, Yang Wanli shows his friend West Lake in June — lotus leaves reaching the sky, lotus flowers redder than red in the morning sun.

中文解析

这首诗是杨万里清早从西湖边的净慈寺出来，送朋友林子方去外地做官时写的。题目说“送”，诗里却一句告别的话也没有，只写西湖的六月。前两句说：到底是六月的西湖，风光和其他季节就是不一样。“毕竟”两个字像脱口而出的赞叹。后两句是全诗最有名的画面：莲叶一片连着一片，一直铺到天边，绿得没有尽头；荷花在朝阳照耀下，红得格外鲜艳。“接天”和“映日”写出画面的大，“碧”和“红”两种颜色对比鲜明，读起来像一幅色彩浓烈的画。为什么送别只写风景？一种理解是，诗人想用西湖的美景留住朋友，或者让朋友记住杭州的好。这首诗被选入小学课本，“接天莲叶无穷碧，映日荷花别样红”是描写荷花最有名的句子。

In English

Yang Wanli wrote this as he walked out of Jingci Temple on the shore of West Lake at dawn, to see his friend Lin Zifang off to a new post. The title says “seeing off”, yet the poem contains no farewell at all — only West Lake in the sixth month. The first two lines are a burst of praise: “It really is West Lake in June — the scenery is like no other season’s.” The word 毕竟 (“after all, truly”) sounds like something said out loud on the spot. The last two lines give the famous picture. Lotus leaves spread all the way to the horizon, as if touching the sky, an endless green; lotus flowers caught in the morning sun are red in a way you have never seen. 接天 (“touching the sky”) and 映日 (“lit by the sun”) make the scene huge, and the green and red clash brightly, like a painting in strong colours. Why describe scenery at a farewell? One reading is that Yang hopes the beauty of the lake will make his friend want to stay, or at least remember Hangzhou. Children in China learn this poem at school, and its last two lines are the best-known description of lotuses in the language.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Yang Wanli (1127–1206), one of the four great poets of the Southern Song, was known for fresh, lively poems about nature and everyday moments. Lin Zifang was a friend and younger colleague; the poem is traditionally dated to around 1187, when Lin left the capital Lin’an (Hangzhou) to take a post in Fuzhou.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

毕竟西湖六月中，

bì jìng xī hú liù yuè zhōng

It really is West Lake in June —

风光不与四时同。

fēng guāng bù yǔ sì shí tóng

no other season looks like this.

接天莲叶无穷碧，

jiē tiān lián yè wú qióng bì

Lotus leaves run out to meet the sky, green without end,

映日荷花别样红。

yìng rì hé huā bié yàng hóng

and lotus flowers, lit by the sun, are red as nothing else is red.
