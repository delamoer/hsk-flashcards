导读About this poem

How did Chinese children learn to write poetry? With rhymes like this: sky pairs with earth, rain with wind. This opening section of a famous primer builds from one-character pairs up to long, balanced couplets.

第一段Part 1
01
天对地，雨对风。

直译LiteralSky pairs with earth; rain pairs with wind.

意译MeaningSky answers earth, and rain answers wind.

02
大陆对长空。

直译LiteralThe great land pairs with the long sky.

意译MeaningThe wide land answers the endless sky.

03
山花对海树，赤日对苍穹。

直译LiteralMountain flowers pair with seaside trees; the red sun pairs with the blue vault.

意译MeaningHill flowers answer trees by the sea; the red sun answers the blue dome of heaven.

04
雷隐隐，雾蒙蒙。

直译LiteralThunder rumbling faintly; mist thick and hazy.

意译MeaningThunder rumbles far away; mist hangs soft and grey.

05
日下对天中。

直译Literal“Under the sun” pairs with “mid-heaven”.

意译MeaningThe capital below answers the heights above.

06
风高秋月白，雨霁晚霞红。

直译LiteralWind high, the autumn moon white; rain cleared, the evening glow red.

意译MeaningHigh wind, and the autumn moon shines white; the rain clears, and the sunset glows red.

第二段Part 2
07
牛女二星河左右，

直译LiteralThe Cowherd and Weaver, two stars, left and right of the River;

意译MeaningThe Cowherd and the Weaver Girl shine on either side of the Milky Way;

08
参商两曜斗西东。

直译LiteralShen and Shang, two lights, west and east of the Dipper.

意译MeaningShen and Shang, two stars that never meet, sit west and east of the Dipper.

09
十月塞边，飒飒寒霜惊戍旅；

直译LiteralIn the tenth month at the frontier, rustling cold frost startles the garrison troops;

意译MeaningIn the tenth month on the frontier, cold frost hisses down and startles the soldiers on guard;

10

直译Literalin the three months of winter on the river, boundless north snow chills the old fisherman.

意译Meaningthrough the long winter on the river, endless north-wind snow chills the old fisherman.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

《笠翁对韵》是清代的启蒙读物，专门教孩子学“对对子”——上下两句字数相同、词性相对、意思相关、声调平仄相反，这叫“对仗”，是写诗、写对联的基本功。这一段是全书开头，押“东”韵（风、空、穹、蒙、中、红、东、翁）。它从最短的一字对开始：天对地，雨对风；然后是二字对、三字对、五字对、七字对，最后是十一字的长对，一步步加长，像爬楼梯。内容包罗天地万物：天空大地、山花海树、雷声雾气、秋月晚霞、银河两边的牛郎织女星、永不相见的参星和商星，最后是边塞的寒霜和江上的雪。读起来朗朗上口，孩子们背熟了，就自然学会了词语怎样搭配成对。对中文学习者来说，这也是一份很好的“反义词、近义词、量词搭配”练习材料。

In English

Li Weng’s Rhyming Pairs is a Qing-dynasty primer that taught children duìzhàng, the art of matching lines: two lines of the same length in which each word answers a word of the same kind (noun for noun, colour for colour) and the tones contrast. This skill was the foundation for writing regulated poems and the couplets Chinese families still paste on their doors at New Year. This is the opening section of the book, and every line ends on the same “-ong” rhyme (风 fēng, 空 kōng, 穹 qióng, 红 hóng…). It climbs like a staircase: one-character pairs (sky / earth, rain / wind), then two, three, five and seven characters, ending with eleven-character couplets. The content sweeps across the whole world — sky and land, mountain flowers and seaside trees, thunder and mist, the autumn moon and the sunset, the Cowherd and Weaver stars across the Milky Way, the stars Shen and Shang that never appear together, frost on the frontier and snow on a winter river. Children chanted it until the pairings became instinct. For learners today it is a wonderful drill in opposites and word partners, and the rhythm makes it easy to read aloud.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Li Yu (1611–1680), who called himself Liweng (“the old man in a bamboo hat”), was a playwright, novelist and essayist of the late Ming and early Qing. The rhyming primer that bears his name was used for generations to teach children the skill of parallel couplets.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

参商 shēn shāng（星名，参不读 cān）。斗 dǒu（北斗）。曜 yào。苍穹 cāng qióng。霁 jì（雨停）。塞 sài（边塞）。飒飒 sà sà。戍 shù。朔 shuò。机器朗读已把“参”“斗”“塞”替换为同音字。

版本说明 About the text

节选上卷“一东”的第一段（全书按三十个平声韵编排，每韵三段），已与维基文库本核对一致。“参商两曜斗西东”的“斗”指北斗，与上句“河”（银河）相对。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

天对地，雨对风。

tiān duì dì, yǔ duì fēng

Sky answers earth, and rain answers wind.

大陆对长空。

dà lù duì cháng kōng

The wide land answers the endless sky.

山花对海树，赤日对苍穹。

shān huā duì hǎi shù, chì rì duì cāng qióng

Hill flowers answer trees by the sea; the red sun answers the blue dome of heaven.

雷隐隐，雾蒙蒙。

léi yǐn yǐn, wù méng méng

Thunder rumbles far away; mist hangs soft and grey.

日下对天中。

rì xià duì tiān zhōng

The capital below answers the heights above.

风高秋月白，雨霁晚霞红。

fēng gāo qiū yuè bái, yǔ jì wǎn xiá hóng

High wind, and the autumn moon shines white; the rain clears, and the sunset glows red.

牛女二星河左右，

niú nǚ èr xīng hé zuǒ yòu

The Cowherd and the Weaver Girl shine on either side of the Milky Way;

参商两曜斗西东。

shēn shāng liǎng yào dǒu xī dōng

Shen and Shang, two stars that never meet, sit west and east of the Dipper.

十月塞边，飒飒寒霜惊戍旅；

shí yuè sài biān, sà sà hán shuāng jīng shù lǚ

In the tenth month on the frontier, cold frost hisses down and startles the soldiers on guard;

三冬江上，漫漫朔雪冷渔翁。

sān dōng jiāng shàng, màn màn shuò xuě lěng yú wēng

through the long winter on the river, endless north-wind snow chills the old fisherman.
