导读About this poem

Twenty years after they last met, Du Fu turns up at an old friend’s door in wartime: the friend now has a house full of children, they eat spring chives cut in the rain and drink cup after cup — knowing tomorrow mountains will part them again.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗写一次战乱中的重逢。杜甫和老朋友卫八已经二十年没见面了，一天晚上突然在他家相聚。开头就是感叹：人生中朋友常常见不到面，就像参星和商星，一个升起另一个就落下。今晚是怎样难得的夜晚啊，我们竟能在同一盏灯下！接着说两人头发都白了，打听旧友，一半已经去世，惊叫起来，心里发热。二十年前分别时你还没结婚，现在儿女已经一大群，他们恭敬地向我问好，问我从哪里来。话还没说完，主人就催孩子们摆上了酒菜：雨夜里剪来的春韭，刚煮好的掺着黄米的饭。主人说见面太难了，一口气敬了十杯，我也没醉，因为感动于你的老交情。最后两句：明天我们又要被山岳隔开，世上的事，两个人都茫然不知了。全诗像讲故事，语言朴素，细节真实，把乱世中友情的珍贵和人生的无常写得非常动人。

In English

This poem records a reunion in wartime. Du Fu and his old friend Wei had not met for twenty years when, one evening, he found himself at Wei’s house. It opens with a sigh: in life, friends often never meet, like the stars Shen and Shang — when one rises, the other has set. What a night this is, that we share the light of the same lamp! Both of them are grey now; asking after old friends, they find half are dead, and they cry out in shock, hearts burning. Twenty years ago Wei wasn’t married; now he has a crowd of children, who greet their father’s friend politely and ask where he has come from. Before the questions are finished, Wei sends the children to lay out wine and food: spring chives cut in the night rain, freshly cooked rice mixed with yellow millet. The host says meetings are hard to come by and toasts ten cups in a row; Du Fu doesn’t get drunk, moved by the warmth of an old friendship. The ending: tomorrow mountains will stand between us, and neither of us knows what the world will bring. Told like a story, in plain words and with true-to-life details, the poem shows how precious friendship is in troubled times, and how uncertain life can be.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Du Fu (712–770) wrote this in spring 759, during the An Lushan Rebellion, on his way back to his post in Huazhou. Nothing is known of Wei the Eighth beyond this poem; “the Eighth” is his rank among his brothers and cousins, and a 处士 is a man of learning who never took office.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

处士 chǔ shì；参与商 shēn（参星，不读 cān）；少壮 shào；半为鬼 wéi；载 zǎi；重上 chóng；成行 háng（成排）；父执 zhí；新炊间黄粱 jiàn（掺杂，不读 jiān）；一举累十觞 lěi（接连）；觞 shāng。

版本说明 About the text

采用《全唐诗》文本。“问答乃未已”，《唐诗三百首》等本作“问答未及已”；“驱儿罗酒浆”，有的选本作“儿女罗酒浆”；“剪春韭”的“剪”，古本作“翦”（同义），意思都相同。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

人生不相见，

rén shēng bù xiāng jiàn

In this life, friends so seldom meet —

动如参与商。

dòng rú shēn yǔ shāng

like the two stars that never share the sky.

今夕复何夕，

jīn xī fù hé xī

What night is this, of all nights,

共此灯烛光。

gòng cǐ dēng zhú guāng

that we sit in the same lamplight?

少壮能几时，

shào zhuàng néng jǐ shí

How long were we ever young?

鬓发各已苍。

bìn fà gè yǐ cāng

Now both of us are grey.

访旧半为鬼，

fǎng jiù bàn wéi guǐ

We ask after old friends — half are dead;

惊呼热中肠。

jīng hū rè zhōng cháng

we cry out, and something burns inside.

焉知二十载，

yān zhī èr shí zǎi

Who knew that after twenty years

重上君子堂。

chóng shàng jūn zǐ táng

I would stand in your hall again?

昔别君未婚，

xī bié jūn wèi hūn

When we parted, you had no wife;

儿女忽成行。

ér nǚ hū chéng háng

now, all at once, a row of children.

怡然敬父执，

yí rán jìng fù zhí

They greet their father’s friend with smiles

问我来何方。

wèn wǒ lái hé fāng

and ask me where I’ve come from.

问答乃未已，

wèn dá nǎi wèi yǐ

Before the questions are done,

驱儿罗酒浆。

qū ér luó jiǔ jiāng

he sends the children to lay out the wine.

夜雨剪春韭，

yè yǔ jiǎn chūn jiǔ

Spring chives cut in the night rain,

新炊间黄粱。

xīn chuī jiàn huáng liáng

rice fresh from the pot, mixed with golden millet.

主称会面难，

zhǔ chēng huì miàn nán

“Meetings are hard to come by,” says my host,

一举累十觞。

yī jǔ lěi shí shāng

and drinks ten cups straight off.

十觞亦不醉，

shí shāng yì bù zuì

Ten cups, and I am not drunk,

感子故意长。

gǎn zǐ gù yì cháng

so moved by your faithful old friendship.

明日隔山岳，

míng rì gé shān yuè

Tomorrow, mountains will lie between us,

世事两茫茫。

shì shì liǎng máng máng

and neither of us knows what the world will bring.
