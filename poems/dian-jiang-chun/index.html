导读About this poem

A girl has just jumped off a swing when a visitor walks in. She runs off shyly, one shoe half on and her hairpin slipping — then stops at the door and pretends to smell a green plum, so she can steal a look back.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首词写一个少女的一瞬间，像一组电影镜头。上阕：她刚荡完秋千，懒懒地揉着细嫩的手；花上的露水还很浓，她出了一层薄汗，透过了轻薄的衣衫。下阕忽然有客人进来，她吓了一跳，来不及穿鞋，只穿着袜子就跑，头上的金钗也滑落了。“和羞走”——又害羞又慌张地跑开；可是跑到门口，她又靠着门回头，假装去闻青梅的香味，其实是想偷偷看一眼来客。最后一个“嗅”字最传神：害羞、好奇、一点小心思全在这个动作里。全词没有写一句心理活动，全靠动作表现人物，非常生动。一般认为是李清照少女时代的作品，也有人怀疑不是她写的。

In English

This lyric captures a few seconds in a girl’s life, almost like a sequence of film shots. In the first half she has just got off the swing and lazily rubs her slender hands. The dew is still heavy on the flowers; she has worked up a light sweat that shows through her thin clothes. Then, in the second half, a visitor walks in unannounced. Startled, she runs — in her stockings, no time for shoes, her gold hairpin sliding out of her hair. She hurries off, embarrassed. But at the doorway she leans against the frame and turns her head, pretending to smell a green plum, when really she wants one more look at the guest. That final verb, 嗅 (“to sniff”), says everything: shyness, curiosity and a little secret wish, all in one small gesture. The poem never tells us what she feels; it shows us only what she does, and that is why it feels so alive. Readers often guess the visitor is a young man she has heard of — perhaps her future husband — but the poem leaves that unsaid. It is traditionally counted among Li Qingzhao’s youthful works, though some scholars doubt she wrote it.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Li Qingzhao (1084–c. 1155) grew up in a cultured official family in Kaifeng. This lyric is traditionally attributed to her teenage years; its authorship has been questioned, and some early anthologies list it as anonymous or under other names.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

蹴 cù；慵 yōng；纤纤 xiān；薄汗 bó；袜刬 chǎn（只穿着袜子）；溜 liū；和羞 hé；嗅 xiù。“刬”“蹴”机器朗读易误，已在朗读用字中替换同音字，音频需人工听验。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行文本。作者有争议：一说为李清照少女时作，宋人词选中亦有署无名氏或他人者，网站按通行说法署李清照。异文：“轻衣透”一作“沾衣透”。
