导读About this poem

Vines creep over a grave; the woman who visits it asks, “Who is with me now? No one.” Often called the earliest Chinese poem of mourning for a husband or wife, it ends with a quiet promise: after a hundred years, I will lie beside you.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗一般被认为是中国最早的“悼亡诗”——为死去的丈夫或妻子写的诗。开头写坟地的景象：葛藤爬满了荆棘，蔹草蔓延在田野和墓地上，一片荒凉。“予美亡此”——我心爱的人不在了，长眠在这里。“谁与？独处！”自问自答，只有三个字，却写尽了孤单。第三章写到家里那只角枕、那床锦被还是那样鲜亮，人却已经不在，只能独自熬到天亮——用旧物写思念，最让人心酸。最后两章，“夏之日，冬之夜”，夏天的白天最长，冬天的夜晚最长，日子漫长难熬；两章把“日”和“夜”调换顺序，好像一年又一年地重复。结尾的心愿很平静：等我百年之后，就到你那里去，和你葬在一起。今天中国人说“百年之后”，就是对“去世”的委婉说法。

In English

This is often called the earliest Chinese poem of mourning for a husband or wife (悼亡诗). It begins at a grave: kudzu vines smother the thorn bushes, bindweed creeps across the fields and the burial ground. 予美亡此 — “the one I love is gone, and lies here.” Then three tiny words, a question and its answer: 谁与？独处！ — “Who is with me? I am alone.” In the third stanza we are back in the house: the pillow inlaid with horn and the brocade quilt still look bright and new, but the person who shared them is gone, and she lies awake alone until dawn. Old belongings bringing back the dead is one of the most moving moves in Chinese poetry. The last two stanzas say 夏之日，冬之夜 — summer days are the longest days, winter nights the longest nights — so time drags endlessly; swapping “day” and “night” in the final stanza makes it feel like year after year going round. The ending is calm: after my hundred years, I will come to your resting place and lie beside you. Chinese still says 百年之后, “after a hundred years”, as a gentle way of saying “after I die”.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

The Book of Songs gathers 305 songs from roughly the 11th to the 6th century BC. This one is from the “Airs of Tang” (唐风), the state of Jin in present-day Shanxi. Traditional commentators linked it to the long wars of Jin; the speaker is usually read as a widow.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

予 yú（我）；谁与 shuí yǔ；独处 chǔ；蔹 liǎn；蔓 màn；角枕 jiǎo zhěn；粲 càn；衾 qīn；域 yù。“谁”按古诗习惯注 shuí，口语读 shéi 亦可。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

葛生蒙楚，蔹蔓于野。

gé shēng méng chǔ, liǎn màn yú yě

Kudzu climbs over the thorn bushes, bindweed creeps across the fields.

予美亡此，谁与？独处！

yú měi wáng cǐ, shuí yǔ, dú chǔ

The one I love is gone and lies here. Who keeps me company? No one — I live alone.

葛生蒙棘，蔹蔓于域。

gé shēng méng jí, liǎn màn yú yù

Kudzu climbs over the thorns, bindweed creeps across the graves.

予美亡此，谁与？独息！

yú měi wáng cǐ, shuí yǔ, dú xī

The one I love is gone and lies here. Who keeps me company? No one — I lie down alone.

角枕粲兮，锦衾烂兮。

jiǎo zhěn càn xī, jǐn qīn làn xī

The pillow inlaid with horn still gleams, the brocade quilt still glows.

予美亡此，谁与？独旦！

yú měi wáng cǐ, shuí yǔ, dú dàn

The one I love is gone and lies here. Who keeps me company? No one — I wait alone for the dawn.

夏之日，冬之夜。

xià zhī rì, dōng zhī yè

Long summer days, long winter nights —

百岁之后，归于其居。

bǎi suì zhī hòu, guī yú qí jū

when my hundred years are done, I will go home to where he lies.

冬之夜，夏之日。

dōng zhī yè, xià zhī rì

Long winter nights, long summer days —

百岁之后，归于其室。

bǎi suì zhī hòu, guī yú qí shì

when my hundred years are done, I will join him in his room.
