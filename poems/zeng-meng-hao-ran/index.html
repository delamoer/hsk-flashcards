导读About this poem

The most famous poet of the Tang writes a fan letter to an older poet he admires: Meng Haoran gave up office for pine trees, clouds, moonlit wine and flowers — and Li Bai can only bow to him from afar.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

孟浩然比李白大十二岁，是唐代著名的山水田园诗人，一生没有做官。李白非常敬佩他，写了这首诗。第一联直接表白：我爱孟先生，他潇洒的风度天下闻名。第二联概括孟浩然的一生：年轻时就放弃了官位和车马，老了还高卧在松林白云之间。“红颜”对“白首”，一生的选择两句话就写完了。第三联写他的生活：在月下喝酒常常喝醉，迷恋山花不去侍奉君王。“中圣”是一个有趣的说法：古人把清酒叫“圣人”，所以“中圣”就是喝醉了。最后一联说：他的品格像高山一样，我怎么能仰望得到呢？只能在这里向他清高的人品行礼致敬。全诗语言自然，感情真诚，可以看到两位大诗人的友谊。

In English

Meng Haoran was twelve years older than Li Bai, a celebrated poet of landscapes and country life who never held office. Li Bai admired him enormously, and this poem says so outright. The first couplet is a plain declaration: I love Master Meng; the whole world knows his free, elegant spirit. The second sums up Meng’s life in two lines: with a youthful face he turned down official carriages and caps; with white hair he still lies at ease among pines and clouds. “Rosy face” against “white head” — a whole life’s choice, start to finish. The third couplet shows how he lives: often drunk under the moon, “struck by the sage”, and so in love with flowers that he will not serve the emperor. “Struck by the sage” is a joke: drinkers once nicknamed clear wine “the sage”, so being “struck” by it meant being drunk. The last couplet says Meng is like a high mountain — how could anyone hope to reach up to him? All Li Bai can do is bow from here to his pure character. The language is natural and the feeling sincere; it is a rare glimpse of one great poet looking up to another.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Li Bai (701–762) met Meng Haoran (689–740) in Xiangyang, Hubei, probably in the late 720s or the 730s, and the two became close friends. Li Bai also wrote the famous farewell “Seeing Meng Haoran off to Guangling at Yellow Crane Tower”.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

中圣 zhòng shèng（中：喝醉，像“中暑”的“中”）；轩冕 xuān miǎn；揖 yī；夫子 fū zǐ。机器朗读“中”易读成 zhōng，已在朗读用字中替换同音字。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行文本。异文：“徒此揖清芬”《全唐诗》作“徒此挹清芬”（挹：舀取，意为只能在此领受他的清香），网站用通行的“揖”（作揖致敬）。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

吾爱孟夫子，

wú ài mèng fū zǐ

I love you, Master Meng —

风流天下闻。

fēng liú tiān xià wén

the whole world knows your free and graceful spirit.

红颜弃轩冕，

hóng yán qì xuān miǎn

Young, you turned away from carriages and official caps;

白首卧松云。

bái shǒu wò sōng yún

white-haired, you lie at ease among pines and clouds.

醉月频中圣，

zuì yuè pín zhòng shèng

Under the moon, you are often happily drunk;

迷花不事君。

mí huā bù shì jūn

lost in flowers, you will not wait on any king.

高山安可仰，

gāo shān ān kě yǎng

You are a mountain too high for my eyes to reach;

徒此揖清芬。

tú cǐ yī qīng fēn

I can only bow from here to the clean fragrance of your name.
