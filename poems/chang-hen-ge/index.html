导读About this poem

The tragic love of Emperor Xuanzong and Yang Guifei, told in 120 lines. These excerpts give her famous smile and the ending — a secret vow on the seventh night of the seventh month, and a sorrow that outlasts heaven and earth.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

《长恨歌》是一首很长的叙事诗，讲唐玄宗和杨贵妃的爱情悲剧：玄宗宠爱杨贵妃，安史之乱中被迫让她死在马嵬坡，之后日夜思念，最后派道士到仙山找到她。网站选了两段。第一段写杨贵妃的美：她回头一笑，千娇百媚，后宫所有的美女都黯然失色。“回眸一笑”今天还常用来形容女子迷人的样子。第二段是结尾：杨贵妃托道士带话给玄宗，说起只有他们两人知道的誓言——七月七日，在长生殿，半夜无人的时候，他们悄悄约定：在天上愿做比翼鸟，在地上愿做连理枝。比翼鸟要两只一起才能飞，连理枝是两棵树的枝条长在一起，都比喻永远不分开。最后两句说：天和地再长久，也有尽头，这份遗憾却永远没有结束。题目里的“长恨”就在这里。

In English

“Song of Everlasting Sorrow” is a long narrative poem — 120 lines — about Emperor Xuanzong and his beloved consort Yang Guifei. He adored her; during the An Lushan Rebellion his own troops forced him to have her put to death; afterwards he longed for her day and night, until a Taoist priest found her spirit on an island of immortals. We give two passages. The first shows her beauty: when she turned her head and smiled, every other lady of the palace lost her colour. 回眸一笑 (“a smile over the shoulder”) is still how Chinese people describe a captivating woman. The second is the ending. Through the priest, she sends the emperor a vow only the two of them knew: on the seventh night of the seventh month, alone in the Hall of Long Life at midnight, they whispered, “In the sky, may we be two birds flying wing to wing; on earth, two trees with branches grown together.” Both images mean two that can never be separated. Then the final lines: heaven and earth, however long they last, will someday end — but this sorrow goes on forever. That is the “everlasting sorrow” of the title.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Bai Juyi (772–846) wrote this in 806, about fifty years after the events, while serving as a county official near Chang’an. The love story and the An Lushan Rebellion (755–763) are historical; the search for Yang Guifei’s spirit is poetic legend.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

一朝 zhāo（一旦，不读 cháo）；回眸 móu；粉黛 dài；重寄词 chóng（再次，不读 zhòng）；殷勤 yīn qín；比翼 bǐ yì；愿为 wéi（成为）；长生殿 cháng。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行文本。全诗 120 句、840 字，网站节选两段：第一段“天生丽质难自弃……六宫粉黛无颜色”（第 5–8 句，写杨贵妃入宫）；第二段“临别殷勤重寄词……此恨绵绵无绝期”（全诗最后 8 句）。两段之间省略了马嵬之变、玄宗思念、道士寻仙等大部分情节。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

天生丽质难自弃，

tiān shēng lì zhì nán zì qì

Beauty like hers could not stay hidden;

一朝选在君王侧。

yī zhāo xuǎn zài jūn wáng cè

one day she was chosen for the emperor’s side.

回眸一笑百媚生，

huí móu yī xiào bǎi mèi shēng

One smile as she turned her head, and a hundred charms bloomed;

六宫粉黛无颜色。

liù gōng fěn dài wú yán sè

every painted beauty of the palace faded beside her.

临别殷勤重寄词，

lín bié yīn qín chóng jì cí

At parting she sent one more message, earnest and warm,

词中有誓两心知。

cí zhōng yǒu shì liǎng xīn zhī

a vow that only their two hearts knew.

七月七日长生殿，

qī yuè qī rì cháng shēng diàn

The seventh night of the seventh month, in the Hall of Long Life,

夜半无人私语时。

yè bàn wú rén sī yǔ shí

at midnight, alone, they had whispered:

在天愿作比翼鸟，

zài tiān yuàn zuò bǐ yì niǎo

“In the sky, let us be two birds flying wing to wing;

在地愿为连理枝。

zài dì yuàn wéi lián lǐ zhī

on earth, two trees with branches grown as one.”

天长地久有时尽，

tiān cháng dì jiǔ yǒu shí jìn

Heaven and earth, long as they last, will one day end;

此恨绵绵无绝期。

cǐ hèn mián mián wú jué qī

this sorrow will go on and on, without end.
