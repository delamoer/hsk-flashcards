导读About this poem

Spring is ending, but the poet refuses to give up: a cuckoo cries until it “spits blood”, sure it can call the east wind back. A short poem about clinging to hope.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗写暮春（春天快结束的时候）。第一句说三月里残花落了，还有花接着开，春天还没走完。第二句写燕子每天飞回低矮的屋檐下，生活照常进行。后两句是全诗的重点：子规（杜鹃鸟）半夜还在啼叫，叫得嘴里都流出血来——这是古代的传说，说杜鹃啼得很苦，口中滴血。它为什么这么拼命？因为它不相信东风（春风）叫不回来。一般的送春诗都写伤感和无奈，这首诗却写出一种不服输的执着：明知春天要走，还要用尽力气把它留住。作者王令一生贫寒、志向很高，可能也把自己的心情写进了杜鹃身上。这种把人的感情放到动物身上的写法叫“拟人”和“寄托”。

In English

The poem is set in late spring. In the third month, the last flowers fall — and yet more keep opening, so spring is not quite over. Swallows fly back every day to nest under the low eaves; life goes on. The last two lines are the heart of the poem. A cuckoo is still crying at midnight, crying until it “weeps blood” — an old Chinese legend says this bird calls so bitterly that blood drips from its beak. Why does it try so hard? Because it refuses to believe the east wind, the spring wind, cannot be called back. Most poems about the end of spring are sad and resigned. This one is stubborn: the bird knows spring is leaving and still spends all its strength trying to keep it. Wang Ling was poor all his life but had great ambitions, and many readers feel he put his own spirit into the bird. Giving an animal human feelings in order to say something about yourself is a common technique in Chinese poetry, called 寄托 (“entrusting” one’s feelings to an image).

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Wang Ling (1032–1059) was a Northern Song poet from a poor family who refused to sit the official exams and made a living as a teacher. Wang Anshi admired his talent greatly. He died at just 27.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

三月残花落更开，

sān yuè cán huā luò gèng kāi

In late spring the last flowers fall, and still more bloom;

小檐日日燕飞来。

xiǎo yán rì rì yàn fēi lái

day after day the swallows return to the low eaves.

子规夜半犹啼血，

zǐ guī yè bàn yóu tí xuè

At midnight the cuckoo still cries till it bleeds —

不信东风唤不回。

bù xìn dōng fēng huàn bù huí

it won’t believe the spring wind can’t be called back.
