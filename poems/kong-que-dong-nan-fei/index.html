导读About this poem

The opening of China’s longest ancient narrative poem: a young wife, worked to exhaustion and still blamed by her mother-in-law, tells her husband she would rather be sent home. A tragedy of love crushed by family pressure.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

《孔雀东南飞》是中国古代最长的叙事诗之一，讲的是汉末一个真实的悲剧：焦仲卿和妻子刘兰芝感情很好，可是焦母不喜欢兰芝，逼儿子休妻，最后两人以死殉情。这里选的是开头。第一句“孔雀东南飞，五里一徘徊”是起兴：孔雀往东南飞去，飞五里就回头盘旋一次，舍不得离开——正像兰芝舍不得离开丈夫。接下来全是兰芝对丈夫说的话。她先说自己从十三岁到十六岁学会了织布、裁衣、弹箜篌、读诗书，是一个能干又有教养的女子；十七岁嫁过来，却常常心里苦。丈夫在官府做事，很少回家；她每天鸡叫就起来织布，夜夜不能休息，三天织五匹布，婆婆还嫌她慢。她说：不是我织得慢，是你家的媳妇太难做了！既然留下也没用，就请你告诉公婆，早点把我送回娘家吧。这段话有委屈，也有骨气。“十三……十四……十五……”的排比，简单明了，像民歌一样好记。

In English

“Southeast the Peacock Flies” is one of the longest narrative poems of ancient China. It tells a real tragedy from the end of the Han: Jiao Zhongqing and his wife Liu Lanzhi love each other, but his mother dislikes Lanzhi and forces her son to send her away. In the end both of them take their own lives. This page gives the opening. The first line is a xìng (“evoking”) image: a peacock flies southeast, but every five li it circles back, unwilling to leave — just as Lanzhi cannot bear to leave her husband. The rest is Lanzhi speaking to him. From thirteen to sixteen she learned to weave silk, cut clothes, play the harp and recite the classics: a capable, well-educated young woman. At seventeen she married him, yet her heart has been full of sorrow. He works at the prefecture office and is rarely home; she is at the loom from the first cockcrow and never rests at night. She weaves five lengths of cloth in three days, and still her mother-in-law calls her slow. “It isn’t that I weave slowly — it’s that being a wife in your family is impossible!” Since staying is no use, she asks him to tell his parents to send her home. Her words are hurt but proud. The counting pattern — thirteen, fourteen, fifteen — is simple and easy to remember, like a folk song.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

The poem first appears in the 6th-century anthology Yutai xinyong (玉台新咏) under the title “An Old Poem for Jiao Zhongqing’s Wife”. Its preface says the events happened in Lujiang (in today’s Anhui) at the end of the Han, around AD 196–219, and that people at the time wrote the poem in the couple’s memory. It was probably polished by later hands before being written down.

版本说明 About the text

节选开头至“及时相遣归”（共二十二句）。全诗三百五十多句、一千七百多字，原题《古诗为焦仲卿妻作》，最早见于《玉台新咏》，诗前有小序（网站未收）。采用通行教材文本，一联一行排版。《玉台新咏》所载正文从“十三能织素”起，“孔雀东南飞，五里一徘徊”两句据通行本（《乐府诗集》等）保留。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

孔雀东南飞，五里一徘徊。

kǒng què dōng nán fēi, wǔ lǐ yī pái huái

Southeast the peacock flies, and every five li it circles back.

“十三能织素，十四学裁衣，

shí sān néng zhī sù, shí sì xué cái yī

“At thirteen I could weave fine silk, at fourteen cut and sew,

十五弹箜篌，十六诵诗书。

shí wǔ tán kōng hóu, shí liù sòng shī shū

at fifteen I played the harp, at sixteen knew the classics by heart.

十七为君妇，心中常苦悲。

shí qī wéi jūn fù, xīn zhōng cháng kǔ bēi

At seventeen I became your wife — and my heart has ached ever since.

君既为府吏，守节情不移，

jūn jì wéi fǔ lì, shǒu jié qíng bù yí

You serve at the prefecture, true to your post and never wavering;

贱妾留空房，相见常日稀。

jiàn qiè liú kōng fáng, xiāng jiàn cháng rì xī

I am left alone in an empty room, and we hardly ever see each other.

鸡鸣入机织，夜夜不得息。

jī míng rù jī zhī, yè yè bù dé xī

At first cockcrow I’m at the loom, and no night brings me rest.

三日断五匹，大人故嫌迟。

sān rì duàn wǔ pǐ, dà rén gù xián chí

Five bolts of cloth in three days — and still your mother calls me slow.

非为织作迟，君家妇难为！

fēi wèi zhī zuò chí, jūn jiā fù nán wéi

It isn’t that I weave too slowly — being a wife in your house is impossible!

妾不堪驱使，徒留无所施，

qiè bù kān qū shǐ, tú liú wú suǒ shī

I can’t bear to be driven like this; staying on does no one any good.

便可白公姥，及时相遣归。”

biàn kě bái gōng mǔ, jí shí xiāng qiǎn guī

Go and tell your parents: send me home, and soon.”
