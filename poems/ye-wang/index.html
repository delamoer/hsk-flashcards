导读About this poem

At dusk a recluse looks out over autumn hills, herdsmen and hunters heading home — and finds no one he knows. An early, quiet example of the Tang regulated poem.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

王绩辞官以后隐居在家乡东皋，这首诗写他傍晚在东皋远望时的心情。第一联：傍晚站在东皋上眺望，走来走去，不知道心该依靠什么。第二联写远景：每一棵树都染上了秋天的颜色，每一座山都只剩下落日的余光。“树树”“山山”两个叠词，让画面一下子铺开，安静又有点冷清。第三联写近景：放牧的人赶着小牛回家，打猎的人骑着马、带着猎物回来。这是一幅很有生活气息的田园画面，大家都有归处。可是第四联一转：彼此看看，却没有一个认识的人，只好放声长歌，怀念古代采薇的隐士伯夷、叔齐。别人都在归家，诗人却找不到知己，孤独感就出来了。这首诗语言朴素自然，格式已经是成熟的五言律诗（八句，中间两联对仗），在唐诗发展史上很重要，统编教材八年级上册也选了它。

In English

After giving up office, Wang Ji lived as a recluse at Donggao (“East Bank”) in his home district. Here he stands there at dusk and looks out. Couplet one: he gazes into the evening, pacing back and forth — what can his heart lean on? Couplet two is the wide view: every tree is coloured by autumn, every hill holds only the last light of sunset. The doubled words 树树 (“tree after tree”) and 山山 (“hill after hill”) spread the scene across the whole horizon, calm but a little bleak. Couplet three comes closer: a herdsman drives his calves home; hunters ride back with birds hanging from their saddles. It is a warm, lively country scene, and everyone in it has somewhere to go. Then couplet four turns: they look at one another, but no one knows him. So he sings aloud and thinks of the ancient hermits Boyi and Shuqi, who lived on wild vetch (薇) in the mountains rather than serve a ruler they thought unjust. Everyone else is heading home; the poet has no kindred spirit. That contrast is where the loneliness comes from. The language is plain and natural, and the form is already a mature five-character regulated poem: eight lines, with the two middle couplets in parallel. That makes it an important early landmark in Tang poetry, and it is taught in Chinese middle schools.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Wang Ji (c. 590–644) lived through the fall of the Sui and the rise of the Tang. He served briefly in office but preferred farming, drinking and writing, calling himself “Master of the East Bank” (东皋子). This poem is thought to date from his years of retirement.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

东皋薄暮望，

dōng gāo bó mù wàng

At dusk I stand on the East Bank and look out,

徙倚欲何依。

xǐ yǐ yù hé yī

pacing back and forth — what is there to lean on?

树树皆秋色，

shù shù jiē qiū sè

Tree after tree has turned to autumn;

山山唯落晖。

shān shān wéi luò huī

hill after hill holds only the fading sunlight.

牧人驱犊返，

mù rén qū dú fǎn

A herdsman drives his calves home;

猎马带禽归。

liè mǎ dài qín guī

hunters ride back with birds across their saddles.

相顾无相识，

xiāng gù wú xiāng shí

We look at each other — I know none of them;

长歌怀采薇。

cháng gē huái cǎi wēi

so I sing out loud, and think of the hermits who lived on wild greens.
