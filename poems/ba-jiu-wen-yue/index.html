导读About this poem

Cup in hand, Li Bai asks the moon how long it has been up there. The answer leads to one of the most quoted couplets in Chinese: people today never saw the ancient moon, yet this moon once shone on the ancients.

第一段Part 1
01
青天有月来几时？我今停杯一问之。

直译LiteralSince when has the blue sky had a moon? I now stop my cup to ask it.

意译MeaningHow long has the moon hung in the blue sky? Tonight I put down my cup to ask.

02
人攀明月不可得，月行却与人相随。

直译LiteralPeople climb for the bright moon and cannot get it; yet the moon travels along with people.

意译MeaningNo one can climb up and reach it, yet wherever we walk, the moon walks with us.

第二段Part 2
03
皎如飞镜临丹阙，绿烟灭尽清辉发。

直译LiteralBright as a flying mirror over the red palace gates; when the green mist is gone, its clear light shines forth.

意译MeaningWhite as a flying mirror above the red palace gates, it pours out cool light once the green mist clears.

04
但见宵从海上来，宁知晓向云间没。

直译LiteralWe only see it come from the sea at night; how would we know it sinks among the clouds at dawn?

意译MeaningWe watch it rise from the sea each night — but where does it slip away among the clouds at dawn?

第三段Part 3
05
白兔捣药秋复春，嫦娥孤栖与谁邻？

直译LiteralThe white rabbit pounds medicine, autumn after spring; Chang’e dwells alone — who is her neighbour?

意译MeaningThe white rabbit pounds its herbs through autumn and spring; Chang’e lives alone — who keeps her company?

06
今人不见古时月，今月曾经照古人。

直译LiteralPeople today do not see the moon of ancient times; today’s moon once shone on the ancients.

意译MeaningWe never saw the moon the ancients saw, yet this same moon once shone on them.

第四段Part 4
07
古人今人若流水，共看明月皆如此。

直译LiteralAncients and moderns are like flowing water; all looked at the bright moon just like this.

意译MeaningPeople then and people now flow past like water, and all of them looked up at the moon like this.

08
唯愿当歌对酒时，月光长照金樽里。

直译LiteralI only wish that when I sing and face my wine, moonlight would always shine into the golden cup.

意译MeaningAll I ask: whenever I sing over my wine, let the moonlight keep shining in my golden cup.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

题下有小注：老朋友贾淳让我问问月亮。诗人端着酒杯问：天上的月亮是什么时候有的？接着写月亮的奇妙：人想攀上去够不着，可人走到哪里它就跟到哪里；它亮得像飞在天上的镜子照着宫殿，云雾散尽，清光四射；只见它夜里从海上升起，谁知道天亮时它在云间消失到哪里去了？又想到月宫里的玉兔年年捣药，嫦娥孤孤单单，有谁和她做邻居？最有名的是“今人不见古时月，今月曾经照古人”：我们看不到古人看过的月亮，但今天的月亮却照过古人。古人和今人都像流水一样过去了，看到的却是同一个月亮。人生短暂，月亮永恒，所以诗人最后说：只希望唱歌喝酒的时候，月光能一直照在我的金杯里。全诗把疑问、想象和人生感慨连在一起，自由奔放。

In English

A note under the title says an old friend, Jia Chun, asked the poet to put a question to the moon. So Li Bai lifts his cup and asks: how long has there been a moon in the sky? Then he marvels at it. People try to climb up to it and can’t, yet wherever they walk it follows them. It shines like a flying mirror over the red palace gates; when the green mist clears, its cool light pours out. We see it rise from the sea at night — but who knows where it vanishes among the clouds at dawn? He wonders about the Jade Rabbit pounding medicine year after year, and Chang’e living alone on the moon: who is her neighbour? Then comes the famous couplet: “People today never see the moon of ancient times, but today’s moon once shone on the ancients.” Generations come and go like flowing water, yet they all look up at the same moon. Life is short and the moon lasts, so he ends simply: all I wish is that whenever I sing and drink, the moonlight will keep shining into my golden cup. Questions, myth and reflection on time run freely together, in Li Bai’s easy, soaring style.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Li Bai (701–762) wrote many poems about the moon and wine. This one is undated; the friend Jia Chun mentioned in the title note is otherwise unknown. The poem is often read alongside Su Shi’s later “Prelude to Water Melody”, which also questions the moon.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

宁知 nìng（岂、难道）；云间没 mò（消失）；嫦娥孤栖 qī；曾经 céng；长照 cháng；唯 wéi；共看 kàn。机器朗读“宁”“没”易读错，已在朗读用字中替换同音字。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行文本，题下原注“故人贾淳令予问之”。全诗十六句，网站按两句一行排为八行。异文：“嫦娥孤栖”《全唐诗》作“姮娥孤栖”（姮娥即嫦娥，汉代避汉文帝刘恒讳改称嫦娥），网站用通行的“嫦娥”。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

青天有月来几时？我今停杯一问之。

qīng tiān yǒu yuè lái jǐ shí, wǒ jīn tíng bēi yī wèn zhī

How long has the moon hung in the blue sky? Tonight I put down my cup to ask.

人攀明月不可得，月行却与人相随。

rén pān míng yuè bù kě dé, yuè xíng què yǔ rén xiāng suí

No one can climb up and reach it, yet wherever we walk, the moon walks with us.

皎如飞镜临丹阙，绿烟灭尽清辉发。

jiǎo rú fēi jìng lín dān què, lǜ yān miè jìn qīng huī fā

White as a flying mirror above the red palace gates, it pours out cool light once the green mist clears.

但见宵从海上来，宁知晓向云间没。

dàn jiàn xiāo cóng hǎi shàng lái, nìng zhī xiǎo xiàng yún jiān mò

We watch it rise from the sea each night — but where does it slip away among the clouds at dawn?

白兔捣药秋复春，嫦娥孤栖与谁邻？

bái tù dǎo yào qiū fù chūn, cháng é gū qī yǔ shuí lín

The white rabbit pounds its herbs through autumn and spring; Chang’e lives alone — who keeps her company?

今人不见古时月，今月曾经照古人。

jīn rén bù jiàn gǔ shí yuè, jīn yuè céng jīng zhào gǔ rén

We never saw the moon the ancients saw, yet this same moon once shone on them.

古人今人若流水，共看明月皆如此。

gǔ rén jīn rén ruò liú shuǐ, gòng kàn míng yuè jiē rú cǐ

People then and people now flow past like water, and all of them looked up at the moon like this.

唯愿当歌对酒时，月光长照金樽里。

wéi yuàn dāng gē duì jiǔ shí, yuè guāng cháng zhào jīn zūn lǐ

All I ask: whenever I sing over my wine, let the moonlight keep shining in my golden cup.
