导读About this poem

A poem about snow that never shows the snow: the poet feels his quilt go cold, sees the window glow, and hears bamboo snapping under the weight. Everything reaches him through touch, light and sound.

中文解析

这首诗写夜里下大雪，可是诗人一直躺在床上，并没有亲眼看雪。他是怎么知道的呢？第一句是感觉：被子和枕头冷得让人吃惊。第二句是视觉：窗户亮了起来——雪光把夜晚照亮了。第三句是推断：夜深了，雪一定下得很大。第四句是听觉：不时听到竹子被雪压断的声音。诗人从触觉、视觉、听觉一步步写，没有正面描写雪花，却让读者感到雪又大又重，这叫“侧面描写”。“折竹声”更是用声音衬出夜的安静：四周静得能听见竹子折断。这首诗写于诗人被贬官的第二年冬天，冷被、寒窗、独自醒着的夜，也透出一点孤单。

In English

This poem is about a heavy snowfall at night — yet the poet never actually looks at the snow; he is lying in bed. So how does he know? Line one is touch: the quilt and pillow are surprisingly cold. Line two is sight: the window has turned bright — snow-light glowing in the dark. Line three is reasoning: it’s late, so the snow must be heavy. Line four is sound: now and then he hears bamboo snapping under its weight. Moving from touch to sight to hearing, he never describes a single snowflake, yet we feel how deep and heavy the snow is. Showing something through its effects instead of describing it directly is called cèmiàn miáoxiě (indirect description). The sound of breaking bamboo also highlights the silence: it is so quiet he can hear it. The poem dates from his second winter after being demoted, and the cold bed, the bright window and the lonely, wakeful night carry a touch of solitude too.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Bai Juyi (772–846) was demoted in 815 to Jiangzhou (today’s Jiujiang) after angering powerful officials. This poem is traditionally dated to the winter of 816, during his exile there.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

已讶衾枕冷，

yǐ yà qīn zhěn lěng

Surprised to find my quilt and pillow cold,

复见窗户明。

fù jiàn chuāng hù míng

I see the window glowing pale.

夜深知雪重，

yè shēn zhī xuě zhòng

Deep in the night, I know the snow lies thick —

时闻折竹声。

shí wén zhé zhú shēng

now and then, the crack of bamboo giving way.
