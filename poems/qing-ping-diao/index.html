导读About this poem

Written on the spot for the emperor and his beloved Yang Guifei as they admired peonies, this quatrain praises a woman and a flower at the same time — so smoothly you can’t tell where one ends and the other begins.

这首诗被谱成了歌Sung as a song

王菲、邓丽君《清平调》Faye Wong & Teresa Teng

Teresa Teng recorded a demo of Li Bai's three "Qingping" poems that was never released; in 2015 Faye Wong added her voice, making a duet across time. The lyric is the three poems in full.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

传说唐玄宗和杨贵妃在宫中沉香亭赏牡丹，叫李白当场写新歌词，李白写了三首《清平调》，这是第一首。第一句非常有名：“云想衣裳花想容”——看到云，就想到她的衣裳；看到花，就想到她的容貌。也可以理解为：云想要像她的衣裳，花想要像她的容貌。一句话里，人、云、花互相比喻，分不清是在写人还是写花。第二句写春风吹过栏杆，花上的露水又多又亮，暗写牡丹正盛开，也暗写贵妃受到宠爱。后两句说：这样的美人，不是在仙山群玉山上才能见到，就是在瑶台的月光下才能遇到——直接把她比作天上的仙女。全诗不说一个“美”字，却写尽了美，想象丰富，语言华丽又自然。

In English

The story goes that Emperor Xuanzong and his consort Yang Guifei were admiring peonies in the palace when he summoned Li Bai to write new song lyrics on the spot. Li Bai produced three “Qingping Songs”; this is the first. Its opening line is famous for being two things at once: 云想衣裳花想容 can mean “clouds make me think of her dress, flowers of her face” — or “the clouds long to be her dress, the flowers long to be her face”. Woman, cloud and flower blur into one another, and we can’t tell whether the poem is about the lady or the peony. Line two shows the spring breeze brushing the railing and dew sparkling thick on the petals: the peonies are at their peak — and so, quietly, is the emperor’s favour towards her. The last two lines say that someone like this could only be seen on the Mountain of Jade or met under the moon at the Jasper Terrace, both homes of goddesses in legend. The poem never uses the word “beautiful”; it simply keeps comparing her to lovelier and lovelier things until she is no longer of this world.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Li Bai (701–762) served briefly at court in Chang’an as a poet attending the emperor, around 742–744. The occasion described — peony viewing at the Aloeswood Pavilion — comes from a Tang anecdote and is traditionally accepted, though details may be embellished.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

云想衣裳花想容，

yún xiǎng yī cháng huā xiǎng róng

Clouds dream of being her gown, and flowers of being her face;

春风拂槛露华浓。

chūn fēng fú jiàn lù huá nóng

the spring breeze strokes the railing, and the petals glisten with dew.

若非群玉山头见，

ruò fēi qún yù shān tóu jiàn

If you have not glimpsed her on the Mountain of Jade,

会向瑶台月下逢。

huì xiàng yáo tái yuè xià féng

you will meet her by moonlight on the Jasper Terrace of the gods.
