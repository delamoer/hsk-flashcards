Su Mu Zhe: Blue Clouds in the Sky

导读About this poem

A wide autumn landscape — blue sky, yellow leaves, misty water, grass stretching past the setting sun — slowly turns into homesickness. Only good dreams let the traveller sleep, and the wine he drinks comes back as tears.

这首诗被谱成了歌Sung as a song

邓丽君《芳草无情》Teresa Teng

Teresa Teng's 1983 album Light Exquisite Feeling (淡淡幽情) set twelve classical poems to new music; this one sings Fan Zhongyan's "Su Mu Zhe" (blue clouds, yellow leaves) in full, twice through.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首词写秋天里游子的思乡之情。上阕是一幅很开阔的秋景图：天上是碧蓝的云，地上铺满黄叶，秋色一直连到水波上，水面上笼罩着带着寒意的翠绿烟雾。远山映着夕阳，天和水连在一起。最后说芳草“无情”，一直长到夕阳以外更远的地方。芳草本身当然没有感情，说它“无情”，是因为芳草一直延伸到天边，让人想到家乡比天边还远，草越远，乡愁越长。这是借景抒情：通过写景来表达感情。下阕直接写愁：思乡让人黯然伤神，旅途中的愁思缠着人不放；除非每天夜里做个回家的好梦，才能睡得着。明月照着高楼，千万不要一个人去靠着栏杆远望，因为越望越想家；想借酒消愁，酒喝进愁苦的心里，却都变成了相思的眼泪。范仲淹是一位刚强的政治家、军事家，这首词却写得非常柔情。开头“碧云天，黄叶地”后来被元代《西厢记》借用，成了名句。

In English

A traveller far from home feels homesick in autumn. The first half is a wide landscape painting: blue clouds above, yellow leaves covering the ground, autumn colour stretching onto the water, and over the waves a chilly green mist. Distant hills catch the setting sun; sky and water meet. Then the poet calls the fragrant grass “heartless”, because it keeps growing on and on, even beyond the setting sun. Grass has no feelings, of course — but grass running to the horizon reminds him that home lies even farther away. This is 借景抒情, “expressing feeling through scenery”, a basic technique of Chinese poetry. The second half names the sorrow directly. Homesickness darkens his spirit and travel-weariness clings to him; he can only sleep on nights when a good dream takes him home. When the moon lights the tall tower, don’t lean on the railing alone — the more you gaze, the more you miss home. He tries to drown his sorrow in wine, but the wine goes into his aching heart and comes out as tears of longing. Fan Zhongyan was a tough statesman and military commander, yet this lyric is remarkably tender. Its opening, 碧云天，黄叶地, was later borrowed by the Yuan play Romance of the Western Chamber and became even more famous.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Fan Zhongyan (989–1052) was a Northern Song statesman and reformer who also defended the northwest frontier. When this lyric was written is uncertain; it is often linked to his years away from the capital on official postings.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

斜阳 xié yáng；更在 gèng；黯 àn；休独倚 yǐ。旅思：按名词“愁思”读 sī（今通行读法）；旧注依古音读 sì，网站暂采用 sī。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行文本，各本基本一致。题目一作《苏幕遮·怀旧》。异文：“黯乡魂”一作“暗乡魂”，网站从通行本作“黯”。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

碧云天，黄叶地，

bì yún tiān, huáng yè dì

Blue clouds above, yellow leaves below;

秋色连波，波上寒烟翠。

qiū sè lián bō, bō shàng hán yān cuì

autumn runs into the water, where a cold green mist hangs over the waves.

山映斜阳天接水，

shān yìng xié yáng tiān jiē shuǐ

Hills glow in the evening sun, the sky meets the river,

芳草无情，更在斜阳外。

fāng cǎo wú qíng, gèng zài xié yáng wài

and the heartless grass runs on past the sunset.

黯乡魂，追旅思，

àn xiāng hún, zhuī lǚ sī

Homesickness dims my spirit, a traveller’s gloom follows me,

夜夜除非，好梦留人睡。

yè yè chú fēi, hǎo mèng liú rén shuì

and every night I sleep only if a sweet dream holds me.

明月楼高休独倚，

míng yuè lóu gāo xiū dú yǐ

When the moon is bright, don’t lean alone on the high tower’s rail;

酒入愁肠，化作相思泪。

jiǔ rù chóu cháng, huà zuò xiāng sī lèi

the wine goes down into a grieving heart and comes back up as tears.
