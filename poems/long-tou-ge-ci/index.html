陇头歌辞

Songs of the Long Mountains

导读About this poem

Three tiny songs from travellers crossing the bleak Long Mountains on the way west: a stream tumbling downhill, a cold so bitter their tongues curl back, and one last look towards the home plain they are leaving.

第一章Stanza 1
01
陇头流水，流离山下。

直译LiteralThe flowing water of Longtou scatters down the mountain.

意译MeaningThe stream on the Long Mountains runs scattering down the slopes.

02
念吾一身，飘然旷野。

直译LiteralI think of myself, one body, drifting in the open wilds.

意译MeaningAnd I, all alone, drift like it across the empty land.

第二章Stanza 2
03
朝发欣城，暮宿陇头。

直译LiteralIn the morning I set out from Xincheng; at dusk I lodge at Longtou.

意译MeaningI leave Xincheng at daybreak and sleep on the Long Mountains at dusk.

04
寒不能语，舌卷入喉。

直译LiteralSo cold I cannot speak; my tongue curls back into my throat.

意译MeaningIt’s too cold to speak — my tongue shrinks back into my throat.

第三章Stanza 3
05
陇头流水，鸣声幽咽。

直译LiteralThe flowing water of Longtou — its sound is a low, choked sobbing.

意译MeaningThe stream on the Long Mountains sounds like someone sobbing.

06
遥望秦川，心肝断绝。

直译LiteralGazing far off at the Qin plain, my heart and liver break.

意译MeaningI gaze back at the Qin plain, far away, and my heart breaks in two.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

陇头就是陇山（在今天陕西、甘肃交界），翻过陇山往西就是荒凉的边地。古代去西北当兵、服役的人，走到这里回头一望，家乡就看不见了，所以陇山成了“离乡”的象征。这组民歌共三首，每首四句、每句四个字，非常简短。第一首：陇山的流水向山下四处流散，我孤零零一个人，就像这水一样飘荡在旷野里——用流水比自己，叫“比喻”，也可以看作“起兴”。第二首写赶路的辛苦：早上从欣城出发，晚上住在陇头，冷得说不出话来，舌头都缩进喉咙里了。这个夸张非常真实，读者好像也感到了那种冷。第三首又回到流水，这次写它的声音低低的，像人在哽咽；远远望着东边的秦川（关中平原，也就是家乡），心肝都要断了。三首从景到人、从身体的苦到心里的苦，层层加深。北朝民歌语言质朴、感情直接，和南朝情歌的柔美很不一样。

In English

Longtou means the Long Mountains, on today’s border between Shaanxi and Gansu. Beyond them lay the dry, empty frontier. Soldiers and labourers heading northwest would turn round here for a last look home — so the Long Mountains came to mean “leaving home”. These three folk songs are tiny: four lines each, four characters per line. In the first, a mountain stream scatters as it runs downhill, and the singer, alone, drifts across the open wilds just like that water. In the second, he sets out from Xincheng at dawn and reaches Longtou by nightfall; it is so cold he can’t speak — his tongue curls back into his throat. It’s an exaggeration, but you can almost feel the cold. The third song returns to the stream, its voice now a low, choked sob. Far away to the east lies the Qin plain, his home, and his heart and liver feel torn apart (in Chinese, the liver is a seat of feeling too). The songs move from scenery to the man, from bodily pain to heartache. Northern folk songs are plain and direct, a world away from the soft love songs of the south.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

These anonymous songs come from the Northern Dynasties (4th–6th century) and were preserved among the “drum and horn” songs of the Liang court in the Collection of Yuefu Poems. Similar lines about the Long Mountain stream were already quoted in earlier records, so the song may be older still.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

朝发欣城 的“朝”读 zhāo（早晨），不读 cháo。幽咽 读 yōu yè，“咽”不读 yān 或 yàn（机器朗读易误，已在“朗读用字”列换成同音字）。舌卷入喉 的“卷”读 juǎn。陇 读 lǒng。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行本（据《乐府诗集》卷二十五“梁鼓角横吹曲”，题作《陇头歌辞》，共三曲）。三首合为一篇，网站按两个四字句为一行排版，每首一段。“鸣声幽咽”一些选本作“鸣声呜咽”。同卷另有《陇头流水歌辞》，文字相近，是另一组歌。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

陇头流水，流离山下。

lǒng tóu liú shuǐ, liú lí shān xià

The stream on the Long Mountains runs scattering down the slopes.

念吾一身，飘然旷野。

niàn wú yī shēn, piāo rán kuàng yě

And I, all alone, drift like it across the empty land.

朝发欣城，暮宿陇头。

zhāo fā xīn chéng, mù sù lǒng tóu

I leave Xincheng at daybreak and sleep on the Long Mountains at dusk.

寒不能语，舌卷入喉。

hán bù néng yǔ, shé juǎn rù hóu

It’s too cold to speak — my tongue shrinks back into my throat.

陇头流水，鸣声幽咽。

lǒng tóu liú shuǐ, míng shēng yōu yè

The stream on the Long Mountains sounds like someone sobbing.

遥望秦川，心肝断绝。

yáo wàng qín chuān, xīn gān duàn jué

I gaze back at the Qin plain, far away, and my heart breaks in two.
