导读About this poem

Four lines, four pictures of a spring day: sunlit hills, a fragrant breeze, swallows building nests, ducks dozing on warm sand. A gentle, easy poem from a poet usually known for sorrow.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这是一首描写春天的小诗，每一句都是一幅画。第一句写大景：春天白天变长了，阳光照着江和山，非常明丽。第二句写感觉：春风吹来，带着花草的香味——不只是看，还能闻到。第三句写动的：泥土化开变软了，燕子飞来飞去，衔泥做窝。第四句写静的：沙滩被太阳晒暖了，一对对鸳鸯在上面睡觉。一动一静，一远一近，春天的温暖、明亮、热闹都写出来了。杜甫一生经历很多苦难，这首诗是他在成都过上一段安定日子时写的，所以特别轻松愉快。诗里的字都很简单，很适合初学者背诵。

In English

Each line of this little spring poem is a picture of its own. The first is the wide view: the days are getting longer, and the river and hills are bright in the sun. The second brings in another sense — the spring breeze carries the smell of flowers and grass. The third line has movement: the earth has thawed into soft mud, and swallows dart about, carrying it off to build their nests. The fourth is stillness: on sand warmed by the sun, pairs of mandarin ducks are asleep. Far and near, moving and still, the four lines together give the whole feel of a warm, bright spring day. Du Fu lived through war, poverty and exile, and most of his famous poems are heavy. This one was written during a rare peaceful stretch in Chengdu, and it shows — it is relaxed and happy. The vocabulary is very simple, which makes it a good poem for beginners to learn by heart.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Du Fu (712–770) wrote this around 764, while living in his thatched cottage outside Chengdu, Sichuan, one of the calmest periods of his life. He wrote many poems simply titled “Quatrain”; this one is known by its first line.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

迟日江山丽，

chí rì jiāng shān lì

The spring days lengthen; river and hills shine bright,

春风花草香。

chūn fēng huā cǎo xiāng

the spring breeze smells of flowers and grass.

泥融飞燕子，

ní róng fēi yàn zi

The mud has softened, and swallows swoop;

沙暖睡鸳鸯。

shā nuǎn shuì yuān yāng

on the warm sand, mandarin ducks doze.
