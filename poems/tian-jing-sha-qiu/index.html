导读About this poem

Same tune, same season, same trick of stringing nouns together as “Autumn Thoughts” — but the mood turns from grey to full colour. A great poem to read side by side with Ma Zhiyuan’s.

中文解析

这首小令和马致远的《天净沙·秋思》用的是同一个曲牌，也同样用名词意象叠加的写法，可以放在一起读。前两句写的是一个有点冷清的秋日黄昏：孤零零的村庄、落日、残霞，淡淡的炊烟、老树、寒鸦。接着“一点飞鸿影下”，一只大雁的影子从天上掠过，画面从静变动。最后两句一下子变了：青山、绿水、白草、红叶、黄花——青、绿、白、红、黄，五种颜色排在一起，像一幅明亮的彩色画。前面冷清，后面明丽，秋天不只是悲凉的，也是绚烂的。和《秋思》比较：《秋思》最后落到“断肠人在天涯”，满是思乡的愁；这首没有写人，只写景，情绪从寂寞慢慢转向开阔明朗。同样的写法，可以写出不同的心情。

In English

This song uses the same tune as Ma Zhiyuan’s “Autumn Thoughts” and the same technique of stacking noun images, so the two are perfect to compare. The first two lines paint a rather lonely autumn evening: a solitary village, the setting sun, the last red clouds; a thin wisp of smoke, an old tree, crows in the cold. Then “a single wild goose’s shadow drops past” — something moves across the still picture. The final two lines change everything: blue-green hills, green water, white grass, red leaves, yellow flowers. Five colours in a row, like a bright painting. The lonely first half gives way to a brilliant second half: autumn is not only sad, it is also gorgeous. Compare the two songs. “Autumn Thoughts” ends with a heartbroken traveller far from home — it is all about homesickness. Bai Pu puts no person in the scene at all; the mood simply moves from quiet loneliness to openness and light. The same method can carry very different feelings.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Bai Pu (1226–after 1306) lost his mother in the fall of the Jin dynasty as a child and never took office under the Yuan, spending his life as a writer. He is one of the “Four Great Masters” of Yuan drama. 天净沙 is the name of the tune.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

孤村落日残霞，

gū cūn luò rì cán xiá

A lonely village, the setting sun, the last red clouds;

轻烟老树寒鸦，

qīng yān lǎo shù hán yā

thin smoke, an old tree, crows in the cold;

一点飞鸿影下。

yī diǎn fēi hóng yǐng xià

the shadow of one wild goose flicks down the sky.

青山绿水，

qīng shān lǜ shuǐ

Blue-green hills, green water,

白草红叶黄花。

bái cǎo hóng yè huáng huā

white grass, red leaves, yellow flowers.
