白马篇

Bái Mǎ Piān
The White Horse
曹植Cáo Zhí · Cao Zhi魏晋南北朝 Wei, Jin & Southern and Northern Dynasties

导读About this poem

A young rider on a white horse with a golden bridle gallops to the northwest frontier — a dazzling archer who will give his life for his country without a second thought. This is the poem that created the Chinese image of the youthful knight-errant.

第一段Part 1
01
白马饰金羁，连翩西北驰。

直译LiteralA white horse adorned with a golden bridle, gallops on and on toward the northwest.

意译MeaningA white horse in a golden bridle flashes off toward the northwest.

02
借问谁家子，幽并游侠儿。

直译LiteralMay I ask whose son he is? A wandering knight from You and Bing.

意译Meaning“Whose son is that?” — a young knight-errant from the northern borderlands.

第二段Part 2
03
少小去乡邑，扬声沙漠垂。

直译LiteralYoung, he left his home town; he made his name at the desert’s edge.

意译MeaningHe left home as a boy and won his fame at the edge of the desert.

04
宿昔秉良弓，楛矢何参差。

直译LiteralDay and night he held a fine bow; his thorn-wood arrows, how uneven they stand!

意译MeaningDay and night a good bow in his hand, a quiver bristling with arrows.

05
控弦破左的，右发摧月支。

直译LiteralDrawing the string, he splits the left target; shooting right, he smashes the yuezhi target.

意译MeaningHe draws and splits the target on his left, turns and shatters the one on his right.

06
仰手接飞猱，俯身散马蹄。

直译LiteralRaising his hand, he meets a leaping monkey; bending down, he scatters the horse-hoof target.

意译MeaningHe shoots up to hit a leaping ape, leans down and blasts the low target apart.

07
狡捷过猴猿，勇剽若豹螭。

直译LiteralNimble and quick beyond monkeys and apes; brave and fierce like leopards and dragons.

意译MeaningQuicker than any monkey, fierce as a leopard or a dragon.

第三段Part 3
08
边城多警急，虏骑数迁移。

直译LiteralThe border towns have many alarms; the enemy horsemen often shift and move.

意译MeaningAlarms keep coming from the border towns; enemy riders strike here, then there.

09
羽檄从北来，厉马登高堤。

直译LiteralA feathered war dispatch comes from the north; he urges his horse up the high bank.

意译MeaningAn urgent call to arms arrives from the north; he spurs his horse up the high embankment.

10
长驱蹈匈奴，左顾凌鲜卑。

直译LiteralDriving far, he tramples the Xiongnu; turning left, he crushes the Xianbei.

意译MeaningHe charges deep and tramples the Xiongnu, wheels left and overruns the Xianbei.

第四段Part 4
11
弃身锋刃端，性命安可怀？

直译LiteralThrowing his body onto the points of blades, how can he cherish his life?

意译MeaningWhen you hurl yourself at the blades, how can you cling to your life?

12
父母且不顾，何言子与妻！

直译LiteralEven his parents he cannot care for — what to say of children and wife!

意译MeaningI can’t even look after my parents — never mind a wife and children.

13
名编壮士籍，不得中顾私。

直译LiteralHis name is entered in the roll of soldiers; he may not think of private matters in his heart.

意译MeaningMy name is on the soldiers’ roll; there is no room in my heart for myself.

14
捐躯赴国难，视死忽如归。

直译LiteralGiving up his body, he rushes to the nation’s danger; he sees death, lightly, as going home.

意译MeaningI give my life to meet my country’s danger, and face death as if I were going home.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗塑造了一个少年英雄。开头像电影镜头：一匹白马戴着金色的笼头，飞快地向西北奔去。“这是谁家的孩子？”原来是幽州、并州一带的游侠少年。接下来写他的本领：从小离家，在边疆出了名；箭法神准，左右开弓都能射中靶子，抬手能接住飞跳的猿猴，俯身能射碎箭靶，比猴子还灵活，像豹子一样勇猛。然后写形势紧急：边城告急，敌人的骑兵不断进犯，紧急军书从北方传来，他立刻策马登上高堤，冲进敌阵，打败匈奴和鲜卑。最后八句是他的内心独白：上了战场，生命就不再属于自己；父母都顾不上，更别说妻子儿女；名字已经写在壮士的名册上，就不能再想个人的事。最后两句“捐躯赴国难，视死忽如归”——为国家献出生命，把死看得像回家一样平常——成了千古名句。诗里大量用对偶（两句结构相同、意思相对），读起来节奏明快，充满力量。

In English

This poem creates a young hero. It opens like a film shot: a white horse with a golden bridle racing toward the northwest. “Whose son is that?” A young knight-errant from Youzhou and Bingzhou, the tough northern borderlands. Then we see what he can do. He left home as a boy and made his name on the desert frontier. He shoots with either hand and never misses; he can reach up and catch a leaping monkey, or lean down from the saddle and shatter a target. He is quicker than an ape and fierce as a leopard. Then the crisis: the border town is under attack, enemy horsemen keep raiding, an urgent war dispatch arrives from the north. He spurs his horse up onto the high bank and charges, trampling the Xiongnu and crushing the Xianbei. The last eight lines are his own thoughts. Once you are on the battlefield, your life is no longer yours; he can’t even think of his parents, let alone a wife and children. His name is on the roll of soldiers, so private worries must go. The final couplet — “to give my life for my country in danger, and face death as if going home” — is still quoted today. Many lines are paired in parallel (two lines with the same structure), which gives the poem its quick, driving beat.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Cao Zhi (192–232), Cao Cao’s gifted younger son, was the most admired poet of his age. As a young man he hoped to win glory for his country; after his brother Cao Pi became emperor he was kept far from power and closely watched. This poem is usually seen as an expression of his early ambitions.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

幽并 bīng（并州，古地名）；少小 shào；楛矢 hù（一种木头做的箭杆）；参差 cēn cī；左的 dì（箭靶）；勇剽 piāo；豹螭 chī；数迁移 shuò（屡次）；羽檄 xí；中顾私 zhōng。虏骑：古代名词“骑”（骑兵）旧读 jì，统编教材现统一读 qí，网站按 qí 注音。“月支”是箭靶名，读 yuè zhī。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

白马饰金羁，连翩西北驰。

bái mǎ shì jīn jī, lián piān xī běi chí

A white horse in a golden bridle flashes off toward the northwest.

借问谁家子，幽并游侠儿。

jiè wèn shuí jiā zǐ, yōu bīng yóu xiá ér

“Whose son is that?” — a young knight-errant from the northern borderlands.

少小去乡邑，扬声沙漠垂。

shào xiǎo qù xiāng yì, yáng shēng shā mò chuí

He left home as a boy and won his fame at the edge of the desert.

宿昔秉良弓，楛矢何参差。

sù xī bǐng liáng gōng, hù shǐ hé cēn cī

Day and night a good bow in his hand, a quiver bristling with arrows.

控弦破左的，右发摧月支。

kòng xián pò zuǒ dì, yòu fā cuī yuè zhī

He draws and splits the target on his left, turns and shatters the one on his right.

仰手接飞猱，俯身散马蹄。

yǎng shǒu jiē fēi náo, fǔ shēn sàn mǎ tí

He shoots up to hit a leaping ape, leans down and blasts the low target apart.

狡捷过猴猿，勇剽若豹螭。

jiǎo jié guò hóu yuán, yǒng piāo ruò bào chī

Quicker than any monkey, fierce as a leopard or a dragon.

边城多警急，虏骑数迁移。

biān chéng duō jǐng jí, lǔ qí shuò qiān yí

Alarms keep coming from the border towns; enemy riders strike here, then there.

羽檄从北来，厉马登高堤。

yǔ xí cóng běi lái, lì mǎ dēng gāo dī

An urgent call to arms arrives from the north; he spurs his horse up the high embankment.

长驱蹈匈奴，左顾凌鲜卑。

cháng qū dǎo xiōng nú, zuǒ gù líng xiān bēi

He charges deep and tramples the Xiongnu, wheels left and overruns the Xianbei.

弃身锋刃端，性命安可怀？

qì shēn fēng rèn duān, xìng mìng ān kě huái

When you hurl yourself at the blades, how can you cling to your life?

父母且不顾，何言子与妻！

fù mǔ qiě bù gù, hé yán zǐ yǔ qī

I can’t even look after my parents — never mind a wife and children.

名编壮士籍，不得中顾私。

míng biān zhuàng shì jí, bù dé zhōng gù sī

My name is on the soldiers’ roll; there is no room in my heart for myself.

捐躯赴国难，视死忽如归。

juān qū fù guó nàn, shì sǐ hū rú guī

I give my life to meet my country’s danger, and face death as if I were going home.
