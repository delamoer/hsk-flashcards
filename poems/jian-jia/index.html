导读About this poem

On a frosty autumn morning, someone stands among the reeds looking for “that person” across the water — upstream the road is hard and long, downstream she seems to stand just out of reach. One of the most beautiful poems of longing in Chinese.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗写秋天清晨的河边：芦苇一片青苍，露水结成了霜。诗人想念的“伊人”就在河的另一边，可是逆着水流去找，路又难走又远；顺着水流去找，她又好像站在水的中央，看得见却走不到。“伊人”到底是谁，诗里没有说——可以是心爱的姑娘，也可以是一种追求不到的理想，所以两千多年来大家读出了很多不同的意思。全诗三章，每章只换几个字（苍苍—萋萋—采采，为霜—未晞—未已），这叫“重章叠句”：时间从霜到露水慢慢变干，追寻却一直没有结果。先写芦苇、白露，再引出心中的人，也是《诗经》常用的“起兴”。“所谓伊人，在水一方”今天还常被引用，形容想念的人可望而不可即。

In English

It is early on an autumn morning by a river. The reeds stand thick and grey-green, and the dew has turned to frost. The person the poet longs for — simply called 伊人, “that person” — is somewhere on the other side of the water. Going upstream, the path is hard and long; going downstream, she seems to be standing right in the middle of the river, visible but never reachable. The poem never says who “that person” is. She may be a woman he loves, or an ideal he can never quite reach, and readers have argued about it for two thousand years — which is part of its charm. Each of the three stanzas repeats the same pattern with only a few words changed (苍苍, 萋萋, 采采 for the reeds; frost, dew not yet dry, dew not yet gone). This “repeated stanza” style made the song easy to sing, and here it also lets time pass quietly: the sun climbs, the frost melts, and the search still goes nowhere. Opening with the reeds and dew before turning to the person is the Book of Songs’ classic move called xìng, “evoking”. The line 所谓伊人，在水一方 is still quoted today for someone you can see but never reach.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

The poem comes from the “Airs of Qin” (秦风) in the Book of Songs, songs from the state of Qin in the northwest, gathered roughly between the 11th and 6th centuries BC. Its author is unknown. Traditional commentators read it as political advice to a ruler; most modern readers take it as a poem of longing.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

生僻字：蒹葭 jiān jiā、晞 xī、湄 méi、跻 jī、坻 chí（不读宝坻的 dǐ）、涘 sì、沚 zhǐ、溯洄 sù huí。多音字：白露为霜的“为”读 wéi；采采 cǎi cǎi；宛 wǎn。

蒹葭苍苍，白露为霜。

jiān jiā cāng cāng, bái lù wéi shuāng

Reeds stand thick and grey-green; the white dew has turned to frost.

所谓伊人，在水一方。

suǒ wèi yī rén, zài shuǐ yī fāng

The one I long for is somewhere across the water.

溯洄从之，道阻且长。

sù huí cóng zhī, dào zǔ qiě cháng

I go upstream to find her — the path is rough and long.

溯游从之，宛在水中央。

sù yóu cóng zhī, wǎn zài shuǐ zhōng yāng

I go downstream — and there she seems to stand, in the middle of the river.

蒹葭萋萋，白露未晞。

jiān jiā qī qī, bái lù wèi xī

所谓伊人，在水之湄。

suǒ wèi yī rén, zài shuǐ zhī méi

溯洄从之，道阻且跻。

sù huí cóng zhī, dào zǔ qiě jī
