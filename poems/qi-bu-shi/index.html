导读About this poem

Legend says an emperor ordered his younger brother to compose a poem in seven steps — or die. The brother answered with beans cooking over a fire made of their own stalks: why should one root destroy itself?

中文解析

传说曹丕当了皇帝以后，想除掉很有才华的弟弟曹植，命令他在走七步的时间里作一首诗，作不出来就杀了他。曹植走了不到七步就念出这首诗。诗里只写了一件很平常的事：煮豆子。烧的是豆子的茎（豆萁），锅里的豆子好像在哭。豆和萁本来长在同一棵植物上，为什么萁要这样急着煎熬豆呢？这里用豆比喻自己，用萁比喻哥哥，用“同根生”比喻亲兄弟——这种手法叫“比喻”。诗人没有直接指责哥哥，只问了一句“相煎何太急”，委屈和悲愤却说得很清楚。据说曹丕听了很惭愧，放过了弟弟。这个故事真假难定，但“本是同根生，相煎何太急”已经成了中国人常说的话，用来劝说自家人、同胞不要互相伤害。

In English

The story goes that after Cao Pi became emperor, he wanted to get rid of his brilliant younger brother Cao Zhi. He ordered him to make up a poem while taking seven steps — fail, and he would be put to death. Before the seventh step, Cao Zhi spoke these four lines. The poem describes something ordinary: cooking beans. The fire is made from the bean plant’s own dry stalks, and in the pot the beans seem to be weeping. Beans and stalks grew from the same root — so why are the stalks in such a hurry to cook them? The beans are Cao Zhi, the stalks are his brother, and “born from the same root” means brothers of the same parents. This is a metaphor (bǐyù), and it lets the poet protest without ever accusing the emperor directly. All he does is ask one question, yet the hurt and anger are unmistakable. It is said Cao Pi felt ashamed and let his brother go. Whether the story is true no one knows, but the last two lines are still quoted today, whenever people want to say that family — or one people — should not hurt each other.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Cao Zhi (192–232), a son of Cao Cao, was the most gifted poet of his age; his elder brother Cao Pi became the first emperor of Wei in 220. The poem is traditionally attributed to Cao Zhi, but the seven-step story first appears in an anecdote collection written some two centuries later, and many scholars doubt he wrote it.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

生僻字：豆萁 qí、釜 fǔ。“相煎”的“相”读 xiāng，这里表示一方对另一方（萁对豆），不是“互相”。

版本说明 About the text

采用最通行的四句本（见于小说《三国演义》，也是语文课本和儿童读物通用的版本）。南朝《世说新语》记载的是六句本：“煮豆持作羹，漉菽以为汁。萁在釜下燃，豆在釜中泣。本自同根生，相煎何太急？”（“本自”一作“本是”）。网站只采用四句本。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

煮豆燃豆萁，

zhǔ dòu rán dòu qí

They boil the beans on a fire of bean stalks;

豆在釜中泣。

dòu zài fǔ zhōng qì

in the pot, the beans are weeping.

本是同根生，

běn shì tóng gēn shēng

We grew up from the same root —

相煎何太急？

xiāng jiān hé tài jí

why are you in such a rush to burn me?
