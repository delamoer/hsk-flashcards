导读About this poem

A young widow, falsely accused of murder, is led to her execution — and on the way she curses Heaven and Earth for failing to tell good from evil. The most famous cry of protest in Chinese drama.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

《窦娥冤》是元代最有名的悲剧。窦娥是一个善良的年轻寡妇，被坏人张驴儿诬陷杀人，贪官严刑逼供，判她死刑。这段唱词是第三折里窦娥被押去刑场的路上唱的。古人相信天地日月和鬼神主持公道，好人有好报，坏人有恶报。可窦娥看到的却相反：做好事的人又穷又短命，做坏事的人又富又长寿；盗跖是古代有名的大盗，颜渊是孔子最好的学生，天地却把他们弄颠倒了。她骂天地“怕硬欺软”“顺水推船”，最后喊出：“地也，你不分好歹何为地！天也，你错勘贤愚枉做天！”在古代，天地是最神圣的，敢这样指责天地，说明窦娥的冤屈已经到了极点。这段唱词用的是元代口语，排比句（结构相同的句子连在一起）一句比一句激烈，读起来很有力量。“六月飞雪”“感天动地”这些说法，也都来自这部戏。

In English

The Injustice to Dou E is the most famous tragedy of the Yuan dynasty. Dou E is a kind young widow. A villain falsely accuses her of murder, and a corrupt judge tortures her into a confession and sentences her to death. She sings this aria in Act Three, on the way to the execution ground. People then believed that Heaven and Earth, the sun, the moon and the spirits kept the world fair: good people are rewarded, bad people punished. Dou E sees the opposite. Those who do good are poor and die young; those who do evil grow rich and live long. Heaven and Earth have mixed up Robber Zhi, a legendary bandit, with Yan Yuan, Confucius’s most virtuous student. She accuses them of bullying the weak and fearing the strong, of simply pushing the boat the way the current flows. Then she cries out: “Earth, if you can’t tell good from bad, what makes you Earth? Heaven, if you mistake the wise for the foolish, you are Heaven in name only!” Heaven and Earth were the most sacred things in old China, so blaming them shows how complete her despair is. The aria is in the spoken Chinese of the time, and its parallel lines — sentences built the same way, one after another — grow fiercer as they go. Sayings like “snow in June” for a terrible injustice come from this play.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Guan Hanqing (c. 1220s–c. 1300) was the leading playwright of Yuan zaju drama, credited with more than sixty plays. Under Mongol rule many educated Chinese were shut out of official careers, and drama like his often speaks for ordinary people against corrupt power.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

朝暮 zhāo mù（早晚，不读 cháo）；掌着 zhe；只合 zhǐ hé（只应该）；清浊 zhuó；盗跖 zhí；为善的、何为地的“为”读 wéi（做；作为）；错勘 kān；只落得 luò de；哎 āi。“做得个”的“个”按本调标 gè，口语可读轻声。

版本说明 About the text

采用统编语文教材必修下册文本。节选自杂剧《感天动地窦娥冤》第三折中的一支曲子【正宫·滚绣球】，全曲十一句，此处完整收录这一支（全剧共四折一楔子）。异文：明代刊本作“却元来也这般顺水推船”，教材作“却原来”；“何为地”句末明刊本用句号，教材用感叹号。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

有日月朝暮悬，

yǒu rì yuè zhāo mù xuán

The sun and the moon hang in the sky morning and night,

有鬼神掌着生死权。

yǒu guǐ shén zhǎng zhe shēng sǐ quán

and the spirits hold the power of life and death.

天地也只合把清浊分辨，

tiān dì yě zhǐ hé bǎ qīng zhuó fēn biàn

Heaven and Earth, your one task is to tell the clean from the foul —

可怎生糊突了盗跖、颜渊？

kě zěn shēng hú tū le dào zhí yán yuān

so how could you mix up a robber with a saint?

为善的受贫穷更命短，

wéi shàn de shòu pín qióng gèng mìng duǎn

Those who do good stay poor and die young;

造恶的享富贵又寿延。

zào è de xiǎng fù guì yòu shòu yán

those who do evil grow rich and live long.

天地也，做得个怕硬欺软，

tiān dì yě, zuò de gè pà yìng qī ruǎn

Heaven and Earth, you fear the strong and bully the weak —

却原来也这般顺水推船！

què yuán lái yě zhè bān shùn shuǐ tuī chuán

so you too just drift along with whoever is winning!

地也，你不分好歹何为地！

dì yě, nǐ bù fēn hǎo dǎi hé wéi dì

Earth! If you can’t tell good from bad, what right have you to be Earth?

天也，你错勘贤愚枉做天！

tiān yě, nǐ cuò kān xián yú wǎng zuò tiān

Heaven! You mistake the good for the wicked — you are Heaven in name only!

哎，只落得两泪涟涟。

āi, zhǐ luò de liǎng lèi lián lián

Ah — nothing is left to me but my endless tears.
