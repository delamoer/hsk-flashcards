导读About this poem

Most poets find early winter sad; Su Shi tells an older friend it is the best time of the year. A short, warm poem of encouragement, set among oranges and tangerines.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗是苏轼送给朋友刘景文的。前两句写初冬的景色：荷花谢了，连像雨伞一样托着雨水的荷叶也没有了；菊花也枯萎了，可是枝干还挺立着，不怕寒霜。一个“尽”，一个“残”，写出了花草凋零；但“犹有傲霜枝”一转，突出了一种不怕冷、不低头的精神。后两句是对朋友说的话：一年里最好的风景，你一定要记住，就是橙子黄了、橘子还绿着的时候。一般诗人写秋冬，多写悲伤，苏轼却说这是一年中最好的时候，因为这是收获的季节。刘景文当时已经五十多岁，生活不太如意，苏轼借景物鼓励他：人到晚年，也正是有成就、有收获的好时光。

In English

Su Shi wrote this for his friend Liu Jingwen. The first two lines paint early winter. The lotus flowers are gone — even the big round leaves that once held up the rain like umbrellas have withered away. The chrysanthemums have faded too, yet their stems still stand straight, “proud against the frost”. The words 尽 (“all gone”) and 残 (“fading”) show the plants dying back, but the phrase 傲霜枝 turns the picture around: what matters is what refuses to bend in the cold. The last two lines speak directly to the friend: of all the fine scenes in a year, remember this one — when oranges are turning yellow and tangerines are still green. Many Chinese poems treat late autumn and winter as sad; Su Shi calls it the best time, because it is harvest time, when fruit hangs ripe on the trees. Liu Jingwen was in his late fifties and had had a hard career. Through the scenery, Su Shi is telling him that later life, too, can be a season of fruit rather than of loss.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Su Shi (1037–1101), also called Su Dongpo, was a Song-dynasty official and one of China’s most loved poets. He wrote this in Hangzhou around 1090 for Liu Jisun (style name Jingwen), a military officer whom he admired and recommended for promotion.

版本说明 About the text

采用统编小学语文教材版本。异文：“最是橙黄橘绿时”，《东坡诗集注》等作“正是橙黄橘绿时”；“一年好景”，《皇朝文鉴》作“一年好处”。题目一作《冬景》。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

荷尽已无擎雨盖，

hé jìn yǐ wú qíng yǔ gài

The lotus is gone, and the leaves that held off the rain;

菊残犹有傲霜枝。

jú cán yóu yǒu ào shuāng zhī

the chrysanthemums fade, but their stems still stand up to the frost.

一年好景君须记，

yī nián hǎo jǐng jūn xū jì

Of all the year’s fine sights, my friend, remember this one:

最是橙黄橘绿时。

zuì shì chéng huáng jú lǜ shí

the days when oranges turn gold and tangerines are still green.
