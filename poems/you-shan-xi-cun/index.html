导读About this poem

A walk to a country village, warm hospitality and festival drums — and the famous couplet about thinking you are lost, then finding a village hidden among willows and flowers, which Chinese people still quote whenever hope reappears.

中文解析

陆游被罢官后回到家乡山阴（今浙江绍兴）闲住，这首诗写他到山西边一个村子游玩。开头两句写农家好客：别笑农家腊月酿的酒浑浊，丰收的年景，留客人吃饭，鸡肉猪肉都很丰盛。三、四句是千古名句：山一重又一重，水一道又一道，正怀疑前面没路了，忽然柳树成荫、鲜花明艳，又出现一个村庄。这两句本来写走山路的真实感受，后来人们用它比喻：困难到了尽头，常常会出现新的希望。五、六句写快到春社节了，村里吹箫打鼓，人们穿戴简朴，保留着古老的风俗。最后说：如果以后允许我趁着月色闲游，我会随时拄着拐杖来敲门。全诗充满对农村生活的喜爱，“柳暗花明”今天已经是常用成语。

In English

After being dismissed from office, Lu You went home to Shanyin (today’s Shaoxing in Zhejiang), and this poem describes a walk to a village west of the hills. It opens with country hospitality: don’t laugh at the farmers’ cloudy home-brewed wine — in a good harvest year they keep a guest with plenty of chicken and pork. The second couplet is one of the most quoted in Chinese: hill after hill, stream after stream, and just when you think there is no way forward, there among shady willows and bright flowers is another village. It began as an honest description of walking in hilly country, but people now use it for any moment when, at the end of your troubles, a new way suddenly opens. The third couplet shows the village getting ready for the spring festival to the god of the soil, with flutes and drums; people dress simply, and old customs live on. The last couplet is an invitation to himself: if I may wander here by moonlight from now on, I’ll take my walking stick and knock on your door at any hour. The whole poem glows with affection for country life, and 柳暗花明 is now an everyday idiom.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Lu You (1125–1210) was the most prolific poet of the Southern Song. He wrote this in 1167, at home in Shanyin after losing his post for supporting war against the Jin.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

莫笑农家腊酒浑，

mò xiào nóng jiā là jiǔ hún

Don’t laugh at the farmers’ cloudy winter wine —

丰年留客足鸡豚。

fēng nián liú kè zú jī tún

in a good year there’s chicken and pork enough for any guest.

山重水复疑无路，

shān chóng shuǐ fù yí wú lù

Hill after hill, stream after stream — I was sure the path had ended,

柳暗花明又一村。

liǔ àn huā míng yòu yī cūn

then, through shady willows and bright flowers, another village.

箫鼓追随春社近，

xiāo gǔ zhuī suí chūn shè jìn

Flutes and drums keep sounding: the spring festival is close,

衣冠简朴古风存。

yī guān jiǎn pǔ gǔ fēng cún

and in their plain clothes the old ways live on.

从今若许闲乘月，

cóng jīn ruò xǔ xián chéng yuè

From now on, if I may stroll here by moonlight,

拄杖无时夜叩门。

zhǔ zhàng wú shí yè kòu mén

I’ll come with my walking stick and knock on your door at any hour.
