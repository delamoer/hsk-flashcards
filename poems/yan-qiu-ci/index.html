导读About this poem

A sixteen-year-old sees a wild goose kill itself after its mate is shot, buries the pair, and writes this. Its first line — “What is love, that it makes two lives promise each other death?” — is one of the most quoted in Chinese.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

作者十六岁赴考途中，遇到一个捕雁人，说今天杀了一只雁，另一只逃脱的雁在空中悲鸣不去，最后撞地而死。元好问买下两只雁，葬在汾水边，堆石为记，叫“雁丘”，写了这首词。开头一问：世间的情到底是什么，竟让人生死相许？突兀有力，成了千古名句。接着写这对雁天南地北一起飞，经历多少寒暑，有欢聚也有离别，就像人间痴情的儿女。“君应有语”是替孤雁说话：前面万里层云、千山暮雪，只剩一个影子，还能飞向哪里？下阕写葬雁之地：当年汉武帝在汾水上箫鼓喧天，如今只剩荒烟野树；招魂已来不及，只有山鬼在风雨里哭泣。最后说连老天都嫉妒，这对雁不会像普通莺燕一样化为黄土，会留待千秋万代的诗人来凭吊。把动物写得比人还深情，最打动人。

In English

On his way to the civil examination at sixteen, Yuan Haowen met a man who caught wild geese. The man said he had caught and killed one goose that morning; its mate escaped, circled crying overhead, and then dashed itself to the ground and died. Yuan bought both birds, buried them by the Fen River, piled stones on the grave and called it the “Wild Goose Mound”. The poem opens with a sudden question: what is love, that it makes two beings promise each other life and death? That line has been quoted ever since. Then the pair: they flew together north and south through many winters and summers, knowing the joy of being together and the pain of parting — as devoted as any human lovers. Next the poet speaks for the surviving goose: ahead lie ten thousand miles of cloud and a thousand snowy mountains at dusk — with only its own shadow left, where could it fly? The second half turns to the burial place. Emperor Wu of Han once sailed here with flutes and drums; now there is only wild mist and woods. It is too late to call back the souls; only mountain spirits weep in the wind and rain. Finally: even Heaven is jealous — these geese will not simply turn to dust like ordinary birds; poets will come for a thousand years to sing and drink at their grave. Making animals more faithful than people is what moves readers most.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Yuan Haowen (1190–1257) was the leading poet of the Jin dynasty, which was conquered by the Mongols in 1234; he lived on under early Yuan rule, which is why he is grouped here with the Yuan. His preface dates the event to 1205, when he was sixteen; he revised the poem later in life.

版本说明 About the text

采用通行版本，只收正文。开头“问世间”有本作“恨人间”“问人间”；“情为何物”《词综》等作“情是何物”；“就中”有本作“是中”；“未信与”有本作“未信欤”（与、欤相通）。网站用最通行的版本。原有小序，大意：泰和五年（1205），作者赴并州应试，路遇捕雁者，说今天捕到一只雁杀了，另一只脱网后悲鸣不去，竟投地而死；作者买下两雁，葬在汾水边，垒石为识，号曰“雁丘”，同行者多作诗，作者写了这首《雁丘词》，旧作不合音律，后来改定。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

问世间、情为何物，直教生死相许？

wèn shì jiān, qíng wéi hé wù, zhí jiào shēng sǐ xiāng xǔ

Tell me, world — what is love, that two lives pledge to live and die as one?

天南地北双飞客，老翅几回寒暑。

tiān nán dì běi shuāng fēi kè, lǎo chì jǐ huí hán shǔ

Two travellers flying south and north together, their wings grown old through so many winters and summers.

欢乐趣，离别苦，就中更有痴儿女。

huān lè qù, lí bié kǔ, jiù zhōng gèng yǒu chī ér nǚ

The sweetness of being together, the pain of parting — they loved like the most devoted of lovers.

君应有语：

jūn yīng yǒu yǔ

You, lone goose, must be saying:

渺万里层云，千山暮雪，只影向谁去？

miǎo wàn lǐ céng yún, qiān shān mù xuě, zhī yǐng xiàng shuí qù

“Endless miles of piled cloud, a thousand hills of evening snow — alone with my shadow, who is there to fly to?”

横汾路，寂寞当年箫鼓，荒烟依旧平楚。

héng fén lù, jì mò dāng nián xiāo gǔ, huāng yān yī jiù píng chǔ

By the Fen, where an emperor once sailed, the flutes and drums are long silent; wild mist still hangs over the woods.

招魂楚些何嗟及，山鬼暗啼风雨。

zhāo hún chǔ suò hé jiē jí, shān guǐ àn tí fēng yǔ

Too late for ancient chants to call their souls back; only mountain spirits weep in the wind and rain.

天也妒，未信与，莺儿燕子俱黄土。

tiān yě dù, wèi xìn yú, yīng ér yàn zi jù huáng tǔ

Even Heaven envies them; they will not crumble to dust like common orioles and swallows.

千秋万古，为留待骚人，

qiān qiū wàn gǔ, wèi liú dài sāo rén

Through the ages this grave will wait for poets

狂歌痛饮，来访雁丘处。

kuáng gē tòng yǐn, lái fǎng yàn qiū chù

to come and sing and drink their fill at the Wild Goose Mound.
