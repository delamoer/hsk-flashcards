导读About this poem

A Mid-Autumn night, the festival of family reunion — and Su Shi has cheap wine, no guests, and clouds over the moon. He raises his cup alone and looks north.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首词写一个人过中秋。上阕感叹人生：世上的事就像一场大梦，人这一辈子能经历几次秋凉呢？夜里风吹树叶，在回廊上沙沙作响；看看自己的眉头和鬓角，愁容和白发都已经出现了。下阕写中秋的冷清：酒很便宜，却常常发愁没有客人来一起喝；月亮很明亮，却常常被云遮住。中秋本来是团圆的节日，谁能和我一起欣赏这孤零零的月光呢？我只能端着酒杯，凄凉地朝北方望去。“酒贱”“客少”“云妨”都是不如意的事，写出诗人在贬谪中的孤独。“北望”一般认为是思念远方的弟弟苏辙，也有人认为是心系朝廷。和他有名的《水调歌头》比，这首词更低沉，但同样真诚动人。

In English

A Mid-Autumn Festival spent alone. The first half reflects on life: the affairs of the world are one big dream; how many cool autumns does a person get? At night the wind rattles leaves along the corridor, and when he looks at his brow and temples, worry lines and white hairs are already there. The second half turns to the lonely festival. Wine is cheap, but he keeps worrying that no guests will come to share it; the moon is bright, but clouds keep getting in its way. Mid-Autumn is meant for family reunion — so who will share this solitary moonlight with him? All he can do is hold his cup and gaze sadly northward. Cheap wine with no company, a bright moon blocked by clouds: little disappointments add up to the loneliness of exile. Looking north is usually read as missing his younger brother Su Zhe, far away; some readers think he is also thinking of the court and the emperor. Compared with his famous Mid-Autumn poem “Prelude to Water Melody”, this one is darker and quieter, but just as sincere.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Su Shi (1037–1101), also called Su Dongpo, was very close to his younger brother Su Zhe. This poem is traditionally dated to the Mid-Autumn Festival of 1080, his first in exile at Huangzhou, though some scholars place it in his later exile on Hainan.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

版本说明 About the text

采用通行版本。“人生几度秋凉”，《东坡乐府》等古本作“人生几度新凉”（维基文库同），今通行本多作“秋凉”。题一作“黄州中秋”，写作年代有黄州、海南等不同说法。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

世事一场大梦，

shì shì yī chǎng dà mèng

All the world’s business is one long dream;

人生几度秋凉。

rén shēng jǐ dù qiū liáng

how many cool autumns does one life hold?

夜来风叶已鸣廊，

yè lái fēng yè yǐ míng láng

Tonight the wind sets leaves rattling down the corridor —

看取眉头鬓上。

kàn qǔ méi tóu bìn shàng

look at the lines on my brow, the grey at my temples.

酒贱常愁客少，

jiǔ jiàn cháng chóu kè shǎo

Wine is cheap, but there are few to drink it with;

月明多被云妨。

yuè míng duō bèi yún fáng

the moon is bright, but clouds keep drifting across it.

中秋谁与共孤光，

zhōng qiū shuí yǔ gòng gū guāng

Mid-Autumn, and who is here to share this lonely light?

把盏凄然北望。

bǎ zhǎn qī rán běi wàng

I lift my cup and look sadly to the north.
