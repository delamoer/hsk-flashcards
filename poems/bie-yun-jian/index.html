导读About this poem

Written by a sixteen-year-old resistance fighter on his way to execution, saying goodbye to his hometown. It is short, plain and unbearably calm — a boy who knows he will die and promises to come back as a spirit to keep fighting.

中文解析

明朝灭亡后，清兵南下，少年夏完淳跟着父亲和老师起兵抗清，前后三年。后来他被捕，押往南京受审，临走前写下这首诗告别家乡云间（今上海松江）。开头说自己在外奔走三年，今天成了囚犯——“南冠”本是楚国人戴的帽子，后来成了囚犯的代称。第二联最有力量：山河破碎，泪流不尽，谁还能说天地宽广？天地再大，已经没有他的容身之处。第三联平静地说出死亡——“泉路”就是黄泉路，他知道自己快死了，真正难的是离开家乡。最后一联不写悲伤，而写希望：等我坚毅的魂魄回来，要在空中看见招魂的灵旗、抗清的战旗。全诗没有一句喊口号，却让人读出一个十六岁少年的勇气。

In English

After the Ming dynasty fell in 1644, the Manchu Qing armies swept south. The teenage Xia Wanchun joined his father and his teacher in armed resistance for three years. Captured in 1647, he was sent to Nanjing for trial, and before leaving he wrote this farewell to his hometown, Yunjian (today’s Songjiang, in Shanghai). The opening is factual: three years on the road, and now he wears the “southern cap” — an old phrase for a prisoner, from a captive of the state of Chu who kept wearing his native hat. The second couplet is the heart of the poem: rivers and mountains soaked in endless tears — who says heaven and earth are wide? For him the world has shrunk to nothing. The third couplet speaks of death with almost no emotion: he knows the road to the underworld is near; what is hard is leaving home. The last couplet turns grief into resolve: on the day his unyielding spirit returns, it will look for the battle flags in the sky. There are no slogans here, just a sixteen-year-old facing death with his head up — which is why Chinese readers still find it so moving.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Xia Wanchun (1631–1647) was a child prodigy from Songjiang who fought against the Qing conquest. He was captured in 1647 and executed in Nanjing at the age of sixteen; this poem was written as he was taken away from home.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

冠：南冠 nán guān（名词，帽子，不读 guàn）。羁 jī。看：本诗押平声韵（冠、宽、难、看），按格律读 kān；现代口语多读 kàn。网站暂注 kān。机器朗读把“冠”“看”替换为同音字，音频需人工听验。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行文本（《夏完淳集》）。题目一作《别云间》，云间是松江的古称。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

三年羁旅客，

sān nián jī lǚ kè

Three years I roamed, a man without a home;

今日又南冠。

jīn rì yòu nán guān

today I wear a prisoner’s cap.

无限山河泪，

wú xiàn shān hé lèi

Endless tears for our broken land —

谁言天地宽！

shuí yán tiān dì kuān

who still says the world is wide?

已知泉路近，

yǐ zhī quán lù jìn

I know the road to the dead is close;

欲别故乡难。

yù bié gù xiāng nán

what is hard is leaving home.

毅魄归来日，

yì pò guī lái rì

When my stubborn soul comes back one day,

灵旗空际看。

líng qí kōng jì kān

I’ll watch for our banners in the sky.
