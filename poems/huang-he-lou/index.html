Huáng Hè Lóu Sòng Mèng Hàorán Zhī Guǎnglíng
Seeing Meng Haoran Off to Guangling at Yellow Crane Tower

导读About this poem

Li Bai waves goodbye to an older poet friend sailing down the Yangtze to Yangzhou in spring — and keeps watching until the sail disappears into the sky. A farewell poem that never says “goodbye” or “sad”.

中文解析

这首诗写李白在黄鹤楼送别好朋友孟浩然。孟浩然要坐船顺长江东下，去繁华的扬州（广陵）。前两句交代人物、地点和时间：“烟花三月”写出春天花开如烟、一片朦胧的美景，送别的季节很明亮。后两句只写一个画面：朋友的船越走越远，最后只剩一片小小的帆影，消失在蓝天尽头，眼前只有长江向天边流去。诗人一直站在楼上望着，没有一个字说“舍不得”，可是“望到看不见”这个动作本身就是深深的友情。这种把感情放进景物里的写法，叫“借景抒情”。“烟花三月下扬州”今天还常被用来形容春天去扬州旅游。

In English

Li Bai is standing at Yellow Crane Tower in Wuhan, seeing off his friend Meng Haoran, a poet about twelve years older whom he admired. Meng is sailing east down the Yangtze to Guangling, the old name for Yangzhou, one of the richest and liveliest cities of the Tang dynasty. The first two lines give us who, where and when. “Smoke and flowers in the third month” paints late spring: blossoms everywhere, soft and hazy like mist. It is a bright, beautiful season for a farewell. The last two lines are a single long shot, like a film camera that never cuts away. The boat grows smaller, until only one lonely sail is left; then even that vanishes into the blue sky, and all that remains is the river flowing to the edge of heaven. Li Bai never says he is sad or that he will miss his friend. But think about what the picture tells you: he stood there watching until there was nothing left to see. Putting feelings into a scene instead of stating them is called “expressing emotion through scenery” (借景抒情), one of the most important habits of Chinese poetry. Today 烟花三月下扬州 is still quoted — and used in tourism slogans — whenever people talk about visiting Yangzhou in spring.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Li Bai (701–762) was in his late twenties, travelling along the Yangtze, when he met Meng Haoran (689–740), already a well-known poet. The farewell is traditionally dated to around 730. Yellow Crane Tower stands on the riverbank in today’s Wuhan.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

“唯见”的“唯”有的版本作“惟”，读音相同（wéi）。题目中“之”是“去、往”的意思。其余无特殊读音。

版本说明 About the text

采用统编小学语文教材版本（“孤帆远影碧空尽”“唯见长江天际流”）。异文：一作“孤帆远影碧山尽”“惟见长江天际流”，网站不用。广陵即今江苏扬州。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

故人西辞黄鹤楼，

gù rén xī cí huáng hè lóu

My old friend says goodbye at Yellow Crane Tower, here in the west,

烟花三月下扬州。

yān huā sān yuè xià yáng zhōu

and sails down to Yangzhou in the third month, through a haze of blossoms.

孤帆远影碧空尽，

gū fān yuǎn yǐng bì kōng jìn

One small sail, a far-off shadow, fades into the blue —

唯见长江天际流。

wéi jiàn cháng jiāng tiān jì liú

now there is only the Yangtze, running on to the edge of the sky.
