导读About this poem

Sleepless until dawn, the poet thinks of Chang’e, who stole the elixir of immortality and now lives alone in the moon forever. Does she regret it? A short poem about loneliness that never ends.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

嫦娥是中国神话里的月亮女神：她偷吃了长生不老的灵药，飞到月宫，从此一个人住在那里。这首诗前两句写诗人自己：云母屏风上烛影越来越深，银河渐渐西斜，晨星也慢慢沉落——他整夜没有睡。后两句由眼前的夜空想到嫦娥：她应该后悔偷了灵药吧，现在只能夜夜对着碧海一样的青天，孤孤单单。“碧海青天夜夜心”七个字写尽了没有尽头的寂寞。诗人为什么想到嫦娥？说法很多：有人说是写自己清高孤独、不被理解，有人说是写修道的女子，也有人说是写思念的人。其实嫦娥的孤独和诗人的孤独是一样的，他是借嫦娥写自己，这叫“借物抒情”。

In English

In Chinese myth, Chang’e is the goddess of the moon. She swallowed an elixir of immortality, floated up to the moon palace, and has lived there alone ever since. The first two lines are about the poet himself. The candle shadows behind a screen of mica grow darker, the Milky Way slowly slides down the sky, the morning stars sink — he has been awake all night. Looking at that sky, he thinks of Chang’e. She must regret stealing the elixir, he says, for now night after night she faces the endless blue of sea and sky, all on her own. The phrase 碧海青天夜夜心 — “blue sea, blue sky, a heart alone every night” — captures a loneliness that never ends. Why does the poet think of her? Readers suggest different answers: he may mean his own proud, misunderstood isolation, or a Daoist nun who gave up the world, or someone he loved and lost. In any case Chang’e’s loneliness mirrors his own; he speaks of her in order to speak of himself.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Li Shangyin (c. 813–858) was one of the great poets of the late Tang, famous for dense, beautiful and often mysterious verse. Caught between two rival court factions, he spent most of his life in minor posts. The poem’s occasion is unknown; readers have linked it to his own isolation or to his youthful acquaintance with Daoist nuns.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

云母屏风烛影深，

yún mǔ píng fēng zhú yǐng shēn

Behind the mica screen the candle shadows deepen;

长河渐落晓星沉。

cháng hé jiàn luò xiǎo xīng chén

the Milky Way slips down, the morning stars go under.

嫦娥应悔偷灵药，

cháng é yīng huǐ tōu líng yào

Chang’e must be sorry she stole the elixir:

碧海青天夜夜心。

bì hǎi qīng tiān yè yè xīn

now, night after night, her heart faces only blue sky like an empty sea.
