望洞庭

Wàng Dòngtíng
Gazing at Dongting Lake
刘禹锡Liú Yǔxī · Liu Yuxi唐 Tang

导读About this poem

On a still autumn night, the moon shines on Dongting Lake, and the water is as smooth as an unpolished mirror. Seen from afar, the island in the middle looks like a little green snail on a silver plate.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

洞庭湖是中国最有名的大湖之一，湖中有一座君山。这首诗写秋天月夜远望洞庭湖的景色，用了两个漂亮的比喻。第一句写湖光和秋月交相辉映，非常和谐。第二句写湖面没有风，平静得像一面还没打磨过的铜镜——古代的镜子是铜做的，没磨过的镜面朦朦胧胧，正像月光下的湖面。后两句从远处看整个洞庭湖：湖水翠绿，君山青青，就像白银盘子里放着一只青色的田螺。白银盘是月光下的湖面，青螺是湖中的君山，一大一小，一白一青，颜色和形状都很生动。诗人把八百里洞庭写成桌上的一个小摆设，想象奇特，也显出他开阔的胸怀。

In English

Dongting is one of China’s most famous lakes, with a small mountain island, Junshan, in the middle. This poem shows the lake seen from afar on an autumn night, and it rests on two lovely comparisons. The first line: the shimmer of the lake and the autumn moon match each other in perfect harmony. The second: with no wind, the surface is as calm as a bronze mirror that hasn’t been polished. Ancient Chinese mirrors were made of bronze, and an unpolished one gives a soft, hazy reflection — just like the lake under moonlight. The last two lines take in the whole lake from a distance: green water, green hills, like a little green snail lying on a silver plate. The silver plate is the moonlit lake; the snail is Junshan island. One large, one small, one white, one green — the colours and shapes are vivid. The poet turns a vast lake, “eight hundred li” across, into a small ornament on a table. The image is surprising and playful, and it suggests a man with a wide, easy view of the world.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Liu Yuxi (772–842) was a Tang poet and official. This poem is traditionally dated to 824, when he passed Dongting Lake on his way to a new post as prefect of Hezhou.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

“两相和”的“和”：教材注 hé（和谐），旧注有读 hè（应和）；网站读 hé。“相”读 xiāng；“青螺”的“螺”读 luó。

版本说明 About the text

采用统编教材（三年级上册）版本：“遥望洞庭山水翠”。不少古本和选本作“遥望洞庭山水色”。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

湖光秋月两相和，

hú guāng qiū yuè liǎng xiāng hé

Lake-light and autumn moon shine in harmony;

潭面无风镜未磨。

tán miàn wú fēng jìng wèi mó

no wind on the water — a mirror still unpolished.

遥望洞庭山水翠，

yáo wàng dòng tíng shān shuǐ cuì

From afar, Dongting’s hills and water glow green:

白银盘里一青螺。

bái yín pán lǐ yī qīng luó

a little green snail on a plate of silver.
