采莲曲（菱叶萦波荷飐风）

导读About this poem

A girl picking lotus meets the young man she likes, tries to speak, smiles and looks down — and her jade hairpin slips into the water. Four lines, one tiny, perfectly caught moment of first love.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

“采莲曲”是乐府民歌的老题目，常写江南姑娘划船采莲。白居易这首只有四句，却像一段小电影。前两句是背景：菱角的叶子在水波上缠绕，荷叶在风中摇动，小船穿过荷花最深的地方。后两句是故事：姑娘遇见了心上人，想跟他说话，却只是低头一笑；头一低，碧玉簪子就“扑通”掉进了水里。诗人没有写一个“爱”字，也没有写她心里怎么想，只写动作——想说又不说、低头、笑、簪子落水——害羞和慌张全在里面了。这种用一个细节写出人物心情的方法，正是这首小诗动人的地方。

In English

“Lotus-Picking Song” was an old folk-song title, usually about girls rowing among the lotus in the watery south. Bai Juyi’s version is only four lines long but plays like a short film. The first two lines set the scene: water-chestnut leaves twist on the ripples, lotus leaves sway in the breeze, and a small boat slips through the thickest part of the lotus. The last two lines tell the story. The girl runs into the young man she likes. She wants to say something, but all she manages is to lower her head and smile — and as her head dips, her green jade hairpin drops into the water. The poet never uses the word “love” and never tells us what she is thinking. He only shows actions: wanting to speak and not speaking, looking down, smiling, the hairpin falling. Her shyness and her flustered heart are all there in that one small accident. Catching a whole feeling in one telling detail is what makes this little poem so charming, and it is easy enough for a beginner to read.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Bai Juyi (772–846) was one of the most-read Tang poets; he is said to have read his poems to an old woman and revised any line she could not understand. The year of this short poem is not known; it borrows an old folk-song title from the south.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

飐 zhǎn（风吹颤动）；萦 yíng；菱 líng；搔 sāo。“飐”机器朗读容易读错，已在“朗读用字”列替换同音字，音频需人工听验。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行版本。第一句“荷飐风”，一作“荷飐水”，网站用“风”。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

菱叶萦波荷飐风，

líng yè yíng bō hé zhǎn fēng

Water-chestnut leaves curl on the ripples, lotus leaves shiver in the breeze,

荷花深处小船通。

hé huā shēn chù xiǎo chuán tōng

and a little boat slips through the deepest lotus.

逢郎欲语低头笑，

féng láng yù yǔ dī tóu xiào

She meets her sweetheart, means to speak — just smiles and looks down,

碧玉搔头落水中。

bì yù sāo tóu luò shuǐ zhōng

and her green jade hairpin drops into the water.
