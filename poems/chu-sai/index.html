导读About this poem

The same moon, the same pass, for a thousand years — and still the soldiers do not come home. Wang Changling longs for a great general who could end the wars for good.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

《出塞》是唐代边塞诗的名篇。第一句“秦时明月汉时关”很特别：不是说月亮属于秦朝、关口属于汉朝，而是“秦汉时的明月、秦汉时的关塞”，前后互相补充，这种写法叫“互文”。一句话把时间拉回到一千年前：从秦汉起，这里就是战场，月亮和关塞一直都在。第二句“万里长征人未还”写出无数士兵远离家乡，一直没能回来。前两句把历史和现实连在一起，写出战争的漫长和人民的痛苦。后两句表达希望：如果像“飞将军”李广那样的名将还在，就不会让敌人的骑兵越过阴山来侵犯了。诗人既同情士兵，又希望国家有好将领守卫边疆，感情很深沉。明代有人称这首诗为唐人七绝第一。

In English

This is one of the most celebrated frontier poems of the Tang dynasty. Look closely at the first line: “Qin-dynasty bright moon, Han-dynasty pass.” It does not mean the moon belonged to the Qin and the pass to the Han. The two halves fill each other in — the moon and the pass of Qin and Han times. Chinese critics call this hùwén (互文, “mutually completing phrases”). With one line the poem reaches back nearly a thousand years: ever since the Qin and Han, this border has been a battlefield, and the same moon has shone on the same fortress. The second line brings us to the present: men marched ten thousand li and have still not come home. Together the two lines say that war here is endless, and that ordinary soldiers have always paid for it. The second half is a wish. The “Flying General” refers to Li Guang, a Han-dynasty general so feared that the Xiongnu nomads were said to avoid his territory (some scholars think it points to Wei Qing at Longcheng instead). If only such a general were alive today, the poet says, the horsemen of the steppe would never cross the Yin Mountains. The poem mixes pity for the soldiers with a hope for strong, wise leadership at the border. A Ming-dynasty critic called it the finest seven-character quatrain of the Tang.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Wang Changling (698?–757?) was known as the “master of the seven-character quatrain” and wrote many frontier poems after travelling to the northwest borders as a young man. This is the first of two poems titled “Beyond the Frontier”.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

“人未还”的“还”读 huán。“飞将”的“将”读 jiàng（将领）。“不教”的“教”读 jiāo（使、让），统编教材注 jiāo。

版本说明 About the text

采用统编小学语文教材版本，原为《出塞二首》其一。异文：“龙城飞将”一作“卢城飞将”；“飞将”一般认为指汉代名将李广（也有学者认为指卫青等），网站采用通行说法。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

秦时明月汉时关，

qín shí míng yuè hàn shí guān

The moon of Qin, the fortress of Han —

万里长征人未还。

wàn lǐ cháng zhēng rén wèi huán

men marched ten thousand li, and none has come home.

但使龙城飞将在，

dàn shǐ lóng chéng fēi jiàng zài

If the Flying General were here today,

不教胡马度阴山。

bù jiāo hú mǎ dù yīn shān

no raider’s horse would cross the Yin Mountains.
