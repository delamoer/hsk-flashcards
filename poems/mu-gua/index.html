导读About this poem

Someone tosses me a piece of fruit; I give back a piece of fine jade — not to repay them, but so we stay close forever. Three short verses, almost the same words, about why we give gifts.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗很短，三章几乎一样，只换了几个字：你送我木瓜，我回赠你美玉；你送我木桃，我回赠琼瑶；你送我木李，我回赠琼玖。木瓜、木桃、木李都是普通的水果，“琼琚”“琼瑶”“琼玖”却是珍贵的美玉——收到的少，回送的多。可是诗人马上说明：“匪报也”，这不是为了还礼，而是希望我们“永以为好”，永远相亲相爱。所以这首诗讲的不是交换礼物，而是真心：情意比东西贵重得多。古人在男女之间、朋友之间都用它来表达心意。成语“投桃报李”就和这首诗有关，意思是你对我好，我也对你好。

In English

This short song has three verses that are almost the same, changing only a couple of words: you toss me a quince, I give you fine jade; you toss me a “tree peach”, I give you jade; you toss me a “tree plum”, I give you jade again. The fruits are ordinary, but 琼琚, 琼瑶 and 琼玖 are all names for beautiful, precious jade — so the speaker gives back far more than he receives. Then he explains himself: 匪报也, “this isn’t a repayment”; it is 永以为好, “so that we can be close forever.” In other words the song isn’t about trading gifts at all. It is about what a gift means: the feeling is worth more than the thing. Tossing fruit to someone you liked was an old courting custom, and the poem has been read as a love song, but also as a song about friendship and loyalty. The idiom 投桃报李, “give a plum in return for a peach”, meaning to return kindness with kindness, grew out of the same idea. With its easy repetition it is a perfect first poem from the Book of Songs.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

The Book of Songs gathers 305 songs from about the 11th to the 6th century BC. This one is from the state of Wey (卫), in today’s northern Henan. The author is unknown, and readers have long disagreed whether it is a love song or a song between friends.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

琼琚 qióng jū；琼玖 qióng jiǔ；匪 fěi（通“非”）；好 hǎo（相好，不读 hào）。“好”机器朗读可能读成 hào，已在“朗读用字”列替换同音字。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

投我以木瓜，报之以琼琚。

tóu wǒ yǐ mù guā, bào zhī yǐ qióng jū

You tossed me a quince — I gave you a pendant of fine jade.

匪报也，永以为好也！

fěi bào yě, yǒng yǐ wéi hǎo yě

Not to pay you back — but so our friendship lasts forever.

投我以木桃，报之以琼瑶。

tóu wǒ yǐ mù táo, bào zhī yǐ qióng yáo

You tossed me a peach — I gave you a precious jade stone.

匪报也，永以为好也！

fěi bào yě, yǒng yǐ wéi hǎo yě

Not to pay you back — but so our friendship lasts forever.

投我以木李，报之以琼玖。

tóu wǒ yǐ mù lǐ, bào zhī yǐ qióng jiǔ

You tossed me a plum — I gave you a piece of dark jade.

匪报也，永以为好也！

fěi bào yě, yǒng yǐ wéi hǎo yě

Not to pay you back — but so our friendship lasts forever.
