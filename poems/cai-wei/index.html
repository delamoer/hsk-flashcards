导读About this poem

A soldier walks home after years at the frontier: when he left, the willows were swaying; now snow is falling. Eight short phrases that many Chinese readers consider the most beautiful lines in the Book of Songs.

直译LiteralLong ago, when I went away, the willows were soft and swaying.

直译LiteralMy heart is wounded and sad; no one knows my grief!

意译MeaningMy heart aches — and no one knows my sorrow.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

《采薇》写的是一个在边疆打了多年仗的士兵。全诗六章，这里选的是最后一章，写他终于走在回家的路上。前四句是一个时间的对比：当年出发的时候，杨柳轻轻摇摆，是春天；现在回来，大雪纷纷，是冬天。“杨柳依依”像是舍不得他走，“雨雪霏霏”又写出归途的寒冷和漫长——中间过去了多少年，诗里没说，读者却感受得到。后四句写路上又渴又饿、走得很慢，心里满是悲伤，却没有人懂。清代王夫之说这几句是“以乐景写哀”：用美好的春景来写离别的悲伤，悲伤反而更深。东晋的谢玄认为这是《诗经》里最好的句子。“柳”和“留”读音相近，从这里开始，杨柳就成了中国诗歌里离别的象征。

In English

The poem follows a soldier who has spent years fighting on the northern frontier. It has six stanzas; this is the last one, where he is finally walking home. The first four phrases set two moments side by side. When he left, the willows were swaying gently — it was spring. Now, coming back, snow is falling thick — it is winter. The swaying willows seem reluctant to let him go; the heavy snow makes the road home cold and endless. How many years passed in between? The poem doesn’t say, but you feel it. The last four phrases are plain and heavy: he walks slowly, thirsty and hungry, full of grief, and no one understands what he has been through. The critic Wang Fuzhi (17th century) said these lines “use a happy scene to write sorrow”: the lovely spring picture makes the parting even sadder. The general Xie Xuan (4th century) called them the finest lines in the whole Book of Songs. Because 柳 (willow) sounds like 留 (“to stay”), willows became, from this poem on, the classic Chinese image of saying goodbye.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

The poem is one of the “Minor Odes” (小雅) of the Book of Songs. Its author is unknown. It is traditionally dated to the Western Zhou (roughly 11th–8th century BC), when Zhou soldiers were sent to fight the Xianyun (猃狁), nomadic peoples to the north.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

雨雪霏霏的“雨”作动词“下（雪）”，教材和通行注本注 yù，日常朗读也常听到 yǔ；此处采用 yù。霏霏 fēi fēi；载渴载饥的“载”读 zài；来思的“思”是句末语气词，读 sī。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行的《毛诗》文本。全诗六章，每章八句，共四十八句；这里节选末章（第六章）八句，即通常被教材选入、最为人熟知的部分，网站按两个分句一行排版，共四行。题目中的“薇”（野豌豆）出现在前几章，节选部分没有。

昔我往矣，杨柳依依。

xī wǒ wǎng yǐ, yáng liǔ yī yī

When I marched away, the willows waved, soft and green.

今我来思，雨雪霏霏。

jīn wǒ lái sī, yù xuě fēi fēi

Now I am coming home, and the snow is falling thick.

行道迟迟，载渴载饥。

xíng dào chí chí, zài kě zài jī

Slowly I trudge along the road, thirsty and hungry.

我心伤悲，莫知我哀！

wǒ xīn shāng bēi, mò zhī wǒ āi

My heart aches — and no one knows my sorrow.
