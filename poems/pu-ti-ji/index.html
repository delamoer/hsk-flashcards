导读About this poem

A kitchen worker who could not read answered a senior monk’s poem with this one — and became the Sixth Patriarch of Chan (Zen) Buddhism. Its third line, “from the start there is not a single thing”, is among the most famous lines in Buddhist writing.

中文解析

“偈”（jì）是佛教里用来讲道理的短诗。据《六祖坛经》记载，五祖弘忍让弟子们各写一首偈，看谁真正懂了佛法。大弟子神秀写道：“身是菩提树，心如明镜台。时时勤拂拭，勿使惹尘埃。”意思是：身体像菩提树，心像明亮的镜子，要时时擦拭，不让灰尘落上去——也就是说要努力修行，去掉坏念头。在寺里舂米的慧能不识字，请人代写了这首偈来回应：菩提本来就不是树，明镜也不是台子；本来就什么都没有，灰尘又能落在哪里呢？慧能的意思是：心的本性本来就是清净的、空的，不需要一遍遍地擦。这种“一下子明白”的想法，就是禅宗讲的“顿悟”。五祖认为慧能悟得更深，把衣钵传给了他。读这首诗，要和神秀的那首对比着读。

In English

A jì (gatha) is a short Buddhist verse that teaches an idea. According to the Platform Sutra, the Fifth Patriarch of Chan, Hongren, asked his monks to each write a verse showing their understanding. The senior disciple Shenxiu wrote: “The body is the bodhi tree, the mind a bright mirror on a stand. Wipe it clean diligently at all times, and let no dust settle.” In other words: keep working at your practice and polish away bad thoughts. Huineng, an illiterate young man pounding rice in the kitchen, had someone write this reply on the wall: bodhi was never a tree; the bright mirror has no stand; from the very beginning there is not one thing — so where could dust land? His point is that the mind’s true nature is already pure and empty; there is nothing to wipe. This idea of seeing it all at once is “sudden enlightenment” (dùnwù), the heart of Chan, later known as Zen. Hongren judged Huineng the deeper of the two and secretly passed him the robe and bowl of succession. The poem is best read side by side with Shenxiu’s, line by line.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Huineng (638–713), the Sixth Patriarch of Chan Buddhism, came from a poor family in Guangdong. The story of this verse comes from the Platform Sutra, compiled by his followers; historians debate how much of it is literal history, and the earliest (Dunhuang) copy of the sutra gives a different wording.

版本说明 About the text

采用流传最广的元代宗宝本《六祖坛经》文本。敦煌本《坛经》作“菩提本无树，明镜亦无台。佛性常清净，何处有尘埃”，“本来无一物”一句为后来版本所有。末句标点有作“！”的，网站用问号。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

菩提本无树，

pú tí běn wú shù

Awakening was never a tree,

明镜亦非台。

míng jìng yì fēi tái

and the bright mirror stands on nothing.

本来无一物，

běn lái wú yī wù

From the start there is not a single thing —

何处惹尘埃？

hé chù rě chén āi

so where would the dust come to rest?
