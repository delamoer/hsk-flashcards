导读About this poem

“If only life were always like the first time we met” — one of the most quoted lines in Chinese. In the voice of a woman who has been abandoned, Nalan Xingde writes about how love changes, using famous old love stories.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首词用一个被抛弃的女子的口吻来写。第一句“人生若只如初见”最有名：如果人和人永远像第一次见面时那样美好就好了。接着用了一个典故（古代的故事）：汉代班婕妤被皇帝冷落，写诗把自己比作秋天被丢在一边的扇子。为什么到了秋天，扇子就让人伤心呢？下面两句说：你轻易就变了心，却反过来说是我的心容易变。下阕用唐明皇和杨贵妃的故事：他们曾在骊山长生殿半夜发誓永远相爱，后来杨贵妃死了，唐明皇在雨中听到铃声，作了《雨霖铃》曲怀念她；可他总还有过当年“比翼鸟、连理枝”的誓言，现在这个薄情的你，连他都比不上。全词几乎句句用典，所以是挑战级；但感情直接，第一句不用懂典故也能打动人。题目里的“柬友”说明是写给朋友的，有人认为是借爱情写友情的变化。

In English

The lyric is spoken by a woman who has been left by her lover. Its first line is one of the most quoted in Chinese: “If only life were always like the first time we met.” First meetings are perfect; it is time that spoils things. The second line uses an allusion, a reference to an old story. In the Han dynasty, Lady Ban lost the emperor’s favour and wrote a poem comparing herself to a fan thrown aside when autumn comes. Why should the autumn wind make a painted fan so sad? The next lines answer: you changed your heart so easily — and then said it was my heart that changed. The second half turns to the Tang emperor Xuanzong and his beloved Yang Guifei. At midnight on Mount Li they swore to love each other forever. After she died, the emperor heard bells ringing in the rain and composed the tune “Bells in the Rain” in her memory. They once vowed to be like birds sharing one pair of wings and trees with joined branches. Even that emperor, the speaker says, was better than you, my faithless man in fine clothes. Nearly every line hides a story, which is why this is a challenge poem. Yet the feeling is direct, and the first line moves people even without the stories. The title says it was “sent to a friend”, so some readers think it is really about a friendship that changed.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Nalan Xingde (1655–1685) was a Manchu nobleman and imperial bodyguard to the Kangxi Emperor, and one of the finest ci poets of the Qing; he died at just thirty. Who the “friend” in the title was, and whether the lyric is about love or friendship, is still debated.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

人生若只如初见，

rén shēng ruò zhǐ rú chū jiàn

If only life were always like the day we first met —

何事秋风悲画扇。

hé shì qiū fēng bēi huà shàn

why should the autumn wind make the painted fan grieve?

等闲变却故人心，

děng xián biàn què gù rén xīn

You changed your heart as if it were nothing,

却道故人心易变。

què dào gù rén xīn yì biàn

and then said it was my heart that changed.

骊山语罢清宵半，

lí shān yǔ bà qīng xiāo bàn

On Mount Li they made their vows at midnight;

泪雨霖铃终不怨。

lèi yǔ lín líng zhōng bù yuàn

later came tears, rain and bells — and still she did not blame him.

何如薄幸锦衣郎，

hé rú bó xìng jǐn yī láng

But you, faithless man in your fine silk clothes —

比翼连枝当日愿。

bǐ yì lián zhī dāng rì yuàn

he at least once swore to be wings side by side, branches grown as one.
