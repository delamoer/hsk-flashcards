导读About this poem

How do you tell a good person from a fake? Not by fortune-telling, says Bai Juyi — just wait. Time is the only real test.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

“放言”就是放开来说话。这首诗讲一个道理：判断人和事的真假，要靠时间来检验。开头说：送你一个解决疑惑的办法，不用烧龟甲、摆蓍草去占卜。接着打了两个比方：试验玉的真假，要烧满三天（据说真玉烧三天也不发热）；辨别木材好坏，要等七年（据说豫章树长七年才能和别的树分清）。五、六句举历史人物：周公辅佐年幼的成王，曾被人造谣说他想篡位；王莽篡夺汉朝之前，却表现得非常谦虚恭敬。最后两句说：假如他们在那时就死了，一个忠臣、一个奸臣的真面目，还有谁知道呢？诗写于白居易被贬途中，也是在为自己辩白：请给时间，真相自会清楚。

In English

放言 means “speaking freely”. The poem makes one point: the only reliable way to tell true from false — in people and in things — is to give it time. It opens: “Let me give you a way to settle your doubts, and you won’t need to crack tortoise shells or count yarrow stalks” (the two classic ways of fortune-telling). Then come two comparisons. To test jade, burn it for three full days (real jade, it was said, stays cool); to judge timber, wait seven years (the camphor tree was said to be indistinguishable from others until then). Lines 5–6 turn to history. The Duke of Zhou, who loyally served a child king, was once rumoured to be plotting to seize the throne; Wang Mang, who really did seize the Han throne, looked humble and respectful right up until he did it. The ending drives it home: if either man had died at that moment, who would ever have known which was loyal and which was false? Bai Juyi wrote this on his way into exile, so it is also a quiet defence of himself: give it time, and the truth will come out.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Bai Juyi (772–846) was one of the most popular Tang poets, known for plain, clear language. In 815 he was demoted to Jiangzhou for speaking out at court; on the way he wrote five poems “Speaking My Mind” in reply to a set by his friend Yuan Zhen, who had been exiled earlier.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

钻龟 zuān；蓍 shī（蓍草，占卜用）；与 yǔ；篡 cuàn；便 biàn；谁 shuí（诗词中多读 shuí，口语读 shéi）。机器朗读“蓍”可能读错，已在“朗读用字”列替换同音字。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行本。白居易原诗有自注：“真玉烧三日不热”“豫章木生七年而后知”。“辨材”一作“辨才”。《全唐诗》异文：“流言日”一作“流言后”，“未篡时”一作“下士时”，“向使当初”一作“若使当年”，末句一作“至今真伪有谁知”。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

赠君一法决狐疑，

zèng jūn yī fǎ jué hú yí

Let me give you a way to settle every doubt —

不用钻龟与祝蓍。

bù yòng zuān guī yǔ zhù shī

no need to crack tortoise shells or cast the yarrow.

试玉要烧三日满，

shì yù yào shāo sān rì mǎn

To test real jade, you burn it three whole days;

辨材须待七年期。

biàn cái xū dài qī nián qī

to know good timber, you wait for seven years.

周公恐惧流言日，

zhōu gōng kǒng jù liú yán rì

Think of the Duke of Zhou, when rumours made him tremble,

王莽谦恭未篡时。

wáng mǎng qiān gōng wèi cuàn shí

and Wang Mang, so modest, before he stole the throne.

向使当初身便死，

xiàng shǐ dāng chū shēn biàn sǐ

Had either man died at that very moment,

一生真伪复谁知？

yī shēng zhēn wěi fù shuí zhī

who would ever have known the true face of his life?
