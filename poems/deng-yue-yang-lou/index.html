导读About this poem

Old, ill and adrift, Du Fu finally sees the great Dongting Lake — a lake so vast it seems to split two ancient kingdoms and float the whole universe. Then he looks north to the war and weeps.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

岳阳楼在湖南岳阳，下面就是洞庭湖。第一、二句很平淡：从前就听说过洞庭湖，今天终于登上了岳阳楼——多年的心愿实现了。第三、四句写湖的壮阔，是写洞庭湖最有名的句子：吴国和楚国的土地好像被湖水从东南方向分开；天和地、日和月，好像日夜都漂浮在湖面上。“坼”是裂开，“浮”是漂浮，两个字把湖写得无边无际。第五、六句一下子转到自己：亲戚朋友连一封信都没有，年老多病的我只有一条小船。前面的湖有多大，这里的人就有多孤单。最后两句又从个人转到国家：北方关山一带还在打仗，我靠着楼上的栏杆，眼泪鼻涕一起流下来。诗人在自己最困难的时候，心里想的仍然是国家，这正是杜甫被称为“诗圣”的原因之一。

In English

Yueyang Tower stands in Hunan province, looking over Dongting Lake. The opening couplet is deliberately plain: long ago I heard of Dongting's waters; today I climb Yueyang Tower — a lifelong wish fulfilled. The second couplet is perhaps the most famous description of the lake ever written. The ancient lands of Wu and Chu seem to be split apart by it to the east and south; heaven and earth, sun and moon, seem to float on it day and night. Two verbs — 坼, “to crack open”, and 浮, “to float” — make the water seem boundless. Then the poem turns sharply inward. Not a single letter comes from family or friends; old and sick, he has nothing but a lone boat. The vaster the lake, the lonelier the man. The final couplet turns outward again, to the country: war horses still fill the passes to the north, and leaning on the railing, he weeps. Even at his lowest, Du Fu's thoughts go to the nation's suffering — one reason he is remembered as the “Sage of Poetry”.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Du Fu (712–770) wrote this in the winter of 768, when he had drifted down the Yangtze to Yueyang with his family, living mostly on a boat. Tibetan forces were then raiding the northwest frontier. He died about two years later.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

昔闻洞庭水，

xī wén dòng tíng shuǐ

For years I had heard of Dongting Lake;

今上岳阳楼。

jīn shàng yuè yáng lóu

today I climb Yueyang Tower at last.

吴楚东南坼，

wú chǔ dōng nán chè

It splits the old lands of Wu and Chu to the east and south;

乾坤日夜浮。

qián kūn rì yè fú

heaven and earth float on it, day and night.

亲朋无一字，

qīn péng wú yī zì

From family and friends, not a single word;

老病有孤舟。

lǎo bìng yǒu gū zhōu

old and sick, I have only a lonely boat.

戎马关山北，

róng mǎ guān shān běi

War horses still beyond the northern passes —

凭轩涕泗流。

píng xuān tì sì liú

I lean on the railing, and my tears run down.
