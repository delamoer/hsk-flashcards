导读About this poem

Two orioles in a willow, a line of egrets in the blue sky, snow on the western peaks, boats at the door bound for the far east — four lines, four pictures, all seen from Du Fu’s cottage window.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这是杜甫住在成都草堂时写的一首诗，四句写了四幅画面。前两句一近一远：两只黄鹂在翠绿的柳树上唱歌，一行白鹭飞向蓝天。黄、翠、白、青四种颜色放在一起，鲜艳明亮；有声音（鸣）也有动作（上），充满春天的生气。后两句一句写窗，一句写门：从窗口望出去，能看到西边雪山上千年不化的积雪，窗户像一个画框把雪山“含”在里面；门前停着从东吴来的、走了万里路的船。“千秋”是时间，“万里”是空间，诗的眼界一下子变得很大。这首诗还有一个特点：每两句都是工整的“对仗”——字数相同、词性相对，如“两个”对“一行”、“黄鹂”对“白鹭”，读起来非常整齐。当时战乱刚刚平息，杜甫回到草堂，心情愉快，所以诗写得这样明朗。

In English

Du Fu wrote this at his thatched cottage in Chengdu, and it is really four pictures in four lines. The first two lines move from near to far: two orioles singing in a green willow, and a line of white egrets flying up into the blue sky. Count the colours — yellow, green, white, blue — and notice there is both sound (singing) and movement (flying up). It is a bright spring day. The last two lines are framed by the house itself. Through the window he sees the snow of the Western Hills, snow that has lain there for a thousand autumns; the word 含 (“to hold in the mouth”) makes the window a picture frame holding the mountains. At the door are moored boats that have come ten thousand li from Eastern Wu, the lower Yangtze region near today’s Shanghai. “A thousand autumns” is time; “ten thousand li” is distance; suddenly a small cottage looks out on the whole country. The poem is also a perfect example of duìzhàng (对仗, parallelism): in each pair of lines the words match one for one — “two” against “one line”, “yellow orioles” against “white egrets”, “window” against “door”. Du Fu was happy when he wrote it: the war had just calmed down and he had come back to his home; and boats from the east meant the roads were open again.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Du Fu (712–770), honoured as the “Sage of Poetry”, spent most of his life in poverty and wandering during the An Lushan Rebellion. In 764 he returned to his cottage in Chengdu; this is the third of four quatrains he wrote there.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

“一行”的“行”读 háng（行列），不读 xíng。“门泊”的“泊”读 bó（停船）。“两个”的“个”按统编教材读 gè。其余无特殊读音。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

两个黄鹂鸣翠柳，

liǎng gè huáng lí míng cuì liǔ

Two orioles sing in the green willow,

一行白鹭上青天。

yī háng bái lù shàng qīng tiān

a line of white egrets climbs the blue sky.

窗含西岭千秋雪，

chuāng hán xī lǐng qiān qiū xuě

My window frames the snow of the western peaks, a thousand years old;

门泊东吴万里船。

mén bó dōng wú wàn lǐ chuán

at my door are boats that sailed ten thousand li from the east.
