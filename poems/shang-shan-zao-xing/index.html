导读About this poem

A traveller sets off before dawn in the mountains, missing home. Its third and fourth lines are famous: ten characters, six things, no verbs — and you can see the whole cold morning.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗写一个人在商山（今陕西商洛一带）的旅店里天不亮就起床赶路，心里想着家乡。第一联直接说事：车马的铃铛响了，旅人想起故乡，心里难过。第二联“鸡声茅店月，人迹板桥霜”是千古名句：十个字全是名词，没有一个动词——鸡叫声、茅草小店、天上残月、木板桥、桥上的霜和霜上的脚印——几样东西排在一起，就画出了清冷的早晨，还告诉我们：有人比他走得更早。第三联写路上的景色：槲树叶落在山路上，白色的枳花把驿站的墙映得发亮。最后一联点明心情：他想起昨夜梦见的杜陵（指长安，诗人心中的家），那里野鸭和大雁挤满了弯弯的池塘，温暖热闹，和眼前的孤单正好相反。用梦中的家乡景色结尾，思乡之情不用直说也很清楚。

In English

A traveller staying at an inn on Mount Shang, in today’s Shaanxi, gets up before dawn to continue his journey. The first couplet states the situation plainly: the bells on the carriage begin to ring, and he feels the ache of being far from home. The second couplet is one of the most admired in Chinese poetry. Its ten characters are all nouns — a rooster’s crow, a thatched inn, the moon; footprints, a plank bridge, frost — set side by side with no verb at all. Yet the picture is complete: a cold, half-dark morning, and footprints in the frost showing that someone has already set out before him. The third couplet shows the road itself: oak leaves fallen on the mountain path, white trifoliate-orange blossoms brightening the wall of the post station. Only in the last couplet does he reveal his heart. He remembers last night’s dream of Duling, near the capital Chang’an, where wild ducks and geese crowded a winding pond. That warm, busy scene is the opposite of his lonely road, so the poem ends on homesickness without ever naming it.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Wen Tingyun (c. 812–866) was a late Tang poet famous for his quick wit and his elegant song lyrics, though he repeatedly failed the civil service exam and held only minor posts. This poem is traditionally dated to around 859, when he left Chang’an and passed through the Shangshan mountains on his way to a post in the south.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

晨起动征铎，

chén qǐ dòng zhēng duó

Up at dawn, the carriage bells begin to shake —

客行悲故乡。

kè xíng bēi gù xiāng

a man on the road, his heart turned toward home.

鸡声茅店月，

jī shēng máo diàn yuè

A rooster crows; the moon hangs over the thatched inn;

人迹板桥霜。

rén jì bǎn qiáo shuāng

footprints already cross the frost on the plank bridge.

槲叶落山路，

hú yè luò shān lù

Oak leaves lie scattered on the mountain path,

枳花明驿墙。

zhǐ huā míng yì qiáng

white blossoms light up the wall of the post house.

因思杜陵梦，

yīn sī dù líng mèng

It brings back last night’s dream of Duling —

凫雁满回塘。

fú yàn mǎn huí táng

ducks and wild geese crowding the curving pond.
