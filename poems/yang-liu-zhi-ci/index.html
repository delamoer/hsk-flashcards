导读About this poem

A woman begs her man not to stay away too long — but almost every line says it through a pun. It ends with one of the best-known love lines in Chinese: red beans set in dice of bone, “longing that goes to the bone — do you know?”

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗是一个女子对即将远行的爱人说的话，妙在几乎每一句都用了“双关”——一个词同时有两个意思。第一句“井底点灯深烛伊”：在井底点灯，是“深烛”，听起来像“深嘱”，意思是“我深深地嘱咐你”（伊：你）。第二句“共郎长行莫围棋”：“长行”是一种古代的博戏，也是“长久远行”；“围棋”谐音“违期”，就是不按时回来。表面说“陪你玩长行，别下围棋”，实际是说“你出远门，千万别误了归期”。第三、四句最有名：精巧的骰子是骨头做的，上面嵌着红色的点，像一颗颗红豆。红豆在中国又叫“相思豆”，象征思念；骰子是骨头做的，所以说“入骨相思”——思念已经深入骨头。最后一问“知不知”，又娇嗔又真诚。整首诗用游戏的语言说最认真的感情，十分俏皮动人。

In English

A woman speaks to the man she loves before he leaves on a journey, and nearly every line hides a pun — one phrase carrying two meanings. Line one: “lighting a lamp deep in a well” is 深烛 shēn zhú, which sounds like 深嘱 shēn zhǔ, “I earnestly urge you”; 伊 means “you”. Line two plays with two board games. 长行 was a popular dice game, but the words also mean “a long journey”; 围棋 (weiqi, the game of Go) sounds like 违期 wéi qī, “to miss the agreed date”. So “play changxing with me, not weiqi” secretly means “on your long trip, don’t miss the day you promised to come back.” The last two lines are the famous ones. Fine dice were made of bone, with red pips inlaid like little red beans. In Chinese, red beans are called “love-longing beans”, and since the dice are bone, the longing has gone “into the bone”. She ends with a question — “do you know, or not?” — half teasing, half pleading. Serious love, said in the playful language of games: that is the charm of this little song.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Wen Tingyun (c. 812–866) wrote many lyrics to popular tunes of his day. The “Willow Branch” was a well-known tune; “newly added sounds” means it was a newer, expanded musical version. This is the second of two pieces in the set, both written in a woman’s voice.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

骰子 tóu zi（不读 shǎi，口语“色子 shǎi zi”是另一种说法）；伊 yī（你）；烛 zhú。“长行”是古代一种博戏名，网站读 cháng xíng，与“远行”双关（有注本将博戏“长行”读作 cháng háng）。机器朗读“长行”可能读成 cháng háng，已在“朗读用字”列替换为“常行”，音频需人工听验。

井底点灯深烛伊，

jǐng dǐ diǎn dēng shēn zhú yī

A lamp lit deep in a well — so deep is what I ask of you:

共郎长行莫围棋。

gòng láng cháng xíng mò wéi qí

go off on your long journey, love, but don’t you miss the day you’re due.

玲珑骰子安红豆，

líng lóng tóu zi ān hóng dòu

Into these delicate dice of bone, red beans are set —

入骨相思知不知。

rù gǔ xiāng sī zhī bù zhī

a longing gone into the bone. Do you know? Do you?
