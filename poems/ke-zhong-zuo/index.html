导读About this poem

Most homesick poems are sad. This one turns the theme upside down: the wine is so good and the host so generous that the traveller forgets he is far from home.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗是李白在东鲁兰陵（今山东一带）做客时写的。前两句写酒：兰陵的美酒带着郁金香草的香气，倒在玉碗里，闪着琥珀一样的金黄色光彩——有香气、有颜色、有精美的酒器，让人还没喝就想醉。后两句是全诗的妙处：只要主人能让客人喝醉，我就不知道哪里是异乡了。一般的客中诗都写思乡的愁苦，李白却反过来写，显得豪爽、乐观。不过，“不知何处是他乡”本身也说明他心里明白自己是在他乡，只是借美酒和友情把乡愁放下了。这种“反着写”的方法，让短短四句诗有了更多味道。

In English

Li Bai wrote this while staying as a guest in Lanling, in today’s Shandong. The first two lines are all about the wine: Lanling’s famous brew is scented with turmeric, and poured into a jade bowl it glows like amber. Smell, colour and a beautiful cup — you feel tipsy before you drink. The last two lines are the clever part: “As long as my host can get his guest drunk, I won’t know where the foreign land is.” Most poems written away from home dwell on homesickness. Li Bai flips the theme and sounds cheerful and carefree instead. Yet the line “I won’t know where the foreign land is” quietly admits that he knows perfectly well he is far from home; good wine and good company simply let him set the feeling aside for one evening. Saying the opposite of what readers expect gives four short lines an extra layer — open-hearted on the surface, a little wistful underneath.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Li Bai (701–762) lived for some years in the Eastern Lu region (today’s Shandong) in the 730s–740s. The poem is traditionally dated to that period; another version is titled “Song of the Guest” (客中行).

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

盛来 chéng（盛装，不读 shèng）；琥珀 hǔ pò；郁金香 yù jīn xiāng。机器朗读“盛”容易读错，已在朗读用字中替换同音字。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行文本。题目一作《客中行》。“郁金香”指用郁金草浸泡的香气，不是今天的郁金香花（tulip）。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

兰陵美酒郁金香，

lán líng měi jiǔ yù jīn xiāng

The fine wine of Lanling, sweet with spice,

玉碗盛来琥珀光。

yù wǎn chéng lái hǔ pò guāng

poured into a jade bowl, glows like amber.

但使主人能醉客，

dàn shǐ zhǔ rén néng zuì kè

If only my host keeps his guest well drunk,

不知何处是他乡。

bù zhī hé chù shì tā xiāng

I’ll forget which place is not my home.
