蝶恋花·春景（花褪残红青杏小）

Dié Liàn Huā · Chūn Jǐng
Butterflies Love Flowers: A Spring Scene (“The last red petals fade, green apricots are small”)

导读About this poem

Late spring, catkins blowing away — and a traveller hears a girl laughing on a swing behind a garden wall. Her laughter fades, and he is left feeling foolish. A light, funny and slightly sad little poem.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

上阕写暮春景色：花瓣褪去了红色、快要落尽，青青的杏子还很小；燕子飞来飞去，绿水绕着人家流过。枝头的柳絮被风吹得越来越少，可是天涯海角，哪里没有芳草呢？“天涯何处无芳草”一句，既有春天将尽的惋惜，又有一种自我安慰的豁达。下阕写一件小事：墙里有人荡秋千，墙外是一条路。墙外的行人走过，听到墙里的姑娘在笑。笑声渐渐听不见了，四周也安静下来，多情的行人反倒被无情的姑娘惹得烦恼起来。其实姑娘根本不知道墙外有人，她并不是“无情”，只是行人自作多情。这首词把伤春和一点幽默结合在一起，读来轻松又带惆怅。今天“天涯何处无芳草”常用来劝失恋的人：好的人到处都有。

In English

The first half paints late spring. The flowers have lost their red, the green apricots are still small; swallows dart about, and clear water winds around the cottages. The willow catkins on the branches grow fewer each time the wind blows — yet “where under heaven is there no sweet grass?” That line carries both regret that spring is ending and a cheerful shrug: there is beauty everywhere. The second half tells a tiny story. Inside a garden wall there is a swing; outside, a road. A traveller walking past hears a girl laughing on the swing. Her laughter fades, the voices grow quiet, and the passer-by, full of feeling, is left upset by someone who feels nothing. Of course she never knew he was there — she isn’t heartless; he is simply being sentimental. Mixing the sadness of spring’s end with a gentle joke at his own expense, the poem is light but wistful. Today 天涯何处无芳草 is what friends say to someone after a breakup: there are plenty of good people out there.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Su Shi (1037–1101), also called Su Dongpo, was a Song official, poet and calligrapher. The date of this poem is uncertain. A well-known story says that in his last exile, in Huizhou, his companion Wang Zhaoyun could never sing the line about the willow catkins without weeping.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

花褪残红青杏小。

huā tùn cán hóng qīng xìng xiǎo

The last red fades from the blossoms; the green apricots are tiny.

燕子飞时，绿水人家绕。

yàn zi fēi shí, lǜ shuǐ rén jiā rào

Swallows on the wing, clear water winding round the houses.

枝上柳绵吹又少，

zhī shàng liǔ mián chuī yòu shǎo

Each gust leaves fewer catkins on the willow,

天涯何处无芳草！

tiān yá hé chù wú fāng cǎo

but where in the world is there no sweet grass?

墙里秋千墙外道。

qiáng lǐ qiū qiān qiáng wài dào

A swing inside the wall, a road outside it.

墙外行人，墙里佳人笑。

qiáng wài xíng rén, qiáng lǐ jiā rén xiào

A traveller outside; inside, a pretty girl laughing.

笑渐不闻声渐悄，

xiào jiàn bù wén shēng jiàn qiǎo

Her laughter fades, the voices die away,

多情却被无情恼。

duō qíng què bèi wú qíng nǎo

and the one who felt too much is left fretting over one who never knew.
