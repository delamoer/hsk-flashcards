导读About this poem

Thrown into prison, the poet hears a cicada singing outside in autumn. Its voice, heavy with dew and drowned by wind, becomes his own — honest, unheard, and alone.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗是骆宾王在监狱里写的。他多次上书批评朝政，被人诬陷入狱。秋天，他听到窗外的蝉在叫。第一联“西陆”指秋天，“南冠”指囚犯（春秋时楚国的囚犯戴着南方式样的帽子），蝉声一响，囚徒的愁思就更深了。第二联说：自己已经白了头，怎么受得了那黑亮的蝉翼对着自己唱歌？蝉的“玄鬓”和人的“白头”形成对比。第三联写蝉：露水重，飞不起来；风太大，叫声被淹没。这两句表面写蝉，其实写自己——处境艰难，说话没人听。最后一联直接问：没有人相信我的清白，谁能替我表白这颗心？古人认为蝉只喝露水、住在高处，象征高洁。借写一个东西来写自己的品格和遭遇，这种写法叫“托物言志”。这首诗和虞世南的《蝉》、李商隐的《蝉》并称唐代咏蝉三绝。

In English

Luo Binwang wrote this in prison. He had sent memorials criticising the government, and was jailed on false charges. One autumn day he heard a cicada singing outside. The first couplet uses two learned phrases: 西陆 (“the western path” of the sun) means autumn, and 南冠 (“southern cap”) means a prisoner — in an old story, a captive from the southern state of Chu kept wearing his native cap in jail. When the cicada sings, the prisoner’s sorrow deepens. The second couplet sets the cicada’s glossy black wings (玄鬓, literally “black temples”, a hairstyle named after cicada wings) against the poet’s white head: how can he bear such a young creature singing at an old, broken man? The third couplet describes the insect — the dew is heavy, so it cannot fly far; the wind is strong, so its voice is swallowed up. On the surface this is about the cicada; underneath it is about the poet, weighed down and unable to make himself heard. The last couplet says it outright: no one believes he is pure — who will speak up for his heart? The Chinese believed cicadas drank only dew and lived high in trees, so they stood for purity. Writing about an object to speak about yourself is called tuō wù yán zhì. This poem is often grouped with Yu Shinan’s and Li Shangyin’s cicada poems as the three great Tang poems on the cicada.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Luo Binwang (c. 619–c. 684) was one of the “Four Paragons of the Early Tang”. In 678 he was imprisoned after offending powerful officials; the poem, originally with a long prose preface (not included here), dates from that year. He later joined a rebellion against Empress Wu and disappeared after its defeat.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

西陆蝉声唱，

xī lù chán shēng chàng

Autumn has come, and a cicada is singing;

南冠客思深。

nán guān kè sī shēn

in my cell, a prisoner’s sorrow runs deeper still.

不堪玄鬓影，

bù kān xuán bìn yǐng

How can I bear those glossy black wings

来对白头吟。

lái duì bái tóu yín

coming to sing to a man whose hair is white?

露重飞难进，

lù zhòng fēi nán jìn

The dew is heavy — it can barely fly;

风多响易沉。

fēng duō xiǎng yì chén

the wind is strong — its song is easily drowned.

无人信高洁，

wú rén xìn gāo jié

No one believes that it is pure;

谁为表予心？

shuí wèi biǎo yú xīn

who will speak for my heart?
