导读About this poem

A man who can’t sleep gets up to play his zither by moonlight, hears lonely birds crying in the dark, and paces the room with a sadness he never explains — the opening poem of Ruan Ji’s famous series of eighty-two.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗写一个失眠的夜晚。半夜睡不着，诗人干脆起来弹琴。薄薄的帐子映着明月，清风吹动衣襟——这本来是很美的夜景，可是在他眼里却很冷清。远处传来孤单的大雁在野外哀叫，飞鸟在北边的树林里鸣叫，这些声音更让人不安。最后两句：走来走去，又能看到什么呢？只有满怀忧思，独自伤心。全诗没有说他为什么忧愁，只写了动作（起坐、弹琴、徘徊）和景物（明月、清风、孤鸿），让读者自己去体会。这和阮籍的处境有关：他生活在政治非常危险的年代，说错一句话就可能被杀，所以只能把心里的苦闷藏在景物里，写得含蓄、隐晦。“含蓄”就是话不说透，意思留在言外。这首诗是阮籍八十二首《咏怀》的第一首，奠定了整组诗孤独、忧伤的基调。

In English

This poem is about a sleepless night. Unable to sleep, the poet gets up and plays his zither. The moon shines through the thin bed curtain and a cool breeze stirs his robe — a lovely scene, but to him it feels cold and empty. From the open country comes the cry of a lone wild goose; birds call in the woods to the north. These sounds only make him more uneasy. The last two lines: pacing back and forth, what is there to see? Only worry, and a heart that aches alone. The poem never tells us why he is sad. It gives us only actions — sitting up, playing, pacing — and things around him — moonlight, breeze, lonely birds — and leaves the rest to us. That restraint came from Ruan Ji’s situation. He lived in a dangerous political time, when one careless word could cost a man his life, so he hid his feelings inside images and kept his meaning just out of reach. Chinese critics call this hánxù: holding something back, leaving meaning beyond the words. As the first of Ruan Ji’s eighty-two “Singing My Thoughts” poems, it sets the lonely, troubled tone for the whole series.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Ruan Ji (210–263) was the leading figure of the “Seven Sages of the Bamboo Grove”, scholars famous for drinking, music and avoiding politics. As the Sima clan seized power from the Cao family’s Wei state, many critics were executed; Ruan Ji survived by acting the eccentric drunk and writing poems whose meaning is deliberately veiled.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

夜中不能寐，起坐弹鸣琴。

yè zhōng bù néng mèi, qǐ zuò tán míng qín

Deep in the night I cannot sleep, so I sit up and play my zither.

薄帷鉴明月，清风吹我襟。

bó wéi jiàn míng yuè, qīng fēng chuī wǒ jīn

The bright moon glows through the thin curtain; a cool wind tugs at my robe.

孤鸿号外野，翔鸟鸣北林。

gū hóng háo wài yě, xiáng niǎo míng běi lín

A lone goose cries out in the far fields; birds call, circling, in the northern woods.

徘徊将何见？忧思独伤心。

pái huái jiāng hé jiàn, yōu sī dú shāng xīn

I pace and pace — what is there to see? Only worry, and a heart that aches alone.
