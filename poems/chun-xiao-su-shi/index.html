导读About this poem

“A moment of a spring night is worth a thousand in gold” — the poem behind one of China’s best-known sayings about how precious time is.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗写春天的夜晚。第一句开门见山：春天的夜晚，哪怕短短一刻，也值得上千两黄金。为什么这么宝贵？第二句回答：花儿散发着清香，月光下花影朦胧。这是从鼻子闻到的、眼睛看到的美。后两句换一个角度：高楼上，富贵人家还在唱歌奏乐，乐声细细柔柔；而挂着秋千的院子里，夜已经深沉、寂静了。一边热闹，一边安静，对比之中，春夜更显得美好又短暂。有人认为后两句也带一点讽刺：有钱人只顾通宵享乐。今天“春宵一刻值千金”常用来比喻美好的时光非常宝贵，要好好珍惜。

In English

A poem about a spring night. The first line gets straight to the point: even a brief moment of a spring night is worth a thousand pieces of gold. Why so precious? The second line answers: the flowers give off a clear, fresh scent, and under the moon their shadows lie soft on the ground — beauty for the nose and for the eyes. The last two lines change the angle. Up in a tower, singing and pipes are still playing, thin and delicate; down in a courtyard where a swing hangs, the night has grown deep and still. Noise on one side, silence on the other: the contrast makes the night feel lovely and fleeting at the same time. Some readers also hear a little criticism in these lines — the rich partying through the night while time slips by. Today 春宵一刻值千金 is a common saying meaning that good times are precious and should not be wasted. (Note: 一刻 was a short unit of time, about fifteen minutes.)

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Su Shi (1037–1101), also called Su Dongpo, was a Song-dynasty official, poet, essayist and calligrapher. The date and place of this poem are not known.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

春宵一刻值千金，

chūn xiāo yī kè zhí qiān jīn

One moment of a spring night is worth a fortune in gold:

花有清香月有阴。

huā yǒu qīng xiāng yuè yǒu yīn

the flowers smell sweet, the moon casts soft shadows.

歌管楼台声细细，

gē guǎn lóu tái shēng xì xì

From the towers drift faint songs and pipes;

秋千院落夜沉沉。

qiū qiān yuàn luò yè chén chén

in the courtyard, the swing hangs still, and the night grows deep.
