导读About this poem

A desert storm darkens the sun; a half-furled red flag rides out of camp — and before the reinforcements even reach the front, news arrives that the enemy chief has been captured. A battle poem told entirely through speed.

中文解析

这是王昌龄《从军行》七首中的第五首，写唐军在边塞打了一场漂亮的胜仗。第一句写环境：大沙漠里狂风卷起沙尘，连太阳都暗了下来，气氛紧张。第二句写出发：红旗半卷着，部队冲出军营大门——旗子“半卷”，是因为风大，也是因为急行军，写出了速度和气势。后两句最妙：前方部队昨夜已经在洮河北岸开战，这支后援部队还在路上，就传来捷报——已经活捉了敌人的首领！诗人没有正面写战斗的过程，只写了出发和捷报，中间的激战留给读者去想象，这种写法叫“侧面描写”。全诗节奏很快，读起来让人热血沸腾。“吐谷浑”是古代西北的一个民族政权，这里代指敌军首领。

In English

This is the fifth of Wang Changling’s seven “Joining the Army” poems, and it tells of a quick, brilliant Tang victory on the frontier. The first line sets the scene: in the great desert, wind whips up so much sand and dust that the sun turns dim. The second line is the departure: red flags half-rolled, the troops charge out through the camp gate. The flags are half-furled because of the wind — and because the soldiers are moving fast. Then comes the twist. The vanguard has already fought through the night north of the Tao River, and before this second force even arrives, the news comes back: the enemy chief has been taken alive! The poet never shows the battle itself, only the setting out and the report of victory, leaving the fighting to our imagination. Describing something indirectly like this is called cèmiàn miáoxiě, “side description”. The pace is breathless, and the poem leaves you with the excitement of the moment rather than its blood. Tuyuhun was a kingdom in the northwest that fought the Tang; here the name stands for the enemy’s leader.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Wang Changling (c. 698–c. 756) was a Tang poet known as the master of the seven-character quatrain, especially frontier poems. He is believed to have travelled to the northwestern frontier as a young man, which gave his war poems their vivid detail.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

大漠风尘日色昏，

dà mò fēng chén rì sè hūn

Desert wind and dust have turned the sun dim;

红旗半卷出辕门。

hóng qí bàn juǎn chū yuán mén

red flags half-furled, the troops charge out of camp.

前军夜战洮河北，

qián jūn yè zhàn táo hé běi

The vanguard fought all night north of the Tao —

已报生擒吐谷浑。

yǐ bào shēng qín tǔ yù hún

and word is already here: the enemy chief is captured!
