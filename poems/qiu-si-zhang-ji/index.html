秋思（洛阳城里见秋风）

Qiū Sī

导读About this poem

Anyone who has rewritten a message home will recognise this one: a man far from home writes a letter, then, as the messenger is about to leave, opens it again to add one more thing.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

诗人住在洛阳，看到秋风起了，想念家乡，想写一封家信，心里的话却有千重万重。信写好了，他还是担心匆匆忙忙没写完；送信的人临出发前，他又把信封拆开再看一遍。全诗没有直接写“想家”，只写了一个小小的动作——“又开封”，却把那种说不完、放不下的思念写活了。这种用平常细节表达深情的写法，很容易引起共鸣。第一句的“秋风”也有来历：晋朝的张翰在洛阳看到秋风起，想起家乡的菜和鱼，就辞官回家了。张籍在这里暗用这个典故，表达思乡之情。

In English

The poet is living in Luoyang. When the autumn wind starts to blow he thinks of home and decides to write a letter, but there are a thousand layers of things he wants to say. The letter is finished, yet he worries that in his hurry he has left something out — so, just as the traveller who will carry it is setting off, he opens the envelope one more time. The poem never says “I miss home.” It shows one small, familiar action, and that is enough: we feel how much he has to say and how hard it is to let the letter go. Small everyday details like this are what make the poem so easy to relate to. The autumn wind in the first line also carries an old story. In the Jin dynasty a man named Zhang Han, serving as an official in Luoyang, saw the autumn wind rise, remembered the vegetables and fish of his home region, and resigned on the spot to go back. Zhang Ji quietly borrows that story to show how strong his own homesickness is.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Zhang Ji (c. 766–c. 830) came from the south and held modest posts in the capital; he was a friend of Han Yu and Bai Juyi. He is known for plain, sincere poems about ordinary people and everyday feelings.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

洛阳城里见秋风，

luò yáng chéng lǐ jiàn qiū fēng

In Luoyang the autumn wind has come;

欲作家书意万重。

yù zuò jiā shū yì wàn chóng

I sit to write home, with a thousand things to say.

复恐匆匆说不尽，

fù kǒng cōng cōng shuō bù jìn

Then I worry that in my rush I’ve left something out,

行人临发又开封。

xíng rén lín fā yòu kāi fēng

and as the courier turns to go, I open the letter again.
