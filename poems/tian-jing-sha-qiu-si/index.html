导读About this poem

Twenty-eight characters, and the first three lines have no verbs at all — just nine things set side by side: dry vines, an old tree, crows at dusk… A perfect first lesson in how Chinese poetry paints with images.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首小令被称为“秋思之祖”，是学习“意象”最好的入门作品。“意象”就是诗里带着感情的景物。前三句没有一个动词，只是把九个名词词组排在一起：枯藤、老树、昏鸦；小桥、流水、人家；古道、西风、瘦马。像电影镜头一个接一个，读者自己就能把画面拼起来。这些意象大多带着萧条、衰老的感觉：干枯的藤、老树、黄昏的乌鸦、荒凉的古道、寒冷的西风、瘦弱的马——都写出了秋天的凄凉。中间只有“小桥流水人家”是温暖的：别人家里炊烟袅袅，一家团圆，更衬托出旅人无家可归的孤单，这叫“反衬”。第四句“夕阳西下”点明时间，天快黑了，该回家了。最后一句才直接说出感情：心里极度悲伤的人，还漂泊在遥远的天边。前面所有的景物，都是为这一句做准备的。

In English

This short song is called the “ancestor of autumn poems”, and it is the best place to start learning about yìxiàng — images that carry feeling. The first three lines contain no verbs. They are just nine noun phrases placed side by side: dry vines, old tree, crows at dusk; little bridge, flowing water, a family’s home; ancient road, west wind, thin horse. Like shots in a film, one after another, they let the reader build the picture. Most of the images feel tired and bleak: withered vines, an old tree, crows at nightfall, a lonely old road, a cold west wind, a skinny horse — all autumn sadness. Only the middle line is warm: a little bridge, a stream and a house where a family is at home together. That warmth makes the traveller’s loneliness even sharper, a technique called fǎnchèn (setting off by contrast). The fourth line gives the time: the sun is setting, the hour when people go home. Only in the last line does the poet name the feeling — a heartbroken man is still at the far edge of the world. Everything before it was preparing for this moment.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Ma Zhiyuan (c. 1250–c. 1321) was one of the great playwrights and song writers of the Yuan dynasty. He spent years in minor posts and wandering, and this song is thought to come from that time. 天净沙 (“Sky-Clear Sand”) is the name of the tune, not the title.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

无特殊读音。（“断肠”的“肠”读 cháng。）

版本说明 About the text

采用统编语文教材七年级上册文本，全曲五句二十八字，此处完整收录。另一版本（元代《中原音韵》）作“小桥流水平沙”，通行本作“小桥流水人家”。题目中“天净沙”是曲牌，“秋思”是题目，“越调”是宫调。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

枯藤老树昏鸦，

kū téng lǎo shù hūn yā

Dry vines, an old tree, crows at nightfall;

小桥流水人家，

xiǎo qiáo liú shuǐ rén jiā

a small bridge, a stream, someone’s home;

古道西风瘦马。

gǔ dào xī fēng shòu mǎ

an old road, a west wind, a thin horse.

夕阳西下，

xī yáng xī xià

The sun goes down in the west —

断肠人在天涯。

duàn cháng rén zài tiān yá

and a heartbroken traveller is still at the end of the earth.
