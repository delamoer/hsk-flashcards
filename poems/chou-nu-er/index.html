导读About this poem

Young, he climbed towers to write sad poems about a sorrow he had never felt. Old, he knows sorrow through and through — and all he says is “What a nice cool autumn.” Eight short lines about growing up.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首词用“少年”和“而今”作对比。上阕写年轻时：根本不知道什么是真正的愁，却喜欢登上高楼，为了写新词，硬说自己很愁——“强说愁”就是没有愁也装出愁的样子。下阕写现在：经历了国家分裂、理想落空、被朝廷冷落，什么样的愁都尝尽了，真想说的时候反而说不出口，最后只淡淡说一句“天凉了，好一个秋天啊”。愁到深处，反而不说。两阕都用了重复：“爱上层楼，爱上层楼”“欲说还休，欲说还休”，前一个写少年的轻快，后一个写中年的欲言又止，一样的句式，完全不同的心情。这首词语言像口语，却说出了很多人都有的人生体会，所以“为赋新词强说愁”“欲说还休”到今天还常被引用。

In English

The whole lyric is built on one contrast: “when I was young” versus “now”. In the first half the young poet has no idea what real sorrow is, yet he loves climbing tall towers — the classic place for a sad poem — and to write a new lyric he forces himself to sound sorrowful. 强说愁 means exactly that: putting on a sadness you don’t have. In the second half he has tasted every kind of sorrow: a divided country, a dream of winning back the north that never came true, years pushed aside by the court. Now, when he really has something to say, he can’t bring himself to say it, and all that comes out is “The weather’s turned cool — such a fine autumn.” The deepest sorrow hides behind small talk. Each half repeats a line: “I loved to climb the tower, loved to climb the tower” sounds light and eager; “I want to speak, then stop; I want to speak, then stop” sounds hesitant and heavy. Same pattern, opposite feelings. The language is close to everyday speech, and the insight is universal, so phrases like 为赋新词强说愁 and 欲说还休 are still quoted today.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Xin Qiji (1140–1207) spent about ten years in forced retirement near Shangrao, Jiangxi, after 1181. Mount Bo was nearby, and he often passed through it; he is said to have written this on the wall of a house along that road.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

少年 shào nián；强说愁 qiǎng（勉强，不读 qiáng）；为赋 wèi fù；层楼 céng lóu。“欲说还休”的“还”：本页按 huán 注音（不少读本作 huán），也有很多人读 hái，请按所用教材定。机器朗读“强”“还”已在“朗读用字”列替换同音字。
