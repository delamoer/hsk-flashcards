导读About this poem

Life has no roots — we blow about like dust on a road. So treat everyone as family, share a drink with the neighbours while you can, and don’t waste the years: they won’t wait for you. Its last two lines are still a favourite motto for students.

第一段Part 1
01
人生无根蒂，飘如陌上尘。

直译LiteralHuman life has no root or stem; it drifts like dust on the road.

意译MeaningOur lives have no roots; we drift like dust along a road.

02
分散逐风转，此已非常身。

直译LiteralScattered, it turns with the wind; this is already not the constant self.

意译MeaningScattered, swirling where the wind goes, we are no longer who we were.

03
落地为兄弟，何必骨肉亲！

直译LiteralOnce fallen to earth we become brothers — why must we be kin of bone and flesh?

意译MeaningBorn onto this earth, we are all brothers — why should only blood make us close?

04
得欢当作乐，斗酒聚比邻。

直译LiteralWhen joy comes we should make merry; with a dipper of wine, gather the neighbours.

意译MeaningWhen there is joy, enjoy it: bring out the wine and call the neighbours in.

第二段Part 2
05
盛年不重来，一日难再晨。

直译LiteralThe prime of life does not come twice; a day can hardly have a second morning.

意译MeaningYour best years never come round again; no day has two mornings.

06
及时当勉励，岁月不待人。

直译LiteralWhile there is time we should exert ourselves; the years do not wait for anyone.

意译MeaningMake the most of it while you can — the years will not wait for you.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗谈的是人生。开头用了一个比喻：人生没有根，就像路上的尘土，被风吹散，飘来飘去，早已不是原来的样子。人生无常、身不由己，这是很悲凉的感受。可是诗人没有停在悲伤里，而是从中得出积极的结论：既然大家都是漂泊的尘土，那么一生下来就是兄弟，何必一定要有血缘关系才亲近呢？遇到高兴的事就该及时行乐，备上一斗酒，请左邻右舍来一起喝。最后四句是全诗的重点：壮年不会再来，一天之中不会有第二个早晨；应该趁着好时光努力，岁月是不会等人的。“及时当勉励，岁月不待人”今天常被用来劝人珍惜时间、努力学习。不过在原诗里，“勉励”更接近“抓紧时间好好生活、尽情欢乐”，比今天的理解更宽。全诗从悲观的比喻开始，以积极的态度结束，语言朴素自然，这是陶诗的典型风格。

In English

This poem is about life itself. It opens with an image: human life has no roots; we are like dust on a road, scattered by the wind, blown here and there until we are no longer what we were. Life is uncertain and out of our control — a bleak thought. But the poet doesn’t stay sad. He draws a warm conclusion from it: if we are all drifting dust, then we are brothers from the moment we land on earth, so why should only blood relatives be close? When something good happens, enjoy it — bring out a jug of wine and call the neighbours over. The last four lines are the heart of the poem: your prime years won’t come again, and a day has only one morning; make the most of the time while you have it, because the years wait for no one. Today 及时当勉励，岁月不待人 is often quoted to urge students to study hard. In the original, though, 勉励 is closer to “make an effort to live fully” — including enjoying yourself — which is broader than the modern reading. Starting from a gloomy image and ending in a positive spirit, all in plain everyday words, the poem is typical of Tao Yuanming.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Tao Yuanming (365?–427) left official life to farm and became China’s great poet of rural life. His twelve “Miscellaneous Poems” are thought to have been written around his fiftieth year, as he reflected on ageing and the passing of time.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

人生无根蒂，飘如陌上尘。

rén shēng wú gēn dì, piāo rú mò shàng chén

Our lives have no roots; we drift like dust along a road.

分散逐风转，此已非常身。

fēn sàn zhú fēng zhuǎn, cǐ yǐ fēi cháng shēn

Scattered, swirling where the wind goes, we are no longer who we were.

落地为兄弟，何必骨肉亲！

luò dì wéi xiōng dì, hé bì gǔ ròu qīn

Born onto this earth, we are all brothers — why should only blood make us close?

得欢当作乐，斗酒聚比邻。

dé huān dāng zuò lè, dǒu jiǔ jù bǐ lín

When there is joy, enjoy it: bring out the wine and call the neighbours in.

盛年不重来，一日难再晨。

shèng nián bù chóng lái, yī rì nán zài chén

Your best years never come round again; no day has two mornings.

及时当勉励，岁月不待人。

jí shí dāng miǎn lì, suì yuè bù dài rén

Make the most of it while you can — the years will not wait for you.
