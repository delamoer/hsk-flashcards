春江花月夜

Chūn Jiāng Huā Yuè Yè
A Spring River on a Flowery Moonlit Night
张若虚Zhāng Ruòxū · Zhang Ruoxu唐 Tang

导读About this poem

One night, from moonrise to moonset over a spring river: the poet asks who first saw this moon, and the moon looks down on lovers who are far apart. A long, flowing poem that later readers called “the poem above all poems”.

第一段Part 1
01
春江潮水连海平，海上明月共潮生。

直译LiteralThe spring river’s tide joins the sea, level; on the sea the bright moon rises with the tide.

意译MeaningThe spring tide swells until river and sea lie level, and out of the sea the moon rises with the tide.

02
滟滟随波千万里，何处春江无月明！

直译LiteralShimmering, following the waves for thousands of li — where on the spring river is there no moonlight?

意译MeaningIts light rides the ripples for a thousand miles — is there anywhere on the river the moon does not shine?

第二段Part 2
03
江流宛转绕芳甸，月照花林皆似霰；

直译LiteralThe river winds around the fragrant meadows; the moon shines on flowering woods, all like sleet.

意译MeaningThe river curls around the meadows in bloom; in the moonlight every blossom looks like sleet.

04
空里流霜不觉飞，汀上白沙看不见。

直译LiteralFlowing frost in the air flies unnoticed; the white sand on the shore cannot be seen.

意译MeaningMoonlight drifts through the air like frost you cannot feel, and the white sand on the shore has vanished.

第三段Part 3
05
江天一色无纤尘，皎皎空中孤月轮。

直译LiteralRiver and sky one colour, not a speck of dust; bright, bright in the sky, a lone moon-wheel.

意译MeaningRiver and sky are one colour, without a speck of dust; high in the clear air hangs a single shining moon.

06
江畔何人初见月？江月何年初照人？

直译LiteralWho on the riverbank first saw the moon? In what year did the river moon first shine on a person?

意译MeaningWho was the first on this shore to see the moon? In what year did the moon first shine on someone here?

第四段Part 4
07
人生代代无穷已，江月年年望相似。

直译LiteralHuman lives, generation after generation, never end; the river moon, year after year, looks alike.

意译MeaningPeople come and go, generation after generation, without end; the moon looks much the same, year after year.

08
不知江月待何人，但见长江送流水。

直译LiteralI don’t know whom the river moon waits for; I only see the Long River sending off its flowing water.

意译MeaningWhom is the moon waiting for? I cannot tell — I only see the Yangtze carrying its water away.

第五段Part 5
09
白云一片去悠悠，青枫浦上不胜愁。

直译LiteralA white cloud drifts slowly away; on Green Maple Shore, sorrow too great to bear.

意译MeaningA lone white cloud drifts slowly off; at Green Maple Shore, the sadness is more than one can bear.

10
谁家今夜扁舟子？何处相思明月楼？

直译LiteralWhose traveller in a small boat tonight? Where is the moonlit tower of longing?

意译MeaningWhose man is drifting in a small boat tonight? In which moonlit tower is someone missing him?

第六段Part 6
11
可怜楼上月徘徊，应照离人妆镜台。

直译LiteralPitiful — above the tower the moon lingers, surely shining on the parted one’s mirror stand.

意译MeaningPoor thing — the moon lingers over her tower, shining, surely, on the mirror where she does her hair.

12
玉户帘中卷不去，捣衣砧上拂还来。

直译LiteralIn the curtain of the jade door, it can’t be rolled away; on the washing-stone, brushed off, it comes back.

意译MeaningRoll up the blind and the moonlight stays; brush it from the washing-stone and back it comes.

第七段Part 7
13
此时相望不相闻，愿逐月华流照君。

直译LiteralAt this time we gaze at the same moon but cannot hear each other; I wish to follow the moonlight and flow to shine on you.

意译MeaningWe look at the same moon and cannot hear each other; I wish I could flow with its light and shine on you.

14
鸿雁长飞光不度，鱼龙潜跃水成文。

直译LiteralWild geese fly far but the light does not cross; fish and dragons dive and leap, the water makes ripples.

意译MeaningThe wild geese fly on and on, but never past the moonlight; fish leap from the deep and leave only ripples.

第八段Part 8
15
昨夜闲潭梦落花，可怜春半不还家。

直译LiteralLast night I dreamed of falling flowers by a quiet pool; pitiful — spring half gone and not returned home.

意译MeaningLast night he dreamed of blossoms falling on a quiet pool — spring is half over, and still he has not gone home.

16
江水流春去欲尽，江潭落月复西斜。

直译LiteralThe river water carries spring away, almost gone; over the river pool the setting moon slants west again.

意译MeaningThe river is carrying spring away, almost all of it; over the water the sinking moon leans west again.

第九段Part 9
17
斜月沉沉藏海雾，碣石潇湘无限路。

直译LiteralThe slanting moon sinks deep, hidden in sea mist; from Jieshi to Xiaoxiang, endless roads.

意译MeaningThe low moon sinks and hides in the sea mist; from the far north to the far south, the roads go on forever.

18
不知乘月几人归，落月摇情满江树。

直译LiteralI don’t know how many ride the moonlight home; the setting moon stirs feeling in the trees all over the river.

意译MeaningWho knows how many travel home by moonlight? The setting moon stirs longing through every tree along the river.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

《春江花月夜》原是乐府旧题。全诗三十六句，从月亮升起写到月亮落下，像一幅慢慢展开的长卷。开头写春潮连海，明月随潮升起，月光照在花林上像细雪，天地一片洁白。然后诗人发问：江边什么人最早看见月亮？江上的月亮哪一年最早照到人？人一代一代没有穷尽，江月年年都差不多——个人很短暂，人类和自然却在不停延续，这里有哲理，却不悲观。后半首转写离别：江上漂泊的游子，楼上思念的妇人，月光照着她的镜台和捣衣石，赶也赶不走；她想随月光流到远方照着心上人，可是音信传不过去。最后春天快过完了，月亮西斜，沉入海雾，“不知乘月几人归”，只有落月的余光摇动着满江的树。全诗每四句换一次韵，景、情、理融为一体。闻一多称它是“诗中的诗，顶峰上的顶峰”。

In English

“A Spring River on a Flowery Moonlit Night” is an old ballad title, and Zhang Ruoxu’s 36 lines follow a single night from moonrise to moonset, like a long scroll slowly unrolling. It opens with the spring tide rising until the river seems level with the sea, and the moon rising with it, its light rippling over a thousand li of water. Moonlight on the flowering woods looks like fine sleet. Then the poet asks the famous questions: who on this shore first saw the moon? In what year did the moon first shine on a person? Generations of people come and go without end, while the moon looks much the same each year. Each life is short, but humanity and nature go on — a thought that is calm and wondering rather than gloomy. The second half turns to separation. Somewhere a traveller drifts in a small boat; somewhere a woman in a moonlit tower misses him. The moonlight lingers on her mirror stand, her curtains and her washing-stone — she cannot roll it up or brush it away. She wishes she could flow with the moonlight to shine on him, but even the wild geese cannot fly beyond the light, and the fish can only leap in the water: no message gets through. At the end, spring is half gone, the moon sinks westward into the sea mist, and “who knows how many ride the moonlight home?” Only the last light stirs the trees along the river. The rhyme changes every four lines. The scholar Wen Yiduo called it “the poem of poems, the peak above peaks”.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Zhang Ruoxu (c. 660–c. 720) came from Yangzhou on the lower Yangtze and held only minor posts. Just two of his poems survive, and this one was largely overlooked until Ming and Qing critics rediscovered it; it is now counted among the greatest Tang poems.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

滟滟 yàn；芳甸 diàn；似霰 xiàn；汀 tīng；不胜 bù shèng（古读 shēng）；扁舟 piān zhōu（不读 biǎn）；应照 yīng；拂还来 huán、不还家 huán；碣石 jié；乘月 chéng；几人 jǐ；鸿雁长飞 cháng；水成文 的“文”同“纹”，读 wén。西斜、斜月 的“斜”古音押韵读 xiá，今按普通话读 xié，网站采用 xié。机器朗读“扁”“还”“应”容易读错，已在“朗读用字”列替换同音字。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行文本（《乐府诗集》卷四十七所录，各种唐诗选本同）。全诗三十六句，每四句一韵，网站每行排两句（一联），按换韵分为九段。重要异文：“江月年年望相似”一作“江月年年只相似”；“何处春江无月明”无重要异文。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

春江潮水连海平，海上明月共潮生。

chūn jiāng cháo shuǐ lián hǎi píng, hǎi shàng míng yuè gòng cháo shēng

The spring tide swells until river and sea lie level, and out of the sea the moon rises with the tide.

滟滟随波千万里，何处春江无月明！

yàn yàn suí bō qiān wàn lǐ, hé chù chūn jiāng wú yuè míng

Its light rides the ripples for a thousand miles — is there anywhere on the river the moon does not shine?

江流宛转绕芳甸，月照花林皆似霰；

jiāng liú wǎn zhuǎn rào fāng diàn, yuè zhào huā lín jiē sì xiàn

The river curls around the meadows in bloom; in the moonlight every blossom looks like sleet.

空里流霜不觉飞，汀上白沙看不见。

kōng lǐ liú shuāng bù jué fēi, tīng shàng bái shā kàn bù jiàn

Moonlight drifts through the air like frost you cannot feel, and the white sand on the shore has vanished.

江天一色无纤尘，皎皎空中孤月轮。

jiāng tiān yī sè wú xiān chén, jiǎo jiǎo kōng zhōng gū yuè lún

River and sky are one colour, without a speck of dust; high in the clear air hangs a single shining moon.

江畔何人初见月？江月何年初照人？

jiāng pàn hé rén chū jiàn yuè, jiāng yuè hé nián chū zhào rén

Who was the first on this shore to see the moon? In what year did the moon first shine on someone here?

人生代代无穷已，江月年年望相似。

rén shēng dài dài wú qióng yǐ, jiāng yuè nián nián wàng xiāng sì

People come and go, generation after generation, without end; the moon looks much the same, year after year.

不知江月待何人，但见长江送流水。

bù zhī jiāng yuè dài hé rén, dàn jiàn cháng jiāng sòng liú shuǐ

Whom is the moon waiting for? I cannot tell — I only see the Yangtze carrying its water away.

白云一片去悠悠，青枫浦上不胜愁。

bái yún yī piàn qù yōu yōu, qīng fēng pǔ shàng bù shèng chóu

A lone white cloud drifts slowly off; at Green Maple Shore, the sadness is more than one can bear.

谁家今夜扁舟子？何处相思明月楼？

shuí jiā jīn yè piān zhōu zǐ, hé chù xiāng sī míng yuè lóu

Whose man is drifting in a small boat tonight? In which moonlit tower is someone missing him?

可怜楼上月徘徊，应照离人妆镜台。

kě lián lóu shàng yuè pái huái, yīng zhào lí rén zhuāng jìng tái

Poor thing — the moon lingers over her tower, shining, surely, on the mirror where she does her hair.

玉户帘中卷不去，捣衣砧上拂还来。

yù hù lián zhōng juǎn bù qù, dǎo yī zhēn shàng fú huán lái

Roll up the blind and the moonlight stays; brush it from the washing-stone and back it comes.

此时相望不相闻，愿逐月华流照君。

cǐ shí xiāng wàng bù xiāng wén, yuàn zhú yuè huá liú zhào jūn

We look at the same moon and cannot hear each other; I wish I could flow with its light and shine on you.

鸿雁长飞光不度，鱼龙潜跃水成文。

hóng yàn cháng fēi guāng bù dù, yú lóng qián yuè shuǐ chéng wén

The wild geese fly on and on, but never past the moonlight; fish leap from the deep and leave only ripples.

昨夜闲潭梦落花，可怜春半不还家。

zuó yè xián tán mèng luò huā, kě lián chūn bàn bù huán jiā

Last night he dreamed of blossoms falling on a quiet pool — spring is half over, and still he has not gone home.

江水流春去欲尽，江潭落月复西斜。

jiāng shuǐ liú chūn qù yù jìn, jiāng tán luò yuè fù xī xié

The river is carrying spring away, almost all of it; over the water the sinking moon leans west again.

斜月沉沉藏海雾，碣石潇湘无限路。

xié yuè chén chén cáng hǎi wù, jié shí xiāo xiāng wú xiàn lù

The low moon sinks and hides in the sea mist; from the far north to the far south, the roads go on forever.

不知乘月几人归，落月摇情满江树。

bù zhī chéng yuè jǐ rén guī, luò yuè yáo qíng mǎn jiāng shù

Who knows how many travel home by moonlight? The setting moon stirs longing through every tree along the river.
