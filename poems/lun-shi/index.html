导读About this poem

Even Li Bai and Du Fu start to sound old after a thousand years, says this poet — every age produces its own great talents. A short, confident poem about not living in the shadow of the past.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

李白和杜甫是唐代最伟大的诗人，他们的诗千万人传诵。可是赵翼说：到了今天，已经让人觉得不新鲜了。这话听起来很大胆，他的意思不是李杜不好，而是诗歌要跟着时代变化，不能永远模仿古人。后两句是全诗的中心：国家每一代都有有才能的人出现，各自引领潮流几百年。“风骚”本来指《诗经》里的“国风”和屈原的《离骚》，后来代指诗歌和文学，“领风骚”就是在文坛上领先。这首诗是议论诗（直接讲道理的诗），语言很浅，道理清楚，表达了一种向前看、敢于创新的精神。“江山代有才人出，各领风骚数百年”今天常被用来说新一代人超过前人，不只用在文学上。

In English

Li Bai and Du Fu are the two greatest poets of the Tang dynasty; ten thousand mouths recite their poems. Yet Zhao Yi says that by now they no longer feel fresh. That sounds bold, but he is not saying they are bad poets. His point is that poetry has to change with the times instead of copying the ancients forever. The second couplet is the heart of the poem: every generation, this land produces new talents, and each leads the way for a few hundred years. 风骚 originally meant two classics — the “Airs” (风) of the Book of Songs and Qu Yuan’s “Encountering Sorrow” (离骚) — and later came to stand for poetry and literature in general, so 领风骚 means to lead the literary world. This is a poem that argues a point directly rather than painting a scene, and the language is plain and clear. It expresses a forward-looking, confident spirit: don’t worship the past, make something new. Today 江山代有才人出，各领风骚数百年 is quoted far beyond literature — whenever people talk about a new generation rising to take the place of the old.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Zhao Yi (1727–1814) was a Qing scholar, historian and poet. He wrote a set of five poems “On Poetry”, arguing that writers should create rather than imitate; this is the second.

李杜诗篇万口传，

lǐ dù shī piān wàn kǒu chuán

The poems of Li Bai and Du Fu are on everyone’s lips,

zhì jīn yǐ jué bù xīn xiān

but by now they no longer feel new.

江山代有才人出，

jiāng shān dài yǒu cái rén chū

Every age, this land brings forth new talent,

各领风骚数百年。

gè lǐng fēng sāo shù bǎi nián

and each leads the way for a few hundred years.
