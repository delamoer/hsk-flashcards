导读About this poem

Only 36 characters: a lonely man climbs a tower on an autumn night, sees a hook-shaped moon over a locked courtyard, and finds his sorrow is like a tangled thread — you can’t cut it, and you can’t sort it out.

这首诗被谱成了歌Sung as a song

From Teresa Teng's 1983 album 淡淡幽情 (Light Exquisite Feelings), which set classical ci poems to new melodies. The lyric is 相见欢 by Li Yu, the last ruler of Southern Tang, written after he lost his kingdom. The MV comes from the album's TV special.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首词据说也是李煜亡国以后写的。上阕写景：一个人默默无言，独自登上西楼，天上是一弯像钩子一样的残月，楼下深深的院子里种着梧桐，好像连整个秋天都被锁在里面。“锁”字用得很妙：锁住的是秋天，也是他自己——一个被关起来的人。下阕写情：离愁像一团乱丝，“剪不断，理还乱”，用剪刀剪不断，想理清楚又越理越乱。这是一个比喻，把说不清的愁写成摸得着的丝线。最后一句说，这种滋味特别，说不出来，只能留在心头。全词没有一个难字，却把孤独写得非常深，“剪不断，理还乱”今天还是常用的说法。

In English

This short lyric is also traditionally dated to Li Yu’s years as a prisoner after his kingdom fell. The first half is all scene. Without a word, he climbs the west tower alone. The moon is a thin hook. Below, in a deep courtyard, wutong trees stand in the clear autumn air — and the whole autumn seems “locked” inside. That verb 锁 (“to lock”) does a lot of work: what is locked up is the season, but also the man himself. The second half turns to feeling. His sorrow is like a tangle of silk: “cut it, and it won’t break; try to sort it out, and it only gets more tangled.” This comparison makes a feeling you can’t describe into something you can almost touch. The last line admits he cannot really name it — it is a taste all of its own, sitting in his heart. There are no hard words here, which is part of the poem’s power. Chinese speakers still say 剪不断，理还乱 about any messy, unresolved feeling or relationship.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Li Yu (937–978) was the last ruler of the Southern Tang kingdom in the Five Dynasties period, grouped with the Song on this site. Most scholars date this lyric to his captivity in the Song capital after 975, though some think it may be earlier. The tune is also known as 乌夜啼 (“Crows Calling at Night”).

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

理还乱：还 hái（仍然、更加），不读 huán。别是一般：一 本调 yī（网站拼音不标变调，朗读时按变调读 yì）。

版本说明 About the text

采用中学语文通行版本。词牌《相见欢》又名《乌夜啼》《秋夜月》，所以本词也常题作《乌夜啼》。异文：“别是一般滋味在心头”一作“别是一番滋味在心头”。全词上下两阕，共六句，网站分为四行。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

无言独上西楼，月如钩。

wú yán dú shàng xī lóu, yuè rú gōu

Without a word I climb the west tower alone. The moon is a thin hook.

寂寞梧桐深院锁清秋。

jì mò wú tóng shēn yuàn suǒ qīng qiū

Lonely wutong trees in a deep courtyard, where the cold clear autumn is locked in.

剪不断，理还乱，是离愁。

jiǎn bù duàn, lǐ hái luàn, shì lí chóu

Cut it, and it will not break; sort it, and it tangles more — this is the grief of separation.

别是一般滋味在心头。

bié shì yī bān zī wèi zài xīn tóu

It leaves a taste like no other, lodged deep in my heart.
