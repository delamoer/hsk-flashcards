导读About this poem

Home late and tipsy, Su Shi finds his servant snoring and the door shut. So he leans on his stick, listens to the river — and dreams of drifting away in a small boat for the rest of his life.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首词写苏轼在黄州的一个夜晚。上阕写一件生活小事：夜里在东坡喝酒，醒了又醉，回到家好像已经三更了。看门的小童睡得正香，鼾声像打雷一样，怎么敲门都没人答应，他只好拄着拐杖，站在门外听江水的声音。被关在门外本来是件尴尬事，诗人却不着急，反而静静听起江声来，很有趣味。下阕写由江声引起的思考：常常遗憾这个身子不属于自己（“此身非我有”出自《庄子》），什么时候才能忘掉那些忙忙碌碌的追求呢？夜深了，风停了，江面的波纹平平的。不如乘一只小船从此离去，在江海上度过余生。他当然没有真的走，但这几句写出了一个被名利和官场束缚的人，对自由的深深向往。

In English

One night in Huangzhou. The first half is a small, real-life scene. Su Shi has been drinking at Dongpo, his farm plot — sober, then drunk again — and gets home around midnight. His boy servant is fast asleep, snoring like thunder; no amount of knocking gets an answer. So he leans on his stick and listens to the river. Being locked out could be annoying, but he doesn’t fuss; he just stands and listens, which is the charm of it. The second half follows where the sound of the river leads. He has long regretted that “this body is not my own” — a phrase from the Taoist book Zhuangzi: his life is tied up by duty, office and ambition. When can he forget all that busy striving? The night is late, the wind has dropped, the ripples on the water are smooth as silk. Why not slip away in a little boat and spend the rest of his days on rivers and seas? He never actually left, of course. But these lines voice the longing of anyone tied down by career and obligation for a life that is truly free. A story says that next day the rumour spread that Su Shi had sailed off, and the local governor rushed over — to find him still snoring at home.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Su Shi (1037–1101) wrote this in 1082 in Huangzhou, where he lived in exile at Lingao, a riverside lodging, and farmed a slope he called “East Slope” (Dongpo) — the source of his famous name.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

夜饮东坡醒复醉，

yè yǐn dōng pō xǐng fù zuì

Drinking at East Slope tonight, sobering up, then drunk again,

归来仿佛三更。

guī lái fǎng fú sān gēng

I get home, it must be near midnight.

家童鼻息已雷鸣。

jiā tóng bí xī yǐ léi míng

The boy is snoring like thunder.

敲门都不应，

qiāo mén dōu bù yìng

I knock, and no one answers,

倚杖听江声。

yǐ zhàng tīng jiāng shēng

so I lean on my stick and listen to the river.

长恨此身非我有，

cháng hèn cǐ shēn fēi wǒ yǒu

How I hate that this life is not my own —

何时忘却营营。

hé shí wàng què yíng yíng

when can I forget all this restless chasing?

夜阑风静縠纹平。

yè lán fēng jìng hú wén píng

Late night, no wind, the ripples smooth as silk.

小舟从此逝，

xiǎo zhōu cóng cǐ shì

I’ll take a little boat and drift away from here,

江海寄余生。

jiāng hǎi jì yú shēng

and give what’s left of my life to the rivers and the sea.
