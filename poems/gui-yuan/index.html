导读About this poem

A carefree young wife dresses up on a spring day — until one glance at the green willows makes her regret sending her husband away to seek glory.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

“闺怨”是古诗里一类常见的题材，写女子在家里思念远方丈夫的哀怨。可这首诗开头却说这位年轻的妻子“不知愁”：春天到了，她精心打扮，高高兴兴地登上翠楼看风景。转折发生在第三句：她忽然看见路边的杨柳又绿了。柳树代表春天，也代表离别（古人送别时折柳相赠），这一眼让她想到：又一年过去了，丈夫还在远方。最后一句写出她的心事：真后悔当初让他去求取功名！诗人没有写她哭，也没有写她说什么，只写了一个“忽见”、一个“悔”字，情绪就从无忧无虑变成了深深的思念和后悔。这种先扬后抑、在一个瞬间里写出心情突变的写法，非常巧妙。诗的背后也有对唐代人追求边功、夫妻长久分离的感叹。

In English

Poems called guī yuàn, “boudoir laments”, were a standard type in Chinese poetry: a woman at home pining for a husband far away. But this one starts by telling us the young wife “knows no sorrow”. It is spring; she does her make-up carefully and climbs her green-painted tower in high spirits to enjoy the view. The turn comes in line three: she suddenly sees the willows along the road have turned green again. Willows mean spring — and partings, since people broke off a willow twig when seeing someone off. That one glance reminds her that another year has passed and her husband is still far away. The last line gives us her thought: how she regrets telling him to go off and win a noble title! The poet doesn’t show her crying or speaking; with just “suddenly saw” and “regret” her mood flips from carefree to longing and remorse. Building her up as happy and then letting one small sight bring her down is what makes the poem so neat. Behind it is a quiet comment on the Tang fashion for seeking fame on the frontier, which kept so many couples apart.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Wang Changling (c. 698–757), a leading High Tang poet, wrote many quatrains in a woman’s voice as well as frontier poems. Seeking a title (封侯) through service in the army was a common ambition of young men in his time.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

少妇 shào fù（年轻的妇女，不读 shǎo）。“悔教”的“教”表示“让、使”，按《现代汉语词典》读 jiào；也有注本读 jiāo。机器朗读可能读成 jiāo，已在“朗读用字”换成同音字“叫”。凝妆 níng zhuāng。觅 mì。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

闺中少妇不知愁，

guī zhōng shào fù bù zhī chóu

The young wife in her chamber has never known sorrow;

春日凝妆上翠楼。

chūn rì níng zhuāng shàng cuì lóu

one spring day, dressed in her finest, she climbs the jade-green tower.

忽见陌头杨柳色，

hū jiàn mò tóu yáng liǔ sè

Then she sees the willows greening along the road,

悔教夫婿觅封侯。

huǐ jiào fū xù mì fēng hóu

and wishes she had never sent her husband off to chase a title.
