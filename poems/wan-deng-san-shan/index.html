晚登三山还望京邑

Wǎn Dēng Sān Shān Huán Wàng Jīngyì
Climbing Three Hills at Dusk and Looking Back at the Capital
谢朓Xiè Tiǎo · Xie Tiao魏晋南北朝 Wei, Jin & Southern and Northern Dynasties

导读About this poem

Leaving the capital for a post in the provinces, the poet climbs a hill and looks back at the city in the evening light — and writes one of the loveliest river scenes in Chinese poetry: “the clear river lies still as white silk.”

直译LiteralFrom the bank of the Ba one gazed at Chang’an; from Heyang one looked at the capital.

意译MeaningLike the poet on the Ba looking back at Chang’an, like the one at Heyang gazing toward Luoyang,

02
白日丽飞甍，参差皆可见。

直译LiteralThe sun brightens the flying roof ridges; high and low, all can be seen.

意译MeaningI see the sun gleam on soaring rooftops, every roof, high and low, in view.

03
余霞散成绮，澄江静如练。

直译LiteralThe remaining clouds of sunset spread into patterned silk; the clear river is still as white silk.

意译MeaningThe last of the sunset spreads like brocade; the clear river lies still as white silk.

04
喧鸟覆春洲，杂英满芳甸。

直译LiteralNoisy birds cover the spring islets; mixed flowers fill the fragrant meadows.

意译MeaningChattering birds crowd the spring islands; flowers of every kind fill the sweet meadows.

05
去矣方滞淫，怀哉罢欢宴。

直译LiteralI am leaving, and will stay away long; how I miss it — the happy feasts are over.

意译MeaningI am going, and will be gone a long time; the happy banquets are over, and I already miss them.

06
佳期怅何许，泪下如流霰。

直译LiteralThe day of return — sadly, when will it be? Tears fall like flying sleet.

意译MeaningWhen will the day of return come? My tears fall like sleet.

07
有情知望乡，谁能鬒不变？

直译LiteralAnyone with feelings knows longing for home; whose black hair can stay unchanged?

意译MeaningAnyone with a heart longs for home — whose black hair could stay black?

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

谢朓要离开京城建康（今南京）去外地做官，傍晚登上城外的三山回头看京城。开头借用两个古人的典故（王粲在灞水边回望长安、潘岳在河阳遥看洛阳），说自己也像他们一样恋恋不舍。接着是全诗最美的景色：夕阳照着高高翘起的屋脊，城里的房子高高低低都看得清；晚霞散开像彩色的锦缎，清澈的长江平静得像一匹白绸。“余霞散成绮，澄江静如练”用两个比喻画出一幅色彩分明的画，李白都十分佩服，写过“解道澄江静如练，令人长忆谢玄晖”。后半首转向心情：鸟声喧闹、野花满地，春天越美，离开就越难过；想到归期不知何时，眼泪像雪珠一样落下；最后说，有感情的人都会想念家乡，谁的黑发能不因此变白？前景后情，景越美，情越深。

In English

Xie Tiao was leaving the capital, Jiankang (today’s Nanjing), to take up a post elsewhere. At dusk he climbed the Three Hills outside the city and looked back. He begins with two famous earlier poets who once looked back at the capitals they were leaving — a learned way of saying “I feel exactly as they did.” Then comes the scene that made the poem famous. The setting sun lights up the soaring roof ridges, and the whole city, high and low, is clear to see. “The last clouds of sunset spread into patterned silk; the clear river lies still as a length of white satin.” Two comparisons, two colours — bright brocade above, pale silk below — and the picture is complete. Li Bai admired these lines so much that he wrote a poem saying they made him remember Xie Tiao forever. In the second half the feeling takes over. Birds chatter over the spring islets and wildflowers fill the meadows, and the lovelier the spring, the harder it is to go. He doesn’t know when he will return, and his tears fall like sleet. The last couplet widens it to everyone: anyone with feelings longs for home — whose black hair would not turn grey? The beauty of the scene is what makes the sorrow so deep.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Xie Tiao (464–499) of the Southern Qi dynasty was the leading landscape poet of his generation, and his smooth, musical lines helped prepare the way for Tang regulated verse. This poem is usually dated to 495, when he left the capital to become governor of Xuancheng.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

谢朓 Xiè Tiǎo（朓不读 tiào/zhào）；灞涘 bà sì；京县 xiàn；飞甍 méng；参差 cēn cī；绮 qǐ；澄 chéng；散 sàn；滞淫 zhì yín；霰 xiàn；鬒 zhěn；题目“还望”的“还”读 huán（回头）。机器朗读“涘”“甍”“鬒”容易读错，已在“朗读用字”列替换同音字，音频需人工听验。

版本说明 About the text

采用《文选》本，全诗七联十四句。题中“三山”在今南京西南的长江边，“京邑”指南齐都城建康（今南京）。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

灞涘望长安，河阳视京县。

bà sì wàng cháng ān, hé yáng shì jīng xiàn

Like the poet on the Ba looking back at Chang’an, like the one at Heyang gazing toward Luoyang,

白日丽飞甍，参差皆可见。

bái rì lì fēi méng, cēn cī jiē kě jiàn

I see the sun gleam on soaring rooftops, every roof, high and low, in view.

余霞散成绮，澄江静如练。

yú xiá sàn chéng qǐ, chéng jiāng jìng rú liàn

The last of the sunset spreads like brocade; the clear river lies still as white silk.

喧鸟覆春洲，杂英满芳甸。

xuān niǎo fù chūn zhōu, zá yīng mǎn fāng diàn

Chattering birds crowd the spring islands; flowers of every kind fill the sweet meadows.

去矣方滞淫，怀哉罢欢宴。

qù yǐ fāng zhì yín, huái zāi bà huān yàn

I am going, and will be gone a long time; the happy banquets are over, and I already miss them.

佳期怅何许，泪下如流霰。

jiā qī chàng hé xǔ, lèi xià rú liú xiàn

When will the day of return come? My tears fall like sleet.

有情知望乡，谁能鬒不变？

yǒu qíng zhī wàng xiāng, shuí néng zhěn bù biàn

Anyone with a heart longs for home — whose black hair could stay black?
