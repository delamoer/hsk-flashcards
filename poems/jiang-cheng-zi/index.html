导读About this poem

Ten years after his young wife died, Su Shi dreams that he is home and sees her at the window doing her hair. Neither can speak; they only cry. One of the most moving poems of grief in Chinese.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

苏轼的第一位妻子王弗十六岁嫁给他，二十七岁就去世了，葬在四川眉山老家。十年后，苏轼在山东密州做官，一天夜里梦见了她，醒来写下这首词，这是中国最早、也最有名的悼亡词（怀念去世妻子的词）之一。开头“十年生死两茫茫”，一个活着、一个死了，彼此什么都不知道；“不思量，自难忘”，不刻意去想，却怎么也忘不了。她的坟在千里之外，想说说心里的凄凉都没有地方说。就算真的见面，她恐怕也认不出满脸风尘、头发像霜一样白的我了。下阕写梦：忽然回到家乡，她正坐在小窗前梳妆，和从前一样。两人互相看着，说不出话，只有泪流不止。最后想到：那个月光下长着小松树的山冈，就是年年让我伤心的地方。全词语言朴素，没有华丽的词，感情却真挚得让人心痛。

In English

Su Shi’s first wife, Wang Fu, married him at sixteen and died at twenty-six (twenty-seven by the Chinese count), and was buried in his home town in Sichuan. Ten years later, serving as an official in Mizhou in Shandong, he dreamed of her one night and wrote this when he woke. It is one of the earliest and best-loved poems in Chinese written in mourning for a wife. The first line sets the distance: ten years, one living and one dead, each knowing nothing of the other. “I don’t try to think of you, yet I can’t forget.” Her grave is a thousand miles away, so there is nowhere to tell her how desolate he feels — and even if they met, she might not know him now, his face covered in dust, his hair white as frost. Then the dream: he is suddenly home, and there she is at the little window, doing her hair as she used to. They look at each other without a word, and only tears come. The ending returns to the waking world: every year his heart will break at that hillside of short pines under the moon, where she lies. There are no fancy words here at all. The plainness is what makes it hurt.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Su Shi (1037–1101) married Wang Fu in 1054; she died in 1065 and was buried at Meishan, Sichuan. He wrote this in 1075 (the yimao year), while serving as prefect of Mizhou in Shandong, exactly ten years after her death.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

不思量：量 读 liáng（词中押 ang 韵；现代口语“思量”读 sī liang）。泪千行：行 háng，不读 xíng。忽还乡：还 huán。纵使相逢应不识：应 yīng。料得 liào dé。肠断 cháng duàn。鬓 bìn。轩窗 xuān。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

十年生死两茫茫，

shí nián shēng sǐ liǎng máng máng

Ten years — you dead, I living — and each of us lost to the other.

不思量，自难忘。

bù sī liáng, zì nán wàng

I do not try to think of you, and still I cannot forget.

千里孤坟，无处话凄凉。

qiān lǐ gū fén, wú chù huà qī liáng

Your lonely grave is a thousand miles away; there is no one I can tell my sorrow to.

纵使相逢应不识，

zòng shǐ xiāng féng yīng bù shí

Even if we met, you would not know me now —

尘满面，鬓如霜。

chén mǎn miàn, bìn rú shuāng

my face covered in dust, my hair white as frost.

夜来幽梦忽还乡，

yè lái yōu mèng hū huán xiāng

Last night, in a faint dream, I was suddenly home again;

小轩窗，正梳妆。

xiǎo xuān chuāng, zhèng shū zhuāng

you sat at the little window, doing your hair.

相顾无言，惟有泪千行。

xiāng gù wú yán, wéi yǒu lèi qiān háng

We looked at each other and said nothing, only cried and cried.

料得年年肠断处，

liào dé nián nián cháng duàn chù

I know where my heart will break, year after year:

明月夜，短松冈。

míng yuè yè, duǎn sōng gāng

a moonlit night, the hill of little pines.
