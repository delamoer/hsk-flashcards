导读About this poem

A divorced woman meets her ex-husband on a hillside and asks, politely, how his new wife is. His answer — “she isn’t as good as you” — says everything. A tiny drama in sixteen lines.

上山采蘼芜，下山逢故夫。

直译LiteralGoing up the hill I gathered herbs; coming down I met my former husband.

意译MeaningShe climbed the hill to gather herbs, and coming down she met her former husband.

02
长跪问故夫：“新人复何如？”

直译LiteralKneeling upright, she asked her former husband: “The new one — how is she?”

意译MeaningShe knelt and asked him: “And how is your new wife?”

03
“新人虽言好，未若故人姝。

直译Literal“The new one, though said to be good, is not as lovely as the old one.

意译Meaning“She’s good enough, they say — but not as fine as you.

04
颜色类相似，手爪不相如。”

直译LiteralIn looks you are much alike; in handiwork she is not your equal.”

意译MeaningYou look much alike, but her hands are not as skilled as yours.”

05
“新人从门入，故人从阁去。”

直译Literal“The new one came in by the gate; the old one left by the side door.”

意译Meaning“She came in through the front gate; I went out by the side door.”

06
“新人工织缣，故人工织素。

直译Literal“The new one is skilled at weaving yellow silk; the old one was skilled at weaving white silk.

意译Meaning“She weaves yellow silk; you wove white.

07
织缣日一匹，织素五丈余。

直译LiteralWeaving yellow silk, one bolt a day; weaving white silk, more than five zhang.

意译MeaningShe weaves one bolt a day; you wove five zhang and more.

08
将缣来比素，新人不如故。”

直译LiteralTaking the yellow silk to compare with the white — the new one is not as good as the old.”

意译MeaningPut the two side by side: the new wife is no match for the old.”

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首汉代古诗像一出小短剧。一个被丈夫休掉的女子上山采香草，下山时碰到了从前的丈夫。她恭恭敬敬地跪下问：“你的新妻子怎么样？”前夫回答：新人虽然也不错，但比不上你好。容貌差不多，手艺可差远了。女子听了，忍不住说了一句：“新人从大门风风光光地进来，我却从小门悄悄离开。”这是她心里的委屈。前夫接着比较：新人会织缣（黄色的绢），你会织素（白色的绢）；她一天只能织一匹缣，你一天能织五丈多素。拿缣和素一比，新人不如旧人。整首诗几乎都是对话，没有一句评论，读者却能感到女子的委屈和自尊，也能感到男子的后悔——可是后悔又有什么用呢？诗里男子比较两个妻子，看的主要是织布的本领，这也让我们看到古代妇女的处境：一个女子的价值常常被这样衡量，她自己却没有选择的权利。

In English

This Han poem plays out like a short drama. A woman whose husband divorced her goes up a hill to gather fragrant herbs, and on the way down she runs into him. She kneels politely and asks: “And how is your new wife?” He answers: she is fine, but not as good as you. You two look alike, but her hands are nowhere near as skilled. She can’t help a bitter remark: “The new wife came in through the front gate; I left through the side door.” Then he goes on comparing: the new wife weaves yellow silk, you wove white silk; she manages one bolt a day, you managed over five zhang (about 12 metres). Set the two side by side, and the new one doesn’t measure up to the old. The poem is almost all dialogue, with no comment from the poet, yet we feel the woman’s hurt and pride and the man’s regret — regret that comes too late to change anything. Notice, too, that he judges both wives mainly by their weaving. The poem quietly shows how women were valued in ancient China, and how little say they had in their own lives.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

An anonymous poem of the Han dynasty, preserved in the 6th-century anthology Yutai xinyong (玉台新咏) among the “Old Poems” (古诗). Like the Yuefu folk songs, it tells its story through dialogue.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

版本说明 About the text

采用《玉台新咏》卷一所收“古诗”的通行文本，全诗十六句，网站按一联一行排版。“新人从门入，故人从阁去”一般理解为女子插话，标点从之。“阁”原作“閤”（旁门）。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

上山采蘼芜，下山逢故夫。

shàng shān cǎi mí wú, xià shān féng gù fū

She climbed the hill to gather herbs, and coming down she met her former husband.

长跪问故夫：“新人复何如？”

cháng guì wèn gù fū, xīn rén fù hé rú

She knelt and asked him: “And how is your new wife?”

“新人虽言好，未若故人姝。

xīn rén suī yán hǎo, wèi ruò gù rén shū

“She’s good enough, they say — but not as fine as you.

颜色类相似，手爪不相如。”

yán sè lèi xiāng sì, shǒu zhǎo bù xiāng rú

You look much alike, but her hands are not as skilled as yours.”

“新人从门入，故人从阁去。”

xīn rén cóng mén rù, gù rén cóng gé qù

“She came in through the front gate; I went out by the side door.”

“新人工织缣，故人工织素。

xīn rén gōng zhī jiān, gù rén gōng zhī sù

“She weaves yellow silk; you wove white.

织缣日一匹，织素五丈余。

zhī jiān rì yī pǐ, zhī sù wǔ zhàng yú

She weaves one bolt a day; you wove five zhang and more.

将缣来比素，新人不如故。”

jiāng jiān lái bǐ sù, xīn rén bù rú gù

Put the two side by side: the new wife is no match for the old.”
