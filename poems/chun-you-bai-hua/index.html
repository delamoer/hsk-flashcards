导读About this poem

Flowers in spring, the moon in autumn, cool breezes in summer, snow in winter — every season is a good one if your mind isn’t cluttered with worries. A Zen verse so simple a beginner can read it, so deep people quote it all their lives.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这是一首佛教禅宗的“偈颂”（和尚用来表达修行体会的短诗）。前两句很简单：春天有百花，秋天有明月，夏天有凉风，冬天有白雪。一年四季，每个季节都有自己的美。后两句说出道理：如果心里没有那些没用的烦恼挂着，那么每一天都是人间最好的时节。所谓“闲事”，就是让人放不下的计较、担心和杂念。这首诗的意思是：美好的东西其实一直在身边，看不见它，是因为心太忙、太乱。只要心里干净、轻松，季节怎么变都是好的。语言浅得像儿歌，道理却很深，所以今天很多人还喜欢把它写成书法挂在家里，也常用“好时节”来说心态的重要。

In English

This is a Buddhist gatha (偈颂), a short verse in which a Chan (Zen) monk sums up an insight. The first two lines could hardly be simpler: spring has a hundred flowers, autumn has the moon, summer has cool breezes, winter has snow. Each season has its own beauty. The last two lines give the point: if no “idle matters” hang on your heart, then every day is the best season of the human world. 闲事 here means the worries, grudges and restless thoughts we cannot let go of. The idea is that good things are around us all the time; we fail to see them because our minds are too busy and cluttered. With a clear, light mind, whatever the season is, it is a good one. The language is as plain as a nursery rhyme, but the thought goes deep, which is why many people still hang it as calligraphy at home and quote it as a reminder that happiness depends on how we look at things.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Wumen Huikai (1183–1260) was a Chan Buddhist master of the Southern Song. This verse comes from his “Gateless Barrier” (Wumenguan, 1228), a famous collection of Zen koans with his comments, still widely read in China and Japan.

版本说明 About the text

出自《无门关》第十九则“平常是道”所附颂。原本无题，通常称《颂》或《颂平常心是道》。网站用通行文本。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

春有百花秋有月，

chūn yǒu bǎi huā qiū yǒu yuè

Spring has its flowers, autumn its moon,

夏有凉风冬有雪。

xià yǒu liáng fēng dōng yǒu xuě

summer its cool breeze, winter its snow.

若无闲事挂心头，

ruò wú xián shì guà xīn tóu

If no needless cares weigh on your heart,

便是人间好时节。

biàn shì rén jiān hǎo shí jié

every season on earth is a good one.
