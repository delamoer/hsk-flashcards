导读About this poem

Twenty characters, no difficult words — and the poem every Chinese person knows by heart. A traveller wakes at night, mistakes moonlight for frost, looks up at the moon and thinks of home.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗写一个离家在外的人，在秋天的夜里看到月光，想起了家乡。前两句写一个错觉：月光照在床前，白得像地上的霜。一个“疑”字写出半睡半醒时的恍惚，“霜”字又带出秋夜的寒冷和孤单。后两句只有两个动作——“举头”看月，“低头”思乡。一抬一低之间，心情从看月转到想家，全诗没有一个字说“难过”，思念却很深。语言浅白得像说话一样，所以它成了中国人最熟悉的一首诗，几乎每个孩子都会背。

In English

A man far from home wakes on an autumn night and sees moonlight on the floor. The first two lines are a moment of confusion: the light is so white he half-thinks it is frost. The word 疑 (“to wonder if”) catches that drowsy, uncertain moment, and “frost” quietly adds the cold and loneliness of the night. The last two lines are just two movements — he raises his head to the moon, then lowers it and thinks of home. The poem never says he is sad; the gesture says it for him. Because the language is as plain as speech, this became the best-known poem in China, the first one many children learn.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Li Bai (701–762), called the “Immortal of Poetry”, spent most of his life travelling. He is said to have written this at about 26, staying at an inn in Yangzhou, far from where he grew up in Sichuan.

版本说明 About the text

采用通行的教材版本（明代以后流行）。宋代版本作“床前看月光……举头望山月”，网站不用。“床”的含义有争议：一般理解为床，也有学者认为是井栏或坐具，解析里不展开。
