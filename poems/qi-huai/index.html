导读About this poem

A poet remembers the nights he played the flute under the flowers for a girl he could not have. “These stars are not last night’s stars — for whom do I stand in the wind and dew at midnight?” One of the most loved lines of the Qing dynasty.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

《绮怀》是黄景仁回忆年轻时一段恋情的组诗，共十六首，这是第十五首。开头写过去：多少次在花下坐着吹箫，可是那人住在红墙里面，像隔着天上的银河一样远，只能远远地望着。第三、四句最有名：今晚的星辰已经不是从前那一夜的星辰了，我又是为了谁，在风露里站到半夜？这里化用了唐代李商隐的诗句“昨夜星辰昨夜风”，物是人非的感觉很强。第五、六句用了两个比喻：情思就像抽尽了的残茧，“丝”和“思”同音，是双关（一个词同时有两层意思）；心痛就像剥开以后的芭蕉，一层一层，心已经伤透。最后说：想起她十五岁那年的十五月圆之夜，那一杯酒到今天还没有消散。全诗把思念写得缠绵深切，让人难忘。

In English

“Tender Memories” is a set of sixteen poems in which Huang Jingren looks back on a love from his youth; this is the fifteenth. It opens with the past: how many times he sat under the flowers playing his flute, while she lived behind red walls — as far away as the Milky Way, only to be gazed at from afar. The second couplet is the famous one: these stars are not the stars of that night long ago, so for whom am I standing in the wind and dew until midnight? It echoes a line by the Tang poet Li Shangyin, “last night’s stars, last night’s wind”, and gives a strong sense of the place being the same while the person is gone. The third couplet uses two comparisons. His tangled longing is like the last silk drawn from a spent cocoon — and since 丝 (“silk”) sounds just like 思 (“longing”), the line is a pun with two meanings at once. His heartache is like a banana plant peeled layer by layer until nothing is left. The last couplet remembers a full-moon night when she was fifteen: the wine he drank then has still not worn off. The longing in this poem is soft, lingering and very hard to forget.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Huang Jingren (1749–1783) was a gifted Qing poet who lived in poverty and died at 34. The “Tender Memories” series is traditionally believed to recall his youthful love for a young woman — said to be a cousin — whom he could not marry.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

几回花下坐吹箫，

jǐ huí huā xià zuò chuī xiāo

How often I sat beneath the flowers playing my flute,

银汉红墙入望遥。

yín hàn hóng qiáng rù wàng yáo

your red wall as far from me as the Milky Way.

似此星辰非昨夜，

sì cǐ xīng chén fēi zuó yè

These stars are not the stars of that night,

为谁风露立中宵。

wèi shuí fēng lù lì zhōng xiāo

so for whom do I stand in the wind and dew at midnight?

缠绵思尽抽残茧，

chán mián sī jìn chōu cán jiǎn

My longing is drawn out to the end, like the last silk from a cocoon;

宛转心伤剥后蕉。

wǎn zhuǎn xīn shāng bāo hòu jiāo

my heart, peeled layer by layer like a banana stem, has nothing left.

三五年时三五月，

sān wǔ nián shí sān wǔ yuè

That full-moon night in the year you were fifteen —

可怜杯酒不曾消。

kě lián bēi jiǔ bù céng xiāo

sadly, the wine I drank then has never worn off.
