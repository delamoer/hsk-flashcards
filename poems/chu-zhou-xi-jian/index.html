导读About this poem

Quiet grass by a stream, an oriole in the trees, a spring flood at dusk — and an empty ferry boat turning by itself on the water.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗写诗人在滁州（今安徽滁州）做官时，春天游西涧看到的景色。第一句说：我偏偏喜爱生长在涧边的幽草——不爱热闹的花，而爱安静的草，可以看出诗人的心性。第二句写树林深处黄鹂在婉转地叫。三四句是全诗的名句：傍晚春潮涨起来，又带着雨，水流得更急了；荒野的渡口没有人，一只小船在水面上自己横着漂。“舟自横”画面非常安静：船没有人用，就这样随水漂着，好像也很自在。有人从中读出诗人的闲适，也有人读出他有才能却不被重用、像无人使用的小船一样的无奈。全诗有声有色、有静有动，“野渡无人舟自横”成了中国山水画常画的题材。

In English

Wei Yingwu wrote this while serving as prefect of Chuzhou (in today’s Anhui), describing a spring visit to West Creek. Line one says he especially loves the quiet grass growing along the stream — not showy flowers but modest grass, which tells you something about his character. Line two adds an oriole singing deep in the trees. The last two lines are the famous ones: at dusk the spring flood rises, swollen by rain, and the water rushes faster; at the wild ferry crossing there is no one, and a little boat swings sideways on the current by itself. That final image is wonderfully still: the boat, unused, simply drifts as the water pleases, as if it too were at ease. Some readers see the poet’s calm contentment in it; others see the frustration of a capable man left unused, like the idle boat. With sound and colour, stillness and motion, the poem became a favourite subject for Chinese landscape painters, and 野渡无人舟自横 is often written on paintings.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Wei Yingwu (c. 737–c. 792) served as a palace guard in his youth and later became a respected official and poet, known for calm, clear nature poems. He wrote this around 782, when he was prefect of Chuzhou.

版本说明 About the text

采用通行本（《唐诗三百首》），与《全唐诗》一致。《全唐诗》注异文：“幽草”一作“芳草”，“涧边生”一作“涧边行”，“深树鸣”一作“深处鸣”。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

独怜幽草涧边生，

dú lián yōu cǎo jiàn biān shēng

Most of all I love the quiet grass along the stream,

上有黄鹂深树鸣。

shàng yǒu huáng lí shēn shù míng

and orioles singing deep in the trees above.

春潮带雨晚来急，

chūn cháo dài yǔ wǎn lái jí

At dusk the spring flood, swollen with rain, runs fast;

野渡无人舟自横。

yě dù wú rén zhōu zì héng

at the lonely crossing, an empty boat turns on its own.
