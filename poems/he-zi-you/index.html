导读About this poem

What is a human life like? A wild goose landing on snowy mud: it leaves a few claw prints, then flies off without caring where. A young Su Shi writes this to his brother — and gives Chinese the idiom 雪泥鸿爪.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这是苏轼回赠弟弟苏辙（字子由）的诗。五年前兄弟俩进京赶考，路过渑池，住在一座寺庙里，还在墙上题了诗；这次苏轼去凤翔做官，弟弟送别后写诗回忆渑池，苏轼就“和”了这首。前四句是有名的比喻：人这一生到处奔走，像什么呢？就像飞来的大雁踩在雪地的泥上，偶然留下几个爪印，飞走以后哪里还管东边西边。人生的足迹也是偶然、短暂的。成语“雪泥鸿爪”就出自这里。后四句回到渑池：当年招待他们的老和尚已经去世，只留下一座新的骨灰塔；墙也坏了，看不到当年题的诗。最后问弟弟：还记得那时路途的艰难吗？路很长，人很累，跛脚的驴还在叫。诗里有对人生无常的感叹，但语气平静，兄弟情很深。

In English

Su Shi wrote this in reply to a poem from his younger brother Su Zhe (courtesy name Ziyou). Five years earlier the brothers, on their way to the imperial exams, had passed through Mianchi, stayed at a temple and written poems on its wall. Now Su Shi was leaving for his first post, and his brother wrote a poem remembering that town; this is Su Shi’s answer. The first four lines are a famous comparison: what is a life of travelling like? A wild goose landing on snowy mud — it leaves a few claw prints by chance, then flies away without a thought for east or west. Our traces in the world are just as accidental and brief. The idiom 雪泥鸿爪 (“a goose’s claw prints in snow and mud”) comes from here. The second half returns to Mianchi: the old monk who hosted them has died and now rests in a new pagoda; the wall has crumbled, and their poems are gone. Finally he asks his brother: do you still remember how hard that road was — the long way, our weariness, the lame donkey braying? It is a reflection on impermanence, told calmly, full of a brother’s affection.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Su Shi (1037–1101) wrote this in 1061, aged 25, on his way to his first official post at Fengxiang. In 1056 he and his brother Su Zhe had passed through Mianchi on the way to the capital; Su Shi’s own note says their horse died on the road and they rode a donkey the rest of the way.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

和 hè（依照别人诗的题材或韵律作诗，不读 hé）；渑池 Miǎnchí；应似 yīng；指爪 zhǐ zhǎo；那复 nǎ fù（那，同“哪”）；还记否 hái；蹇驴 jiǎn lǘ。机器朗读“应、爪、那、蹇”容易读错，已在“朗读用字”列换成同音字，音频需人工听验。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行文本（《苏轼诗集》）。题目一作《和子由渑池怀旧》，原诗后有苏轼自注：“往岁，马死于二陵，骑驴至渑池。”

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

人生到处知何似，

rén shēng dào chù zhī hé sì

A life spent wandering from place to place — what is it like?

应似飞鸿踏雪泥。

yīng sì fēi hóng tà xuě ní

Like a wild goose that lands for a moment on snowy mud.

泥上偶然留指爪，

ní shàng ǒu rán liú zhǐ zhǎo

By chance it leaves a few claw prints behind,

鸿飞那复计东西。

hóng fēi nǎ fù jì dōng xī

then flies off, never caring whether east or west.

老僧已死成新塔，

lǎo sēng yǐ sǐ chéng xīn tǎ

The old monk has died; a new pagoda holds his ashes.

坏壁无由见旧题。

huài bì wú yóu jiàn jiù tí

The wall has crumbled; the poems we wrote there are gone.

往日崎岖还记否，

wǎng rì qí qū hái jì fǒu

Do you still remember how hard that road was —

路长人困蹇驴嘶。

lù cháng rén kùn jiǎn lǘ sī

the endless miles, our weariness, the lame donkey braying?
