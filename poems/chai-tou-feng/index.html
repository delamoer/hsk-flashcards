导读About this poem

Traditionally read as Lu You’s cry of regret on meeting his former wife, years after his mother forced them apart: “Wrong, wrong, wrong!” — and then, “Don’t, don’t, don’t.”

红酥手，黄縢酒，满城春色宫墙柳。

直译LiteralRosy, soft hands; yellow-sealed wine; spring colours all over the city, willows by the palace wall.

意译MeaningSoft rosy hands, a jar of yellow-sealed wine; spring filled the city, willows by the palace wall.

02
东风恶，欢情薄。

直译LiteralThe east wind is cruel; joy grows thin.

意译MeaningThen the east wind turned cruel, and our joy blew away.

03
一怀愁绪，几年离索。

直译LiteralA heart full of sorrow; several years apart and alone.

意译MeaningA heart full of grief, years of living apart.

04
错、错、错！

直译LiteralWrong, wrong, wrong!

意译MeaningWrong — all wrong — wrong!

下阕Second stanza
05
春如旧，人空瘦，泪痕红浥鲛绡透。

直译LiteralSpring is as before; the person is thin in vain; tear stains, red and wet, soak through the mermaid silk.

意译MeaningSpring is the same, but she has wasted away; rouge-stained tears soak through her silk handkerchief.

06
桃花落，闲池阁。

直译LiteralPeach blossoms fall; the pond pavilion is idle.

意译MeaningPeach blossoms fall on an empty pavilion by the pond.

07
山盟虽在，锦书难托。

直译LiteralThough the mountain vows remain, a brocade letter is hard to send.

意译MeaningOur vows still stand like mountains, but no letter can reach you now.

08
莫、莫、莫！

直译LiteralDon’t, don’t, don’t!

意译MeaningNo more — no more — let it be.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首词背后有一个著名的爱情故事。陆游年轻时娶了表妹唐琬，两人感情很好，但陆游的母亲不喜欢她，逼他们离婚。几年后，陆游在绍兴沈园春游，偶然遇到已经改嫁的唐琬和她的丈夫，唐琬派人送来酒菜。陆游很伤心，在墙上写下这首词。上阕回忆：红润柔软的手，捧着黄縢酒，满城春色，宫墙边杨柳青青。可是“东风恶”——东风暗指拆散他们的力量，美好的感情被吹散了，几年来只有满怀愁绪和离别的孤单。“错、错、错”，三个字连用，是痛彻心扉的悔恨。下阕写现在：春天还和从前一样，人却白白消瘦，泪水把手帕都湿透了。桃花落了，池阁冷清；当年的誓言还在，可是连一封信也没法寄。“莫、莫、莫”——算了吧，罢了吧。全词句子短促，像哽咽的声音。

In English

A famous love story stands behind this lyric. As a young man Lu You married his cousin Tang Wan, and they were deeply attached, but his mother disliked her and forced them to divorce. Years later, the story goes, Lu You was strolling in the Shen Garden in Shaoxing in spring when he met Tang Wan, now remarried, with her new husband. She sent over wine and food; heartbroken, he wrote this lyric on the garden wall. The first half remembers: soft, rosy hands pouring yellow-sealed wine, spring all over the city, willows by the palace wall. But the east wind is cruel — a hint at the force that blew them apart — and their joy has worn thin, leaving years of sorrow and loneliness. Then three single words: 错，错，错 — “Wrong! Wrong! Wrong!” The second half is the present. Spring is as it was, but she has grown thin for nothing; her tears soak through a silk handkerchief. Peach blossoms fall, the pond pavilion is deserted. Their vows still stand, yet he cannot even send her a letter. 莫，莫，莫 — “Don’t… no more… let it be.” The short, broken lines sound almost like someone choking back tears. Some scholars question whether the poem was really written for Tang Wan, but this is how it has been read for centuries.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Lu You (1125–1210) was the most prolific poet of the Southern Song, best known for patriotic verse. Song-era anecdotes say he wrote this around 1151–1155 at the Shen Garden in Shaoxing after meeting his former wife Tang Wan; some modern scholars think it may have been written elsewhere, for someone else.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

黄縢 téng（封口的酒）；欢情薄 bó；离索 suǒ；红浥 yì（湿润）；鲛绡 jiāo xiāo；几年 jǐ。“縢”“浥”机器朗读易误，已在朗读用字中替换同音字，音频需人工听验。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

红酥手，黄縢酒，满城春色宫墙柳。

hóng sū shǒu, huáng téng jiǔ, mǎn chéng chūn sè gōng qiáng liǔ

Soft rosy hands, a jar of yellow-sealed wine; spring filled the city, willows by the palace wall.

东风恶，欢情薄。

dōng fēng è, huān qíng bó

Then the east wind turned cruel, and our joy blew away.

一怀愁绪，几年离索。

yī huái chóu xù, jǐ nián lí suǒ

A heart full of grief, years of living apart.

错、错、错！

cuò, cuò, cuò

Wrong — all wrong — wrong!

春如旧，人空瘦，泪痕红浥鲛绡透。

chūn rú jiù, rén kōng shòu, lèi hén hóng yì jiāo xiāo tòu

Spring is the same, but she has wasted away; rouge-stained tears soak through her silk handkerchief.

桃花落，闲池阁。

táo huā luò, xián chí gé

Peach blossoms fall on an empty pavilion by the pond.

山盟虽在，锦书难托。

shān méng suī zài, jǐn shū nán tuō

Our vows still stand like mountains, but no letter can reach you now.

莫、莫、莫！

mò, mò, mò

No more — no more — let it be.
