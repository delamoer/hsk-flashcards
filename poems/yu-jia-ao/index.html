导读About this poem

Autumn on the northwest frontier: the geese fly south without a backward glance, horns sound among a thousand peaks, a lone fortress shuts its gates at sunset. A general who has not yet won his war drinks to a home ten thousand li away — and no one sleeps.

直译LiteralOn the frontier, autumn comes and the scenery is different; the geese leave for Hengyang with no wish to stay.

意译MeaningAutumn on the frontier looks like no other; the geese fly south to Hengyang and never look back.

02

直译LiteralOn four sides the frontier sounds rise together with the horns;

意译MeaningFrom every side come frontier sounds and the blare of army horns;

03

直译Literalamong a thousand peaks — long smoke, setting sun, a lone city closed.

意译Meaningamong a thousand peaks, under long smoke and the setting sun, a lone fort shuts its gates.

直译LiteralA cup of cloudy wine, home ten thousand li away; Yanran not yet engraved, no way to return.

意译MeaningOne cup of rough wine, and home is ten thousand li away; no victory carved in stone yet, so no road home.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

范仲淹曾在西北边境带兵防守西夏，这首词写的就是边塞秋天的景象和将士们的心情。上阕写景。“塞下秋来风景异”，边塞的秋天和内地完全不同；大雁飞向南方的衡阳，一点留恋都没有——连大雁都不愿在这里停留，可见边地多么荒凉。接着写声音：四面八方的边地声音和军中的号角声一起响起。千山之中，长长的烟雾，落下的夕阳，一座孤城早早关上城门，气氛紧张又冷清。下阕写人。喝一杯浊酒，想到家在万里之外；可是“燕然未勒”——还没有像东汉将军窦宪那样打败敌人、在燕然山刻石记功，怎么能回家呢？夜里羌笛声悠悠，地上铺满白霜，大家都睡不着：将军急白了头发，士兵流下了眼泪。这首词既有思乡的悲凉，又有守边的责任感，一改当时词多写儿女情长的风气，是宋代边塞词的开山之作。

In English

Fan Zhongyan once commanded troops on the northwest frontier against the Western Xia, and this lyric paints autumn there and what the soldiers felt. The first half is landscape. Autumn on the frontier looks nothing like autumn back home. The wild geese head south to Hengyang without the slightest wish to stay — even the birds don’t want to linger in such a desolate place. Then come the sounds: frontier noises from every side, rising with the army’s horns. Among a thousand peaks, under long trails of smoke and a sinking sun, a lone walled town has already shut its gates — tense and bleak. The second half turns to people. He drinks a cup of cloudy wine and thinks of home, ten thousand li away. But “Yanran is not yet carved”: in the Eastern Han, the general Dou Xian crushed the northern Xiongnu and carved a record of his victory on Mount Yanran. Until this war is won in the same way, there is no going home. At night the Qiang flutes play on, frost covers the ground, and nobody can sleep — the general’s hair has turned white, the soldiers are in tears. The poem holds both homesickness and a stubborn sense of duty. At a time when lyrics were mostly about love and parting, it opened up the frontier as a subject for the form.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Fan Zhongyan (989–1052) was a leading Northern Song statesman, famous for the line “be the first to worry about the world’s troubles and the last to enjoy its pleasures.” From 1040 he helped defend the northwest against the Western Xia; this lyric is believed to date from those years at the frontier.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

塞下 sài（边塞，不读 sāi、sè）；燕然 yān rán（山名，不读 yàn）；勒 lè（刻）；千嶂 zhàng；将军 jiāng；白发 fà；寐 mèi；羌管 qiāng；长烟 cháng。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

塞下秋来风景异，衡阳雁去无留意。

sài xià qiū lái fēng jǐng yì, héng yáng yàn qù wú liú yì

Autumn on the frontier looks like no other; the geese fly south to Hengyang and never look back.

四面边声连角起，

sì miàn biān shēng lián jiǎo qǐ

From every side come frontier sounds and the blare of army horns;

千嶂里，长烟落日孤城闭。

qiān zhàng lǐ, cháng yān luò rì gū chéng bì

among a thousand peaks, under long smoke and the setting sun, a lone fort shuts its gates.

浊酒一杯家万里，燕然未勒归无计。

zhuó jiǔ yī bēi jiā wàn lǐ, yān rán wèi lè guī wú jì

One cup of rough wine, and home is ten thousand li away; no victory carved in stone yet, so no road home.

羌管悠悠霜满地，

qiāng guǎn yōu yōu shuāng mǎn dì

The tribal flutes drift on; frost whitens the ground;

人不寐，将军白发征夫泪。

rén bù mèi, jiāng jūn bái fà zhēng fū lèi

no one sleeps — the general’s hair gone white, the soldiers in tears.
