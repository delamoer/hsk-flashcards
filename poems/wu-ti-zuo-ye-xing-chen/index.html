导读About this poem

The morning after a party, the poet remembers a woman he could only exchange glances with: “No phoenix wings to fly to you, but our hearts, like the rhino’s horn, are linked by a single thread.” Then the drum sounds, and he must hurry off to work.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

李商隐有许多诗不起题目，叫“无题”，大多写说不出口的爱情。这首诗回忆昨夜的一次宴会。开头连用两个“昨夜”，星辰和夜风，画楼西边、桂堂东边，都是那个夜晚难忘的细节。第三、四句是千古名句：我身上没有彩凤那样的翅膀，不能飞到你身边；可我们的心像灵犀一样，一点就通。传说犀牛角中间有一条白纹贯通两头，所以用“灵犀”比喻心意相通。今天汉语里还说“心有灵犀一点通”。第五、六句写宴会上的游戏：隔着座位传钩、分组猜谜，春酒温暖，灯火通红，热闹极了。最后两句急转：可惜听到更鼓，我得去官署上班，骑马赶去兰台，像风里的蓬草一样身不由己。热闹的回忆和无奈的现实对比，相思更显得深长。

In English

Li Shangyin left many poems without titles, simply called “Untitled”; most are about love that could not be spoken openly. This one recalls a party the night before. The opening repeats “last night” twice — the stars, the wind, the painted tower to the west, the cassia hall to the east — the kind of details you remember when an evening mattered. The second couplet is among the most quoted lines in Chinese: I have no bright phoenix wings to fly to you, yet our hearts, like the magic rhinoceros horn, connect at a single point. People believed a white thread ran straight through the centre of a rhino horn, so 灵犀 lingxi came to mean two hearts that understand each other without words; 心有灵犀一点通 is still used today. The third couplet shows the party games: passing a hidden hook across the table, guessing hidden objects in teams, warm spring wine, red candlelight. Then the last couplet turns suddenly. Alas, the drum announces the hour, and he must report for duty, riding off to the palace library like tumbleweed blown by the wind. The bright memory set against dull duty makes the longing all the deeper.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Li Shangyin (c. 813–858) was one of the great poets of the late Tang, famous for dense, beautiful and often mysterious verse. Caught between two rival court factions, he spent most of his life in minor posts. The “Orchid Terrace” at the end refers to the imperial library, where he served as a junior editor around 839; the identity of the woman, if there was one, is unknown.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

昨夜星辰昨夜风，

zuó yè xīng chén zuó yè fēng

Last night’s stars, last night’s breeze —

画楼西畔桂堂东。

huà lóu xī pàn guì táng dōng

west of the painted tower, east of the cassia hall.

身无彩凤双飞翼，

shēn wú cǎi fèng shuāng fēi yì

I have no phoenix wings to fly to you,

心有灵犀一点通。

xīn yǒu líng xī yī diǎn tōng

but like the rhino’s horn, our hearts are joined by one bright thread.

隔座送钩春酒暖，

gé zuò sòng gōu chūn jiǔ nuǎn

Passing the hidden hook across the table, the spring wine warm,

分曹射覆蜡灯红。

fēn cáo shè fù là dēng hóng

guessing in teams what lies under the cup, the candles glowing red.

嗟余听鼓应官去，

jiē yú tīng gǔ yìng guān qù

Alas — the drum sounds, and I must go to work,

走马兰台类转蓬。

zǒu mǎ lán tái lèi zhuǎn péng

riding to the library like tumbleweed blown in the wind.
