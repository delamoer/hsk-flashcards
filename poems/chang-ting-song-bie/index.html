导读About this poem

From China’s most famous love comedy: the heroine sees her lover off on an autumn morning and asks who dyed the frosty woods red overnight — it must be the tears of parting lovers.

中文解析

这段曲子出自元杂剧《西厢记》的“长亭送别”一折。崔莺莺和张生相爱，可是莺莺的母亲要张生先去京城考取功名，才肯把女儿嫁给他。于是莺莺在长亭为张生送行，一出场就唱了这支【端正好】。前四句全是秋景：蓝天白云，满地黄花（菊花），西风吹得很紧，大雁往南飞。这几个景物都带着秋天的凄凉，大雁南飞更让人想到远行。最后两句是全曲的灵魂：早晨是谁把经霜的树林染得这样红，像喝醉了一样？总是离别的人的眼泪啊。树叶变红本来是自然现象，莺莺却说是被离人的泪染红的——泪是血泪，所以染得红。这种把感情加在景物上的写法，叫“移情”，景中有情，情中有景。这几句借鉴了范仲淹《苏幕遮》“碧云天，黄叶地”，却更加深情动人。

In English

This aria comes from the “Farewell at the Long Pavilion” scene of The Romance of the Western Chamber, the most famous Yuan drama. Cui Yingying and the young scholar Zhang have fallen in love, but her mother insists he must first pass the imperial examination in the capital before they can marry. So Yingying sees him off at the roadside pavilion, and as she enters she sings this aria to the tune “Duanzhenghao”. The first four lines are pure autumn scenery: a blue sky with clouds, the ground covered in yellow chrysanthemums, the west wind blowing hard, wild geese flying south. Each image carries autumn’s sadness, and the southbound geese suggest a long journey. The last two lines are the heart of the song: who dyed the frosted woods so red this morning, red as if drunk? It must be the tears of those who part. Leaves turning red is just nature, but Yingying says they were stained by lovers’ tears — tears of blood, which is why they are so red. Pouring human feeling into the landscape like this makes scene and emotion inseparable. The opening borrows from Fan Zhongyan’s ci “blue clouds in the sky, yellow leaves on the ground”, but Wang Shifu makes it more passionate.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Wang Shifu (13th century; little is known of his life) was a Yuan playwright based in the capital, Dadu (Beijing). The Romance of the Western Chamber, based on a Tang story, became the best-known love story on the Chinese stage; this aria opens the farewell scene (Part 4, Act 3).

碧云天，黄花地，

bì yún tiān, huáng huā dì

Clouds in a blue sky, yellow chrysanthemums on the ground,

西风紧，北雁南飞。

xī fēng jǐn, běi yàn nán fēi

the west wind blowing hard, geese from the north flying south.

晓来谁染霜林醉？

xiǎo lái shuí rǎn shuāng lín zuì

Who dyed the frosted woods so red this morning, red as wine?

总是离人泪。

zǒng shì lí rén lèi

It must be the tears of those who part.
