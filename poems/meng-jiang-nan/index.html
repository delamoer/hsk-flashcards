导读About this poem

A woman waits all day in a riverside tower for the boat that will bring her man home. Hundreds of sails go by; none is his. Just 27 characters, and one of the most loved short lyrics in Chinese.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首小词只有二十七个字，写一个女子等待远方的爱人。早上她梳洗打扮好，一个人靠在江边的楼上张望。“独”字说明她是孤单的。江上一只只船开过去，“过尽千帆皆不是”——上千条船都过去了，却没有一条是他的。这一句写出了从希望到失望、一次又一次的心情，是全词最有名的一句。从早上等到傍晚，斜阳的光好像含情脉脉地看着她，江水却悠悠地流走，不管她的心事。最后一句“肠断白蘋洲”，她望着长满白蘋的小洲，伤心得好像肠子都断了（“肠断”是古人形容极度悲伤的说法）。整首词没有写她的长相，只写她的动作和看到的景物，等待的苦却非常真切。

In English

In only 27 characters, this lyric tells of a woman waiting for her absent lover. In the morning she finishes washing and doing her hair, then leans alone on the railing of a tower above the river. The word 独 (“alone”) sets the tone. Boats pass one after another — “a thousand sails go by, and none of them is his.” That line holds a whole day of hope rising and falling, sail after sail, and it is the most quoted line in the piece. By evening the slanting sunlight seems to linger on her tenderly, while the river just flows on, indifferent to her. The last line takes her eyes to a sandbar covered in white duckweed, perhaps where they once parted, and she is “broken-hearted” — literally, her insides torn, the classical way of saying unbearable grief. Notice that we never learn what she looks like or what she thinks; we only see what she does and what she sees, and that is enough to feel her waiting. The landscape does the speaking for her, a technique called “expressing feeling through scenery”.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Wen Tingyun (c. 812–866) was one of the first major writers of ci, lyrics written to fixed tunes, and is regarded as a founder of the ornate Huajian (“Among the Flowers”) school. Like many lyrics of his time, this one is written in the voice of a woman.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

脉脉 mò mò（不读 mài mài）；斜 xié；蘋 pín（白蘋：一种水草，不是“苹果”的苹）。机器朗读易错，已在“朗读用字”列替换同音字，音频需人工听验。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行教材版本，单调二十七字。词牌《梦江南》又名《忆江南》《望江南》。“白蘋洲”有版本作“白苹洲”，此处的“蘋”是水草名，保留繁体写法以免与“苹果”混淆，与通行教材一致。

cháng duàn bái pín zhōu
