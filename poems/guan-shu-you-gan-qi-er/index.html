导读About this poem

A huge warship stuck in the shallows suddenly floats “as light as a feather” when the spring floods come. Zhu Xi uses the picture to describe how understanding arrives: not by forcing, but when the time is right.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这是朱熹两首《观书有感》中的第二首，题目说是“读书的感想”，诗里却只写了一件事：一条大船。昨天夜里江边春水上涨，那艘又大又重的战船变得像一根羽毛那样轻。以前水浅的时候，大家花了很多力气推它、拉它，都是白费；今天水涨起来了，它在江中间自由自在地前进。这首诗用的是比喻和说理的写法：大船好比读书时想不通的问题，春水好比长期积累的学问和功夫。功夫不够时，硬推硬想也没有用；积累够了，水到渠成，问题自然就通了，这就是“豁然开朗”的感觉。诗里没有一个字讲道理，道理全藏在画面里，所以读起来不枯燥。“一毛轻”和“自在行”对比很鲜明，写出了从困难到轻松的变化。

In English

This is the second of Zhu Xi’s two poems “Thoughts on Reading”. The title promises reflections on books, but the poem describes only one thing: a ship. Last night the spring floods came up along the river, and a massive warship, which had been stuck, suddenly became “as light as a single hair”. Before, when the water was low, people wasted their strength pushing and hauling it; today, with the water risen, it moves freely down the middle of the stream. The whole poem is a metaphor. The heavy ship is a problem you cannot understand when you read; the spring water is the knowledge and effort you build up over time. If your foundation is not deep enough, forcing it gets you nowhere. Once enough has built up, the difficulty floats free by itself — that sudden “now I see it” feeling. Zhu Xi never states the lesson; he leaves it inside the picture, so the poem never feels like a lecture. The contrast between 一毛轻 (“light as a hair”) and the earlier wasted effort, and the easy phrase 自在行 (“moves at ease”), let us feel the change from struggle to ease.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Zhu Xi (1130–1200) was the most influential Confucian thinker of the Southern Song; his commentaries on the Confucian classics became the basis of the imperial examinations for some 600 years. He wrote poetry too, often using simple scenes to express ideas about learning.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

昨夜江边春水生，

zuó yè jiāng biān chūn shuǐ shēng

Last night the spring floods rose along the river,

艨艟巨舰一毛轻。

méng chōng jù jiàn yī máo qīng

and the great warship grew as light as a feather.

向来枉费推移力，

xiàng lái wǎng fèi tuī yí lì

All that pushing and hauling before was wasted;

此日中流自在行。

cǐ rì zhōng liú zì zài xíng

today it glides freely down the middle of the stream.
