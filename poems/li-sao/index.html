导读About this poem

Five famous couplets from China’s longest ancient poem, by Qu Yuan — the loyal minister whose death is remembered every year at the Dragon Boat Festival. “The road ahead is long and far; I will search high and low” is still quoted by anyone who refuses to give up.

第一章Stanza 1
01
老冉冉其将至兮，恐修名之不立。

直译LiteralOld age is slowly approaching; I fear a fine name will not be established.

意译MeaningOld age creeps closer day by day; I fear I’ll grow old before my good name is made.

02
朝饮木兰之坠露兮，夕餐秋菊之落英。

直译LiteralIn the morning I drink the dew dripping from magnolias; in the evening I eat the fallen petals of autumn chrysanthemums.

意译MeaningAt dawn I drink dew dripping from the magnolia; at dusk I dine on fallen chrysanthemum petals.

第二章Stanza 2
03
长太息以掩涕兮，哀民生之多艰。

直译LiteralWith a long, deep sigh I wipe away my tears, grieving that the people’s lives are so hard.

意译MeaningI heave a long sigh and wipe my tears — how hard the people’s lives are!

04
亦余心之所善兮，虽九死其犹未悔。

直译LiteralSince this is what my heart holds good, though I die nine times I would still not regret it.

意译MeaningThis is what my heart knows to be right — I could die nine times and never regret it.

第三章Stanza 3
05
路漫漫其修远兮，吾将上下而求索。

直译LiteralThe road is long, so long and far; I will go up and down, searching.

意译MeaningThe road ahead is long and far — I will search for the truth, high and low.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

《离骚》是屈原的代表作，也是中国古代最长的抒情诗。“离骚”大意是“遭遇忧愁”。屈原一心想让楚国强大，却被小人陷害、被国君疏远，这首长诗写的就是他的痛苦和坚持。这里选了五组名句：第一组说自己怕年华老去、来不及建立美好的名声，所以早上喝木兰花上的露水，晚上吃菊花的花瓣——这是用香草比喻自己品德高洁，叫“香草美人”的手法。第二组写他为百姓生活艰难而流泪，并说只要是心中认为对的，就算死九次也不后悔。最后一句“路漫漫其修远兮，吾将上下而求索”，说前面的路又长又远，我要上上下下不停地寻找，今天仍常被用来鼓励人坚持追求。句中的“兮”相当于“啊”，是楚辞最明显的标志。

In English

“Encountering Sorrow” is Qu Yuan’s greatest work and the longest lyric poem of ancient China. Qu Yuan wanted to make his state of Chu strong, but jealous rivals slandered him and the king pushed him away. The poem pours out his pain — and his refusal to bend. Here are five of its best-known couplets. In the first, he fears growing old before he has made a good name, so he drinks dew from magnolias in the morning and eats fallen chrysanthemum petals at night. He is not describing a real diet: fragrant plants stand for a pure, noble character, a technique later called “fragrant plants and fair ladies” (香草美人). In the second group he weeps over how hard the people’s lives are, and declares that for what his heart believes is right, he would die nine times without regret. The last line — “The road ahead is long and far; I will search high and low” — is still quoted to encourage anyone pursuing a hard goal. Notice the character 兮 (xī) in every line: a sigh-like particle, a bit like “oh”, and the clearest mark of the Chu style of poetry, the Chuci.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Qu Yuan (c. 340–278 BC) was a nobleman and minister of the state of Chu during the Warring States period. Slandered and sent into exile, he is said to have drowned himself in the Miluo River when Chu’s capital fell to Qin. Tradition says people raced boats to find him and threw rice into the river to keep fish away from his body — the legendary origin of dragon boat races and zongzi at the Dragon Boat Festival (the fifth day of the fifth lunar month).

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

兮 xī（语气词，相当于“啊”）；朝饮的“朝”读 zhāo（早晨，不读 cháo）；长太息的“长”读 cháng；涕 tì（古义眼泪，不是鼻涕）；冉冉 rǎn rǎn；余 yú（我）；落英的“英”读 yīng（花瓣）。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行的王逸《楚辞章句》系统文本。《离骚》全诗约 373 句、近 2500 字。这里选了三处名句：第 1 段（老冉冉……夕餐秋菊之落英）出自前半部分，两句相连；第 2 段两句出自其后不远（统编教材高中节选部分），两句之间略去“余虽好修姱以鞿羁兮……又申之以揽茝”两句；第 3 段出自中段，诗人上天求索之前。“路漫漫”王逸本作“路曼曼”，今通行写作“漫漫”。“落英”有“落下的花瓣”和“初开的花”两种解释，此处取前者。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

老冉冉其将至兮，恐修名之不立。

lǎo rǎn rǎn qí jiāng zhì xī, kǒng xiū míng zhī bù lì

Old age creeps closer day by day; I fear I’ll grow old before my good name is made.

朝饮木兰之坠露兮，夕餐秋菊之落英。

zhāo yǐn mù lán zhī zhuì lù xī, xī cān qiū jú zhī luò yīng

At dawn I drink dew dripping from the magnolia; at dusk I dine on fallen chrysanthemum petals.

长太息以掩涕兮，哀民生之多艰。

cháng tài xī yǐ yǎn tì xī, āi mín shēng zhī duō jiān

I heave a long sigh and wipe my tears — how hard the people’s lives are!

亦余心之所善兮，虽九死其犹未悔。

yì yú xīn zhī suǒ shàn xī, suī jiǔ sǐ qí yóu wèi huǐ

This is what my heart knows to be right — I could die nine times and never regret it.

路漫漫其修远兮，吾将上下而求索。

lù màn màn qí xiū yuǎn xī, wú jiāng shàng xià ér qiú suǒ

The road ahead is long and far — I will search for the truth, high and low.
