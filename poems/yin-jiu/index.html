导读About this poem

A man who has left official life lives quietly among ordinary people. Picking chrysanthemums by the fence, he looks up and the mountain is simply there — and he finds there is nothing more to say.

中文解析

陶渊明辞官回到乡下，过着种地、喝酒、写诗的生活。这首诗开头说：我把房子盖在人来人往的地方，却听不到车马的喧闹声。为什么？因为“心远地自偏”——心离名利远了，住在哪里都觉得安静。这句话讲的是一个道理：安静不在于地方，而在于心。接下来是最有名的两句：在东边的篱笆下采菊花，悠闲地一抬头，南山就出现在眼前。“见”是无意中看到，不是特意去“望”，所以显得特别自然。傍晚山里的雾气很美，鸟儿结伴飞回山林。最后说：这里面有人生真正的意义，想要说出来，却已经忘了该怎么说。真正的体会是说不出来的。这首诗把景物和道理融合在一起，语言平淡，味道却很深，是中国田园诗的代表作。

In English

Tao Yuanming gave up his government post and went back to the countryside to farm, drink and write. The poem opens with a puzzle: I built my hut where people live, yet I hear no noise of carts and horses. How? “When the heart is far away, the place becomes remote by itself.” Quiet is not about where you live but about your mind — once you stop chasing fame and money, even a busy place feels peaceful. Then come the two most famous lines: picking chrysanthemums under the eastern fence, I calmly see the Southern Mountain. The verb is 见 (to see, to catch sight of), not 望 (to gaze at): he isn’t looking for the mountain, it just comes into view. That casual moment is the whole point. In the evening light the mountain air is lovely, and birds fly home together. The ending says there is a true meaning in all this — but when he tries to put it into words, the words are already gone. Some things can only be felt. Plain on the surface, rich underneath, this is the best-known poem of Chinese “fields and gardens” poetry.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Tao Yuanming (365?–427) served in minor official posts but disliked the politics; at about 41 he resigned for good and lived as a farmer. This is the fifth of twenty poems titled “Drinking Wine”, written in his years of retirement.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

“悠然见南山”的“见”读 jiàn（看见），不读 xiàn。“飞鸟相与还”的“还”读 huán（回来），不读 hái；“何能尔”的“尔”读 ěr（这样）。

版本说明 About the text

采用统编语文教材八年级上册文本，全诗十句，此处完整收录，按一联一行排版。“采菊”旧本作“採菊”；“欲辨已忘言”一作“欲辩已忘言”。宋代苏轼指出有版本作“悠然望南山”，并认为“见”字更好，今通行本均作“见”。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

结庐在人境，而无车马喧。

jié lú zài rén jìng, ér wú chē mǎ xuān

I built my hut where people live, yet no carts or horses clatter by.

问君何能尔？心远地自偏。

wèn jūn hé néng ěr, xīn yuǎn dì zì piān

How can that be, you ask? When the heart is far away, any place grows quiet.

采菊东篱下，悠然见南山。

cǎi jú dōng lí xià, yōu rán jiàn nán shān

Picking chrysanthemums by the eastern fence, I look up — and there is the Southern Mountain.

山气日夕佳，飞鸟相与还。

shān qì rì xī jiā, fēi niǎo xiāng yǔ huán

At dusk the mountain mist is lovely, and birds fly home together.

此中有真意，欲辨已忘言。

cǐ zhōng yǒu zhēn yì, yù biàn yǐ wàng yán

There is a true meaning in all this — but when I try to say it, the words are gone.
