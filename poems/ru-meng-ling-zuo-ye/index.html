导读About this poem

A tiny scene in six lines: after a night of wind and rain, a young woman asks her maid about the flowering crabapple. “Just the same,” says the maid. “Don’t you know?” she replies — “the green must be fat now, and the red thin.”

中文解析

这是李清照少女时代的名作，只有三十三个字，却像一出小戏。昨夜雨点稀疏、风很急，她喝了酒睡得很沉，早上醒来酒意还没散。她心里惦记着院子里的海棠，就问正在卷帘子的丫鬟，丫鬟随口说：“海棠还是老样子。”她却说：“你知道吗？你知道吗？应该是绿叶多了，红花少了。”“绿肥红瘦”四个字最妙：用“肥”“瘦”这样形容人的词来写叶和花，叶子被雨水滋润得更茂盛，花却被风雨打落了。粗心的丫鬟和敏感的主人一对比，写出了少女对春天将逝的惋惜。全词有问有答，口语自然，“知否，知否”的重复把着急的语气写活了。

In English

Li Qingzhao wrote this as a young woman, and in thirty-three characters it plays out like a tiny drama. Last night the rain was light but the wind was fierce; she had been drinking, slept deeply, and wakes still a little tipsy. Her first thought is the crabapple tree in the garden. She asks the maid who is rolling up the blinds, and the maid answers without a glance: “The crabapple’s just the same.” But she knows better: “Don’t you know? Don’t you know? The green must be fat now, and the red thin.” That phrase, 绿肥红瘦, is the heart of the poem. “Fat” and “thin” are words for people, not plants: the leaves have grown lush from the rain, while the blossoms have been battered off by the wind. The careless maid and the sensitive young mistress make a neat contrast, and through it we feel her regret that spring is slipping away. Notice how natural the language is — a question, an answer, and an impatient “don’t you know?” repeated twice, just as someone would really say it. The tune name 如梦令 (“As in a Dream”) is fixed by the music and has nothing to do with the content.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Li Qingzhao (1084–c. 1155), daughter of a scholar-official, was already famous in the capital Kaifeng as a teenager. This lyric is traditionally counted among her early works, written before or soon after her marriage in 1101.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

昨夜雨疏风骤，浓睡不消残酒。

zuó yè yǔ shū fēng zhòu, nóng shuì bù xiāo cán jiǔ

Last night: light rain, a gusting wind. Even a deep sleep hasn’t cleared the wine from my head.

试问卷帘人，却道海棠依旧。

shì wèn juǎn lián rén, què dào hǎi táng yī jiù

I ask the maid rolling up the blinds. “The crabapple’s just the same,” she says.

知否，知否？应是绿肥红瘦。

zhī fǒu, zhī fǒu, yīng shì lǜ féi hóng shòu

Don’t you know? Don’t you see? The leaves must be plump by now, and the blossoms thin.
