导读About this poem

A farewell poem that refuses to be sad: “As long as I have a true friend somewhere within the seas, the ends of the earth are like next door.” Written by a poet barely out of his teens.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

王勃在长安送朋友杜少府去四川做官。前两句写送别的地点和远方：三秦护卫着长安城，透过风烟遥望朋友要去的蜀地五津。一近一远，把两地连起来。三、四句说：我和你一样，都是离开家乡在外做官的人，离别的心情彼此都懂。五、六句是全诗最有名的两句：只要四海之内有知心朋友，就算远在天边，也像住在隔壁一样。这两句把空间的距离变成了心灵的贴近，打破了送别诗一般的伤感，今天还常被写在给远方朋友的信和卡片上。最后两句劝朋友：不要在分别的路口，像小儿女一样哭得泪水湿透手巾。整首诗开阔、乐观，一改离别诗哭哭啼啼的调子。

In English

Wang Bo is in Chang’an seeing off a friend named Du, who is going to take up a minor post in Sichuan. The opening couplet joins the two places: the capital, guarded by the land of Three Qin, and far away through wind and haze, the Five Fords of Shu where Du is heading. Then: “We share the same feeling at parting — we are both men far from home, serving as officials.” The third couplet is why the poem is loved: “As long as there is a true friend within the four seas, the ends of the earth are like next door.” Physical distance becomes closeness of heart, and the usual tearful mood of farewell poems is turned upside down. Chinese people still write these lines in cards and messages to friends abroad. The ending gently tells his friend not to stand at the fork in the road and wet their handkerchiefs with tears like young boys and girls. The whole poem is broad-minded and hopeful, a very different note from most Tang farewells.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Wang Bo (c. 650–676) was a child prodigy, one of the “Four Talents of the Early Tang”. He wrote this as a young man in Chang’an, probably still in his teens or early twenties; he drowned crossing the sea at about 26.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

城阙辅三秦，

chéng què fǔ sān qín

The capital stands guarded by the land of Qin;

风烟望五津。

fēng yān wàng wǔ jīn

through wind and haze I look toward the fords of Shu.

与君离别意，

yǔ jūn lí bié yì

You and I feel this parting the same way:

同是宦游人。

tóng shì huàn yóu rén

we are both far from home, serving away.

海内存知己，

hǎi nèi cún zhī jǐ

If a true friend lives anywhere within the seas,

天涯若比邻。

tiān yá ruò bǐ lín

the ends of the earth are just next door.

无为在歧路，

wú wéi zài qí lù

So let’s not stand here where the road divides

儿女共沾巾。

ér nǚ gòng zhān jīn

wetting our sleeves with tears like children.
