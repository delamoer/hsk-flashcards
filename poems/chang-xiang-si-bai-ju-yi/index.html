导读About this poem

A woman follows two rivers in her mind all the way to the ferry where her man went south, then stands in the moonlight at her window, longing for him. An early song lyric that flows like the water it describes.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首词用一位女子的口吻，写她思念远方的爱人。上阕写景：汴水、泗水一直向南流，流到长江边的瓜洲古渡口——那里也许就是爱人离开的地方，她的思念也跟着河水一路流去。远处吴地的群山一点一点，好像也带着愁。山本来没有感情，是人把自己的愁放了进去，这叫“移情于景”。下阕写情：“思悠悠，恨悠悠”，思念和怨恨都又长又远，要到他回来的那一天才会停止。最后一句“月明人倚楼”又回到画面：月光下，她一个人靠着楼栏，望着远方。全词反复用“流”“悠悠”，句子像水一样连绵不断，读起来有民歌的味道，思念也显得没有尽头。

In English

This song lyric speaks in the voice of a woman missing the man she loves, far away. The first stanza is scenery: the Bian and Si rivers flow south until they reach the old ferry at Guazhou on the Yangtze — perhaps the place where he left — and her thoughts drift along with the water. In the distance the hills of Wu rise point after point, as if they too were full of sorrow. Hills feel nothing, of course; she has put her own sadness into them, a technique Chinese critics call “moving feeling into scenery”. The second stanza is feeling: “longing, on and on; resentment, on and on” — both will only stop on the day he comes home. The last line returns to a picture: in bright moonlight, a woman leans alone on the railing of an upper room, gazing into the distance. Repeated words like 流 (“flow”) and 悠悠 (“long and far”) make the lines run on like water, with the flavour of a folk song, so the longing seems to have no end.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Bai Juyi (772–846) was one of the first literati poets to write ci, song lyrics set to popular tunes. This piece is traditionally attributed to him, though some scholars have questioned it; its date is unknown.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

汴水流，泗水流，

biàn shuǐ liú, sì shuǐ liú

The Bian flows on, the Si flows on,

流到瓜洲古渡头。

liú dào guā zhōu gǔ dù tóu

down to the old ferry at Guazhou.

吴山点点愁。

wú shān diǎn diǎn chóu

The far hills of Wu, every peak a sorrow.

思悠悠，恨悠悠，

sī yōu yōu, hèn yōu yōu

Longing without end, regret without end,

恨到归时方始休。

hèn dào guī shí fāng shǐ xiū

and only your homecoming will put it to rest.

月明人倚楼。

yuè míng rén yǐ lóu

Under a bright moon, I lean alone on the railing.
