菩萨蛮（人人尽说江南好）

Pú Sà Mán
Pusaman: Everyone Says the South Is Lovely
韦庄Wéi Zhuāng · Wei Zhuang唐 Tang

导读About this poem

Everyone says the south is so lovely you should grow old there — blue water, painted boats, a beautiful girl selling wine. And yet: “don’t go home before you’re old; going home would break your heart.” A love letter to Jiangnan with a hidden ache.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首词写的是江南（长江下游以南，今江苏、浙江一带）的美。上阕：人人都说江南好，来这里的游人就应该在江南住到老。春天的江水比天还要蓝，躺在画船上听着雨声睡觉，多么舒服。下阕：卖酒的姑娘像月亮一样美，手腕白得像霜雪。最后两句转入心事：“未老莫还乡，还乡须断肠”——还没老就别回家乡吧，回去一定会伤心欲绝。为什么不回去？因为当时北方战乱（黄巢起义后），家乡已经破败，回去只会更难过。所以这首词表面上赞美江南，实际上写的是有家难回的痛苦。越写江南美，越显出思乡的苦，这叫“以乐景写哀情”——用快乐的景色写悲伤的感情。

In English

This lyric praises Jiangnan, the green, watery region south of the lower Yangtze (today’s Jiangsu and Zhejiang). The first stanza: everyone says the south is wonderful — a traveller ought to stay here until he grows old. In spring the river is bluer than the sky, and you can fall asleep on a painted boat listening to the rain. The second stanza: the girl selling wine by the counter is lovely as the moon, her wrists white as frost and snow. Then the last two lines turn: “Don’t go home before you’re old — going home would break your heart.” Why not go home? Because the north, where the poet came from, had been torn apart by war after the Huang Chao rebellion; home was in ruins, and seeing it would only hurt more. So on the surface this is a postcard of paradise, but underneath it is the pain of someone who cannot go back. The more beautiful the south looks, the sharper the homesickness feels — Chinese critics call this “writing sorrow through a happy scene”.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Wei Zhuang (c. 836–910) was from near Chang’an. When Huang Chao’s rebels took the capital in 880 he fled, and spent years wandering in the south; he later became chief minister of the Former Shu kingdom in Sichuan. He is, with Wen Tingyun, a leading poet of the Huajian school, but his lyrics are plainer and more personal. This is the second of his five Pusaman lyrics.

人人尽说江南好，

rén rén jìn shuō jiāng nán hǎo

Everyone tells me the South is lovely,

游人只合江南老。

yóu rén zhǐ hé jiāng nán lǎo

that a traveller here should stay till he grows old.

春水碧于天，

chūn shuǐ bì yú tiān

In spring the water is bluer than the sky;

画船听雨眠。

huà chuán tīng yǔ mián

I drift to sleep in a painted boat, listening to the rain.

垆边人似月，

lú biān rén sì yuè

The girl who pours the wine is lovely as the moon,

皓腕凝霜雪。

hào wàn níng shuāng xuě

her bare wrists white as frost and snow.

未老莫还乡，

wèi lǎo mò huán xiāng

Don’t go home while you are young —

还乡须断肠。

huán xiāng xū duàn cháng

going home would only break your heart.
