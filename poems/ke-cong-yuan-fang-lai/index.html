导读About this poem

Her husband, ten thousand miles away, sends her a piece of silk woven with mandarin ducks. She turns it into a quilt for two, full of puns on love that cannot be undone. A small, happy poem — rare among the Nineteen Old Poems.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

一位妻子收到远方丈夫托客人带来的一块绸缎。两人相隔万里，他的心却还和从前一样——这是全诗的感情中心。绸缎上织着一对鸳鸯，她把它裁成一床“合欢被”。接下来是一连串巧妙的双关：往被子里装丝绵，“丝”谐音“思”，丝绵又长，所以叫“长相思”；被子四边用丝线打结镶边，结打得解不开，叫“结不解”，暗指两人的感情永远分不开。最后用“胶投漆中”作比：胶和漆混在一起，谁也分不开它们——今天的成语“如胶似漆”就是这个意思。《古诗十九首》大多写离别的愁苦，这首却在思念中带着甜蜜和自信。整首诗从一块布写到一床被子，动作很生活化，感情却很深，是汉代民间夫妻之爱的动人写照。

In English

A wife receives a length of patterned silk, brought by a traveller from her husband far away. Ten thousand li separate them, yet his heart is the same as ever — that is the emotional core of the poem. The silk is woven with a pair of mandarin ducks, the classic symbol of a devoted couple, and she cuts it into a 合欢被, a “joined-joy” quilt for two. Then comes a chain of clever puns. She pads the quilt with silk floss: 丝 (sī, silk) sounds like 思 (sī, longing), and the floss is long — so it is filled with “long longing” (长相思). She trims the edges with knotted silk thread, knots that cannot be untied (结不解) — like a bond that can never be broken. The last image: glue mixed into lacquer — who could ever separate them? The modern idiom 如胶似漆, “like glue and lacquer”, describes a couple who are inseparable. Most of the Nineteen Old Poems are full of the sorrow of separation, but this one, though the couple are apart, is warm and confident. Through ordinary acts — receiving a gift, cutting cloth, sewing a quilt — it shows the love of an ordinary Han-dynasty couple.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

The Nineteen Old Poems (古诗十九首) are anonymous five-character poems from the late Eastern Han (2nd century AD), first collected in the 6th-century anthology Wen Xuan. They are traditionally seen as the foundation of classical five-character verse.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

遗 wèi（赠送，不读 yí）；著 zhuó（装进去）；缘 yuán（给衣物镶边，旧注有读 yuàn 的）；绮 qǐ；尔 ěr；“一端”的“一”逐字注本调 yī。机器朗读的“遗”“著”已在“朗读用字”列替换同音字。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

客从远方来，遗我一端绮。

kè cóng yuǎn fāng lái, wèi wǒ yī duān qǐ

A traveller came from far away and handed me a length of patterned silk.

相去万余里，故人心尚尔。

xiāng qù wàn yú lǐ, gù rén xīn shàng ěr

Ten thousand miles lie between us, and still his heart has not changed.

文彩双鸳鸯，裁为合欢被。

wén cǎi shuāng yuān yāng, cái wéi hé huān bèi

Woven on it, a pair of mandarin ducks — I cut it into a quilt for two.

著以长相思，缘以结不解。

zhuó yǐ cháng xiāng sī, yuán yǐ jié bù jiě

I pad it with silk floss, long as my longing, and trim it with knots no one can undo.

以胶投漆中，谁能别离此？

yǐ jiāo tóu qī zhōng, shuí néng bié lí cǐ

Like glue stirred into lacquer — who could ever pull us apart?
