导读About this poem

Black clouds crush down on a besieged city, armour flashes like golden scales, the sky turns rouge and purple at dusk — a battle painted almost entirely in colour, ending with soldiers ready to die for their ruler.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗写一场边塞守城战，最大的特点是用浓烈的颜色来写战争。第一句写敌军像黑云一样压过来，城好像要被压塌了；守城将士的铠甲在阳光下闪闪发光，像金色的鱼鳞。接着写号角声响满秋天的天空，边塞的泥土在暮色里凝成胭脂一样的紫红色——暗示战场上的血。第三联写援军半卷着红旗赶到易水边，夜里霜很重，战鼓都被冻得敲不响了。最后两句说：为了报答君王的重用，我们愿意手提宝剑为他战死。黑、金、紫、红、白（霜）交错在一起，画面感极强。“黑云压城城欲摧”今天常用来形容形势紧张、压力很大。

In English

This poem describes the defence of a frontier city, and what makes it unforgettable is colour. The enemy arrives like black clouds pressing down until the city walls seem about to crack; the defenders’ armour catches the sun and opens like golden fish scales. Bugle calls fill the autumn sky, and at dusk the frontier earth sets into a rouge-purple — a hint of the blood spilled there. Then reinforcements reach the Yi River with their red banners half furled; the frost lies so heavy that the cold drums will barely sound. In the last couplet the soldiers declare that, to repay their ruler’s trust, they will take up their “jade dragons” — their swords — and die for him. Black, gold, purple, red and the white of frost flash one after another, like scenes in a film. Notice, too, that Li He hardly describes the fighting itself: we see only the sky, the armour, the earth, the banners and the drums, and imagine the rest. The opening line, 黑云压城城欲摧, is still used today for any tense moment when pressure seems overwhelming.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Li He (790–816), a descendant of the Tang royal house, was famous for his dazzling, strange imagery and died at 26. Tradition says that when he showed this poem to the great writer Han Yu, Han Yu was so struck by the first two lines that he hurried to meet him.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

多音字：燕脂 yān（同“胭脂”，不读 yàn）；角声 jiǎo（号角）；塞上 sài；半卷 juǎn；霜重 zhòng；为君死 wèi。易水 yì。

版本说明 About the text

采用统编教材版本。“甲光向日”一作“向月”；“燕脂”一作“胭脂”，意思相同。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

黑云压城城欲摧，

hēi yún yā chéng chéng yù cuī

Black clouds bear down until the walls seem ready to crack;

甲光向日金鳞开。

jiǎ guāng xiàng rì jīn lín kāi

armour flashes in the sun like opening golden scales.

角声满天秋色里，

jiǎo shēng mǎn tiān qiū sè lǐ

Bugles fill the whole autumn sky,

塞上燕脂凝夜紫。

sài shàng yān zhī níng yè zǐ

and the rouge-red frontier earth darkens to purple at nightfall.

半卷红旗临易水，

bàn juǎn hóng qí lín yì shuǐ

Red banners half furled, they come to the Yi River;

霜重鼓寒声不起。

shuāng zhòng gǔ hán shēng bù qǐ

the frost lies thick, and the frozen drums will hardly sound.

报君黄金台上意，

bào jūn huáng jīn tái shàng yì

To repay the trust our lord placed in us,

提携玉龙为君死。

tí xié yù lóng wèi jūn sǐ

we take up our swords and die for him.
