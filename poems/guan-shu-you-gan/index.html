导读About this poem

A clear little pond reflects the sky and clouds. Why is it so clear? Because fresh water keeps flowing in from its source — and a mind stays clear the same way, by always learning.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗题目叫“观书有感”（读书有感想），可是诗里一个字也没写书，只写了一个池塘。前两句写半亩大的方形池塘，像一面打开的镜子，天光和云影都映在水里，一起来回晃动。第三句诗人问池塘：你怎么能这样清澈？第四句回答：因为有源头不断地流来新鲜的活水。这就是诗的道理：人要心里明白、思想清楚，就要不断读书、学习新知识，像池塘需要源头活水一样。诗人把一个抽象的道理放在一个具体的画面里，这叫“寓理于景”（把道理藏在景物里），所以读起来不枯燥。“源头活水”今天是常用语，比喻事物发展的动力和来源。

In English

The title is “Thoughts on Reading”, yet the poem never mentions a book — it describes a pond. A square pond, about half a mu across, lies open like a mirror; the light of the sky and the shadows of clouds move back and forth together across it. Then the poet asks the pond a question: “How can you be so clear?” And answers it: “Because living water keeps coming from the source.” That is the point. A mind stays clear and sharp only if it keeps taking in something new — by reading, studying, thinking — just as a pond stays clean only while fresh water flows in. A pond with no inflow goes stagnant. Zhu Xi puts an abstract idea inside a concrete picture, a method Chinese critics call yù lǐ yú jǐng (“hiding a principle in a scene”), so the lesson never feels like a lecture. The phrase 源头活水 (“living water from the source”) is used every day in modern Chinese for whatever keeps something fresh and growing — new ideas, new people, new learning.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Zhu Xi (1130–1200) was the most influential Confucian philosopher after Confucius himself; his commentaries became the basis of the imperial exams for some 600 years. This is the first of two poems with this title; the second describes a great ship floating free on spring floods.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

那得：那 读 nǎ（同“哪”，“怎么”），按通行注音读 nǎ，不读 nà。为有：为 读 wèi（因为）。一鉴 jiàn。渠 qú（它，指方塘）。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

半亩方塘一鉴开，

bàn mǔ fāng táng yī jiàn kāi

A small square pond lies open like a mirror;

天光云影共徘徊。

tiān guāng yún yǐng gòng pái huái

sky light and cloud shadows drift across it together.

问渠那得清如许？

wèn qú nǎ dé qīng rú xǔ

I ask the pond: how do you stay so clear?

为有源头活水来。

wèi yǒu yuán tóu huó shuǐ lái

Because fresh water keeps flowing from its source.
