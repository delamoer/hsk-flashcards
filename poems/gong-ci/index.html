后宫词（泪湿罗巾梦不成）

导读About this poem

A palace lady who has lost the emperor’s favour lies awake in tears, listening to music from the hall where someone else is now the favourite.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这是一首“宫怨诗”，写皇宫里失宠宫女的痛苦。第一句：她哭得罗巾都湿了，想在梦里见到皇帝也做不成。第二句是一个刺痛人心的对比：夜深了，前殿那边还在按着节拍唱歌——皇帝正和新的宠妃寻欢作乐。第三句是全诗最有名的一句：“红颜未老恩先断”，她还年轻美丽，皇帝的宠爱却已经断了。第四句写她斜靠在熏笼上，一直坐到天亮。从“梦不成”到“坐到明”，写出了一整夜的无眠和绝望。诗人借宫女的遭遇，也写出了古代女子命运完全掌握在别人手里的悲哀。

In English

This is a “palace lament”, a type of poem about women in the imperial palace who have lost the emperor’s favour. Line 1: she has cried until her silk handkerchief is soaked, and she can’t even fall asleep to meet him in a dream. Line 2 is a painful contrast: deep in the night, from the front hall come the beat and sound of singing — the emperor is enjoying himself with a new favourite. Line 3 is the most famous line: 红颜未老恩先断, “her rosy face has not grown old, but his favour has already ended”. She is still young and beautiful, and it hasn’t made any difference. Line 4 shows her leaning against the incense-warmed clothes rack, sitting there until dawn. From “no dream comes” to “sits until morning”, the poem covers one whole sleepless night of despair. Through one palace lady, Bai Juyi also shows the sadness of women in old China, whose whole fate lay in someone else’s hands. The poem is spoken from outside, but the feeling is entirely hers.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Bai Juyi (772–846) wrote many poems about the lives of ordinary and powerless people, including palace women; his long “Song of Everlasting Sorrow” is also set in the imperial court. Thousands of women lived in the Tang palace, most of whom rarely or never saw the emperor.

版本说明 About the text

题一作《宫词》。采用通行本。“泪湿罗巾”一作“泪尽罗巾”。白居易另有一首同题《后宫词》（“雨露由来一点恩”），维基文库“後宮詞”页面收的是那一首，本诗原文按通行选本核对。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

泪湿罗巾梦不成，

lèi shī luó jīn mèng bù chéng

Her silk handkerchief is wet with tears; sleep won’t come;

夜深前殿按歌声。

yè shēn qián diàn àn gē shēng

late at night, from the front hall, the singing goes on.

红颜未老恩先断，

hóng yán wèi lǎo ēn xiān duàn

Her face is still young, but his love has already ended —

斜倚熏笼坐到明。

xié yǐ xūn lóng zuò dào míng

she leans on the warm incense rack and sits till dawn.
