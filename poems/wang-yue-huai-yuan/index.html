导读About this poem

“A bright moon rises over the sea; at the ends of the earth, we share this moment.” A sleepless night of longing, and a wish to send someone a handful of moonlight.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗写在月夜思念远方的人。开头两句气象很大：一轮明月从海上升起，远在天涯的人，此刻也在看着同一轮月亮。不同的地方，同一个时刻，同一个月亮，距离一下子被拉近了。这两句今天常在中秋节被引用。三、四句说：多情的人埋怨夜太长，整夜都在思念。五、六句写细节：吹灭蜡烛，更爱这满屋的月光；披上衣服走出去，感到露水打湿了衣裳——说明在外面站了很久。最后两句最有想象力：月光这么美，可惜不能捧满一手送给你，还是回去睡吧，也许在梦里能和你相会。“情人”在这里可以理解为恋人，也可以理解为多情的人、远方的亲友。全诗感情深而不悲苦，非常温柔。

In English

This is a poem of longing on a moonlit night. It opens on a huge scale: “Over the sea a bright moon rises; at the ends of the earth we share this moment.” Different places, the same moment, the same moon — and the distance between two people shrinks. Chinese people quote these lines every Mid-Autumn Festival. Then: one who loves resents the long night and lies awake thinking all night through. The third couplet gives small, careful details: he puts out the candle, the better to enjoy the moonlight filling the room; he throws on a coat and goes outside, and feels the dew soaking in — he has been standing there a long time. The ending is the most imaginative part: the moonlight is so lovely he wants to send it, but he cannot give anyone a handful of light, so he goes back to bed hoping to meet in a dream. 情人 here may mean a lover, or more broadly anyone full of feeling for someone far away. The longing is deep but gentle, never bitter.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Zhang Jiuling (678–740) was a statesman and poet who served as chief minister under Emperor Xuanzong. He is thought to have written this after 737, when he was demoted and sent south to Jingzhou.

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

海上生明月，

hǎi shàng shēng míng yuè

Over the sea a bright moon rises;

天涯共此时。

tiān yá gòng cǐ shí

far apart, we share this moment.

情人怨遥夜，

qíng rén yuàn yáo yè

One in love resents the endless night,

竟夕起相思。

jìng xī qǐ xiāng sī

lying awake with longing till dawn.

灭烛怜光满，

miè zhú lián guāng mǎn

I blow out the candle to love the moonlit room;

披衣觉露滋。

pī yī jué lù zī

coat round my shoulders, I feel the dew soak through.

不堪盈手赠，

bù kān yíng shǒu zèng

I cannot send you moonlight by the handful,

还寝梦佳期。

huán qǐn mèng jiā qī

so back to bed, to dream of when we meet.
