导读About this poem

An envoy rides out alone into the northwestern desert. The poem’s central couplet — a single column of smoke standing straight, a round sun setting over a long river — is perhaps the most famous desert image in Chinese.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

公元737年，王维奉命出使边塞，慰问打了胜仗的将士。开头说自己轻车简从去边疆，路过居延。接着诗人把自己比作随风飘飞的“征蓬”和北飞的“归雁”，暗含被排挤出朝廷的失落。第三联是千古名句：“大漠孤烟直，长河落日圆。”一望无际的沙漠上，一缕烟直直升起；长长的河上，一轮落日又大又圆。“直”和“圆”两个字简单得像几何线条，却画出了沙漠的辽阔和壮美，《红楼梦》里的香菱就说这两个字“似无理的，想去却是再找不出两个字换这两个来的”。最后写到了萧关，遇见侦察的骑兵，得知主帅还在前线。全诗由孤独转向壮阔，景象雄浑。

In English

In 737 Wang Wei was sent from the capital to the northwestern frontier to bring the emperor’s congratulations to an army that had just defeated the Tibetans. He begins plainly: a light carriage, a few attendants, a long road past Juyan. Then he compares himself to tumbleweed blown out beyond the Han passes and to a wild goose flying north into foreign skies — images of drifting, with a hint that he has been pushed out of court. The third couplet is the one everyone knows: over the vast desert a lone column of smoke rises straight; over the long river the setting sun is round. The two key words, 直 (straight) and 圆 (round), are as simple as lines in a geometry book, yet they capture the emptiness and grandeur of the desert — with no wind and nothing in the way, smoke goes straight up; with nothing on the horizon, the sun looks huge and perfectly round. In the novel Dream of the Red Chamber, a girl learning poetry says these two words seem illogical, yet no other words could replace them. The poem ends at Xiao Pass, where a scout tells him the commander is still at the front.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Wang Wei (701–761) wrote this in 737, when he was sent as an imperial envoy to the Hexi frontier. The mission came soon after his patron Zhang Jiuling lost power, so many readers see it as a polite form of exile.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

候骑 hòu jì（统编教材注 jì，指骑马的人；今天“骑马”读 qí）。属国 shǔ guó（不读 zhǔ）。燕然 Yān rán（不读 yàn）。都护 dū hù（不读 dōu）。单车 dān chē。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行的教材版本，与《全唐诗》文本一致。“长河”一般解释为黄河，也有学者认为指当地的河流，解析里不展开。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

单车欲问边，

dān chē yù wèn biān

With one light carriage I set out to visit the frontier,

属国过居延。

shǔ guó guò jū yán

an envoy on the long road past Juyan.

征蓬出汉塞，

zhēng péng chū hàn sài

Like tumbleweed I blow out past the Han passes,

归雁入胡天。

guī yàn rù hú tiān

like a homing goose I enter the nomads’ sky.

大漠孤烟直，

dà mò gū yān zhí

On the endless desert one thread of smoke stands straight;

长河落日圆。

cháng hé luò rì yuán

over the long river the setting sun hangs round.

萧关逢候骑，

xiāo guān féng hòu jì

At Xiao Pass I meet a scout on horseback:

都护在燕然。

dū hù zài yān rán

“The commander is far ahead, at the front.”
