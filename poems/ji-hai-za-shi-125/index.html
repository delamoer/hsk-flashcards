导读About this poem

China in 1839 felt to Gong Zizhen like ten thousand horses struck dumb. He asks heaven for thunder to wake the country — and to send down talented people of every kind. The last line is still quoted everywhere today.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

1839年（己亥年），龚自珍辞官离京回南方，一路上写了三百多首短诗，总称《己亥杂诗》。这是第一百二十五首：他路过镇江，看到人们祭祀玉皇、风神、雷神，道士请他写祭文，他就写了这首。前两句说：中国要有生气，必须靠风雷一样猛烈的变革；现在“万马齐喑”——上万匹马都沉默不出声，比喻整个社会死气沉沉，没人敢说话，这实在可悲。后两句借着祭神的场合向“天公”请求：请您重新振作精神，不拘一种标准，把各种各样的人才降到人间来。诗人其实是在批评当时埋没人才、压制思想的风气。“万马齐喑”和“不拘一格”今天都是常用成语，“不拘一格降人才”更常被用来谈选拔和培养人才。

In English

In 1839 (the year jǐhài in the traditional calendar), Gong Zizhen resigned his post in Beijing and travelled south, writing more than three hundred short poems along the way — the “Miscellaneous Poems of 1839”. This is number 125. Passing Zhenjiang, he saw people worshipping the Jade Emperor and the gods of wind and thunder, and a Daoist priest asked him to write a prayer; this poem was his answer. The first two lines say that China’s life and energy depend on change as powerful as wind and thunder. Instead, “ten thousand horses all fall silent” — a picture of a whole society too stifled to speak — and that is truly sad. The last two lines, using the setting of a prayer, ask the Lord of Heaven to rouse himself again and send down talent “without sticking to one pattern”. Gong was really attacking a system that wasted able people and silenced new ideas, only a year before the Opium War. Two phrases from the poem, 万马齐喑 (“all voices silenced”) and 不拘一格 (“not bound by one standard”), are everyday idioms today, and the last line is a favourite quotation whenever people talk about finding and training talent.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Gong Zizhen (1792–1841) was a Qing scholar-official and reformist thinker who sharply criticised the stagnation of his time. He resigned and left Beijing in 1839, writing 315 short poems on the journey; he died two years later.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

恃 shì（依靠）。喑 yīn（哑，不能出声）。重 chóng（重新，不读 zhòng）。抖擞 dǒu sǒu。降 jiàng（降下，不读 xiáng）。机器朗读已把“喑”“重”替换为同音字。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

九州生气恃风雷，

jiǔ zhōu shēng qì shì fēng léi

Only wind and thunder can bring this land back to life;

万马齐喑究可哀。

wàn mǎ qí yīn jiū kě āi

ten thousand horses stand mute — a sorry sight indeed.

我劝天公重抖擞，

wǒ quàn tiān gōng chóng dǒu sǒu

Lord of Heaven, I beg you, shake yourself awake

不拘一格降人才。

bù jū yī gé jiàng rén cái

and send us gifted people of every kind.
