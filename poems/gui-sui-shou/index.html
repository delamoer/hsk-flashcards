导读About this poem

Written by a warlord in his fifties, this short poem admits that even a magic tortoise must die — then refuses to give up: an old warhorse in its stall still dreams of galloping a thousand miles.

第一章Stanza 1
01
神龟虽寿，犹有竟时。

直译LiteralThough the divine tortoise lives long, there is still a time when it ends.

意译MeaningThe sacred tortoise lives for ages, yet its days, too, run out.

02
腾蛇乘雾，终为土灰。

直译LiteralThe soaring serpent rides the mist, but finally becomes earth and ash.

意译MeaningThe flying serpent rides the mist, and still it comes to dust.

03
老骥伏枥，志在千里。

直译LiteralThe old thoroughbred lies by the trough; its will is set on a thousand li.

意译MeaningAn old warhorse rests at its manger, its heart still racing a thousand miles.

04
烈士暮年，壮心不已。

直译LiteralA man of ambition in his late years — his bold heart does not stop.

意译MeaningA man of spirit in his evening years never lets his great heart rest.

第二章Stanza 2
05
盈缩之期，不但在天；

直译LiteralThe span of growing and shrinking is not decided only by Heaven;

意译MeaningHow long or short our lives may be is not up to Heaven alone;

06
养怡之福，可得永年。

直译LiteralWith the blessing of nourishing a calm mind, one can gain long years.

意译Meaningkeep body and mind at peace, and you may win many years.

第三章Stanza 3
07
幸甚至哉，歌以咏志。

直译LiteralHow very fortunate! I sing to express my aims.

意译MeaningWhat joy this is! So I sing it out, to tell what my heart is set on.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这首诗是曹操五十多岁时写的，谈的是一个很大的问题：人老了、终究要死，那该怎么活？前四句先承认事实：传说中能活几千年的神龟，也有死的时候；能腾云驾雾的神蛇，最后也化成土灰。连神物都逃不过，何况人。接下来笔锋一转，用一个比喻说出全诗最有名的话：“老骥伏枥，志在千里”——一匹老了的千里马趴在马槽边，心里想的还是奔跑千里。“烈士暮年，壮心不已”，有抱负的人到了晚年，雄心也不会停止。“烈士”在这里不是今天“为国牺牲的人”的意思，而是“有远大志向的人”。后面四句又讲道理：寿命长短不全由老天决定，保养好身心，也能活得长久。全诗先抑后扬，既清醒又乐观，没有一点悲观叹老的味道。“老骥伏枥”后来成了成语，常用来形容老当益壮。

In English

Cao Cao wrote this in his fifties, and it faces a big question head-on: we all grow old and die, so how should we live? The first four lines simply accept the facts. The legendary tortoise that lives thousands of years still dies one day; the flying serpent that rides the mist ends up as dust. If even magical creatures can’t escape, people certainly can’t. Then the poem turns, with its most famous image: an old thoroughbred lies by its feeding trough, yet its heart is still set on running a thousand li. In the same way, a man of ambition keeps his drive even late in life. Note that 烈士 here means “a man of high ambition”, not its modern sense of “martyr”. The next lines argue that how long we live is not decided by Heaven alone — looking after body and mind can earn us more years. The poem moves from a sober admission to a confident lift, with no self-pity at all. 老骥伏枥 is still a common idiom today for someone old in years but young in spirit.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Cao Cao (155–220) was the most powerful warlord at the end of the Han dynasty and laid the foundations of the state of Wei; he was also a leading poet. This is the last of four sections of his suite “Walking Out of Xiamen Gate” (步出夏门行), traditionally dated to 207, after his victorious campaign against the Wuhuan in the north, when he was 53.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

老骥 jì（千里马）；伏枥 lì（马槽）；腾蛇也写作“螣蛇”，读 téng；盈缩之期 qī；终为土灰 wéi。

版本说明 About the text

采用统编教材（八年级上册）文本。本诗是曹操乐府《步出夏门行》四章中的最后一章，全章十四句（四言），网站按两句一行排版。“腾蛇”一作“螣蛇”。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

神龟虽寿，犹有竟时。

shén guī suī shòu, yóu yǒu jìng shí

The sacred tortoise lives for ages, yet its days, too, run out.

腾蛇乘雾，终为土灰。

téng shé chéng wù, zhōng wéi tǔ huī

The flying serpent rides the mist, and still it comes to dust.

老骥伏枥，志在千里。

lǎo jì fú lì, zhì zài qiān lǐ

An old warhorse rests at its manger, its heart still racing a thousand miles.

烈士暮年，壮心不已。

liè shì mù nián, zhuàng xīn bù yǐ

A man of spirit in his evening years never lets his great heart rest.

盈缩之期，不但在天；

yíng suō zhī qī, bù dàn zài tiān

How long or short our lives may be is not up to Heaven alone;

养怡之福，可得永年。

yǎng yí zhī fú, kě dé yǒng nián

keep body and mind at peace, and you may win many years.

幸甚至哉，歌以咏志。

xìng shèn zhì zāi, gē yǐ yǒng zhì

What joy this is! So I sing it out, to tell what my heart is set on.
