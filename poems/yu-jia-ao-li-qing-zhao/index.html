渔家傲（天接云涛连晓雾）

Yú Jiā Ào
Proud Fisherman (Tune): A Dream
李清照Lǐ Qīngzhào · Li Qingzhao宋 Song

导读About this poem

In a dream, Li Qingzhao sails up the Milky Way to the palace of Heaven. The Lord of Heaven asks where she is going; she answers that her road is long and her talent wasted — and asks the great wind to blow her boat to the islands of the immortals.

上阕First stanza
01
天接云涛连晓雾，星河欲转千帆舞。

直译LiteralThe sky meets the waves of cloud and joins the dawn mist; the Star River is about to turn, a thousand sails dance.

意译MeaningSky runs into billowing cloud and the mist of dawn; the Milky Way begins to wheel, and a thousand sails are dancing.

02
仿佛梦魂归帝所，闻天语，殷勤问我归何处。

直译LiteralIt seems my dreaming soul returns to Heaven’s palace; I hear Heaven’s voice, kindly asking me where I am going.

意译MeaningMy dreaming soul seems to reach the Lord of Heaven’s hall. A voice from the sky asks me, gently, where I am bound.

下阕Second stanza
03
我报路长嗟日暮，学诗谩有惊人句。

直译LiteralI reply: the road is long, I sigh that the day is late; I studied poetry, and in vain have startling lines.

意译MeaningI answer: the road is long, and the day is nearly gone. I have written lines that startle people — and what good are they?

04
九万里风鹏正举。

直译LiteralOn a ninety-thousand-li wind the roc is just rising.

意译MeaningOn a wind ninety thousand miles high, the great bird is soaring.

05
风休住，蓬舟吹取三山去！

直译LiteralWind, do not stop! Blow my reed boat away to the Three Mountains!

意译MeaningWind, don’t drop! Blow my little boat all the way to the islands of the immortals!

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

李清照的词大多细腻婉约，这首却气势雄伟，是她唯一一首“豪放”风格的作品。题目一作“记梦”，写一个梦境。上阕：天空连着翻滚的云海和晨雾，银河好像在转动，无数船帆在浪里起舞；她的魂魄好像回到了天帝住的地方，天帝很关心地问她要到哪里去。下阕是她的回答：路很长，天又快黑了；我学写诗，虽然有惊人的句子，却没有用处。接着笔锋一转：大鹏正乘着九万里长风高飞，风啊，不要停，把我的小船一直吹到海上的三座仙山去！“九万里风鹏正举”化用《庄子》里大鹏乘风而起的故事。这首词把她晚年漂泊无依的苦闷，变成了冲向远方的豪情，现实走投无路，梦里却要乘风远去。

In English

Most of Li Qingzhao’s lyrics are delicate and intimate, but this one is vast and bold — the only one of her works in the “heroic” (豪放) style. Its subtitle in some editions is “Recording a Dream”. In the first half, the sky merges with rolling clouds and dawn mist; the Milky Way seems to turn, and a thousand sails dance on the waves. Her spirit seems to return to the dwelling of the Lord of Heaven, who kindly asks where she is going. The second half is her answer: the road is long, and she sighs that the day is almost done; she has written lines that astonish people, but to no purpose. Then the poem takes off. She borrows a famous image from the philosopher Zhuangzi: a giant bird, the péng, that rides a wind ninety thousand lǐ high. “Wind, don’t stop,” she says, “blow my little boat all the way to the Three Isles of the immortals!” In real life she was a widowed refugee with nowhere to go, and a woman whose talent the world had no use for. In the dream she refuses to stay small. That turn from frustration to soaring freedom is what readers remember.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

Li Qingzhao (1084–c. 1155) is traditionally said to have written this around 1130, after following the fleeing Song court along the Zhejiang coast by sea. The sea voyage is often seen as the source of the poem’s images of clouds, waves and sails.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

仿佛 fǎng fú；殷勤 yīn qín；嗟 jiē；谩有 màn（徒然、空有）；鹏 péng；蓬舟 péng。“谩”机器朗读易读成 mán，已在朗读用字中换为同音字“慢”，音频需人工听验。

版本说明 About the text

采用通行教材文本，题一作《渔家傲·记梦》。异文：“仿佛”一作“彷佛”；“谩有”一作“漫有”。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

天接云涛连晓雾，星河欲转千帆舞。

tiān jiē yún tāo lián xiǎo wù, xīng hé yù zhuǎn qiān fān wǔ

Sky runs into billowing cloud and the mist of dawn; the Milky Way begins to wheel, and a thousand sails are dancing.

仿佛梦魂归帝所，闻天语，殷勤问我归何处。

fǎng fú mèng hún guī dì suǒ, wén tiān yǔ, yīn qín wèn wǒ guī hé chù

My dreaming soul seems to reach the Lord of Heaven’s hall. A voice from the sky asks me, gently, where I am bound.

我报路长嗟日暮，学诗谩有惊人句。

wǒ bào lù cháng jiē rì mù, xué shī màn yǒu jīng rén jù

I answer: the road is long, and the day is nearly gone. I have written lines that startle people — and what good are they?

九万里风鹏正举。

jiǔ wàn lǐ fēng péng zhèng jǔ

On a wind ninety thousand miles high, the great bird is soaring.

风休住，蓬舟吹取三山去！

fēng xiū zhù, péng zhōu chuī qǔ sān shān qù

Wind, don’t drop! Blow my little boat all the way to the islands of the immortals!
