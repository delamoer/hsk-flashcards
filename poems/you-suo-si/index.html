有所思

The One I Love

导读About this poem

A young woman hears her lover has been unfaithful — so she smashes the hairpin she meant to give him, burns it, and throws the ashes to the wind. Then, at dawn, she isn’t so sure. Fiery, honest and very modern.

题跋 · 解析Commentary

中文解析

这是一首汉代民歌，写一个女子的爱和恨，感情变化非常快。开头说：我想念的人在大海的南边。拿什么送给他呢？一支镶着两颗珍珠的玳瑁簪子，还用玉缠绕起来——礼物越精致，爱得越深。可是突然听说他变了心，她马上把簪子折断、砸碎、烧掉，烧完还要迎着风把灰扬掉，说：从今以后，不再想你！跟你一刀两断！这一段动作一个接一个，又急又狠，把愤怒写得淋漓尽致。可是最后几句又软了下来：当初我们相会的时候，鸡叫狗吠，哥哥嫂嫂应该早就知道了吧。唉！秋风呼呼地吹，天快亮了，东方马上就白了，到时候就明白该怎么办了。“妃呼豨”是叹气的声音。这首诗用的是长短不齐的句子（杂言），像人说话时的语气，忽长忽短，正好写出她又爱又恨、拿不定主意的心情。

In English

This Han folk song follows a young woman’s love turning into anger — fast. It begins: the one I love is far away, south of the great sea. What shall I send him? A tortoiseshell hairpin set with two pearls, wound round with jade. The finer the gift, the deeper the love. Then she hears he has someone else. At once she breaks the hairpin, smashes it, burns it — and, not satisfied, scatters the ashes into the wind. “From now on, I won’t think of you! I’m done with you!” One sharp action follows another, and her fury leaps off the page. But the last lines soften. When we used to meet, the roosters crowed and the dogs barked — surely my brother and his wife already know. “Fei hu xi!” — a long sigh. The autumn wind howls, dawn is coming, and soon the east will turn white; by then she will know what to do. The lines are of uneven length (a form called zayan, “mixed lines”), so they rise and fall like real speech — perfect for someone who can’t decide whether she loves him or hates him.

作者与背景 The poet & the moment

An anonymous song from the Han “Songs for Drums and Pipes” (鼓吹曲辞, also called 铙歌), a set of eighteen pieces preserved in the Song shu and the Yuefu shiji. Their wording is old and sometimes obscure; this one is among the clearest and most loved.

读音注意 Pronunciation notes

遗 wèi（赠送）；玳瑁 dài mào；簪 zān；绍缭 shào liáo；当风 dāng；嫂 sǎo；飔 sī；高 hào（同“皓”，天亮发白，不读 gāo）。妃呼豨 fēi hū xī：表示叹息的声音，也有注本读 bēi xū xī（同“悲歔欷”），网站暂读 fēi hū xī。

版本说明 About the text

采用《乐府诗集》卷十六所收汉铙歌《有所思》的通行文本，全诗十七句（杂言）。网站按语意断行，分为三段：赠簪、毁簪、犹豫。“妃呼豨”三字是表声的衬词，《乐府诗集》未录，《宋书·乐志》等本有，网站从通行本保留。

全文 · 拼音 Full text with pinyin

有所思，乃在大海南。

yǒu suǒ sī, nǎi zài dà hǎi nán

The one I love lives far away, south of the great sea.

何用问遗君？双珠玳瑁簪，

hé yòng wèn wèi jūn, shuāng zhū dài mào zān

What gift shall I send him? A tortoiseshell hairpin set with two pearls,

用玉绍缭之。

yòng yù shào liáo zhī

wrapped round and round with jade.

闻君有他心，拉杂摧烧之。

wén jūn yǒu tā xīn, lā zá cuī shāo zhī

Now I hear his heart has turned — I break the pin to bits and burn it.

摧烧之，当风扬其灰。

cuī shāo zhī, dāng fēng yáng qí huī

Smash it, burn it, and throw the ashes to the wind!

从今以往，勿复相思！

cóng jīn yǐ wǎng, wù fù xiāng sī

From this day on, I’ll miss you no more!

相思与君绝！

xiāng sī yǔ jūn jué

Between you and me, the longing is over!

鸡鸣狗吠，兄嫂当知之。

jī míng gǒu fèi, xiōng sǎo dāng zhī zhī

When we met, the roosters crowed and the dogs barked — my brother and his wife must know.

妃呼豨！

fēi hū xī

Ah — alas!

秋风肃肃晨风飔，

qiū fēng sù sù chén fēng sī

The autumn wind rushes, the dawn wind is cold;

东方须臾高知之。

dōng fāng xū yú hào zhī zhī

soon the east will grow light, and then I’ll know my mind.
